Cabinet approves expansion of PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana

The Union Cabinet chaired by the Prime Minister on Monday approved the expansion of the PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana to 100 additional districts with an outlay of ₹11,440 crore for 2025-26 to 2030-31.

The scheme converges 36 existing schemes across 11 departments. Districts are selected on three indicators: low crop productivity, moderate crop intensity and below-average credit disbursement.

Each district will prepare a District Agriculture and Allied Activities Plan through a committee chaired by the District Collector. Progress will be tracked monthly on a dashboard against 117 key performance indicators.


The scheme is expected to benefit about 1.7 crore farmers.
