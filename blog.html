💡 CSA Exam Pro Tips

📚 Know the Fundamentals

60% of questions test core concepts: tables, forms, lists, and basic administration. Master these first.

🔧 Hands-On Practice

Get a free PDI (Personal Developer Instance) and practice every concept. Theory alone won't pass the exam.

⏱️ Time Management

60 questions in 90 minutes = 1.5 min per question. Flag difficult ones and move on.

🎯 Focus Areas

UI Policies vs Client Scripts vs Business Rules — know when to use each. This is heavily tested.

📖 Official Docs

The exam references docs.servicenow.com. Familiarize yourself with navigation and terminology.

🔄 Update Sets

Understand the complete lifecycle: create, capture, transfer, preview, commit. Common exam topic.
