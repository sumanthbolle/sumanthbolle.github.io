## Week 1 (5 to 11 October), day by day

Polity first, from Laxmikanth, one hour of new reading on each weekday:

- Monday: chapters 1 and 2 (historical background, making of the Constitution)
- Tuesday: chapters 3 and 4 (salient features, Preamble)
- Wednesday: chapters 5 and 6 (Union and its territory, citizenship)
- Thursday: chapter 7, first half (Fundamental Rights, Articles 12 to 22)
- Friday: chapter 7, second half (Articles 23 to 35) and chapter 8 (Directive Principles)
- Saturday: Spectrum chapters 1 to 3 (advent of the Europeans, Bengal, expansion), three hours, then the CSAT diagnostic: the 2024 CSAT paper from the PYQ repo, timed, two hours. The diagnostic replaces Saturday's usual 45-minute CSAT slot.
- Sunday: a 25-question PYQ test on Polity chapters 1 to 8 drawn from the 2015 to 2026 papers, one hour of error review, two hours of catch-up or Spectrum, and 45 minutes reviewing the diagnostic's errors.

Every day: 10 Polity PYQs and 20 minutes of current affairs from the June 2026 compilation. The revision queue starts on Tuesday with Monday's chapters.
