## Current affairs (20 minutes, from the previous day's official releases)

- Cabinet approved continuing the National Social Assistance Programme for 2026-27 to 2030-31 with Rs 53,428 crore; sub-schemes renamed under 'Pradhan Mantri'; disability pension threshold cut from 80% to 40% disability, eligible from birth. Anchor: Economy and social sector: NSAP (1995), Article 41 DPSP, old-age and widow pensions. Source: [PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2323264). Verified (PIB).
- CCEA approved the strategic sale of 60.72% of IDBI Bank (30.48% from the Government, 30.24% from LIC) to a Fairfax subsidiary for about Rs 52,878 crore; the Government keeps 15%, LIC 19%. Anchor: Economy: disinvestment versus strategic sale, public-sector banks, the 'fit and proper' test. Source: [PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2323468). Verified (PIB).
- Wholesale price inflation for September 2026 was 11.39% year on year, up from 9.92% in August, led by mineral oils, food articles, crude and coal; the Output PPI stood at 112.5. Anchor: Economy: WPI versus CPI, base years, the new producer price index. Source: [PIB](https://pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=2323098). Verified (PIB).
