Until today every scheduled run ended with a message to you and a push notification. Two weeks of those have gone unanswered, and a chat thread is a poor place to see a preparation as a whole. So the destination changes.

From today each run writes one entry here:

- a morning brief around 06:25 Singapore time;
- an evening check-in around 21:20;
- a weekly review on Sunday morning;
- the month's current-affairs note, updated by the morning brief as items pass the examinability test;
- a deadline alarm on 13 January, 25 January, 1 February and 3 May 2027;
- a plan entry whenever the plan is re-cut.

The entries before this one were written back from the daily log so the record starts on Day 0 rather than today.

Two things stay the same. The daily log is still what the coordinator reads, so a day that is not logged is still reported as not logged, and a week that is not followed is still re-cut on Sunday. And nothing is added to the source list from here; the plan's sources are fixed.

One thing you still have to do: a log line each evening. It can be as short as "0 h". The check-in will say what it says either way, but it can only score what it is given.
