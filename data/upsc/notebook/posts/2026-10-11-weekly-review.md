## The decision

The plan's own rule applies: a week that is more than a fifth undone is absorbed by the next week by compressing lower-yield chapters, not by adding hours.

Week 2 (12 to 18 October) is Week 1's content, unchanged, at the same 20 hours. Every subject block in the Build phase shifts one week later: Polity in Weeks 2 to 4 and 5 to 6, Geography in Weeks 7 to 8, Economy in Weeks 9 to 10, Environment in Week 11 and the weekends before it.

The lost week is paid for at the tail. The Week 11 buffer is dropped. Ancient and Medieval history plus Art and Culture, which together carry roughly 6 to 10 questions, go from a week and a half to one weekend (Week 11, Saturday and Sunday) of PYQ-pointed chapters only. Science and Technology basics move to the Converge phase and come in through current affairs. Polity, Modern History, Geography, Economy and Environment keep full depth.

One more thing is on the record. If Week 2 also logs zero, the 20-hours-a-week assumption is wrong, and next Sunday cuts the plan to the hours that actually exist. It will not be repeated a second time.
