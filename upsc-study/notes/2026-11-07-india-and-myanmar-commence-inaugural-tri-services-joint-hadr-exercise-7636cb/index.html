India and Myanmar Launch First Tri-Services Joint HADR Exercise ‘Rahat Mitra’

India and Myanmar started their maiden bilateral Tri-Services Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief (HADR) exercise called ‘Rahat Mitra’ on 5 November 2026 in Chennai. This exercise is important for UPSC aspirants as it highlights India’s defence diplomacy, regional cooperation, and disaster response mechanisms under the ‘Neighbourhood First’ policy.

The inaugural ‘Rahat Mitra’ exercise strengthens India-Myanmar defence cooperation and enhances joint disaster relief capabilities.

Why is this in the news?

India and Myanmar commenced their first-ever bilateral Tri-Services Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief exercise ‘Rahat Mitra’ on 5 November 2026 in Chennai.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is Exercise Rahat Mitra and its purpose?

  • Exercise overview. Rahat Mitra is the inaugural bilateral Tri-Services Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief (HADR) exercise between India and Myanmar conducted from 5 to 6 November 2026 in Chennai.
  • Objective. The exercise aims to strengthen bilateral cooperation, foster mutual trust, and enhance coordination between the Defence Forces of India and Myanmar in disaster relief operations.
  • Theme. The theme of the exercise is ‘Together for Humanity, Stronger through Partnership’, emphasizing humanitarian cooperation and regional disaster response.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does Rahat Mitra enhance India-Myanmar relations?

  • Defence cooperation. The exercise provides a platform for professional exchanges, sharing experiences, and improving interoperability among the three services of both countries.
  • Personnel involvement. More than 300 personnel from the Indian and Myanmar Army, Navy, and Air Force participate, reflecting a shared commitment to humanitarian assistance.
  • Strategic significance. Rahat Mitra reflects India’s ‘Neighbourhood First’ policy and the MAHASAGAR vision, aiming to promote security and growth across regions.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does this exercise matter for disaster management and regional stability?

  • Disaster response readiness. The joint exercise enhances coordination and preparedness for humanitarian assistance and disaster relief during crises in the region.
  • Peace and stability. It reinforces the shared resolve of India and Myanmar to uphold peace, stability, and provide timely humanitarian aid.
  • Regional cooperation. Rahat Mitra strengthens regional collaboration, which is crucial for managing natural disasters and emergencies effectively.

Back to basics: Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief (HADR)

HADR operations involve coordinated efforts by military and civilian agencies to provide aid and relief during natural or man-made disasters. These operations focus on saving lives, alleviating suffering, and restoring normalcy. Joint exercises like Rahat Mitra help improve interoperability, coordination, and readiness among armed forces for effective disaster response.

  1. 1 only

  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 incorrectly states the coordinating service and the number of personnel; the exercise is coordinated by the Indian Navy and involves more than 300 personnel.

Mains question (15 marks)

Examine the significance of India’s bilateral defence exercises like ‘Rahat Mitra’ in enhancing regional cooperation and disaster management.

Examine: Explain how bilateral defence exercises contribute to regional security and humanitarian assistance.

Intro: Bilateral defence exercises are tools to strengthen military cooperation and disaster response capabilities.

  • They enhance interoperability and mutual trust between participating countries’ armed forces.
  • Such exercises improve preparedness for humanitarian assistance and disaster relief operations.
  • They reinforce strategic partnerships and support India’s neighbourhood policy for regional stability.

Conclusion: Defence exercises like Rahat Mitra play a key role in building resilient regional cooperation frameworks.
