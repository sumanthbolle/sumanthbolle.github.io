AI's Expanding Role in Healthcare and Life Sciences in India

The STPI Tech Conclave at IIT BHU and Altimetrik's new Hyderabad centre highlight India's growing focus on AI-driven healthcare innovation. These developments are important for understanding government-industry-academia collaboration and policy support in AI for health.

India is advancing AI applications in healthcare and life sciences through government initiatives, academic partnerships, and industry centres of excellence.

Why is this in the news?

The Ministry of Electronics and IT's STPI Tech Conclave on October 18, 2026, and Altimetrik's opening of a Healthcare and Life Sciences Centre of Excellence in Hyderabad spotlight AI's growing role in healthcare innovation in India.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

What was discussed at the STPI Tech Conclave on AI in healthcare?

  • Event overview. The STPI Tech Conclave at IIT BHU brought together government officials, academia, healthcare experts, and startups to explore AI applications for health, inclusion, and citizen well-being.
  • Keynote insights. Shri S. Krishnan, Secretary of MeitY, emphasized AI's potential to improve healthcare accessibility, clinical decision-making, and service delivery, especially for underserved communities.
  • AI applications highlighted. AI's role in disease screening, medical diagnostics, remote ICU monitoring, and AI-assisted surgical planning was underscored as transformative for healthcare quality and affordability.
  • Collaboration focus. The conclave stressed stronger collaboration among IIT BHU, STPI, industry, and startups to develop scalable healthcare and assistive technology solutions.
  • Startup ecosystem. STPI's role in connecting startups with investors and venture capital was highlighted to build a sustainable innovation ecosystem beyond traditional tech hubs.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How is Altimetrik contributing to AI in healthcare and life sciences?

  • New Centre of Excellence. Altimetrik opened a Healthcare and Life Sciences Centre of Excellence in Hyderabad to support AI work across drug discovery, clinical development, manufacturing, patient engagement, and care delivery.
  • Facility features. The 22,000 sq ft facility houses AI engineers and life sciences specialists collaborating to build AI agents that meet regulatory standards and ensure accountability.
  • Strategic location. Hyderabad's pharma expertise, technology talent, and presence of pharmaceutical companies make it an ideal location for this dedicated AI centre.
  • Global workforce. Altimetrik's global workforce exceeds 11,000, with the Hyderabad centre expanding the role of India teams in global AI engineering delivery.

[2] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does AI in healthcare and life sciences matter for governance and social welfare?

  • Healthcare accessibility. AI can make healthcare more accessible and affordable, especially for communities with limited access to medical services.
  • Innovation ecosystem. Government initiatives like IndiaAI Mission and STPI's support for startups foster innovation, translational research, and entrepreneurship in AI-driven healthcare.
  • Inclusive well-being. AI applications in assistive technologies promote inclusion and improve citizen well-being by addressing diverse healthcare needs.
  • Policy and collaboration. Collaboration among government, academia, healthcare institutions, and startups is vital to translate AI research into scalable, practical solutions.

Back to basics: Artificial Intelligence in Healthcare

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to computer systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as diagnosis and decision-making. In healthcare, AI can analyze medical data to assist in disease detection, treatment planning, and patient monitoring, improving efficiency and accuracy. Life sciences involve the study of living organisms and include drug discovery and clinical research, where AI helps accelerate innovation and regulatory compliance.
