NHRC Takes Suo Motu Cognizance of Deaths and Abuse Cases in Uttar Pradesh

The National Human Rights Commission (NHRC) has taken suo motu cognizance of multiple serious incidents in Uttar Pradesh, including the death of a child due to electrocution, maternal deaths during pregnancy treatment, and sexual abuse allegations in schools. This highlights NHRC's role in protecting human rights and ensuring accountability in governance.

NHRC's suo motu actions in Uttar Pradesh demonstrate its mandate to address human rights violations and hold authorities accountable.

Why is this in the news?

On 5 November 2026, NHRC took suo motu cognizance of reported deaths and abuse cases in Uttar Pradesh, issuing notices to relevant authorities for detailed reports.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau[3] Press Information Bureau

What incidents did NHRC take suo motu cognizance of in Uttar Pradesh?

  • Child's electrocution death. NHRC took suo motu cognizance of the death of a 12-year-old student in Ballia district who died after coming into contact with an 11 KV power line near the school roof while plucking a bottle gourd as instructed by teachers.
  • Maternal deaths in Ghaziabad. NHRC took suo motu notice of 24 women who died during pregnancy treatment or childbirth from April to October 2026 at various government and private hospitals in Ghaziabad district.
  • Sexual abuse allegations in Aligarh. NHRC took suo motu cognizance of reported sexual abuse of two teachers by the brother of a private school owner in Aligarh district, with complaints of harassment and threats of job removal.

Why does NHRC's suo motu action matter for human rights protection?

  • Mandate to address violations. NHRC acts on reports or media coverage to investigate serious human rights violations without waiting for formal complaints, ensuring timely intervention.
  • Accountability of authorities. NHRC issued notices to officials such as District Magistrates, Police Superintendents, and department heads to submit detailed reports within two weeks, promoting governance accountability.
  • Focus on vulnerable groups. The cases involve vulnerable populations including children, pregnant women with high-risk pregnancies, and female teachers facing sexual harassment, highlighting NHRC's role in protecting these groups.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau[3] Press Information Bureau

What actions have been taken following these incidents?

  • Suspensions and recommendations. Following the child's death in Ballia, two teachers were suspended and termination of a Shiksha Mitra's services was recommended.
  • Legal and administrative notices. NHRC issued notices to Uttar Pradesh Power Corporation Ltd., health department officials, and police authorities to investigate and report on the incidents.
  • Victims seeking justice. In the sexual abuse case, victims approached the Senior Superintendent of Police after the school management failed to act on their complaints.

Back to basics: National Human Rights Commission (NHRC)

The NHRC is a statutory body established to protect and promote human rights in India. It can take suo motu cognizance of human rights violations based on media reports or other information, enabling it to initiate investigations without formal complaints. NHRC issues notices to government officials and authorities to ensure accountability and remedial action.
