Union Cabinet Approves Delimitation Bill Increasing Seats by 50%

The Union Cabinet cleared a Constitution Amendment Bill that increases seats in Parliament and state legislatures by 50% across all States while maintaining the current ratio of seats until 2051. This affects electoral representation and federal balance, making it important for UPSC aspirants studying electoral reforms and federalism.

The Union Cabinet approved a delimitation bill that raises seats by 50% for all States, preserving the existing seat ratio until 2051.

Why is this in the news?

On November 27, 2026, the Union Cabinet approved a Constitution Amendment Bill for delimitation, increasing seats by 50% across all States and maintaining the current seat ratio until 2051.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What did the Union Cabinet approve regarding delimitation?

  • Delimitation Bill details. The Cabinet approved a Constitution Amendment Bill that increases the number of seats in Parliament and state legislatures by 50% for all States.
  • Seat ratio preservation. The Bill maintains the present ratio of seats among States until the year 2051.
  • Women’s reservation provision. The Bill also provides for women’s reservation through delimitation.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for elections and federalism?

  • Impact on representation. Increasing seats changes the parliamentary and legislative assembly representation, affecting political power distribution among States.
  • Federal balance maintained. Keeping the current seat ratio until 2051 preserves the relative strength of States in Parliament, maintaining federal balance.
  • Political coordination. The Bill is a key topic among NDA leaders, influencing alliance coordination and political strategies ahead of Parliament’s special sitting.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the political reactions and context?

  • NDA meeting. Andhra Pradesh Chief Minister Chandrababu Naidu attended an NDA leaders’ meeting in Delhi following the Cabinet’s approval to discuss the Bill and related issues.
  • Concerns from southern States. There are concerns about the delimitation’s impact on southern States’ parliamentary representation.
  • Women’s Reservation Bill linkage. The delimitation Bill is linked with the Women’s Reservation Bill, both expected to be discussed prominently in political forums.

Back to basics: Delimitation

Delimitation is the process of redrawing boundaries of electoral constituencies based on population changes to ensure fair representation. It affects the number and distribution of seats in Parliament and state legislatures. This process helps maintain equal representation as populations grow or shift.
