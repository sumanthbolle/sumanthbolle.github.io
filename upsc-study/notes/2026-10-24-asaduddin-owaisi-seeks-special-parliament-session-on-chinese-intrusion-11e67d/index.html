Asaduddin Owaisi Demands Special Parliament Session on Chinese Intrusions in Arunachal Pradesh

MP Asaduddin Owaisi has called for a special session of Parliament to discuss Chinese troop intrusions in Arunachal Pradesh and the government's border security policy. This demand highlights issues of parliamentary accountability and transparency on national security matters.

Asaduddin Owaisi has urged the government to hold a special parliamentary session to address Chinese intrusions in Arunachal Pradesh and to provide a truthful account of the border situation.

Why is this in the news?

On October 24, 2026, MP Asaduddin Owaisi sought a special Parliament session to clarify Chinese troop intrusions in Arunachal Pradesh and the government's handling of border security.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What did Asaduddin Owaisi demand regarding Chinese intrusions?

  • Special Parliament session. Owaisi demanded a special session of Parliament to discuss the Chinese intrusions and the government's China policy.
  • Transparency and accountability. He called for an independent and comprehensive assessment of the border situation and criticized the government for lack of transparency and political accountability.
  • Clarification on intrusions. He sought details on locations of Chinese troops intruding into Arunachal Pradesh and incidents where Indian forces were stopped from patrolling.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for parliamentary oversight and national security?

  • Parliamentary discussion lacking. Owaisi pointed out that no discussion or questions on the border intrusions have taken place in Parliament.
  • Government communication criticized. He criticized Prime Minister Modi for not holding press conferences and for earlier claims denying Chinese presence on Indian territory.
  • Core issue of territorial integrity. The demand emphasizes the need for truth and political accountability on issues affecting India's territorial integrity.

Back to basics: Parliamentary oversight and accountability

Parliamentary oversight is the process by which Parliament monitors and reviews the actions of the executive government to ensure transparency and accountability. It includes debates, question hours, and special sessions to discuss important national issues. This mechanism is crucial for maintaining democratic governance and ensuring that government policies, especially on sensitive matters like national security, are subject to scrutiny and public accountability.
