Why in news

On 26 November 2026, MoSPI launched the State Dashboard on the eSankhyiki portal. It brings selected State-level indicators from multiple statistical products into theme-based profiles, allowing users to explore charts, consult metadata and download data for analysis and planning.

Static anchor

Official statistics are systematically produced data disseminated by public statistical institutions for understanding social, economic and administrative conditions. A statistical dashboard is a digital interface that organises selected indicators for search, visualisation, contextual interpretation and reuse.

Background

  • MoSPI describes eSankhyiki as a one-stop window for official statistics, with searchable datasets, interactive visualisations, metadata, machine-readable downloads and APIs.
  • The VISTAR initiative emphasises strengthening subnational statistics and digital tools across statistical processes, including dissemination.
  • Its Support for Statistical Strengthening component seeks to improve the capacity of States and Union Territories to produce more granular statistics, including at district level, and use them in local planning and policymaking.
