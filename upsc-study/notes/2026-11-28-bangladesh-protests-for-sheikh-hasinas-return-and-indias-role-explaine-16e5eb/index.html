Bangladesh Protests Demand Sheikh Hasina’s Return Amid India-Bangladesh Tensions

Bangladesh is witnessing protests after the government rejected former Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina’s request to return from India, where she has lived since 2024 following her ouster amid unrest. This situation affects India-Bangladesh relations and regional stability, making it relevant for UPSC aspirants studying India’s neighbourhood policy.

Bangladesh’s political unrest over Sheikh Hasina’s denied return highlights challenges in India-Bangladesh relations and regional diplomacy.

Why is this in the news?

The protests erupted after Bangladesh’s government rejected Sheikh Hasina’s request to return on December 16, 2026, the 55th anniversary of Bangladesh’s Liberation War, triggering unrest and diplomatic tensions with India.

[1] The Indian Express · Secondary

What triggered the protests in Bangladesh?

  • Denied re-entry. The Bangladesh government rejected Sheikh Hasina’s application for a fresh passport and National Identity Card, blocking her return from India, which sparked widespread protests.
  • Background unrest. The protests follow earlier unrest including garment workers demanding wage hikes and violent incidents in Hasina’s home districts, reflecting broader political and economic dissatisfaction.

[1] The Indian Express · Secondary

Why is Sheikh Hasina’s return opposed by the Bangladesh government?

  • Ouster after protests. Hasina resigned and left for India in 2024 after mass protests that led to about 1,400 deaths, triggered by a court ruling on government job quotas for war veterans’ descendants.
  • Political rivalry. The current government led by PM Tarique Rahman of the Bangladesh Nationalist Party opposes Hasina’s return and demands her extradition, reflecting a strong anti-incumbency wave after her 16 years in power.
  • Legal conviction. In absentia, Hasina was sentenced to death by Bangladesh’s International Crimes Tribunal in 2025 for crimes against humanity, charges she denies.

[1] The Indian Express · Secondary

What explains public support for Sheikh Hasina?

  • Economic performance. Hasina’s tenure saw Bangladesh’s economy grow from $109 billion in 2009 to $437 billion in 2023, and her ouster coincided with economic slowdown and political instability under the current government.
  • Garment sector impact. The garment industry, which accounts for 80% of exports, suffered due to political unrest and investor hesitation, contributing to public dissatisfaction with the current regime.

[1] The Indian Express · Secondary

How is India involved in this situation?

  • Hasina’s stay in India. Since 2024, Sheikh Hasina has been residing in India, which has caused diplomatic strain as Bangladesh demands her extradition.
  • India’s diplomatic stance. India regards the extradition request as a legal matter and emphasizes a positive bilateral relationship while avoiding interference in Bangladesh’s internal affairs.
  • Dialogue attempts. In 2024, Prime Minister Narendra Modi reached out to Bangladesh leaders for dialogue, but these efforts were not reciprocated, complicating India-Bangladesh relations.

Back to basics: India and its neighbourhood policy

India’s neighbourhood policy focuses on maintaining stable and cooperative relations with neighbouring countries to ensure regional security and economic integration. It involves diplomatic engagement, economic cooperation, and managing cross-border issues while respecting the sovereignty of neighbouring states. Political developments in neighbouring countries can directly affect India’s strategic interests and bilateral ties.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the recent Bangladesh protests and India’s involvement: 1. Sheikh Hasina was sentenced to death in absentia by Bangladesh’s International Crimes Tribunal in 2025. 2. India has officially agreed to extradite Sheikh Hasina to Bangladesh following the government’s request. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because India has stated that the extradition request is a legal matter under consideration and has not agreed to extradite Sheikh Hasina.
