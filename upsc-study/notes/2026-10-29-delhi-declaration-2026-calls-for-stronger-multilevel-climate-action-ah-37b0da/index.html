Delhi Declaration 2026 Urges Stronger Local Climate Action Before COP31

The ARISE Cities Forum 2026 in New Delhi adopted the Delhi Declaration on Local Action for Global Climate Goals, calling for enhanced multilevel collaboration of cities in global climate governance ahead of COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye. This is important for UPSC aspirants to understand India's role in climate diplomacy and the emphasis on urban local bodies in climate action.

The Delhi Declaration 2026 highlights the critical role of cities in implementing global climate goals through multilevel governance and local action.

Why is this in the news?

The Delhi Declaration was adopted at the ARISE Cities Forum 2026 held on 27-28 October 2026 in New Delhi, ahead of COP31 scheduled in Antalya, Türkiye.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the Delhi Declaration 2026 and its significance?

  • Adoption and purpose. The Delhi Declaration on Local Action for Global Climate Goals was adopted at the ARISE Cities Forum 2026 to emphasize the role of cities from the Global South in global climate governance and to carry urban priorities to COP31.
  • Forum details. The Forum was held on 27-28 October 2026 at the India Habitat Centre, organised by ICLEI South Asia and NIUA, supported by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs, with over 200 participants from 50+ cities across 18+ countries.
  • Theme and call. The Forum's theme was 'From Bharat to Antalya — Commit. Implement. Deliver,' calling for stronger multilevel collaboration and recognition of cities as indispensable partners in climate action.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the key commitments of the Delhi Declaration 2026?

  • Ten shared commitments. The Declaration sets out ten commitments including advancing the global climate agenda, accelerating adaptation, promoting circularity, achieving energy transition aligned with 1.5°C, prioritising just transitions, institutionalising multilevel climate finance, upholding science and data, creating joint multilevel action spaces, championing Global South urban leadership, and strengthening youth and community leadership.
  • Focus on integrated local action. It stresses integrated local action, just clean energy transition, accessible finance, and meaningful community participation as priorities for effective climate governance.
  • Link to COP31. The Declaration will be presented at COP31 in Antalya, reinforcing that global climate goals require integrating local priorities and implementation capacities into decision-making.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does the Delhi Declaration matter for climate governance and India?

  • Cities as key climate actors. The Declaration positions cities as key contributors to international climate negotiations, highlighting the need for localizing Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) and closing the gap between national commitments and local implementation.
  • Multilevel governance emphasis. It calls for cooperation among all government levels to meet Paris Agreement targets, addressing urban challenges like heat, water stress, infrastructure, and mobility.
  • India's climate diplomacy. India's approach, reflected in the Declaration, aligns with the philosophy of Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam and promotes collaboration, sustainability, resilience, and equity in climate action.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does the Forum reflect practical climate action in cities?

  • Delhi's urban initiatives. Delhi has promoted public transport, expanded electric bus fleets, and improved last-mile connectivity with support from ICLEI South Asia to improve air quality.
  • Community and leadership roles. The Declaration highlights the importance of emotional connection to cities, youth and community leadership, and the need for local leaders to have finance and partnerships to act effectively.
  • Launch of biodiversity map. The Forum also launched the Delhi Biodiversity Map developed by ICLEI South Asia, marking a step towards integrating biodiversity in urban climate action.

Back to basics: Multilevel Climate Governance

Multilevel climate governance refers to the coordination and cooperation among different levels of government—local, regional, national, and international—to effectively address climate change. Cities play a crucial role as they are centers of population, economic activity, and emissions, and can implement localized climate actions that contribute to national and global goals. Integrating local priorities with national commitments ensures more effective and inclusive climate policies.
