RBI Governor Urges States to Adopt Prudent Borrowing Strategies

RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra advised states to adopt prudent borrowing strategies and ensure quality spending to maintain long-term fiscal health. This guidance was given at the 36th Conference of State Finance Secretaries, highlighting the importance of fiscal federalism and efficient public finance management.

The RBI Governor emphasized prudent borrowing and quality expenditure by states to sustain fiscal health and recommended leveraging digital tools like ULI and P-CBDC for better financial management.

Why is this in the news?

On October 27, 2026, RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra addressed the 36th Conference of State Finance Secretaries, advising states on prudent borrowing and quality spending to ensure fiscal health.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What advice did the RBI Governor give to states on borrowing?

  • Prudent borrowing strategies. The Governor urged states to adopt prudent borrowing strategies to maintain long-term fiscal health and avoid excessive debt.
  • Quality spending importance. He highlighted the need for states to ensure quality expenditure to sustain fiscal stability over time.
  • Efficient cash management. States were advised to implement efficient cash management practices to optimize their financial resources.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How did the RBI Governor suggest states use technology for financial management?

  • Unified Lending Interface (ULI). States were encouraged to onboard onto the ULI platform to streamline and improve lending processes.
  • Programmable Central Bank Digital Currency (P-CBDC). The Governor recommended using P-CBDC for Direct Benefit Transfers (DBT) to enhance transparency and efficiency.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What was the context and significance of the conference?

  • Participants. The conference included finance secretaries from 27 states, three Union Territories, and senior officials from the Union Ministry of Finance, Controller General of Accounts, Comptroller and Auditor General of India, and RBI.
  • Topics reviewed. Discussions covered market borrowings by states, short-term financial accommodation from RBI, and adoption of Benchmark Issuance Strategy.
  • Resource material released. The Governor released a booklet titled 'Compendium on Cash and Debt Management for State Governments' summarizing key concepts and good practices.

Back to basics: Fiscal Federalism and State Borrowing

Fiscal federalism refers to the financial relations between different levels of government, especially between the Union and states. States borrow funds through market borrowings and short-term financial accommodations to meet their expenditure needs. Prudent borrowing involves managing debt sustainably to avoid fiscal stress, while quality spending ensures that borrowed funds are used effectively for development and welfare. Efficient cash and debt management help maintain fiscal discipline and macroeconomic stability.

Mains question (15 marks)

Examine the importance of prudent borrowing strategies and quality expenditure for states in the context of fiscal federalism in India.

Examine: Explain why states need to adopt prudent borrowing and quality spending, and how it affects fiscal federalism and macroeconomic stability.

Intro: Prudent borrowing and quality expenditure are essential for maintaining fiscal health and ensuring sustainable development at the state level.

  • Prudent borrowing helps states avoid excessive debt and fiscal stress, ensuring long-term financial stability.
  • Quality spending ensures that borrowed funds are effectively utilized for development and welfare, enhancing fiscal outcomes.
  • Efficient cash management and adoption of digital tools like ULI and P-CBDC improve transparency and financial discipline.
  • Such practices strengthen fiscal federalism by enabling states to manage their finances responsibly while coordinating with the Union government.

Conclusion: Therefore, prudent borrowing and quality expenditure are critical for sustaining fiscal federalism and promoting economic stability in India.
