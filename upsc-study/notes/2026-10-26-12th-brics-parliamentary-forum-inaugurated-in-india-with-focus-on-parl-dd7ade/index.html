12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum inaugurated in India to boost parliamentary cooperation

India hosted the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum on 26-27 October 2026, focusing on strengthening parliamentary dialogue and cooperation among member and partner countries. This event is important for UPSC aspirants as it highlights India's role in multilateral parliamentary diplomacy and its efforts to address global challenges through parliamentary engagement.

The 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum in India emphasized parliamentary diplomacy to enhance cooperation on global issues like terrorism, climate change, and institutional reforms.

Why is this in the news?

India hosted the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum on 26-27 October 2026, with the Lok Sabha Speaker and Prime Minister engaging with parliamentary delegations to promote dialogue and cooperation.

What is the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum and its focus?

  • Forum overview. The 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum was held in the Parliament House Complex, New Delhi, on 26-27 October 2026, to facilitate parliamentary dialogue among BRICS member and partner countries.
  • Theme and sessions. The forum's overarching theme was 'Strengthening Parliaments for People-Centric Development,' featuring four high-level sessions on Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation, and Sustainability.
  • Special meetings. A special meeting of Chairpersons of International Affairs Committees from BRICS parliaments was held to discuss parliamentary diplomacy, capacity building, and best practice exchanges.

How did India and Russia engage during the forum?

  • Bilateral meeting. Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla held a bilateral meeting with Russian State Duma Chairman Vyacheslav Volodin to discuss strengthening cooperation at global fora and combating terrorism.
  • India-Russia ties. The two countries marked 80 years of diplomatic relations in 2027, with emphasis on their Special and Privileged Strategic Partnership and Russia's support for India's permanent UNSC membership.
  • Parliamentary exchanges. Both sides stressed regular exchanges of parliamentary delegations, especially among young and women parliamentarians, and cooperation in trade, industry, and cultural spheres.

  • Global challenges. The forum emphasized collective efforts to address terrorism, climate change, and the need to make international institutions more responsive to changing global circumstances.
  • Institutional resilience and AI governance. Discussions included institutional resilience, AI governance, South-South cooperation, and sustainability, guided by the four pillars of India's 2026 BRICS Chairship.
  • Parliamentary diplomacy. The forum aimed to strengthen parliamentary dialogue and cooperation to enhance global governance and development outcomes.

Back to basics: Parliamentary diplomacy

Parliamentary diplomacy involves interactions between legislators of different countries to promote mutual understanding, cooperation, and peaceful resolution of issues. It complements traditional diplomacy by enabling dialogue on global challenges, sharing best practices, and fostering people-to-people contacts. Parliamentary forums like BRICS Parliamentary Forum provide platforms for such engagement among member countries.
