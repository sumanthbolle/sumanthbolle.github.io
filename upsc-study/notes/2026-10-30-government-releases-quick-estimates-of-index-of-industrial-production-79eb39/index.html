Government releases quick estimates of Index of Industrial Production for September 2026

The Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation released the quick estimates of the Index of Industrial Production (IIP) for September 2026, showing a 6.7% year-on-year growth. This data is crucial for UPSC aspirants to understand current industrial growth trends and the methodology of economic indicators.

The IIP for September 2026 grew by 6.7%, driven by manufacturing and electricity sectors, reflecting industrial performance and economic health.

Why is this in the news?

The Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation released the quick estimates of the Index of Industrial Production for September 2026 on 28 October 2026.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the key features of the IIP for September 2026?

  • Overall growth. The IIP recorded a 6.7% year-on-year growth in September 2026, down from 8.0% in August 2026.
  • Sectoral performance. Manufacturing grew by 7.6%, Electricity & Gas Supply by 11.2%, while Mining & Quarrying declined by 6.2%.
  • Top manufacturing contributors. Major growth came from motor vehicles (19.0%), electrical equipment (33.9%), and rubber and plastics products (19.9%).
  • Use-based classification. Capital goods grew by 10.4%, Intermediate goods by 12.2%, and Primary goods by 4.0% in September 2026.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why is the base year revision of the IIP important?

  • Base year update. The base year was revised from 2011–12 to 2022–23 to better reflect current industrial structure and production patterns.
  • State-level alignment. A workshop held in October 2026 emphasized aligning State-level IIP with national methodology for consistency and comparability.
  • Methodological improvements. The revision includes adoption of NIC 2025, improved data quality, and better treatment of sectors like gas, water supply, sewerage, and waste management.
  • Capacity building. The workshop aimed to strengthen State statistical offices' technical capacity for effective monthly IIP compilation.

[2] Press Information Bureau

How does the Index of Services Production relate to industrial data?

  • ISP release. MoSPI also released the Index of Services Production (ISP) for August 2026, covering 19 sub-sectors with base year 2024–25.
  • Service sector growth. Notable growth was seen in retail trade (19.9%), repair services (17.6%), and insurance (16.5%), while air transport declined by 9.6%.
  • Experimental data. ISP data is published on an experimental basis to test quality and resilience before regular use.

Back to basics: Index of Industrial Production (IIP)

The IIP measures the short-term changes in the volume of production of a basket of industrial products during a given period relative to a base year. It covers key sectors like mining, manufacturing, electricity, and water supply. The base year is periodically revised to reflect current industrial structures and production patterns, ensuring the index remains relevant and accurate for economic analysis and policy-making.

What happens next?

  • Improving data quality. States need to strengthen primary data collection and minimize measurement errors to ensure accurate industrial statistics.
  • Regular monthly compilation. States are encouraged to build capacity for timely and comparable monthly IIP data compilation aligned with national standards.
