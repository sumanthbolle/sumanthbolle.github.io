Raksha Mantri Highlights Operational Readiness and Cyber Resilience in Defence Forces

Raksha Mantri Rajnath Singh addressed the Indian Air Force personnel at Bagdogra on November 7, 2026, emphasizing the need for high operational readiness with a focus on domain awareness, cyber resilience, and information integrity to counter evolving security threats. This is important for UPSC aspirants to understand the changing nature of security challenges and India's defence preparedness.

The Defence Forces must maintain high operational readiness and strengthen cyber and information security to face modern asymmetric and technological threats.

Why is this in the news?

Raksha Mantri's speech at Air Force Station, Bagdogra on November 7, 2026, highlighted the evolving security threats and the need for cyber resilience and operational readiness.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What did the Raksha Mantri emphasize about defence preparedness?

  • Operational readiness focus. Raksha Mantri called for the Defence Forces to maintain the highest level of operational readiness, emphasizing domain awareness, cyber resilience, and information integrity to counter emerging threats.
  • Complex security threats. He noted that warfare now includes asymmetric warfare, proxy wars, cyber-attacks, drone swarms, information operations, and narrative management beyond conventional border engagements.
  • Role beyond soldiering. The Defence Forces must counter adversaries who avoid direct confrontation and spread misleading information via social media to sow doubt among soldiers and local populations.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why is Bagdogra strategically important for India's security?

  • Strategic location. Bagdogra is a crucial link in India's security framework, especially for the North-Eastern region, serving as a robust shield for the eastern sector.
  • Stability and preparedness. The Air Warriors deployed at Bagdogra play a fundamental role in maintaining regional stability through their courage, dedication, and preparedness.
  • Community engagement. Raksha Mantri urged Defence Forces to strengthen ties with local communities to build trust and weaken adversary conspiracies.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How is technology and indigenous capability shaping defence forces?

  • Indigenous technology. The Government's push for ‘Aatmanirbhar Bharat’ is reflected in growing contributions from Indian start-ups and industries in drones, anti-drone systems, and advanced military equipment.
  • Skill development. Defence Forces are embracing indigenous technologies and acquiring new skills in drone operations and cyber security.
  • Technology-driven military. Making the military increasingly technology-driven is essential to address future security challenges effectively.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What recent policy was highlighted for Agniveers and welfare of Defence personnel?

  • Second Tour of Duty. The Cabinet approved a 'Second Tour of Duty' for Agniveers, allowing them another opportunity to serve the nation and potentially enroll as regular soldiers after two years, based on willingness and selection criteria.
  • Government commitment. Raksha Mantri reiterated the Government's commitment to the welfare of Defence Forces personnel and their families, acknowledging their sacrifices especially during festivals.

Back to basics: Operational Readiness and Cyber Resilience

Operational readiness means the Defence Forces are always prepared to respond quickly and effectively to any security threat. Cyber resilience refers to the ability to protect, detect, and respond to cyber-attacks that can disrupt military operations or spread misinformation. Together, these ensure the military can handle both traditional and modern technological threats.

Mains question (15 marks)

Intro: Modern defence preparedness requires adapting to new types of warfare including cyber and asymmetric threats.

  • Discuss the expanded security threats beyond conventional warfare, such as cyber-attacks and information operations.
  • Explain the importance of operational readiness and domain awareness in maintaining security.
  • Describe the role of indigenous technology and skill development in enhancing military capabilities.
  • Highlight policies like the 'Second Tour of Duty' that support personnel welfare and operational effectiveness.

Conclusion: Strengthening operational readiness and cyber resilience is essential for India's defence forces to meet future security challenges.
