Petrol Export Duty Cut to Nil; Diesel Export Levy Raised by ₹3 per Litre

The government removed the export duty on petrol and increased the export levy on diesel and aviation turbine fuel for a 15-day period starting October 31, 2026. This fiscal policy adjustment aims to balance domestic fuel availability amid global supply challenges and rising diesel exports.

The government cut petrol export duty to zero while raising diesel export levy by ₹3 per litre to manage domestic supply and respond to global fuel market conditions.

Why is this in the news?

On October 31, 2026, the government revised export levies on petrol, diesel, and aviation turbine fuel amid rising diesel exports and global supply constraints.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What changes did the government announce in export duties?

  • Petrol export duty cut. The export duty on petrol was reduced to nil for the next 15-day period starting October 31, 2026.
  • Diesel export levy increased. The export levy on diesel was raised by ₹3 per litre, from ₹8.5 to ₹11.5 per litre.
  • ATF export levy hiked. The export levy on aviation turbine fuel (ATF) was increased by 50 paise per litre, from ₹10.5 to ₹11 per litre.
  • No change in domestic excise duties. Excise duty rates on petrol and diesel for domestic consumption remained unchanged.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why were these export duty changes made?

  • Manage domestic fuel availability. The government adjusts export levies fortnightly to ensure adequate domestic retail fuel supply by discouraging excessive exports.
  • Respond to global supply crunch. The increase in diesel export levy responds to high diesel exports amid a global supply crunch, especially in Europe.
  • Monitor export volumes. India’s diesel exports reached 700 kilo barrels per day in October 2026, the highest since September 2025.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How will diesel export volumes change going forward?

  • Expected decline in Nov-Dec. Diesel exports are expected to ease to 500-550 kilo barrels per day in November-December 2026 as domestic demand peaks seasonally.
  • Recovery in early 2027. Exports may recover to 550-600 kilo barrels per day in the first quarter of 2027.
  • Maintenance and capacity impact. Higher refinery maintenance in the second quarter of 2027 will constrain exports, with new capacity additions expected later in the year.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the mechanism for reviewing export levies?

  • Fortnightly review. Since March 27, 2026, the government conducts fortnightly reviews of export levies based on average international benchmark prices.
  • Purpose of review. These reviews aim to balance domestic fuel availability and international market conditions by adjusting export duties.

Back to basics: Export Duty and Levies

Export duties are taxes imposed by a government on goods leaving the country to regulate exports and protect domestic supply. Levies on fuel exports are adjusted to balance international market demands with domestic availability, especially for essential commodities like petrol and diesel. Fortnightly reviews help the government respond dynamically to changing global prices and supply conditions.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements regarding recent changes in India's fuel export duties: 1. The export duty on petrol was reduced to zero starting October 31, 2026. 2. The export levy on diesel was increased by ₹3 per litre for the same period. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (c). A common trap is confusing the changes in petrol and diesel export duties; petrol duty was cut to nil, while diesel levy was increased by ₹3 per litre.
