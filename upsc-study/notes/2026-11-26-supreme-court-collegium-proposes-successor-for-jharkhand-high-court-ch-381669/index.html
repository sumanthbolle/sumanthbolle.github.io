Supreme Court Collegium Proposes Jharkhand High Court Chief Justice Successor

The Supreme Court Collegium recommended Justice Vibhu Bakhru as the next Chief Justice of Jharkhand High Court days before the incumbent's retirement. This highlights the judicial appointment process and the role of the Collegium system, important for understanding judiciary functioning in India.

The Supreme Court Collegium proposed the transfer of Karnataka High Court Chief Justice Vibhu Bakhru as Jharkhand High Court Chief Justice before the incumbent's retirement.

Why is this in the news?

On November 24, 2026, the Supreme Court Collegium headed by Chief Justice of India Surya Kant proposed the successor for the Jharkhand High Court Chief Justice just days before the incumbent's retirement.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What decision did the Supreme Court Collegium make regarding Jharkhand High Court?

  • Collegium proposal. The Collegium proposed transferring Karnataka High Court Chief Justice Vibhu Bakhru as the new Chief Justice of Jharkhand High Court before the incumbent Justice M.S. Sonak's retirement on November 27, 2026.
  • Related transfers. Justice M.S. Ramachandra Rao, Chief Justice of Tripura High Court, was recommended to be transferred as Chief Justice of Karnataka High Court, and Justice Vivek Singh Thakur of Himachal Pradesh High Court was recommended as Chief Justice of Tripura High Court.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this appointment process matter?

  • State government concerns. Jharkhand Chief Minister Hemant Soren wrote to the Chief Justice of India expressing concern about the delay in appointing a successor and the disruption caused by interim arrangements.
  • Administrative continuity. The Chief Minister emphasized that a regular Chief Justice is necessary for stable judicial administration and institutional planning in the State High Court.
  • Memorandum of Procedure. The appointment follows the Memorandum of Procedure, requiring Collegium recommendation and Union government clearance.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does the Collegium system function in judicial appointments?

  • Collegium composition. The Supreme Court Collegium is headed by the Chief Justice of India and includes senior Supreme Court judges.
  • Role in appointments. It recommends transfers and appointments of Chief Justices and judges of High Courts and the Supreme Court, which the government then clears.

Back to basics: Supreme Court Collegium and Judicial Appointments

The Supreme Court Collegium is a body of senior Supreme Court judges led by the Chief Justice of India that recommends appointments and transfers of judges in the higher judiciary. Its recommendations are crucial for maintaining judicial independence and are followed by the government as per the Memorandum of Procedure. The Collegium system ensures a consultative process among judges for judicial appointments.
