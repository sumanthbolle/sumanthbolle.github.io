RBI Releases 47th Half-Yearly Report on Foreign Exchange Reserves Management

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) published its 47th half-yearly report on the management of foreign exchange reserves for April to September 2026, showing an increase in reserves. This report highlights RBI's commitment to transparency in managing the country's foreign exchange reserves, a key aspect for economic stability and policy formulation.

The RBI's half-yearly report reveals India's foreign exchange reserves rose from US$ 691.11 billion to US$ 737.78 billion between April and September 2026, reflecting effective reserve management and transparency.

Why is this in the news?

On November 25, 2026, the RBI released its 47th half-yearly report on foreign exchange reserves management, continuing its practice since 2004 to promote transparency and disclosure.

What is the RBI's half-yearly report on foreign exchange reserves?

  • Purpose of the report. Since February 2004, the RBI has compiled and published half-yearly reports on the management of foreign exchange reserves to enhance transparency and disclosure.
  • Content focus. The report details the status and management of India's foreign exchange reserves, providing data and insights on reserve levels and their changes over the period.

Back to basics: Foreign Exchange Reserves and Their Management

Foreign exchange reserves are assets held by a country's central bank in foreign currencies, used to back liabilities and influence monetary policy. Managing these reserves involves maintaining adequate levels to support the currency, meet external obligations, and ensure economic stability. Transparency in reporting reserve status helps build trust among investors and supports effective economic governance.
