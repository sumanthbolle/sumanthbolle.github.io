India’s Shift to Proactive Counter-Terrorism Actions Explained by Raksha Mantri

Raksha Mantri Rajnath Singh highlighted India’s transition from diplomatic condemnation to proactive military operations like Operation Sindoor, Balakot Airstrike, and Surgical Strike to eliminate terrorism at its source. This shift and emerging threats such as white-collar terrorism and lone-wolf attacks are crucial for understanding India’s internal security strategy.

India has moved from diplomatic responses to proactive counter-terrorism operations, emphasizing advanced technology and citizen vigilance against evolving threats.

Why is this in the news?

Raksha Mantri Rajnath Singh’s address at the Defence Conclave on October 15, 2026, outlined India’s proactive counter-terrorism approach and emerging security challenges.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What marks India’s shift in counter-terrorism strategy?

  • Proactive military operations. Operations Sindoor, Balakot Airstrike, and Surgical Strike represent India’s move from diplomatic condemnation to direct action targeting terrorism at its source.
  • Stronger response to terrorism. Any terror act originating from Pakistan is met with an even stronger response, reflecting a firm stance on cross-border terrorism.
  • Reduced terrorist shelf life. Since 2014, most terrorists have been neutralised within a year of taking up arms, showing increased security effectiveness.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the emerging security threats India faces?

  • 5D Security Threat. Disinformation, Destabilisation, Disruption, Deception, and Destruction define the complex security challenges in the digital age.
  • Evolving terrorism forms. Terrorism now includes lone-wolf attacks and white-collar terrorism, which are harder to detect and prevent.
  • Citizen vigilance importance. Incidents like the co-pilot’s alleged terror attempt highlight the role of alert individuals in thwarting attacks.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How is India enhancing its defence preparedness?

  • Smart power approach. Combining advanced weaponry, AI, precision-guided weapons, and cost-effective technologies to address modern security challenges.
  • Defence reforms and indigenisation. Focus on building an Aatmanirbhar defence ecosystem with over 500 companies engaged, 75% of modernisation budget for Indian industries, and extensive indigenisation of equipment.
  • Expanding defence industrial base. Inclusion of start-ups, MSMEs, and private sector under a ‘Whole of the People Approach’ to strengthen national defence.

Back to basics: Counter-terrorism strategy

Counter-terrorism involves measures to prevent, detect, and respond to terrorist threats and attacks. It includes intelligence gathering, military operations, law enforcement, and citizen participation. Modern strategies integrate technology, such as AI and precision weapons, to address evolving threats like lone-wolf attacks and cyber terrorism.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about India’s counter-terrorism approach as highlighted by Raksha Mantri Rajnath Singh: 1. Operations Sindoor, Balakot Airstrike, and Surgical Strike represent a shift to proactive military action against terrorism. 2. Since 2014, the shelf life of most terrorists has increased due to enhanced security challenges.

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the shelf life of most terrorists has actually fallen to less than a year since 2014, not increased.
