Lok Sabha Speaker Urges AI Use in Defence Land Management and Highlights Women’s Leadership

Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla called for strengthening defence land management using modern technology and AI to safeguard over 1.7 million acres and preserve cantonment heritage. He also emphasized women’s leadership and innovation at the BRICS Women Leaders Summit, advocating a shift from women empowerment to women-led development.

Lok Sabha Speaker stressed AI-driven defence land governance and the critical role of women’s leadership in addressing global challenges.

Why is this in the news?

On 20-21 November 2026, Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla addressed the Defence Estates Organisation centenary and the BRICS Women Leaders Summit, highlighting AI in defence land management and women’s leadership in global governance.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

What did the Lok Sabha Speaker say about defence land management?

  • Modern technology and AI. Om Birla urged the Defence Estates Organisation to use AI, digitisation, GIS, and drone technology to manage and protect over 1.7 million acres of defence land effectively.
  • Key priorities. He emphasized securing defence assets, curbing encroachments, ensuring timely land availability for armed forces, and preserving cantonment heritage and green cover.
  • Balancing needs. The Speaker highlighted the need to balance operational requirements, anti-encroachment efforts, and civilian welfare while avoiding disputes.
  • Civic initiatives. He praised initiatives like schools, healthcare, skill training, water conservation, and environmental protection in cantonments.
  • Institutional continuity. Om Birla encouraged combining the organisation’s century-old institutional knowledge with younger personnel’s technical agility for stronger future systems.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How did the Lok Sabha Speaker highlight women’s leadership at the BRICS Summit?

  • Collective leadership and innovation. He called on women leaders from BRICS countries to use their leadership, experience, and innovation to address global challenges like climate change, terrorism, and cybercrime.
  • Shift to women-led development. The Speaker stressed moving beyond women empowerment to women-led development, ensuring women shape development direction, not just participate.
  • Technology and ethics. He advocated responsible AI use guided by human-centred approaches, ethics, compassion, and accountability across sectors including defence and women’s entrepreneurship.
  • India’s initiatives. Om Birla cited Indian efforts like Self-Help Groups, Lakhpati Didi, Drone Didi, and the Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam to promote women’s economic independence and political participation.
  • Legacy of Banasthali Vidyapith. He lauded the institution’s growth from five to 18,000 women students trained in diverse fields, contributing to leadership in armed forces and science.

[2] Press Information Bureau

Why do these developments matter for UPSC aspirants?

  • Governance and technology. The use of AI and digital tools in defence land management exemplifies modern governance innovation and public resource protection.
  • Women’s role in policy. Understanding the shift from women empowerment to women-led development is key for questions on gender equality, social inclusion, and leadership.
  • Global cooperation. The BRICS Summit highlights multilateral collaboration on shared challenges like climate change and security, relevant for international relations.
  • Constitutional and social context. References to India’s constitutional guarantee of equality and political participation of women link to polity and society syllabus.

Back to basics: Artificial Intelligence in Governance

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to computer systems that can perform tasks typically requiring human intelligence, such as data analysis and decision-making. In governance, AI can improve efficiency, transparency, and accuracy in managing public resources and services. Its ethical use requires human-centred design, accountability, and safeguarding citizens’ rights.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements: 1. The Defence Estates Organisation manages over 1.7 million acres of defence land and uses AI and drone technology for land management. 2. The BRICS Women Leaders Summit emphasized moving from women empowerment to women-led development and highlighted initiatives like Self-Help Groups and Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam in India.

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (c). Statement 1 is correct about defence land management and technology use; Statement 2 correctly reflects the BRICS Summit's focus on women-led development and Indian initiatives.

Mains question (15 marks)

Examine how the integration of modern technology and AI in defence land management and the emphasis on women’s leadership contribute to effective governance and national security.

Examine: Analyze the role of technology and gender leadership in governance and security with examples from recent developments.

Intro: Modern governance increasingly relies on technology and inclusive leadership to address complex challenges.

  • Explain how AI and digital tools enhance defence land management, ensuring asset protection and operational readiness.
  • Discuss the importance of preserving cantonment heritage while balancing military and civilian needs.
  • Analyze the shift from women empowerment to women-led development and its implications for policy and social transformation.
  • Highlight the role of international cooperation, such as BRICS, in addressing global challenges through collective leadership.

Conclusion: Integrating technology with inclusive leadership strengthens governance frameworks and supports national security and social progress.
