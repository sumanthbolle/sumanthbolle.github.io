India Extends ₹15,000 Crore Aid to Nepal for Flood Reconstruction

India announced a ₹15,000 crore development package to assist Nepal in post-flood reconstruction and long-term disaster management after the August floods in the Bhotekoshi River. This aid highlights India’s role in regional cooperation and disaster resilience in its neighbourhood.

India’s ₹15,000 crore assistance to Nepal supports reconstruction and strengthens disaster preparedness, reflecting its neighbourhood policy.

Why is this in the news?

On November 23, 2026, India announced a ₹15,000 crore development package for Nepal following devastating floods in August, during talks between Prime Ministers Narendra Modi and Balendra Shah.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What does the ₹15,000 crore aid package to Nepal include?

  • Package components. The package consists of ₹11,500 crore as a rupee-denominated concessionary line of credit for energy projects and ₹3,500 crore in direct grants for infrastructure reconstruction.
  • Purpose of aid. The assistance aims to support Nepal’s reconstruction after the Bhotekoshi River flood and to strengthen its capacity to manage future natural disasters.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does this aid fit into India-Nepal bilateral relations?

  • Diplomatic engagement. The announcement was made during the visit of Nepal’s Prime Minister Balendra Shah to India, marking a significant step in bilateral cooperation.
  • Border and connectivity initiatives. Both leaders instructed their foreign secretaries to work on outstanding boundary issues and launched infrastructure projects like the Sarayu-Kamala Express train and Dharchula road bridge.
  • Trade and transit facilitation. India agreed to provide Nepal access to transit cargo at Deendayal Port in Kandla and Paradip Port in Odisha, enhancing trade connectivity.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this aid matter for UPSC preparation?

  • Neighbourhood policy example. This aid package exemplifies India’s neighbourhood policy focused on regional cooperation, disaster management, and infrastructure development.
  • Disaster management cooperation. The assistance strengthens Nepal’s disaster resilience, aligning with UPSC topics on disaster management and bilateral relations.
  • Strategic and cultural ties. The visit and aid reinforce cultural and strategic ties, including tourism promotion and educational collaboration such as the upcoming IIT campus in Nepal.

Back to basics: India’s Neighbourhood Policy

India’s neighbourhood policy involves fostering friendly relations, economic cooperation, and strategic partnerships with neighbouring countries. It includes providing development assistance and disaster relief to support regional stability and growth. Disaster management cooperation is a key aspect, enabling India and its neighbours to jointly prepare for and respond to natural calamities.
