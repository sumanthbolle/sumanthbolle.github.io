Island Fisheries Central to India’s Blue Economy Strategy

India is focusing on developing fisheries in Lakshadweep and Andaman & Nicobar Islands to boost sustainable marine economy and exports. This initiative is key for UPSC aspirants to understand India's Blue Economy and sustainable fisheries management.

India’s Blue Economy push prioritizes island fisheries in Lakshadweep and Andaman & Nicobar Islands to enhance sustainable marine resource use and economic growth.

Why is this in the news?

On 10 November 2026, the Ministry of Fisheries highlighted island fisheries as central to India’s Blue Economy, focusing on Lakshadweep and Andaman & Nicobar Islands for sustainable fisheries development.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the significance of island fisheries in India’s Blue Economy?

  • Marine resource base. India has a coastline of approximately 11,099 km and an Exclusive Economic Zone (EEZ) of nearly 24 lakh sq. km, with a marine fisheries potential of 58.6 lakh MT supporting nearly 50 lakh fishers.
  • Economic contribution. The fisheries sector contributes significantly to food security, nutrition, employment, income generation, and seafood exports valued at around ₹73,890 crore in FY 2025-26.
  • Island territories’ role. Lakshadweep and Andaman & Nicobar Islands together account for nearly 40% of India’s EEZ, providing access to rich oceanic fishing grounds and major tuna migration routes.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How is the government promoting fisheries development in these islands?

  • Investment allocation. Under PMMSY, ₹218 crore has been allocated for Andaman & Nicobar Islands and ₹175 crore for Lakshadweep to strengthen fisheries development.
  • Strategic focus. The Union Budget 2025-26 emphasized sustainable harnessing of fisheries resources from the EEZ and High Seas, focusing on these island groups.
  • Infrastructure modernization. Investments are directed towards fish landing centres, ice plants, cold-chain networks, onboard preservation, fish aggregating devices, boat-building yards, and value-addition units.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the key fisheries and economic activities targeted in the islands?

  • Tuna fisheries. The government aims to double tuna production in Lakshadweep to 50,000 tonnes in five years using modern vessels and sustainable fishing methods; Andaman & Nicobar Islands have a dedicated Tuna Cluster to modernize fleets and expand cold storage.
  • Seaweed cultivation. Lakshadweep is notified as a dedicated Seaweed Cluster to develop large-scale cultivation, seed banks, hatcheries, and capacity building for applications in food, nutraceuticals, cosmetics, pharmaceuticals, and bio-industrial products.
  • Ornamental fisheries. Lakshadweep hosts over 300 reef-associated ornamental species; the plan includes establishing rearing units, promoting conservation-based collection, and improving market access for sustainable aquarium species.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why do these initiatives matter for sustainable development and strategic interests?

  • Sustainability and value creation. The strategy balances production growth with ecological safeguards, aiming to move beyond resource extraction towards value addition within island economies.
  • Livelihood and community benefits. The focus is on enabling fishers, local enterprises, and island communities to participate meaningfully in domestic and export value chains.
  • Strategic maritime presence. These islands extend India’s presence across the Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal, important for accessing underutilized marine resources and enhancing maritime security.

Back to basics: Blue Economy

The Blue Economy refers to the sustainable use of ocean resources for economic growth, improved livelihoods, and jobs while preserving the health of ocean ecosystems. It includes activities like fisheries, aquaculture, maritime transport, and marine biotechnology. Sustainable fisheries management ensures that fish stocks are harvested at sustainable levels, balancing economic benefits with ecological conservation.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about India’s island fisheries development: 1. Lakshadweep and Andaman & Nicobar Islands together account for nearly 40% of India’s Exclusive Economic Zone. 2. The government has allocated ₹218 crore for fisheries development in Lakshadweep under PMMSY. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because ₹218 crore is allocated for Andaman & Nicobar Islands, while Lakshadweep has been allocated ₹175 crore.

Mains question (15 marks)

Examine the role of island fisheries in India’s Blue Economy strategy and their significance for sustainable economic development.

Examine: Explain how island fisheries contribute to India’s Blue Economy and sustainable development goals.

Intro: India’s island fisheries are central to its Blue Economy strategy, focusing on sustainable resource use and economic growth.

  • Discuss the strategic importance of Lakshadweep and Andaman & Nicobar Islands in India’s marine geography and fisheries potential.
  • Explain government initiatives including investment, infrastructure modernization, and value chain development in tuna, seaweed, and ornamental fisheries.
  • Analyze the balance between economic growth, ecological sustainability, and community participation in these initiatives.

Conclusion: Island fisheries development exemplifies India’s approach to sustainable Blue Economy growth, integrating economic, ecological, and strategic objectives.
