PM-AJAY Advances Inclusive Development Through Adarsh Grams and Projects

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment implements PM-AJAY to empower Scheduled Caste communities via Adarsh Gram development, socio-economic projects, and educational infrastructure. This scheme's progress and funding details are essential for understanding government efforts in inclusive rural development and social welfare.

PM-AJAY promotes socio-economic and educational empowerment of Scheduled Castes through model village development, livelihood projects, and hostel infrastructure.

Why is this in the news?

On October 16, 2026, the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment reported progress under PM-AJAY, highlighting Adarsh Gram declarations, socio-economic projects, and hostel completions.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the PM-AJAY scheme and its objectives?

  • Scheme overview. PM-AJAY is a centrally funded scheme by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment aimed at socio-economic and educational empowerment of Scheduled Caste communities.
  • Three components. The scheme includes development of Scheduled Caste-dominated villages into Adarsh Grams, Grants-in-Aid for socio-economic projects at State and District levels, and construction and repair of hostels.
  • Adarsh Gram concept. An Adarsh Gram is a model village where basic services are accessible, minimum needs of all sections are met, and disparities are minimized, focusing on Scheduled Caste-majority villages.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What progress has PM-AJAY made so far?

  • Village coverage. Over 47,200 villages benefiting more than 47.79 lakh citizens have been covered, with 18,093 villages declared Adarsh Grams as of September 8, 2026.
  • Development works and plans. A total of 46,944 development works have been completed and 27,032 Village Development Plans prepared under the scheme.
  • Hostel infrastructure. 785 student hostels have been completed, with Rs 61.46 crore allocated for FY 2026-27 to build two hostels accommodating 264 students.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How is PM-AJAY funded and monitored?

  • Funding details. Approximately Rs 1,730 crore was released as Grants-in-Aid directly to States and Districts from FY 2021-22 to FY 2025-26, supporting livelihood and skill development projects.
  • Fund utilization. Up to 50% of funds may be used for gap-filling in selected Adarsh Gram villages; 5% for Technical Support Groups and Project Implementation Units; 2% for hostel construction and repair.
  • Monitoring mechanisms. The PM-AJAY Mobile App and Portal enable real-time digital processing, improving transparency, accountability, and efficiency in scheme implementation.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does PM-AJAY matter for inclusive growth and social welfare?

  • Empowerment focus. The scheme addresses multiple empowerment dimensions including infrastructure, livelihoods, skill development, and education for Scheduled Castes.
  • Reducing disparities. By developing Adarsh Grams and improving socio-economic indicators, PM-AJAY aims to reduce disparities in Scheduled Caste-majority villages.
  • Supporting education. Hostel construction under the scheme facilitates access to quality education for Scheduled Caste students from rural and remote areas.

Back to basics: Inclusive Growth and Welfare Schemes

Inclusive growth aims to ensure that economic benefits reach all sections of society, especially marginalized groups. Welfare schemes are government initiatives designed to improve socio-economic conditions by providing infrastructure, education, and livelihood opportunities. Such schemes often focus on vulnerable communities to reduce disparities and promote equitable development.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about PM-AJAY: 1. PM-AJAY includes development of Adarsh Grams, Grants-in-Aid for socio-economic projects, and hostel construction. 2. The scheme is funded partly by the Central Government and partly by State Governments equally. Which of the statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because PM-AJAY is a 100% centrally funded scheme, though States and UTs may supplement with additional resources.

Mains question (15 marks)

Discuss how schemes like PM-AJAY contribute to inclusive growth and socio-economic empowerment of marginalized communities in India.

Discuss: Explain the role of PM-AJAY in promoting inclusive development through infrastructure, livelihood, and education interventions for Scheduled Castes.

Intro: Inclusive growth requires targeted welfare schemes to uplift marginalized communities.

  • PM-AJAY develops Scheduled Caste-majority villages into model Adarsh Grams with improved infrastructure and services.
  • The scheme provides Grants-in-Aid for livelihood and skill development projects to increase income and reduce disparities.
  • Hostel construction under PM-AJAY facilitates access to quality education for Scheduled Caste students from rural areas.
  • Use of technology in monitoring enhances transparency and efficient delivery of welfare benefits.

Conclusion: PM-AJAY exemplifies a multi-dimensional approach to inclusive growth by addressing infrastructure, livelihood, and education needs of Scheduled Castes.
