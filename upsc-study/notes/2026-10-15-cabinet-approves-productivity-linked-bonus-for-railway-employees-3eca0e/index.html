Cabinet approves 78-day Productivity Linked Bonus for Railway Employees

The Union Cabinet approved a Productivity Linked Bonus (PLB) equivalent to 78 days' wages, amounting to ₹2009.61 crore, for about 11.40 lakh non-gazetted railway employees. This incentive rewards the excellent performance of railway staff during 2025-26, supporting motivation and improved service delivery.

The Cabinet approved a ₹2009.61 crore PLB for 78 days to 11.40 lakh railway employees recognizing their performance in 2025-26.

Why is this in the news?

On 14 October 2026, the Union Cabinet chaired by the Prime Minister approved the payment of a Productivity Linked Bonus for railway employees for the year 2025-26.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the Productivity Linked Bonus approved for railway employees?

  • Bonus details. The Cabinet approved a PLB equivalent to 78 days' wages, totaling ₹2009.61 crore, to be paid to about 11,40,770 non-gazetted railway employees.
  • Purpose of PLB. The PLB acts as an incentive to motivate railway employees to improve the performance of the Railways.
  • Payment timing. The PLB is paid annually before the Durga Puja/Dusshera holidays.
  • Maximum amount per employee. The maximum amount payable per eligible railway employee is ₹17,951 for 78 days.
  • Eligible categories. The bonus is paid to various categories including Track Maintainers, Loco Pilots, Train Managers (Guards), Station Masters, Supervisors, Technicians, Technician Helpers, Pointsmen, Ministerial staff, and other Group 'C' staff.

Why was the PLB approved and what was the performance context?

  • Performance recognition. The PLB was approved in recognition of the excellent performance by the Railway staff during the year 2025-26.
  • Railway performance in 2025-26. In 2025-26, Indian Railways loaded a record cargo of 1670 Million Tonnes and carried nearly 7.41 Billion passengers.
  • Freight growth. There was a 6.14% growth in freight loading in September 2026 compared to September 2025.

Back to basics: Productivity Linked Bonus (PLB)

Productivity Linked Bonus is a monetary incentive given to employees based on the performance of their organization. It is intended to motivate employees to improve productivity and efficiency. In public sector units like Indian Railways, PLB is paid annually and linked to the organization's operational performance, such as freight loading and passenger services.
