Cabinet Approves Rs.2,814 Crore Scheme for Critical Mineral Processing

The Union Cabinet approved a Capex Support Scheme with an outlay of Rs.2,814 crore over five years to promote domestic processing of lithium and nickel. This scheme aims to reduce import dependence, strengthen supply chain resilience, and support sectors like electric mobility and defence.

The Cabinet's Capex Support Scheme provides 30% capital subsidy to set up lithium and nickel processing plants, enhancing India's critical mineral value chain and energy security.

Why is this in the news?

On November 4, 2026, the Union Cabinet chaired by the Prime Minister approved the Capex Support Scheme to Promote Critical Mineral Processing with a financial outlay of Rs.2,814 crore for lithium and nickel processing.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the Capex Support Scheme for Critical Mineral Processing?

  • Scheme overview. The scheme provides targeted capital expenditure support of Rs.2,814 crore over five years (FY 2026-27 to 2030-31) to establish domestic processing facilities for critical minerals, specifically lithium and nickel.
  • Objectives. It aims to develop domestic processing capabilities to add value within India, reduce import dependence, and build resilient end-to-end value chains for critical minerals.
  • Subsidy details. A capital subsidy of 30% of eligible investment will be provided for setting up four processing plants: two lithium plants (minimum 30 kt capacity each) and two nickel plants (minimum 50 kt capacity each), with maximum support capped at Rs.500 crore per lithium beneficiary and Rs.900 crore per nickel beneficiary.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

Why is this scheme important for India?

  • Supply chain risks. Critical mineral processing is currently concentrated globally in few locations, exposing supply chains to geopolitical disruptions, price volatility, and supply restrictions, which threaten economic security and national security.
  • Import dependence. India is substantially dependent on imports for refined intermediates and high-purity materials like lithium, nickel, and cobalt due to limited domestic beneficiation and processing infrastructure.
  • Strategic sectors supported. Processed minerals will support electric mobility, Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), electronics, defence, and speciality steel sectors, aiding decarbonisation and industrial growth.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

How will beneficiaries be selected and supported?

  • Selection process. Beneficiaries will be selected through a two-stage process: first, technical qualification assessment; second, financial bidding based on the least capital expenditure support sought.
  • Number of beneficiaries. For each mineral, two beneficiaries will be selected, making a total of four processing plants supported under the scheme.
  • Disbursement of incentives. Incentives will be disbursed in three tranches linked to achievement of specified project milestones.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

How does this scheme fit into India's broader energy and industrial strategy?

  • Atmanirbhar Bharat. The scheme advances the Atmanirbhar Bharat objective by promoting resilient domestic processing infrastructure and reducing import dependence in critical mineral value chains.
  • Energy transition support. By supporting lithium and nickel processing, the scheme underpins sectors essential for energy transition such as electric vehicles and battery storage.
  • Complementary initiatives. It complements other government schemes like the Rs.14,500 crore Viability Gap Funding for grid-scale energy storage systems, which also aims to strengthen renewable energy integration and domestic manufacturing.

Back to basics: Critical Minerals and Their Processing

Critical minerals like lithium and nickel are essential raw materials for advanced technologies including batteries and electronics. Processing involves refining ores into high-purity compounds usable in manufacturing. Domestic processing reduces reliance on imports, enhances supply chain security, and adds economic value within the country.
