Why in news

The Press Information Bureau issued a 20 October 2026 backgrounder marking ten years of UDAN. It presents Modified UDAN as the next phase, focused on stronger aviation infrastructure, operational sustainability, wider regional connectivity and greater Centre-State/UT partnership.

Static anchor

Regional connectivity is the provision of affordable and reliable transport links between major centres and unserved or underserved areas. Under RCS-UDAN, the stated mechanism combines market-based selection, airfare caps, concessions and Viability Gap Funding to encourage airline participation on regional routes.

Background

  • The National Civil Aviation Policy 2016 prioritised affordable regional connectivity through financial support and infrastructure development.
  • RCS-UDAN was launched on 21 October 2016; its first operational RCS-UDAN flight, from Shimla to Delhi, was inaugurated in April 2017.
  • The scheme uses market-based selection, airfare caps, concessions and Viability Gap Funding to encourage airline participation.

India implications

  • Improves territorial and economic integration by linking remote and underserved regions with markets and services.
  • May generate positive spillovers for tourism, healthcare access, agricultural trade and local employment.
  • North-Eastern connectivity has strategic and developmental relevance because difficult hilly terrain contributes to under-connectivity.
  • A stronger Centre-State/UT partnership is necessary for infrastructure development, route support and sustainable operations.

Way forward

  • Link route support to transparent viability, reliability and passenger-access indicators.
  • Coordinate airport, heliport, airstrip and surface-transport planning with State/UT governments.
  • Integrate regional aviation with tourism, healthcare, agricultural logistics and local employment strategies.
  • Review subsidised routes periodically and strengthen operational sustainability rather than measuring success only by infrastructure created.
