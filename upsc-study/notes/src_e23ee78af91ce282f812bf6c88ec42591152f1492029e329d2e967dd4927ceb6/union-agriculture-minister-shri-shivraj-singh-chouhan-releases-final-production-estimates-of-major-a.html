Crop-production estimates are successive official assessments of agricultural output; a final estimate concerns a completed reference period, while an advance estimate is an earlier projection subject to revision.

Background

  • The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers’ Welfare released final production estimates for major crops for 2025-26 and first advance estimates for the 2026-27 kharif season on 26 November 2026.
  • Final estimates concern the completed 2025-26 crop year, while first advance estimates are an early assessment for the 2026-27 kharif season; the two should not be treated as equivalent levels of certainty.
