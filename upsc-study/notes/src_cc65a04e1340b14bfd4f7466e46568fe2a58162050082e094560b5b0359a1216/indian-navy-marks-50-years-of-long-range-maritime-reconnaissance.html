Static anchor

Long-range maritime reconnaissance is the use of shore-based maritime patrol aircraft to conduct wide-area observation and related maritime-security missions, including intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance and anti-submarine warfare. In the source’s account, these aircraft work alongside ships and helicopters.

Background

  • The Indian Navy assumed responsibility for long-range maritime reconnaissance after the 1971 war because shore-based maritime patrol aircraft were needed to work alongside ships and helicopters.
  • INAS 312, the Albatross, was commissioned at INS Hansa, Goa, on 18 November 1976 with five Lockheed Super Constellation aircraft transferred from the Indian Air Force.
  • The source records subsequent induction of the Ilyushin Il-38 in 1977, Tupolev Tu-142M in 1988 and Boeing P-8I at INS Rajali, Arakkonam, in 2013.
