Reserve Bank holds 36th Conference of State Finance Secretaries on Fiscal Sustainability

The Reserve Bank of India held its 36th Conference of State Finance Secretaries on October 27, 2026, focusing on expenditure quality, cash, and debt management to enhance states' fiscal sustainability. This event is important for UPSC aspirants to understand fiscal federalism, state financial management, and RBI's role in governance.

The RBI's 36th Conference emphasized prudent fiscal management by states to ensure long-term fiscal health and introduced tools like Unified Lending Interface and Programmable CBDC for better financial governance.

Why is this in the news?

The Reserve Bank of India held the 36th Conference of State Finance Secretaries on October 27, 2026, at Mumbai to discuss enhancing states' fiscal sustainability.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

What was the focus of the 36th Conference of State Finance Secretaries?

  • Conference theme. The theme was ‘Role of Quality of Expenditure, Effective Cash and Prudent Debt Management in Enhancing States’ Fiscal Sustainability’.
  • Participants. Finance Secretaries of 27 States and three Union Territories, along with senior officials from the Ministry of Finance, Controller General of Accounts, and Comptroller and Auditor General of India attended.
  • Key discussions. The meeting reviewed market borrowings by States, short-term financial accommodation from RBI, and adoption of Benchmark Issuance Strategy by States.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

What key recommendations and tools did RBI emphasize for states?

  • Quality of expenditure. The RBI Governor stressed maintaining quality expenditure to ensure long-term fiscal health of States.
  • Prudent borrowing and cash management. States were urged to adopt prudent borrowing strategies and efficient cash management practices.
  • Digital tools for financial management. States were encouraged to onboard onto the Unified Lending Interface (ULI) and use Programmable Central Bank Digital Currency (P-CBDC) for Direct Benefit Transfers (DBT).
  • Use of forums. Effective use of the State Level Bankers’ Committee (SLBC) forum was advised for better coordination.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

How does this conference relate to fiscal federalism and state financial governance?

  • Fiscal federalism context. The conference highlights RBI’s role in guiding states on fiscal discipline and sustainability, key aspects of fiscal federalism.
  • State financial governance. It promotes transparency and efficiency in states’ cash and debt management, crucial for fiscal health and governance.
  • Capacity building. The release of a booklet on cash and debt management provides states with operational guidance to improve financial management.

Back to basics: Fiscal federalism

Fiscal federalism refers to the financial relations between different levels of government, especially between the central and state governments. It involves the allocation of taxation powers, expenditure responsibilities, and fiscal transfers to ensure balanced economic development and fiscal sustainability. The Reserve Bank of India plays a key role in advising and regulating state finances to maintain fiscal discipline and sustainability.
