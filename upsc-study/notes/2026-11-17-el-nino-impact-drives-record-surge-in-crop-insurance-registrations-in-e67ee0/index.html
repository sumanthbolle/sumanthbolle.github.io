El Nino Drives Record Crop Insurance Uptake in Karnataka

El Nino caused over 90% crop loss in Karnataka in 2026, leading to a record 37.5 lakh farmers registering for crop insurance, nearly doubling from 20.91 lakh in 2025. This surge highlights the role of climate impact on agriculture and the importance of risk mitigation policies for farmers.

El Nino-induced crop losses in Karnataka in 2026 led to the highest-ever crop insurance registrations, underscoring climate risks and insurance challenges.

Why is this in the news?

In November 2026, The Hindu reported a record surge in crop insurance registrations in Karnataka due to El Nino-driven crop losses.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What caused the surge in crop insurance registrations in Karnataka?

  • El Nino impact. El Nino caused more than 90% crop loss across Karnataka in 2026, prompting farmers to seek insurance protection.
  • Record registrations. Around 37.5 lakh farmers registered for crop insurance in Kharif 2026, compared to 20.91 lakh in Kharif 2025, the highest in the state's history.
  • Government awareness efforts. The agriculture department increased awareness about crop loss insurance and taluk level offices actively helped farmers register.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What challenges have emerged with crop insurance payouts?

  • Private insurer issues. Farmers have complained that private insurance companies often fail to pay the full compensation promised during registration.
  • Government concerns. Union Minister Shobha Karandlaje urged the state agriculture minister to discuss these compensation issues with insurance companies.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does crop insurance relate to agricultural value addition?

  • Need for profitable agriculture. The Union Minister emphasized turning agriculture into a profitable business through food processing of harvested crops.
  • Reducing post-harvest losses. Fruits and vegetables should not rot in markets but reach consumers timely to ensure better profits for farmers.
  • Role of MSMEs. Many MSMEs in Karnataka and across India work on food processing to add value to farmers' yields.

Back to basics: Crop Insurance

Crop insurance is a risk management tool that protects farmers against losses due to natural calamities like droughts or floods. It helps stabilize farmers' income by providing compensation for crop damage, encouraging investment in agriculture. Awareness and timely registration are crucial for farmers to benefit from such schemes.

What happens next?

  • Address compensation delays. The state agriculture minister should engage with private insurance companies to ensure timely and full compensation to farmers as per their eligibility.
  • Enhance value addition. Promoting food processing and reducing post-harvest losses can improve farmers' profitability beyond crop insurance.
