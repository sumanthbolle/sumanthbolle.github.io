EAM Jaishankar Discusses Ukraine and West Asia with Russian, Polish, South Korean Counterparts

External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar met the foreign ministers of Russia, Poland, and South Korea on October 31, 2026, during the G20 Foreign Ministers' Meeting in Atlanta. These discussions covered the Ukraine conflict, West Asia situation, bilateral ties, and multilateral issues, reflecting India's active diplomatic engagement on global conflicts.

EAM Jaishankar held key talks with Russian, Polish, and South Korean counterparts on Ukraine, West Asia, and multilateral cooperation at the G20 meeting in 2026.

Why is this in the news?

The meetings took place on October 31, 2026, on the sidelines of the G20 Foreign Ministers' Meeting in Atlanta, where EAM Jaishankar engaged with his Russian, Polish, and South Korean counterparts.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What were the key topics discussed by EAM Jaishankar with his counterparts?

  • Ukraine conflict. Jaishankar discussed the Ukraine conflict with Russian Foreign Minister Sergey Lavrov and Polish Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Radoslaw Sikorski.
  • West Asia situation. The Gulf situation was a topic of discussion with the Russian Foreign Minister, reflecting India's concern over stability in West Asia.
  • Bilateral and multilateral ties. Discussions with the South Korean Foreign Minister Cho Hyun focused on bilateral cooperation and multilateral issues including the G20.
  • India-EU relations and G20. With Poland, talks also covered India-EU ties and Poland's interest in permanent G20 membership.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why do these diplomatic meetings matter for India’s foreign policy?

  • Engagement in global conflicts. India’s talks with Russia and Poland on Ukraine show its active role in addressing major international conflicts.
  • Balancing relations. Engaging with diverse countries like Russia, Poland, and South Korea helps India maintain balanced diplomatic ties amid global tensions.
  • Strengthening multilateralism. Discussions at the G20 level emphasize India’s commitment to multilateral cooperation on global issues.

Back to basics: Multilateral Diplomacy

Multilateral diplomacy involves multiple countries working together on common issues through international forums like the G20. It allows countries to discuss global challenges, coordinate policies, and build consensus. India actively participates in such platforms to advance its foreign policy goals and engage with diverse nations on issues like conflict resolution and economic cooperation.
