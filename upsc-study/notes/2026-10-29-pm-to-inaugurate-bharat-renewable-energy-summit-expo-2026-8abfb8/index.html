PM to Inaugurate Bharat Renewable Energy Summit & Expo 2026

The Ministry of New and Renewable Energy is organizing the Bharat Renewable Energy Summit & Expo 2026 from 2 to 5 November at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, inaugurated by the Prime Minister. This summit is a key platform to connect global investors, industry leaders, and policymakers to accelerate India's clean energy transition and meet its 500 GW renewable energy target by 2030.

The Bharat Renewable Energy Summit & Expo 2026 aims to boost investment, innovation, and international cooperation to advance India's renewable energy goals.

Why is this in the news?

The Ministry of New and Renewable Energy announced the Bharat Renewable Energy Summit & Expo 2026 to be held from 2–5 November 2026 in New Delhi, inaugurated by the Prime Minister.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the Bharat Renewable Energy Summit & Expo 2026?

  • Event details. The summit is organized by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy from 2 to 5 November 2026 at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, and will be inaugurated by the Prime Minister.
  • Participants. Over 130 countries, including Australia, Denmark, the European Union, France, Germany, and the United Kingdom, will participate as country partners, along with thirteen Indian states showcasing their renewable energy potential.
  • Key attractions. The event includes a national hackathon, renewable energy quiz, REGen Pavilion, and an exhibition featuring over 400 manufacturers, developers, and research institutions.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does the summit matter for India's energy goals?

  • Installed capacity and targets. India currently has 308 GW of installed non-fossil fuel capacity and aims to reach 500 GW by 2030.
  • Investment needs. Achieving this target requires approximately USD 220 billion in investment between 2027 and 2030, along with enhanced energy storage, transmission, and domestic manufacturing.
  • Global leadership. The summit, alongside the International Solar Alliance Assembly and International Conference on Green Hydrogen, reinforces India's leadership in renewable energy deployment and international cooperation.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How will the summit facilitate renewable energy development?

  • Platform for collaboration. The summit connects global capital, technology, and industry expertise with India's renewable energy opportunities to translate investment into projects and partnerships.
  • Focus areas. Discussions will cover financing, policy, technology, manufacturing, energy storage, workforce development, and greater participation of women in the energy transition.
  • Exhibition and events. The exhibition and events like the hackathon and quiz provide opportunities for innovation, networking, and showcasing emerging renewable energy technologies.

Back to basics: Renewable Energy Transition

Renewable energy transition refers to the shift from fossil fuel-based energy sources to cleaner, sustainable sources like solar, wind, and green hydrogen. This transition involves increasing installed capacity, mobilizing investments, developing technology, and building infrastructure to reduce carbon emissions and combat climate change. International cooperation and policy support are essential to accelerate this process.
