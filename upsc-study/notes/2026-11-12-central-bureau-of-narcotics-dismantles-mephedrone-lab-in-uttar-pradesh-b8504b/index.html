CBN Busts Mephedrone Lab in Uttar Pradesh under Operation Vajra 3.0

The Central Bureau of Narcotics dismantled a clandestine mephedrone manufacturing lab in Mau, Uttar Pradesh, seizing drugs, precursor chemicals, and equipment, and arresting two suspects. This operation highlights India's ongoing efforts to combat synthetic drug trafficking and is relevant for understanding internal security and narcotics control mechanisms.

The Central Bureau of Narcotics disrupted an interstate synthetic drug network by busting a mephedrone lab in Uttar Pradesh under Operation Vajra 3.0.

Why is this in the news?

On 8 November 2026, the Central Bureau of Narcotics conducted a coordinated operation under Operation Vajra 3.0 to dismantle a clandestine mephedrone lab in Mau, Uttar Pradesh.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What happened in the Operation Vajra 3.0 bust?

  • Lab dismantling. CBN busted a clandestine synthetic drug lab manufacturing mephedrone in the Tajopur Industrial Area, Mau, Uttar Pradesh.
  • Seizures. The operation recovered 11.7 kg of mephedrone at various processing stages, 20.9 kg of a key precursor chemical (2-bromo-4-methylpropiophenone), 3.5 kg acetic anhydride, and about 119.9 kg/L of other chemicals and solvents.
  • Equipment seized. Industrial grinders, mixers, steel tanks, heating equipment, glass reaction and distillation apparatus, and other lab infrastructure were seized and dismantled.
  • Arrests. Two suspects involved in the illicit manufacturing network were arrested under the NDPS Act, 1985.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does this operation matter for internal security and drug control?

  • Interstate synthetic drug network. The bust targeted an interstate drug synthetic network, disrupting the manufacture and distribution of illicit mephedrone.
  • Preventing precursor diversion. Seizure of precursor chemicals prevents their diversion for illegal drug manufacture.
  • Legal enforcement. The operation was conducted under the NDPS Act, 1985, reflecting enforcement of narcotics laws.
  • Contribution to national mission. This contributes to the national mission of a Nasha Mukt Bharat by combating illicit drug trafficking.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the broader context of CBN's efforts in 2026?

  • Multiple lab busts. In 2026, CBN has busted six clandestine labs, seizing 130.2 kg of mephedrone and 1465.4 kg/L of precursor chemicals.
  • Arrests made. Twenty suspects have been arrested in connection with these cases.
  • Ongoing investigations. Further investigations are underway to identify wider interstate networks, sources of precursors, financial links, and distribution channels.

Back to basics: Narcotics Control and NDPS Act

The Central Bureau of Narcotics (CBN) is a government agency responsible for enforcing laws related to narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances. The NDPS Act, 1985, is the primary legislation in India that regulates operations related to narcotics, including manufacture, possession, trafficking, and consumption. It empowers agencies like the CBN to conduct investigations, seizures, and arrests to prevent illicit drug activities and protect public health and security.
