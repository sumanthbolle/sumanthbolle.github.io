Farmers Continue Protest Demanding Revision of MSP for Sugarcane

Farmers in Belagavi, Karnataka, are continuing their protest demanding a revision of the minimum support price (MSP) for sugarcane, which has remained unchanged for nine years. This ongoing agitation highlights key issues in agricultural pricing policy and farmer welfare relevant for UPSC aspirants.

Farmers are protesting to demand a revision of the MSP for sugarcane, which has not been updated for nine years, reflecting challenges in agricultural pricing and government response.

Why is this in the news?

On October 13, 2026, farmers in Belagavi district vowed to continue their protest until the Union Food Minister assures a revision of the MSP for sugarcane.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the farmers demanding and why?

  • Demand for MSP revision. Farmers demand a revision of the minimum support price for sugarcane, which has remained at ₹31 for nine years, and the fair and remunerative price has stayed below the cost of cultivation.
  • Protest continuation. Farmers have resolved not to withdraw their protest until the Union Food Minister gives a concrete assurance of MSP revision within a week.
  • Inter-state solidarity. The protest involves multiple farmers' organisations from Karnataka and has received solidarity messages from leaders in Uttar Pradesh and Punjab.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What has been the government's response?

  • Union Minister's denial. Union Food Minister Pralhad Joshi denied receiving farmers' demands well in advance, which farmers claim is untrue as they had met him and submitted memorandums eight months ago.
  • Committee formation proposal. The Minister spoke vaguely about forming a committee to study the problems, which farmers reject, citing existing reports like the M.S. Swaminathan Commission.
  • Security and protest management. Police have made strict security arrangements and diverted traffic to manage the protest, while respecting farmers' right to peaceful demonstration.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for UPSC aspirants?

  • MSP policy relevance. The MSP is a key agricultural policy tool affecting farmer incomes and rural economy, making the ongoing protest significant for understanding farmer welfare and agricultural marketing.
  • Farmer-government relations. The protest illustrates challenges in government responsiveness and policy implementation in the agricultural sector.
  • Inter-state farmer movements. The involvement of farmers from multiple states highlights the pan-India nature of agricultural issues and collective action.

Back to basics: Minimum Support Price (MSP)

The Minimum Support Price is a government-declared price at which it purchases crops from farmers to ensure them a minimum profit for their harvest. It aims to protect farmers from price fluctuations and market uncertainties, thereby supporting agricultural income and food security.

Consider the following statements regarding the recent farmers' protest in Belagavi demanding MSP revision: 1. The MSP for sugarcane has remained at ₹31 for nine years. 2. The Union Food Minister acknowledged receiving farmers' demands well in advance and immediately revised the MSP.

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the Union Food Minister denied receiving the demands well in advance and has not revised the MSP yet.
