NHRC Intervention Establishes Gender-Neutral Cell in Hyderabad Central Prison

The National Human Rights Commission (NHRC) of India prompted the Hyderabad Central Prison to create a Gender-Neutral Cell for persons with diverse gender identities and expressions. This development is significant for UPSC aspirants studying social justice, human rights, and prison reforms.

NHRC's intervention led to the establishment of a Gender-Neutral Cell at Hyderabad Central Prison to uphold dignity and rights of persons with diverse gender identities.

Why is this in the news?

On 8th October 2026, NHRC’s Special Monitor visited Hyderabad Central Prison, emphasizing implementation of advisories for transgender welfare, leading to the establishment of the Gender-Neutral Cell.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What prompted the establishment of the Gender-Neutral Cell?

  • NHRC visit and advisories. The NHRC’s Special Monitor visited Hyderabad Central Prison on 8th October 2026 to assess conditions and emphasized implementing NHRC and Ministry of Home Affairs advisories for transgender persons' welfare.
  • Commission's role. Following the visit, the NHRC recommended setting up a Gender-Neutral Cell to ensure dignity and appropriate living arrangements for persons with diverse gender identities.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What changes were implemented in the prison?

  • Separate Gender-Neutral Cell. The Hyderabad Central Prison authorities established a separate Gender-Neutral Cell specifically for persons with diverse gender identities and expressions.
  • Affirmative clothing permitted. Prison authorities also allowed affirmative clothing for persons accommodated in the Gender-Neutral Cell to respect their gender identity.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does this matter for human rights and prison reforms?

  • Protection of dignity. The initiative promotes protection of human rights and dignity of transgender and LGBTQ+ persons in correctional institutions.
  • Focus on vulnerable groups. It reflects efforts to address the specific needs and vulnerabilities of persons with diverse gender identities within the prison system.

Back to basics: Human Rights Protection for Vulnerable Groups

Human rights bodies like the NHRC monitor and recommend measures to protect the rights and dignity of vulnerable groups, including transgender persons. Prisons must ensure appropriate living conditions and respect for gender identity to uphold constitutional rights and human dignity.
