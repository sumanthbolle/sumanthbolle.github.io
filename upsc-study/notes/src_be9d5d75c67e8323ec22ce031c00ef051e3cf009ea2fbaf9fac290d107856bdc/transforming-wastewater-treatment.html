India implications

  • Urban local bodies need to connect treatment capacity with sewerage coverage, quality testing, reuse demand and financially sustainable operations.
  • Agriculture, horticulture, industry and urban services may use treated water where applicable standards and monitoring protect public health and ecosystems.[1]
  • Water reuse can reduce dependence on freshwater sources, but implementation must account for local hydrology, affordability, institutional capacity and inter-governmental coordination.

Way forward

  • Expand collection and treatment coverage while preventing untreated sewage bypasses.
  • Set use-specific quality standards and maintain independent, routine monitoring.
  • Plan reuse around local water demand, infrastructure, affordability and ecological conditions.
  • Strengthen operation and maintenance, energy efficiency, sludge management and public disclosure of water-quality data.
