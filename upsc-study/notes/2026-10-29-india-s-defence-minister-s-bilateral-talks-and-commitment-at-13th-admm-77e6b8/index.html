India's Defence Minister Strengthens Strategic Ties at 13th ADMM-Plus

Raksha Mantri Shri Rajnath Singh held bilateral talks with defence ministers of the US, Singapore, and South Korea on October 28, 2026, during the 13th ASEAN Defence Ministers’ Meeting-Plus (ADMM-Plus) in the Philippines. These discussions focused on enhancing defence cooperation, technology sharing, and regional security in the Indo-Pacific, which are key to India's security and foreign policy.

India advanced its defence partnerships and regional security cooperation with key Indo-Pacific nations at the 13th ADMM-Plus.

Why is this in the news?

On October 28, 2026, India's Defence Minister held bilateral talks with counterparts from the US, Singapore, and South Korea during the 13th ADMM-Plus in the Philippines, focusing on defence cooperation and counter-terrorism.

What were the key outcomes of India's bilateral talks at the 13th ADMM-Plus?

  • India-US Defence Partnership. India and the US reviewed progress under their 10-year Defence Framework, emphasizing operational readiness, technology sharing, and defence industrial integration, with a joint commitment to eliminate terrorism.
  • India-Singapore Defence Cooperation. Discussions focused on expanding joint military exercises across the Army, Navy, and Air Force, enhancing operational synergy, and reaffirming India's commitment to ASEAN centrality and a free, open Indo-Pacific.
  • India-South Korea Strategic Partnership. Both nations explored co-development of military technologies, joint industry collaboration, and strengthening maritime security to enhance regional security in the Indo-Pacific.

Why do these defence engagements matter for India’s regional security?

  • Counter-terrorism Cooperation. India and the US emphasized zero tolerance towards nations supporting terrorism, with the US reaffirming support against cross-border terrorism.
  • Strengthening Indo-Pacific Security. Collaborations with Singapore and South Korea aim to promote a free, open, and inclusive Indo-Pacific, which is central to India's Act East Policy and regional stability.
  • Enhancing Defence Industry and Technology. Joint efforts in technology sharing and defence industrial integration improve India's defence capabilities and self-reliance.

How does India’s participation in ADMM-Plus reflect its foreign policy priorities?

  • Commitment to ASEAN Centrality. India reiterates its support for ASEAN centrality in regional security architecture, strengthening ties with ASEAN members and dialogue partners.
  • Act East Policy Implementation. Engagements with Singapore and other Indo-Pacific nations align with India's Act East Policy to deepen strategic and defence cooperation.
  • Multilateral Security Cooperation. ADMM-Plus serves as a platform for India to collaborate on security challenges with ASEAN and its eight dialogue partners, enhancing regional peace.

Back to basics: ASEAN Defence Ministers’ Meeting-Plus (ADMM-Plus)

ADMM-Plus is a platform where ASEAN member states and eight dialogue partners, including India, discuss and coordinate on regional security issues. It facilitates cooperation on defence and strategic matters to promote peace and stability in the Indo-Pacific region.
