Ministry of Rural Development Notifies Grievance Redressal Rules under VB–G RAM G Act

The Ministry of Rural Development notified the Grievance Redressal Rules, 2026 under the VB–G RAM G Act, establishing a multi-tier, time-bound mechanism for addressing grievances related to the scheme's implementation. This notification is important for UPSC aspirants as it highlights governance reforms aimed at transparency, accountability, and citizen-centric service delivery in rural welfare schemes.

The VB–G RAM G Grievance Redressal Rules, 2026, create a structured, multi-level, and time-bound framework to ensure prompt and transparent resolution of grievances within 15 days.

Why is this in the news?

On November 2, 2026, the Ministry of Rural Development notified the VB–G RAM G Grievance Redressal Rules, 2026, to institutionalize a transparent and accountable grievance redressal mechanism under the scheme.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the key features of the VB–G RAM G Grievance Redressal Rules, 2026?

  • Multi-tier mechanism. The Rules establish a time-bound, multi-tier grievance redressal system at Gram Panchayat, Block, and District levels with designated officers responsible for grievance handling.
  • Multiple submission channels. Grievances can be submitted in person, by speed post, via a digital platform designated by the Central Government, telephone helplines, or other modes decided by State Governments.
  • Mandatory registration and acknowledgement. All grievances must be registered on the digital platform on the day of receipt or the next working day, with complainants receiving an acknowledgement containing registration details.
  • Defined timelines. Enquiry into grievances must be completed within seven working days and resolution within 15 days from receipt.
  • Digital tracking and escalation. The digital infrastructure enables end-to-end tracking of grievances and automatic escalation to higher authorities if unresolved within 15 days.
  • Provision for appeals. The Rules allow appeals against orders passed under the grievance redressal mechanism, to be disposed of as per the VB–G RAM G Act, 2025.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

How do these rules improve grievance redressal compared to previous frameworks?

  • Accessibility and inclusiveness. No grievance can be refused registration due to format, language, manner of submission, or procedural deficiencies, ensuring wider citizen access.
  • Transparency and accountability. Digital recording of enquiries, findings, and actions strengthens transparency and administrative accountability.
  • Prompt resolution. Clear deadlines for enquiry and resolution ensure timely attention to grievances.
  • Dropped provisions. Unlike the earlier MGNREGA rules, the new Rules do not mandate FIR registration for financial irregularities or placing action-taken reports before panchayat meetings.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for governance and rural welfare?

  • Strengthening rural schemes. The Rules reinforce rights and entitlements of rural households under VB–G RAM G by ensuring grievances related to scheme implementation are addressed systematically.
  • Enhancing administrative responsiveness. The multi-tier and digital grievance redressal framework improves government responsiveness and accountability.
  • Citizen-centric governance. Multiple channels and mandatory registration make grievance redressal more accessible and citizen-friendly.

Back to basics: Grievance Redressal Mechanism

A grievance redressal mechanism is a structured process through which citizens can raise complaints or concerns about government schemes or services. It typically involves multiple levels of authorities who receive, investigate, and resolve grievances within specified timelines. Digital platforms enhance transparency and tracking, ensuring accountability and timely resolution.

Prelims MCQ

The Rules mandate FIR registration in cases of prima facie financial irregularities detected during grievance enquiry. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the Rules dropped the provision requiring FIR registration for financial irregularities, unlike the earlier MGNREGA rules.

Quick revision

What is the maximum time allowed to resolve grievances under the VB–G RAM G Rules, 2026?
Grievances must be resolved within 15 days from the date of receipt.
Can grievances be refused registration under the new Rules due to procedural deficiencies?
No, grievances cannot be refused registration on grounds of format, language, manner of submission, or procedural deficiencies.
What digital facility is used in the grievance redressal process under the Rules?
A designated Digital Public Infrastructure is used for registration, tracking, and recording of grievances.
