Ayodhya-Janakpur Saryu Kamala Express Train Service Connects India and Nepal

The Ministry of Railways launched the Ayodhya Cantt-Janakpur Dham weekly express train on November 23, 2026, linking pilgrimage sites in India and Nepal. This service enhances bilateral connectivity, trade, and cultural ties between the two countries, relevant for India and its neighbourhood studies.

The Ayodhya-Janakpur Saryu Kamala Express weekly train strengthens India-Nepal ties by connecting key pilgrimage sites and boosting cross-border cooperation.

Why is this in the news?

On November 23, 2026, Prime Ministers of India and Nepal virtually flagged off the Ayodhya Cantt-Janakpur Dham Saryu Kamala Express to enhance connectivity and bilateral relations.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the Ayodhya-Janakpur Saryu Kamala Express train service?

  • Service launch. The weekly express train service was launched by the Ministry of Railways on November 23, 2026, connecting Ayodhya Cantt in Uttar Pradesh, India, with Janakpur Dham in Nepal.
  • Route and distance. The train covers approximately 604 kilometres in 19 hours, passing through important towns in Bihar and Uttar Pradesh before reaching Nepal.
  • Train schedule. It departs Ayodhya Cantt every Monday at 5:50 p.m. IST, reaching Janakpur the next day at 12:40 p.m. Nepali time, and returns from Janakpur every Saturday at 1:10 p.m. Nepali time, arriving at Ayodhya Cantt on Sunday at 8:10 a.m. IST.
  • Stations en route. The train halts at 10 stations including Jaynagar, Madhubani, Sitamarhi, Raxaul, Gorakhpur, and Basti, besides its terminal stations.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does the Ayodhya-Janakpur train service matter for India-Nepal relations?

  • Pilgrimage connectivity. It links two significant pilgrimage sites, Janakpur Dham and Ayodhya, associated with the Hindu epic Ramayana, fostering cultural and religious ties.
  • Bilateral ties enhancement. The service is expected to boost trade and cultural exchanges, strengthening the longstanding friendly relations between India and Nepal.
  • Cross-border travel facilitation. Indian citizens can travel to Nepal without a visa if they have a valid passport, simplifying cross-border movement via this train.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the features and facilities of the train service?

  • Train composition. The Saryu Kamala Express uses modern 17-coach LHB rakes, including eight air-conditioned coaches, seven sleeper-class coaches, and special luggage and power cars.
  • Ticket fares. Ticket prices range from ₹365 for sleeper class to ₹1,360 for AC 2 Tier, with other options like AC 3 Economy and AC 3 Tier available.
  • Booking options. Passengers can reserve tickets online through IRCTC or at railway reservation counters.

Back to basics: Cross-border connectivity

Cross-border connectivity refers to transport links such as roads, railways, and air routes that connect neighbouring countries to facilitate trade, travel, and cultural exchange. Such connectivity strengthens diplomatic relations and regional cooperation by making movement of people and goods easier and more efficient.
