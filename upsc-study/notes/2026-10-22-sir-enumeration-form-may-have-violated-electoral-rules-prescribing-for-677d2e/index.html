Legal Concerns Over SIR Enumeration Form Versus Prescribed Form-4 in Electoral Rolls

The Telangana Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls used an Enumeration Form format differing from the legally prescribed Form-4 under the Registration of Electors Rules, 1960. This raises questions about the Election Commission of India's authority and compliance with electoral laws, relevant for understanding electoral reforms and voter registration safeguards.

The use of a non-prescribed Enumeration Form in Telangana's SIR challenges the legal framework for electoral roll revisions under the Registration of Electors Rules, 1960.

Why is this in the news?

On October 22, 2026, The Hindu reported that the Telangana Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls used an Enumeration Form format not prescribed by the Registration of Electors Rules, 1960, raising legal questions about the Election Commission of India's authority.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the legal issue with the SIR Enumeration Form?

  • Form-4 prescribed by law. Rule 8 of the Registration of Electors Rules, 1960 mandates the use of Form-4 issued by Electoral Registration Officers to collect voter details during all types of electoral roll revisions.
  • Differences in SIR Enumeration Form. The Enumeration Form used in Telangana's SIR includes pre-printed electoral details and seeks information about voters' parents or relatives from previous rolls, unlike the prescribed Form-4 which lacks pre-printed data and only collects basic voter information.
  • Legal compliance questioned. Since Rule 25 requires electoral rolls to be prepared afresh during intensive revisions applying rules for first preparation, the use of a different form format raises questions about compliance with the Registration of Electors Rules.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for electoral roll revisions?

  • De novo preparation debate. The Telangana Chief Electoral Officer described the SIR as a 'de novo' preparation of electoral rolls, but critics argue the reliance on previous rolls and pre-printed data contradicts this claim.
  • Potential voter deletions. The SIR process may lead to a net deletion of 70 lakh or more names from Telangana's electoral rolls, impacting voter rights and representation.
  • Procedural integrity. Using a non-prescribed form and referencing past rolls could undermine the procedural integrity and transparency of the voter registration process.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the implications for electoral reforms and voter rights?

  • Authority of Election Commission. The Election Commission's decision to use a different form format raises questions about its authority and adherence to established electoral laws.
  • Legal safeguards for voters. The controversy highlights the need for clear legal safeguards and adherence to prescribed procedures to protect voter registration rights.
  • Role of civil society. Activists like Lubna Sarwath have raised objections, emphasizing citizen vigilance and demand for lawful electoral processes.

Back to basics: Electoral Roll Revision and Forms

Electoral rolls are updated periodically to include eligible voters and remove ineligible ones. The Registration of Electors Rules, 1960, prescribe specific forms like Form-4 for collecting voter details during roll revisions. These forms ensure uniformity and legal compliance in voter registration processes, maintaining the integrity of elections.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls in Telangana: 1. The SIR used the legally prescribed Form-4 under the Registration of Electors Rules, 1960. 2. The SIR Enumeration Form included pre-printed voter details and information about relatives from previous rolls. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2

Answer and trap

Answer: (b). Statement 1 is incorrect because the SIR used a different Enumeration Form, not the prescribed Form-4; statement 2 correctly describes the SIR form's features.
