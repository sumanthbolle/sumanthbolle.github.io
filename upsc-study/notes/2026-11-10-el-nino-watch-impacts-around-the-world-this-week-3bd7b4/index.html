El Nino Watch: Global Weather Disruptions and Impacts in 2026

An exceptionally strong El Nino is driving global weather disruptions in 2026, raising concerns about food security and extreme weather events. UPSC aspirants should understand El Nino's climatic effects and its socio-economic consequences worldwide.

The strong El Nino of 2026 is intensifying global warming effects, causing floods, droughts, and food insecurity across multiple continents.

Why is this in the news?

On November 10, 2026, The Hindu reported that an exceptionally strong El Nino is causing widespread weather disruptions globally, impacting food security and increasing the likelihood of 2026 being one of the hottest years on record.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is El Nino and how does it affect global weather?

  • Definition and mechanism. El Nino is a warming phase of a natural climate cycle that temporarily raises sea temperatures in the tropical Pacific, triggering changes in weather patterns worldwide.
  • Weather disruptions. It can increase risks of droughts and floods by disturbing normal rainfall and temperature patterns, exacerbating human-driven global warming.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the current global impacts of the 2026 El Nino?

  • Temperature records. 2026 is on track to be the second-hottest year on record, with October showing remarkable global and ocean surface warmth intensified by El Nino.
  • Flood emergencies in Latin America. Peru declared a state of emergency due to El Nino-linked flooding affecting northern regions, with over nine million people at high risk of floods and landslides; Bolivia also declared a national emergency.
  • Drought and power issues in Asia. El Nino-linked drought threatens hydropower shortages in Sri Lanka, which currently meets 70% of electricity demand from renewables but expects severe drought in early 2027.
  • Agricultural impact in the Philippines. The Philippines allocated over $980 million for an El Nino response plan to mitigate agricultural damage, as rice production fell 18% due to drought and price shocks.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does El Nino matter for food security and economies?

  • Crop and market effects. El Nino causes heavy rains or droughts that damage crops and infrastructure, disrupting food supply chains and pushing up prices even before harvests are complete.
  • Vulnerable populations. Poor households are especially affected as they spend a large share of income on food and have limited capacity to absorb price shocks, increasing food insecurity and malnutrition risks.
  • Compounding factors. The impacts are worsened by fuel and fertiliser price increases due to the Iran war, which raises costs for farmers and consumers globally.

Back to basics: El Nino and Climate Variability

El Nino is a periodic warming of sea surface temperatures in the central and eastern tropical Pacific Ocean that influences global weather patterns. It is part of the larger El Nino-Southern Oscillation (ENSO) cycle, which also includes La Nina, a cooling phase. These cycles affect monsoons, droughts, floods, and temperature extremes worldwide, impacting agriculture, water resources, and economies.

Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because Sri Lanka meets about 70% of its electricity demand from renewables, mainly hydro and solar, not thermal power.
