Union Minister Rajiv Ranjan Singh Advocates ‘Atmanirbhar Panchayats’ for Rural Development

Union Minister Rajiv Ranjan Singh called for collaborative efforts to build self-reliant Panchayats at a national workshop on the People’s Plan Campaign 2026-27. The event highlighted the launch of the Pradhan Mantri Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana and a new MoU to establish EV charging stations on Panchayat land, emphasizing decentralised governance and sustainable revenue generation.

The government is promoting ‘Atmanirbhar Panchayats’ through participatory planning, enhanced own-source revenue, and integration of schemes to achieve rural development and Viksit Bharat 2047.

Why is this in the news?

On 30 October 2026, Union Minister Rajiv Ranjan Singh addressed the National Workshop on the People’s Plan Campaign 2026-27, urging efforts to build self-reliant Panchayats and announcing key initiatives including the Pradhan Mantri Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana and an MoU for EV infrastructure on Panchayat land.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the key initiatives announced for Panchayati Raj institutions?

  • Pradhan Mantri Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana. A governance transformation initiative approved by the Union Cabinet aiming to create awareness about government schemes, promote grassroots discussions, integrate Centre and State schemes digitally, ensure timely Gram Panchayat Development Plans (GPDPs), and build Panchayat capacities.
  • People’s Plan Campaign 2026-27. A campaign launched on 2 October 2026 that places citizens at the centre of planning, emphasizing participatory planning, digital platforms, and Jan Bhagidari to strengthen local governance.
  • MoU for EV Charging Stations. The Ministry of Panchayati Raj signed an MoU with Convergence Energy Services Limited to establish Electric Vehicle charging and battery swapping stations on Panchayat land, generating new own-source revenue and promoting clean energy.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why is ‘Atmanirbhar Panchayat’ important for rural governance?

  • Foundation of democracy. The government views Panchayats as the foundation of democracy and believes that the vision of Viksit Bharat can be realized only through developed and self-reliant Panchayats.
  • Own Source Revenue (OSR). Panchayats become truly self-reliant when they augment their OSR steadily, which is linked to eligibility for performance grants and financial sustainability.
  • Convergence and local priorities. Effective convergence of government schemes and active participation in Gram Sabhas ensure that local development is driven by local priorities.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does the government plan to improve planning and accountability at the Panchayat level?

  • Comprehensive GPDPs. Gram Panchayat Development Plans should be based on people's needs and aspirations, not just available schemes, and include an OSR component for performance-based grants.
  • Technology-enabled convergence. Integration of Centre and State portals like eGramSwaraj and Samarth Panchayat Portal will support better outcome-based planning using integrated data.
  • Capacity building and participation. Building capacities of Panchayat representatives and stakeholders, encouraging active Gram Sabha participation, and sharing experiences among States are emphasized.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What role do Panchayats play in water, sanitation, and sustainable revenue?

  • Water and sanitation services. Panchayats are central to sustained delivery of drinking water and sanitation, which impacts health and dignity, and their GPDPs should address these issues comprehensively.
  • User charges and financial self-reliance. Introducing user charges for water and sanitation services is important for sustaining these services and moving Panchayats towards financial self-reliance.
  • Accountability through Gram Sabhas. Greater accountability is sought by using data in Gram Sabhas to support better planning, investment, and informed decision-making.

Back to basics: Panchayati Raj and Decentralised Governance

Panchayati Raj Institutions are local self-government bodies at the village, block, and district levels in India, responsible for planning and implementing development programs. Decentralisation involves transferring decision-making powers and resources to these local bodies to ensure governance is closer to the people, enabling participatory democracy and tailored local development.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the recent initiatives for Panchayati Raj institutions: 1. The Pradhan Mantri Viksit Gram Swaraj Yojana aims to integrate Centre and State schemes through a common digital platform. 2. The Ministry of Panchayati Raj signed an MoU with Energy Efficiency Services Limited (EESL) to establish EV charging stations on Panchayat land.

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (c). Statement 2 is correct but the MoU was signed with Convergence Energy Services Limited (CESL), a wholly owned subsidiary of EESL, not directly with EESL.
