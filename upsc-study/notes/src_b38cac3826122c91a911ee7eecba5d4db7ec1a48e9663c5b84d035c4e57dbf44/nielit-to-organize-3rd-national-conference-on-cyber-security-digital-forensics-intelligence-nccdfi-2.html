Background

  • NIELIT, under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, is organising the third National Conference on Cyber Security, Digital Forensics & Intelligence (NCCDFI 2026) in New Delhi on 16–17 November 2026.
  • The conference follows editions held in Kohima in 2024 and Guwahati in 2025, indicating an expansion from a regional initiative into a broader national platform.
  • Its stated theme is “Cyber Secure Bharat: Fortifying India’s Digital Future”.
  • The programme covers cybersecurity, digital forensics, AI security, critical infrastructure, threat intelligence, blockchain and crypto, drone and IoT forensics, digital evidence, cyber law and cloud security.
