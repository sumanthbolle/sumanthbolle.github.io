Inter-Caste Marriage Incentive Scheme Promotes Social Inclusion

The Government of Maharashtra launched the Inter-Caste Marriage Incentive Scheme to provide financial support to couples entering inter-caste marriages, aiming to reduce caste-based discrimination and promote social harmony. UPSC aspirants should understand this scheme as an example of welfare policy addressing social justice and integration.

The Inter-Caste Marriage Incentive Scheme offers financial aid to eligible couples to encourage social equality and reduce caste barriers.

Why is this in the news?

On 15 October 2026, the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment highlighted the scheme through the story of a beneficiary couple, illustrating the scheme's role in supporting inter-caste marriages and social integration.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the Inter-Caste Marriage Incentive Scheme?

  • Scheme purpose. The scheme provides financial assistance to couples in inter-caste marriages to help them overcome initial financial challenges and promote social equality.
  • Support provided. Financial aid helps couples purchase essential household items, manage daily expenses, and build a stable, self-reliant future.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does the scheme matter for social inclusion?

  • Reducing caste discrimination. By supporting inter-caste marriages, the scheme encourages breaking caste-based social barriers and fosters mutual respect and harmony.
  • Promoting social integration. Inter-caste marriages bring together families from different social backgrounds, creating opportunities for acceptance and unity.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does the scheme impact beneficiaries?

  • Financial stability. The scheme eases financial pressures on newly married couples, enabling them to start their married life on a stronger footing.
  • Institutional encouragement. Assistance under the scheme symbolizes government support for couples choosing equality and social harmony over caste distinctions.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What values does the scheme reinforce?

  • Equality and fraternity. The scheme reinforces values of equality, unity, and fraternity, which are foundational to an inclusive society.
  • Mutual respect and dignity. It promotes relationships based on mutual respect, dignity, and shared aspirations rather than caste.

Back to basics: Social Welfare Schemes

Social welfare schemes are government initiatives designed to support vulnerable sections of society by providing financial aid, services, or incentives. These schemes aim to promote social justice, reduce inequalities, and foster inclusive development by addressing economic and social barriers faced by disadvantaged groups.
