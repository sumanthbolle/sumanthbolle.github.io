India-Nepal Joint Military Exercise SURYAKIRAN XX Begins in Nepal

The 20th edition of the India-Nepal joint military exercise SURYAKIRAN started on 25 November 2026 at Nepal Army Battle School, Saljhandi. This annual exercise enhances bilateral defence cooperation and operational coordination between the two armies, relevant for understanding India’s neighbourhood policy and regional security dynamics.

The SURYAKIRAN XX exercise strengthens India-Nepal military ties through joint training in counter-insurgency, disaster response, and humanitarian assistance.

Why is this in the news?

The 20th edition of the India-Nepal joint military exercise SURYAKIRAN commenced on 25 November 2026, highlighting ongoing defence cooperation between the two countries.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the SURYAKIRAN military exercise?

  • Annual bilateral exercise. SURYAKIRAN is an annual joint military exercise conducted alternately in India and Nepal to enhance interoperability and mutual understanding between the two armies.
  • Focus areas. The exercise includes joint tactical operations, heliborne operations, disaster response, medical training, counter-insurgency, and counter-terrorism operations in challenging terrain.
  • Troop participation. The 20th edition involves 334 personnel each from the Indian Army's KUMAON Regiment and Nepal Army's Shri Jawla Dal Battalion.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does SURYAKIRAN matter for India-Nepal relations?

  • Strengthening defence cooperation. The exercise fosters mutual trust, camaraderie, and operational coordination, reinforcing bilateral defence ties.
  • Regional peace and security. It reaffirms the shared commitment of India and Nepal towards maintaining regional peace, stability, and security.
  • Professional skill enhancement. Troops exchange operational experiences and best practices, improving professional skills and joint operational effectiveness.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the key features of the 20th edition?

  • Location and duration. Held at Nepal Army Battle School, Saljhandi, Rupandehi district, Nepal, from 25 November to 8 December 2026.
  • Expanded scope. Includes disaster management, forest warfare, humanitarian assistance, and medical training alongside counter-insurgency operations.
  • Equal troop strength. Both Indian and Nepalese contingents comprise 334 personnel each, reflecting parity and cooperation.

Back to basics: Bilateral Military Exercises

Bilateral military exercises are joint training activities conducted by two countries' armed forces to improve interoperability, share operational knowledge, and build mutual trust. They often focus on specific operational areas like counter-terrorism, disaster response, or tactical operations, enhancing coordination during joint missions or emergencies.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the India-Nepal joint military exercise SURYAKIRAN XX: 1. The exercise is held annually and alternates between India and Nepal. 2. The 20th edition includes troops from the Kumaon Regiment and Shri Jawla Dal Battalion, with a total of 668 personnel participating. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because while the exercise involves troops from the Kumaon Regiment and Shri Jawla Dal Battalion, each side contributes 334 personnel, making the total 668, but the statement implies 668 personnel per side.

Mains question (15 marks)

Examine how joint military exercises like SURYAKIRAN contribute to India’s neighbourhood policy and regional security.

Examine: Analyze the role of joint military exercises in strengthening bilateral relations and enhancing regional security.

Intro: Joint military exercises are a key tool in India's neighbourhood policy to build trust and cooperation with neighbouring countries.

  • They enhance interoperability and operational coordination between the armed forces of India and its neighbours.
  • Such exercises foster mutual trust and camaraderie, reinforcing defence cooperation and diplomatic ties.
  • They contribute to regional peace and stability by preparing forces for counter-terrorism, disaster response, and humanitarian assistance.
  • The exchange of best practices improves professional skills and joint operational effectiveness.

Conclusion: Thus, joint military exercises like SURYAKIRAN play a strategic role in advancing India's neighbourhood policy and regional security objectives.
