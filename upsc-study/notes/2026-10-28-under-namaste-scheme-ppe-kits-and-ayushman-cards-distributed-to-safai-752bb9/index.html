Distribution of PPE Kits and Ayushman Cards to Safai Mitras under NAMASTE Scheme

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment distributed Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) kits and Ayushman Cards to Safai Mitras in Moradabad, Uttar Pradesh, under the NAMASTE Scheme. This initiative aims to enhance the safety, health, and social security of sanitation workers, a key vulnerable group in India.

The NAMASTE Scheme provides PPE kits and health insurance coverage to Safai Mitras to ensure their safety and dignity.

Why is this in the news?

On 27 October 2026, the Union Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment organized a distribution event of PPE kits and Ayushman Cards to Safai Mitras in Moradabad, Uttar Pradesh, under the NAMASTE Scheme.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the NAMASTE Scheme and who benefits?

  • Scheme objective. NAMASTE (National Action for Mechanised Sanitation Ecosystem) aims to ensure the dignity, safety, and social and economic empowerment of sanitation workers.
  • Beneficiary groups. The scheme targets Sewer and Septic Tank Workers (SSWs) and waste pickers, providing them with PPE kits and health insurance coverage through Ayushman Cards.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What was distributed and where?

  • Distribution event. On 27 October 2026, PPE kits and Ayushman Cards were distributed to Safai Mitras in Moradabad, Uttar Pradesh, including symbolic distribution to 20 workers on stage and PPE kits to an additional 813 workers.
  • Local impact. In Moradabad, 307 Sewer and Septic Tank Workers were profiled and provided PPE kits, while 1,190 waste pickers were validated through profiling.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How extensive is the scheme nationally?

  • National coverage. Nationwide, 90,194 Sewer and Septic Tank Workers have been verified, 88,222 provided with PPE kits, and 80,180 covered under health insurance via Ayushman Cards.
  • Waste picker profiling. Additionally, 3,36,492 waste pickers have been profiled across India under the scheme.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does the NAMASTE Scheme matter for social welfare?

  • Safety and dignity. The scheme promotes the safety and dignity of sanitation workers by providing protective equipment and health coverage.
  • Government commitment. It reflects the government's commitment to prioritizing underprivileged and marginalized communities, aligning with the vision of Viksit Bharat 2047.

Back to basics: Social Welfare Schemes for Vulnerable Sections

Social welfare schemes are government initiatives designed to improve the living conditions and provide support to vulnerable and marginalized groups in society. These schemes often include health insurance, safety equipment, and financial aid to ensure dignity, health, and economic empowerment. Sanitation workers are a vulnerable group due to occupational hazards and social stigma, making targeted welfare schemes essential for their protection and upliftment.

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the NAMASTE Scheme is implemented by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, not the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.
