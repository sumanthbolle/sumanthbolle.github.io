India to Host First SCO Civilisation Dialogue Forum by End of 2026

India announced it will host the inaugural Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) Civilisation Dialogue Forum by the end of 2026, inviting all member countries to participate. This event aims to strengthen people-to-people ties and showcase the richness of ancient civilisations, art, literature, and traditions among SCO nations.

India will host the first SCO Civilisation Dialogue Forum in 2026 to promote cultural exchange and deepen regional cooperation.

Why is this in the news?

Union Defence Minister Rajnath Singh announced India's hosting of the first SCO Civilisation Dialogue Forum during the SCO Council of Heads of Government meeting in Dushanbe, Tajikistan, on October 20, 2026.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the SCO Civilisation Dialogue Forum?

  • Forum purpose. The SCO Civilisation Dialogue Forum is designed to strengthen people-to-people ties among member countries by sharing the richness of ancient civilisations, art, literature, and traditions on a global stage.
  • Forum proposal. The idea for the forum was proposed by Prime Minister Narendra Modi during the SCO summit of the Council of Heads of State in Tianjin in 2025.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does India hosting the forum matter?

  • Cultural diplomacy. Hosting the forum allows India to showcase its civilisational heritage and promote cultural diplomacy within the SCO, enhancing regional cooperation.
  • People-to-people ties. The forum aims to deepen people-to-people connections among SCO member states, fostering mutual understanding beyond political and economic cooperation.
  • India's active SCO role. India has been a full SCO member since 2017 and actively participates in its mechanisms, reflecting its commitment to regional multilateralism.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What was discussed at the 25th SCO Council of Heads of Government meeting?

  • Joint Communiqué adoption. The Heads of Delegation adopted the Joint Communiqué of the 25th Meeting, including the SCO budget for 2027 and the PMTEC Action Plan for 2026-2030.
  • India's global initiatives. India highlighted its global initiatives such as the International Solar Alliance, Coalition for Disaster Resilient Infrastructure, Mission LiFE, and the Global Biofuel Alliance as contributions to affordable clean energy and climate-resilient development.

Back to basics: Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO)

The SCO is a regional intergovernmental organisation focused on political, economic, and security cooperation among member states primarily in Eurasia. It aims to promote stability, mutual trust, and development through mechanisms like summits and councils of heads of government. India joined as a full member in 2017 and participates actively in its initiatives.

Mains question (15 marks)

Examine the significance of India hosting the inaugural SCO Civilisation Dialogue Forum in the context of regional cooperation and cultural diplomacy.

Examine: Analyze how hosting the forum contributes to India's foreign policy objectives and regional multilateral engagement.

Intro: India hosting the SCO Civilisation Dialogue Forum marks a strategic effort to deepen cultural ties within the regional grouping.

  • The forum promotes people-to-people connections by sharing the richness of ancient civilisations, art, and traditions among SCO members.
  • It enhances India's role and influence within the SCO, reflecting its commitment to regional cooperation since joining in 2017.
  • The initiative complements India's broader global efforts in sustainable development and climate resilience, showcasing soft power.
  • Hosting the forum strengthens diplomatic engagement in Central Asia, a region of growing geopolitical importance.

Conclusion: Thus, the forum serves as a platform for India to advance cultural diplomacy and regional cooperation within the SCO framework.
