Finance Minister Concludes Pre-Budget Consultations for Union Budget 2027-28

Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman chaired extensive pre-budget consultations from 16 to 26 November 2026 with over 200 stakeholders across 13 sectors. These consultations aim to gather diverse inputs to shape the Union Budget 2027-28, reflecting India's development priorities and economic growth agenda.

Pre-budget consultations led by the Finance Minister gather multi-sectoral recommendations to inform the Union Budget 2027-28.

Why is this in the news?

Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman concluded the pre-budget consultations for the Union Budget 2027-28 on 26 November 2026, involving over 200 stakeholders from 13 sectors.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What were the pre-budget consultations for Union Budget 2027-28?

  • Consultation purpose. The consultations aimed to collect wide-ranging recommendations from stakeholders to shape the Union Budget 2027-28, focusing on India's development priorities and economic growth.
  • Stakeholder participation. More than 200 stakeholders from 13 sectors including agriculture, manufacturing, MSMEs, capital markets, IT, infrastructure, employment, housing, health, education, youth affairs, and economics participated.
  • Government involvement. Senior officials including the Secretary of the Department of Economic Affairs and the Chief Economic Adviser participated alongside the Finance Minister.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What key recommendations emerged from the consultations?

  • Agriculture and biotechnology. Measures to promote domestic manufacturing of biofertilisers and advanced biotechnology-based agricultural inputs were suggested.
  • Artificial Intelligence. Initiatives to enhance India's competitiveness in AI through sector-specific models optimized for local hardware were recommended.
  • MSMEs and cyber hygiene. Programs to strengthen cyber-hygiene awareness among MSMEs and smaller financial institutions were proposed.
  • Youth employment. Targeted interventions to support rural youth and workers transitioning to productive non-farm employment and sustainable enterprises were emphasized.
  • Education and reading. A nationwide reading movement was recommended to ensure every child can read and comprehend a newspaper by the end of Grade 5.
  • Housing and urban development. Promotion of rental housing for affordable urban housing and accelerated development of Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities to support youth transitions were highlighted.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why do these consultations matter for fiscal policy and governance?

  • Participatory budgeting. These consultations reflect the Government's participatory and consultative approach to budget-making, incorporating diverse stakeholder views.
  • Policy focus. Recommendations focus on innovation-led growth, employment expansion, social and economic infrastructure strengthening, and building a resilient economy.
  • Economic governance. The process helps align fiscal policy with broad-based and inclusive development goals, ensuring the budget addresses current and future challenges.

Back to basics: Union Budget and Pre-Budget Consultations

The Union Budget is the annual financial statement of the Government of India, outlining revenue and expenditure for the next fiscal year. Pre-budget consultations are a key part of the budget preparation process where the Finance Ministry engages with various stakeholders to gather inputs and suggestions. This participatory approach helps the government align fiscal policy with economic priorities and stakeholder needs.

Mains question (15 marks)

Examine the role of pre-budget consultations in shaping India's fiscal policy and economic governance.

Examine: Explain how pre-budget consultations contribute to participatory budgeting and influence fiscal policy decisions.

Intro: Pre-budget consultations are a participatory process where the government seeks inputs from diverse stakeholders before finalizing the Union Budget.

  • They enable incorporation of sector-specific and ground-level recommendations, ensuring the budget addresses real economic needs.
  • Consultations promote transparency and inclusiveness in fiscal policy formulation, strengthening economic governance.
  • They help align budgetary allocations with priorities like innovation, employment, infrastructure, and social development.
  • The process fosters consensus-building and responsiveness to emerging challenges and opportunities in the economy.

Conclusion: Thus, pre-budget consultations are vital for informed, inclusive, and effective fiscal policymaking in India.
