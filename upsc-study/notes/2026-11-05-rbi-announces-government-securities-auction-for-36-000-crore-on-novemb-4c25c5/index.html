RBI Announces ₹36,000 Crore Government Securities Auction on November 6, 2026

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has scheduled an underwriting auction for the sale of Government Securities worth ₹36,000 crore on November 6, 2026. This auction is significant for understanding government debt management and fiscal policy implications.

RBI's auction of ₹36,000 crore in Government Securities reflects ongoing government borrowing and debt management strategies.

Why is this in the news?

On November 5, 2026, the RBI announced an underwriting auction for Government Securities worth ₹36,000 crore to be held on November 6, 2026.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

What is the Government Securities auction announced by RBI?

  • Auction details. The Government of India will sell Government Securities worth ₹36,000 crore through an underwriting auction conducted by RBI on November 6, 2026.
  • Securities involved. The auction includes 7.06% GS 2041 with a notified amount of ₹23,000 crore and 7.43% GS 2076 with ₹13,000 crore.
  • Auction method. The auction will use a multiple price-based method, and Primary Dealers (PDs) must meet minimum underwriting and bidding commitments.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

Why does this auction matter for fiscal policy and economy?

  • Government borrowing. The auction reflects the government's need to raise funds through debt issuance to finance its fiscal deficit.
  • Debt management. Issuing long-term securities like those maturing in 2041 and 2076 helps manage the maturity profile of government debt.
  • Monetary policy interface. Auctions influence liquidity and interest rates, affecting broader economic conditions.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

How do Primary Dealers participate in the auction?

  • Minimum underwriting commitment. Each Primary Dealer must commit to underwriting amounts of ₹548 crore for 7.06% GS 2041 and ₹310 crore for 7.43% GS 2076.
  • Bidding process. PDs submit bids electronically via RBI’s e-Kuber system between 9:00 AM and 9:30 AM on auction day.
  • Commission payment. Underwriting commission is credited to PDs’ current accounts with RBI on the issue date.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

Back to basics: Government Securities and Auctions

Government Securities (G-Secs) are debt instruments issued by the government to borrow money from the public and institutions. Auctions are the primary method through which these securities are sold, involving competitive bidding by Primary Dealers and other investors. The auction process helps determine the interest rates and maturity structure of government debt, influencing fiscal management and monetary policy.
