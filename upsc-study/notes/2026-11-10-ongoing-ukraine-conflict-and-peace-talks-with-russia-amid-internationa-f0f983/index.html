India's Diplomatic Role in Ukraine Conflict Peace Talks

Prime Minister Narendra Modi spoke with Ukraine's President Zelenskyy on November 9, 2026, reaffirming India's commitment to peaceful resolution through dialogue. This highlights India's ongoing diplomatic engagement amid complex international efforts to resolve the Russia-Ukraine conflict.

India maintains a human-centric approach advocating dialogue and diplomacy to resolve the Ukraine conflict while strengthening bilateral ties with Ukraine.

Why is this in the news?

On November 9, 2026, Prime Minister Modi held a telephone conversation with President Zelenskyy discussing recent conflict developments and India’s readiness to support peaceful resolution efforts.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What was discussed in the recent India-Ukraine dialogue?

  • Conflict developments. President Zelenskyy shared his perspective on recent developments in the ongoing conflict with Prime Minister Modi.
  • India's stance. Prime Minister Modi reiterated India's consistent position that the conflict must be resolved through dialogue and diplomacy, emphasizing a human-centric approach.
  • Peace efforts. India conveyed readiness to contribute to all efforts aimed at the peaceful resolution of the conflict.
  • Bilateral cooperation. The leaders discussed ways to further strengthen India-Ukraine cooperation in various sectors for mutual benefit.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the history of peace talks in the Russia-Ukraine conflict?

  • Early talks. Initial talks began in February 2022 in Belarus focusing on humanitarian corridors but yielded limited progress.
  • Subsequent negotiations. Multiple rounds of talks involving Russia, Ukraine, the U.S., and other countries took place in locations like Antalya, Istanbul, Saudi Arabia, and Geneva with intermittent prisoner swaps and ceasefire proposals.
  • Challenges faced. Despite numerous meetings, including trilateral talks in Abu Dhabi and Geneva, no comprehensive peace agreement has been reached due to disagreements on security guarantees and territorial issues.
  • International mediation. Countries like Turkey, the U.S., and the UN have played roles in mediating talks, but consensus remains elusive.

[2] The Hindu · Secondary

Back to basics: Diplomacy and Conflict Resolution

Diplomacy involves managing international relations through dialogue and negotiation to resolve conflicts peacefully. Conflict resolution seeks to end disputes by addressing underlying issues and reaching agreements acceptable to all parties. Multilateral talks often include mediators to facilitate communication and build consensus.
