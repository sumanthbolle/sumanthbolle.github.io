RBI Releases Scheduled Banks’ Statement of Position as on September 30, 2026

The Reserve Bank of India published the financial position of scheduled banks in India as of September 30, 2026, detailing liabilities, assets, deposits, borrowings, and advances. This data is crucial for understanding the health and liquidity of the banking sector, which impacts monetary policy and financial stability.

The RBI's statement of position on scheduled banks provides key indicators of banking sector performance and liquidity as of September 30, 2026.

Why is this in the news?

The Reserve Bank of India released the Scheduled Banks’ Statement of Position on October 15, 2026, reflecting the banks' financial status as of September 30, 2026.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

What does the Scheduled Banks’ Statement of Position show?

  • Liabilities to banking system. Demand and time deposits from banks stood at ₹453,266.26 crore for all scheduled banks as of September 30, 2026, indicating interbank liquidity positions.
  • Deposits from others. Deposits other than from banks reached ₹28,955,375.06 crore, comprising demand deposits of ₹3,797,943.37 crore and time deposits of ₹25,157,431.69 crore, showing public confidence and funds mobilised.
  • Borrowings and cash balances. Scheduled banks' borrowings excluding RBI stood at ₹982,507.14 crore, while borrowings from RBI were ₹6,451 crore; cash in hand was ₹89,420.84 crore.
  • Assets and investments. Banks held ₹7,660,207.72 crore in investments mainly in government securities, and advances (loans and credit) amounted to ₹23,509,866.26 crore, reflecting credit flow to the economy.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

Why does this data matter for UPSC aspirants?

  • Indicator of banking sector health. The statement reveals liquidity, credit growth, and deposit mobilisation, which are key to assessing the banking sector's stability and performance.
  • Monetary policy implications. Data on borrowings from RBI and interbank deposits help understand liquidity management and the effectiveness of monetary policy tools.
  • Economic growth linkage. Advances data indicates credit availability to various sectors, impacting economic development and employment, relevant for GS3 topics.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

How are scheduled banks classified in this statement?

  • Scheduled commercial banks. Includes public, private, regional rural banks, small finance banks, and payments banks, with 120 scheduled commercial banks reported.
  • Scheduled cooperative banks. Includes 26 scheduled state cooperative banks and 55 scheduled primary (urban) cooperative banks, reflecting cooperative banking sector data.

Back to basics: Scheduled Banks and Their Role

Scheduled banks are those included in the Second Schedule of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934, and they must satisfy certain criteria including minimum paid-up capital. They play a key role in the Indian banking system by mobilising deposits and providing credit, and are subject to RBI regulation and supervision. The statement of position provides a snapshot of their financial health and liquidity.
