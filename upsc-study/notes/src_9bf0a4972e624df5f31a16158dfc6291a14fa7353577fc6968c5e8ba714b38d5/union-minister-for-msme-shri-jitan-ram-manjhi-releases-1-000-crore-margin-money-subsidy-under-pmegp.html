Static anchor

A credit-linked subsidy programme provides public financial support alongside institutional credit, reducing the effective financing burden while retaining lender appraisal and beneficiary participation. In PMEGP, the support is directed to new non-farm micro-enterprises. [P1][5]

Background

  • PMEGP combines bank-sanctioned project loans with margin money subsidy for new non-farm micro-enterprises. The Ministry of MSME administers the scheme, while KVIC implements it nationally with support from KVIBs, District Industries Centres
  • The subsidy is not an unconditional grant: official scheme information states that banks independently evaluate viability, beneficiary contribution is 5–10%, and margin money remains locked for three years, subject to specified conditions.

Way forward
