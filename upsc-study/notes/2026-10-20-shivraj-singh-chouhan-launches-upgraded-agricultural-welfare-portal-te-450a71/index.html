Shivraj Singh Chouhan Launches Upgraded Agri Welfare Portal for Farmers

Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan launched an upgraded agricultural welfare portal on October 19, 2026, providing farmers direct mobile access to government schemes and reliable agricultural information. This digital initiative aims to simplify scheme access, reduce intermediaries, and support farmers with timely data on weather, inputs, and markets, relevant for UPSC aspirants studying agricultural policies and digital governance.

The upgraded Agri Welfare Portal offers farmers a single digital platform to access government schemes, financial assistance, and authentic agricultural information on their mobile phones.

Why is this in the news?

On October 19, 2026, Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan launched the upgraded Agri Welfare Portal to digitally empower farmers with easy access to government schemes and agricultural information.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the upgraded Agri Welfare Portal and its features?

  • Single digital platform. The portal consolidates all Ministry of Agriculture schemes, subsidies, rules, and digital services into one mobile-friendly platform, reducing the need for farmers to visit multiple offices or websites.
  • Direct access and transparency. It enables online eligibility checks, direct application submission, and financial assistance transfers through Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT), minimizing intermediaries and enhancing transparency.
  • Designed for rural users. The portal is lightweight and user-friendly, functioning smoothly on basic smartphones and in areas with weak internet connectivity.
  • Reliable agricultural information. Farmers can access authentic data on weather conditions, scientific farming practices, seed and fertiliser availability, and prevailing market prices.
  • Accessibility features. Compliant with government accessibility standards, the portal supports screen readers and simple navigation for persons with disabilities.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

How does the portal support farmers and the agricultural ecosystem?

  • Empowering farmers digitally. The portal eliminates procedural hurdles and intermediaries, making government assistance more accessible directly on farmers’ mobile phones.
  • Inclusive design. Processes and language are simplified to be usable by small and marginal farmers, including those with limited formal education.
  • Integration of stakeholders. It brings together Farmer Producer Organisations, Primary Agricultural Credit Societies, agricultural scientists, researchers, and startups on a common digital platform.
  • Training support. Krishi Vigyan Kendras will conduct special training campaigns to help farmers effectively use the portal and other digital tools.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this portal matter for agricultural policy and governance?

  • Supports Digital India vision. The portal advances the Prime Minister’s vision of making government services accessible anytime and anywhere through mobile technology.
  • Improves scheme delivery. By providing a single-window gateway to major initiatives like PM-KISAN, AgriStack, and Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana, it streamlines scheme access and benefit delivery.
  • Enhances transparency and trust. Regular updates and accurate information are emphasized to maintain public trust and ensure the portal’s success.

Back to basics: Digital Governance in Agriculture

Digital governance uses technology to make government services more accessible, transparent, and efficient. In agriculture, digital platforms help farmers access schemes, financial aid, and information directly, reducing intermediaries and delays. Mobile-friendly portals are crucial in rural areas where internet connectivity and digital literacy may be limited, enabling wider participation and empowerment.
