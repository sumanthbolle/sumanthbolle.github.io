Piyush Goyal Urges Boost in Export Competitiveness and Market Diversification

Union Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal met with Export Promotion Councils to review India's export scenario and called for enhanced export competitiveness and diversification of markets. He highlighted government efforts to facilitate exports, the importance of free trade agreements, and the role of MSMEs in expanding India's global trade footprint.

Piyush Goyal emphasized improving product quality, leveraging free trade agreements, and supporting MSMEs to strengthen India's export competitiveness and market diversification.

Why is this in the news?

On 28 October 2026, Union Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal interacted with Export Promotion Councils to discuss India's export competitiveness and strategies for market diversification.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What did Piyush Goyal highlight about India's export scenario?

  • Global trade environment. Goyal noted challenges like geopolitical uncertainties, tariff changes, supply chain shifts, and new regulations but saw opportunities for India to become a reliable global trading partner.
  • Exporter focus areas. He urged exporters to improve product quality, innovation, cost competitiveness, supply reliability, and compliance with international standards to enhance global value chain participation.
  • Role of Free Trade Agreements. Goyal stressed leveraging India's expanding free trade agreements to increase exports, market penetration, and participation of Indian enterprises in international trade.
  • Support for MSMEs. He highlighted MSMEs' contribution to exports and called for expanding outreach, practical support, market intelligence, and integration of smaller enterprises into global markets.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does India’s trade policy and industrial transformation support export growth?

  • Trade agreements as partnerships. Goyal described India's trade agreements as comprehensive partnerships covering tariffs, rules of origin, market access, services, investments, technology, and climate cooperation.
  • Market access expansion. Nine recent free trade and bilateral agreements opened preferential market access to economies with an aggregate GDP of USD 60 trillion, compared to USD 10 trillion before 2014.
  • Industrial transformation drivers. Quality, economies of scale, global market access, and inclusivity are key to India's industrial transformation, with innovation driven by government, private sector, and academia collaboration.
  • Make in India and manufacturing. Initiatives like Make in India and phased manufacturing have increased domestic production, shifting India from a buyer to a recognized manufacturing nation.

[2] Press Information Bureau

What are the specific export and growth strategies for Telangana?

  • FTA utilization. Goyal urged Telangana to maximize benefits from free trade agreements by identifying top export products and setting up FTA help desks to assist MSMEs, farmers, and startups.
  • Pharma and GI exports. He called for innovation in Telangana's pharmaceutical sector and promotion of GI-tagged products like Tandur red gram for global markets.
  • Industrial infrastructure. Projects like Zaheerabad Industrial Smart City and Kakatiya Mega Textile Park aim to boost industrial growth aligned with the 5F vision from farm to foreign markets.
  • Ease of doing business. Goyal recommended reforms such as decriminalization of minor offences, reduced compliance, and simplified procedures to improve business operations in Telangana.

Back to basics: Free Trade Agreements (FTAs)

Free Trade Agreements are treaties between countries that reduce or eliminate tariffs, import quotas, and preferences on most goods and services traded between them. They aim to increase trade by providing preferential market access, improving competitiveness, and fostering economic cooperation. FTAs often include provisions on standards, intellectual property, and investment to facilitate smoother trade relations.

What happens next?

  • Enhance FTA utilization. States like Telangana should identify key export products and establish support desks to help MSMEs and farmers leverage FTAs effectively.
  • Promote innovation and R&D. Strengthening industry-academia collaboration and investing in innovation are essential to drive industrial transformation and export competitiveness.
  • Improve ease of doing business. Simplifying compliance, decriminalizing minor offences, and providing single-window clearances can boost business operations and attract investment.
Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about India's export competitiveness and trade agreements: 1. India has concluded nine recent free trade and bilateral trade agreements covering economies with an aggregate GDP of USD 60 trillion. 2. The preferential market access through India's FTAs covers approximately 76% of the global GDP. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (c). The trap is confusing the GDP coverage before and after 2014; the recent agreements cover USD 60 trillion, and overall preferential access covers 76% of global GDP.

Mains question (15 marks)

Examine: Analyze how FTAs and government initiatives contribute to India's export growth and industrial transformation.

Intro: India's export competitiveness is increasingly shaped by strategic free trade agreements and targeted export promotion efforts.

  • Explain the scope and impact of India's recent FTAs in expanding preferential market access and fostering complementary trade.
  • Discuss government measures to improve export competitiveness, including support for MSMEs, product quality, and market diversification.
  • Analyze the role of industrial transformation driven by quality, scale, innovation, and collaboration in supporting exports.
  • Highlight state-level strategies, such as those in Telangana, to leverage FTAs and improve ease of doing business.

Conclusion: Effective utilization of FTAs combined with focused export promotion and industrial reforms can significantly enhance India's global trade position.
