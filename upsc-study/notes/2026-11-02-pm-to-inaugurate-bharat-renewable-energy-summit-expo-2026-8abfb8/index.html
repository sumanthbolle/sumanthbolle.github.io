PM to Inaugurate Bharat Renewable Energy Summit & Expo 2026

The Prime Minister will inaugurate the Bharat Renewable Energy Summit & Expo 2026 on 2 November at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi. The event will gather global participants to promote investment, innovation, and partnerships in clean energy, crucial for India's energy transition and climate goals.

The Bharat Renewable Energy Summit & Expo 2026 aims to accelerate clean energy deployment through global cooperation and investment.

Why is this in the news?

The Prime Minister will inaugurate the Bharat Renewable Energy Summit & Expo 2026 on 2 November 2026, a major event focusing on clean energy investment and technology partnerships.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the Bharat Renewable Energy Summit & Expo 2026?

  • Event details. The Summit will be held from 2 to 5 November 2026 at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, with the theme 'Invest. Innovate. Inspire.'
  • Participants. It will attract participants from over 130 countries, including more than 40 ministers, policymakers, investors, financial institutions, and industry leaders.
  • Purpose. The Summit serves as a global platform for clean energy investment, industry engagement, and technology partnerships.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Which countries and states are involved?

  • Country partners. Australia, Denmark, the European Union, France, Germany, the Netherlands, Norway, Sweden, and the United Kingdom are participating as Country Partners.
  • State participation. Thirteen States and Union Territories including Andhra Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Delhi, Gujarat, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Odisha, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, and West Bengal will showcase renewable energy potential and investment opportunities.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does the Summit integrate with other clean energy initiatives?

  • Concurrent events. The Summit will be held alongside the 9th Session of the International Solar Alliance Assembly and the 4th International Conference on Green Hydrogen.
  • Focus areas. While Bharat RE focuses on investment, industry, and technology partnerships, the ISA Assembly advances international solar energy cooperation, and the ICGH focuses on green hydrogen technologies and scale-up.
  • Combined impact. Together, these platforms strengthen international cooperation and accelerate clean energy deployment.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What activities and features will the Summit include?

  • Sessions and discussions. The Summit will host plenary sessions, ministerial discussions, CEO roundtables, and country and state sessions to promote investment and partnerships.
  • Exhibition. The Expo will feature over 400 exhibitors showcasing advanced technologies, Indian manufacturing capabilities, and clean energy solutions across solar, wind, green hydrogen, and energy storage.

Back to basics: Energy Transition

Energy transition refers to the global shift from fossil fuel-based energy systems to renewable and cleaner energy sources like solar, wind, and green hydrogen. This transition aims to reduce carbon emissions, combat climate change, and promote sustainable development by adopting advanced technologies and international cooperation.
