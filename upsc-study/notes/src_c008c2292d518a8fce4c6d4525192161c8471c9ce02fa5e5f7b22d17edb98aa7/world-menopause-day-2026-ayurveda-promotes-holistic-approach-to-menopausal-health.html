  • A rights-based and inclusive approach should account for differences in health literacy, affordability, access to qualified practitioners and individual clinical needs.

Way forward

  • Treat menopause as part of life-course women’s healthcare through awareness and preventive counselling.
  • Integrate nutrition, emotional support, lifestyle guidance and referral within accessible health services.
  • Ensure Ayurvedic formulations are professionally guided and evaluated through scientific research and validation.
  • Avoid presenting traditional interventions as universally suitable or as substitutes for necessary clinical care.
