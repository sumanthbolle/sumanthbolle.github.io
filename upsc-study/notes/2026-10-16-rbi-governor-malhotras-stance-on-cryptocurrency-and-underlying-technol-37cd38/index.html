RBI Governor Malhotra's Cautious Approach to Cryptocurrency and Support for Blockchain Technology

RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra stated that India supports innovation in distributed ledger technology and tokenisation but remains cautious about cryptocurrencies due to concerns over monetary sovereignty and capital flows. This stance is important for understanding India's regulatory approach to digital currencies and financial technology.

RBI promotes blockchain innovation while exercising caution on cryptocurrencies to protect monetary policy and capital controls.

Why is this in the news?

On October 3, 2026, RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra articulated India's position on cryptocurrencies and underlying technologies at the Kautilya Economic Conclave.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is RBI's stance on cryptocurrencies and blockchain technology?

  • Support for underlying technology. India supports innovation in distributed ledger technology and tokenisation, which are the foundational technologies behind crypto assets.
  • Caution on cryptocurrencies. RBI remains cautious about cryptocurrencies due to concerns related to monetary sovereignty, monetary policy, and capital flows.
  • Use in public-private partnerships. Some blockchain technologies are being used by the central bank and outside entities in public-private partnership modes.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does RBI remain cautious about cryptocurrencies?

  • Monetary sovereignty concerns. Cryptocurrencies pose challenges to the 'singleness of money' which can affect the effectiveness of monetary policy.
  • Capital flow restrictions. Emerging market economies like India have capital flow restrictions, and cryptocurrencies could undermine these controls.
  • Limited domestic payment issues. Domestic payments in India are already fast, cheap, and convenient, so cryptocurrencies do not solve a major domestic problem.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What alternatives does RBI consider for payment challenges?

  • Focus on cross-border payments. The significant challenge lies in cross-border payments, where cryptocurrencies are often proposed as a solution.
  • Exploration of CBDCs. RBI is exploring central bank digital currencies (CBDCs) as an alternative to address cross-border payment issues.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does RBI view the global economic context affecting monetary policy?

  • Impact of global interest rates. Global interest rates influence India's real interest rates and monetary policy decisions.
  • Rising government and private spending. Increased spending, including on artificial intelligence, has led to hardening bond yields and higher public debt.

Back to basics: Distributed Ledger Technology and Central Bank Digital Currency

Distributed ledger technology (DLT) is a decentralized database system that records transactions across multiple locations, enhancing transparency and security. Blockchain is a type of DLT that underpins cryptocurrencies. Central Bank Digital Currency (CBDC) is a digital form of a country's official currency issued by the central bank, aimed at improving payment systems while maintaining monetary control.
