India Commits Over 7 Million Euros to Elettra 2.0 Synchrotron Project in Italy

India has announced a financial commitment of more than 7 million Euros over five years starting 2026-27 to co-partner in the redevelopment of research beamlines at Italy's Elettra Sincrotrone Trieste under the Elettra 2.0 programme. This investment strengthens India-Italy scientific collaboration and expands research opportunities in advanced scientific fields, benefiting Indian and international researchers.

India's investment in Elettra 2.0 enhances bilateral scientific cooperation and research infrastructure in advanced materials and biology.

Why is this in the news?

On November 13, 2026, Union Minister Dr. Jitendra Singh announced India's commitment of over 7 million Euros to the Elettra 2.0 synchrotron project in Italy during his visit to the Elettra Sincrotrone Trieste facility.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the Elettra 2.0 project and India's role in it?

  • Project overview. Elettra 2.0 is a redevelopment programme of research beamlines at the Elettra Sincrotrone Trieste synchrotron facility in Italy, aiming to upgrade and replace existing beamlines with enhanced capabilities.
  • India's investment. India has committed more than 7 million Euros over five years starting 2026-27 as a co-partner to support the development of next-generation synchrotron beamlines.
  • New beamlines. The existing India-centric XRD2 and Xpress beamlines will be decommissioned and replaced by two new beamlines: micro-XRD for macromolecular crystallography and Xpress Superbend for high-pressure diffraction.
  • Scientific fields benefited. The upgraded facilities will expand research opportunities in Chemistry, Physics, Advanced Materials, Structural Biology, and other scientific areas.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does India's participation in Elettra 2.0 matter?

  • Longstanding collaboration. India's participation builds on over 25 years of scientific collaboration with Elettra, with Indian scientists conducting experiments there since 1998.
  • Significant user engagement. India is the second-largest user of Elettra after Italy, submitting over 1,100 experimental proposals, reflecting sustained scientific engagement.
  • Research infrastructure development. India's investment supports the creation of upgraded research infrastructure and human resource capacity for advanced scientific research.
  • International scientific cooperation. The partnership exemplifies India's commitment to global scientific collaboration and science diplomacy.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How has India supported research access and collaboration at Elettra?

  • Previous investments. India jointly supported the construction of the existing XRD2 and XPRESS beamlines with a contribution of approximately Rs. 22 crore, inaugurated in 2016.
  • Researcher visits. About 50-60 Indian scientists visit Elettra annually to conduct experiments, facilitated by the Department of Science and Technology.
  • Indo-Italian Executive Programme of Cooperation. DST sanctioned Rs. 2 crores during 2022-2024 to support 50 Indian researchers annually for experiments at Elettra, with further support proposed for 2025-2027.
  • Letter of Intent. DST signed a Letter of Intent to participate in the redevelopment of beamlines under Elettra 2.0, reaffirming India's commitment.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the significance of the Indian diaspora's engagement in Italy?

  • Diaspora participation. The Indian diaspora in Italy, including students, women, entrepreneurs, and professionals, actively participated in events such as World Diabetes Day, reflecting strong ties to India's scientific progress.
  • Pride in India's progress. The diaspora expressed pride in India's advancements in science, technology, research, and healthcare under Prime Minister Narendra Modi's leadership.
  • Science diplomacy impact. Diaspora engagement highlights India's growing global scientific standing and its contribution to international scientific advancement.

Back to basics: International Scientific Collaboration

International scientific collaboration involves countries partnering to share resources, infrastructure, and expertise to advance research. Such collaborations enable access to advanced facilities and foster innovation. They also strengthen diplomatic ties and contribute to global scientific progress.
