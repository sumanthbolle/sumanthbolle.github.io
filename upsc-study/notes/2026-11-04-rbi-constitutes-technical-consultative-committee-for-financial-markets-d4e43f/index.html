RBI Constitutes Technical Consultative Committee for Financial Markets

The Reserve Bank of India has formed the Technical Consultative Committee for Financial Markets (TCCFM) to engage with market participants on policy and operational issues in money, government securities, foreign exchange, and derivative markets. This committee will help the RBI address market functioning, infrastructure, and emerging risks, which is important for understanding financial market regulation and economic governance.

The RBI's Technical Consultative Committee for Financial Markets advises on policy and operational matters to improve market functioning and infrastructure.

Why is this in the news?

The RBI announced the constitution of the Technical Consultative Committee for Financial Markets on November 4, 2026, following its October 7, 2026 policy statement.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

What is the Technical Consultative Committee for Financial Markets?

  • Committee purpose. The TCCFM is a forum for structured engagement between the RBI and market participants on policy and operational matters related to money, government securities, foreign exchange, and derivative markets.
  • Scope of work. It addresses issues of market functioning, efficiency, liquidity, resilience, and infrastructure including trading, clearing, settlement, and reporting frameworks.

[1] Reserve Bank of India[2] The Hindu · Secondary

Who are the members and leadership of the Committee?

  • Chairperson. The Committee is chaired by the Deputy Governor in charge of the Financial Markets Regulation Department of the RBI.
  • External members. Members include former SEBI Whole Time Member Ananth Narayan, leaders from major banks and financial institutions such as SBI, Axis Bank, ICICI Bank, Citibank India, Clearing Corporation of India, and Morgan Stanley India.

[1] Reserve Bank of India[2] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the Committee's terms of reference?

  • Market issues. The Committee focuses on improving the functioning, liquidity, and resilience of financial markets and identifying measures to deepen and broaden them.
  • Infrastructure and regulation. It examines trading, clearing, settlement, reporting, and market infrastructure along with regulatory and operational frameworks.
  • Emerging challenges. The Committee monitors emerging policy and operational issues, implementation of RBI measures, unintended market frictions, financial innovations, and risks affecting Indian financial markets.
  • Additional referrals. It also considers specific market or macroeconomic issues referred by the RBI.

[1] Reserve Bank of India[2] The Hindu · Secondary

How will the Committee function?

  • Meeting frequency. The Committee will meet at least once every quarter to discuss relevant issues.

Back to basics: Financial Market Regulation

Financial market regulation involves rules and policies that ensure the smooth functioning, transparency, and stability of markets where money, securities, and foreign exchange are traded. The central bank plays a key role in regulating these markets to maintain liquidity, prevent systemic risks, and support economic growth.
