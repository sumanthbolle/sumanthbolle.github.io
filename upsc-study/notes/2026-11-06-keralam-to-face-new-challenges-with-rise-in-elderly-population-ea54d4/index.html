Keralam Faces Challenges from Rising Elderly Population

Keralam's elderly population is expected to increase significantly within the next decade, creating new challenges for elderly care in the state. Understanding these demographic changes is important for questions on social welfare and vulnerable sections in UPSC exams.

Keralam's rising elderly population will require enhanced social security and healthcare measures to address emerging challenges.

Why is this in the news?

The Hindu reported on November 6, 2026, that Keralam is preparing to face new challenges due to a significant rise in its elderly population over the next decade.

What is the demographic change in Keralam?

  • Rising elderly population. Keralam's elderly population is poised to increase significantly in the coming decade, indicating a demographic shift towards an ageing society.

Why does this matter for elderly care?

  • New challenges in care. The increase in elderly population will bring new challenges for the state in providing adequate elderly care, including healthcare and social support.
  • Current care scenario. Many elderly depend on family members for care, but economic and social constraints, such as unemployment and migration, complicate support systems.

How is Keralam responding to these challenges?

  • Dedicated elderly department. Keralam has taken a significant step by establishing a dedicated department focused on elderly care to address the emerging needs of this demographic.

Back to basics: Population Ageing

Population ageing refers to the increasing median age in a population due to declining fertility rates and/or rising life expectancy. It results in a higher proportion of elderly people, which impacts social welfare, healthcare systems, and economic productivity. Governments need to adapt policies to provide adequate support and care for the elderly.
