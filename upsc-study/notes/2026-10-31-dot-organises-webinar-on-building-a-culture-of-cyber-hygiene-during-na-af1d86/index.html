DoT Webinar Promotes Cyber Hygiene During National Cyber Security Awareness Month 2026

The Department of Telecommunications' Telecom Cyber Security Incident Response Team (T-CSIRT) organized a webinar on cyber hygiene as part of the National Cyber Security Awareness Month in October 2026. This initiative highlights the importance of individual vigilance and proactive digital safety to counter growing cyber threats, relevant for UPSC aspirants studying cyber security governance and internal security.

The DoT's T-CSIRT webinar emphasized cyber hygiene and vigilance as key defenses against sophisticated cyber threats during National Cyber Security Awareness Month 2026.

Why is this in the news?

The Department of Telecommunications' Telecom-CSIRT organized an awareness webinar on cyber hygiene on 29 October 2026 during the National Cyber Security Awareness Month.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What was the purpose of the DoT webinar on cyber hygiene?

  • Awareness on cyber threats. The webinar aimed to educate participants on cyber frauds, cyber attacks, and cyber hygiene practices to build a culture of digital safety.
  • Promoting vigilance. Officials stressed that individual vigilance is vital as cyber threats grow in scale and sophistication, making proactive digital safety the first line of defense.
  • Practical insights. Experts explained social engineering, malware types, cyber kill chain, and common website attacks, including how attackers operate and how to detect and counter threats.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Who participated and what topics were covered?

  • Participants. Over 190 participants attended, including officials from DoT HQ, Licensed Service Areas, NCA-T, NCCS, Telecom Service Providers, and Internet Service Providers.
  • Topics covered. The session covered phishing and fraud awareness, safe handling of links and attachments, timely software updates, and prompt reporting of cyber incidents.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does this matter for UPSC aspirants?

  • Cyber security governance. The webinar reflects government efforts to strengthen cyber security awareness, an important aspect of internal security and digital governance.
  • Role of individual vigilance. It highlights the role of citizens and organizations in maintaining cyber hygiene, relevant to questions on governance, accountability, and security.
  • Technology and policy link. Understanding such initiatives helps aspirants analyze how technology policies are implemented to secure India's digital future.

Back to basics: Cyber Hygiene

Cyber hygiene refers to practices and steps that users of computers and other devices take to maintain system health and improve online security. It includes actions like updating software, avoiding suspicious links, and reporting cyber incidents promptly to prevent attacks and data breaches.
