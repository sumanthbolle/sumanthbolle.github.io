Recent Security Developments: Naxalism Victory and Riyadh Missile Attack

The Prime Minister celebrated Diwali with security forces in Bastar, marking the country's victory over Naxalism after decades of conflict. Concurrently, Riyadh's financial district was targeted in a missile attack by Yemen's Houthis, escalating regional security tensions.

India marked a historic victory over Naxalism with development in Bastar, while Saudi Arabia faced a missile attack on Riyadh's financial district by Houthi forces.

Why is this in the news?

On November 8, 2026, the Prime Minister celebrated Diwali with security personnel in Bastar, Chhattisgarh, highlighting the end of Naxalism in India, while on the same day, Riyadh's financial district was hit by a missile attack attributed to Yemen's Houthis.

What is the significance of the Prime Minister's Diwali visit to Bastar?

  • Victory over Naxalism. The Prime Minister celebrated the first Diwali after India won its decades-long battle against Naxalism and Maoist terror, with every district now free from Naxalism.
  • Security forces' role. Lakhs of security personnel, including CRPF, CoBRA, BSF, ITBP, and special forces, were credited for their sacrifice and dedication in defeating Naxalism.
  • Development in Bastar. Bastar, once heavily affected by Naxalism, is now witnessing flourishing development with infrastructure like roads, mobile towers, schools, and skill centers established.
  • Policy changes. Post-2014, the government gave security forces freedom to act decisively and targeted the financial networks of Naxalites, breaking their supply chains.
  • Continuing challenges. Although armed Naxalites have been eliminated, intellectual Naxalites remain active, infiltrating political parties and organizations, posing ongoing security challenges.

What happened in the missile attack on Riyadh's financial district?

  • Attack details. On November 8, 2026, Riyadh's King Abdullah Financial District, including the Aramco tower, was targeted in a missile attack by Yemen's Houthi forces, causing visible damage.
  • Saudi response. The Saudi-led coalition intercepted and destroyed two ballistic missiles launched by the Houthi militia towards Riyadh.
  • Context of conflict. The attack is part of ongoing hostilities after the collapse of a 2022 truce in Yemen, with the Houthis seizing parts of the Red Sea coast and targeting Saudi infrastructure.
  • Regional implications. The missile attack marks a major escalation in the conflict, threatening Saudi Arabia's economic and social reforms symbolized by the financial district.
  • International involvement. Thousands of Syrian troops are deploying to Saudi Arabia and Yemen amid the conflict, which has caused over 1,600 deaths and 300,000 displacements since fighting resumed.

Back to basics: Internal Security and Terrorism

Internal security involves protecting a nation from threats such as terrorism, insurgency, and extremism within its borders. Terrorism refers to violent acts intended to create fear and achieve political aims. Effective internal security requires coordinated efforts by security forces, intelligence agencies, and government policies to prevent and respond to such threats.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements: 1. The Prime Minister celebrated Diwali in Bastar to mark the end of Naxalism and to honor security forces. 2. The missile attack on Riyadh targeted the King Abdullah Financial District and was launched by Iranian troops. Which of the statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 incorrectly attributes the missile attack to Iranian troops, whereas it was launched by Yemen's Houthi militia.
