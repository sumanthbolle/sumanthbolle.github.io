Israeli Airstrikes Kill Senior Hamas Officials in Gaza

Israeli military airstrikes in Gaza killed senior Hamas security officials including Mohammad Abuelouf and his family. This ongoing conflict affects regional security and has implications for India's foreign policy and internal security considerations.

Israeli strikes targeted senior Hamas security officials in Gaza, continuing hostilities despite a ceasefire.

Why is this in the news?

On November 25, 2026, Israeli airstrikes killed senior Hamas security official Mohammad Abuelouf and his family in Gaza, escalating tensions in the Israel-Hamas conflict.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What happened in the recent Israeli strikes in Gaza?

  • Targeted killing. Israeli military killed Mohammad Abuelouf, a senior Hamas security official, along with his wife and two children in an airstrike on their Gaza apartment.
  • Second senior official killed. This was the second Israeli strike in 24 hours targeting senior Hamas security officials, following the killing of Naeem Abu Nahel, head of Hamas internal security.
  • Locations of strikes. The airstrikes hit Abuelouf's apartment in the Beach refugee camp in western Gaza City and another strike targeted Gaza City's northern Karama neighbourhood.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does Israel target these Hamas officials?

  • Role of Abuelouf. Abuelouf was described by the Israeli military as a key figure who helped advance attacks against Israeli forces and civilians and worked to restore Hamas capabilities violating the 2025 ceasefire.
  • Hamas internal security. Hamas' internal security service, led by Nahel and with Abuelouf as a senior official, counters suspected collaborators and gathers intelligence on Israeli plans.
  • Israeli military justification. Israel considers these strikes as self-defence measures against Hamas militants who killed civilians and took hostages in 2023.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the broader context and impact of these strikes?

  • Ceasefire and ongoing violence. Despite a ceasefire in October 2025, violence continues with over 1,600 Palestinians and 5 Israeli soldiers killed since then.
  • Territorial control. Israeli troops now control about two-thirds of Gaza, while Hamas holds a small coastal strip.
  • Humanitarian crisis. Israel's assault since 2023 has killed over 74,000 Palestinians, caused hunger crises, displacement, and prompted UN inquiries into possible genocide.

Back to basics: Ceasefire and conflict dynamics

A ceasefire is an agreement to stop hostilities temporarily or permanently between conflicting parties. However, ceasefires may not end all violence, as underlying tensions and violations can continue, leading to renewed clashes. Understanding ceasefire agreements and their enforcement is crucial to analyzing ongoing conflicts like the Israel-Hamas situation.
