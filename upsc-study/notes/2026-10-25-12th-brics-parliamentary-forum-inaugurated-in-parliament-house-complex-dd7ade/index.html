12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum Inaugurated in Parliament House Complex

India hosted the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum on 26-27 October 2026, focusing on strengthening parliaments for people-centric development. The event highlighted India's role in parliamentary diplomacy and multilateral cooperation among BRICS nations, relevant for understanding India's global parliamentary engagements.

The 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum in India emphasized parliamentary diplomacy and cooperation among BRICS countries to address global challenges.

Why is this in the news?

The 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum was inaugurated in the Parliament House Complex on 26 October 2026, with participation from BRICS member countries and key parliamentary leaders.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum?

  • Forum overview. The 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum was held on 26-27 October 2026 in India's Parliament House Complex, focusing on 'Strengthening Parliaments for People-Centric Development.'
  • Event structure. The forum included four high-level sessions on Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation, and Sustainability, along with parallel meetings, bilateral exchanges, and a cultural programme.
  • Special meetings. A special meeting of Chairpersons of the International Affairs Committees of BRICS parliaments was held to discuss parliamentary diplomacy, capacity building, and best practices exchange.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does the BRICS Parliamentary Forum matter for India?

  • India's BRICS Presidency. India held the BRICS Presidency in 2026, guiding the forum with a people-centric approach to strengthen cooperation among BRICS parliaments.
  • Parliamentary diplomacy. The forum emphasized sustained institutional engagement and regular exchanges of parliamentary delegations, especially among young and women parliamentarians.
  • Global challenges. Discussions focused on collective efforts to address terrorism, climate change, and making international institutions more responsive to global changes.
  • UN Security Council reforms. India reiterated the need for reforms of the United Nations Security Council and appreciated Russia's support for India's permanent membership.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does the forum reflect India-Russia parliamentary cooperation?

  • Bilateral meeting. Ahead of the forum, Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla met Russian State Duma Chairman Vyacheslav Volodin to discuss strengthening cooperation at global fora and combating terrorism.
  • Strategic partnership. The meeting highlighted the 80 years of India-Russia diplomatic relations and the Special and Privileged Strategic Partnership strengthened under current leadership.
  • Trade and cultural ties. Both sides stressed promoting joint business ventures, removing trade obstacles, and enhancing cultural and people-to-people exchanges.
  • Parliamentary exchanges. Regular meetings of the India-Russia Inter-Parliamentary Commission and exchanges of young and women parliamentarians were emphasized.

Back to basics: Parliamentary Diplomacy

Parliamentary diplomacy refers to the interactions and cooperation between the legislative bodies of different countries to promote mutual understanding, peace, and collaboration. It complements traditional diplomacy by involving parliamentarians in dialogue, capacity building, and exchange of best practices. This form of diplomacy helps address global challenges through multilateral forums and strengthens bilateral relations.
