Static anchor

Electoral awareness is citizens’ understanding of voting rights, democratic procedures, civic responsibilities and the need to evaluate public information critically. In this issue, MY Bharat operationalises it through interactive youth dialogues rather than treating participation as voting alone.

Background

  • Mera Yuva Bharat (MY Bharat) is a national platform established by the Department of Youth Affairs, Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, and launched on 31 October 2023.[2]
  • The platform is intended to support youth empowerment, social mobility, educational equity and practical skills.[2][5]
  • The campaign uses Yuva Samvads—interactive discussions involving youth, Youth Icons and public representatives—to explain democratic processes, rights and responsibilities.

India implications

  • Supports democratic citizenship by making voting rights and civic duties more accessible to young and first-time voters.
  • May improve the quality of public participation when electoral awareness is combined with fact-checking, critical thinking and interaction with elected representatives.
  • A nationwide, decentralised format can widen outreach across diverse regions, but effectiveness should be assessed through transparent outcome indicators rather than event counts alone.

Way forward

  • Publish outcome indicators beyond programme counts, including knowledge gains, registration support and sustained civic engagement.
  • Ensure participation reaches first-time voters and socially, geographically and digitally marginalised youth.
  • Provide accessible, neutral and procedure-focused electoral information and clear referral to competent election authorities.
  • Institutionalise post-dialogue activities such as voter education resources, question tracking and feedback mechanisms.
