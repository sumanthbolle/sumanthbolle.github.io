Why in news

The Press Information Bureau source record reports that Mizoram has received approval for new road works under PMGSY-IV to strengthen rural connectivity. The source title establishes the programme, State and action, but not the project’s quantities, cost, timeline or outcomes.

Static anchor

PMGSY is a rural-road connectivity programme of the Ministry of Rural Development that seeks to provide all-weather road access to eligible unconnected rural habitations; PMGSY-IV applies prescribed population and planning criteria.[2][3]

Background

  • PMGSY-IV was approved on 11 September 2024 to provide all-weather connectivity to about 25,000 unconnected habitations; its eligibility uses Census 2011 population criteria, including 250-plus population for North-Eastern and hill States.[2
  • The PMGSY-IV guidelines state that the Core Network is the basis for selection of works and that planning uses tools including the PMGSY Gram Sadak Survey App, Geo-Sadak and the PM Gati Shakti Portal.[3]
  • The guidelines also provide for e-procurement and scrutiny of project proposals through designated institutional arrangements.[3]
