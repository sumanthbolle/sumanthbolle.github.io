India-France MoU to Preserve and Digitise Indian Manuscripts

On 26 October 2026, India's Ministry of Culture signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the Bibliothèque nationale de France (BnF) to collaborate on preserving and digitising Indian-origin manuscripts. This partnership under the Gyan Bharatam Mission is key for aspirants studying cultural heritage preservation and international cooperation in the UPSC syllabus.

The Ministry of Culture and BnF signed an MoU to cooperate on digitisation, conservation, and research of Indian manuscripts under the Gyan Bharatam Mission.

Why is this in the news?

The Ministry of Culture signed an MoU with the Bibliothèque nationale de France on 26 October 2026 in Paris to strengthen preservation and digitisation of India's manuscript heritage.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What does the MoU between India and France entail?

  • Partnership scope. The MoU establishes cooperation between the Ministry of Culture, National Archives of India, National Library Kolkata, and BnF focusing on digitisation, cross-sharing on the National Digital Repository, technology exchange, conservation, and research & development.
  • Key activities. It includes identifying and digitising Indic-origin manuscripts held by BnF, exchanging expertise on preservation technologies, cataloguing standards, OCR/HTR tools for Indian scripts, digital humanities, and manuscript conservation.
  • Collaborative initiatives. The partnership plans joint workshops, capacity-building programmes, research initiatives, publications, exhibitions, and scholar exchanges.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why is this MoU significant for India’s cultural heritage?

  • Gyan Bharatam Mission. The MoU supports the Gyan Bharatam Mission, the Ministry of Culture's flagship initiative for identifying, documenting, conserving, digitising, translating, researching, and disseminating India's manuscript heritage.
  • National Digital Repository. The mission’s National Digital Repository aims to securely store Indian-origin manuscripts and knowledge systems from India and worldwide, enabling structured and permission-based access for research.
  • International cooperation. This partnership strengthens international collaboration for preserving, studying, and disseminating India's manuscript heritage, creating opportunities for scholarly and technological exchange.

Back to basics: Manuscript Preservation and Digitisation

Manuscript preservation involves protecting ancient and historical documents from physical deterioration through conservation techniques. Digitisation converts these manuscripts into digital formats, enabling easier access, research, and long-term preservation. National digital repositories serve as secure platforms to store and share these digitised manuscripts with scholars and the public, often requiring international cooperation for manuscripts held abroad.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the MoU signed between India's Ministry of Culture and the Bibliothèque nationale de France: 1. The MoU focuses on digitisation, technology exchange, conservation, and research of Indic-origin manuscripts. 2. The MoU was signed under the Gyan Bharatam Mission by the President of India and the Director of BnF. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the MoU was signed by the Secretary of the Ministry of Culture and the President of BnF, not the President of India or the Director of BnF.

Mains question (15 marks)

Intro: International cooperation plays a vital role in preserving and promoting India's manuscript heritage.

  • The MoU under the Gyan Bharatam Mission facilitates digitisation, conservation, and research of Indic-origin manuscripts held in India and abroad.
  • Such partnerships enable technology exchange, capacity building, and scholarly collaboration, enhancing preservation standards.
  • The National Digital Repository ensures secure, permission-based access to manuscripts, promoting wider dissemination and study.
  • International cooperation helps bridge knowledge gaps and safeguards heritage that is geographically dispersed.

Conclusion: The India-France MoU exemplifies how global collaboration strengthens cultural heritage preservation and research.
