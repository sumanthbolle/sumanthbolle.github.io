Andhra Pradesh Cabinet Approves Policies to Boost AI, Electronics, Renewable Energy

The Andhra Pradesh Cabinet approved multiple policy frameworks and investment proposals on November 10, 2026, to attract investments in artificial intelligence, electronics, renewable energy, pharmaceuticals, and manufacturing. This development is important for UPSC aspirants to understand state-level initiatives in technology-driven economic growth and energy transition.

Andhra Pradesh's Cabinet approved policies and major investments to promote AI, electronics, renewable energy, and related sectors, reflecting the state's focus on technology and sustainable development.

Why is this in the news?

On November 10, 2026, the Andhra Pradesh Cabinet chaired by Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu approved policy frameworks and investment proposals to attract investments in AI, electronics, renewable energy, and other sectors.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What policy frameworks did Andhra Pradesh Cabinet approve?

  • AI and Robotics policies. The Cabinet approved the Animation, Visual Effects, Gaming, Comics and Extended Reality (AVGC-XR) Policy 2026-31 and the Robotics and Physical AI Policy 2026-36.
  • Semiconductor policy amendment. Amendments to the Semiconductor and Display Fab Policy 2024-29 were cleared to enhance investment attractiveness.
  • Traffic management setup. A dedicated Directorate of Traffic Management and Road Safety was approved under the supervision of the Director-General of Police, headquartered at Mangalagiri.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What major investment proposals were approved?

  • AI data centre investment. AM Intelligence received approval for a ₹31,387 crore investment in a 512 MW green integrated AI data centre at Amanam in Visakhapatnam district.
  • Other key projects. The Cabinet approved a ₹4,000.20 crore advanced materials manufacturing project by Carbon Cosmo at Naidupeta, and land allotment to Dixon Technologies in Tirupati for ₹700 crore investment.
  • Reliance AI data centre. About 25 acres were allocated to Reliance Industries for a gigascale AI data centre at Bhogapuram in Vizianagaram district.
  • Additional manufacturing units. An Epack unit for AI data centre equipment was approved at Rambilli with ₹570.20 crore investment, expected to create 2,565 jobs.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What initiatives were taken in renewable energy and infrastructure?

  • Compressed biogas plants. The Cabinet approved establishing compressed biogas plants in NTR, Prakasam, and Vizianagaram districts.
  • Waste-to-energy plant. 5.50 acres of APGENCO land was transferred to Vijayawada Municipal Corporation for a 15 MW waste-to-energy plant.
  • Solar power project. A 30-year lease of 192.68 acres of Wakf land in Anantapur district was approved for a solar power project.
  • Nuclear power feasibility. The Cabinet agreed to examine the feasibility of setting up nuclear power plants using Small Modular Reactor technology as an alternative to thermal power.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What other infrastructure and social initiatives were approved?

  • Industrial parks for women and MSMEs. A 30-acre FICCI FLO industrial park exclusively for women entrepreneurs at Jaggayyapet and an MSME park at Thamballapalle were approved.
  • Education and training facilities. Allotment of land for a Kendriya Vidyalaya at Shakamuru and a Government Industrial Training Institute at Penukonda was approved.
  • Temple administration posts. Creation of posts including Principal Sannidhi Yadava and Chief Sthapathi in the Endowments Department was approved.

Back to basics: State policy frameworks for economic development

State governments formulate policy frameworks to attract investments and promote sectors like technology, manufacturing, and renewable energy. These policies provide incentives, infrastructure support, and regulatory clarity to encourage private and public sector participation. Understanding such frameworks helps explain how states contribute to economic growth and energy transition within the federal structure.
