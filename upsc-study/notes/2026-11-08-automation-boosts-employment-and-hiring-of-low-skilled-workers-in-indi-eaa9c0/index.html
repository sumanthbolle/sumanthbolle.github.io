Automation Increases Employment and Low-Skill Hiring in India: ABB Insight

ABB India reports that automation has expanded its workforce and enabled hiring of lower-skilled workers by simplifying tasks. This challenges common fears that automation reduces jobs, especially for less skilled labor, making it relevant for understanding technology's impact on employment.

Automation in ABB India's factories has increased employment and allowed the hiring and training of lower-skilled workers by reducing skill requirements.

Why is this in the news?

ABB India's CEO and managing director highlighted in November 2026 that automation has boosted employment and the hiring of low-skilled workers in India, alongside a $75 million investment plan.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How has automation affected employment at ABB India?

  • Employment growth. ABB India increased its workforce by 57%, from around 7,000 workers in 2021 to over 11,000 in 2026, driven by automation-enabled growth.
  • Lower skill requirements. Automation reduced the need for highly skilled labor by using robotics and guiding screens, enabling the employment and quick training of younger, lower-skilled workers.
  • Quality and precision. Tools like sensor-equipped screwdrivers ensure quality and precision, allowing less skilled workers to perform complex tasks reliably.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What investments and expansion plans does ABB India have?

  • Investment commitment. ABB India committed $75 million (around ₹708 crore) for 2026-2027 to expand operations, focusing on Tier-II and Tier-III cities.
  • Capital expenditure. The company has maintained an average capital expenditure of ₹300 crore annually over the past 5-6 years to modernize factories.
  • Geographic focus shift. ABB shifted focus from major metros to smaller cities, creating cluster offices to serve growing industrial demand in India's hinterland.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for employment and inclusive growth?

  • Contrary to fears. Automation is often feared to cause job losses, especially for low-skilled workers, but ABB India's experience shows it can increase employment and inclusivity.
  • Skill development. Automation facilitates training of younger and less skilled workers, helping integrate them into modern industrial jobs.
  • Economic expansion. Automation-driven productivity growth supports business expansion and job creation, contributing to inclusive economic development.

Back to basics: Automation and Employment

Automation uses technology like robotics and sensors to perform tasks with minimal human intervention. It can change the nature of work by reducing the need for certain skills but also creating new roles that require different skills. Understanding how automation affects employment helps analyze economic growth and inclusive development.
