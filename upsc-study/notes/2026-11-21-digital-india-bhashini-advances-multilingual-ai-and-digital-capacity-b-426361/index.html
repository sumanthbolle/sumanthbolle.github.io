Digital India BHASHINI Advances Multilingual AI and Digital Capacity Building

The Digital India BHASHINI Division and NIELIT have signed an MoU to collaborate on multilingual AI, technology education, and capacity building under the BHASHINI for Seva/Sanchalan programme. This partnership aims to reduce language barriers in technical education and digital learning, making digital knowledge and emerging technologies accessible across India's linguistic diversity.

BHASHINI's collaboration with NIELIT leverages multilingual AI to enhance inclusive access to technology education and digital services in India.

Why is this in the news?

On 19 November 2026, the Digital India BHASHINI Division and NIELIT exchanged an MoU to advance multilingual AI applications, technology education, and digital capacity building under the BHASHINI for Seva/Sanchalan programme.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the BHASHINI and NIELIT collaboration about?

  • MoU details. The Digital India BHASHINI Division and NIELIT signed a Memorandum of Understanding to collaborate on advancing multilingual AI applications, technology education, research, and capacity building under the BHASHINI for Seva/Sanchalan programme.
  • Focus on language barriers. The collaboration aims to reduce language barriers in technical education and digital learning by enabling access to technology knowledge and training in preferred Indian languages.
  • Integration and innovation. Both organisations will explore integrating BHASHINI's AI-powered language technologies into NIELIT's educational and training ecosystems, supporting multilingual access to learning resources and promoting AI-powered translation and speech technologies.
  • Inclusive learning environments. The partnership seeks to create inclusive and accessible learning environments where language does not constrain engagement with emerging technologies and technical subjects.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How is BHASHINI advancing multilingual AI in healthcare?

  • Healthcare applications. At the Global Digital Health Summit 2026, BHASHINI showcased multilingual AI applications supporting healthcare professionals through speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and translation technologies across institutions like AIIMS, JIPMER, and the Directorate General of Medical Services (Army).
  • Clinical workflow integration. BHASHINI's technologies enable voice-based documentation of clinical histories, prescriptions, and discharge instructions, facilitating doctor-patient communication across languages and simplifying clinical workflows.
  • Speech-to-text capability. The speech-to-text tool 'Shrutlekh' supports real-time multilingual transcription and translation, helping overcome language barriers in healthcare settings.
  • Impact on healthcare. These multilingual AI applications improve efficiency, accessibility, and inclusivity in healthcare delivery, reflecting the potential of India's indigenous language technology ecosystem.

[3] Press Information Bureau

Why does this matter for digital governance and capacity building?

  • Government initiatives. BHASHINI's work aligns with the Government of India's vision of a digitally empowered and technology-driven nation, contributing towards Viksit Bharat 2047 by making language an enabler rather than a barrier.
  • Capacity building focus. The collaboration with NIELIT and applications in healthcare demonstrate efforts to expand digital literacy, technical education, and skill development across linguistic groups.
  • Enhancing public services. Multilingual AI can strengthen delivery of institutional services and public-facing digital platforms, making them more accessible to citizens in their preferred languages.
  • Research and innovation. The partnership encourages research, innovation, and community participation to strengthen India's indigenous language technology ecosystem.

Back to basics: Multilingual Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Multilingual AI refers to artificial intelligence systems designed to understand, process, and generate multiple languages. This technology helps overcome language barriers by enabling translation, speech recognition, and text-to-speech services across diverse linguistic groups. In India, with its vast linguistic diversity, multilingual AI facilitates inclusive access to digital services, education, and governance by allowing people to interact in their preferred languages.
