Inaugural BIMSTEC Maritime Security Exercise Advances Regional Cooperation

The first BIMSTEC Maritime Security Exercise was held from 30 October to 3 November 2026, involving Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, and Thailand. This exercise enhances maritime cooperation and security in the Bay of Bengal, a vital area for trade and connectivity.

The inaugural BIMSTEC Maritime Security Exercise strengthens regional maritime security and cooperation among South and South-East Asian nations.

Why is this in the news?

The BIMSTEC Maritime Security Exercise was conducted from 30 October to 3 November 2026 to boost maritime cooperation among member states in the Bay of Bengal region.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the BIMSTEC Maritime Security Exercise?

  • Exercise details. The inaugural exercise was conducted from 30 October to 3 November 2026, involving seven BIMSTEC member states: Bangladesh, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, and Thailand.
  • Purpose. It aims to enhance interoperability, maritime domain awareness, coordination mechanisms, and exchange of best practices among member states.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does the exercise matter for regional security?

  • Bay of Bengal's strategic role. The Bay of Bengal connects South and South-East Asia and supports trade, connectivity, energy security, and economic growth, making it a critical maritime space.
  • Regional stability. The exercise reinforces member states' commitment to regional security, stability, and prosperity, promoting a secure, open, and inclusive maritime domain.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the significance of the Andaman and Nicobar Islands in this context?

  • Strategic location. The Andaman and Nicobar Islands are strategically located astride critical sea lanes of communication in the Bay of Bengal.
  • Role of ANC. India’s Andaman and Nicobar Command (ANC), the first integrated tri-service command, plays a key role in maritime surveillance, humanitarian assistance, disaster relief, search and rescue, and regional engagement.

Back to basics: Maritime Security Cooperation

Maritime security cooperation involves collaboration among countries to ensure safe and secure seas, which are vital for trade, energy, and regional stability. Exercises like BIMSTEC's help improve coordination, share best practices, and build trust among navies and coast guards to address common challenges such as piracy, trafficking, and disaster response.
