PMEGP Releases ₹1,000 Crore Margin Money Subsidy to Support 11,333 Micro Enterprises

The Ministry of MSME released ₹1,000 crore as margin money subsidy under PMEGP to facilitate bank loans of ₹3,260 crore for new micro enterprises, generating over 1.02 lakh jobs. This scheme is crucial for UPSC aspirants to understand government efforts in promoting inclusive entrepreneurship and rural employment.

PMEGP's ₹1,000 crore subsidy supports 11,333 new micro enterprises, fostering rural employment and inclusive growth.

Why is this in the news?

On 28 October 2026, the Union Minister for MSME released ₹1,000 crore margin money subsidy under PMEGP to support new micro enterprises across India.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the PMEGP scheme and how does it work?

  • Scheme overview. PMEGP is a flagship credit-linked subsidy programme by the Ministry of MSME aimed at creating sustainable self-employment through new micro-enterprises in the non-farm sector.
  • Implementation. The scheme is implemented nationally by the Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC) with support from agencies like KVIBs, District Industries Centres, and Coir Board.
  • Financial assistance. Entrepreneurs receive margin money subsidy on bank-sanctioned project loans to set up micro-enterprises, along with awareness, capacity building, market linkages, and post-establishment support.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the recent developments and impact of PMEGP?

  • Recent subsidy release. ₹1,000 crore margin money subsidy was released to support 11,333 beneficiaries who secured bank loans totaling ₹3,260 crore for new micro-enterprises.
  • Employment generation. These enterprises are expected to generate over 1.02 lakh sustainable employment opportunities, mainly in rural areas.
  • Inclusive focus. The assistance targets women-led entrepreneurship, SC/ST communities, and focuses on North Eastern Region and Aspirational Districts for balanced regional development.
  • Performance since 2014-15. Since FY 2014-15, PMEGP has facilitated over 8.38 lakh micro enterprises with bank loans exceeding ₹70,000 crore and margin money subsidy over ₹25,193 crore, generating about 75 lakh jobs.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does PMEGP matter for economic growth and social inclusion?

  • Rural and social inclusion. Nearly 80% of assisted units are in rural areas, with about 50% beneficiaries from SC, ST, and OBC categories, and 40% enterprises owned by women entrepreneurs.
  • Entrepreneurship and local economies. PMEGP promotes grassroots entrepreneurship, strengthens local economies, and supports sustainable livelihoods, aligning with the government's vision of an inclusive, self-reliant economy.
  • Government commitment. The Ministry of MSME is committed to expanding PMEGP's reach and enhancing the entrepreneurship ecosystem to promote local manufacturing and services.

Back to basics: Credit-linked subsidy schemes

Credit-linked subsidy schemes provide financial assistance by subsidizing part of the loan amount to reduce the upfront cost for entrepreneurs. They aim to encourage self-employment and enterprise creation by making credit more affordable and accessible, especially for disadvantaged groups and in rural areas.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the Prime Minister’s Employment Generation Programme (PMEGP): 1. PMEGP is implemented by the Ministry of MSME through the Khadi and Village Industries Commission (KVIC) and other agencies. 2. The recent ₹1,000 crore margin money subsidy release under PMEGP supports over 11,000 new micro enterprises and facilitates bank loans of about ₹3,260 crore. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (c). Statement 1 is correct as PMEGP is implemented by Ministry of MSME through KVIC and other agencies; Statement 2 is correct as per the recent subsidy release details.
