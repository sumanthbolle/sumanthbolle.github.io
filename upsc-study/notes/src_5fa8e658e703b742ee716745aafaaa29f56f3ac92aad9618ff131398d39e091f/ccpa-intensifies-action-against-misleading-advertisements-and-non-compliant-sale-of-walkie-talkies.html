India implications

  • Consumers may face legal or financial consequences when products are advertised without clear information on lawful operating conditions.
  • E-commerce platforms and sellers may need stronger product-information governance, including frequency, licensing and approval disclosures.
  • The issue illustrates the need to align consumer protection with sectoral regulation of wireless devices.

Way forward

  • Create standardised, prominent fields for operating frequency, licensing status and regulatory approvals in online listings of wireless devices.
  • Require platforms to use risk-based checks for products whose lawful use depends on technical specifications or authorisations.
  • Issue simple seller guidance distinguishing licence-exempt devices from devices requiring regulatory compliance.
  • Combine penalties with correction, delisting and consumer-facing awareness so enforcement improves future compliance.
