Back to basics: Bilateral Trade Agreements

Bilateral trade agreements are treaties between two countries to facilitate trade by reducing tariffs, import quotas, and other trade barriers. They aim to enhance economic cooperation and can cover sectors like goods, services, investment, and intellectual property. Such agreements are tools of economic diplomacy and influence international relations.
