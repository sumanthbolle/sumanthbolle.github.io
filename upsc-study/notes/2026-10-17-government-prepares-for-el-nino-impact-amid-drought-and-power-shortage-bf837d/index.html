Government Prepares for El Nino Impact Amid Drought and Power Shortages

The Telangana government is taking measures to prevent power shortages due to El Nino conditions, including purchasing electricity from other states and promoting renewable energy. This is important for UPSC aspirants to understand the climate-related challenges affecting agriculture and energy security in India.

El Nino conditions are causing drought and power shortages, prompting government action to secure electricity and support renewable energy.

Why is this in the news?

On October 15, 2026, Telangana's Revenue Minister Ponguleti Srinivas Reddy announced government steps to prevent power shortages amid El Nino conditions.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What measures is the government taking to address power shortages caused by El Nino?

  • Electricity purchase. The Telangana government is prepared to buy electricity from other states at ₹12 per unit to prevent power shortages.
  • Renewable energy focus. The government is promoting solar and wind energy projects to meet the growing power demand sustainably.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How is the government managing water and agriculture amid El Nino?

  • Water supply ensured. Despite El Nino, the government has ensured drinking and irrigation water availability, aided by recent cyclone-induced rainfall.
  • Welfare initiatives. The government continues welfare measures like free electricity up to 200 units for eligible households and farmers' support schemes.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does El Nino matter for India’s climate and economy?

  • Impact on monsoon. El Nino disrupts normal monsoon patterns, leading to drought conditions that affect agriculture and water resources.
  • Energy security challenge. Reduced hydroelectric power generation during droughts increases the risk of power shortages, necessitating alternative energy sources.

Back to basics: El Nino and its impact on Indian monsoon

El Nino is a climate phenomenon characterized by the warming of sea surface temperatures in the central and eastern Pacific Ocean, which affects global weather patterns. In India, El Nino often leads to weaker monsoon rains, causing droughts and water shortages. This impacts agriculture, water availability, and power generation, especially hydroelectricity, making it a critical factor in planning for climate resilience and energy security.
