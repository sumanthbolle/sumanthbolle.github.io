Coal Stocks at Thermal Power Plants Remain Above 18 Million Tonnes

As of October 10, 2026, coal stocks at domestic thermal power plants held steady above 18 million tonnes, with supply deficits narrowing due to improved coal deliveries and rail freight movement. This update is crucial for understanding India's energy security and the stability of thermal power generation.

Coal stocks at thermal power plants have stabilized above 18 MT, ensuring adequate supply for power generation amid improved coal transport and supply management.

Why is this in the news?

On October 12, 2026, The Hindu reported that coal stocks at thermal power plants remained above 18 MT as supply deficits narrowed, reflecting improved coal supply and rail freight movement.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the current status of coal stocks at thermal power plants?

  • Stable coal stocks. Coal stocks at domestic thermal power plants were 18.205 million tonnes as of October 10, 2026, remaining stable above 18 MT since September 29.
  • Daily consumption and supply. Thermal power plants consume less than 3 MT of coal daily, with supplies averaging 2.49 MT per day, closely matching consumption.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why do coal stocks at thermal plants matter for India’s power sector?

  • Thermal power’s share. Thermal power accounts for more than 60% of electricity generated during peak demand hours, making coal availability critical.
  • Supply-demand balance. The narrowing gap between coal consumption and supply, from a deficit of 0.24 MT per day in September to 0.06 MT per day in early October, helps ensure uninterrupted power generation.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How has coal supply and transportation improved recently?

  • Increased rail freight. Rail freight movement for coal transport increased, with daily rake loading rising 10% to an average of 469 rakes in the first 10 days of October compared to September.
  • Supply monitoring. The Coal Ministry monitors coal supply closely, redistributing coal to plants with critical stocks to maintain adequate supply.

Back to basics: Thermal Power Generation and Coal Supply

Thermal power plants generate electricity by burning coal to produce steam that drives turbines. Coal supply is critical for continuous power generation, especially during peak demand. Coal stocks at power plants act as a buffer against supply disruptions. Railways play a key role in transporting coal from mines to power plants, affecting the reliability of coal supply.
