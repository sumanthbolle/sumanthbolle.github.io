Jaishankar and UAE Foreign Minister Discuss West Asia Situation in Mumbai

External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar welcomed UAE Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed Al Nahyan in Mumbai on October 19, 2026, ahead of bilateral talks. They discussed enhancing bilateral ties and the overall situation in West Asia, including recent attacks on Saudi Arabia.

India and UAE held high-level talks in Mumbai focusing on bilateral cooperation and West Asia regional security.

Why is this in the news?

The UAE Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister visited Mumbai on October 19, 2026, for talks with India's External Affairs Minister, focusing on bilateral ties and West Asia security.

What was the purpose of the UAE Foreign Minister's visit to Mumbai?

  • Bilateral talks. Sheikh Abdullah bin Zayed Al Nahyan visited Mumbai for a two-day trip to hold wide-ranging talks with External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar.
  • Focus areas. The discussions aimed to enhance cooperation in trade, energy, defence, fintech, and connectivity.
  • Regional security. They also deliberated on the overall situation in West Asia, including recent attacks on Saudi Arabia.

  • West Asia security. India’s engagement with the UAE is important for understanding and influencing the security dynamics in West Asia.
  • Economic and defence ties. Enhancing cooperation in trade, energy, and defence aligns with India’s broader foreign policy goals in the region.

Back to basics: Bilateral Diplomacy

Bilateral diplomacy involves direct talks and cooperation between two countries to strengthen political, economic, and security ties. It helps countries address mutual interests and regional issues through dialogue and agreements. Such diplomacy is crucial for managing regional stability and advancing national interests.
