IAF Partners with IIT Madras for Indigenous Secure Airborne Communication

The Indian Air Force's Software Development Institute signed a Memorandum of Agreement with IIT Madras and IITM Pravartak Technologies Foundation to jointly develop an indigenous digital secure communication system for airborne applications. This collaboration aims to reduce dependency on imported systems and enhance operational efficiency, aligning with the Atmanirbhar Bharat vision.

The IAF's partnership with IIT Madras and Pravartak Technologies Foundation focuses on creating an indigenous secure digital communication system for airborne use to strengthen national security and self-reliance.

Why is this in the news?

On 6 November 2026, the IAF's Software Development Institute signed an MoA with IIT Madras and IITM Pravartak Technologies Foundation to develop an indigenous digital secure communication system for airborne applications.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the MoA between IAF, IIT Madras, and Pravartak Technologies?

  • Partnership details. The Software Development Institute of the Indian Air Force signed a Memorandum of Agreement with IIT Madras and IITM Pravartak Technologies Foundation for jointly designing and developing an indigenous digital communication system for airborne applications.
  • Signatories. The MoA was signed by Air Vice Marshal Sarabjit Singh, Commandant SDI, and Dr MJ Shankar Raman, CEO of IITM Pravartak Technologies Foundation, in the presence of Prof V Kamakoti, Director of IIT Madras.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why is this collaboration significant?

  • Indigenous development. The collaboration aims to develop advanced algorithms for communication systems and networking indigenously, reducing reliance on imported systems.
  • Operational efficiency. The indigenous secure communication system is expected to bolster the operational efficiency of the Indian Air Force.
  • Atmanirbhar Bharat vision. This initiative contributes to the Atmanirbhar Bharat vision by promoting self-reliance in critical defence technology.

Back to basics: Indigenous Defence Technology Development

Indigenous defence technology development involves designing and producing defence equipment and systems within the country to reduce dependence on foreign imports. It enhances national security by ensuring control over critical technologies and supports self-reliance initiatives like Atmanirbhar Bharat. Collaboration between defence institutions and academic or technology organizations is a common approach to achieve this goal.
