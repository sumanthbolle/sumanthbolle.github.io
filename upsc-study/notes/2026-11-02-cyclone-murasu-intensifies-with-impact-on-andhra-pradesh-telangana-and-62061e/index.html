Back to basics: Cyclones and Disaster Management

Cyclones are intense circular storms that originate over warm ocean waters and can cause heavy rainfall, strong winds, and flooding when they make landfall. Disaster management involves preparedness, response, and recovery efforts by governments and agencies to minimize damage and protect lives during such natural calamities. Effective coordination and early warning systems are crucial to managing cyclone impacts.
