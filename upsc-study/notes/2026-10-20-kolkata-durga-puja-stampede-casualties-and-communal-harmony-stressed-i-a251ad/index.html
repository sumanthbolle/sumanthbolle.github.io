Kolkata Durga Puja Stampede and Communal Harmony Emphasised in Bangladesh

A stampede during Durga Puja in Kolkata caused casualties, highlighting public safety concerns during major festivals. Meanwhile, Bangladesh celebrated Durga Puja under heavy security with leaders stressing communal harmony and equal rights, relevant for understanding social cohesion and communalism in South Asia.

What happened during Durga Puja in Kolkata and Bangladesh?

  • Kolkata stampede casualties. A stampede occurred during Durga Puja festivities in Kolkata, resulting in casualties and raising concerns about public safety during large cultural events.
  • Bangladesh Durga Puja celebrations. Bangladesh held Durga Puja with extensive security measures, including police surveillance, SWAT teams, drones, and a mobile app for reporting incidents, to ensure safety and communal harmony.

How did Bangladesh's leadership respond to Durga Puja celebrations?

  • Prime Minister's message. Prime Minister Tarique Rahman urged Hindus to celebrate with joy and without fear, emphasizing that religion belongs to individuals but the state belongs to all, guaranteeing security regardless of faith.
  • President's message. President Mirza Fakhrul Islam Alamgir highlighted Durga Puja's cultural significance beyond religion and called for free, discrimination-free festival observance, underscoring government support for all religious communities.

Back to basics: Communalism and Secularism

Communalism refers to tensions and conflicts between different religious or ethnic communities, often leading to social unrest. Secularism is the principle of separating religion from the state, ensuring equal treatment and freedom for all religions. In India and Bangladesh, secularism aims to protect minority rights and maintain social harmony in diverse societies.
