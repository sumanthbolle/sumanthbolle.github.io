India raises concerns over UN's unpaid assessments and liquidity in 2026

India highlighted the United Nations' challenging liquidity situation in 2026, pointing out unacceptably high unpaid assessments mainly from one member state. This matters for UPSC as it reflects India's position on international financial obligations and multilateral governance.

India emphasized the need for full and timely payment of UN dues to ensure the organization's financial health in 2026.

Why is this in the news?

India voiced concerns at the UN General Assembly's Fifth Committee on October 19, 2026, about the UN's liquidity challenges and high unpaid assessments.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What concerns did India raise about the UN's financial situation in 2026?

  • Liquidity challenges. India noted that the UN faced a challenging liquidity situation in 2026 due to collection uncertainties and low cash balances.
  • High unpaid assessments. India pointed out that unpaid assessments for the UN's regular budget remained at unacceptably high levels, with over 90% owed by a single member state.
  • Peacekeeping budget arrears. India described the financial situation of UN peacekeeping as more worrisome, with unpaid assessments reaching $4.1 billion, mainly from two member states.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for India's global role and UPSC preparation?

  • India's commitment to UN dues. India has consistently paid its UN assessments in full, on time, and often in advance, demonstrating its commitment to international obligations.
  • Impact on multilateral governance. India's stance highlights the importance of financial discipline among UN member states for the organization's effective functioning.
  • Relevance for UPSC. Understanding India's position on UN financing helps in analyzing its role in global governance and international diplomacy.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How are UN budget contributions and arrears distributed?

  • US as largest contributor and defaulter. The US pays 22% of the UN regular budget and 27% of the peacekeeping budget but still owes over $4 billion in arrears.
  • Other major contributors. China is the second-largest contributor, having paid $641.4 million in September for its regular budget share.
  • Member states' payment status. By September 30, 2026, 141 member states had paid their assessments in full, up from 136 the previous year.

Back to basics: UN Budget and Member Contributions

The United Nations is funded by assessed contributions from its member states based on agreed scales reflecting their capacity to pay. These contributions finance the regular budget and peacekeeping operations. Timely payment of dues is essential for the UN's liquidity and operational effectiveness. Unpaid assessments can create financial challenges and affect the organization's ability to carry out its mandates.

Common mistake. The high unpaid assessments are not a general issue among all member states but are concentrated mainly with one or two states.
