NICDC Launches Bihar Integrated Manufacturing City Gaya Limited

The National Industrial Corridor Development Corporation (NICDC) launched Bihar Integrated Manufacturing City Gaya Limited (BIMCGL) to boost industrial development in Bihar. This initiative is important for UPSC aspirants as it highlights government efforts in regional industrial infrastructure, investment promotion, and employment generation.

NICDC's BIMCGL project aims to develop a manufacturing hub in Gaya with investments of ₹16,500 crore and employment for 1.10 lakh people.

Why is this in the news?

NICDC formally launched BIMCGL on 24 October 2026 to develop an integrated manufacturing cluster in Gaya, Bihar, under the Amritsar–Kolkata Industrial Corridor.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is Bihar Integrated Manufacturing City Gaya Limited (BIMCGL)?

  • Special Purpose Vehicle. BIMCGL is a joint venture between Bihar Industrial Area Development Authority and National Industrial Corridor Development and Implementation Trust, incorporated on 6 January 2025 to implement the Integrated Manufacturing Cluster at Gaya.
  • Project scale and approval. Spread over 1,670 acres in Dobhi block, Gaya district, the project was approved by the Government of India on 28 August 2024 at a total cost of ₹1,339 crore.
  • Investment and employment potential. The project is expected to attract investments of approximately ₹16,500 crore and generate employment for around 1.10 lakh people.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How will BIMCGL boost industrial development in Bihar?

  • Infrastructure development. The industrial cluster is being developed with integrated infrastructure including roads, water supply, sewerage, power distribution, solid waste management, and fire safety systems.
  • Strategic location. Located near NH-22 and NH-19 with Gaya Airport about 39 km away, the site offers connectivity to major industrial and commercial centers.
  • Sector focus. IMC Gaya will focus on agro-based industries, textiles, readymade garments, furniture, handicrafts, handloom, engineering, and fabrication.
  • Investor facilitation. BIMCGL launched an Integrated Land Allotment Portal and official website to improve transparency, streamline land allotment, and enhance investor facilitation.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the recent developments and investments at IMC Gaya?

  • MoUs signed. 12 Memoranda of Understanding were signed for approximately 300 acres with proposed investments of ₹4,200 crore and estimated employment of 16,095 opportunities.
  • Earlier MoUs. Earlier, MoUs covering 2,586 acres with proposed investments of ₹31,750 crore and employment potential of 52,050 were executed.
  • Infrastructure progress. The State Government has taken possession of the entire project land; physical construction has started, including an exclusive helipad to improve accessibility.
  • Financial support. The Government of India released the first tranche of equity amounting to ₹132 crore in September 2025 against the transfer of 415 acres.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does BIMCGL matter for Bihar and national industrial policy?

  • Regional industrial transformation. BIMCGL aims to accelerate Bihar's industrial development by creating a modern, investment-ready manufacturing hub in eastern India.
  • Employment generation. The project is expected to generate employment opportunities for over one lakh people, supporting local entrepreneurship and MSMEs.
  • Alignment with national vision. BIMCGL contributes to the national goals of Atmanirbhar Bharat and Viksit Bharat 2047 by promoting manufacturing and industrialisation.
  • Institutional framework. The dedicated SPV structure enables coordinated planning, faster decision-making, effective monitoring, and timely resolution of implementation issues.

Back to basics: Industrial Corridors and Special Purpose Vehicles

Industrial corridors are planned zones designed to boost manufacturing and infrastructure development by connecting industrial hubs with transport and logistics networks. Special Purpose Vehicles (SPVs) are dedicated entities created to implement specific projects, enabling focused management, faster decision-making, and better coordination among stakeholders.
