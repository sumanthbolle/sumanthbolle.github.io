India’s Space Economy Projected to Reach USD 40-45 Billion in 7-8 Years

India’s space economy, currently valued at around USD 9 billion, is expected to grow to USD 40-45 billion over the next seven to eight years, driven by increasing private investments and government reforms. This growth highlights the transition from demonstrating space capabilities to building globally competitive commercial space companies, a key topic for UPSC science and technology and economy syllabus.

India’s space economy is set to expand from USD 9 billion to USD 40-45 billion in 7-8 years due to private sector participation and supportive policies.

Why is this in the news?

On October 17, 2026, Union Minister Dr. Jitendra Singh highlighted India’s space economy growth prospects and the role of private investments at a Bloomberg New Economy Forum roundtable in New Delhi.

What is the current status and future projection of India’s space economy?

  • Current value. India’s space economy is currently valued at around USD 9 billion.
  • Future projection. It is expected to reach USD 40-45 billion over the next seven to eight years.
  • Growth drivers. The growth is driven by increasing private investments, faster regulatory approvals, predictable demand, and sustainable business models.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How is private sector participation shaping India’s space economy?

  • Opening to private sector. India’s space sector was opened to private participation in 2019-2020 under Prime Minister Narendra Modi’s leadership.
  • Results in 5-6 years. This reform has produced significant results within five to six years by bringing government institutions and private industry together.
  • Need for demand generation. Generating predictable demand through anchor government customers and proof-of-concept support is essential for private companies to invest and scale.
  • Regulatory and cost factors. Timely regulatory approvals, lower business costs, and stronger supply-chain integration are necessary for sustainable investment.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does India’s space economy matter for development and global competitiveness?

  • Technological to commercial shift. India aims to move beyond demonstrating technological capabilities to building globally competitive commercial space companies.
  • Applications on Earth. Space technology supports infrastructure monitoring, agriculture, disaster management, project clearances, and geotagging development works.
  • International cooperation. India is promoting domestic and international public-private partnerships to integrate into global supply chains while safeguarding national interests.
  • Strategic autonomy. Strengthening indigenous capabilities remains important for national security and strategic autonomy.

Back to basics: Space Economy

The space economy includes all public and private activities related to the manufacturing and provision of space goods and services. It covers satellite communications, Earth observation, launch services, and downstream applications such as agriculture monitoring and disaster management. Growth in the space economy depends on technological capabilities, regulatory frameworks, investment, and market demand.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about India’s space economy: 1. India’s space economy is currently valued at around USD 9 billion and is expected to reach USD 40-45 billion in 7-8 years. 2. The space sector was opened to private participation in 2019-2020, leading to significant growth within 5-6 years.

  1. Both 1 and 2
  2. 1 only
  3. 2 only
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Both statements are correct; a trap would be confusing the timeline of private sector opening or the current valuation.
