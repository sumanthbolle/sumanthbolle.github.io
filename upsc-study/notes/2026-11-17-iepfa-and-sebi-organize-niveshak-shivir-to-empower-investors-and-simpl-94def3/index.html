IEPFA and SEBI Organize 'Niveshak Shivir' to Empower Investors

The Investor Education and Protection Fund Authority (IEPFA) and SEBI are organizing a 'Niveshak Shivir' in New Delhi on 17 November 2026 to help investors resolve grievances related to unclaimed dividends and shares. This initiative simplifies grievance redressal and promotes financial literacy, which is crucial for investor protection and market transparency.

The 'Niveshak Shivir' facilitates direct investor engagement with companies and authorities to recover unclaimed dividends and shares, enhancing financial literacy and grievance redressal.

Why is this in the news?

IEPFA and SEBI announced the 'Niveshak Shivir' event on 16 November 2026 to empower investors and simplify grievance redressal related to unclaimed dividends and shares.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the purpose of the Niveshak Shivir?

  • Investor grievance resolution. The Shivir is designed to help investors and shareholders resolve issues related to unclaimed dividends, shares transferred to the Investor Education and Protection Fund (IEPF), and other claimant grievances.
  • Direct facilitation services. It provides direct facilitation for recovery of unclaimed dividends and shares held for six to seven years, on-the-spot KYC updation, nomination services, and immediate assistance for pending IEPFA claims.
  • Promoting direct engagement. The model promotes intermediary-free engagement between investors, companies, and Registrars and Transfer Agents (RTAs), with dedicated service desks for prompt grievance redressal.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Who is organizing and participating in the Niveshak Shivir?

  • Organizers. The event is organized by the Investor Education and Protection Fund Authority (IEPFA) under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, in collaboration with SEBI and Market Infrastructure Institutions (MIIs).
  • Government participation. Shri Harsh Malhotra, Minister of State for Road Transport and Highways and Ministry of Corporate Affairs, and Dr. Pallavi Jain Govil, Secretary of the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, are invited to the event.
  • Company involvement. For the first time, companies with substantial unclaimed dividend accounts are participating directly to guide rightful claimants.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does the Niveshak Shivir matter for investors and governance?

  • Financial literacy and transparency. The Shivir is part of IEPFA’s nationwide outreach to strengthen financial literacy and enhance transparency in reclaiming unclaimed investments.
  • Simplified grievance redressal. By enabling direct interaction without intermediaries and establishing service desks, it simplifies and speeds up grievance resolution.
  • Investor-centric ecosystem. The initiative supports building a secure, accessible, and investor-centric financial ecosystem across India.

Back to basics: Investor Education and Protection Fund Authority (IEPFA)

IEPFA is a government authority under the Ministry of Corporate Affairs dedicated to promoting investor education, awareness, and protection. It conducts financial literacy initiatives and outreach programs to help investors understand their rights and facilitate recovery of unclaimed dividends and shares. It collaborates with SEBI and other institutions to strengthen investor protection mechanisms.
