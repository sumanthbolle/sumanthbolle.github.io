Reimagining Agricultural Higher Education Through Global Partnerships

Dr. M. L. Jat, Secretary of DARE and DG of ICAR, called for transforming agricultural higher education to address global challenges like climate change and food security. He emphasized international collaboration, industry–academia partnerships, and policy dialogue to prepare future professionals and strengthen agri-food systems.

Agricultural higher education must evolve through global partnerships and integrated approaches to meet emerging global challenges and support sustainable agri-food systems.

Why is this in the news?

On October 22, 2026, Dr. M. L. Jat addressed the World Food Prize Foundation programme virtually, calling for reimagining agricultural higher education through global partnerships.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What changes are proposed in agricultural higher education?

  • Focus on global challenges. Agricultural education should prepare professionals to tackle issues like climate change, natural resource degradation, biodiversity loss, and demand for nutritious food.
  • Shift from production-centric to integrated systems. The approach must move beyond just production to an integrated agri-food systems and value-chain perspective.
  • Industry–academia collaboration. Stronger partnerships between academia and industry are needed to develop industry-ready human resources and involve industry across the agri-food value chain.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How do global partnerships enhance agricultural education?

  • Mutual learning and knowledge exchange. International collaboration enables sharing scientific and educational advancements, benefiting all partners.
  • Connecting Indian agricultural diaspora. Linking diaspora with domestic institutions facilitates international knowledge exchange and enriches education.
  • New institutional partnerships. An MoU with the Council for Agricultural Science and Technology (CAST) aims to foster cooperation in research, capacity building, and knowledge sharing.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does this matter for agricultural policy and research?

  • Role of NAAS in policy dialogue. The National Academy of Agricultural Sciences (NAAS) contributes through policy papers and briefs addressing critical agricultural R&D issues.
  • Collaborative solutions for emerging challenges. Bringing together researchers, policymakers, academia, and industry helps develop effective responses to evolving agri-food system needs.
  • Advancing agriculture in the Global South. Stronger international partnerships support sustainable agricultural research, education, and extension relevant to developing countries.

Back to basics: Agricultural Higher Education and Global Partnerships

Agricultural higher education trains professionals in farming, food production, and related sciences. Global partnerships involve collaboration between institutions across countries to share knowledge, research, and innovations. Such partnerships help address worldwide challenges like climate change and food security by combining expertise and resources.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the recent call to reimagine agricultural higher education: 1. The call emphasized moving from production-centric agriculture to an integrated agri-food systems approach. 2. A Memorandum of Understanding was signed with the Council for Agricultural Science and Technology (CAST) to enhance international cooperation. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (c). A trap could be confusing the specific international partner; the MoU was signed with CAST, not a generic global body.

Mains question (15 marks)

Examine the significance of reimagining agricultural higher education through global partnerships in addressing contemporary challenges in agriculture.

Examine: Discuss how transforming agricultural education via international collaboration can help tackle issues like climate change, food security, and sustainable development.

Intro: Agricultural education is crucial for preparing professionals to address evolving global challenges in food systems.

  • Explain the need to shift from production-centric to integrated agri-food systems education.
  • Discuss the role of global partnerships in knowledge exchange, research collaboration, and capacity building.
  • Highlight the importance of industry–academia collaboration for developing industry-ready human resources.
  • Analyze the impact of policy dialogue and diaspora engagement in strengthening agricultural research and education.

Conclusion: Global partnerships in agricultural education are essential for building resilient, sustainable agri-food systems worldwide.
