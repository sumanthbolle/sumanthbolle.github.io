Mirwaiz Umar Farooq Welcomes Pakistan's Participation in SCO Forum

Mirwaiz Umar Farooq praised Pakistan's acceptance of India's invitation to the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) Cultural Dialogue Forum in New Delhi as a positive step towards improving bilateral relations. This development is significant for understanding recent shifts in India-Pakistan ties and regional diplomacy under the SCO framework.

Mirwaiz Umar Farooq sees Pakistan's participation in the SCO Cultural Dialogue Forum as an opportunity to promote dialogue and improve India-Pakistan relations.

Why is this in the news?

On November 21, 2026, Mirwaiz Umar Farooq welcomed Pakistan's acceptance of India's invitation to the SCO Cultural Dialogue Forum in New Delhi, marking a positive development in bilateral engagement.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What happened with Pakistan's participation in the SCO forum?

  • Pakistan accepted India's invitation. Pakistan sent a minister to attend the SCO Cultural Dialogue Forum in New Delhi, which was invited by the Indian government.
  • Mirwaiz's positive response. Kashmir chief cleric Mirwaiz Umar Farooq called this acceptance a positive step towards increasing engagement and improving relations between India and Pakistan.
  • Context of strained ties. India-Pakistan relations had deteriorated after the Pahalgam attack in 2025, which led to a war between the two countries.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for India-Pakistan relations and regional diplomacy?

  • Opportunity for dialogue. Mirwaiz emphasized that dialogue and sustained engagement are essential for mutual understanding and peaceful relations between the two countries.
  • Multilateral platform significance. Although the SCO forum is multilateral, Pakistan's participation in an event hosted by India creates a favorable environment for bilateral dialogue.
  • Broader regional concerns. Issues like political disputes, climate change, and water management require cooperation, which can be facilitated by such engagements.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the reactions and implications for future engagement?

  • Support from regional leaders. Peoples Democratic Party legislator Muntazir Mehdi and Jammu and Kashmir Deputy Chief Minister Surinder Choudhary welcomed the visit and urged resumption of dialogue.
  • Potential for peace and development. The visit is seen as a step that could help reduce hostility and promote peace and development in the subcontinent.

Back to basics: Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO)

The SCO is a regional multilateral organization that promotes cooperation on political, economic, and security issues among member states. It provides a platform for dialogue and engagement among countries in Eurasia, including India and Pakistan, facilitating regional stability and collaboration.
