Kerala's Strategy to Prevent Child Deaths from Malnutrition and Neglect

Kerala plans a State-wide vulnerability mapping to identify children at risk of malnutrition, neglect, and poor health, followed by regular follow-ups and interdepartmental coordination. This approach is crucial for UPSC aspirants studying social welfare schemes and child health policies.

Kerala is implementing vulnerability mapping and coordinated child protection measures to prevent malnutrition-related child deaths.

Why is this in the news?

The plan follows a tragic child death in Alappuzha district in October 2026 linked to severe malnutrition and neglect, prompting government action.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What triggered Kerala's new child protection measures?

  • Child death incident. A two-year-old boy died in Alappuzha district in October 2026 due to severe malnutrition and acute anaemia, raising concerns about child protection effectiveness.
  • Family background. The family lived an itinerant life, avoiding sustained contact with authorities, and had multiple children with poor health and vaccination status.
  • Government response. The Kerala State Commission for Protection of Child Rights registered a suo motu case and the child welfare committee took charge of the surviving children.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How is Kerala planning to prevent similar child deaths?

  • Vulnerability mapping. A State-wide vulnerability mapping will identify children living in conditions that risk their health, education, and safety.
  • Regular follow-ups. After identifying vulnerable children, authorities will conduct regular follow-ups to monitor their well-being.
  • Interdepartmental coordination. Coordination between departments will be strengthened with regular meetings of child protection and welfare committees from district to village levels.
  • Accountability of officers. Women and Child Development Officers in districts will be fully responsible for ensuring children's safety and rehabilitation.
  • Social investigation and care plans. Social investigation reports must be submitted timely to child welfare committees, and individual care plans will be prepared for children in care institutions.
  • Post-rehabilitation monitoring. Even after rehabilitation, anganwadi workers, ICDS supervisors, and psychosocial counsellors will continue monitoring children's nutrition and growth.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for social welfare and child health policies?

  • Systemic challenges. The incident highlights systemic problems such as parental resistance and the need for persistent implementation beyond issuing orders.
  • Custodianship mindset. The government emphasizes that departments must act as custodians of every child, ensuring safety even amid resistance.
  • Preventing malnutrition deaths. Kerala's approach aims to prevent malnutrition-related deaths through early identification, continuous monitoring, and coordinated interventions.

Back to basics: Child Vulnerability Mapping and Protection

Child vulnerability mapping is a process to identify children at risk due to factors like malnutrition, neglect, or unsafe living conditions. It helps authorities target interventions effectively. Child welfare committees and officers coordinate to ensure protection, rehabilitation, and monitoring of vulnerable children, involving social investigations and care plans to prevent harm and promote well-being.

Answer and trap
