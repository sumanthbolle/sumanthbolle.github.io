Digi Yatra Trials for International Passengers Begin at Seven Airports

The Ministry of Civil Aviation started Digi Yatra trials for international passengers at seven Indian airports from November to December 2026. This initiative tests digital boarding using e-passports and facial recognition, aiming for a wider rollout in 2027.

Digi Yatra's international passenger trials at seven airports mark a step towards seamless, contactless air travel using e-passport-based digital credentials.

Why is this in the news?

The Ministry of Civil Aviation announced the start of Digi Yatra international passenger trials at seven airports in November 2026 to collect feedback for a wider rollout in 2027.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the Digi Yatra international passenger trial?

  • Trial scope. Digi Yatra trials for international passengers are being conducted at Delhi, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Cochin, Mumbai, Chennai and Amritsar airports from November to December 2026.
  • Eligible users. The trials allow Indian nationals holding e-passports to use Digi Yatra on select international flights operated by Air India and IndiGo.
  • Usage points. Passengers can use Digi Yatra at terminal entry points, Security Hold Areas, and boarding gates where infrastructure is available.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

How does Digi Yatra work for international passengers?

  • Credential creation. Eligible passengers create an e-passport-based Verifiable Credential by scanning the Machine Readable Zone and embedded chip using NFC, capturing a selfie, and verifying details via the Digi Yatra app.
  • Credential storage. The Verifiable Credential is stored in the passenger's Digi Yatra wallet on their smartphone and used at enabled airport touchpoints.
  • Complementary process. Digi Yatra complements existing travel procedures; passengers must still carry travel documents and complete airline and immigration checks.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does Digi Yatra matter for India’s aviation sector?

  • Digital adoption. Digi Yatra represents a step forward in adopting digital technology to improve airport operations and passenger experience.
  • User base and expansion. The platform already has over 26.5 million users and has facilitated more than 110 million journeys across 39 domestic airports, with plans to cover 25 more by 2027.
  • Government vision. The initiative aligns with the Government of India's Digital India and Ease of Flying visions to make air travel seamless and technology-driven.

Back to basics: Digital Boarding and E-passports

Digital boarding systems use technology such as facial recognition and mobile applications to enable contactless passenger processing at airports. E-passports contain embedded electronic chips storing biometric and personal data, facilitating secure and faster identity verification during travel. Digi Yatra leverages these technologies to streamline airport procedures while complementing existing travel document checks.

Consider the following statements about Digi Yatra international passenger trials: 1. The trials are being conducted at seven airports including Delhi and Mumbai. 2. Eligible passengers must hold Aadhaar cards to use Digi Yatra for international flights.

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because Digi Yatra trials require e-passports, not Aadhaar cards, for international passengers.
