RBI Auctions Treasury Bills and Conducts Variable Rate Reverse Repo

On November 18, 2026, the Reserve Bank of India conducted auctions for 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day Treasury Bills and held an Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction. These operations influence liquidity management and short-term interest rates, important for understanding monetary policy and financial markets.

RBI's treasury bill auctions and variable rate reverse repo operations regulate liquidity and short-term interest rates in the economy.

Why is this in the news?

RBI conducted Treasury Bill auctions and an Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction on November 18, 2026, impacting liquidity and money market conditions.

What are the Treasury Bill auctions conducted by RBI?

  • Auction details. RBI auctioned 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day Treasury Bills with notified amounts of ₹8,000 crore, ₹8,000 crore, and ₹7,000 crore respectively.
  • Bids and acceptance. Competitive bids received were ₹25,216.18 crore (91-day), ₹26,523.5 crore (182-day), and ₹25,170.5 crore (364-day), with accepted amounts of ₹7,600 crore, ₹7,600 crore, and ₹6,650 crore respectively.
  • Yield and price. Cut-off yields were 5.8602% (91-day), 6.5300% (182-day), and 6.6300% (364-day), with corresponding cut-off prices of ₹98.5600, ₹96.8466, and ₹93.7982.

What is the Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction?

  • Operation purpose. The Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo (VRRR) auction is a tool for RBI to absorb excess liquidity from banks by borrowing funds overnight at a variable rate.
  • Auction results. On November 18, 2026, RBI notified ₹50,000 crore for the VRRR auction, received offers worth ₹9,388 crore, and accepted the full ₹9,388 crore at a cut-off rate of 5.49%.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

Why do these operations matter for monetary policy?

  • Liquidity management. Treasury Bill auctions help the government raise short-term funds, while VRRR auctions help RBI manage liquidity by absorbing surplus cash from banks.
  • Interest rate signaling. The yields on Treasury Bills and the cut-off rate in VRRR auctions influence short-term interest rates, affecting borrowing costs and monetary conditions.
  • Monetary policy implementation. These instruments are part of RBI's toolkit to maintain price stability and control inflation by regulating money supply and liquidity.

Back to basics: Monetary Policy Instruments

Monetary policy uses tools like Treasury Bills and reverse repo operations to regulate liquidity and interest rates in the economy. Treasury Bills are short-term government securities issued to raise funds. Reverse repo operations involve the central bank borrowing money from banks, thereby absorbing excess liquidity and influencing short-term interest rates.

Mains question (10 marks)

Explain how Treasury Bill auctions and Variable Rate Reverse Repo operations serve as tools for monetary policy implementation in India.

Explain: Explain the mechanism and significance of Treasury Bill auctions and VRRR operations in monetary policy.

Intro: Monetary policy uses various instruments to regulate liquidity and interest rates in the economy.

  • Treasury Bill auctions help the government raise short-term funds and influence short-term interest rates through yield determination.
  • Variable Rate Reverse Repo operations allow RBI to absorb excess liquidity from banks, controlling money supply and stabilizing overnight rates.
  • Together, these tools help RBI maintain price stability and support economic growth by managing liquidity and monetary conditions.

Conclusion: Thus, Treasury Bill auctions and VRRR operations are key instruments for effective monetary policy implementation in India.
