Cabinet approves schemes to boost sports goods and container manufacturing

The Union Cabinet approved the Khelo India Sports Goods Manufacturing Scheme and the Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme to strengthen India's manufacturing ecosystem, reduce import dependence, and enhance global competitiveness. These initiatives align with Make in India and aim to generate employment, attract investment, and support exports.

The government approved two major schemes to promote domestic manufacturing of sports goods and shipping containers, enhancing India's industrial and export capabilities.

Why is this in the news?

On 28 October 2026, the Union Cabinet chaired by the Prime Minister approved the Khelo India Sports Goods Manufacturing Scheme and the Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme to promote domestic manufacturing and global competitiveness.

What is the Khelo India Sports Goods Manufacturing Scheme?

  • Scheme objective. The scheme aims to build a globally competitive sports goods manufacturing ecosystem by promoting investment, innovation, exports, and strengthening India's position in global supply chains.
  • Financial outlay and duration. It has a total outlay of Rs.1,856.67 crore for the period 2026 to 2033-34.
  • Key components. The scheme includes development of Sports Goods Manufacturing Zones, investment support, turnover linked incentives, MSME cluster strengthening, quality certification, research and innovation, skill development, and market expansion.
  • Alignment with policies. It aligns with Khelo Bharat Niti 2025, Make in India, and Atmanirbhar Bharat to attract investment, create jobs, and enhance manufacturing capabilities.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What does the Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme entail?

  • Scheme objective. CMPS aims to establish a globally competitive container manufacturing ecosystem, reduce import dependence, and strengthen supply-chain resilience.
  • Financial outlay and timeline. The scheme has a budgetary outlay of Rs.10,000 crore up to FY 2039-40.
  • Major components. It includes Interest Subvention Assistance (Rs.859 crore), Operational Expenditure Assistance (Rs.8,891 crore), and Testing and Certification Assistance (Rs.150 crore).
  • Eligibility criteria. Greenfield projects require at least 1,00,000 TEUs annual capacity; brownfield expansions must add 25,000 TEUs and comply with global standards.
  • Expected impact. The scheme aims to increase domestic container manufacturing capacity ten-fold to 7.5 lakh TEUs, generate 3,000 direct and over 50,000 indirect jobs, and support ancillary industries.

[2] Press Information Bureau[3] Press Information Bureau

How do these schemes impact India's economy and manufacturing sector?

  • Employment generation. Both schemes are expected to create significant employment opportunities across manufacturing and allied sectors.
  • Boost to Make in India. They support Make in India by promoting domestic manufacturing, reducing import dependence, and enhancing export potential.
  • Supply chain resilience. The Container scheme strengthens maritime and logistics supply chains, while the sports goods scheme enhances global supply chain integration.
  • Innovation and skill development. Emphasis on research, innovation, and skill development will improve product quality and competitiveness.

Back to basics: Industrial policy and manufacturing schemes

Industrial policies are government initiatives aimed at promoting manufacturing and economic growth by providing financial support, infrastructure, and regulatory facilitation. Schemes often focus on attracting investment, enhancing technology adoption, developing skilled workforce, and improving competitiveness to reduce import dependence and boost exports.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the Container Manufacturing Promotion Scheme (CMPS): 1. It provides Interest Subvention Assistance of 3% per annum on eligible term loans for container manufacturers. 2. The scheme aims to increase annual domestic container manufacturing capacity to 7.5 lakh TEUs. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (c). Statement 1 is correct as CMPS provides 3% interest subvention; statement 2 is also correct as the scheme targets increasing capacity to 7.5 lakh TEUs.
