RBI Conducts Treasury Bills and Overnight Variable Rate Repo Auctions

On October 21, 2026, the Reserve Bank of India conducted auctions for treasury bills and an overnight Variable Rate Repo (VRR). These auctions are important monetary policy tools used by the RBI to manage liquidity and influence short-term interest rates.

The RBI uses treasury bills and VRR auctions to regulate liquidity and maintain stability in the financial system.

Why is this in the news?

The Reserve Bank of India held treasury bills and overnight Variable Rate Repo auctions on October 21, 2026, as part of its liquidity management operations.

[1] Reserve Bank of India[2] Reserve Bank of India[3] Reserve Bank of India

What are the results of the Overnight Variable Rate Repo auction?

  • Auction details. The RBI conducted a 1-day VRR auction with a notified amount of ₹50,000 crore and received bids worth ₹93,241 crore.
  • Amount allotted and rates. The RBI allotted ₹50,007 crore at a cut-off and weighted average rate of 5.52%, with a partial allotment percentage of 87.99% at the cut-off rate.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

What happened in the Treasury Bills auction?

  • Auction notified amounts. The RBI notified amounts of ₹8,000 crore for 91-day and 182-day T-Bills, and ₹7,000 crore for 364-day T-Bills.
  • Bids received and acceptance. Competitive bids exceeded notified amounts but no bids were accepted, resulting in no allotment for any maturity period.

[2] Reserve Bank of India[3] Reserve Bank of India

Why do these auctions matter for monetary policy?

  • Liquidity management tool. The VRR auction helps the RBI absorb excess liquidity from banks by borrowing funds at a variable rate, influencing short-term interest rates.
  • Market signaling. Treasury bills auctions reflect government borrowing and market demand for short-term securities, impacting money market conditions.

Back to basics: Monetary Policy Instruments

Monetary policy instruments are tools used by central banks like the RBI to regulate money supply and interest rates to maintain economic stability. Treasury bills are short-term government securities used to manage liquidity and government borrowing. The Variable Rate Repo (VRR) auction is a liquidity absorption mechanism where the RBI borrows funds from banks at a variable interest rate to control excess liquidity in the banking system.

Mains question (10 marks)

Explain the role of Treasury Bills and Variable Rate Repo auctions in the Reserve Bank of India's monetary policy framework.

Explain: Explain how Treasury Bills and VRR auctions function as monetary policy tools and their impact on liquidity management.

Intro: Monetary policy uses various instruments to regulate liquidity and interest rates in the economy.

  • Treasury Bills are short-term government securities issued to manage government borrowing and liquidity in the money market.
  • Variable Rate Repo auctions allow the RBI to absorb excess liquidity from banks by borrowing funds at variable interest rates.
  • These auctions influence short-term interest rates and help maintain monetary stability by controlling money supply.
  • The RBI's auction results reflect market demand and provide signals for monetary policy adjustments.

Conclusion: Thus, Treasury Bills and VRR auctions are key tools for the RBI to implement its monetary policy and manage liquidity effectively.
