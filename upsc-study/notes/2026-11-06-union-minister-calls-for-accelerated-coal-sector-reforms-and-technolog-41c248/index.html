Union Minister Advocates Coal Sector Reforms and Tech-Driven Mining for Energy Security

At the 4th Conference of Mining Ministers in November 2026, Union Minister Shri G. Kishan Reddy emphasized accelerating coal gasification, adopting AI and digital technologies, and enhancing Centre-State collaboration to strengthen India's coal sector and energy security. These reforms aim to improve transparency, efficiency, and sustainability in mining, which are key for India's economic growth and reducing import dependence.

Accelerated coal sector reforms and technology-driven mining are essential to unlock India's coal potential, ensure energy security, and promote sustainable economic development.

Why is this in the news?

The 4th Conference of Mining Ministers held on 5–6 November 2026 in Goa triggered renewed calls by the Union Minister of Coal for reforms and technology adoption in coal mining to boost India's energy security.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What reforms did the Union Minister call for in the coal sector?

  • Accelerate coal gasification. The Minister stressed the need to speed up coal gasification to optimally use India's abundant coal, unlocking economic value, promoting industrial diversification, generating employment, and strengthening long-term energy security.
  • Enhance technology use. He urged States to adopt Artificial Intelligence, digitalisation, and advanced technologies in exploration, mining operations, and mineral recovery to improve productivity, efficiency, and sustainability.
  • Promote private sector participation. Reforms like Commercial Coal Mining and the revised SHAKTI framework aim to simplify processes, increase private investment, and accelerate operationalisation of coal mines.
  • Strengthen Centre-State collaboration. The Minister called for stronger coordination between the Centre and States to unlock mineral and coal potential, ensuring sustainable and inclusive growth aligned with the vision of Viksit Bharat 2047.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why do these coal sector reforms matter for India’s energy security and economy?

  • Energy self-reliance. Domestic coal production surpassed one billion tonnes for the second consecutive year, reducing dependence on imported coal and enhancing energy security.
  • Economic growth and employment. Reforms have attracted investments, generated employment, strengthened State revenues, and promoted development in mining-affected regions.
  • Strategic mineral ecosystem. Developing domestic capabilities in exploration, mining, processing, refining, and recycling of critical minerals reduces import dependence and strengthens strategic resilience.
  • Sustainable mining practices. Emphasis on environmental protection, mine rehabilitation, and welfare of mining-affected communities ensures mining development is responsible and inclusive.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How are technology and innovation shaping the coal and mining sectors?

  • AI and digitalisation. Artificial Intelligence and digital tools are being promoted to enhance exploration accuracy, operational efficiency, and mineral recovery.
  • Modern mining technologies. Safe and modern mining technologies, along with innovations in coal utilisation like First Mile Connectivity, are being encouraged to increase production sustainably.
  • Value addition and industrial diversification. Coal gasification and other technological advancements offer opportunities for value addition, industrial diversification, and employment generation.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What role does cooperative federalism play in coal sector reforms?

  • Centre-State coordination. Stronger collaboration is essential to expedite mine auctions, operationalise mines, expand exploration, and implement reforms effectively.
  • Sharing best practices. States are encouraged to share experiences and address challenges collectively to promote a competitive and sustainable mining ecosystem.
  • Inclusive growth focus. Cooperative efforts aim to ensure that mining benefits reach local communities through employment, revenue generation, and environmental safeguards.

Back to basics: Coal sector reforms and energy security

Coal sector reforms involve changes in policies and practices to improve transparency, efficiency, and investment attractiveness in coal mining. Energy security means ensuring reliable and sufficient energy supply for the country’s needs. Technology-driven mining uses advanced tools like AI and digitalisation to enhance productivity and sustainability. Cooperative federalism refers to collaboration between the Centre and States to achieve common goals in resource management.
