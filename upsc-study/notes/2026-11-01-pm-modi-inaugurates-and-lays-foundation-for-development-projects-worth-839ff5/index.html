PM Modi inaugurates and lays foundation for ₹14,000 crore projects in Gujarat

On October 30, 2026, PM Narendra Modi inaugurated and laid the foundation stones for development projects worth over ₹14,000 crore in Banaskantha, Gujarat, covering agriculture, animal husbandry, infrastructure, and social welfare. These projects highlight government efforts to boost farmer incomes, rural prosperity, and regional connectivity, relevant for UPSC topics on infrastructure and economic development.

PM Modi launched multiple development initiatives worth ₹14,000 crore in Gujarat focusing on agriculture, animal husbandry, infrastructure, and social welfare to enhance rural incomes and connectivity.

What were the key projects inaugurated and laid foundation for?

  • Agriculture and farmer welfare. Projects included disbursal of fresh PM Kisan Samman Nidhi installments to crores of farmers, cluster development for fruit and vegetable cultivation, and support for new and rejuvenated orchards with subsidies.
  • Animal husbandry and dairy. PM inaugurated the Bharat Bovine Breeding and Research Centre (BBBRC) at Bhildi, Banaskantha, a 127-acre ₹325 crore facility focusing on indigenous cattle breeds and advanced breeding techniques like IVF to improve milk production.
  • Infrastructure and connectivity. Foundation stones were laid for Ahmedabad Metro Phase 2A and 2B, and the Ahmedabad-Mehsana highway was approved for expansion from four to eight lanes at a cost of around ₹2,600 crore.
  • Social welfare. Over 4,000 modern child-friendly Anganwadi centres were inaugurated, benefiting about 4 lakh children and pregnant women.

[2] Press Information Bureau[3] The Hindu · Secondary

How do these projects impact farmers and rural economy?

  • Financial support to farmers. More than ₹18,000 crore has been transferred to about 10 crore farmers under PM Kisan Samman Nidhi, with over ₹1,000 crore reaching 50 lakh farmers in Gujarat alone.
  • Cost savings and income increase. Government policies have delivered or saved farmers over ₹50 lakh crore through schemes like PM-Kisan, fertilizer subsidies costing ₹16 lakh crore, MSP payments exceeding ₹24 lakh crore, crop insurance compensations over ₹2 lakh crore, and distribution of nearly 12 lakh solar pumps reducing irrigation costs.
  • Animal husbandry benefits. Free vaccination campaigns administering about 150 crore doses have protected livestock of over 8 crore farmers, increasing milk production from under 5 litres to nearly 8 litres per day on average, making India the world's largest milk producer.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

What is the significance of the Bharat Bovine Breeding and Research Centre?

  • Focus on indigenous breeds. The centre conserves and improves indigenous cow breeds like Kankrej, Gir, Sahiwal, and buffalo breeds like Mehsana, Murrah, and Jaffarabadi, being the first facility for Kankrej breed.
  • Advanced breeding techniques. It uses IVF and embryo transfer to produce 25,000 high-quality embryos annually, helping farmers pass superior traits to future generations and increase milk yields.
  • Support for livestock farmers. The centre provides affordable high-quality semen, embryos, and breeding services to livestock farmers, aiming to increase incomes and reduce maintenance costs amid fodder scarcity.

[2] Press Information Bureau[3] The Hindu · Secondary

What other development initiatives were highlighted?

  • Fisheries and beekeeping. India's seafood exports have more than doubled, and honey production has doubled over 12 years, with India becoming the world's second-largest exporter of natural honey, supported by women's self-help groups.
  • Cooperative movement. Banas Dairy's cooperative model is expanding beyond Gujarat, including to Kashi, supporting dairy farmers and rural prosperity.
  • Resource conservation. PM Modi emphasized saving water and electricity, citing the transformation of Banaskantha through Narmada waters and drip irrigation technologies.

Back to basics: Public Infrastructure Development

Public infrastructure development involves government-led projects to build or upgrade facilities such as roads, metros, agricultural support systems, and social welfare centres. These projects aim to improve economic activity, connectivity, and quality of life, especially in rural and semi-urban areas. Government schemes often integrate technology and scientific methods to enhance productivity and sustainability in sectors like agriculture and animal husbandry.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the development projects inaugurated by PM Modi in Banaskantha, Gujarat: 1. The Bharat Bovine Breeding and Research Centre focuses on improving indigenous cattle breeds using advanced techniques like IVF. 2. The Ahmedabad-Mehsana highway expansion project will widen the road from four lanes to eight lanes at a cost of around ₹2,600 crore. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (c). Statement 1 is correct as the BBBRC uses IVF and embryo transfer for indigenous breeds. Statement 2 is also correct with the highway expansion costing around ₹2,600 crore. A trap would be confusing the cost or the lane expansion details.
