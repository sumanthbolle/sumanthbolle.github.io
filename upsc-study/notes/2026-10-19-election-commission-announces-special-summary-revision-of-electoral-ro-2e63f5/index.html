Election Commission Reviews Special Summary Revision of Electoral Rolls

The Election Commission conducted a review of the ongoing special summary revision of electoral rolls in Visakhapatnam and Alluri Sitharama Raju districts. This process ensures accurate voter registration and deletion, crucial for free and fair elections.

The Election Commission's special summary revision updates electoral rolls by verifying, adding, or deleting voter entries to maintain electoral integrity.

Why is this in the news?

On October 8, 2026, electoral authorities reviewed the special summary revision process in Visakhapatnam and Alluri Sitharama Raju districts to ensure proper verification and deletion of voter entries.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the special summary revision of electoral rolls?

  • Purpose of revision. The special summary revision updates the electoral rolls by verifying existing entries, adding eligible voters, and deleting ineligible ones to maintain accurate voter lists.
  • Verification process. Electoral officials conduct field-level verification, especially for rejected or doubtful applications, ensuring data in the ECINet matches field information.
  • Deletion criteria. Voters are deleted only after recording clear reasons and verifying cases such as absent, shifted, duplicate, or deceased entries.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How was the revision process reviewed in Visakhapatnam and ASR districts?

  • Review directives. Roll Observer S. Bhargavi directed officials to conduct thorough field verification and special scrutiny in areas with large additions or deletions.
  • Verification notices issued. In Visakhapatnam, 2,29,924 notices were issued for entries under absent, shifted, duplicate, and deceased categories, with verification following prescribed procedures.
  • ASR district progress. In Alluri Sitharama Raju district, 4,088 notices were issued, and 133 persons were found ineligible after verification; door-to-door verification by Booth Level Officers began from October 8.
  • Final publication. The final electoral roll for ASR district was scheduled for publication on October 16, 2026.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for elections and democratic participation?

  • Ensuring voter eligibility. Accurate rolls prevent ineligible voters from participating and ensure all eligible voters are included, strengthening electoral integrity.
  • Preventing electoral fraud. Field verification and scrutiny of mismatches reduce chances of duplicate or fraudulent entries.
  • Continuous updation. Eligible voters left out can apply through Form-6, and their names can be included after verification, supporting inclusive participation.

Back to basics: Electoral Roll Revision

Electoral rolls are official lists of eligible voters maintained by the Election Commission. Periodic revision ensures the inclusion of new voters, removal of ineligible or deceased voters, and correction of errors. This process involves verification of voter details through field checks and documentation, maintaining the credibility and accuracy of voter lists essential for conducting free and fair elections.

  • The process prevents electoral fraud by identifying duplicates and deceased voters.

  • Accurate rolls enhance the credibility of elections and promote inclusive democratic participation.

Conclusion: Thus, the special summary revision strengthens the democratic process by ensuring free, fair, and inclusive elections.
