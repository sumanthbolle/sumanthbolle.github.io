UN Experts Criticize India’s Police Actions Against Protesters

UN human rights experts have stated that police actions during protests in Delhi violate India’s core international legal obligations. This matters for UPSC aspirants as it highlights India’s commitments under international law and the challenges in balancing security with fundamental rights.

UN human rights experts say India’s police violated international legal obligations by using excessive force and arbitrary detentions against peaceful protesters.

Why is this in the news?

On October 20, 2026, UN human rights experts issued a joint statement condemning police actions against protesters in Delhi as violations of India’s international legal obligations.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What did the UN human rights experts say about police actions?

  • Violation of legal obligations. The experts stated that police actions during protests in Delhi violated India’s core international legal obligations, including rights to peaceful assembly, expression, liberty, and security of the person.
  • Excessive force and arbitrary detentions. They highlighted the use of excessive force, arbitrary detentions of over 7,000 youths, journalists, and human rights defenders, and reported sexual violence during the crackdown.
  • Conditions for restricting assembly. The experts emphasized that any restrictions on peaceful assembly must be legal, pursue a legitimate aim, and be necessary and proportionate.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for India’s international obligations?

  • Core rights under international law. India is bound by international human rights laws guaranteeing freedoms of assembly, expression, and security, which the experts say were violated.
  • Broader pattern of concern. The crackdown reflects a troubling pattern of undue restrictions on peaceful assembly, enforced disappearances, and targeting of student activists.
  • Call for investigations and reparations. The experts called for independent, impartial investigations into law enforcement actions and reparations for affected individuals.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What should be remembered for UPSC exams?

  • UN experts’ role. UN human rights experts monitor compliance with international legal obligations and can publicly criticize member states.
  • India’s obligations. India must ensure that restrictions on peaceful protests are legal, necessary, and proportionate to comply with international law.
  • Impact on governance and diplomacy. Such critiques affect India’s global image and raise questions about balancing security and fundamental rights.

Back to basics: International Human Rights Obligations

Countries that are members of the United Nations agree to respect certain fundamental human rights such as freedom of expression, peaceful assembly, and security of persons. These rights are protected under international treaties and conventions. When states restrict these rights, such restrictions must be lawful, necessary, and proportionate to a legitimate aim. UN human rights experts monitor and report on compliance with these obligations.
