India implications

  • Gender-biased sex selection undermines gender equality, the dignity of the girl child and balanced social development.
  • Effective implementation requires cooperative federalism: States and UTs must inspect registered facilities and act on violations, while the Centre supports coordination, digital processes and capacity building.
  • Medical professionals, regulatory authorities and communities are all relevant stakeholders; enforcement alone may be insufficient without education and attitude change.

Way forward

  • Strengthen regular, risk-based inspections and prompt, legally sound follow-up by State and UT authorities.
  • Build the capacity of Appropriate Authorities and engage medical professionals through continuing sensitisation and clear compliance processes.
  • Use digital systems for traceability, data coordination and monitoring with privacy, due process and accountability safeguards.
  • Share effective State-level practices and improve Centre-State institutional coordination.
