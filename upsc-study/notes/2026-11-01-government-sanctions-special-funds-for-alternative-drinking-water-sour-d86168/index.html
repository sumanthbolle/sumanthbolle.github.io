Government sanctions ₹120 crore for alternative drinking water amid El Nino

The Telangana government approved ₹120 crore for alternative drinking water arrangements linked to the Singur reservoir to address potential scarcity caused by El Nino. This move is crucial for UPSC aspirants to understand government response to climate-induced water stress and disaster management.

Telangana sanctioned ₹120 crore for alternative drinking water sources to mitigate El Nino-related scarcity.

Why is this in the news?

On October 31, 2026, Telangana's government sanctioned special funds to ensure uninterrupted drinking water supply amid concerns of scarcity due to El Nino conditions.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What funds were sanctioned for alternative drinking water?

  • Special fund amount. The Telangana government approved ₹120 crore for alternative drinking water arrangements linked to the Singur reservoir to serve Sangareddy, Medak, Kamareddy, and Nizamabad districts.
  • Additional district funds. ₹1 crore each was sanctioned for other districts to meet immediate drinking water needs.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What measures are being taken to ensure water supply continuity?

  • Mapping vulnerable areas. Officials were directed to map villages where Mission Bhagiratha water supply might be disrupted and where groundwater levels have depleted.
  • Alternative water sources. Local water sources such as hand pumps, single-phase and three-phase water supply schemes, and open wells are being utilized.
  • Water transportation. In scarcity-hit habitations, hiring private borewells and arranging water tankers are being explored.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How is water conservation and recharge being promoted?

  • Awareness campaigns. Awareness campaigns on judicious water use and El Nino impact are being conducted at the village level.
  • Groundwater recharge. Steps for groundwater recharge, especially in Hyderabad, Medchal, Khammam, and Yadadri-Bhuvanagiri, are being implemented, including functional soak pits and magic pits near drinking water sources.
  • Completion timeline. All ongoing recharge works are to be completed within two days as per government orders.

Back to basics: El Nino and water scarcity

El Nino is a climatic phenomenon characterized by the warming of the central and eastern tropical Pacific Ocean, which disrupts normal weather patterns. It often causes reduced monsoon rainfall in India, leading to drought and water scarcity. Governments respond by planning alternative water sources and conservation measures to mitigate the impact on drinking water supply.
