SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum in Delhi: China, Saudi, Iran Confirm; Pakistan's Role Uncertain

The inaugural SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum will be held in New Delhi from November 20 to 22, 2026, with 15 countries including China, Saudi Arabia, and Iran confirming participation. Pakistan's attendance remains uncertain amid tensions over the Indus Waters Treaty and recent military hostilities.

The SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum aims to promote shared cultural heritage among member states, but Pakistan's participation is uncertain due to ongoing Indus water disputes.

What is the SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum and who is participating?

  • Forum purpose. The forum aims to strengthen people-to-people ties and cultural engagement across SCO member states by showcasing shared heritage, art, literature, and traditions of ancient civilisations.
  • Confirmed participants. Fifteen countries including China, Saudi Arabia, Iran, Turkiye, Kazakhstan, Tajikistan, Uzbekistan, Belarus, Azerbaijan, Armenia, Bahrain, Nepal, and Sri Lanka have confirmed participation.
  • Event details. The inaugural edition will be held from November 20 to 22, 2026, inaugurated by Prime Minister Narendra Modi, with sessions on civilisational expression, art, architecture, language, literature, and manuscripts.

[1] The Indian Express · Secondary

Why is Pakistan's participation uncertain despite an invitation?

  • Invitation extended. India invited Pakistan’s National Heritage and Culture Minister Aurangzeb Khan Khichi, marking the first such ministerial invitation since military hostilities after the 2025 Pahalgam terror attack.
  • Pakistan's nominee. Pakistan nominated its Federal Minister for Climate Change and Environmental Coordination, Musadik Masood Malik, a vocal critic of India’s decision to place the Indus Waters Treaty in abeyance.
  • Indus water tensions. The uncertainty is linked to ongoing tensions over the Indus Waters Treaty, with Pakistan asserting claims on the Indus and highlighting its own Indus Valley civilisation heritage.

[1] The Indian Express · Secondary[2] The Hindu · Secondary

How does the forum relate to India-Pakistan relations and regional diplomacy?

  • Shared civilisational past. India’s Culture Minister Gajendra Shekhawat emphasized the shared civilisational and cultural history between India and Pakistan, noting their evolution together before separation less than 100 years ago.
  • Diplomatic aims. The forum is intended to move beyond geopolitical and strategic issues to build cultural ties that could improve future bilateral relations and communication.
  • Current challenges. Despite hopes for Pakistan’s participation, India’s Ministry of External Affairs indicated adherence to national policy concerning Pakistan due to security concerns following the Pahalgam attack.

Back to basics: Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO)

The SCO is a Eurasian political, economic, and security alliance that promotes cooperation among member states. It includes countries from Central and South Asia and aims to enhance regional stability, economic development, and cultural exchange. The Civilisational Dialogue Forum is a new initiative under SCO to foster cultural ties beyond geopolitical interests.
