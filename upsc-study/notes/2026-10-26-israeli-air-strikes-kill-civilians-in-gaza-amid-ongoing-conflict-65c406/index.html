Israeli Air Strikes Kill Civilians in Gaza Amid Ongoing Conflict

On October 25, 2026, Israeli air strikes targeted Gaza City and other areas in the Gaza Strip, killing several civilians including university staff and students. This incident highlights the continuing violence despite a truce and raises concerns about attacks on education and civilian infrastructure.

What happened in the Israeli air strikes on Gaza?

  • Casualties and targets. Israeli air strikes killed three people at the Islamic University in Gaza City, including administrative staff, and wounded eight students, according to hospital and university sources.
  • Other fatalities. Four additional people were killed in air strikes in other parts of Gaza, including Khan Yunis, Deir al Balah, and northern Gaza.

Why does this matter for the ongoing conflict and education?

  • Damage to education sector. Gaza's Education Ministry stated that 95 percent of academic institution buildings have been damaged since the war began, with recent attacks killing 10 students and educators in six days.
  • Impact on civilians. The strikes on educational institutions and civilians highlight the humanitarian toll and raise concerns about the protection of non-combatants during the conflict.

What is the status of the conflict despite the truce?

  • Truce limitations. Although a truce has been in effect in Gaza for over a year, it has not fully stopped violence, as demonstrated by these recent air strikes.

Back to basics: Conflict and Humanitarian Impact

Conflicts often involve attacks on civilian areas, which can include educational institutions, leading to loss of life and damage to infrastructure. Truces or ceasefires may reduce but not always stop violence. Protection of civilians and education during conflicts is a key concern in international humanitarian law.
