Why in news

On 24 October 2026, the Ministry of Railways dedicated the Satna-Devendranagar and Rewa-Churhat rail sections in Madhya Pradesh and flagged off a Devendranagar-Churhat passenger service. The event brings the Vindhya and Bundelkhand regions closer and is presented as particularly beneficial for Sidhi district.
