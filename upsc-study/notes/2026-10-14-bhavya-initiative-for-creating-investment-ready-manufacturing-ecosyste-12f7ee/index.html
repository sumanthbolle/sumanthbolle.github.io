BHAVYA Initiative to Develop 100 Plug-and-Play Industrial Parks

The Government of India launched the Bharat Audyogik Vikas Yojna (BHAVYA), a ₹33,660 crore scheme to create 100 plug-and-play industrial parks nationwide, aiming to boost manufacturing capacity and ease of doing business. This initiative is crucial for UPSC aspirants to understand evolving industrial policies and India's manufacturing growth strategy.

BHAVYA is a ₹33,660 crore government scheme to develop 100 plug-and-play industrial parks to accelerate manufacturing growth and investment in India.

Why is this in the news?

The Cabinet approved BHAVYA in March 2026 and it was notified on 10 April 2026 as a ₹33,660 crore initiative to develop 100 plug-and-play industrial parks across India.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the BHAVYA initiative and its objectives?

  • Scheme overview. BHAVYA is a ₹33,660 crore initiative to develop 100 plug-and-play industrial parks across India, providing modern infrastructure, reliable services, and faster approvals to reduce setup time for new industries.
  • Key objectives. The scheme aims to attract investment, support MSMEs, strengthen supply chains, create jobs, and drive industrial growth across regions.
  • Implementation partners. BHAVYA is implemented in partnership with States and private developers, emphasizing ease of doing business through streamlined approvals and single-window systems.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How has India's manufacturing policy evolved to BHAVYA?

  • Pre-2020 phase. The focus was on industrial estates and Special Economic Zones (SEZs) providing land, infrastructure, and fiscal incentives; by February 2026, India had 370 notified SEZs attracting ₹7.82 lakh crore investment and generating over 31.7 lakh jobs.
  • National Industrial Corridor Development Programme. NICDP expanded from land provision to integrated manufacturing zones with utilities, logistics, and urban services, creating smart industrial cities like Dholera SIR.
  • Plug-and-play model. BHAVYA represents the nationwide rollout of plug-and-play parks, building on sector-specific parks and cluster models introduced in Budget 2026-27.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the features and benefits of plug-and-play industrial parks under BHAVYA?

  • Infrastructure components. Financial assistance up to ₹1 crore per acre supports internal roads, power, water, drainage, effluent treatment, ICT-enabled administration, and underground utility corridors for maintenance.
  • Facilities for industries and workers. Parks include ready-built factory sheds, built-to-suit units, testing facilities, warehousing, worker housing, healthcare, skill development centers, and social amenities.
  • Connectivity and integration. Up to 25% of funding supports external infrastructure like roads, rail, and last-mile logistics to ensure seamless integration with transport networks.
  • Implementation and selection. The scheme spans FY2026–27 to FY2031–32; initially, up to 50 parks will be selected through challenge mode based on project readiness, land availability, and industrial potential.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does BHAVYA matter for India's manufacturing sector?

  • Manufacturing sector growth. India's manufacturing contributes about 16–17% of GDP and employs over 27 million workers; the National Manufacturing Mission targets raising this to 25% by 2035.
  • Shift to technology-intensive industries. Medium- and high-technology industries now account for 46.3% of manufacturing value addition, reflecting a move towards higher-value manufacturing.
  • Reducing entry barriers. Plug-and-play parks reduce time and cost for new units by providing essential infrastructure and streamlined approvals before operations begin.
  • Global supply chain relevance. Diversifying global supply chains increase demand for well-connected, infrastructure-ready industrial locations, which BHAVYA aims to provide.

Back to basics: Plug-and-Play Industrial Parks

Plug-and-play industrial parks are pre-developed industrial areas where essential infrastructure like roads, power, water, and utilities are provided in advance. They often include streamlined regulatory approvals through single-window systems, allowing industries to start operations faster and at lower initial cost. This model supports faster industrial growth by reducing entry barriers and improving ease of doing business.
