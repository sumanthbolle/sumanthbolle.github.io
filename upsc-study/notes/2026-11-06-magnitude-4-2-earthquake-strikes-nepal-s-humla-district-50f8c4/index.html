What happened during the earthquake in Nepal's Humla district?

  • Earthquake details. A 4.2 magnitude earthquake struck Humla district in Nepal's Karnali Province at 7:10 a.m. on November 6, 2026.

Back to basics: Earthquake and disaster management

An earthquake is a sudden shaking of the ground caused by movement of the Earth's tectonic plates. Disaster management involves preparedness, response, and mitigation strategies to reduce damage and protect lives during such natural events. Understanding earthquake-prone zones and regional cooperation is crucial for effective disaster response.
