Nepal PM Balendra Shah's First Official Visit to India Strengthens Bilateral Ties

Nepal Prime Minister Balendra Shah arrived in Delhi on November 22, 2026, for his first official visit to India. This visit is important for UPSC aspirants as it marks a key development in India-Nepal relations and regional diplomacy.

Nepal PM Balendra Shah's inaugural official visit to India aims to set political direction for bilateral cooperation in areas like energy, connectivity, and climate change.

Why is this in the news?

Nepal Prime Minister Balendra Shah arrived in Delhi on November 22, 2026, for his first official visit to India, with talks scheduled on November 23 to guide future bilateral relations.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the significance of Nepal PM Balendra Shah's visit to India?

  • First official visit. Balendra Shah arrived in Delhi on November 22, 2026, marking his first official visit to India since becoming Nepal's Prime Minister in March 2026.
  • Political direction. The talks on November 23 between PM Shah and PM Modi are expected to provide political direction to India-Nepal bilateral relations for the coming years.
  • Project inaugurations. PM Shah is set to inaugurate key projects including the Dharchula Bridge, the Banbasa land port, and a train connection between Janakpur and Gorakhpur.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the key areas of cooperation discussed during the visit?

  • Development partnership. Agreements are expected in development partnership, energy, hydropower, railways, connectivity, climate change, disaster resilience, infrastructure, and technology.
  • Energy imports. Nepal has received approval to import 350 MW of power from India during the winter season.
  • Rail connectivity. Discussions include new railway links between Raxaul and Kathmandu to enhance freight and passenger transport.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Who accompanied PM Balendra Shah and how was the visit conducted?

  • Delegation details. PM Shah was accompanied by a 35-member team including Nepal’s Finance Minister Swarnim Wagley and Foreign Minister Shisir Khanal.
  • No-frills approach. Departing from tradition, Shah's departure from Kathmandu lacked ceremonial farewell and the Nepal Embassy is unlikely to host a civic reception.
  • Ceremonial reception in India. In India, PM Shah will receive a ceremonial reception at Rashtrapati Bhavan and visit the Mahatma Gandhi memorial before talks and meeting the Indian President.

Back to basics: India-Nepal Bilateral Relations

India and Nepal share a long-standing relationship based on cultural, economic, and political ties. Their bilateral relations include cooperation in trade, energy, infrastructure, and connectivity projects. High-level visits between the two countries help set the agenda for future collaboration and strengthen diplomatic ties.
