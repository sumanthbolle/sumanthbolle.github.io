India's IT Spending to Reach ₹16.9 Trillion in 2027 Driven by AI and Cloud

India's IT expenditure is expected to grow by 10.6% in 2027, reaching ₹16.9 trillion, propelled by investments in AI infrastructure, cloud services, data centres, and cybersecurity. This growth highlights India's strategic focus on digital sovereignty and technology-driven economic expansion, relevant for UPSC aspirants studying economy and technology.

India's IT spending will grow to ₹16.9 trillion in 2027, driven mainly by AI, cloud infrastructure, and cybersecurity investments.

Why is this in the news?

Gartner forecast on November 17, 2026, projects India's IT spending to reach ₹16.9 trillion in 2027, driven by AI and cloud investments.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is driving India's IT spending growth?

  • AI and cloud investments. India's IT spending growth is driven by rising investments in artificial intelligence infrastructure, cloud infrastructure, data centres, and cybersecurity.
  • Strategic priorities. AI infrastructure and digital sovereignty have become strategic priorities for India, influencing technology investment decisions.
  • Sectoral demand. Financial services and government sectors are major contributors to the demand for cloud infrastructure and AI-optimised services.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How are specific IT segments expected to grow?

  • Infrastructure as a Service (IaaS). Spending on IaaS is expected to grow by 40.3% in 2027 over 2026, reflecting strong demand for cloud infrastructure.
  • Data centre systems. Investment in data centre systems is projected to increase by 28.4%, supported by government policies and global hyperscaler commitments.
  • Software spending. Software spending is forecast to reach ₹2.6 trillion in 2027, growing 19.3% due to demand for AI-enabled applications and cloud software platforms.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for India's economy and governance?

  • Digital competitiveness. AI is becoming a foundational layer for business growth and digital competitiveness in India.
  • Policy support. Favourable government policies and incentives are expanding India's compute capacity and ambition to be a global AI infrastructure hub.
  • Shift in service demand. As AI adoption matures, demand is shifting towards higher-value consulting, implementation, and advisory services.

Back to basics: Information Technology Spending

Information Technology (IT) spending refers to the total expenditure by businesses and governments on IT products and services, including hardware, software, cloud services, and infrastructure. It reflects the level of technological adoption and digital transformation in an economy. Growth in IT spending often indicates increased investment in digital capabilities, which can drive economic growth and employment.
