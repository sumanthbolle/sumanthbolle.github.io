Tribal Communities Recognized as Guardians of Biodiversity in India

Union Minister Dr Jitendra Singh highlighted the role of tribal communities as guardians of biodiversity during Janjatiya Gaurav Divas on November 17, 2026. This recognition links tribal knowledge and practices with India's scientific progress and biodiversity conservation, relevant for both Prelims and Mains preparation.

Tribal communities live in harmony with nature and their traditional knowledge inspires modern science, making them key to biodiversity conservation and sustainable development.

Why is this in the news?

On November 17, 2026, Dr Jitendra Singh addressed Janjatiya Gaurav Divas, emphasizing tribal communities as biodiversity guardians and releasing a technology compendium for tribal empowerment.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How are tribal communities linked to biodiversity conservation?

  • Guardians of biodiversity. Tribal communities live in harmony with forests, rivers, and mountains, acting as natural custodians of biodiversity through their traditional knowledge and practices.
  • Inspiration for modern science. Their indigenous knowledge continues to inspire modern scientific research and innovation, especially in sustainable natural resource management.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What initiatives support tribal empowerment through science and technology?

  • STEM training and digital education. Digital education and STEM training aim to enable tribal youth to lead India’s scientific journey and innovation efforts.
  • Technology-based enterprises. Support through skills, mentoring, finance, and market linkages helps create livelihoods and strengthen local economies, especially in the North Eastern Region.
  • Projects and hubs. The Department of Science and Technology has implemented 124 projects benefiting over 75,000 tribal people and established 25 Science, Technology and Innovation Hubs nationwide.
  • Startup promotion. Proposals have been invited to establish NIDHI Technology Business Incubator hubs to promote startups with at least one Scheduled Tribe founder.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does tribal empowerment matter for India’s development?

  • Inclusive development. Empowering tribal communities supports social justice, equality, and inclusive growth, aligning with the vision of Viksit Bharat @2047.
  • Local and affordable technology. Affordable, accessible technologies suited to local conditions are essential for sustainable development in remote and challenging regions.
  • Health innovation example. A multi-institutional CRISPR-Cas9 gene therapy effort addresses sickle cell anaemia prevalent in tribal populations, showcasing science serving tribal health needs.

Back to basics: Role of Indigenous Knowledge in Biodiversity Conservation

Indigenous knowledge refers to the traditional understanding and practices developed by local communities over generations. It often includes sustainable ways to manage natural resources and conserve biodiversity. Recognizing this knowledge helps integrate community participation in environmental protection and scientific innovation, ensuring culturally appropriate and effective conservation strategies.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about tribal communities and biodiversity conservation: 1. Tribal communities are recognized as guardians of biodiversity and their traditional knowledge inspires modern science. 2. The Department of Science and Technology has established over 20 Science, Technology and Innovation Hubs exclusively for tribal empowerment. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because 25 STI Hubs have been established, not 'over 20' exclusively for tribal empowerment; the exact number is important.

Mains question (15 marks)

Conclusion: Integrating tribal knowledge with science and technology is vital for sustainable development and inclusive growth in India.
