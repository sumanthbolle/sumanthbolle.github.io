World Bank Raises India's GDP Growth Forecast to 7.1% for FY27

The World Bank upgraded India's GDP growth projection for FY27 to 7.1% from 6.6% in April, citing better-than-expected economic performance despite global uncertainties. This update highlights key growth drivers such as exports, private consumption, and industrial activity, which are crucial for understanding India's economic outlook.

The World Bank projects India's GDP growth at 7.1% for FY27, driven by strong exports, private consumption, and industrial growth.

Why is this in the news?

On October 6, 2026, the World Bank released its India Development Update, raising the GDP growth forecast for FY27 to 7.1%.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the reasons behind the World Bank's upgraded GDP forecast for India?

  • Growth drivers. The World Bank attributed the upgrade to better-than-expected first quarter GDP growth of 7.8%, strong exports, solid private consumption, and robust industrial activity.
  • Exports impact. Exports have outperformed expectations and are seen as the main upside factor for the FY27 growth outlook compared to earlier forecasts.
  • Consumption trends. Rural consumption initially led growth supported by agricultural performance and income support, while urban consumption strengthened later due to tax relief and GST cuts.
  • Industrial performance. Industrial activity exceeded expectations with infrastructure and construction goods growth accelerating to 7.2% in Q1 and electricity sector expanding by 9.3%.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How do external and domestic factors influence India's growth outlook?

  • Global uncertainties. Despite trade tensions and geopolitical uncertainties, India's growth held up better than expected, although global uncertainty weighs on private investment.
  • Agricultural challenges. A rainfall deficit during the southwest monsoon weakened agricultural prospects, which could modestly affect rural demand.
  • Policy support. Supportive financial and policy conditions, including stronger public investment, help offset some global risks and sustain growth momentum.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What sectors are expected to contribute to growth in FY27?

  • Services sector. The services sector growth remains elevated despite a slowdown from a high base in FY26.
  • Industry sector. Industry is forecasted to perform better than initially expected, driven by infrastructure, construction, and electricity sectors.
  • Agriculture sector. Agricultural growth is expected to be weaker due to monsoon rainfall shortages.

Back to basics: GDP Growth and Economic Indicators

Gross Domestic Product (GDP) measures the total value of goods and services produced in a country and is a key indicator of economic health. Growth projections help policymakers and analysts understand the expected pace of economic expansion. Factors like private consumption, investment, exports, and sectoral performance influence GDP growth. External factors such as global trade conditions and domestic factors like monsoon impact agricultural output and overall economic activity.
