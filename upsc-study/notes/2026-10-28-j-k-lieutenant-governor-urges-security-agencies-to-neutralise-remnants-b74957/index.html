J&K Lieutenant Governor urges neutralisation of terrorist remnants

Jammu and Kashmir Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha directed security agencies to continue operations to dismantle the remnants of the terrorist ecosystem and disrupt their logistical and financial support. This is important for UPSC aspirants to understand ongoing counter-terrorism strategies and internal security challenges in J&K.

The J&K Lieutenant Governor has called for sustained counter-terrorism operations to neutralise terrorist networks and cut off their support systems.

Why is this in the news?

On October 27, 2026, J&K Lieutenant Governor Manoj Sinha chaired a security meeting in Srinagar urging continued operations against terrorist remnants to enhance regional security.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What directives did the J&K Lieutenant Governor give to security agencies?

  • Neutralise terrorist remnants. Manoj Sinha directed security forces to continue operations aimed at neutralising the remnants of the terrorist ecosystem in Jammu and Kashmir.
  • Disrupt support networks. He emphasized implementing a foolproof counter-strategy to choke off logistical and financial support for terrorists.
  • Counter infiltration and radicalisation. The Lieutenant Governor called for stepped-up measures to disrupt infiltration and aggressively target networks that misguide and radicalize people.
  • Maintain vigilance in winter. He reviewed operational preparedness for the winter season and stressed the need for vigilance against emerging threats.
  • Build public trust. Security forces were directed to build public trust by listening to people's grievances and ensuring their voices are heard.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for UPSC aspirants?

  • Internal security challenge. Terrorism in Jammu and Kashmir remains a critical internal security issue requiring coordinated counter-terrorism operations.
  • Role of security agencies. Understanding the roles of the army, police, CRPF, and intelligence agencies in neutralising terrorist threats is essential for the security syllabus.
  • Counter-terrorism strategies. The focus on disrupting terrorist logistics, finances, infiltration, and radicalisation reflects key counter-terrorism approaches.
  • Public cooperation importance. Building public trust is vital for effective security operations and maintaining peace in conflict-prone areas.

Back to basics: Counter-terrorism in internal security

Counter-terrorism involves coordinated efforts by security agencies to prevent, detect, and neutralise terrorist activities within a country. It includes disrupting terrorist networks, cutting off their financial and logistical support, preventing infiltration, and countering radicalisation. Public cooperation and intelligence sharing are critical components for successful counter-terrorism operations.

Mains question (15 marks)

Examine: Explain the challenges faced in counter-terrorism in J&K and discuss the strategies used to overcome them.

Intro: Counter-terrorism in Jammu and Kashmir is a complex challenge due to persistent terrorist threats and local dynamics.

  • Challenges include infiltration, logistical and financial support to terrorists, and radicalisation of local populations.
  • Strategies involve coordinated operations by security forces to neutralise terrorist remnants and disrupt their support networks.
  • Emphasis on intelligence sharing, operational preparedness especially during winters, and building public trust to counter radicalisation.
  • Recent directives by the J&K Lieutenant Governor highlight the need for a comprehensive approach combining security measures and public engagement.

Conclusion: Effective counter-terrorism in J&K requires sustained operations, intelligence coordination, and community cooperation.
