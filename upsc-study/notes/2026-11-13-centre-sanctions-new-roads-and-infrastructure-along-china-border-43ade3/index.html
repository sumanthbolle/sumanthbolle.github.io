Centre Sanctions New Roads and Bridges Along China Border in Arunachal Pradesh

The Indian government has approved the construction of 26 new roads and multiple pedestrian and log bridges along the Line of Actual Control (LAC) in Arunachal Pradesh to strengthen border infrastructure. This development is significant for UPSC aspirants as it highlights India's strategic infrastructure initiatives in response to ongoing border tensions with China.

The government sanctioned 26 roads and several bridges along the Arunachal Pradesh-China border to enhance connectivity and security amid continuing face-offs.

Why is this in the news?

The Cabinet Committee on Security cleared the third phase of the Indo-China Border Roads project in November 2026, amid ongoing border tensions with China in Arunachal Pradesh.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What infrastructure projects have been sanctioned along the China border?

  • Road construction. The Cabinet Committee on Security approved the construction of 26 new roads along the LAC in Arunachal Pradesh as part of the Indo-China Border Roads (ICBR-III) project.
  • Bridge construction. The Indo-Tibetan Border Police sanctioned 15 pedestrian suspension bridges and 18 log bridges in Arunachal Pradesh's Upper Subansiri and Kurung Kumey districts.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why is this infrastructure development important?

  • Response to border tensions. These projects come after continuing face-offs with Chinese troops along the Arunachal Pradesh border since July 2026, aiming to improve connectivity and security.
  • Post-Galwan strategic push. This is the second major infrastructure push after the 2020 Galwan clashes, with earlier phases of the ICBR project initiated in 2005 and expanded in 2020.
  • Support for patrols and logistics. Improved roads and bridges facilitate long-range patrols and logistics in difficult terrain where patrols operate mainly between June and October.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What diplomatic efforts accompany these infrastructure measures?

  • Border talks. Despite infrastructure build-up, India and China held three high-level border talks between July and September 2026 to manage tensions.
  • Emphasis on peace. At the 18th BRICS Summit in September 2026, both countries stressed the need for peace and tranquility in border areas to maintain bilateral relations.

Back to basics: Border Infrastructure Development

Border infrastructure includes roads, bridges, and facilities that enable military and administrative access to remote border areas. Such infrastructure is critical for effective border management, rapid troop movement, and logistical support, especially in difficult terrains like Arunachal Pradesh. The Indo-China Border Roads project is a government initiative to build and upgrade roads along the Line of Actual Control to strengthen India's border security and connectivity.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about recent infrastructure developments along the India-China border in Arunachal Pradesh: 1. The Cabinet Committee on Security approved 26 new roads under the ICBR-III project. 2. The Indo-Tibetan Border Police sanctioned the construction of 15 pedestrian suspension bridges and 18 log bridges in Arunachal Pradesh. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (c). A trap could be confusing the number of roads or bridges or the approving authority; the CCS approved the roads, and ITBP sanctioned the bridges.
