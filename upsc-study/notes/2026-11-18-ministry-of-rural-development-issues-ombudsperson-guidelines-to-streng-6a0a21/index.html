Ministry of Rural Development Issues Ombudsperson Guidelines under VB-G RAM G

The Ministry of Rural Development has issued guidelines to strengthen transparency, accountability, and grievance redressal under the Viksit Bharat – Guarantee for Rozgar and Ajeevika Mission (Gramin) Act, 2025. These guidelines enhance institutional mechanisms to safeguard rural workers’ rights and promote citizen participation in governance.

The Ombudsperson guidelines under VB-G RAM G improve grievance redressal and accountability for rural workers’ statutory rights.

Why is this in the news?

On 18 November 2026, the Ministry of Rural Development issued guidelines for Ombudspersons and Appellate Authorities under the VB-G RAM G Act, 2025 to strengthen transparency and accountability.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the Ombudsperson guidelines under VB-G RAM G?

  • Purpose of guidelines. The guidelines aim to reinforce the VB-G RAM G Act by strengthening institutional mechanisms to safeguard rural workers’ statutory rights and ensure accountable delivery of programme benefits.
  • Role of Ombudsperson. A dedicated Ombudsperson at the district level will address grievances related to job cards, employment, wage payments, delay compensation, worksite facilities, muster rolls, denial of statutory entitlements, discrimination, harassment, and violations of workers’ rights.
  • Eligibility and capacity building. The guidelines expand the pool of eligible candidates to include those with experience in rural development, public administration, law, academics, social work, and management, and revise tenure and remuneration provisions to support continuity and effective functioning.
  • Digital platform and documentation. A dedicated digital platform will facilitate online complaint registration, tracking, recording proceedings, uploading awards, and monitoring actions, supported by geo-tagged and time-stamped photographs during field visits.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How do the guidelines strengthen transparency and accountability?

  • Social audit integration. Ombudspersons are required to attend at least three Social Audit public hearings or Gram Sabhas monthly, enabling direct citizen engagement and follow-up on grievances.
  • Community feedback mechanism. Social audit findings and community feedback, along with individual grievances, help identify discrepancies and initiate corrective actions as per prescribed procedures.
  • Complementary accountability measures. The Ombudsperson and Appellate Authority framework complements digital monitoring, public disclosure, and social audits to promote transparent implementation and strengthen oversight.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does this matter for governance and rural development?

  • Accessible grievance redressal. The guidelines provide rural workers with accessible mechanisms to raise complaints and seek redressal, ensuring their statutory rights are protected.
  • Enhanced citizen-centric governance. By institutionalizing transparency and accountability, the guidelines aim to make implementation of rural development schemes more responsive and accountable to citizens.

Back to basics: Ombudsperson and Transparency Mechanisms

An Ombudsperson is an official appointed to investigate complaints against maladministration or violation of rights, providing an accessible grievance redressal mechanism. Transparency mechanisms like social audits and digital monitoring ensure public schemes are implemented openly and accountably by involving citizens and enabling oversight.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the Ombudsperson guidelines under VB-G RAM G: 1. The Ombudsperson addresses grievances related to wage payments, discrimination, and denial of statutory entitlements. 2.   1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because Ombudspersons must attend at least three Social Audit public hearings or Gram Sabhas monthly, not five.
