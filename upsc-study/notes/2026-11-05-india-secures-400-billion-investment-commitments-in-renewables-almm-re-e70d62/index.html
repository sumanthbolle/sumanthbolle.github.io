India Secures $400 Billion Investment in Renewables; ALMM Review Pending

India has obtained preliminary commitments of $400 billion from global investors for renewable energy projects, including green hydrogen and energy storage. The government will decide on extending the Approved List of Models and Manufacturers (ALMM) framework within 10-15 days after consulting stakeholders.

India's $400 billion renewable energy investment commitments and upcoming ALMM review highlight its push for sustainable energy and domestic manufacturing.

Why is this in the news?

On November 4, 2026, Union Minister Pralhad Joshi announced $400 billion in preliminary renewable energy investment commitments and stated the ALMM framework review would occur in 10-15 days.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the details of the $400 billion investment commitments?

  • Investment scope. The $400 billion commitments cover renewable energy sectors such as green hydrogen, energy storage, and transmission.
  • Investor confidence. Global investors and fund managers expressed confidence in India as a safe investment destination during a CEO roundtable.
  • Preliminary nature. These commitments are preliminary, with final details still being worked out.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the Approved List of Models and Manufacturers (ALMM) framework and its current status?

  • ALMM purpose. The ALMM framework mandates the use of domestically approved solar module manufacturers for specified projects to promote local manufacturing.
  • Current extension. The framework was last extended in July 2026 until December 31 for solar PV cells.
  • Upcoming review. The government will consult industry stakeholders over the next 10-15 days before deciding whether to extend the ALMM framework further.
  • Industry views. There are divergent views within the industry, with some supporting extension and others opposing it.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for India’s energy policy and economy?

  • Renewable energy targets. Securing large investments supports India’s renewable energy expansion and sustainability goals.
  • Domestic manufacturing. The ALMM framework encourages domestic solar manufacturing capacity, which currently stands at 41 GW with commitments to add 70 GW by year-end.
  • Investment climate. Positive investor sentiment reflects confidence in India’s policy environment and economic stability.

Back to basics: Renewable Energy Investment and Domestic Manufacturing Policy

Renewable energy investment involves funding projects that generate energy from sustainable sources like solar, wind, and green hydrogen. Governments often promote domestic manufacturing to reduce reliance on imports and boost local industry, using policies such as the Approved List of Models and Manufacturers (ALMM) which restricts procurement to approved local manufacturers to encourage self-reliance and economic growth.
