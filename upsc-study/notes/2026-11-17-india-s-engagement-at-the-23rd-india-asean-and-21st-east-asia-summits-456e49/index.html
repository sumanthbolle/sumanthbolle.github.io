India's Role in the 2026 ASEAN and East Asia Summits in Manila

India participated in the 2026 ASEAN and East Asia Summits held in Manila, engaging with regional leaders and the Quad grouping. This matters for UPSC as it highlights India's diplomatic efforts and strategic partnerships in the Indo-Pacific region.

India's active engagement at the 2026 ASEAN and East Asia Summits in Manila reflects its commitment to regional cooperation and multilateral diplomacy in the Indo-Pacific.

Why is this in the news?

The 2026 ASEAN and East Asia Summits held in Manila saw India participate actively, including in the Quad Summit on the sidelines, marking key diplomatic engagements in November 2026.

What were the key features of the 2026 ASEAN Summit in Manila?

  • UN Secretary-General's address. UN Secretary-General António Guterres emphasized stronger cooperation with ASEAN and highlighted challenges like conflicts, climate change, and humanitarian needs affecting the region.
  • Focus on Myanmar crisis. Guterres expressed deep concern over the ongoing crisis in Myanmar since the military coup, urging protection of civilians and humanitarian access.
  • Regional security and cooperation. The summit discussed the need for peace in conflict zones and reforms in multilateral institutions to better represent emerging powers.

[1] United Nations News

What was India's role and agenda at the Quad Summit in Manila?

  • Quad leaders' meeting. Prime Minister Narendra Modi met leaders of the US, Japan, and Australia at the Quad Summit held alongside ASEAN meetings, marking the first such summit since 2024.
  • Key agenda items. The Quad focused on securing critical mineral supply chains, safeguarding commercial shipping routes, and addressing attacks on commercial ships, which concern India due to seafarer casualties.
  • Significance of Manila hosting. Manila was the first non-Quad country to host the Quad Summit, reflecting challenges India faced in hosting and its intent to hold a summit in Delhi next year.

Why does India's engagement in these summits matter for UPSC aspirants?

  • Regional diplomacy. India's participation demonstrates its strategic diplomacy in the Indo-Pacific, balancing relations with ASEAN and Quad partners.
  • Multilateral cooperation. The summits highlight India's role in multilateral forums addressing security, economic, and humanitarian issues.
  • Geopolitical context. India's concerns over maritime security and regional conflicts are crucial for understanding its foreign policy priorities.

Back to basics: ASEAN and Quad Grouping

The Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) is a regional organization of 11 countries promoting political and economic cooperation in Southeast Asia. The Quad is a strategic dialogue among India, Australia, Japan, and the United States focusing on security and economic issues in the Indo-Pacific region. Understanding these groupings helps explain India's regional diplomacy and multilateral engagements.
