Why in news

The Vice-President inaugurated Deepotsav 2026 celebrations at Ram Ki Paidi in Ayodhya on 7 November 2026. The official account frames the event as Jan Bhagidari and links its cultural programme with local livelihood opportunities, technology use and pilgrimage-related infrastructure.

Background

  • The Vice-President inaugurated Deepotsav 2026 at Ram Ki Paidi, Ayodhya, on 7 November 2026 and witnessed lamp illumination, cultural presentations, musical fireworks and Saryu Aarti.
  • The source states that Deepotsav grew from over 1.7 lakh lamps in 2017 to over 26 lakh in 2025; the tenth edition envisaged more than 30 lakh diyas at Ram Ki Paidi and over 70 lakh across Ayodhya.
  • The programme also included more than 2,100 indigenous drones depicting Ramayana scenes and around 1,000 artists in cultural programmes.

Way forward
