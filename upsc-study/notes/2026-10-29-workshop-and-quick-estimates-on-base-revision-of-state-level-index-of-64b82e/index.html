Workshop on Revising Base Year of State-level Index of Industrial Production

The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) held a three-day workshop from 28 to 30 October 2026 in Thiruvananthapuram to revise the base year of the State-level Index of Industrial Production (IIP) to 2022-23. This revision updates the industrial performance measurement framework, which is crucial for economic analysis and policy formulation.

MoSPI's base year revision of the State-level IIP to 2022-23 aligns industrial statistics with current production patterns and improves data quality for policymaking.

Why is this in the news?

What is the purpose of revising the base year of the State-level IIP?

  • Update industrial measurement. The base year revision from 2011-12 to 2022-23 ensures the IIP reflects current industrial structures, product ranges, and production patterns.
  • Improve data quality. Revisions include updating the item basket and weights, factory panels, and expanding coverage to sectors like Gas Supply, Water Supply, Sewerage, and Waste Management.
  • Enhance comparability. Aligning State-level IIP with the national methodology improves consistency and comparability across States and Union Territories.

[1] Press Information Bureau[3] Press Information Bureau

How does the workshop support the revision process?

  • Technical guidance. The workshop familiarizes State and UT officials with revised IIP structures, methods, and helps finalize item lists and reporting factories.
  • Capacity building. It strengthens the technical capacity of State statistical offices for effective implementation of the base-year revision.
  • Stakeholder interaction. The workshop provides a platform for feedback on National Industrial Classification-2025 and National Product Classification revisions.

[1] Press Information Bureau[3] Press Information Bureau

What are the recent trends in India's industrial production as per the revised base year?

  • Growth in September 2026. The Index of Industrial Production recorded a 6.7% year-on-year growth, with manufacturing growing 7.6% and electricity & gas supply growing 11.2%.
  • Sectoral performance. Mining & Quarrying declined by 6.2%, while Water Supply, Sewerage & Waste Management grew by 5.8% in September 2026.
  • Manufacturing highlights. 17 out of 23 manufacturing industry groups showed positive growth, with motor vehicles, electrical equipment, and rubber & plastics products as top contributors.

[2] Press Information Bureau

Why does this revision matter for economic policy and analysis?

  • Accurate industrial tracking. A current base year helps policymakers and economists assess real-time industrial performance and trends.
  • Supports growth estimation. IIP serves as an important input in estimating overall economic growth and guiding policy decisions.
  • Improves data reliability. Methodological improvements reduce errors and enhance the credibility of industrial statistics.

Back to basics: Index of Industrial Production (IIP)

The IIP measures the short-term changes in the volume of production of a basket of industrial products during a given period compared to a base period. It is a key economic indicator used to assess industrial sector performance and overall economic health. The base year is the reference year against which changes in production are measured, and updating it ensures the index reflects current industrial realities.
