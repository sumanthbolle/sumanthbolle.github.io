NHRC Takes Suo Motu Cognizance of Trafficking of Minor Girls to Maharashtra

The National Human Rights Commission (NHRC) has taken suo motu cognizance of media reports about minor girls being sold by parents in Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan to middlemen for trafficking to Maharashtra. This case highlights the violation of child rights and the NHRC's role in addressing human trafficking, relevant for governance and social issues in UPSC preparation.

The NHRC has initiated action on reports of trafficking of minor girls from MP and Rajasthan to Maharashtra, underscoring its role in protecting human rights.

Why is this in the news?

On November 3, 2026, the NHRC took suo motu cognizance of media reports alleging trafficking and sale of minor girls from border districts of Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan to cities in Maharashtra.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What did the NHRC observe and do regarding the trafficking reports?

  • Suo motu cognizance. The NHRC took suo motu cognizance of media reports about minor girls being sold by their parents to middlemen for Rs. 3 lakh to Rs. 5 lakh in border districts of Madhya Pradesh and Rajasthan.
  • Human rights violation. The Commission noted that if the reports are true, they indicate serious violations of the human rights of the victim girl children.
  • Issued notices. NHRC issued notices to the Chief Secretaries and Directors General of Police of Madhya Pradesh, Rajasthan, and Maharashtra, requesting detailed reports within two weeks.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

How does the trafficking operation reportedly work?

  • Sale by parents. Parents reportedly sell their minor daughters to middlemen due to poverty and other reasons for Rs. 3 lakh to Rs. 5 lakh.
  • Use of false documents. Middlemen use forged documents to show the girls as their adopted children to facilitate trafficking.
  • Resale for sex trade. Upon reaching adulthood, the girls are sold for Rs. 35 lakh to Rs. 40 lakh in Maharashtra for sex trade, with their personal details altered to hide their identities.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for governance and social issues?

  • Child rights violation. The trafficking involves exploitation and violation of the rights of minor girls, raising concerns about child protection.
  • Role of NHRC. NHRC's suo motu action exemplifies the role of statutory bodies in protecting vulnerable sections and ensuring accountability.
  • Interstate coordination. The case involves multiple states, highlighting the need for coordinated governance and law enforcement across borders.

Back to basics: Suo Motu Cognizance by Statutory Bodies

Suo motu cognizance is the power of a statutory body or court to take notice of an issue on its own, without a formal complaint. The National Human Rights Commission (NHRC) uses this power to address violations of human rights proactively. This mechanism helps in prompt intervention in cases involving vulnerable groups such as children and ensures accountability of state authorities.
