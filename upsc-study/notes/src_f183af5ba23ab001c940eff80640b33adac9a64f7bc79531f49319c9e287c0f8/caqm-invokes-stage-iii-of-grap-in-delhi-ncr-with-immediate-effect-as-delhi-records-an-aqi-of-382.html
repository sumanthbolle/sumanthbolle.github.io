  • Delhi-NCR air pollution demonstrates the need for coordinated regional governance because atmospheric pollution does not respect administrative boundaries.
  • Public health protection requires combining emergency response with sustained emission reduction, cleaner mobility, dust control and prevention of open burning.
  • Citizen-oriented instructions can improve compliance, but they should complement—not substitute for—agency-level enforcement and source control.
  • Stage-based restrictions require transparent communication, targeted support for affected workers and periodic review to balance environmental protection with economic continuity.
