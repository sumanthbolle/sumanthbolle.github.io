Supreme Court Petitions Allege Police Excesses in Delhi CEC Protests

Petitions filed in the Supreme Court claim police used excessive force during protests against the Delhi Chief Election Commissioner in October 2026. These raise important questions on civil liberties, police accountability, and constitutional rights relevant for UPSC exams.

Petitions in the Supreme Court allege police brutality and illegal detention during Delhi protests demanding the CEC's resignation, highlighting constitutional rights violations.

Why is this in the news?

Following protests against the Delhi Chief Election Commissioner on October 10-12, 2026, detainees filed petitions in the Supreme Court alleging police excesses and seeking independent inquiry and protection of evidence.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What allegations have been made against the police during the protests?

  • Physical assault and illegal detention. Protesters, including women, minors, journalists, and lawyers, alleged being dragged, slapped, beaten, and detained illegally without grounds or access to legal counsel.
  • Sexual assault and abuse. Women protesters reported sexual assault by police personnel and hooligans while police allegedly remained passive spectators.
  • Procedural violations. Detentions occurred in areas without proper legal orders, detainees were not informed of reasons, and juveniles were held without guardian notification.
  • Selective targeting. Petitioners claimed police acted selectively based on appearance or perceived identity, violating Articles 14 and 15 of the Constitution.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What legal and constitutional issues do these petitions raise?

  • Violation of fundamental rights. The petitions allege violations of the right to peaceful protest, protection against arbitrary arrest, and equal treatment under the law.
  • Supreme Court guidelines on arrest. Petitioners argue police actions contravened Supreme Court rules on arrest and detention, which protect citizens' rights during protests.
  • Demand for independent inquiry. The petitions seek an independent investigation into police conduct, preservation of CCTV and bodycam footage, and return of confiscated belongings.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How did the police response affect public order and protest rights?

  • Disruption of public services. Mobile internet was suspended, trains cancelled, buses diverted, roads closed, and metro stations shut around protest areas.
  • Impact on journalists and legal aid. Women journalists faced physical handling and detention while performing duties; lawyers were denied access to detainees and legal aid was delayed.
  • Alleged excessive use of force. Multiple detainees reported injuries including bruises and dislocated shoulders due to police action.

Back to basics: Fundamental Rights and Police Accountability

Fundamental rights in the Constitution protect citizens against arbitrary arrest and ensure the right to peaceful protest. The judiciary enforces these rights by setting guidelines for police conduct during protests and detention. Police accountability mechanisms include independent inquiries and judicial review to prevent abuse of power.

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the petitions were filed by detainees and activists alleging police excesses, not by the police.

Mains question (15 marks)

Examine the role of the judiciary in upholding civil liberties and ensuring police accountability during protests in India.

Examine: Discuss how the judiciary protects fundamental rights and checks police excesses during protests, using recent Supreme Court petitions as an example.

Intro: The judiciary safeguards civil liberties by enforcing constitutional protections against police excesses during protests.

  • Judicial oversight ensures police follow legal procedures during arrests and detentions, protecting citizens' rights.
  • Independent inquiries and preservation of evidence are tools used by courts to hold police accountable.
  • Recent petitions alleging police brutality during Delhi CEC protests highlight the judiciary's role in addressing violations.
  • The judiciary balances maintaining public order with protecting the right to peaceful protest.

Conclusion: Judicial intervention is essential to uphold democratic rights and ensure police accountability in protest situations.
