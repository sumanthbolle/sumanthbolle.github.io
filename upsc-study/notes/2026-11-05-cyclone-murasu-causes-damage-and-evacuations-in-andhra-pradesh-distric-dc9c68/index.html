Cyclone Murasu Triggers Evacuations and Safety Measures in Andhra Pradesh

Cyclone Murasu crossed the coast near Ongole, Andhra Pradesh, causing heavy rainfall and prompting district administrations to activate control rooms and issue public safety advisories. This event highlights disaster management practices and climate impact on vulnerable regions, relevant for UPSC preparation.

Cyclone Murasu's landfall near Ongole led to heavy rains and precautionary measures including control room activation and public advisories in Andhra Pradesh districts.

Why is this in the news?

On November 3, 2026, Cyclone Murasu crossed the coast near Ongole, Andhra Pradesh, leading to activation of control rooms and public advisories in affected districts.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What actions were taken in response to Cyclone Murasu?

  • Control room activation. A control room was activated at the Bhadrachalam Sub-Collector’s office in Bhadradri Kothagudem district to coordinate disaster response.
  • Public safety advisory. The district administration issued advisories urging people to avoid unnecessary travel and stay away from water bodies due to forecasted heavy rainfall.
  • Weather conditions. Several areas in the erstwhile undivided Khammam district experienced drizzle and cloudy conditions following the cyclone's landfall.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does Cyclone Murasu matter for UPSC preparation?

  • Climate impact on vulnerable regions. Cyclone Murasu's impact on Andhra Pradesh highlights challenges posed by cyclones and heavy rainfall in coastal and adjoining districts.

Back to basics: Disaster management

Disaster management involves preparing for, responding to, and recovering from natural or man-made disasters. It includes early warning systems, evacuation plans, control rooms for coordination, and public advisories to minimize harm to people and property.
