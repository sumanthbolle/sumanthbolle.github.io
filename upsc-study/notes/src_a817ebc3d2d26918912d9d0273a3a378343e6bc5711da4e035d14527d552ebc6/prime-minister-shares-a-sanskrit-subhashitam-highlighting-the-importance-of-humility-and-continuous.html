Why in news

On 22 October 2026, the Prime Minister shared a Sanskrit subhashitam highlighting humility and continuous learning. The message is relevant to ethics because it presents limited success as a reason for perspective and further growth, not arrogance.

Static anchor

Humility is an ethical disposition of recognising one’s limitations and remaining open to correction; continuous learning is the sustained effort to update knowledge, skills and judgement. In public administration, both should support competence and accountability rather than replace them.

Background

  • A Sanskrit subhashitam is a concise wisdom verse used to express a moral or practical lesson. The reported verse uses the changing visibility of a firefly, moon and sun as an analogy for relative achievement.
  • The Prime Minister’s Office release explains the verse through two linked ideas: limited success should not produce arrogance, and learning and growth should continue.
