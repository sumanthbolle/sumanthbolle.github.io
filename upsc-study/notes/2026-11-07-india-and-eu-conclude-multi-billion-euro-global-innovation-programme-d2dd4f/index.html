India and EU Finalize Association with Horizon Europe Innovation Programme

India and the European Union have concluded negotiations for India’s association with Horizon Europe, the EU’s flagship €93.5 billion research and innovation programme. This association enables Indian researchers and institutions to collaborate on cutting-edge projects, aligning with India’s scientific priorities and enhancing bilateral strategic cooperation.

India’s association with Horizon Europe marks a strategic partnership to boost collaborative research and innovation with the European Union.

Why is this in the news?

On November 7, 2026, India and the European Union completed negotiations for India’s association with Horizon Europe, paving the way for expanded scientific collaboration.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the Horizon Europe programme and India’s association?

  • Programme overview. Horizon Europe is the EU’s flagship research and innovation programme running from 2021 to 2027 with a budget of €93.5 billion, focusing on global challenges and European industrial competitiveness.
  • India’s association. India’s association allows its researchers, institutions, and industries to participate in funding calls, collaborate in European consortia, access advanced research infrastructure, and co-develop technologies.
  • Scope of collaboration. The collaboration covers thematic clusters like health, digital technologies, industry, space, climate, energy, mobility, food, and bioeconomy, aligning with India’s research priorities.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does India’s association with Horizon Europe matter for India-EU relations?

  • Strategic partnership. The association strengthens the long-term institutional framework for research cooperation under the India–EU Agreement on Science and Technology Cooperation and the India-EU Trade and Technology Council.
  • Enhanced scientific cooperation. It deepens talent mobility, facilitates co-development of critical technologies, and creates opportunities for joint solutions to societal and technological challenges.
  • Broader economic context. This scientific cooperation complements the upcoming India-EU free trade agreement, expanding collaboration in a market of two billion people.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

How was the association process conducted and what are the next steps?

  • Negotiation timeline. Exploratory discussions began in February 2026, formal negotiations started in July 2026, and concluded successfully by November 7, 2026.
  • Formal agreement. The signature of the association agreement is expected in January 2027 after completing necessary procedures on both sides.
  • Financial contribution. As an associated country, India will contribute financially to the Horizon Europe programme and participate on equal terms with EU countries.

Back to basics: International Research Collaboration

International research collaboration involves countries working together on scientific and technological projects to share expertise, resources, and infrastructure. Such cooperation helps address global challenges, accelerates innovation, and fosters diplomatic and economic ties between nations.
