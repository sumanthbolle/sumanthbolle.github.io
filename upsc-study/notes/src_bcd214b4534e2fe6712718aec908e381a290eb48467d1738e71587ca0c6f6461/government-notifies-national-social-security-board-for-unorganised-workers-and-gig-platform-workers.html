Why in news

On 30 October 2026, the Ministry of Labour and Employment notified the constitution of the National Social Security Board for Unorganised Workers and Gig & Platform Workers under the Labour Codes. The Board is intended to recommend, advise, monitor and review welfare administration for these worker categories.

Static anchor

A National Social Security Board is a statutory institutional mechanism under the Code on Social Security, 2020 for recommending and advising on welfare schemes and monitoring social-security administration for unorganised, gig and platform workers.

  • Can improve policy visibility for workers outside the traditional employer-employee relationship by creating a dedicated national institutional forum.

  • Could encourage more responsive social protection for workers facing income volatility, work-related risks and limited access to conventional employment-linked benefits.
  • Effective impact will depend on worker identification, interoperable records, adequate financing, enforceable scheme design and accessible grievance redressal.
