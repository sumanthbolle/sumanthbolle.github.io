Chief Secretary Advocates AI for Inclusive Government Benefit Delivery

The Chief Secretary of Karnataka urged the use of artificial intelligence to ensure government benefits reach all eligible citizens, especially the underprivileged and excluded groups. This highlights the role of AI in promoting inclusive governance and entitlement-based welfare delivery.

AI can transform welfare schemes by identifying excluded and vulnerable groups to deliver government benefits as entitlements rather than applications.

Why is this in the news?

On November 23, 2026, Karnataka's Chief Secretary Shalini Rajneesh emphasized using AI for inclusive delivery of government benefits at the Centre for Smart Governance annual meeting.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What did the Chief Secretary say about AI in governance?

  • AI for inclusivity. The Chief Secretary stated that AI's true promise lies in inclusivity by helping identify underprivileged persons, including those with disabilities and families excluded from housing and guarantee schemes.
  • Shift to entitlement-based delivery. She advocated moving from application-based to entitlement-based delivery of government benefits using AI to ensure rightful beneficiaries receive support.
  • Role of e-governance. She advised the e-governance department to harness AI to identify excluded households and needy families so that benefits reach them as entitlements.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for governance and welfare delivery?

  • Addressing exclusion. AI can help overcome challenges in identifying and reaching marginalized groups often left out of welfare schemes.
  • Improving efficiency. Using AI enables more accurate targeting and reduces dependency on application processes that may exclude eligible beneficiaries.
  • Promoting sustainability. The Chief Secretary emphasized AI as a force for sustainability and good governance by ensuring benefits are inclusive.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Back to basics: Artificial Intelligence in Governance

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to computer systems that can perform tasks requiring human intelligence, such as identifying patterns and making decisions. In governance, AI can analyze large data sets to identify eligible beneficiaries for welfare schemes, reduce errors and exclusion, and improve delivery efficiency. This supports inclusive development by ensuring government benefits reach all rightful recipients.

Prelims MCQ

Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the Chief Secretary advocated moving away from application-based delivery to entitlement-based delivery using AI.

Mains question (15 marks)

Examine how artificial intelligence can enhance inclusive delivery of government welfare schemes.

Examine: Discuss the role of AI in identifying excluded groups and improving entitlement-based welfare delivery.

Intro: Artificial intelligence offers new tools to improve the inclusivity and efficiency of government welfare schemes.

  • AI helps identify underprivileged and excluded groups such as persons with disabilities and families left out of housing and guarantee schemes.
  • It enables a shift from application-based to entitlement-based delivery, ensuring rightful beneficiaries receive benefits without procedural hurdles.
  • AI supports sustainability and good governance by reducing exclusion and improving targeting accuracy.
  • Challenges include data privacy, digital divide, and ensuring AI systems are transparent and accountable.

Conclusion: Harnessing AI in governance can significantly improve inclusive welfare delivery if implemented with safeguards.
