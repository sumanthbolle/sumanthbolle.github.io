India and Belarus Renew Scientific Partnership for Joint Research and Innovation

India and Belarus signed the Second Programme of Cooperation (2027–2029) to strengthen bilateral scientific collaboration through the Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) and the National Academy of Sciences of Belarus (NASB). This partnership aims to promote joint research, innovation, and technology development, relevant for understanding India's science diplomacy and international cooperation.

India and Belarus renewed their scientific partnership through CSIR and NASB to enhance joint research and innovation from 2027 to 2029.

Why is this in the news?

On 16 November 2026, India and Belarus signed the Second Programme of Cooperation between CSIR and NASB during the 12th India–Belarus Inter-Governmental Commission meeting in New Delhi.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

What is the renewed scientific partnership between India and Belarus?

  • Second Programme of Cooperation. India's CSIR and Belarus's NASB signed the Second Programme of Cooperation for 2027–2029 to expand bilateral scientific collaboration and joint research initiatives.
  • Building on previous MoU and projects. This programme builds on the 2023 MoU and the first cooperation phase (2024–2026), which approved seven joint research projects currently in their second year.
  • Key initiatives. The partnership includes launching a Second Joint Call for Research, Development and Innovation proposals from 27 November 2026 to encourage collaborative projects in priority areas.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does this partnership impact bilateral economic and scientific cooperation?

  • Strengthening institutional linkages. The renewed cooperation aims to strengthen linkages between research institutions and scientists of both countries, fostering innovation-driven partnerships.
  • Broader cooperation areas. The 12th India–Belarus Inter-Governmental Commission also discussed expanding cooperation in industry, agriculture, pharmaceuticals, healthcare, tourism, education, culture, science and technology, among others.
  • Business and investment engagement. The India–Belarus Business and Investment Forum encouraged joint ventures, technology partnerships, and investment opportunities in sectors like pharmaceuticals, machinery, digital technologies, and unmanned aerial systems.

Back to basics: Science Diplomacy

Science diplomacy refers to the use of scientific collaborations among countries to address common problems and build constructive international partnerships. It involves joint research, technology exchange, and innovation initiatives that support economic growth and global development. Such diplomacy helps countries leverage their scientific strengths to foster cooperation beyond traditional political and economic ties.
