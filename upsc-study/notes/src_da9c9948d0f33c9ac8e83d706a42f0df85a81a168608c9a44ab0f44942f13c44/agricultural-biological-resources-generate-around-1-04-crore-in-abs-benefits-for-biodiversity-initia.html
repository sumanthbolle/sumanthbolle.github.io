Static anchor

Access and Benefit-Sharing is a biodiversity-governance mechanism that seeks fair and equitable sharing of benefits arising from the utilisation of biological resources, with benefits directed toward conservation and sustainable use.

Background

  • The National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) administers the reported disbursal of ABS benefits to State Biodiversity Boards and Union Territory Biodiversity Councils.
  • The mechanism concerns benefits arising from utilisation of agricultural biological resources, including crops used for research and commercial purposes.
  • The source states that the framework is governed by the Biological Diversity Act, 2002, as amended in 2023, the Biological Diversity Rules, 2024, and the Access and Benefit-Sharing Regulations, 2025.

Way forward

  • Ensure that SBBs and UTBCs use released funds transparently for conservation, sustainable use, documentation, research, capacity building and local biodiversity governance.
  • Improve traceability of biological-resource supply chains so identifiable communities or benefit claimers are not overlooked.
  • Combine area-based allocation with participatory local verification wherever feasible.
