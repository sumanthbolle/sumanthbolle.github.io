Kerala Health Minister on Drug Procurement for Rare Disease Treatment

Kerala's Health Minister K. Muraleedharan announced orders to procure 2,159 bottles of Risdiplam, an oral treatment for spinal muscular atrophy (SMA), under the Kerala United Against Rare Diseases (KARE) scheme. This update is relevant for understanding state-level health governance and drug availability in rare disease management.

Kerala ensures continuous availability of Risdiplam for SMA treatment through government procurement and the KARE scheme.

Why is this in the news?

On October 29, 2026, Kerala's Health Minister addressed the procurement of SMA drug Risdiplam to ensure treatment continuity under the KARE scheme.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What steps has Kerala taken to ensure treatment for spinal muscular atrophy?

  • Drug procurement orders. Kerala government issued orders to procure 2,159 bottles of Risdiplam, the first at-home oral treatment for SMA, to maintain medicine availability.
  • KARE scheme role. The Kerala United Against Rare Diseases (KARE) scheme facilitates timely treatment and life-saving medicines for children affected by SMA.
  • Cost reduction measures. Since October 19, Kerala has been providing a less expensive generic version of Risdiplam, Natsmart, costing around ₹11,000 per bottle, significantly lowering treatment costs.
  • Additional support. Besides medicines, the government provides physiotherapy, respiratory therapy, rehabilitation, and surgery to SMA patients.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for health governance and epidemic response?

  • Ensuring continuous treatment. The government’s commitment to uninterrupted medicine supply reduces financial burden on families and supports life-long treatment for eligible SMA patients.
  • State-level health policy. Kerala’s approach demonstrates how state governments manage rare disease treatment through schemes like KARE and negotiated drug procurement.

Back to basics: Rare Disease Treatment Schemes

Rare diseases affect a small percentage of the population and often require specialized treatment and medicines. Governments may create dedicated schemes to ensure timely access to expensive or hard-to-procure drugs. These schemes also provide supportive therapies and financial aid to reduce the burden on affected families.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about Kerala's SMA drug procurement: 1. Kerala government ordered 2,159 bottles of Risdiplam to ensure availability for SMA treatment. 2. The generic version of Risdiplam provided by Kerala costs around ₹5.5 lakh per bottle. Which of the statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the generic version costs around ₹11,000 per bottle, while ₹5.5 lakh was the price for the original drug before the generic was introduced.
