Digital India BHASHINI Showcases Multilingual AI for Accessible Research

The Digital India BHASHINI Division demonstrated its multilingual AI tools at ETD-2026, IIT Delhi, focusing on making academic research accessible across Indian languages. This is relevant for UPSC aspirants studying e-governance and digital public infrastructure.

BHASHINI uses multilingual AI to bridge language barriers in academic research, supporting over 36 Indian languages and enabling access to theses and scholarly content.

Why is this in the news?

The Digital India BHASHINI Division showcased its multilingual AI solutions at the 29th International Symposium on Electronic Theses and Dissertations (ETD-2026) held at IIT Delhi in October 2026.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are BHASHINI's multilingual AI capabilities?

  • Language support. BHASHINI supports 36 Indian languages for text, 23 Indian languages for voice, and 35 international languages, enabling broad multilingual access.
  • AI tools demonstrated. The division demonstrated voice-first AI, speech-to-text, translation, optical character recognition (OCR), and document translation to aid academic and research communication.
  • Live demonstration. Shrutlekh was showcased for real-time speech transcription with simultaneous translation, useful in lectures, seminars, and conferences.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does BHASHINI enhance access to academic research?

  • Focus on theses and dissertations. BHASHINI aims to extend AI-powered translation to electronic theses and dissertations (ETDs), including lengthy and specialized academic documents.
  • Document translation workbench. BHASHINI Pragati, developed for the Comptroller and Auditor General of India, supports translating large documents of 100 to 500 pages with quality checks and data security.
  • Collaboration with repositories. The division explores adapting workflows for research repositories like Shodhganga and universities to make scholarly material available across Indian languages while preserving structure and terminology.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does BHASHINI matter for digital governance and inclusivity?

  • Inclusive knowledge ecosystem. BHASHINI’s multilingual AI capabilities help universities, libraries, and research repositories reach wider audiences, supporting inclusive access to scholarly knowledge.
  • Citizen contribution. Initiatives like BhashaDaan involve citizens in building language resources through speech and text contributions, enhancing language technology development.
  • Preserving linguistic diversity. Tools like SHABD UDYAN for terminology management and Granthika for digitizing literary works help preserve technical terms and cultural heritage across scripts.

Back to basics: Digital Public Infrastructure and Multilingual AI

Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) refers to foundational digital systems and services that support public needs, such as government websites and e-governance platforms. Multilingual AI involves artificial intelligence technologies designed to process and understand multiple languages, enabling services like translation, speech recognition, and text transcription. Together, they help bridge language barriers and improve access to information in diverse linguistic contexts.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the Digital India BHASHINI Division: 1. It supports 36 Indian languages for text and 23 for voice. 2. BHASHINI Pragati is a tool developed to translate documents up to 50 pages. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because BHASHINI Pragati supports documents ranging from 100 to 500 pages, not up to 50 pages.
