Coal India’s Coal Production and Supply Rise 19% Post-Monsoon

Coal India Limited recorded a 19% year-on-year increase in coal production and supply in October 2026 after the monsoon season, achieving record monthly figures. This matters for UPSC aspirants as it highlights India's energy security dynamics and the operational challenges in coal mining affecting the power sector.

Coal India’s 19% growth in coal production and supply in October 2026 reflects recovery after monsoon disruptions and supports rising power demand.

Why is this in the news?

Coal India announced record-breaking coal production and supply figures for October 2026 on its 51st Foundation Day, highlighting a 19% year-on-year growth post-monsoon recovery.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the key production and supply achievements of Coal India in October 2026?

  • Record coal production. Coal India produced 67.2 million tonnes (MT) of coal in October 2026, surpassing the previous October record of 62.47 MT in 2024, marking a 19% year-on-year increase.
  • Highest coal supply. The total coal supply reached 70.3 MT in October 2026, exceeding the previous record of 69.4 MT set in March 2026, also reflecting a 19% year-on-year growth.
  • Power sector supply milestone. Coal supply to the power sector hit 57.1 MT, breaking the earlier record of 56.5 MT from May 2024, with a 19% increase compared to the previous year.
  • Railway evacuation record. Daily rake loading for coal evacuation to the power sector reached 310 rakes in October 2026, surpassing the previous record of 302.8 rakes per day in March 2025.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why did Coal India’s production increase by 19% in October 2026?

  • Post-monsoon recovery. Mining activity picked up after being dampened by monsoon rains, which typically disrupt coal extraction and supply.
  • Rising power demand. The increase in coal supply was driven by the need to augment coal stocks at thermal power plants amid growing electricity demand.
  • Operational challenges overcome. Despite disruptions from unseasonal rainfall, Puja festivities, and cyclone impacts, Coal India managed to achieve record production and supply.

  • Monsoon impact on economy. The data illustrates how climatic factors like monsoon affect industrial production and resource availability, relevant for geography and economy topics.
  • Infrastructure and logistics. Record railway rake loading highlights the importance of coal evacuation infrastructure in supporting energy supply chains.

Back to basics: Coal mining and energy supply chain

Coal mining is a major source of energy in India, supplying fuel primarily to thermal power plants. Production and supply depend on factors like mining capacity, transportation infrastructure, and seasonal weather conditions such as monsoons which can disrupt operations. Efficient coal evacuation by rail is critical to ensure steady power generation.
