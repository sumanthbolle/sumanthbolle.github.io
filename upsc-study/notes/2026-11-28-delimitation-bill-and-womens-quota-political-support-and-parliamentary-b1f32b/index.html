Political Support and Parliamentary Session for Delimitation Bill

The Nationalist Congress Party (Sharad Pawar) and Congress have issued whips to their MPs to attend a special Parliament session starting November 30, 2026, where the amended Delimitation Bill is expected to be tabled. This session and the bill are significant for UPSC aspirants as they involve changes in parliamentary representation and federal balance.

The special Parliament session from November 30, 2026, will consider the amended Delimitation Bill with key political parties ensuring full attendance and support from their MPs.

What is the significance of the special Parliament session starting November 30, 2026?

  • Session purpose. The session is convened to transact essential government business, including likely tabling of the amended Delimitation Bill.
  • Party whips issued. Both the Nationalist Congress Party (Sharad Pawar) and Congress have issued whips directing their MPs to be present without fail and support the party stand during the session.
  • Session dates. Lok Sabha will meet from November 30 to December 4, 2026, while Rajya Sabha will reconvene on December 2, 2026.

What is the political stance on the Delimitation Bill?

  • NCP(SP) support. NCP(SP) MPs are directed to support the bill, with some leaders like Supriya Sule advocating for a uniform 50 percent increase in Lok Sabha seats across states.
  • Congress position. Congress objects to the proposed increase in seats based on population and clarifies that its past delimitation only changed reserved constituencies without increasing total seats.
  • YSR Congress Party. YSRCP is likely to support the bill if it ensures regional balance.

Back to basics: Delimitation and Parliamentary Whips

Delimitation is the process of redrawing the boundaries of electoral constituencies to reflect population changes, affecting representation in Parliament. Parliamentary whips are directives issued by political parties to their members to attend sessions and vote according to party lines, ensuring discipline and unity in legislative proceedings.
