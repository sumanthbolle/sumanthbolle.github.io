Core Industrial Growth Reaches Historic 7.9% in September 2026

India's core industrial sector recorded a 7.9% growth in September 2026, the highest since the new data series began in April 2024. This growth reflects a strong performance in coal, refinery products, and iron ore sectors, important for understanding industrial trends in the economy.

The core industrial growth rate of 7.9% in September 2026 marks a historic high driven by coal, refinery products, and iron ore sectors.

Why is this in the news?

The Hindu reported on October 21, 2026, that India's core industrial growth hit a historic high of 7.9% in September 2026, the highest since April 2024.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What caused the historic core industrial growth in September 2026?

  • Growth drivers. The 7.9% growth was driven by a turnaround in the coal sector, which grew 10.7%, a 13-month high, along with robust growth in refinery products at 10.3% and strong double-digit growth in iron ore at 18.4%.
  • Other sectors' performance. Electricity grew 11.4%, steel growth quickened to 4.9%, and cement grew 9.8%, while domestic crude oil and natural gas sectors contracted slightly.
  • Context of growth. The growth in coal was aided by a low base from the previous year and low stocks at power plants, contributing to the sequential uptick.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this core industrial growth matter for the economy?

  • Indicator of industrial health. Core industrial growth reflects the performance of key sectors like coal, steel, cement, and refinery products, which are foundational to overall industrial activity.
  • Economic growth implications. A high core sector growth rate signals increased industrial production, which can boost GDP growth and employment opportunities.
  • Policy relevance. Tracking core sector growth helps policymakers assess the effectiveness of industrial and infrastructure policies and make informed decisions.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What should UPSC aspirants remember about the core industrial growth data?

  • Highest growth rate. The 7.9% growth in September 2026 is the highest recorded since the new data series started in April 2024.
  • Sector-specific growth. Coal grew 10.7%, refinery products 10.3%, iron ore 18.4%, electricity 11.4%, cement 9.8%, and steel 4.9% in September 2026.
  • Contraction sectors. Domestic crude oil contracted 1.4%, natural gas contracted 4.6%, and fertiliser sector contracted 11.7% in September 2026.

Back to basics: Index of Core Industries (ICI)

The Index of Core Industries measures the performance of eight core sectors of the economy: coal, crude oil, natural gas, refinery products, fertilisers, steel, cement, and electricity. These sectors together have a significant weight in the overall industrial production and are considered indicators of industrial growth trends in the country. The ICI data helps assess the health of the industrial sector and informs economic policy decisions.
