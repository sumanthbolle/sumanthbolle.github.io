Pakistan’s Role in SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum in New Delhi

Pakistan has nominated its Minister for Climate Change and Coordination to participate in the inaugural SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum held in New Delhi from November 20-22, 2026. This event is significant for understanding India's regional diplomacy and multilateral engagements within the SCO framework.

Pakistan’s participation in the SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum highlights ongoing regional cooperation and diplomatic discourse under the SCO structure.

Why is this in the news?

Pakistan nominated its Minister for Climate Change and Coordination, Musadik Masud Malik, to represent the government at the first SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum in New Delhi from November 20-22, 2026.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum?

  • Inaugural event. The SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum is a new platform organized by the Ministry of Culture and the Ministry of External Affairs, aimed at connecting the ancient civilisations of SCO member countries.
  • Location and timing. The first forum was held from November 20-22, 2026, at the Purana Qila (Old Fort) in New Delhi.
  • Part of SCO structure. This dialogue will continue as a regular part of the SCO institutional framework.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does Pakistan’s participation matter?

  • Pakistan’s representative. Pakistan nominated Musadik Masud Malik, its Minister for Climate Change and Coordination, as its representative at the forum.
  • Diplomatic context. Musadik Masud Malik has been a vocal critic of India’s decision to put the Indus Waters Treaty in abeyance since April 2025 following the Pahalgam terror attack.
  • Significance for regional diplomacy. Pakistan’s participation despite bilateral tensions indicates the forum’s role as a platform for dialogue among SCO members.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the forum’s broader significance for India and SCO?

  • Cultural connection. The forum aims to connect the ancient civilisations of SCO member countries, fostering cultural and civilisational dialogue.
  • Regional cooperation. It represents India’s effort to strengthen regional cooperation and multilateral engagement within the SCO framework.

Back to basics: Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO)

The SCO is a regional intergovernmental organisation focused on political, economic, and security cooperation among member states. It includes countries with shared historical and civilisational ties and serves as a platform for dialogue and collaboration on regional issues.
