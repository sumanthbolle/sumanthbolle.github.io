Israel Launches Large-Scale West Bank Operation After Deadly Hebron Attack

Following a deadly attack that killed two Israeli soldiers near Hebron, Israel ordered a major military operation in the West Bank involving closures, house raids, and demolitions. The UN expressed deep concern over the escalating violence and its humanitarian impact on Palestinians.

Israel's intensified military actions in the West Bank after a deadly attack have raised UN alarm over restrictions and settler violence affecting Palestinians.

Why is this in the news?

On October 23, 2026, Israel launched a large-scale military operation in the West Bank after Palestinian gunmen killed two Israeli soldiers near Hebron, prompting UN alarm over the humanitarian impact.

[1] United Nations News[2] The Hindu · Secondary

What triggered Israel's West Bank operation?

  • Deadly attack near Hebron. Two Israeli soldiers were killed and at least five injured when Palestinian gunmen opened fire near a military checkpoint outside Hebron on October 23, 2026.
  • Israeli response. Prime Minister Netanyahu ordered the sealing of Hebron, house-to-house raids, home demolitions, and warned of a wider military operation not seen since 1967.
  • Hamas reaction. Hamas praised the attack as a 'heroic operation' and called for more attacks, though it did not claim direct responsibility.

[1] United Nations News[2] The Hindu · Secondary

How has the UN reacted to the situation?

  • UN alarm over closures. UN Secretary-General António Guterres expressed deep concern about Israel's sweeping closures in the West Bank, which severely restrict Palestinians' freedom of movement and access to basic services.
  • Condemnation of settler violence. The UN condemned record levels of attacks by Israeli settlers against Palestinians in 2026 and urged Israel to protect Palestinians and hold perpetrators accountable.
  • Humanitarian impact. The UN highlighted that closures during the olive harvest season prevent thousands of Palestinian farmers from accessing their crops, threatening livelihoods.

[1] United Nations News

What are the key features of the Israeli military operation?

  • House raids and demolitions. Israeli forces are conducting house-to-house searches, demolishing homes, and destroying what they call 'terrorist infrastructure' in Hebron.
  • Displacement of residents. The operation involves evacuating residents from neighborhoods linked to the attackers, following a model used in earlier West Bank operations in 2025.
  • Scale of operation. The operation is described as potentially the largest in the West Bank since the 1967 Arab-Israeli war.

[2] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for UPSC aspirants?

  • Regional security implications. The escalation in the West Bank affects regional stability in West Asia, a key area of India's foreign policy interest.
  • Human rights and international law. The UN's concerns highlight issues of occupation, collective punishment, and protection of civilians under international law.
  • India's diplomatic challenges. India must balance its relations with Israel and the Palestinian territories amid rising tensions and violence.

Back to basics: Occupation and Conflict in the West Bank

The West Bank is a territory occupied by Israel since the 1967 Arab-Israeli war. It is home to a large Palestinian population and Israeli settlements. The area is frequently a flashpoint for violence between Israeli forces, settlers, and Palestinians. Military operations often involve closures, raids, and demolitions, which impact civilian life and raise international concerns about human rights and collective punishment.
