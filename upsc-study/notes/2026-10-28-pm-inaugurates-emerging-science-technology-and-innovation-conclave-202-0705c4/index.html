PM Modi inaugurates Emerging Science, Technology and Innovation Conclave 2026

The Prime Minister inaugurated the three-day Emerging Science, Technology and Innovation Conclave 2026 in New Delhi, focusing on India's priorities in emerging technologies and innovation. This event highlights India's shift from technology adoption to original innovation and leadership in critical technologies, relevant for understanding India's science and technology policy and development.

The Conclave showcases India's growing capabilities and confidence in emerging technologies, aiming for self-reliance and global leadership in science and innovation.

Why is this in the news?

Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated the Emerging Science, Technology and Innovation Conclave 2026 on 27 October 2026 in New Delhi, emphasizing India's innovation priorities and technological achievements.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

What is the Emerging Science, Technology and Innovation Conclave 2026?

  • Event overview. The Conclave is a three-day event held from 27 to 29 October 2026 at Bharat Mandapam, New Delhi, bringing together scientists, policymakers, industry leaders, startups, and international experts to deliberate on emerging technologies and India's innovation priorities.
  • Thematic areas. Deliberations cover 12 thematic areas including artificial intelligence, biotechnology, advanced materials, manufacturing, healthcare, energy, climate, and ocean sciences.
  • Exhibition and startups. The Conclave features an extensive exhibition showcasing indigenous scientific achievements and emerging technologies, including around 150 deep-tech startup stalls highlighting technological developments and applications.
  • Global Connect Networking. A new initiative, the Global Connect Networking Event, brings over 300 participants from academia, research institutions, and industry in India and abroad to foster international research partnerships and industry-academia collaboration.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the key messages from PM Modi's address at the Conclave?

  • Shift to innovation leadership. PM Modi highlighted India's transition from adopting foreign technologies to creating original technologies, setting global directions, and solving worldwide challenges.
  • Recent technological achievements. He cited indigenous COVID-19 vaccines, Mars mission success on the first attempt, lunar South Pole landing, and the 500-Megawatt Prototype Fast Breeder Reactor achieving First Criticality as examples of India's scientific progress.
  • Focus on critical technologies. India is advancing in nuclear energy, space technology, AI, semiconductors, and battery storage using domestically available raw materials, aiming for self-reliance and expanding its footprint in critical technologies.
  • Science for society. The Prime Minister emphasized that scientific success is measured by improving ordinary lives and solving large problems in healthcare, agriculture, education, employment, and disaster resilience.
  • Research and innovation culture. He noted a surge in patent filings from fewer than 50,000 ten years ago to nearly 1.5 lakh last year, reflecting a strong culture of research and innovation in India.
  • AI and scientific judgement. PM Modi warned that while AI can provide quick answers and boost productivity, verifying their accuracy remains the responsibility of scientists, and AI should enrich independent thinking.
  • Call for measurable outcomes. He urged participants to set concrete three-year goals for technology development and innovation to build a Developed India and make India a global hub for solutions.

[2] Press Information Bureau[3] Press Information Bureau

Why does this Conclave matter for India's science and technology policy?

  • Focus on self-reliance. The Conclave reflects India's policy emphasis on achieving technological self-reliance and strengthening long-term capabilities in critical sectors like nuclear energy and semiconductors.
  • Promotion of innovation ecosystem. By showcasing startups and fostering international collaborations, the event supports the growth of a vibrant innovation ecosystem linking research, industry, and global partners.
  • National missions and future readiness. PM Modi highlighted ongoing National Missions in semiconductors, quantum technologies, biotechnology, advanced materials, robotics, space, clean energy, and next-generation communications to prepare India for future challenges.
  • Security and safety in emerging tech. The Conclave stresses the importance of safety and security in AI, synthetic biology, autonomous systems, and cybersecurity to protect national systems and serve humanity.

Back to basics: Science and Technology Policy

Science and technology policy refers to government strategies and initiatives aimed at promoting research, innovation, and development of new technologies to support economic growth and societal welfare. It includes fostering an innovation ecosystem, supporting startups, ensuring self-reliance in critical technologies, and addressing safety and security concerns in emerging fields.
