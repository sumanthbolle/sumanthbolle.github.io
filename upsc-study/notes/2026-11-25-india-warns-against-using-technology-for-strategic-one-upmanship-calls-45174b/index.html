India Advocates Inclusive and Responsible AI Governance at UN

India cautioned against using technology for strategic dominance and stressed inclusive governance of AI at the UN Security Council debate on technological change in conflict. This stance highlights India's emphasis on ethical AI use, human control over lethal autonomous weapons, and addressing inequalities in technology access, important for understanding India's science and technology policy.

India called for human-centric, responsible AI governance that respects sovereignty and international law, opposing technology denial and strategic one-upmanship.

Why is this in the news?

India's Permanent Representative to the UN, Ambassador Harish Parvathaneni, spoke at the UN Security Council high-level open debate on Conflict Resolution in an Era of Rapid Technological Change on November 24, 2026.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What was India's position on AI and technology use at the UN debate?

  • Human-centric technology. India emphasized that technology must remain human-centric, serve peace, and not replace human judgment.
  • Opposition to strategic one-upmanship. India warned against the use of technology for strategic dominance through export controls and licensing, which risks treating Global South countries as mere consumers without agency.
  • Inclusive governance. India supports inclusive international cooperation on technology governance that respects national priorities and developmental needs.
  • Ethical use of lethal autonomous weapons. India asserted that responsibility for lethal autonomous weapons must always rest with humans and called for adherence to international law and humanitarian considerations.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does India's stance on AI governance matter for international peace and security?

  • New vulnerabilities from AI. Rapid AI integration in military and intelligence spheres introduces risks and vulnerabilities across conflict cycles.
  • Risks of misuse. India highlighted concerns about AI misuse by terrorists and non-State actors and the dangers of manipulated data undermining decision-making.
  • Respect for sovereignty and law. India stressed that emerging technologies in conflict must respect sovereignty and comply with international law.
  • Protection of civilians. Technology should be leveraged to protect civilians, peacekeepers, and humanitarian personnel.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What challenges and ethical issues did India highlight regarding AI in conflict?

  • Legal and ethical questions. Emerging lethal autonomous weapons raise legitimate legal and ethical concerns that India seeks to address through international frameworks.
  • Human oversight essential. India underscored that human judgment and oversight must remain central in the use of AI-enabled weapons.
  • Avoiding reduction of humans to data. India warned against AI reducing humans to mere data points, emphasizing technology must serve humanity.

Back to basics: Artificial Intelligence Governance

Artificial Intelligence (AI) governance refers to the frameworks and policies that regulate the development, deployment, and use of AI technologies. It aims to ensure AI is used ethically, responsibly, and in ways that respect human rights and international law. Governance includes addressing risks such as bias, misuse, and impacts on security, while promoting inclusive participation of all countries in decision-making.

Answer and trap
