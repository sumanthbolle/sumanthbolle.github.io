Bangladesh Seeks Extradition of Ousted PM Hasina from India

Bangladesh's Law Minister Md Asaduzzaman called former Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina a 'convicted criminal' and announced that Dhaka has formally requested India to extradite her. This development highlights diplomatic and legal challenges in India-Bangladesh relations and the functioning of extradition treaties.

Bangladesh has requested India to extradite former PM Sheikh Hasina, convicted by a Bangladeshi tribunal, underscoring complexities in bilateral legal cooperation.

Why is this in the news?

On November 8, 2026, Bangladesh's Law Minister publicly declared Sheikh Hasina a convicted criminal and confirmed that an extradition request has been sent to India.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What led to the extradition request?

  • Conviction in absentia. Bangladesh's International Crimes Tribunal convicted Sheikh Hasina in November 2025 of crimes against humanity during the 2024 uprising, sentencing her to death on one charge and life imprisonment on another.
  • Hasina's exile. Sheikh Hasina fled to India on August 5, 2024, after a mass uprising ended her 15-year rule, and has since been living in exile there.
  • Government change in Bangladesh. Following the uprising, an interim government led by Muhammad Yunus took charge, and in February 2026, the Bangladesh Nationalist Party formed the government.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How is the extradition process being handled?

  • Legal steps underway. Bangladesh is taking all necessary legal steps to bring Hasina back from India through due process under the extradition treaty between the two countries.
  • Role of ministries. The Law Ministry in Bangladesh is not directly involved; the Ministry of Home Affairs handles the extradition process as it relates to the treaty with India.
  • India's response. India has acknowledged receiving Bangladesh's extradition requests but has not announced any decision, stating that the requests are under examination through established procedures.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for India-Bangladesh relations?

  • Diplomatic sensitivity. The extradition request involves complex diplomatic considerations, as Hasina is a former head of government currently residing in India.
  • Legal and political implications. Handling the request requires balancing legal treaty obligations with political and humanitarian concerns, impacting bilateral trust and cooperation.
  • Media and public reactions. Hasina's virtual press conference from India in August 2026 drew sharp reactions from Dhaka, which was outraged at her media engagement, while India distanced itself from the event.

Back to basics: Extradition Treaty

An extradition treaty is an agreement between two countries to surrender a person accused or convicted of a crime in one country to the other for trial or punishment. It involves legal procedures and diplomatic coordination, ensuring that requests comply with both countries' laws and respect human rights. Extradition is sensitive when political figures or contentious cases are involved, affecting bilateral relations.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the extradition request for Sheikh Hasina: 1. Bangladesh's Law Ministry is directly handling the extradition process with India. 2. India has officially decided to hand over Sheikh Hasina to Bangladesh. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Neither 1 nor 2
  4. Both 1 and 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (c). Statement 1 is incorrect because the Bangladesh Ministry of Home Affairs, not the Law Ministry, is involved in the extradition process; Statement 2 is incorrect as India has only acknowledged receiving the request but not decided on handing her over.

Examine: Discuss the legal, diplomatic, and political challenges in extraditing political leaders between neighbouring countries, using the Bangladesh-India case as an example.

Intro: Extradition of political figures involves complex legal and diplomatic challenges.

  • Legal procedures under extradition treaties require strict adherence to due process and respect for human rights.
  • Diplomatic sensitivities arise due to the political status of the individual and potential impact on bilateral relations.
  • Political considerations, including public opinion and domestic politics in both countries, influence decisions on extradition.

Conclusion: Extradition of political leaders demands balancing legal obligations with diplomatic and political realities to maintain neighbourly relations.
