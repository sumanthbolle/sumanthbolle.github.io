AICC President Mallikarjun Kharge plans to discuss issues related to Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls, delimitation, and women’s reservation with PM Modi on October 21, 2026. Back to basics: Delimitation and Electoral Roll Revision

Delimitation is the process of redrawing the boundaries of electoral constituencies to reflect population changes, ensuring fair representation. Electoral roll revision updates the list of eligible voters to maintain accuracy. Both processes are essential for conducting free and fair elections in a democracy.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements: 1. The Special Intensive Revision (SIR) involves updating electoral rolls to ensure voter accuracy. 2. Delimitation affects the allocation of seats in legislatures based on population changes. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (c). A trap could be confusing SIR with delimitation; SIR updates voter lists, while delimitation redraws constituency boundaries.

Mains question (15 marks)

Discuss the significance of electoral roll revision, delimitation, and women’s reservation in strengthening Indian democracy.

Discuss: Explain how these electoral reforms impact representation, federalism, and gender inclusion in India.

Intro: Electoral roll revision, delimitation, and women’s reservation are key reforms aimed at enhancing democratic representation.

  • Electoral roll revision ensures accurate voter lists, preventing disenfranchisement and electoral fraud.
  • Delimitation adjusts constituency boundaries to reflect demographic changes, maintaining equitable representation and affecting federal balance.
  • Women’s reservation promotes gender inclusion in legislatures, addressing historical underrepresentation and empowering women politically.

Conclusion: Together, these reforms contribute to a more inclusive, fair, and representative democratic process in India.
