Tamil Nadu Considers Supreme Court Review Petition on Karthigai Deepam Dispute

Tamil Nadu government is consulting legal experts on filing a review petition against the Supreme Court's interim order permitting a one-time lighting of the Karthigai Deepam lamp atop Thirupparankundram hill. This case highlights the judiciary's role in religious disputes and Centre-state legal interactions, relevant for understanding judicial review and federalism.

Tamil Nadu is planning a Supreme Court review petition challenging the interim order on lighting the Karthigai Deepam lamp at Thirupparankundram hill.

Why is this in the news?

On November 22, 2026, Tamil Nadu's Energy Resources and Law Minister stated the government is consulting experts about filing a Supreme Court review petition against the interim order on lighting the lamp at Thirupparankundram hill.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the current status of the Karthigai Deepam dispute?

  • Interim Supreme Court order. The Supreme Court stayed the Madras High Court judgment but allowed a limited one-time lighting of the lamp on the stone pillar atop Thirupparankundram hill.
  • State government's response. Tamil Nadu government is consulting legal experts to decide on filing a review petition against this interim order.
  • Opposition views. The Viduthalai Chiruthaigal Katchi (VCK) party opposes implementing the Supreme Court's direction and fears communal tensions.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this dispute matter for judicial review and federalism?

  • Judicial intervention in religious matters. The Supreme Court's order involves balancing religious customs (agama rules) and legal directives, showing judicial activism in sensitive cultural issues.
  • Centre-state legal dynamics. The Tamil Nadu government's move to file a review petition reflects state assertion in response to a Supreme Court order, highlighting federalism and dispute redressal mechanisms.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the concerns raised about the Supreme Court order?

  • Confusing and contradictory order. Advocate S. Vanchinathan described the interim order as contradictory, especially regarding structural alterations and lighting the lamp without damaging the monument.
  • Religious customs ignored. The lamp was traditionally lit at a different temple mandapam as per agama rules, and lighting it on the stone pillar may violate these customs.
  • Potential communal tensions. There are fears that implementing the order could be portrayed as a communal victory, possibly leading to violence.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Back to basics: Judicial Review

Judicial review is the power of courts to examine the constitutionality and legality of legislative and executive actions. It allows the judiciary to uphold fundamental rights and ensure laws comply with constitutional provisions. In India, the Supreme Court plays a key role in adjudicating disputes involving constitutional issues, including those related to religion and federal relations.
