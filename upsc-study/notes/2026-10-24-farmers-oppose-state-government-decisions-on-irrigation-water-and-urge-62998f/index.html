Farmers Oppose State Decision on Almatti Dam Water Release for Irrigation

Farmers in Karnataka's Vijayapura and Bagalkot districts oppose the state government's decision to withhold irrigation water from Almatti and Narayanpur reservoirs for the rabi season, threatening protests. This issue highlights challenges in water resource management affecting agricultural productivity, relevant for UPSC preparation on agriculture and resource distribution.

Farmers have opposed the state's decision to reserve Almatti dam water for drinking and industrial use, demanding irrigation water release to protect rabi crops.

Why is this in the news?

On October 23, 2026, farmers protested against the Karnataka government's decision not to release Almatti dam water for irrigation during the rabi season, citing adverse impacts on crops in Vijayapura and Bagalkot districts.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What decision did the state government take regarding Almatti dam water?

  • Water release withheld. The Irrigation Consultative Committee decided not to release water from Almatti and Narayanpur reservoirs for irrigation during the upcoming rabi season, reserving it exclusively for drinking and industrial purposes due to drought and limited water availability.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why are farmers opposing this decision?

  • Impact on rabi crops. Farmers argue that withholding irrigation water will jeopardize crops already sown for the rabi season, especially in the Upper Krishna Project command area covering Vijayapura and Bagalkot districts.
  • Demand for reassessment. They claim that despite reservoirs holding substantial water stocks earlier, adequate water has not reached agricultural fields or local tanks, and urge authorities to reassess water availability prioritizing farmers' livelihoods.
  • Protest threats. Farmer leaders have threatened protests, including entering Almatti reservoir backwaters, if irrigation water is denied.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the concerns about water allocation priorities?

  • Industrial vs. agricultural use. Farmers question why the state government guarantees water supply to industries but not to farming, criticizing the decision as unjust and harmful to agriculture.
  • Lack of written orders. It was alleged that water was released to Telangana on oral orders from the Chief Minister, without written directives, raising concerns about transparency.

Back to basics: Irrigation and Water Resource Management

Irrigation involves supplying water to agricultural fields to support crop growth, especially during dry seasons. Water resource management includes decisions on allocating limited water among competing uses such as agriculture, drinking, and industry. Effective management is crucial for sustaining agricultural productivity and rural livelihoods.
