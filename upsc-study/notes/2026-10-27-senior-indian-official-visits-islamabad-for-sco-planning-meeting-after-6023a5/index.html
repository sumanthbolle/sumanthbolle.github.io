Back to basics: Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO)

The SCO is a regional intergovernmental organisation founded to promote political, economic, and security cooperation among member states in Eurasia. It includes India, Pakistan, China, Russia, and Central Asian countries. The SCO provides a platform for dialogue and coordination on issues such as terrorism, border security, and economic collaboration.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO): 1. The SCO includes India and Pakistan as member states. 2. The SCO primarily focuses on cultural exchange and has no role in regional security. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the SCO focuses significantly on regional security cooperation, not just cultural exchange.
