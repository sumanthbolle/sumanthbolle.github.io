COP31 in Antalya Focuses on Climate Action Amid Geopolitical Tensions

The 31st Conference of the Parties (COP31) to the UNFCCC is being held in Antalya, Türkiye, with India reaffirming its commitment to climate multilateralism and equity. The summit faces challenges from geopolitical conflicts and financial constraints, making global cooperation on climate action complex.

COP31 highlights the need for equitable climate action, finance obligations by developed countries, and just transition, especially for the Global South.

Why is this in the news?

COP31 opened on November 9, 2026, in Antalya, Türkiye, amid rising climate urgency and geopolitical pressures, with India emphasizing equity and climate finance obligations under the Paris Agreement.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the focus of COP31 and India's stance?

  • India's commitment. India reaffirmed its commitment to climate multilateralism, equity, and the principle of Common But Differentiated Responsibilities and Respective Capabilities (CBDR-RC), calling for a fair and ambitious outcome at COP31.
  • Key themes. The conference focuses on implementation, emphasizing dialogue, consensus, and action to translate commitments into progress, with special attention to just transition and adaptation needs of the Global South.
  • Climate finance. India stressed the importance of developed countries fulfilling their climate finance obligations under Article 9.1 of the Paris Agreement to support developing countries.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the challenges facing COP31?

  • Geopolitical tensions. Ongoing conflicts such as the U.S. war with Iran and Russia’s conflict in Ukraine complicate negotiations and global cooperation.
  • Financial constraints. There is pressure on developed countries to provide predictable climate finance, but officials warn of limited political will for new funding pledges.
  • Climate emergency. Extreme weather events and a strong El Nino forecast heighten urgency, with global temperatures expected to exceed 1.5°C above preindustrial levels.

[2] The Hindu · Secondary

What initiatives and debates are prominent at COP31?

  • Electrification drive. Türkiye promotes raising the share of global energy demand met by electricity from 20% to 35% by 2035, which may cause debate over the role of fossil fuels versus clean energy.
  • Fossil fuel phase-out. Despite a landmark pact at COP28 to transition away from fossil fuels, COP30 ended without explicit fossil fuel mention due to opposition from oil-producing countries; voluntary roadmaps are proposed.
  • Adaptation finance. African countries push for tripling adaptation finance to help poorer nations manage climate impacts like sea walls and drought-resistant crops.

Back to basics: UNFCCC and Paris Agreement

The United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC) is an international treaty to combat climate change by limiting global warming. The Paris Agreement under the UNFCCC sets targets to keep temperature rise below 2°C, preferably 1.5°C, and includes obligations for developed countries to provide financial support to developing countries for mitigation and adaptation.

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the United States did not send its President to COP31; a congressional delegation attended instead, following the US withdrawal from the Paris Agreement.
