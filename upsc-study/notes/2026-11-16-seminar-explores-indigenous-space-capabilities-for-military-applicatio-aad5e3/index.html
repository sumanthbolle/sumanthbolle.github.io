Seminar Highlights Indigenous Space Capabilities for Military Use

The Military College of Electronics and Mechanical Engineering (MCEME), Secunderabad, hosted a seminar on November 14, 2026, focusing on space as a military domain and India's indigenous space capabilities. This event is relevant for UPSC aspirants as it connects science and technology with national security and defence strategy.

MCEME's seminar emphasized strengthening India's self-reliant military space capabilities through collaboration among armed forces, scientific institutions, and industry.

Why is this in the news?

MCEME, Secunderabad, organised a seminar on November 14, 2026, to discuss indigenous military space capabilities and their strategic importance.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What was the seminar about?

  • Seminar theme. The seminar focused on 'Space as a Military Domain' and brought together military officers, academicians, and private sector representatives to discuss the role of space in modern warfare.
  • Key sessions. Sessions covered geopolitical competition and deterrence in space, indigenous military space capabilities, and emerging space technologies like quantum communication and satellite imagery.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does indigenous military space capability matter?

  • Technological self-reliance. Lt. Gen. I.S. Rathore highlighted the need for resilient space capabilities and technological self-reliance through collaboration among the Armed Forces, scientific institutions, and industry.
  • Strategic importance. The seminar aligned with the Indian Army’s Decade of Transformation and the vision of Viksit Bharat 2047, emphasizing space as a critical domain for national security.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How are emerging technologies shaping military space operations?

  • Quantum technology. Quantum technology is being explored to secure space communications, enhancing the security and reliability of military communication channels.
  • Satellite-based surveillance. Indigenous satellites and satellite imagery are crucial for surveillance and intelligence gathering in military operations.

Back to basics: Indigenisation in Science and Technology

Indigenisation refers to developing and producing technology within the country to reduce dependence on foreign sources. In defence, it ensures strategic autonomy by enabling self-reliance in critical technologies like space capabilities. Collaboration among military, scientific institutions, and industry is essential to achieve this goal.
