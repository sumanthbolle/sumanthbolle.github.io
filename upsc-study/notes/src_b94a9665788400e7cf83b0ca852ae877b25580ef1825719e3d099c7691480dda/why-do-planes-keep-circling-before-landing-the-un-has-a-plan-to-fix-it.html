Static anchor

Air-traffic flow management is the anticipatory coordination of aircraft movements with available airport and airspace capacity through shared operational information and flow adjustments.

Background

  • Air-traffic flow management is a coordination approach in which operational actors share information and adjust traffic flows to match available airspace and airport capacity.
  • A holding pattern is an airborne manoeuvre used to keep an aircraft in a controlled route when immediate landing is unavailable; it is not necessarily caused by a technical fault or bad weather.
  • The source describes aviation as a cross-border network in which disruption in one location can propagate to later flights and other airspaces.
