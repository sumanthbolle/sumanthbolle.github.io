India-Egypt Talks to Boost Trade, Infrastructure, and Manufacturing

Union Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal visited Egypt on 15-16 November 2026 to co-chair the 7th India-Egypt Joint Trade Committee and engage in business forums aimed at strengthening bilateral trade, investment, and industrial cooperation. This visit highlights expanding economic ties and cooperation opportunities in sectors like infrastructure, manufacturing, logistics, and regional value chains.

India and Egypt are enhancing their strategic economic partnership through ministerial-level trade talks and business forums focused on infrastructure, manufacturing, and logistics cooperation.

Why is this in the news?

Union Minister Piyush Goyal's official visit to Egypt on 15-16 November 2026 to co-chair the 7th India-Egypt Joint Trade Committee and participate in business forums aimed at boosting bilateral trade and investment.

[2] Press Information Bureau[4] The Hindu · Secondary

What was the purpose of Piyush Goyal's visit to Egypt?

  • Strengthening bilateral ties. The visit aimed to deepen the longstanding India-Egypt partnership by expanding bilateral trade, promoting investment, enhancing industrial cooperation, and strengthening business-to-business engagement.
  • Ministerial-level trade talks. Goyal co-chaired the 7th India-Egypt Joint Trade Committee meeting with Egypt's Minister of Investment and Foreign Trade to review trade progress and discuss diversifying and expanding the bilateral trade basket.
  • Business forums and delegation. A large Indian business delegation of over 130 CEOs and startups participated in the India-Egypt Joint Business Council Meeting and Business Forum to explore joint ventures, technology partnerships, and industrial collaboration.

[2] Press Information Bureau[4] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the key areas of cooperation discussed between India and Egypt?

  • Infrastructure and logistics. Discussions focused on cooperation opportunities in the Suez Canal Economic Zone, ports, logistics, and regional value chains to leverage Egypt's strategic geographical location.
  • Manufacturing and industrial development. Both sides explored collaboration in manufacturing sectors including pharmaceuticals, automobiles, textiles, chemicals, engineering goods, and food processing.
  • Emerging sectors and sustainability. The talks included renewable energy, green hydrogen, transport, and services, reflecting a broad agenda to diversify economic ties.
  • Trade facilitation and market access. Measures to address market access issues, non-tariff barriers, trade remedies, customs cooperation, and MSME participation were discussed to enhance bilateral trade.

[2] Press Information Bureau[4] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the current status of India-Egypt trade relations?

  • Trade growth. Bilateral merchandise trade during April–September 2026 stood at USD 5.04 billion, marking a 64% increase over the same period the previous year.
  • Export-import figures. India's exports to Egypt in FY 2025-26 were USD 3.87 billion, while imports from Egypt were USD 2.60 billion.
  • Major traded goods. India exports meat and poultry products, petroleum products, organic chemicals, cotton, and machinery to Egypt, and imports crude petroleum, fertilisers, cotton, and mineral products.

Back to basics: Bilateral Trade and Economic Cooperation

Bilateral trade involves the exchange of goods and services between two countries, often supported by agreements to reduce tariffs and non-tariff barriers. Economic cooperation can include joint ventures, investment promotion, and collaboration in sectors like manufacturing, infrastructure, and technology. Strategic partnerships enhance political and economic ties, facilitating market access and regional integration.
