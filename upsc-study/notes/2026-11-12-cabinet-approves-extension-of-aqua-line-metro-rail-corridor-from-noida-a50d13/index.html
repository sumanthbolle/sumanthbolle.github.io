Cabinet Approves Extension of Aqua Line Metro Rail Corridor in Noida

The Union Cabinet approved extending the Aqua Line Metro Rail Corridor from Noida Sector-51 to Greater Noida Sector-04, adding 8.449 km and 5 stations. This expansion enhances connectivity, reduces congestion, and promotes economic growth in Noida and Greater Noida, important for urban infrastructure and development topics in UPSC.

The Cabinet approved an 8.449 km extension of the Aqua Line Metro Rail Corridor, adding 5 stations and increasing the Noida metro network to 71.878 km.

Why is this in the news?

On November 11, 2026, the Union Cabinet chaired by the Prime Minister approved the extension of the Aqua Line Metro Rail Corridor from Noida Sector-51 to Greater Noida Sector-04.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What did the Cabinet approve?

  • Extension details. The Cabinet approved an 8.449 km extension of the Aqua Line Metro Rail Corridor with 5 elevated stations, increasing the active metro network in Noida and Greater Noida to 71.878 km.
  • Project significance. This extension is a major expansion of the Noida Metro Rail Project, aimed at improving urban infrastructure and connectivity.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

How will the extension improve connectivity?

  • Integration with key zones. The extension connects commercial hubs, major MNCs and IT offices, educational institutes, healthcare facilities, and residential societies across Noida and Greater Noida.
  • Interchange facility. It provides direct connectivity with high-demand corridors, including an interchange at Noida Sector-61 Blue Line, enhancing seamless travel.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

Why does this extension matter for urban development?

  • Traffic congestion reduction. The metro extension offers an efficient alternative to road transport, expected to alleviate traffic congestion and reduce travel time.
  • Environmental benefits. Expanding the metro network can significantly reduce carbon emissions compared to fossil fuel-based transport.
  • Economic growth. Improved access to transportation hubs and reduced travel times can enhance productivity and stimulate local businesses near new stations.
  • Social impact. The extension promotes equitable access to public transport, benefiting diverse socio-economic groups and improving quality of life.

Back to basics: Metro Rail System

A metro rail system is an urban public transport network using trains running on dedicated tracks, often elevated or underground, to provide fast, efficient, and high-capacity transit. It helps reduce road traffic congestion, lowers pollution, and supports sustainable urban development by connecting key areas within cities.
