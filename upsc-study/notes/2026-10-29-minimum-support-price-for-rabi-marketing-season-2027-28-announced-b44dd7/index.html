Minimum Support Price for Rabi Marketing Season 2027-28 Announced

The Government approved increased Minimum Support Prices (MSPs) for all mandated Rabi crops for the 2027-28 marketing season, with an estimated ₹90,962 crore to be paid to farmers for 324 lakh metric tonnes of produce. This update is important for understanding agricultural price support mechanisms and their role in stabilizing farmer incomes and promoting crop diversification.

The MSP for Rabi crops in 2027-28 ensures remunerative prices with a minimum 50% margin over cost, supporting farmers against price fluctuations and encouraging crop diversification.

Why is this in the news?

On 30 September 2026, the Government approved increased MSPs for all mandated Rabi crops for the Rabi Marketing Season 2027-28.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the Minimum Support Price and why is it important?

  • Price assurance mechanism. MSP provides a guaranteed price to farmers for eligible crops, protecting them from adverse market price fluctuations and ensuring remunerative returns.
  • Supports agricultural planning. By assuring minimum returns, MSP encourages farmers to invest in improved seeds, technology, and inputs, aiding informed agricultural decisions.
  • Stabilizes farm income. MSP reduces uncertainty for small and marginal farmers by providing a fallback price when market prices fall below the announced MSP.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How are MSPs determined and what are the recent changes for Rabi 2027-28?

  • CACP recommendations. MSPs are based on the Commission for Agricultural Costs and Prices' recommendations considering production costs, demand-supply, price parity, and a minimum 50% margin over cost.
  • Rabi 2027-28 MSP increases. MSPs for all mandated Rabi crops were increased, with safflower seeing the highest absolute increase of ₹675 per quintal and wheat having the highest margin over cost at 106%.
  • Estimated payments and produce. For the 2027-28 Rabi season, ₹90,962 crore is expected to be paid to farmers for 324 lakh metric tonnes of produce.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does MSP procurement work and what are recent improvements?

  • Procurement agencies. Cereals and coarse cereals are procured by the Food Corporation of India and state agencies, while pulses and oilseeds are procured under the Price Support Scheme via NAFED and NCCF.
  • Digital platforms and transparency. Platforms like e-Samriddhi and e-Samyukti facilitate farmer registration, procurement, and direct payment, enhancing transparency and reducing intermediaries.
  • Biometric authentication. Mandatory biometric authentication from 2025-26 ensures procurement benefits reach genuine farmers, improving verification and reducing fraud.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What trends show the impact of MSP on agriculture?

  • Rising procurement volumes. Procurement of six Rabi crops increased from 2,302 lakh metric tonnes (2004-05 to 2013-14) to 3,921 lakh metric tonnes (2014-15 to 2025-26).
  • Increasing MSP payments. MSP payments rose from ₹2.65 lakh crore to ₹8.36 lakh crore over the same periods, reflecting expanded government support.
  • Case study of improved farming. A Karnataka millet farmer doubled productivity and income by adopting improved varieties and MSP-based assured procurement.

Back to basics: Minimum Support Price (MSP)

Minimum Support Price is a government-set price at which it purchases crops from farmers to ensure they receive a minimum profit margin over production costs. It acts as a safety net against price volatility in agricultural markets, encouraging farmers to invest in production and secure stable incomes. MSP is announced annually for mandated crops based on cost and market factors.
