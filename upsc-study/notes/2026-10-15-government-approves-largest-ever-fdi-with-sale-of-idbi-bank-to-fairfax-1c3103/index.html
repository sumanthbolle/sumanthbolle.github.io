Government approves largest-ever FDI with sale of IDBI Bank to Fairfax for ₹52,878 crore

The Union Cabinet approved the strategic restructuring of IDBI Bank by selling a combined 60.72% stake held by the government and LIC to Fairfax for ₹52,878 crore, marking the largest foreign direct investment in India's financial sector. This move reduces the government's stake to 15% and LIC's to 19%, while maintaining RBI's regulatory oversight and customer protections.

The government approved Fairfax's ₹52,878 crore acquisition of a 60.72% stake in IDBI Bank, reducing its own shareholding to 15% and marking the largest FDI in India's financial sector.

Why is this in the news?

On October 14, 2026, the Union Cabinet approved the sale of a 60.72% stake in IDBI Bank to Fairfax for ₹52,878 crore, the largest FDI in India's financial sector.

What did the Cabinet approve regarding IDBI Bank?

  • Stake sale approval. The Cabinet approved the strategic restructuring of IDBI Bank by selling a combined 60.72% equity stake held by the government and LIC to Fairfax Financial Holdings for ₹52,878 crore.
  • Change in shareholding. Post-sale, the government's stake will reduce from 45.5% to 15%, and LIC's stake will reduce from 49.2% to 19%.
  • Nature of transaction. The government termed this deal a strategic restructuring rather than a strategic disinvestment.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this sale matter for banking and financial stability?

  • Largest FDI in financial sector. This transaction represents the largest foreign direct investment ever in India's financial sector.
  • Regulatory oversight unchanged. The Reserve Bank of India continues full regulatory and prudential supervision of IDBI Bank despite the change in ownership.
  • Customer protections maintained. Depositors remain insured up to ₹5 lakh, and the bank continues to meet mandatory 40% Priority Sector Lending targets.
  • Financial turnaround. Since becoming a private bank, IDBI Bank's gross non-performing asset ratio fell from 31.8% in September 2018 to 2.3% in March 2026, and it posted a net profit of ₹9,513 crore in 2025-26.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How did IDBI Bank's ownership and financial health evolve before this sale?

  • LIC's earlier intervention. In January 2019, LIC took control by infusing ₹21,624 crore into IDBI Bank, reducing the government's stake below 51%, leading RBI to classify it as a private bank.
  • PCA framework history. IDBI Bank was under RBI's Prompt Corrective Action framework since 2017 due to poor financial health.
  • Improved capital adequacy. The bank's capital adequacy ratio improved from 6.22% in 2018 to 26.65% in March 2026.

Back to basics: Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) in Banking

Foreign Direct Investment refers to investment by a foreign entity in an Indian company, often involving ownership or control. In banking, FDI can bring capital, expertise, and improved governance but is regulated to maintain financial stability and protect depositors. The Reserve Bank of India oversees banks' operations and ensures compliance with prudential norms regardless of ownership changes.
