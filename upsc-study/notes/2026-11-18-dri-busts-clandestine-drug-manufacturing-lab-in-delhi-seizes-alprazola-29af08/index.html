DRI Busts Clandestine Alprazolam Manufacturing Lab in Delhi

The Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI) seized 148 kg of alprazolam intermediate and over 1,046 kg of raw materials from a secret drug lab disguised as a computer shop in Delhi. This bust highlights ongoing efforts to combat illicit drug manufacturing under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act, 1985.

DRI dismantled a clandestine alprazolam manufacturing lab in Delhi, seizing large quantities of drug intermediates and chemicals and arresting one person under the NDPS Act.

Why is this in the news?

On 15–16 November 2026, the DRI conducted an intelligence-led operation in Ghazipur, New Delhi, busting a clandestine alprazolam drug manufacturing lab disguised as a computer shop.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What happened in the DRI drug lab bust?

  • Lab location and disguise. The illicit drug manufacturing lab was operating under the guise of a computer shop in Ghazipur, New Delhi.
  • Seized items. DRI seized 148 kg of alprazolam intermediate in solid and liquid forms, about 1,046 kg of raw materials and chemicals, and 188 litres of additional chemicals used in production.
  • Equipment found. The lab was equipped with reaction apparatus, a water chiller, a vacuum exhaust machine, and other manufacturing equipment.
  • Arrest and legal provisions. One person operating the lab was arrested under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does this bust matter for law enforcement and public health?

  • Alprazolam misuse risks. Alprazolam is a benzodiazepine used medically for anxiety and panic disorders but has misuse, dependence, and diversion risks.
  • Legal status. Alprazolam is a notified psychotropic substance under the NDPS Act, and unauthorized manufacture, possession, or sale is punishable.
  • DRI's intensified focus. This bust follows a similar major synthetic drug lab seizure in Uttar Pradesh, showing DRI's focus on dismantling illicit drug manufacturing networks.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does this bust relate to industrial policy and manufacturing?

  • Clandestine manufacturing challenges. Illicit drug labs mimic legitimate industrial setups, complicating detection and enforcement.
  • Supply chain disruption. Seizing intermediates and chemicals disrupts the illegal drug supply chain and manufacturing capacity.
  • Regulatory enforcement. The NDPS Act regulates psychotropic substances to prevent unauthorized industrial-scale production.

Back to basics: Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985

The NDPS Act is an Indian law that regulates the manufacture, possession, sale, and distribution of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances. It aims to prevent illicit drug trafficking and abuse by imposing strict penalties on unauthorized activities involving controlled substances.
