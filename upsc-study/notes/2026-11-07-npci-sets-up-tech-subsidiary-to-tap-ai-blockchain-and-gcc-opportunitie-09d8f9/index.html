NPCI Launches Tech Subsidiary to Advance AI and Blockchain

The National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) has established a wholly owned subsidiary, NPCI Tech Solutions Limited (NTSL), to enhance its technology capabilities in artificial intelligence, blockchain, and digital financial infrastructure. This move signals NPCI's strategic expansion beyond core payments to support emerging digital public infrastructure initiatives.

NPCI's creation of NTSL aims to strengthen India's digital financial ecosystem through AI, blockchain, and innovation in public digital infrastructure.

Why is this in the news?

On November 6, 2026, NPCI announced the formation of NPCI Tech Solutions Limited to focus on AI, blockchain, and digital financial infrastructure, marking a significant step in its technology expansion.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is NPCI Tech Solutions Limited and its purpose?

  • New subsidiary setup. NPCI has created a wholly owned subsidiary named NPCI Tech Solutions Limited (NTSL) to expand technology capabilities beyond its core payments mandate.
  • Focus areas. NTSL will focus on artificial intelligence, blockchain, digital financial infrastructure, and Global Capability Centre (GCC) services.
  • Support for digital initiatives. The subsidiary will provide technology services for initiatives like eKYC Setu, the Unified Ticketing Platform (UTP), and other Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) programmes.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Who leads NTSL and what is its significance?

  • Leadership appointment. Vishal Kanvaty, associated with NPCI for nearly a decade, has been appointed as the Managing Director and CEO of NTSL.
  • Role of leadership. Kanvaty has led the development and commercialization of several market innovation platforms that align with NTSL’s focus.
  • Strategic importance. NPCI’s Managing Director Dilip Asbe highlighted that NTSL will leverage advancements in AI and emerging technologies to strengthen NPCI Group’s technology capabilities.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does NTSL fit within NPCI’s organizational structure?

  • Subsidiary status. NTSL is the fourth wholly owned subsidiary of NPCI, alongside NPCI International Payments Limited, NPCI Bharat BillPay Limited, and NPCI BHIM Services Limited.
  • Approvals. The formation of NTSL was approved by the NPCI Board and the Reserve Bank of India.

Back to basics: Digital Public Infrastructure

Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI) refers to the foundational digital systems and services that enable public and private sector digital interactions, such as payment systems, identity verification, and ticketing platforms. These infrastructures support inclusive access to digital services and promote efficiency in governance and commerce.
