PM Modi Highlights Tribal Progress Amid Decline of Naxalism and Poverty

Prime Minister Narendra Modi stated that tribal communities have improved their lives by escaping extreme poverty and Naxalism, citing government welfare schemes and development projects. This is relevant for UPSC as it links tribal welfare, internal security, and poverty alleviation policies.

The decline of Maoist violence and targeted government schemes have contributed to improved living conditions and opportunities for tribal communities in India.

Why is this in the news?

On November 15, 2026, PM Modi addressed the 'Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas' programme in Gadchiroli, Maharashtra, marking the 151st birth anniversary of tribal freedom fighter Birsa Munda, highlighting tribal welfare progress and the decline of Maoist violence.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What did PM Modi say about tribal communities and Naxalism?

  • Impact of Maoist violence. Tribal communities suffered the most due to Maoist violence, including denial of voting rights and abduction of children and young women, with previous governments remaining silent.
  • Change after Maoism decline. The elimination of Maoist violence has made tribal areas safer, with tribal mothers and sisters expressing gratitude as children now have better education and employment opportunities.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How has poverty alleviation contributed to tribal development?

  • Reduction in extreme poverty. Nearly 50 crore people have escaped extreme poverty over the past 12-13 years, aided by government welfare schemes and collective societal efforts.
  • Government schemes for tribal welfare. The Centre allocated over ₹1 lakh crore for schemes like PM-JANMAN and Dharti Aaba Janjatiya Gram Utkarsh Abhiyan to improve basic facilities in tribal areas.
  • Infrastructure and healthcare. More than four crore pucca houses were built, electricity extended to 18,000 villages, and over 1.75 lakh Ayushman Arogya Mandirs established, improving living standards and healthcare access.
  • Livelihood support. Collateral-free loans under the MUDRA scheme have been provided to create livelihood opportunities for poor tribal families.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What development initiatives target tribal and Maoist-affected areas?

  • Aspirational districts focus. Special development campaigns are underway in over 100 aspirational districts and 500 aspirational blocks with substantial tribal populations.
  • Agricultural support. The PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana targets 100 districts lagging in agriculture to create better opportunities for farmers and tribal communities.
  • Recent project inaugurations. On Janjatiya Gaurav Diwas 2026, more than 170 tribal development projects worth around ₹30,000 crore were inaugurated, including educational institutions, healthcare facilities, and marketing centres.

Back to basics: Poverty alleviation and tribal welfare schemes

Poverty alleviation involves government programs aimed at improving income and living standards of poor populations. Tribal welfare schemes focus on addressing the unique social, economic, and infrastructural challenges faced by tribal communities, often including housing, education, healthcare, and livelihood support. These schemes are crucial for inclusive development and reducing inequalities.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about tribal welfare and Naxalism as mentioned by PM Modi: 1. The government has allocated over ₹1 lakh crore for tribal development schemes like PM-JANMAN. 2. Maoist violence had no impact on tribal voting rights in affected areas. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because PM Modi stated that Maoists denied tribal communities the right to vote.

Mains question (15 marks)

Intro: Tribal communities in India have historically faced challenges from poverty and internal security threats like Naxalism.

  • Explain the impact of Maoist violence on tribal rights and safety, including denial of voting rights and social disruption.
  • Describe government welfare schemes and infrastructure projects aimed at improving tribal living conditions and livelihoods.
  • Discuss the role of poverty alleviation in tribal development and the reduction of extremist influence.
  • Evaluate the effectiveness of recent development initiatives and the significance of improved security for tribal progress.

Conclusion: Addressing tribal welfare and internal security together is essential for inclusive development and lasting peace.
