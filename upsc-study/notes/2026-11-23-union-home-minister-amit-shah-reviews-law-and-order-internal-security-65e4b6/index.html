Amit Shah Reviews Security and Launches Development Projects in Arunachal Pradesh

Union Home Minister Amit Shah presented the President’s Colour to Arunachal Pradesh Police and launched major development initiatives worth over ₹5,610 crore in Arunachal Pradesh. This visit highlights the strategic importance of the state in internal security, border management, and regional development, relevant for UPSC aspirants studying internal security and Centre-state relations.

Amit Shah’s visit to Arunachal Pradesh focused on enhancing internal security, curbing drug trafficking, and accelerating development in border areas.

Why is this in the news?

On November 21-22, 2026, Union Home Minister Amit Shah reviewed law and order, presented the President’s Colour to Arunachal Pradesh Police, and launched development projects in the state.

What is the significance of the President’s Colour awarded to Arunachal Pradesh Police?

  • Recognition of service. The President’s Colour was awarded to Arunachal Pradesh Police in Itanagar, marking it as the 17th police force in India to receive this honour, which recognises courage, discipline, and professional excellence over more than 15 years.
  • Security role. Arunachal Pradesh Police plays a key role in curbing insurgency, organised crime, drug trafficking, and cybercrime in a strategically sensitive border state sharing 1,863 km of international border.
  • Historical growth. The police force was established in 1971 and has grown to over 16,000 personnel, including armed and reserve battalions, reflecting the state's evolving security needs.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What development initiatives were launched in Arunachal Pradesh?

  • Major schemes launched. Amit Shah launched four major initiatives worth ₹5,610.95 crore, including Mission Shikshit Arunachal – Phase II (₹1,500 crore), Chief Minister’s Comprehensive State Power Development Programme (₹2,000 crore), and Rural Road Development Programme (₹2,000 crore), plus 199 projects under the Vibrant Villages Programme.
  • Cooperative sector focus. The event included unveiling the Arunachal Pradesh Cooperative Vision 2047, distribution of loans and certificates to cooperative societies, and launching an e-commerce webpage to enhance market access for rural products.
  • Infrastructure and welfare. Additional launches included a foodgrain storage warehouse, a Mobile Medical Van, and the SARAS Mela to promote local products and women’s entrepreneurship.

[3] Press Information Bureau[4] The Hindu · Secondary

How does Arunachal Pradesh fit into India’s internal security and drug control efforts?

  • Strategic border state. Arunachal Pradesh shares borders with China, Myanmar, and Bhutan, making its security vital for national interests and preventing infiltration and cross-border crimes.
  • Drug trafficking challenges. Situated near the gateway to the Golden Triangle, the state is critical in preventing narcotics smuggling into India; the police are tasked with ensuring no drugs enter or leave the country through its territory.
  • Operation Monsoon Thunder. The Union Home Minister congratulated the Narcotics Control Bureau and US DEA for their joint success in Operation Monsoon Thunder, which disrupted transnational drug trafficking networks, underscoring India-US cooperation.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

What is the impact of development on border villages and regional security?

  • Vibrant Villages Programme. The Centre’s flagship Vibrant Villages Programme targets 1,954 border villages, with 199 projects worth ₹110.95 crore launched in Arunachal Pradesh to improve healthcare, electricity, education, and drinking water.
  • Reverse migration. Development efforts have led to reverse migration, with about 40 border villages seeing residents return, strengthening the first line of defence along international borders.
  • Cooperative-led economy. The Indian Army and ITBP support border villagers in increasing agricultural production and marketing, with plans for Arunachal Pradesh to become self-sufficient in milk, eggs, fish, and meat through cooperatives.

Back to basics: President’s Colour and Internal Security

The President’s Colour is a prestigious honour awarded to military and police units for distinguished service, symbolising courage and professionalism. Internal security in India involves maintaining law and order, countering insurgency, drug trafficking, and cybercrime, especially in sensitive border states like Arunachal Pradesh, which share long international borders requiring special policing and development focus to ensure national security.

What happens next?

  • Dialogue on Siang Upper Multipurpose Project. The government plans to engage in constructive dialogue with local people on the Siang Upper Multipurpose Project to balance development, energy security, and preservation of local culture with an attractive rehabilitation package.
  • Strengthening drug control cooperation. Further investigations and international cooperation are underway to dismantle drug trafficking networks, tracing masterminds and financial proceeds, building on successes like Operation Monsoon Thunder.
Common mistake. The increase in development funding for Arunachal Pradesh is not just a slogan but a substantial rise in actual expenditure and support compared to previous governments.
Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about Arunachal Pradesh Police and development initiatives: 1. Arunachal Pradesh Police received the President’s Colour for its service spanning over 15 years. 2. The Vibrant Villages Programme launched 199 projects worth over ₹5,600 crore across Arunachal Pradesh. Which of the statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 incorrectly states the total value of Vibrant Villages Programme projects as over ₹5,600 crore, but the actual figure is ₹110.95 crore; the ₹5,610 crore figure refers to total development initiatives launched.
