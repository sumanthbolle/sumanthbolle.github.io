Social Justice Ministry's IITF Pavilion and SHRESHTA National Entrance Test 2027

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment showcased indigenous artisans at the 45th IITF 2026, recording ₹29.56 lakh in sales, highlighting economic opportunities for marginalized communities. Concurrently, the Ministry opened online registration for the National Entrance Test for SHRESHTA 2027, aiming to expand quality residential education for meritorious Scheduled Caste and De-notified Tribe students.

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment promotes socio-economic empowerment through artisan market linkages at IITF and educational access via SHRESHTA NETS 2027.

Why is this in the news?

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment's pavilion at the 45th IITF 2026 recorded significant sales by promoting indigenous artisans, while online registration opened for SHRESHTA NETS 2027 to facilitate quality education access for marginalized communities.

What was achieved at the Ministry's pavilion at IITF 2026?

  • Sales and growth. The Ministry's pavilion recorded cumulative sales of approximately ₹29.56 lakh as of November 17, 2026, marking a 70% increase from the previous day and more than fivefold growth since November 15.
  • Artisan participation. Over 130 artisans from 19 states showcased handicrafts, handlooms, textiles, jewellery, and other indigenous products through the Ministry’s three Apex Corporations: NSFDC, NBCFDC, and NSKFDC.
  • Economic empowerment. The pavilion facilitated direct interaction between artisans and consumers, promoting entrepreneurship and socio-economic empowerment of Scheduled Castes, Backward Classes, Safai Mitras, and De-notified, Nomadic and Semi-Nomadic Tribes.
  • TULIP brand promotion. The Ministry promoted the TULIP (Traditional Artisans’ Upliftment Livelihood Programme) brand to create a distinct market identity for artisan-made products and enhance recognition among domestic and international consumers.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the SHRESHTA Scheme and the National Entrance Test for SHRESHTA (NETS)?

  • Scheme objective. SHRESHTA aims to provide quality education to meritorious Scheduled Caste and eligible De-notified Tribe students by facilitating access to reputed private residential schools.
  • Registration details. Online registration for NETS 2027 opened on November 14, 2026, and will remain open until November 30, 2026, with a correction window from December 2 to 4, 2026.
  • Test schedule and portal. The National Entrance Test for SHRESHTA is scheduled for January 10, 2027, and candidates can apply through the official National Testing Agency portal.
  • Educational empowerment. The scheme seeks to bridge educational disparities, promote social mobility, and empower students from targeted communities to realize their academic potential.

[3] Press Information Bureau

How do these initiatives contribute to social justice and empowerment?

  • Inclusive development. Both initiatives promote inclusive socio-economic development by supporting marginalized communities through economic opportunities and quality education.
  • Government collaboration. The Ministry works with Apex Corporations and the National Testing Agency to strengthen market linkages and educational access for disadvantaged groups.
  • Alignment with national vision. These efforts align with the vision of ‘Sabka Saath, Sabka Vikas’ and Viksit Bharat@2047, focusing on self-reliance and empowerment of Scheduled Castes, Backward Classes, Safai Mitras, and De-notified Tribes.

What is the significance of the Departmental Summit on Social Justice and Empowerment?

  • Summit details. The two-day summit held on November 18-19, 2026, brought together political leaders and officials from States to discuss social justice and empowerment, including persons with disabilities.
  • Key themes. The summit deliberated on Nasha Mukt Bharat, One Nation One Scholarship portal, Early Intervention and Reduction of Disability Burden, and Sugamya Bharat for Viksit Bharat.
  • Cooperative federalism. It aimed to strengthen Centre-State collaboration, share best practices, and improve governance and service delivery in social justice sectors.

Back to basics: Social Justice and Empowerment Schemes

Social justice schemes aim to uplift marginalized communities such as Scheduled Castes, Backward Classes, and De-notified Tribes by providing economic opportunities and access to education. These schemes often involve targeted interventions like financial support, skill development, and educational scholarships to promote inclusive growth and reduce disparities. The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment coordinates such initiatives to foster socio-economic empowerment and social inclusion.
