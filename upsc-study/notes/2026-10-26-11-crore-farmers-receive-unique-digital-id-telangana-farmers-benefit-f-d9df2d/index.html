11 Crore Farmers Receive Unique Digital ID Under Digital Agriculture Mission

The Union Agriculture Ministry created 11 crore unique digital identity cards for farmers by October 23, 2026, five months ahead of schedule. This digital ID aims to simplify farmers' access to government schemes, bank credit, crop insurance, and disaster relief, reducing paperwork and delays.

The Digital Agriculture Mission issued 11 crore unique farmer IDs to improve transparency and ease of access to agricultural services.

Why is this in the news?

On October 23, 2026, the Union Agriculture Ministry announced the creation of 11 crore unique digital IDs for farmers under the Digital Agriculture Mission.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the unique digital ID for farmers and its purpose?

  • Digital ID creation. The Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers’ Welfare created 11 crore unique digital identity cards for farmers by October 23, 2026, ahead of the March 31, 2027 deadline.
  • Purpose of the ID. The digital ID aims to enable transparent, efficient, and timely access to government schemes, bank credit, crop insurance, and disaster relief for farmers.
  • Benefits. It reduces paperwork and repeated verification, simplifying farmers' access to key services and improving transparency and accountability in agricultural service delivery.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Who implemented the Digital Agriculture Mission and how was the milestone achieved?

  • Implementing body. The Digital Agriculture Mission is implemented by the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers’ Welfare in coordination with State governments and field-level officials.
  • Achievement. The milestone of creating 11 crore farmer IDs was achieved five months ahead of schedule due to coordinated efforts.
  • Minister's statement. Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan described the Farmer ID as an important step towards making agriculture more efficient, transparent, and prosperous through modern technology.

Back to basics: Digital Public Infrastructure for Agriculture

Digital public infrastructure refers to technology platforms and digital tools provided by the government to facilitate services efficiently. In agriculture, such infrastructure includes digital IDs for farmers that help streamline access to government schemes, credit, insurance, and disaster relief by reducing paperwork and enabling transparency.
