Cabinet Sub-Committee Plans Department-wise Action for El Nino Drought

The Telangana Cabinet Sub-Committee chaired by Deputy Chief Minister M. Bhatti Vikramarka resolved to prepare a detailed department-wise plan to address drought conditions caused by El Nino. This plan focuses on water supply, electricity, agricultural support, and rural employment to mitigate hardships during drought.

Telangana's Cabinet Sub-Committee is preparing a comprehensive plan to tackle El Nino-induced drought by coordinating efforts across departments to safeguard water, power, and agriculture.

Why is this in the news?

On October 23, 2026, Telangana's Cabinet Sub-Committee on Drought and El Nino conditions met to review the drought situation and plan government measures.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the Cabinet Sub-Committee's mandate on El Nino drought?

  • Committee composition. The Sub-Committee is chaired by Deputy Chief Minister M. Bhatti Vikramarka and includes ministers and senior officials to coordinate drought response.
  • Focus areas. It aims to prepare a department-wise action plan focusing on water supply for drinking and irrigation, uninterrupted electricity, agricultural support, and rural employment.
  • Assessment and planning. Officials are tasked with scientifically assessing drought conditions, identifying drought-affected mandals, and preparing advance impact assessments to enable timely assistance.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How are drought-affected areas identified and monitored?

  • Drought criteria. 358 mandals were provisionally identified as drought-affected based on deficient rainfall and crop coverage below 50%.
  • Monitoring mechanisms. District Collectors are instructed to conduct weekly review meetings and release weekly reports on groundwater levels, temperatures, and other indicators.
  • Emergency funds. ₹1 crore each has been released to District Collectors as emergency funds, with a proposal for ₹7,000 crore special allocation for drought-related works.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for disaster management and climate adaptation?

  • El Nino impact. El Nino causes drought conditions affecting agriculture, water availability, and power supply, requiring coordinated government action.
  • Proactive governance. Scientific assessment and inter-departmental coordination help minimize drought impact and ensure timely delivery of assistance.
  • Public awareness. Regular data dissemination to the public and farmers helps prepare communities for drought challenges.

Back to basics: El Nino and drought management

El Nino is a climatic phenomenon that disrupts normal weather patterns, often causing droughts by reducing rainfall. Drought management involves assessing affected areas, ensuring water and power supply, supporting agriculture, and providing rural employment to reduce hardship. Coordinated government response across departments is essential for effective disaster mitigation.
