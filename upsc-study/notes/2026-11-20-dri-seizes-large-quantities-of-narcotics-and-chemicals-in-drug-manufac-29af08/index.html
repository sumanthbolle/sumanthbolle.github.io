DRI Busts Major Narcotics Labs and Seizes Large Drug Consignments

The Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI) recently seized large quantities of alprazolam intermediates and heroin in separate operations in Delhi and Chennai, arresting suspects involved in illicit drug manufacturing and trafficking. These actions highlight India's efforts to combat narcotics production and cross-border drug smuggling, relevant for internal security and law enforcement.

DRI's seizures of alprazolam intermediates and heroin demonstrate intensified enforcement against illicit drug manufacturing and international trafficking in India.

Why is this in the news?

In November 2026, the DRI seized 148 kg of alprazolam intermediate and over 1,046 kg of chemicals in Delhi, and over 71 kg of heroin in Chennai destined for smuggling to Sri Lanka.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

What happened in the DRI's recent drug busts?

  • Alprazolam lab busted. DRI uncovered a clandestine alprazolam manufacturing lab disguised as a computer shop in Ghazipur, Delhi, seizing 148 kg of alprazolam intermediate and over 1,046 kg of raw materials and chemicals, and arrested one person.
  • Heroin smuggling foiled. In Chennai, DRI intercepted 71.24 kg of heroin being smuggled to Sri Lanka via the coastal route, arrested two traffickers, and seized the vehicles used for transport.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

Why do these seizures matter for internal security and law enforcement?

  • Disrupting illicit drug supply. The operations removed large quantities of narcotics and precursor chemicals from illegal channels, disrupting drug manufacturing and trafficking networks.
  • Cross-border trafficking challenge. The heroin seizure highlights the use of coastal routes for smuggling drugs to neighbouring countries like Sri Lanka, posing a regional security challenge.
  • Intelligence-led enforcement. These busts demonstrate DRI's enhanced intelligence gathering, surveillance, and operational readiness to tackle narcotics crimes.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

What legal provisions govern these actions?

  • NDPS Act enforcement. All seizures and arrests were made under the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985, which penalises unauthorised manufacture, possession, sale, and distribution of narcotics.
  • Alprazolam classification. Alprazolam is a benzodiazepine-class psychotropic substance notified under the NDPS Act, used medically but prone to misuse and illicit diversion.

Back to basics: Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985

The NDPS Act regulates the manufacture, possession, sale, transport, and consumption of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances in India. It aims to prevent drug abuse and trafficking by imposing strict penalties for violations. Enforcement agencies like the DRI use this Act to seize illegal drugs and arrest offenders involved in illicit drug operations.
