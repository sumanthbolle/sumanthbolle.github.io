India implications

  • Health-system capacity must extend beyond testing to treatment initiation, follow-up, nutrition and prevention.
  • Technology can improve outreach and prioritisation, but implementation should preserve human oversight and avoid exclusion of digitally or geographically marginalised groups.
  • Community participation can reduce stigma and improve the reach of public-health interventions.
  • Domestic resource mobilisation for TB can improve programme ownership and resilience.

Way forward

  • Combine active case finding with reliable confirmatory diagnosis and prompt treatment initiation.
  • Design screening and digital tools around vulnerable populations, with human oversight and non-digital access routes.
  • Track treatment continuity, adherence, nutrition and follow-up alongside detection figures.
  • Strengthen community participation while protecting patients from stigma and exclusion.
