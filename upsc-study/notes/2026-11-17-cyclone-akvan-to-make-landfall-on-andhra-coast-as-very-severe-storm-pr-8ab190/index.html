Cyclone Akvan to hit Andhra coast as very severe storm on November 20

Cyclone Akvan is forecast by the India Meteorological Department to make landfall between Nellore and Tuni in Andhra Pradesh as a very severe cyclonic storm on November 20, 2026. This event is significant for UPSC aspirants due to its implications for disaster management, coastal vulnerability, and climate impact on the region.

Cyclone Akvan will strike Andhra Pradesh’s coast between Nellore and Tuni as a very severe storm with winds up to 130 kmph on November 20, requiring extensive disaster preparedness.

Why is this in the news?

On November 16, 2026, the India Meteorological Department forecast Cyclone Akvan to intensify and make landfall on Andhra Pradesh’s coast between Nellore and Tuni by November 20 as a very severe cyclonic storm.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the forecast and path of Cyclone Akvan?

  • Formation and movement. Cyclone Akvan formed as a deep depression over the Bay of Bengal and is moving north-northwestwards at about 6-8 kmph.
  • Intensification stages. It is expected to intensify into a cyclonic storm on November 16 evening, a severe cyclonic storm by November 17 evening, and a very severe cyclonic storm by November 20.
  • Landfall location and timing. The cyclone is forecast to make landfall between Nellore and Tuni on the Andhra Pradesh coast by noon on November 20, with maximum sustained winds of 120-130 kmph gusting to 145 kmph.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does Cyclone Akvan matter for disaster management?

  • Coastal vulnerability. The cyclone threatens nearly half of Andhra Pradesh’s 1,053-km coastline, including vulnerable districts like Krishna where Machilipatnam is located.
  • Preparedness measures. District administrations have been placed on high alert, offshore operations suspended, and fishermen advised to stay ashore until November 20.
  • Previous cyclone impacts. Recent cyclones Arnab and Murasu caused significant damage to fisheries and coastal areas, highlighting the need for timely warnings and compensation mechanisms.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the implications of Cyclone Akvan for climate and society?

  • Recurring cyclone threats. Frequent cyclones in the Bay of Bengal region stress coastal communities and fisheries, affecting livelihoods especially during low sales periods like Karteeka Masam.
  • Economic losses. Cyclone Arnab caused ₹307 lakh in fisheries damage in Visakhapatnam district, with compensation decisions pending.
  • Environmental conditions. Sea conditions are expected to be rough to very rough until November 17, worsening with the cyclone’s intensification.

Back to basics: Cyclones and Disaster Management

A cyclone is a large-scale air mass that rotates around a strong center of low atmospheric pressure, often causing heavy rain and strong winds. Disaster management involves preparedness, mitigation, response, and recovery efforts to reduce the impact of such natural hazards on human life and property. Coastal regions are particularly vulnerable to cyclones due to storm surges and flooding.

Mains question (15 marks)

Discuss the challenges and strategies in managing cyclones along the Andhra Pradesh coast, with reference to Cyclone Akvan.

Discuss: Explain the difficulties faced in cyclone management and the measures taken to mitigate their impact, using Cyclone Akvan as an example.

Intro: Cyclones pose significant risks to coastal Andhra Pradesh, requiring effective disaster management.

  • Challenges include frequent cyclone occurrences, high wind speeds, and vulnerable coastal populations dependent on fisheries.
  • Preparedness involves early warnings, suspension of offshore activities, and evacuation advisories for fishermen.
  • Post-cyclone response includes damage assessment, compensation for losses, and infrastructure repair.
  • Climate change may increase cyclone intensity, necessitating improved forecasting and resilient infrastructure.

Conclusion: Effective cyclone management combines timely warnings, community preparedness, and post-disaster recovery to reduce losses.
