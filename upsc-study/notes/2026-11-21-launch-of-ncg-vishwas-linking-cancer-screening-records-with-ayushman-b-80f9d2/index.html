Launch of NCG VISHWAS Linking Cancer Screening with Ayushman Bharat

The Union Health Secretary launched NCG VISHWAS, an AI-enabled patient education initiative by the National Cancer Grid, emphasizing linking cancer screening records with the Ayushman Bharat Health Account (ABHA) for continuity of care. This integration leverages India's digital health infrastructure to improve cancer awareness, screening, treatment affordability, and data-driven healthcare delivery.

NCG VISHWAS integrates cancer screening records with ABHA IDs to ensure continuity and affordability of cancer care under Ayushman Bharat.

Why is this in the news?

On 21 November 2026, the Union Health Secretary launched NCG VISHWAS at the National Cancer Grid Annual Meeting, highlighting its linkage with Ayushman Bharat Health Account for cancer care continuity.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is NCG VISHWAS and its purpose?

  • Patient education initiative. NCG VISHWAS (Videos to Support Health, Wellness, Awareness and Self-care) is an AI-enabled patient education program covering breast, cervical, and oral cancers in multiple languages, providing reliable information on prevention, diagnosis, treatment, and long-term care.
  • Dissemination and reach. The initiative will be shared through the National Cancer Grid’s network of over 400 cancer centres and healthcare partners, including community health workers like ASHA and Anganwadi workers.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does linking with Ayushman Bharat Health Account help?

  • Digital health integration. Linking screening records with ABHA IDs under the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission enables interoperable health records, supporting continuity of care across primary, secondary, and tertiary facilities.
  • Scale of implementation. Around 99 crore ABHA IDs have been created, with about 100 crore health records linked, facilitating longitudinal health records accessible with patient consent.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the scale and impact of cancer screening under national programmes?

  • Screening coverage. Over 153 crore screenings have been conducted for oral (37.1 crore), breast (17.4 crore), and cervical (9.7 crore) cancers at 1.87 lakh Ayushman Arogya Mandirs across India.
  • Awareness and affordability. Awareness campaigns like Swasth Nari Sashakt Parivar Abhiyaan reached 11 crore footfalls, while financial support worth approximately ₹27,000 crore has been provided for cancer care under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does the initiative support cancer care and research?

  • Collaboration and research. NCG encourages member hospitals to partner with the Pan-AIIMS Research Consortium to use patient-consented data for advancing cancer care and research.
  • Infrastructure strengthening. Efforts include expanding comprehensive primary healthcare packages, establishing Day Care Cancer Centres at district hospitals, technology-enabled radiology services, and strengthening oncology at 22 new AIIMS.

Back to basics: Digital Health Records and Ayushman Bharat

Digital health records enable storing and sharing patient health information electronically for better continuity and quality of care. The Ayushman Bharat Health Account (ABHA) is a unique digital ID that links health records across healthcare providers, facilitating seamless access and management of patient data with consent. This supports evidence-based healthcare delivery and integration of various health services.
