Supreme Court Orders Stricter Front-of-Package Nutrition Labelling

The Supreme Court directed the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) to tighten front-of-package nutrition labelling (FoPNL) norms, focusing on total sugars and saturated fats as nutrients of concern. This ruling impacts consumer protection and food safety regulations, relevant for governance and public health topics in UPSC.

The Supreme Court mandated that FoPNL must highlight total sugars, saturated fat, and salt separately with standardized pictorial warnings to help consumers make informed dietary choices.

Why is this in the news?

On November 26, 2026, the Supreme Court directed the FSSAI to enhance front-of-package nutrition labelling standards for packaged foods.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What changes did the Supreme Court direct for front-of-package nutrition labelling?

  • Nutrients of concern. The Court ordered that FoPNL should focus on 'total sugars' and 'saturated fat' instead of 'added sugar' and 'added fat', alongside salt, as key nutrients of concern.
  • Separate hexagons for nutrients. Each nutrient—fat, sugar, and salt—must have its own hexagon on the label with standardized pictorial representations and corresponding words like 'High Fat', 'High Sugar', or 'High Salt'.
  • Threshold levels revision. The FSSAI was directed to revisit and reformulate threshold levels that define when a nutrient is considered 'high'.
  • Label clarity for beverages. For sweetened beverages below threshold limits, no label resembling the warning for high nutrient content should be displayed to avoid consumer confusion.
  • Monitoring additives. The FSSAI must closely monitor product reformulation concerning artificial preservatives and additives following FoPNL implementation.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this Supreme Court order matter for UPSC aspirants?

  • Consumer protection and public health. The order strengthens consumer rights by ensuring clearer nutritional information, aiding healthier food choices and addressing public health concerns.
  • Regulatory governance. It highlights the role of judiciary in enforcing food safety regulations and overseeing regulatory bodies like the FSSAI.
  • Policy implementation challenges. The Court emphasized strict adherence to timelines and comprehensive stakeholder consultation, reflecting governance and policy execution issues.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How will the FSSAI implement these directions?

  • Single-phase rollout. The FSSAI plans to implement the FoPNL regime in a single phase, finalizing draft regulations after a 60-day public and WTO stakeholder consultation.
  • Timeline adherence. The Supreme Court mandated that the FSSAI take pre-emptive and necessary steps to ensure the regulations come into force within the proposed timeline.

Back to basics: Front-of-Package Nutrition Labelling (FoPNL)

FoPNL is a graphical nutritional information system displayed on the front of packaged food products. It helps consumers quickly identify high levels of nutrients of concern such as sugar, fat, and salt, enabling informed and healthier food choices. This labelling complements detailed nutritional information usually found on the back or side of packaging.
