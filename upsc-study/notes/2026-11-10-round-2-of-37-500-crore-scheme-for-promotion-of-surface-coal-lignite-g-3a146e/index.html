Round 2 of ₹37,500 Crore Coal Gasification Scheme Ends; Round 3 Begins

The Ministry of Coal concluded Round 2 of the ₹37,500 crore Scheme for Promotion of Surface Coal/Lignite Gasification Projects with two applications from NTPC Limited. Round 3 opened on 10th November 2026, aiming to boost coal gasification capacity to 100 million tonnes by 2030.

The government is promoting surface coal and lignite gasification through a ₹37,500 crore scheme to enhance domestic production of industrial products and meet energy transition goals.

Why is this in the news?

On 10th November 2026, the Ministry of Coal announced the conclusion of Round 2 and the opening of Round 3 of the ₹37,500 crore Scheme for Promotion of Surface Coal/Lignite Gasification Projects.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the Scheme for Promotion of Surface Coal/Lignite Gasification Projects?

  • Scheme approval and purpose. The Union Cabinet approved the scheme on 13th May 2026 with a financial outlay of ₹37,500 crore to accelerate development of surface coal and lignite gasification projects in India.
  • Focus on industrial products. The scheme supports production of critical industrial products like Urea, Methanol, and Dimethyl Ether (DME) using coal gasification technology.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What happened in Round 2 and Round 3 of the Scheme?

  • Round 2 applications. Round 2 concluded with two applications from NTPC Limited for producing Urea, Methanol, and DME through coal gasification.
  • Round 3 launch. Round 3 opened on 10th November 2026, inviting eligible entities to submit proposals until 11th January 2027.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does this Scheme matter for India's energy and industrial goals?

  • Boosting domestic resource use. The scheme diversifies the use of India's indigenous coal and lignite resources for industrial production, reducing import dependence.
  • Target for coal gasification capacity. It aims to achieve 100 million tonnes of coal gasification capacity by 2030, supporting energy transition and industrial growth.

Back to basics: Coal Gasification

Coal gasification is a process that converts coal or lignite into synthetic gas (syngas), which can be used to produce chemicals like methanol and fertilizers like urea. It allows the use of domestic coal resources to create cleaner fuels and industrial products, supporting energy diversification and reducing reliance on imports.
