12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum 2026 Strengthens Parliamentary Dialogue

The 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum held in New Delhi focuses on enhancing parliamentary cooperation among member and partner countries, discussing key issues like AI governance and South-South cooperation. This forum is significant for UPSC aspirants as it highlights India's role in global parliamentary diplomacy and multilateral governance reforms.

The 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum advances parliamentary dialogue and cooperation, guided by India's 2026 BRICS Chairship priorities.

Why is this in the news?

What is the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum and its focus?

  • Forum's role. The 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum aims to strengthen parliamentary dialogue and cooperation among BRICS member and partner countries.
  • Key discussion topics. The forum discussed institutional resilience, AI governance, South-South cooperation, and sustainability, guided by the four pillars of India's 2026 BRICS Chairship.

How does the forum enhance parliamentary diplomacy?

  • Parliamentary exchanges. The forum facilitates strengthening parliamentary exchanges and capacity-building among member and partner countries, including cooperation in healthcare and pharmaceuticals.
  • Use of technology. Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla highlighted efforts to make parliamentary proceedings more accessible and people-centric through technology like Sansad Bhashini.

What are the geopolitical and bilateral contexts of the forum?

  • Cuba's participation. Cuba, a BRICS partner country, used the forum to seek support for its UN General Assembly resolution against the US economic blockade.
  • India-Russia ties. India and Russia marked the 80th anniversary of bilateral ties with parliamentary-level exchanges reviewed amid global developments.

Back to basics: Parliamentary Diplomacy

Parliamentary diplomacy involves interactions and cooperation between the legislatures of different countries to promote mutual understanding, peace, and development. It complements traditional diplomacy by enabling dialogue on governance, policy, and international issues through parliamentary forums and exchanges.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum 2026: 1. It focused on discussions about institutional resilience, AI governance, and South-South cooperation. 2. The forum was chaired by the Lok Sabha Speaker Shri Om Birla who also highlighted the use of Sansad Bhashini technology. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the forum is under India's BRICS Chairship, but it is not chaired by the Lok Sabha Speaker; he highlighted technology use but did not chair the forum.

Mains question (15 marks)

Discuss the role of parliamentary diplomacy in strengthening international cooperation, with reference to the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum 2026.

Discuss: Explain how parliamentary diplomacy contributes to international cooperation using the example of the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum.

Intro: Parliamentary diplomacy complements traditional diplomacy by fostering dialogue among legislatures to address global challenges.

  • The 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum enhanced cooperation among member and partner countries on issues like AI governance and sustainability.
  • It facilitated parliamentary exchanges and capacity-building, promoting mutual understanding and collaboration.
  • The forum also provided a platform for geopolitical dialogue, such as Cuba seeking support against economic blockades and India-Russia bilateral ties.
  • Technological initiatives like Sansad Bhashini improve accessibility and transparency in parliamentary functioning, strengthening democratic accountability.

Conclusion: Thus, parliamentary diplomacy through forums like BRICS strengthens international cooperation and global governance.
