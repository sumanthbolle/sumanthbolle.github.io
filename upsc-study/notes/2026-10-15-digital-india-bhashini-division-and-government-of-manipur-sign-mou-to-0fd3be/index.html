Digital India BHASHINI and Manipur Partner to Advance Multilingual AI

The Digital India BHASHINI Division signed an MoU with the Government of Manipur to develop multilingual AI and language technology, aiming to make digital services more accessible in local languages. This collaboration highlights India's focus on inclusive digital governance and the promotion of linguistic diversity through technology.

The BHASHINI-Manipur MoU advances multilingual AI to enhance digital service accessibility in diverse Indian languages.

Why is this in the news?

On 14 October 2026, the Digital India BHASHINI Division and the Government of Manipur signed a Memorandum of Understanding to collaborate on multilingual AI and language technology during the BHASHINI Rajyam Workshop in Imphal.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the BHASHINI-Manipur MoU about?

  • Purpose of MoU. The MoU aims to strengthen cooperation in language technology, improve language resources, and explore multilingual digital service solutions for Manipur.
  • Parties involved. The agreement was signed by Shri Amitabh Nag, CEO of Digital India BHASHINI Division, and Shri Somorjit Salam, IAS, Secretary of Information Technology, Government of Manipur.
  • Workshop context. The MoU was a key outcome of the BHASHINI Rajyam Workshop, which focused on advancing multilingual AI and integrating Indian language solutions into government and citizen-facing digital services.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does this collaboration matter for digital governance?

  • Language-first digital infrastructure. BHASHINI provides a Language-First Digital Public Infrastructure that supports multilingual AI models, datasets, APIs, and applications to enable accessible digital services across languages.
  • Addressing language barriers. The initiative aims to make digital services accessible and meaningful by overcoming language barriers, ensuring citizens can confidently use government platforms in their native languages.
  • Linguistic diversity focus. Manipur's linguistic diversity, including Manipuri and several tribal languages, is leveraged to develop language technologies that reflect regional communication needs.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How is BHASHINI advancing multilingual AI technology?

  • Technology components. BHASHINI integrates language datasets, AI models, APIs, and applications to support over 36 Indian languages for text and 23 for voice, powering more than 800 government websites.
  • Real-time translation demo. A live demonstration of English-to-Manipuri translation showcased the potential of language technology to bridge communication gaps.
  • Community involvement. The BHASHINI Mitra initiative encourages state institutions and language communities to contribute language data to strengthen multilingual AI resources.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the next steps in this partnership?

  • Use case identification. DIBD and the Government of Manipur will identify priority use cases and assess language-data requirements for integrating BHASHINI technologies into government platforms.
  • Collaboration with experts. The partnership will work with language experts and local communities to develop and validate AI solutions that reflect Manipur's linguistic diversity and public service needs.

Back to basics: Digital Public Infrastructure and Multilingual AI

Digital Public Infrastructure refers to foundational digital systems and services that enable accessible and inclusive digital interactions for citizens. Multilingual AI involves artificial intelligence technologies designed to understand and process multiple languages, helping bridge communication gaps in diverse linguistic contexts. Together, they support government services that can be accessed in various regional languages, enhancing inclusivity and participation.

What happens next?

  • Expanding language resources. The collaboration plans to strengthen language datasets and validate AI models with inputs from language experts and local communities to improve real-world usefulness.
  • Integration into government platforms. Future efforts will focus on integrating BHASHINI’s language technologies into relevant government and citizen-facing digital services to enhance accessibility.
