US Secretary Blinken affirms India's right to self-defence and zero tolerance for terrorism

Former US Secretary of State Antony Blinken stated that the US must respect India's right to defend itself and maintain a zero-tolerance policy towards terrorism, highlighting the fundamentally different nature of US relations with India and Pakistan. This statement is important for understanding the strategic partnership between India and the US and their cooperation on counter-terrorism.

Antony Blinken emphasized the US respect for India's self-defence rights and zero tolerance for terrorism, underscoring the strategic US-India partnership distinct from US-Pakistan relations.

Why is this in the news?

On November 14, 2026, former US Secretary of State Antony Blinken publicly affirmed the US stance supporting India's right to self-defence and zero tolerance towards terrorism during a summit in New Delhi.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What did Antony Blinken say about US-India relations and terrorism?

  • Respect for self-defence. Blinken stated that the US must respect India's right to defend itself and maintain a zero-tolerance policy towards terrorism.
  • Different relations with Pakistan. He described US relations with Pakistan as regional and fundamentally different from the global nature of US-India ties.
  • Counter-terrorism cooperation. Blinken emphasized the importance of counter-terrorism cooperation with both India and Pakistan, while supporting India's efforts to respond to attacks and prevent escalation.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for India's foreign policy and security?

  • Strategic partnership. Blinken highlighted the transformation of US-India ties from estrangement to a strategic partnership based on shared interests and people-to-people connections.
  • Technology cooperation. He identified emerging technologies such as space, quantum technology, and artificial intelligence as key areas for future cooperation.
  • Managing regional tensions. The US approach includes diplomatic efforts to prevent escalation between India and Pakistan, both nuclear-armed neighbors with destructive potential.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does Blinken view India’s relations with other countries?

  • India-Russia ties. Blinken acknowledged the historical depth of India-Russia relations and stated that the US does not expect India to sever those ties.
  • US domestic and global challenges. He noted rapid technological change, inequality, and shifting power dynamics as global challenges affecting US policy and cooperation.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Back to basics: Counter-terrorism and strategic partnerships

Counter-terrorism involves coordinated efforts by countries to prevent and respond to terrorist activities. Strategic partnerships are long-term alliances between countries based on shared interests such as security, economic cooperation, and technology. India and the US have developed a strategic partnership that includes cooperation on security and counter-terrorism, distinguishing it from US relations with other regional countries.

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (c). A careless reader might confuse the nature of US relations with Pakistan as global instead of regional, which Blinken explicitly differentiated.
