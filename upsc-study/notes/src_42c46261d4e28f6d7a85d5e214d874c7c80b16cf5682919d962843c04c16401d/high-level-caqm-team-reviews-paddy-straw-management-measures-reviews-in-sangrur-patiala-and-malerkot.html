In-situ crop-residue management treats or incorporates paddy straw in the field, whereas ex-situ management removes or collects it for utilisation outside the field, such as industrial or energy-related use. Way forward

  • Ensure CRM machinery is available at the right time, in usable condition and through arrangements responsive to farmer constraints.
  • Build local collection, aggregation and transport systems where ex-situ utilisation is feasible.
  • Link industrial users and biomass facilities with predictable residue-supply arrangements while maintaining applicable environmental compliance.
  • Use hotspot-based field monitoring and publicly accountable district action plans.
