RBI Announces ₹33,000 Crore Government Securities Auction for November 20, 2026

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) announced an underwriting auction for the sale of government securities worth ₹33,000 crore on November 20, 2026. This auction is important for government debt management and monetary policy operations.

RBI's auction of ₹33,000 crore government securities on November 20, 2026, reflects ongoing government borrowing and debt management strategies.

Why is this in the news?

RBI announced the underwriting auction for government securities worth ₹33,000 crore to be held on November 20, 2026.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

What is the RBI government securities auction announced for November 20, 2026?

  • Auction details. The Government of India will sell government securities worth ₹33,000 crore through an underwriting auction conducted by RBI on November 20, 2026.
  • Security types and amounts. The auction includes ₹19,000 crore of 6.53% GS 2031 and ₹14,000 crore of 7.71% GS 2066 securities.
  • Underwriting commitments. Each Primary Dealer (PD) has a Minimum Underwriting Commitment (MUC) of ₹453 crore for GS 2031 and ₹334 crore for GS 2066, with the same minimum bidding commitment under Additional Competitive Underwriting (ACU) auction.
  • Bidding process. Primary Dealers submit bids electronically via RBI’s e-Kuber system between 9:00 AM and 9:30 AM on the auction day.
  • Commission payment. The underwriting commission is credited to the PDs' current accounts with RBI on the day the securities are issued.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

Why does this government securities auction matter for UPSC aspirants?

  • Government borrowing. The auction reflects the government's method of raising funds to meet its fiscal deficit and expenditure requirements.
  • Debt management. Understanding such auctions helps explain how government debt is managed and rolled over through securities issuance.
  • Monetary policy interface. Auctions influence liquidity and interest rates, linking fiscal operations with RBI’s monetary policy.

Back to basics: Government Securities Auction

Government securities are debt instruments issued by the government to borrow money from the public. Auctions are the process through which these securities are sold to investors, including Primary Dealers, who bid to buy them at competitive prices. These auctions help the government raise funds to finance its fiscal deficit and manage public debt. The Reserve Bank of India conducts these auctions and manages the bidding process electronically.
