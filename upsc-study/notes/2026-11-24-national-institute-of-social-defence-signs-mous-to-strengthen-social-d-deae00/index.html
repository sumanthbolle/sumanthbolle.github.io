NISD Signs 23 MoUs to Strengthen Social Defence Initiatives Across India

The National Institute of Social Defence (NISD) under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment signed 23 Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs) with universities and institutions nationwide to enhance social defence efforts. This expansion of partnerships aims to improve training, research, capacity building, and advocacy in areas like drug abuse prevention, senior citizen welfare, transgender welfare, and prevention of beggary.

NISD has expanded its institutional collaborations to 30 partners through 23 new MoUs to advance social defence initiatives for vulnerable groups.

Why is this in the news?

On 21-22 November 2026, the National Institute of Social Defence (NISD), under the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, exchanged 23 MoUs with universities and institutions across India to strengthen social defence initiatives.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

What are the recent developments in NISD's institutional partnerships?

  • MoU exchange event. NISD held an MoU exchange ceremony in New Delhi where 23 MoUs were signed with universities and institutions, increasing its total partnerships to 30.
  • Participation details. Out of the 23 institutions, 15 participated in person while 8 joined virtually, reflecting broad geographic and institutional representation.
  • Institutions involved. Partner institutions include State Institutes of Rural Development from Jharkhand, Meghalaya, Manipur, Telangana; Central Universities of Andhra Pradesh and Karnataka; National Law Universities; and other reputed universities and training institutes.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

What is the scope and significance of these MoUs?

  • Areas covered. The MoUs focus on prevention education, research, training, capacity building, curriculum development, outreach, and advocacy in social defence fields such as drug abuse prevention, welfare of senior citizens, transgender welfare, and prevention of beggary.
  • Expected outcomes. These partnerships aim to facilitate institutional collaboration, knowledge exchange, training opportunities, and development of effective interventions for vulnerable populations.
  • Strengthening social inclusion. The initiative supports rehabilitation, empowerment, and social inclusion of individuals affected by substance use, transgender persons, senior citizens, and those vulnerable to beggary.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

Why do these partnerships matter for social welfare and policy?

  • Role of educational institutions. Educational institutions are crucial in preventing drug abuse by organizing awareness activities and anti-drug pledges, which help spread the message from campuses to communities.
  • Capacity building and training. Regular coordination, monitoring, and short-term training programs between NISD and partner institutions are emphasized to strengthen collaborative efforts in drug abuse prevention.
  • Government commitment. The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment’s commitment to institutional collaboration aims to create a socially secure, inclusive, and empowered India.

Back to basics: Social Defence

Social defence refers to government and institutional efforts aimed at protecting vulnerable groups in society through prevention, rehabilitation, and welfare measures. It includes initiatives to prevent substance abuse, support senior citizens, empower transgender persons, and prevent beggary. Effective social defence requires collaboration between government bodies, educational institutions, and civil society to provide training, awareness, research, and capacity building.
