Moody’s Raises India’s FY27 GDP Growth Forecast to 7%

Moody’s has revised India’s GDP growth forecast for fiscal year 2026-27 to 7% due to broad-based economic momentum and resilience to energy supply shocks. This forecast is important for UPSC aspirants as it reflects India’s macroeconomic health and influences policy and development discussions.

Moody’s projects India’s FY27 GDP growth at 7% driven by strong consumption, investment, and energy shock resilience.

Why is this in the news?

On November 11, 2026, Moody’s revised India’s GDP growth forecast for FY27 to 7% citing broad-based momentum and resilience to energy supply shocks.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the reasons behind Moody’s upward revision of India’s FY27 GDP growth forecast?

  • Broad-based economic momentum. Moody’s attributes the upward revision to robust consumption, improving investment dynamics, and strong macroeconomic fundamentals.
  • Energy supply shock resilience. India’s ability to diversify crude and gas supplies and buffer consumers through state-backed oil marketing companies and tax cuts helped it adjust better to energy price shocks.
  • Manufacturing and export strength. India’s large refining capacity limits fuel supply disruptions and supports its role as a major exporter of refined petroleum products.
  • Private investment prospects. High frequency indicators like capital goods production, industrial credit growth, and capacity utilisation suggest a new private capital expenditure cycle may be starting.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the risks and challenges to India’s growth outlook?

  • Inflation pressures. Household consumption remains vulnerable due to intensifying price pressures with inflation near the upper end of the RBI’s 2-6% target range.
  • Agricultural output concerns. A deficient southwest monsoon and strengthening El Nino conditions are expected to weigh on agricultural output and rural demand.
  • Monetary policy impact. Further rate hikes by the RBI could delay recovery in private investment, potentially slowing growth.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does Moody’s FY27 growth forecast matter for UPSC aspirants?

  • Indicator of economic health. The forecast reflects India’s macroeconomic fundamentals and growth potential, relevant for questions on economic development and policy.
  • Policy implications. Understanding growth drivers and risks helps in analysing government strategies for inclusive growth and employment generation.
  • Global economic positioning. India’s growth relative to other G20 economies influences its global economic role and bilateral relations.

Back to basics: GDP Growth Forecasting

GDP growth forecasts estimate the expected increase in a country’s economic output over a fiscal year. They are based on factors like consumption, investment, government spending, and external trade. These forecasts guide policymakers and investors by indicating economic health and potential challenges.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about Moody’s FY27 GDP growth forecast for India: 1. Moody’s raised India’s GDP growth forecast to 7% due to broad-based economic momentum and energy supply shock resilience. 2. The forecast assumes no impact from inflation or monsoon variability on growth. Which of the statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because Moody’s forecast acknowledges risks from inflation pressures and deficient monsoon affecting growth.
