C-DAC and Bank of India Collaborate on Indigenous Quantum Technologies for Banking

On 22 October 2026, C-DAC and Bank of India signed an MoU to jointly develop and implement quantum and emerging technologies for banking applications. This collaboration aims to prepare the banking sector for future technological challenges and enhance security using indigenous innovations.

C-DAC and Bank of India are jointly advancing indigenous quantum technologies to create a secure and future-ready financial ecosystem.

Why is this in the news?

The Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology announced on 23 October 2026 that C-DAC and Bank of India signed an MoU on 22 October 2026 to collaborate on quantum financial innovation and emerging technologies in banking.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the nature of the collaboration between C-DAC and Bank of India?

  • MoU signing. C-DAC, an autonomous scientific society under MeitY, and Bank of India signed a Memorandum of Understanding on 22 October 2026 to collaborate on quantum financial innovation and emerging banking technologies.
  • Scope of collaboration. The partnership includes joint research, development, pilot projects, technology demonstrations, and capacity-building initiatives focused on technologies relevant to banking and financial services.
  • Technology areas. The collaboration covers High Performance Computing, Artificial Intelligence, Quantum Technologies, Blockchain, Cybersecurity, and indigenous technology development.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why is this collaboration significant for the banking sector?

  • Quantum ecosystem development. A key focus is building a Quantum Ecosystem for Financial Innovation to prepare banking systems for opportunities and security challenges posed by quantum technologies.
  • Security and resilience. The collaboration aims to explore quantum-inspired and quantum-safe technologies to enhance security, trust, resilience, and decision-making in banking.
  • Future readiness. The partnership seeks to develop roadmaps for adopting emerging technologies, demonstrating proofs of concept, and building a future-ready talent ecosystem through training programs.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the broader technology applications covered under the MoU?

  • Beyond quantum. The MoU also includes collaboration on Blockchain, Artificial Intelligence, and other emerging technologies relevant to financial services.
  • Potential use cases. Applications include intelligent document processing, customer and employee assistance, fraud analysis, credit and financial analytics, secure AI platforms, smart contracts, digital identity, and secure data sharing.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Who are the key stakeholders and what are their roles?

  • C-DAC. C-DAC provides expertise in indigenous quantum and emerging technologies, aiming to translate research into practical banking solutions.
  • Bank of India. Bank of India contributes banking domain expertise, a nationwide presence, and a high-volume technology environment to test and implement innovations.
  • Government support. The Ministry of Electronics and IT supports the collaboration, emphasizing the need for a quantum-resilient financial ecosystem amid growing cybersecurity concerns.

Back to basics: Quantum Technologies in Banking

Quantum technologies use principles of quantum mechanics to process information in ways that classical computers cannot, potentially enhancing computing power and security. In banking, quantum computing and quantum-safe cryptography can improve cybersecurity, data analysis, and decision-making. Indigenous development ensures technological sovereignty and resilience against emerging threats.
