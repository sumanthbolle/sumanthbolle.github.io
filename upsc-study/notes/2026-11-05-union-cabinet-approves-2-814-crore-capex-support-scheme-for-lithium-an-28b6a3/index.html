Union Cabinet Approves ₹2,814 Crore Scheme for Lithium and Nickel Processing

The Union Cabinet approved a ₹2,814 crore Capex Support Scheme to promote domestic processing of critical minerals lithium and nickel over five years. This scheme aims to reduce import dependence, strengthen supply chain resilience, and support sectors like electric mobility and defence.

The government’s ₹2,814 crore Capex Support Scheme will fund lithium and nickel processing plants to build domestic critical mineral value chains and reduce import reliance.

Why is this in the news?

On 4 November 2026, the Union Cabinet chaired by the Prime Minister approved the Capex Support Scheme to Promote Critical Mineral Processing with an outlay of ₹2,814 crore for lithium and nickel processing.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the Capex Support Scheme for critical mineral processing?

  • Scheme approval and duration. The Union Cabinet approved the scheme on 4 November 2026, with a financial outlay of ₹2,814 crore over five years from FY 2026-27 to FY 2030-31.
  • Objective. The scheme aims to develop domestic processing capabilities for lithium and nickel to add value within India, reduce import dependence, and build resilient supply chains.
  • Target minerals and plants. It targets processing lithium and nickel ores/intermediates into high purity compounds, supporting sectors like electric mobility, battery energy storage, electronics, defence, and speciality steel.
  • Financial support. Capital subsidy of 30% of eligible investment will be provided for setting up four processing plants: two lithium plants (minimum 30 kt capacity each) and two nickel plants (minimum 50 kt capacity each).
  • Capital support caps. Maximum capital support is capped at ₹500 crore per lithium beneficiary and ₹900 crore per nickel beneficiary.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau[3] Press Information Bureau

How are beneficiaries selected and supported?

  • Two-stage selection process. Applicants first undergo technical qualification; only technically qualified applicants proceed to financial bidding where two beneficiaries per mineral are selected based on the lowest capital expenditure support sought.
  • Disbursement linked to milestones. Incentives are disbursed in three tranches linked to project milestones: 25% subsidy after 25% CAPEX spent and 15% equity contributed, 50% subsidy after 50% CAPEX spent and 50% equity contributed, and 100% subsidy upon plant commissioning with full equity.
  • Commissioning timeline. Beneficiaries must commission plants within three years of the Letter of Award, with a maximum of five years allowed; delays beyond three years trigger a tapering mechanism on remaining support.

[1] Press Information Bureau[3] Press Information Bureau

Why does domestic critical mineral processing matter for India?

  • Strategic and economic importance. Processing capacity is vital for national security, economic sovereignty, and achieving decarbonisation targets by supporting clean energy, defence, aerospace, and speciality steel sectors.
  • Reducing import dependence. India currently depends heavily on imports for refined intermediates and high-purity materials due to limited domestic processing infrastructure.
  • Supply chain resilience. Global processing is concentrated in few countries, exposing supply chains to geopolitical risks, price volatility, and export restrictions.
  • Investment barriers. High capital costs, limited technology availability, and raw material access constrain commercial-scale projects without targeted government support.

[1] Press Information Bureau[3] Press Information Bureau

What are the expected outcomes and governance of the scheme?

  • Investment and capacity creation. The scheme is expected to anchor about ₹10,000 crore investment and create 60 kt lithium and 100 kt nickel processing capacity by 2030.
  • Value addition and import substitution. Processing ores and intermediates domestically will increase value capture within India and reduce the import bill.
  • Employment generation. The scheme will generate direct and indirect employment in critical mineral processing and related manufacturing.
  • Governance framework. A three-tier governance structure includes an Apex Governing Council chaired by the Secretary of Mines, a Techno-Financial Committee for evaluation, and a Project Management Agency for implementation.

Back to basics: Critical Minerals and Their Processing

Critical minerals are essential raw materials used in advanced technologies, clean energy, defence, and electronics. Processing these minerals involves converting ores and concentrates into high-purity compounds suitable for manufacturing. Domestic processing reduces dependence on imports, mitigates supply chain risks, and adds economic value within the country.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the Capex Support Scheme to Promote Critical Mineral Processing: 1. It provides a 30% capital subsidy for setting up lithium and nickel processing plants. 2. The maximum capital support is capped at ₹900 crore per lithium beneficiary and ₹500 crore per nickel beneficiary. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 reverses the capital support caps; ₹500 crore is the cap for lithium beneficiaries and ₹900 crore for nickel beneficiaries.
