RBI Releases Reserve Money, Money Supply Data and Announces Treasury Bill Auctions

The Reserve Bank of India released data on reserve money and money supply for the fortnight ended October 15, 2026, and announced auctions for 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day treasury bills. This update is important for understanding monetary policy operations and government debt management relevant to UPSC Economy syllabus.

RBI's fortnightly data on reserve money and money supply, along with treasury bill auctions, reflect key monetary policy and fiscal management tools.

What data did RBI release on reserve money and money supply?

  • Reserve Money Data. RBI released data on reserve money for the fortnight ended October 15, 2026, which reflects the base money circulating in the economy.
  • Money Supply Data. RBI also released data on money supply for the same fortnight, indicating the total currency and demand deposits in the economy.

What treasury bill auctions did RBI announce?

  • Auction Details. RBI announced auctions for 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day treasury bills with notified amounts of ₹8,000 crore, ₹8,000 crore, and ₹7,000 crore respectively, totaling ₹23,000 crore.
  • Auction Schedule and Participation. The auctions are scheduled for October 28, 2026, with settlement on October 29, 2026; participation includes state governments, provident funds, foreign central banks, and retail investors via the Retail Direct portal.
  • Auction Method. The auction uses a price-based multiple price method with competitive and non-competitive bidding windows.

Back to basics: Monetary Policy and Treasury Bills

Monetary policy involves managing money supply and liquidity to control inflation and support economic growth. Reserve money is the base money created by the central bank, while money supply includes currency and deposits circulating in the economy. Treasury bills are short-term government securities issued to meet temporary funding needs and manage public debt.

Mains question (10 marks)

Explain the role of Reserve Bank of India’s monetary data releases and treasury bill auctions in monetary policy and fiscal management.

Explain: Explain how RBI’s data on reserve money and money supply, along with treasury bill auctions, reflect and influence monetary policy and fiscal operations.

Intro: RBI’s monetary data releases and treasury bill auctions are key tools for monetary policy and fiscal management.

  • Reserve money data indicates liquidity and base money created by RBI, influencing inflation and credit conditions.
  • Money supply data reflects the total currency and deposits circulating, showing the broader liquidity in the economy.
  • Treasury bill auctions are short-term government borrowing instruments that help manage public debt and influence money market rates.
  • Together, these tools help RBI and the government coordinate monetary and fiscal policies to maintain economic stability.

Conclusion: Thus, RBI’s data releases and auctions are essential for understanding and implementing monetary and fiscal policy.
