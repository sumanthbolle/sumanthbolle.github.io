Union Health Minister inaugurates Advanced Radiotherapy Facility at AIIMS Rajkot

The Union Health Minister inaugurated a ₹39 crore Advanced Radiotherapy Facility at AIIMS Rajkot to improve cancer care access for 1.54 crore people in Saurashtra. This development highlights government efforts to strengthen tertiary healthcare and cancer screening under national health policies.

The Advanced Radiotherapy Facility at AIIMS Rajkot enhances specialised cancer treatment access for the Saurashtra region under the Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana.

Why is this in the news?

On 2 November 2026, the Union Health Minister inaugurated the Advanced Radiotherapy Facility at AIIMS Rajkot, marking a key step in expanding cancer care infrastructure.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the Advanced Radiotherapy Facility at AIIMS Rajkot?

  • Facility details. The facility, established with an investment of approximately ₹39 crore under the Pradhan Mantri Swasthya Suraksha Yojana, is equipped with advanced radiation therapy technologies including a High Energy Linear Accelerator, Wide Bore CT Simulator, and High Dose Rate Brachytherapy Unit.
  • Treatment capabilities. It supports advanced radiotherapy techniques such as IMRT, VMAT, IGRT, SRS, SRT, and SBRT, enabling precise radiation delivery for cancers like head and neck, gynaecological, breast, bone, and soft tissue malignancies.
  • Integrated cancer care. The Department of Radiation Oncology operates within a multidisciplinary cancer care framework, providing radiotherapy, chemotherapy, and surgical services within a single institution.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Who benefits from this facility and why does it matter?

  • Regional impact. The facility serves an estimated 1.54 crore people across Saurashtra, including rural and semi-urban populations in districts like Rajkot, Jamnagar, Junagadh, and others, reducing the need for patients to travel to distant cities for specialised treatment.
  • Patient load and accessibility. It is expected to manage 500–700 cancer cases annually, providing affordable or free treatment to eligible Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY beneficiaries and subsidised care to others.
  • Addressing cancer burden. Cancer imposes significant medical, emotional, social, and financial burdens; timely and local access to quality treatment improves patient outcomes and reduces hardship.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does this facility fit into broader health policies and initiatives?

  • National Health Policy 2017. The facility aligns with the National Health Policy 2017’s goal of comprehensive healthcare including preventive, promotive, curative, rehabilitative, and palliative services aiming for Universal Health Coverage.
  • Cancer screening efforts. Since 2018, extensive screening initiatives have been conducted: 37.7 crore oral cancer screenings, 44 crore each for hypertension and diabetes, 17.7 crore women for breast cancer, and 9.8 crore women for cervical cancer.
  • Financial protection. Expansion of Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY has improved timely access to treatment by reducing financial constraints for economically vulnerable patients.
  • Healthcare infrastructure expansion. India has expanded AIIMS institutions from one to 18 operational centres, strengthening tertiary healthcare access nationwide.

Back to basics: Healthcare infrastructure and cancer care

Healthcare infrastructure includes facilities, equipment, and services that provide medical care to populations. Cancer care involves prevention, early detection, diagnosis, treatment, and rehabilitation. Radiotherapy is a treatment using targeted radiation to destroy cancer cells, often requiring advanced technology for precision. National health policies aim to improve access, affordability, and quality of such care for all citizens.
