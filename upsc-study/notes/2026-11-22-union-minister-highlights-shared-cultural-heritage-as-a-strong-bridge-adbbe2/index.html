Union Minister Highlights Shared Cultural Heritage Connecting SCO Nations

The Union Minister of Culture and Tourism, Shri Gajendra Singh Shekhawat, emphasized the role of shared civilizational knowledge in strengthening ties among Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) member states during the SCO Civilizational Dialogue Forum held in New Delhi from November 20 to 22, 2026. This event is significant for UPSC aspirants as it showcases India's cultural diplomacy and efforts to foster regional cooperation through cultural dialogue within a major Eurasian multilateral grouping.

Shared cultural heritage among SCO nations serves as a bridge for mutual understanding and cooperation, as highlighted by India's Union Minister of Culture.

Why is this in the news?

The SCO Civilizational Dialogue Forum, hosted by India in New Delhi from November 20 to 22, 2026, focused on cultural expressions, shared heritage, and historical trade relations among SCO member states, with Union Minister Shri Gajendra Singh Shekhawat chairing key sessions.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What was the purpose of the SCO Civilizational Dialogue Forum?

  • Forum objective. The Forum aimed to strengthen cultural ties among SCO member states, promote mutual understanding and cooperation through shared civilizational heritage, and create new opportunities for cultural dialogue.
  • Participants and themes. It brought together ministers, senior representatives, academicians, historians, and experts from SCO member states and dialogue partners to deliberate on cultural expressions, art and architecture, and historical trade relations.
  • Knowledge partner. The Indira Gandhi National Centre for the Arts (IGNCA) served as the knowledge partner for the event.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How did the Union Minister describe the role of shared cultural heritage?

  • Historical connections. Union Minister Shri Gajendra Singh Shekhawat stated that historical connections have played a significant role in bringing countries closer and highlighted the potential of cultural cooperation in fostering mutual understanding, trust, and friendship.
  • Living traditions. He emphasized that civilisations are living traditions whose knowledge continues to illuminate the present and inspire future generations, not just legacies of the past.
  • Collective efforts. He urged harnessing shared civilizational knowledge through dialogue, institutional partnerships, cultural exchanges, and meaningful cooperation to advance collective efforts among SCO nations.
  • Vedic prayer inspiration. The deliberations were rooted in the spirit of the eternal Vedic prayer: 'Om Sangachchhadhwam Samvadadhwam Sam Vo Manamsi Janatam,' meaning 'Let us move together, deliberate together, and let our minds be in harmony.'

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

What perspectives were shared on cultural diversity and dialogue?

  • Dialogue amid diversity. Pakistan’s Federal Minister for Climate Change and Environmental Coordination, Dr. Musaddiq Masood Malik, highlighted that dialogue takes place amid diversity and distinct identities have their own significance.
  • Purana Qila example. He cited Purana Qila in Delhi as a living story of shared cultural heritage, where architectural contributions from rulers of Central Asian and Afghan origins coexist, symbolizing cultural layers and diversity.
  • Importance of embracing diversity. He emphasized the importance of acknowledging, respecting, and moving forward together with diverse identities to facilitate the free flow of ideas.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

What future directions for the SCO were discussed?

  • Beyond security focus. Shri Alok Dimri, Additional Secretary, Ministry of External Affairs and SCO National Coordinator, noted that the organisation needs to move beyond its security-centric approach.
  • Emerging common themes. Discussions identified cultural networks, climate, youth, digitalisation, and joint projects as common themes for future cooperation.
  • Culture closer to people. It was highlighted that culture should be taken beyond state and government institutions to connect more directly with the people.

Back to basics: Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO)

The SCO is a Eurasian political, economic, and security alliance comprising member states that cooperate on issues such as regional security, economic development, and cultural exchange. It aims to promote mutual trust and collaboration among its members, which include countries from Central Asia, South Asia, and beyond. Understanding SCO's role helps contextualize India's engagement in regional multilateral diplomacy.
