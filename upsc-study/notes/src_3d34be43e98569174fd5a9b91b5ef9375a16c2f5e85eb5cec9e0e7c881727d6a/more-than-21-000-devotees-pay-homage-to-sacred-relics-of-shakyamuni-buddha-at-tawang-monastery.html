Background

  • The Ministry of Culture organised the exposition at Gaden Namgyal Lhatse, or Tawang Monastery, in collaboration with the International Buddhist Confederation and the Government of Arunachal Pradesh.
  • The sacred relics were discovered at Piprahwah in Uttar Pradesh in 1898 and are preserved at the National Museum, New Delhi.
  • The release states that the relics returned to Arunachal Pradesh after a gap of 13 years. Their movement is scheduled from Tawang to TGL Monastery, Bomdilla, then the Golden Pagoda, Namsai, before return to the National Museum.
