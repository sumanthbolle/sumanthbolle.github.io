DRI Seizes Foreign Currency and E-Cigarettes in Major Anti-Smuggling Operations

The Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI) intercepted attempts to smuggle foreign currency worth Rs. 3.5 crore at Kolkata Airport and seized 13,000 e-cigarettes valued over Rs. 4 crore at JNPT Port. These actions highlight DRI's role in protecting India's economic stability and public health by enforcing customs laws and prohibitions.

DRI's seizures of illicit foreign currency and banned e-cigarettes demonstrate its enforcement against economic offences and public health risks.

Why is this in the news?

On 10 November 2026, the Ministry of Finance reported DRI's interception of foreign currency smuggling at Kolkata Airport and seizure of banned e-cigarettes at JNPT Port.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

What did the DRI seize at Kolkata Airport and why?

  • Foreign currency seizure. DRI intercepted seven passengers attempting to smuggle foreign currency equivalent to Rs. 3.12 crore concealed ingeniously in baggage and clothing at Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose International Airport, Kolkata.
  • Modus operandi. The currency was hidden inside notebooks, two-layered compartments in trolleys, shoes, and pockets of clothes in both hand and checked-in baggage.
  • Legal action. Seven syndicate members were arrested and charged under the Customs Act and FEMA regulations for smuggling foreign currency out of India.
  • Additional seizures. Other passengers were also found carrying foreign currency worth Rs. 0.39 crore and were handed over to Customs for further action.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What was seized at JNPT Port and why does it matter?

  • E-cigarette seizure. DRI seized around 13,000 e-cigarettes worth over Rs. 4 crore concealed in a container misdeclared as carrying 'Mop Rod' handles at JNPT Port.
  • Prohibition context. Electronic cigarettes and all Electronic Nicotine Delivery Systems (ENDS) are banned in India under the Prohibition of Electronic Cigarettes Act, 2019, to protect public health.
  • Scale of enforcement. In the financial year 2026-27, DRI has seized approximately 3,55,000 e-cigarettes valued at more than Rs. 135 crore across various ports and airports.
  • Public health and economic impact. The seizures prevent illicit trade that threatens public health and undermine India's economic interests.

[2] Press Information Bureau

Why do these seizures matter for India’s economy and security?

  • Economic stability. Illicit outflow of foreign currency harms India's economic and fiscal stability by bypassing regulatory controls.
  • Customs enforcement. DRI's intelligence-led enforcement dismantles organized smuggling networks, safeguarding India's economic frontiers.
  • Public health protection. Banning e-cigarettes and seizing illicit imports protects citizens from health risks associated with nicotine delivery systems.
  • Internal security. Controlling smuggling contributes to internal security by preventing illegal economic activities and associated criminal networks.

Back to basics: Role of Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI)

The DRI is India's premier anti-smuggling agency responsible for enforcing customs laws and preventing illicit trade. It conducts intelligence-led operations to intercept smuggling of goods and currency, protecting the country's economic interests and internal security.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about recent DRI seizures: 1. DRI seized foreign currency worth Rs. 3.5 crore at Kolkata Airport involving seven arrested syndicate members. 2. DRI seized 13,000 e-cigarettes at JNPT Port, which are legal under Indian law. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because e-cigarettes are prohibited in India under the Prohibition of Electronic Cigarettes Act, 2019.
