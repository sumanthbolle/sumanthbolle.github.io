Osmania General Hospital Enhances Gender-Affirming Surgery for Transgender People

Osmania General Hospital in Hyderabad has set up a dedicated gender-affirming surgery service in Telangana's government healthcare sector to improve access for transgender persons. This initiative is important for UPSC aspirants to understand transgender healthcare policies and social inclusion efforts in India.

Osmania General Hospital's multidisciplinary gender-affirming surgery service improves safe and affordable healthcare access for transgender people in Telangana.

Why is this in the news?

On October 25, 2026, Osmania General Hospital in Hyderabad launched a dedicated gender-affirming surgery service to improve specialized healthcare access for transgender persons in Telangana.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the new service introduced by Osmania General Hospital?

  • Dedicated surgery service. The hospital has established a gender-affirming surgery service within the government healthcare sector to provide specialized surgical care for transgender people.
  • Multidisciplinary team. The service involves specialists from plastic surgery, anaesthesiology, endocrinology, urology, gynaecology, psychiatry, nursing, and other healthcare professionals working together.
  • Types of procedures. Procedures performed include frontal bossing reduction, breast augmentation (with implants or fat grafting), penile inversion vaginoplasty, and nipple-areola complex-sparing mastectomy.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this service matter for transgender healthcare and social inclusion?

  • Improves accessibility. It provides safe, affordable gender-affirming surgical care within the public healthcare system, addressing financial and logistical barriers faced by transgender individuals.
  • Inclusive healthcare. The initiative promotes dignified and comprehensive care by integrating medical, psychological, and social support tailored to transgender patients.
  • Community support. Support from groups like the Queer Badhu Parents Association helped renovate a dedicated ward for transgender patients recovering from surgery.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How is the service structured and what are future plans?

  • Patient-centered approach. The program emphasizes patient evaluation, individualized surgical planning, perioperative care, and post-operative follow-up.
  • Funding support. Breast implants are facilitated through letters of credit under the Chief Minister’s Relief Fund to reduce costs for patients.
  • Expansion plans. The hospital intends to expand the range of gender-affirming procedures and strengthen the multidisciplinary program further.

Back to basics: Gender-affirming surgery

Gender-affirming surgery refers to medical procedures that help transgender individuals align their physical characteristics with their gender identity. These surgeries can include procedures like breast augmentation, genital reconstruction, and facial feminization. Such surgeries are part of comprehensive care that also involves psychological support and hormone therapy to improve the well-being and social acceptance of transgender people.
