Highlights of the 625th RBI Central Board Meeting

The 625th meeting of the Reserve Bank of India's Central Board of Directors was held on October 30, 2026, in Mumbai under Governor Sanjay Malhotra's chairmanship. This meeting covered economic conditions, risks, and reviewed key RBI departments, relevant for understanding RBI's governance and policy directions.

What happened at the 625th RBI Central Board meeting?

  • Meeting details. The meeting took place in Mumbai on October 30, 2026, chaired by RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra.
  • Integrity pledge. The Central Board took the Integrity Pledge as part of Vigilance Awareness Week 2026 observance.
  • Economic review. The Board deliberated on prevailing economic conditions, associated challenges, and potential risks to the economic outlook.
  • Institutional review. The Board reviewed activities of the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation and select Central Office Departments of the RBI.
  • Attendees. Deputy Governors and other Directors including officials from the Department of Economic Affairs attended the meeting.

Back to basics: Reserve Bank of India Central Board

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Central Board of Directors is the apex decision-making body of the RBI. It oversees the bank's functions including monetary policy, regulation of banks, and financial stability. The Board includes the Governor, Deputy Governors, government nominees, and independent directors. Its meetings guide RBI's policy and governance.
