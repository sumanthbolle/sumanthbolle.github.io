India and Mexico Launch Negotiations for Preferential Trade Agreement

India and Mexico have formally started negotiations for a Preferential Trade Agreement (PTA) by signing the Terms of Reference, aiming to enhance bilateral trade and investment. This development is important for UPSC aspirants as it highlights India's economic diplomacy and efforts to diversify trade partnerships.

India and Mexico initiated PTA negotiations to expand trade, investment, and economic cooperation across key sectors.

Why is this in the news?

On October 28, 2026, India and Mexico signed the Terms of Reference to begin negotiations for a Preferential Trade Agreement, following an understanding between their leaders at the G7 Summit in June 2025.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the significance of the India-Mexico Preferential Trade Agreement negotiations?

  • Framework for negotiations. The Terms of Reference signed by India and Mexico establish the framework, scope, modalities, and roadmap for negotiating a mutually beneficial and balanced Preferential Trade Agreement.
  • Economic opportunities. The PTA aims to expand bilateral trade, create business opportunities, enhance investment flows, and contribute to resilient and diversified supply chains between the two countries.
  • Strategic partnership. The negotiations follow an understanding between Prime Minister Narendra Modi and Mexican President Claudia Sheinbaum to deepen the India-Mexico partnership.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

Which key areas are covered in the PTA negotiations?

  • Trade facilitation and market access. Negotiations will cover market access for goods, rules of origin, customs procedures, and trade facilitation to ease cross-border trade.
  • Regulatory and dispute mechanisms. The Terms of Reference include sanitary and phytosanitary measures, technical barriers to trade, trade remedies, and dispute settlement mechanisms.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

How will the PTA impact trade and investment between India and Mexico?

  • Leveraging complementarities. The agreement seeks to leverage complementarities between the two economies to expand opportunities for Mexican products in India and vice versa.
  • Sectoral cooperation. Sectors with significant potential include automotive, chemicals, electronics, pharmaceuticals, agri-food, energy, critical minerals, medical devices, and advanced manufacturing.
  • Business engagement. Both sides discussed organizing a Mexican business mission to India in early 2027 to facilitate business-to-business meetings and strengthen commercial ties.

Back to basics: Preferential Trade Agreement (PTA)

A Preferential Trade Agreement is a trade pact between countries that reduces tariffs on certain goods to promote trade. It is less comprehensive than a Free Trade Agreement but helps countries enhance economic cooperation by easing market access and addressing trade barriers. PTAs often cover rules of origin, customs procedures, and dispute settlement to facilitate smoother trade flows.
