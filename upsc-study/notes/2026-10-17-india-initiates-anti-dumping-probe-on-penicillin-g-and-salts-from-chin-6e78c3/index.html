India launches anti-dumping probe on Penicillin G imports from China

India has initiated an anti-dumping investigation into imports of Penicillin G and its salts from China following a complaint by domestic manufacturer Lyfius Pharma. This probe is significant for understanding India's trade policy measures to protect domestic industries from unfair foreign competition.

India's Directorate General of Trade Remedies has started an anti-dumping probe on Penicillin G imports from China after evidence of dumping harmed domestic production.

Why is this in the news?

On October 17, 2026, the Directorate General of Trade Remedies (DGTR) initiated an anti-dumping investigation into Penicillin G and salts imported from China after a complaint by Lyfius Pharma.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What triggered the anti-dumping investigation?

  • Complaint by domestic producer. Lyfius Pharma, the sole eligible Indian producer of Penicillin G, alleged that imports from China were causing material retardation to the domestic industry.
  • Evidence of dumping. The DGTR found sufficient prima facie evidence that Penicillin G and its salts were being dumped into the Indian market by Chinese exporters.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the significance of Penicillin G production in India?

  • Revival after decades. Lyfius Pharma, a subsidiary of Aurobindo Pharma, restarted Penicillin G production in India after about three decades under the government’s production linked incentive (PLI) scheme.
  • Strategic investment. The new Penicillin G facility in Kakinada, Andhra Pradesh, represents a ₹2,500 crore investment and was inaugurated by Prime Minister Narendra Modi in October 2024.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for India's trade policy and economy?

  • Protecting domestic industry. The anti-dumping probe aims to shield the domestic pharmaceutical sector from unfairly low-priced imports that threaten local production.
  • Ensuring self-reliance. Reviving Penicillin G production aligns with government efforts to reduce dependency on imports for key pharmaceutical ingredients.

Back to basics: Anti-dumping investigation

An anti-dumping investigation is initiated when a domestic industry alleges that imported goods are being sold at unfairly low prices (dumped) and causing injury to the domestic producers. The investigating authority examines evidence of dumping, injury, and causal link to decide whether to impose anti-dumping duties to protect local industries.

Quick revision

Who filed the complaint for the anti-dumping probe on Penicillin G imports?
Lyfius Pharma filed the complaint alleging material retardation caused by imports.
What is the period of investigation for the anti-dumping probe?

What government scheme supported the revival of Penicillin G production in India?
The production linked incentive (PLI) scheme supported the revival.
