Railway Minister Reviews Passenger Facilities and Security at New Delhi Station

Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw inspected New Delhi Railway Station on 6 November 2026 to assess passenger amenities and security ahead of the Diwali and Chhath festive rush. The review highlights Indian Railways’ efforts to manage increased passenger volumes through infrastructure upgrades and enhanced security.

The Railway Minister’s inspection at New Delhi Station reflects Indian Railways’ focus on improving passenger convenience and safety during peak festive travel periods.

Why is this in the news?

On 6 November 2026, Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw conducted a comprehensive review of passenger facilities and security arrangements at New Delhi Railway Station ahead of the festive season.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What facilities and security measures were reviewed at New Delhi Railway Station?

  • Passenger facilities upgrade. A dedicated holding area for reserved ticket passengers was created alongside the existing unreserved passenger facility, streamlining entry and passenger movement.
  • Luggage and waiting area changes. Luggage checking facilities were moved outside the station building, converting former ticketing space inside into a comfortable waiting area to reduce congestion.
  • Security enhancements. Additional 700 AI cameras were installed to strengthen surveillance, and Railway Protection Force deployment was increased to manage the festive rush.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How is Indian Railways managing the festive season passenger rush?

  • Increased train services. Over 18,000 additional special trains were arranged for the festive period, surpassing the 12,000 special trains from the previous year, enabled by track additions.
  • Crowd monitoring and management. A government 'war room' monitors crowd levels across 28 key stations with live dashboards, enabling timely interventions to ease congestion.
  • Passenger amenities. Facilities such as additional ticketing counters, baggage screening, drinking water, and clean washrooms were enhanced at major stations to improve travel comfort.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the scale of passenger movement during the festive period?

  • Delhi area footfall. Major Delhi stations recorded about 2 crore passenger movements from 1 October to 5 November 2026, with daily footfall rising from 5 lakh to 8 lakh during peak days.
  • Other major regions. Mumbai area stations saw 1.5 crore movements, Hyderabad area 90 lakh, Bengaluru area 1 crore, and Surat/Udhna 50 lakh in the same period, all showing increased daily footfall during peak festive days.

Back to basics: Railway Infrastructure and Passenger Management

Railway stations handle large volumes of passengers daily, requiring infrastructure like holding areas, ticket counters, and security systems to manage flow and safety. During festivals, passenger numbers surge, demanding additional trains, enhanced security, and better facilities to ensure smooth operations and passenger convenience.
