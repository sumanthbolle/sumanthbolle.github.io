Legal Action Over Voter List Irregularities in Pilibhit, Uttar Pradesh

The Uttar Pradesh Chief Electoral Officer ordered legal and departmental action against officials responsible for discrepancies in the Pilibhit Assembly constituency voter list. This case highlights the Election Commission's role in maintaining electoral roll integrity and accountability of election officials.

The Election Commission took legal and departmental action against officials for voter list irregularities in Pilibhit, demonstrating enforcement of electoral roll accuracy.

Why is this in the news?

On October 23, 2026, the Uttar Pradesh Chief Electoral Officer ordered legal action against officials for discrepancies in the voter list of Pilibhit Assembly Constituency 127.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What irregularities were found in the Pilibhit voter list?

  • Discrepancies in voter details. Searching certain voters’ EPIC numbers online showed details of other electors, indicating mismatches in the electoral rolls.
  • Unauthorized data entries. The computer operator entered 13,779 Form-8s between May 31 and August 29, 2026, but no corresponding forms were found in office records, revealing discrepancies in 10,470 entries.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Who is held responsible and what actions were taken?

  • Officials held accountable. Legal action under Section 32 of the Representation of the People Act, 1950, and relevant IPC sections was ordered against the computer operator and election registration officers responsible between July 16 and 17, 2026.
  • Departmental actions and investigations. The computer support staff will be terminated, and a thorough investigation into assistant election registration officers' roles was directed, with reports to be submitted.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How is the Election Commission ensuring electoral roll integrity?

  • Review of voter registration forms. The District Election Officer was instructed to review all Form 6s and Form 7s submitted for voter registration and deletion between April 10 and October 21, 2026, and submit a detailed report within one day.
  • Addressing discrepancies systematically. All discrepancies found in Form 8 entries are being addressed according to established procedures and standards.

Back to basics: Role of Election Commission in Electoral Roll Management

The Election Commission of India is responsible for preparing and maintaining accurate electoral rolls to ensure free and fair elections. It oversees voter registration, updates, and deletions through prescribed forms and procedures. Legal provisions empower the Commission to take action against officials for irregularities to uphold electoral integrity.
