Indian Railways Freight Loading Grows 6.14% in September 2026

Indian Railways reported a 6.14% increase in freight loading in September 2026 compared to the same month in 2025, with notable growth in key commodities. This reflects sustained demand for rail transport and is an important indicator of economic activity and infrastructure development.

Indian Railways freight loading rose by 6.14% in September 2026, driven by growth in food grains, clinker, coal, and other commodities.

Why is this in the news?

The Ministry of Railways released data on October 13, 2026, showing a 6.14% growth in freight loading in September 2026 over September 2025.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the recent freight loading growth figures for Indian Railways?

  • Overall growth. Indian Railways loaded 135.35 million tonnes of freight in September 2026, a 6.14% increase over 127.52 million tonnes in September 2025.
  • Cumulative growth. From April to September 2026, cumulative freight loading reached 833.7 million tonnes, marking a 4.1% growth compared to the same period last year.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Which commodity segments showed significant growth?

  • Food grains. Food grain loading increased by 25% in September 2026 compared to the previous year.
  • Clinker and containers. Clinker loading grew by 20%, and domestic container freight rose by 10%.
  • Coal and others. Coal loading increased by 9%, mineral oil by 6%, finished steel by 5.5%, and iron ore by 5.2%.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How has coal supply to power houses changed?

  • Increased coal rake supply. The average number of coal rakes supplied per day rose to 443 in September 2026 from 397 in September 2025, an 11.6% increase.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What about freight revenue growth?

  • Monthly revenue increase. Freight revenue in September 2026 increased by ₹1,266 crore, a 9.3% rise over September 2025.
  • Cumulative revenue growth. For April-September 2026, freight revenue grew by 3.7% compared to the same period last year.

Back to basics: Freight loading and its economic significance

Freight loading refers to the total quantity of goods transported by rail. It is a key indicator of industrial and economic activity because railways carry bulk commodities essential for manufacturing, power generation, and agriculture. Growth in freight loading reflects increased demand for transportation and infrastructure utilization, signaling economic expansion.
