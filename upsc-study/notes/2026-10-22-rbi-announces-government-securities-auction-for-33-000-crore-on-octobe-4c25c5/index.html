RBI Announces ₹33,000 Crore Government Securities Auction on October 23, 2026

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) will conduct an underwriting auction for Government Securities worth ₹33,000 crore on October 23, 2026. This auction is important for understanding government borrowing, debt management, and monetary policy operations.

RBI's auction of ₹33,000 crore in Government Securities reflects ongoing government borrowing and debt management strategies.

Why is this in the news?

On October 22, 2026, RBI announced an underwriting auction for Government Securities totaling ₹33,000 crore to be held on October 23, 2026.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

What is the Government Securities auction announced by RBI?

  • Auction details. The Government of India will sell Government Securities worth ₹33,000 crore through an underwriting auction conducted by RBI on October 23, 2026.
  • Securities involved. The auction includes 6.53% GS 2031 with a notified amount of ₹19,000 crore and 7.71% GS 2066 with ₹14,000 crore.
  • Auction method and timing. The auction will use a multiple price-based method, with Primary Dealers submitting bids electronically between 09:00 A.M. and 09:30 A.M. on the auction day.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

Why does this auction matter for government borrowing and fiscal policy?

  • Government borrowing. The auction reflects the government's need to raise funds by issuing debt securities to finance its expenditure.
  • Role of Primary Dealers. Primary Dealers have minimum underwriting commitments and bidding commitments, ensuring the auction's success and liquidity in government securities.
  • Monetary policy implications. Issuance of government securities affects liquidity and interest rates, which are key tools in monetary policy management.

Back to basics: Government Securities and Auctions

Government Securities (G-Secs) are debt instruments issued by the government to borrow money from the public. Auctions are the process through which these securities are sold to investors, often involving Primary Dealers who have underwriting commitments to ensure the auction's success. The issuance of G-Secs is a key method for the government to finance its fiscal deficit and manage public debt.
