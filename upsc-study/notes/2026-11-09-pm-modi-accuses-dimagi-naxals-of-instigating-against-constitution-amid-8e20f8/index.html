PM Modi on 'Dimagi Naxals' Instigating Against Constitution Post Maoist Elimination

Prime Minister Narendra Modi stated that while armed Maoists have been eliminated, ideological supporters termed 'dimagi Naxals' continue to instigate people against India's Constitution and democracy. This highlights ongoing internal security challenges and the need to safeguard constitutional order.

PM Modi emphasized that despite the elimination of armed Maoists, ideological supporters infiltrating political parties pose a threat to the Constitution and democracy.

Why is this in the news?

On November 8, 2026, PM Modi addressed security personnel in Bastar, Chhattisgarh, highlighting the elimination of armed Maoists but warning about ideological supporters instigating against the Constitution.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What did PM Modi say about Maoist elimination and ongoing threats?

  • Armed Maoists eliminated. PM Modi declared that armed Maoists have been eliminated from the forests, marking a significant security achievement.
  • Threat from 'dimagi Naxals'. He warned that 'dimagi Naxals', or ideological supporters of Maoism, have infiltrated political parties and organisations and continue to instigate people against the Constitution and democracy.
  • Need to identify and defeat. The Prime Minister stressed the importance of identifying and defeating these ideological supporters to secure the country.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for India's internal security and constitutional order?

  • Continuing internal security challenge. Despite the elimination of armed insurgents, ideological threats persist, posing challenges to national security and democratic stability.
  • Infiltration of political parties. The infiltration of ideological supporters into political parties risks undermining democratic processes and constitutional values.
  • Government measures. The government has taken tough decisions to cut funding and supply lines for Naxalites and secessionists, strengthening national security.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How has the government addressed Naxalism and its ideological support?

  • Freedom to security forces. The government empowered security forces with freedom to take necessary decisions during operations, unlike previous governments.
  • Crackdown on funding and fake currency. Measures were taken to destroy fake currency networks and choke financial support to Naxalites, disrupting their recruitment and weapon supply.
  • Focus on development. Alongside security efforts, the government worked to transform lives in affected areas, gaining support from local populations.

Back to basics: Naxalism and internal security

Naxalism refers to armed insurgency movements inspired by Maoist ideology, primarily active in certain forested and rural areas of India. These groups have historically challenged the state's authority through violence and have been linked to issues of poverty and underdevelopment. The government combats Naxalism through security operations, cutting funding and supply lines, and development initiatives to win local support.
