Kerala High Court on Protecting Sensitive VACB Information from RTI

The Kerala High Court was informed by the Vigilance and Anti-Corruption Bureau (VACB) that T1 and T2 sections contain sensitive information requiring protection from Right to Information (RTI) disclosure. This raises important questions on balancing transparency with confidentiality in governance.

The Kerala High Court heard that certain VACB sections hold sensitive intelligence and inquiry data exempt from RTI to protect investigations and informants.

Why is this in the news?

The Kerala High Court received a clarification from the VACB on November 19, 2026, about the sensitive nature of information in T1 and T2 sections and their exemption from RTI.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the T1 and T2 sections and why are they sensitive?

  • T2 Section contents. The T2 Section handles source reports, intelligence inputs, orders for surprise checks, confidential verifications, petitions, inquiries, and sensitive transferred cases.
  • T1 Section contents. The T1 Section deals with similar sensitive matters plus annual confidential reports (ACRs) of bureau staff and personal files of executive staff.
  • Nature of sensitivity. Disclosing intelligence inputs could alert public servants under investigation, allowing them to conceal evidence and intimidate informants.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does the VACB justify exemption from RTI?

  • Protection of investigations. The VACB argued that revealing intelligence or inquiries would compromise investigations and expose informants to retaliation.
  • Functions of VACB. VACB conducts surprise checks, surveils officers with doubtful integrity, maintains dossiers on corrupt officials, collects intelligence, and lays traps.
  • Processing of petitions. Only petitions requiring confidential verification or surprise checks are transferred to T1 or T2 sections; others are handled separately.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What information remains accessible under RTI despite the exemption?

  • Reassignment of certain functions. Functions related to ACRs of ministerial, executive, and legal staff and personal files were moved to the establishment section, which remains under RTI.
  • No blanket exclusion. The affidavit clarified that the GO did not exclude the entire VACB from RTI, only sensitive intelligence and inquiry data.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Back to basics: Right to Information Act and exemptions

The Right to Information Act allows citizens to access government information to promote transparency and accountability. However, it also provides exemptions to protect sensitive information related to security, investigations, and privacy to prevent misuse or harm. Balancing transparency with confidentiality is essential for effective governance and law enforcement.

Quick revision

What types of information are handled in the VACB's T2 section?
The T2 section handles source reports, intelligence inputs, surprise check orders, confidential verifications, petitions, and sensitive inquiries.
Why are T1 and T2 sections exempted from the RTI Act?
Because disclosing their intelligence and inquiry information could alert suspects, compromise investigations, and endanger informants.
Is the entire VACB excluded from the RTI Act?
No, only the sensitive T1 and T2 sections are exempted; other functions remain under RTI.
