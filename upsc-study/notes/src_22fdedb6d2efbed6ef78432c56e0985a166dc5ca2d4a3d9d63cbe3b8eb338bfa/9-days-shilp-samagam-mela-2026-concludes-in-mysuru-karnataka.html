Background

  • The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment organises Shilp Samagam Melas for beneficiaries from Scheduled Castes, Other Backward Classes and Safai Karamcharis, including artisans supported by NSFDC, NBCFDC and NSKFDC.
  • The source document states that Shilp Samagam Melas have been organised since 2022 and that the Mysuru edition was the first such mela of financial year 2026–27.
  • The mechanism is market linkage: artisans receive an organised venue and participation support, display products, meet potential customers and receive information about relevant schemes.
  • The source also describes BHARAT TULIP as a digital-platform initiative intended to expand market access and brand products made by the target groups.
