US-China AI Cooperation and Competition: Implications for Global Tech Governance

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent highlighted the need for balanced cooperation and competition between the US and China on artificial intelligence (AI) risks and leadership. This dialogue aims to manage shared risks while maintaining national interests, a dynamic relevant for India's technology diplomacy and AI governance.

The US and China are engaging in a structured dialogue on AI to balance competition with cooperation on risk management, influencing global AI governance frameworks.

Why is this in the news?

On November 10, 2026, US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent outlined the US-China approach to AI cooperation and competition ahead of a bilateral dialogue on superintelligence at the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation summit.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the nature of US-China cooperation and competition on AI?

  • Dual approach. The US and China aim to cooperate on mitigating AI risks while competing for AI supremacy, acknowledging their differing interests and approaches.
  • Bilateral dialogue. A new dialogue on 'superintelligence' is planned to manage shared risks, exchange best practices for AI model evaluation, and maintain human oversight.
  • Incident alert system. Both countries agreed to establish a direct channel to alert each other about serious AI-related incidents to prevent misunderstandings.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this US-China AI dynamic matter for India and global governance?

  • Global AI leadership. US-China competition and cooperation set precedents for AI governance, impacting global standards and norms that India must navigate.
  • Technology diplomacy. India's engagement in AI governance will be influenced by the frameworks and risk management practices developed by these major powers.
  • Risk management emphasis. The focus on balancing competition with responsibility highlights the need for robust AI risk frameworks relevant to India's policy decisions.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the trade and strategic contexts linked to AI discussions?

  • Trade initiatives. The US-China AI dialogue occurs alongside trade talks involving tariff cuts and market access, reflecting intertwined economic and technological relations.
  • Risk management over decoupling. The US aims to 'de-risk' rather than decouple from China, indicating a nuanced approach to economic and technological interdependence.

Back to basics: AI Governance and International Cooperation

Artificial Intelligence governance involves creating policies and frameworks to manage AI development and deployment risks. International cooperation is essential to address cross-border challenges such as security, ethics, and economic impacts. Major powers often balance competition with collaboration to shape global standards and prevent conflicts.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about US-China cooperation on AI: 1. They have agreed to establish a direct alert channel for serious AI incidents. 2. The US aims to decouple its trade and technology relations from China completely. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the US aims to 'de-risk' rather than completely decouple from China.
