RBI Grants In-Principle Approval to Paytm to Revive Wallet Business

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has given Paytm Payments Services Ltd an in-principle approval to operate prepaid payment instruments (PPIs), including digital wallets. This development is important for UPSC aspirants as it reflects regulatory oversight in the digital payments sector and impacts financial inclusion and digital economy policies.

RBI’s in-principle nod allows Paytm to restart its wallet business subject to compliance with regulatory conditions.

Why is this in the news?

On November 13, 2026, Paytm Payments Services Ltd received in-principle approval from RBI to operate prepaid payment instruments, enabling it to revive its wallet business.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the RBI’s in-principle approval to Paytm about?

  • In-principle approval granted. RBI has given Paytm Payments Services Ltd an in-principle approval to offer prepaid payment instruments (PPIs), including digital wallets, allowing the company to potentially restart its wallet business.
  • Conditions for final authorisation. Paytm must meet RBI’s prescribed conditions, including submitting a system audit report and a cybersecurity audit by June 2027, before receiving final authorisation.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why did Paytm’s wallet business stop earlier?

  • RBI restrictions in 2024. In 2024, RBI imposed restrictions on Paytm Payments Bank due to repeated compliance failures, barring it from accepting fresh deposits or wallet top-ups, effectively halting its wallet business.
  • Licence cancellation. Earlier in 2026, RBI cancelled Paytm Payments Bank’s licence, further impacting its wallet operations.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for UPSC aspirants?

  • Regulatory oversight in digital payments. The RBI’s approval process highlights the regulatory framework governing digital wallets and prepaid payment instruments, which is crucial for understanding financial sector reforms.
  • Impact on financial inclusion. Revival of Paytm’s wallet business can influence digital payments adoption and financial inclusion in India.
  • Economic performance context. Paytm’s improved financial performance, including a nearly 10-fold increase in net profit in Q2 FY27, provides context to the RBI’s decision.

Back to basics: Prepaid Payment Instruments (PPIs)

Prepaid Payment Instruments are digital instruments that allow users to store money in advance and use it for transactions like purchasing goods, transferring funds, and remittances. They include digital wallets and are regulated by the Reserve Bank of India to ensure security and compliance in digital payments.
