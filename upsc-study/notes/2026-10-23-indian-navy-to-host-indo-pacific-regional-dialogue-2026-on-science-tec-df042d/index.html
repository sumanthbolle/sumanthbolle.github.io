Indian Navy to Host Indo-Pacific Regional Dialogue 2026 on Science and Technology

The Indian Navy will hold the Indo-Pacific Regional Dialogue 2026 in New Delhi from 3 to 5 November, focusing on science, technology, and academic cooperation among 18 countries. This event is important for understanding India's maritime strategy and regional cooperation in the Indo-Pacific.

The Indian Navy's Indo-Pacific Regional Dialogue 2026 aims to advance science, technology, and academic collaboration to strengthen maritime security and governance in the Indo-Pacific region.

Why is this in the news?

The Indian Navy announced on 23 October 2026 that it will host the Indo-Pacific Regional Dialogue 2026 from 3 to 5 November in New Delhi, focusing on science, technology, and academic cooperation in the Indo-Pacific region.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the Indo-Pacific Regional Dialogue 2026?

  • Annual international conference. The Indo-Pacific Regional Dialogue (IPRD) is the Indian Navy’s annual apex-level international outreach conference held at the strategic level.
  • 2026 edition details. The 2026 edition will be held at the Manekshaw Centre, New Delhi from 3 to 5 November, organized with the National Maritime Foundation as the knowledge partner.
  • Theme focus. The theme is ‘Science, Technology and Academic Cooperation in the Indo-Pacific’, emphasizing multidisciplinary scientific and technological collaboration.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does this dialogue matter for India and the Indo-Pacific region?

  • Supports India's maritime vision. The dialogue translates the Indo-Pacific Oceans Initiative (IPOI) framework into specific, actionable proposals supporting India’s MAHASAGAR vision for security and growth across regions.
  • Addresses uneven scientific capabilities. It recognizes that scientific knowledge and technological capabilities in maritime domains are unevenly distributed, requiring cooperation and human capital development.
  • Focus on broad maritime issues. The conference covers climate change, maritime trade vulnerabilities, marine resource pressures, and threats to maritime infrastructure, which transcend national boundaries.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the key features and sessions of IPRD-2026?

  • Five thematic professional sessions. Sessions include Academic Networks and Human Capital Development, Maritime Situational Awareness, Oceanographic Science and Climate Resilience, Ocean Governance, and Transformative Maritime Technologies.
  • Special sessions. Two special sessions are ‘Perspectives from the Flag Bridge’ with former naval chiefs from India and Sri Lanka, and ‘Sahyog Ka Samvaad’, an ambassadorial interaction on cooperation and dialogue.
  • Ministerial addresses. The Defence Minister of India and the Minister of State for Science and Technology will deliver commemorative and special addresses respectively.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How will the dialogue impact regional cooperation and policy?

  • Solution-oriented dialogue. IPRD-2026 emphasizes identifying specific, actionable measures at national, sub-regional, and pan-regional levels for maritime cooperation.
  • Strengthening partnerships. The conference aims to enhance joint research, technology development, responsible governance, data access, and institutional partnerships across the Indo-Pacific.
  • Leveraging existing mechanisms. Outcomes will be addressed through bilateral, trilateral, and plurilateral engagements to promote cooperation and collaboration in the Indo-Pacific.

Back to basics: Indo-Pacific Oceans Initiative (IPOI)

The IPOI is a framework announced by India to promote cooperation in maritime security, ocean governance, and sustainable development across the Indo-Pacific region. It connects multiple maritime policies and initiatives to enhance regional security and economic growth through collaboration among countries bordering the Indo-Pacific oceans.
