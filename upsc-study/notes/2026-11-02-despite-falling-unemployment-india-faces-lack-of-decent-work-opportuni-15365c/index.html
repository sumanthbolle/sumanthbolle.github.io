India’s Falling Unemployment Masks Lack of Decent Work Opportunities

India’s unemployment rate declined from 6.1% in 2017-18 to 3.2% in 2023-24, with female workforce participation rising. However, many jobs lack decent work conditions such as social security, formal contracts, and workplace rights, posing challenges for inclusive growth.

Despite lower unemployment, India faces persistent informality and insecure employment that undermine decent work and economic security.

Why is this in the news?

The Hindu reported on November 1, 2026, that while unemployment has fallen, India still struggles with poor-quality employment lacking social security and formal protections.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the current employment situation in India?

  • Unemployment rate decline. India’s unemployment rate fell from 6.1% in 2017-18 to 3.2% in 2023-24, indicating more people are employed.
  • Female workforce participation. Female workforce participation increased for the first time in decades during this period.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does falling unemployment not mean decent work?

  • Definition of decent work. The International Labour Organization defines decent work as employment with equitable income, workplace rights, social security, and dignity.
  • Informality and insecurity. Many workers are in informal jobs without formal contracts, social security, or paid leave, such as garment workers, gig workers, and construction laborers.
  • Consequences of poor-quality jobs. Workers without social security spend and save less and remain vulnerable to economic shocks, limiting economic growth.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for India’s economic growth and policy?

  • Link to Sustainable Development Goals. Decent work is integral to SDG-8, which promotes inclusive and sustainable economic growth through decent work for all.
  • Challenge for inclusive growth. High informality and insecure employment hinder equitable growth and social protection, requiring policy focus beyond just reducing unemployment.

Back to basics: Decent Work

Decent work means employment that provides fair income, workplace rights, social security, and dignity. It is a concept promoted by the International Labour Organization and is part of the Sustainable Development Goals. Decent work ensures that employment contributes to economic security and social inclusion, beyond just having a job.
