Telangana's El Niño Plan Cuts Hyderabad Water Use by 20%

Telangana is implementing a contingency plan to address El Niño-related water shortages in Hyderabad by urging a 20% reduction in household water use and expanding treated wastewater use. This is important for UPSC aspirants to understand climate change impacts on urban water management and government adaptation strategies.

Telangana's El Niño contingency plan includes a 20% cut in Hyderabad's water use and increased use of treated wastewater to manage drought conditions.

Why is this in the news?

On October 18, 2026, Telangana government responded to El Niño concerns by urging water conservation and implementing measures to manage water scarcity in Hyderabad.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What triggered Telangana's water conservation measures?

  • El Niño impact. El Niño caused deficient rainfall in Hyderabad district, with only 3 of 16 mandals receiving normal rainfall, leading to water scarcity.
  • Groundwater depletion. Groundwater levels dropped from 3.55 metres below ground level last year to 10 metres this year, indicating severe depletion.
  • Reservoir inflow stopped. No inflows were recorded into Manjeera and Singur reservoirs, with current water supplies sufficient only until December.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the key components of the contingency plan?

  • Water use reduction. Households have been urged to reduce water consumption by at least 20% and repair leaks promptly to conserve water.
  • Treated wastewater use. The government plans to use treated wastewater for construction, landscaping, and industrial activities, aiming for 90% of construction water needs to be met this way.
  • Expansion of filling stations. Of 13 treated-water filling stations, 3-4 are operational, with plans to make all functional by November 1 and add 20 more within 15 days.
  • Restrictions on borewells. Ground Water Department officials have been directed not to issue new borewell permissions to prevent groundwater misuse.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How is the government managing electricity amid water scarcity?

  • Electricity conservation. The Telangana government urged reducing electricity consumption, especially between 5 p.m. and 10 p.m., and maintaining air conditioners at 25°C.
  • Record electricity demand. Electricity demand reached a record peak of 20,018 MW, with expectations of further increases in March and April.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the rainfall outlook and monitoring strategy?

  • Rainfall deficit. Rainfall during the northeast monsoon withdrawal phase is expected to be below normal, with hopes for cyclone-related rains in November.
  • Monitoring groundwater. Groundwater levels and rainfall are monitored using 33 piezometers across Hyderabad district.
  • Official oversight. Special officers monitor water conditions in all 16 mandals to manage the situation proactively.

Back to basics: El Niño and Water Resource Management

El Niño is a climate phenomenon characterized by warming of the Pacific Ocean, which disrupts normal weather patterns including monsoon rains in India. This can lead to droughts and water scarcity. Water resource management during such events involves conservation measures, use of alternative water sources like treated wastewater, and monitoring groundwater levels to ensure sustainable supply.
