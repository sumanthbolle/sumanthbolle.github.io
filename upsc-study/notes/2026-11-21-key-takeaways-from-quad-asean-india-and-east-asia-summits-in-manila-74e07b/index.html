Key Takeaways from Quad, ASEAN-India, and East Asia Summits in Manila

Prime Minister Narendra Modi participated in three major summits—Quad, ASEAN-India, and East Asia—held in Manila in November 2026. These meetings highlighted India's efforts to strengthen its Act East and Indo-Pacific policies amid shifting global power dynamics.

India's engagement in the Quad, ASEAN-India, and East Asia Summits in Manila reflects its strategic focus on broadening regional cooperation and Indo-Pacific security.

Why is this in the news?

Prime Minister Narendra Modi attended the Quad, ASEAN-India, and East Asia Summits in Manila in November 2026, signaling India's active role in regional multilateral diplomacy.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What happened at the Manila summits?

  • Multiple summits attended. PM Modi participated in the Quad Summit, ASEAN-India Summit, and East Asia Summit held back-to-back on the sidelines of ASEAN summits in Manila.
  • Key participants. The summits included leaders from the US, Japan, Australia, ASEAN countries, Russia, China, South Korea, and India, reflecting a broad regional engagement.
  • Concrete outcomes. The meetings concluded with several concrete announcements and reinforced India's strategic policies in the Indo-Pacific.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for India's foreign policy?

  • Strengthening Act East Policy. India's active participation shows its intent to deepen ties with ASEAN and other Indo-Pacific nations, broadening its Act East policy.
  • Indo-Pacific focus. The summits emphasized India's role in shaping the Indo-Pacific security architecture alongside major powers and regional groupings.
  • Improved bilateral ties. India's relations with the Philippines improved notably after President Bongbong Marcos' visit to Delhi in August 2025, enhancing cooperation during the summits.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the broader significance of these summits?

  • Multilateral diplomacy. India's engagement in these summits reflects a preference for multilateral cooperation amid a global trend of unilateral actions by major powers.
  • Balancing global powers. The summits occurred just before the Trump-Xi-Putin trilateral, highlighting India's strategic positioning among competing global influences.
  • Quad Summit's importance. The Quad Summit was held for the first time during President Trump's presidency, addressing concerns about his commitment to the Indo-Pacific grouping.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Back to basics: Multilateral Summits and Indo-Pacific Strategy

Multilateral summits are meetings involving multiple countries to discuss common issues and cooperation. The Indo-Pacific strategy is a regional approach focusing on security, economic, and diplomatic collaboration among countries bordering the Indian and Pacific Oceans. India's Act East policy aims to strengthen economic and strategic relations with Southeast Asian and East Asian countries.
