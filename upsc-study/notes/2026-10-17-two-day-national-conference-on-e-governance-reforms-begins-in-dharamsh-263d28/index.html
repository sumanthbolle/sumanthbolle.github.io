National Conference on e-Governance Reforms begins in Dharamshala

A two-day National Conference on e-Governance Reforms started on 16 October 2026 in Dharamshala, Himachal Pradesh, focusing on digital transformation and citizen-centric governance. This event is important for UPSC aspirants to understand current government efforts to improve public service delivery through technology.

The National Conference on e-Governance Reforms promotes innovative, technology-driven governance to enhance public service delivery across India.

Why is this in the news?

The National Conference on e-Governance Reforms was inaugurated on 16 October 2026 in Dharamshala, bringing together central and state government representatives to discuss digital governance innovations.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the purpose of the National Conference on e-Governance Reforms?

  • Conference objective. The conference aims to strengthen the exchange of ideas among government institutions and promote effective, innovative, and citizen-centric governance through digital transformation.
  • Scaling innovations. A key goal is to scale up successful pilot projects and replicate innovations that work in one place across many locations.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Who participated and what were the key highlights on Day One?

  • Participants. Representatives from Central and State/UT governments, including the Chief Minister and Chief Secretary of Himachal Pradesh, and officials from the Department of Administrative Reforms and Public Grievances (DARPG) participated.
  • Key initiatives showcased. Himachal Pradesh presented five initiatives such as Him Parivar and Sukhashraya, covering sectors like health, social justice, education, environment, and forest conservation.
  • Technology in governance. Presentations included award-winning digital platforms and AI-enabled systems like AgriStack, e-Jagriti, AI Clinical Decision Support in e-Sanjeevani, and others demonstrating emerging technologies in governance.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does e-governance improve service delivery according to the conference?

  • Reducing procedural hurdles. E-governance enables citizens to access government services at their doorsteps and reduces procedural barriers.
  • Promoting equality. The objective includes eliminating barriers and promoting equality to ensure efficient delivery of public services.
  • Citizen-centric focus. The reforms focus on making governance more robust and centered around the needs of citizens.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What topics will Day Two cover in the conference?

  • Focus areas. Day Two will include sessions on cybersecurity, best e-governance practices, replication of successful initiatives, data analytics, and grassroots-level governance efforts.

Back to basics: E-governance

E-governance refers to the use of digital tools and technologies by government institutions to provide services, improve transparency, and enhance the efficiency of public administration. It aims to make governance more accessible and citizen-friendly by reducing physical and procedural barriers.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the National Conference on e-Governance Reforms held in Dharamshala: 1. The conference aims to scale up successful digital governance innovations across India. 2. The conference was inaugurated by the Union Minister of Health and Family Welfare. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the conference was inaugurated by the Secretary of DARPG and the Chief Minister and Chief Secretary of Himachal Pradesh, not the Union Minister of Health.
