Delhi to Host Inaugural SCO Civilisational Dialogue Including Pakistan Minister

India will organise the first Civilisational Dialogue Forum of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) in Delhi from November 20-22, 2026, inviting Pakistan's Minister for National Heritage and Culture. This event marks a notable diplomatic engagement between India and Pakistan within the SCO framework after a period of strained relations.

India's hosting of the SCO Civilisational Dialogue with Pakistan's participation signals a diplomatic outreach within the multilateral SCO framework.

Why is this in the news?

India announced the inaugural SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum to be held in Delhi in November 2026, inviting Pakistan's Culture Minister, marking a first since 2023 and after the 2025 India-Pakistan conflict.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum?

  • Forum purpose. The SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum aims to promote awareness and appreciation of the shared civilisational heritage among SCO member countries through thematic sessions on religion, art, language, and cultural traditions.
  • Organisers and participants. The event is organised by India's Ministry of Culture and Ministry of External Affairs, with participation from culture ministers and representatives of the 10 SCO member states, 15 dialogue partners, and observer countries.
  • Venue significance. The dialogue will be held at Delhi's Purana Quila, a 16th-century fort with archaeological significance showcasing India's ancient past across multiple historical periods.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does Pakistan's invitation matter?

  • First ministerial invite since 2023. Pakistan's Minister for National Heritage and Culture Aurungzeb Khan Khichi has been invited, marking the first time India is hosting a Pakistani minister since the 2023 SCO Foreign Ministers meeting.
  • Post-conflict engagement. This invitation is significant as it comes after the four-day India-Pakistan conflict in May 2025, indicating a diplomatic channel within the SCO framework despite bilateral tensions.
  • India's selective multilateral engagement. While India has avoided SAARC meetings in Islamabad due to differences with Pakistan since 2016, it continues to engage with Pakistan in SCO meetings, reflecting a strategic approach to regional cooperation.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does this fit into India's foreign policy?

  • PM Modi's initiative. The Civilisational Dialogue Forum was proposed by Prime Minister Narendra Modi at the SCO leaders’ Summit in Tianjin and adopted by the SCO Council, showing India's active role in shaping SCO's cultural diplomacy.
  • Multilateral engagement focus. India treats the SCO as an important multilateral entity and engages with it collectively rather than focusing solely on bilateral issues with member states.
  • Upcoming SCO events. India is preparing for the SCO Heads of State Summit in 2027 in Pakistan, with prior coordination meetings held, indicating ongoing diplomatic engagement within the SCO despite bilateral challenges.

Back to basics: Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO)

The SCO is a Eurasian political, economic, and security alliance comprising 10 member states including India and Pakistan. It focuses on regional cooperation in areas such as security, culture, and economic development. The organisation holds summits and meetings to foster multilateral dialogue and collaboration among member and observer states.
