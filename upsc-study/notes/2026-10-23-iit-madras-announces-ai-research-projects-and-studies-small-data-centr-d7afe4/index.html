IIT-Madras Launches AI Research and Studies Small Data Centres

IIT-Madras announced research projects on artificial intelligence including a feasibility study for small, frugal data centres called pico centres. This matters for UPSC aspirants as it highlights India's approach to AI development, resource-efficient technology, and responsible AI governance.

IIT-Madras is pioneering frugal AI research and exploring small data centres to support diverse sectors with energy-efficient AI solutions.

Why is this in the news?

On October 23, 2026, IIT-Madras announced new AI research projects and a study on the feasibility of small data centres for diverse applications.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What AI research projects did IIT-Madras announce?

  • Frugal AI data centres. IIT-Madras plans to study the feasibility of small, distributed 'pico centres' that consume less electricity and resources, supporting inference tasks across sectors like agriculture, space, manufacturing, health, and law.
  • Bodhan AI initiative. Bodhan AI, a Centre of Excellence for AI in Education funded by the Union Ministry of Education and incubated at IIT-Madras, is working on frugal AI data centres to provide personalized AI tutors to 25 crore school students in India.
  • Responsible AI efforts. The Centre for Responsible AI (CeRAI) at IIT-Madras, in partnership with The Better India, will produce 500 videos showcasing case studies on responsible and effective AI use.
  • AI evaluation platform. IIT-Madras launched a software platform to evaluate AI tools using over 50 metrics and 100 sub-metrics in multiple languages.
  • Trusted AI Commons. Work will begin on a Trusted AI Commons to enhance AI accountability and responsibility.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why are small data centres important for AI?

  • Energy efficiency. Small data centres, developed with frugal engineering, consume less electricity and resources compared to large data centres used for training large language models.
  • Distributed model. A federated and distributed model of pico centres can serve diverse end-user groups effectively across multiple sectors.
  • Sectoral applications. These centres can support inference tasks in agriculture, space, manufacturing, health, law, and education, making AI more accessible and practical.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does IIT-Madras contribute to AI governance?

  • Case studies on responsible AI. CeRAI's partnership with The Better India will produce 500 videos demonstrating responsible AI use across India.
  • Accountability frameworks. The Trusted AI Commons initiative aims to improve AI accountability and responsibility.
  • Evaluation metrics. The AI evaluation platform assesses tools on extensive metrics to ensure quality and trustworthiness.

Back to basics: Artificial Intelligence and Data Centres

Artificial Intelligence (AI) involves computer systems performing tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as learning and decision-making. Data centres are facilities that house computer systems and associated components to store and process data. Large data centres support training of complex AI models, while smaller, distributed data centres can perform inference tasks closer to end-users, improving efficiency and reducing resource consumption.
