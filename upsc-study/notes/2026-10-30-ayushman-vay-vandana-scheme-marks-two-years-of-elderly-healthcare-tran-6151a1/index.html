Ayushman Vay Vandana Scheme Completes Two Years of Elderly Healthcare

Launched on 29 October 2024, Ayushman Vay Vandana extends Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY benefits to all senior citizens aged 70 and above, irrespective of socio-economic status. This scheme has provided 23.46 lakh hospital admissions valued at ₹5,668.80 crore, marking a significant step in inclusive healthcare for the elderly.

Ayushman Vay Vandana provides cashless health coverage up to ₹5 lakh per year to senior citizens aged 70+, enhancing financial protection and access to hospital care.

Why is this in the news?

The Ministry of Health and Family Welfare marked the completion of two years of the Ayushman Vay Vandana scheme on 29 October 2026.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the Ayushman Vay Vandana scheme?

  • Scheme overview. Ayushman Vay Vandana extends the benefits of Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY to all senior citizens aged 70 years and above, regardless of socio-economic status.
  • Implementation. Implemented by the National Health Authority under the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, it aims to provide inclusive and accessible healthcare for approximately 6 crore senior citizens across 4.5 crore families.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What benefits does the scheme provide?

  • Health coverage. Senior citizens aged 70+ receive health coverage of up to ₹5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation.
  • Additional top-up cover. For those already covered under AB PM-JAY, an exclusive additional top-up cover of ₹5 lakh per year is available for senior citizens aged 70+, separate from other family members.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How has the scheme impacted elderly healthcare?

  • Hospital admissions. As of 20 October 2026, 23.46 lakh authorised hospital admissions have been recorded under the scheme, valued at ₹5,668.80 crore.
  • Common treatments. Cataract surgery is the most frequent procedure with 4.71 lakh treatments, followed by chronic haemodialysis with 2.90 lakh treatments.
  • High-cost care. Percutaneous Transluminal Coronary Angioplasty (PTCA) accounts for the largest authorised value at ₹649 crore, highlighting support for advanced cardiac care.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does this scheme matter for social welfare and healthcare?

  • Inclusive healthcare. By covering all senior citizens aged 70 and above irrespective of socio-economic status, the scheme promotes universal and dignified access to hospital care.
  • Financial protection. It strengthens financial protection for elderly families by reducing the burden of hospitalisation costs.
  • Government commitment. The scheme reflects the Government of India's commitment to building an accessible, world-class healthcare system for vulnerable elderly populations.

Back to basics: Health Welfare Schemes for Vulnerable Sections

Health welfare schemes provide financial protection and access to healthcare services for vulnerable populations, such as senior citizens. These schemes often cover hospitalisation costs for secondary and tertiary care to reduce out-of-pocket expenses and promote inclusive healthcare. They are implemented by government bodies to improve health outcomes and social security for targeted groups.
