Lok Sabha Speaker Highlights Dissent and Accountability in Democracy

Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla emphasized the importance of dissent as a democratic strength and urged lawmakers to focus on serving the most vulnerable in society. He also called for strict accountability and transparency in public spending, highlighting the role of Public Accounts Committees in ensuring every rupee is used for its intended purpose.

The Lok Sabha Speaker underscored dissent as vital for democracy and stressed accountability in public expenditure to ensure governance benefits the last person in society.

Why is this in the news?

Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla made these remarks during the Janpratinidhi Sammelan in Haryana on November 2, 2026, and at the Public Accounts Committees conference in Thiruvananthapuram on November 3, 2026.

Why is dissent important in a democracy?

  • Dissent as democratic strength. The Lok Sabha Speaker stated that agreement and disagreement are two sides of the same coin in a healthy democracy, and dissent must be welcomed as it brings fresh perspectives and improves governance.
  • Respect for differing views. He emphasized that despite political or ideological differences, lawmakers must work collectively for the progress of the state and nation.
  • Democracy's foundation. Dissent provides direction, vision, and inspires people to work, making it a vital catalyst for democratic functioning.

[1] Press Information Bureau[3] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the role of Public Accounts Committees (PACs) in governance?

  • Guardians of public money. PACs examine public expenditure, ensure financial discipline, and hold the Executive accountable to prevent misuse of public funds.
  • Beyond audit scrutiny. PACs must assess whether public funds achieve their intended objectives and deliver tangible outcomes for citizens.
  • Political inclusiveness. The Chairperson of the PAC is traditionally a Member of the Opposition, reflecting parliamentary maturity and ensuring diverse perspectives.
  • Use of technology. The Speaker urged PACs to leverage AI, data analytics, and responsible technology to strengthen financial oversight and timely resolution of audit observations.

[2] Press Information Bureau

How does accountability in public spending impact governance?

  • Every rupee must be accounted. The Speaker stressed that every rupee drawn from the public exchequer must be properly accounted for and utilised fully for its sanctioned purpose.
  • Outcome-oriented scrutiny. PACs should go beyond figures and examine the quality, timely completion, and impact of projects and welfare schemes.
  • Time-bound audit follow-up. Long-pending audit observations must be resolved promptly through systematic action plans involving Union and State Governments.
  • Public trust foundation. Transparency and accountability in public expenditure build citizens' trust, which is fundamental to democratic governance.

[2] Press Information Bureau

What broader democratic values did the Speaker highlight?

  • Constitution as moral compass. The Speaker described the Constitution not just as a governing framework but as the living soul and moral compass of India.
  • Commitment to public service. He urged lawmakers to view governance as an instrument of grassroots empowerment, aiming to bring socio-economic transformation to the most vulnerable.
  • Legislative dignity. Haryana Legislative Assembly was praised for high-quality debates and adherence to legislative dignity, serving as an inspiration nationally.
  • Federal democracy resilience. India's diverse federal polity, built through inclusive constitutional processes, stands as a global example of democratic resilience.

Back to basics: Democratic accountability and parliamentary oversight

Democratic accountability ensures that elected representatives and government institutions are answerable to the people for their actions and use of public resources. Parliamentary oversight, through bodies like Public Accounts Committees, involves scrutinizing government expenditure and policies to ensure transparency, prevent misuse of funds, and promote good governance. This process strengthens public trust and the effective functioning of democracy.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the role of Public Accounts Committees (PACs): 1. The Chairperson of the PAC is traditionally a Member of the Opposition to ensure bipartisan oversight. 2. PACs are responsible only for examining audit reports and not for assessing the impact of public expenditure. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because PACs also assess whether public funds have achieved their intended objectives and delivered outcomes, beyond just examining audit reports.

Mains question (15 marks)

Examine the importance of dissent and accountability in strengthening Indian democracy with reference to recent statements by the Lok Sabha Speaker.

Examine: Discuss how dissent and accountability contribute to democratic governance, citing the Lok Sabha Speaker's views.

Intro: Dissent and accountability are fundamental pillars of a vibrant democracy.

  • Dissent fosters diverse perspectives, improves governance, and is essential for democratic health as emphasized by the Lok Sabha Speaker.
  • Accountability in public spending, ensured through institutions like Public Accounts Committees, promotes transparency and effective use of public funds.
  • The Speaker highlighted the constitutional values underpinning democracy and the role of lawmakers in serving the most vulnerable sections of society.
  • Use of technology and timely audit follow-ups are necessary to strengthen financial oversight and public trust.

Conclusion: Dissent and accountability together uphold democratic values and enable governance that truly serves the people.
