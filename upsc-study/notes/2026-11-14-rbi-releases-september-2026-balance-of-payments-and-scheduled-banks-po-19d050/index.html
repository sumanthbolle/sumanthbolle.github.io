RBI Releases September 2026 Balance of Payments and October 2026 Scheduled Banks Data

The Reserve Bank of India published preliminary data on India's Balance of Payments for September 2026 and the statement of position of scheduled banks as of October 31, 2026. These indicators provide insights into India's external sector performance and banking system health, relevant for understanding macroeconomic stability.

RBI's September 2026 BoP data showed a widened current account deficit and a strong capital account surplus, while October 2026 bank data reflected growth in deposits and advances.

Why is this in the news?

RBI released the preliminary Balance of Payments data for September 2026 and the statement of position of scheduled banks as of October 31, 2026, on November 13, 2026.

What does the September 2026 Balance of Payments data show?

  • Current account deficit widened. India's current account deficit increased from US$ -5.2 billion in September 2025 to US$ -7.4 billion in September 2026, reflecting a larger gap between imports and exports.
  • Merchandise trade deficit narrowed. Merchandise exports rose to US$ 45.3 billion and imports to US$ 75.3 billion, reducing the merchandise trade deficit from US$ -33.4 billion to US$ -30.0 billion.
  • Services surplus increased. Net services exports improved slightly from US$ 18.8 billion to US$ 19.2 billion, with exports at US$ 39.3 billion and imports at US$ 20.1 billion.
  • Capital account showed strong inflows. The capital account recorded a net inflow of US$ 16.2 billion in September 2026 compared to an outflow of US$ -0.4 billion in September 2025, driven by banking capital and foreign direct investment.
  • Overall balance turned positive. Combining current and capital accounts, the overall balance was a surplus of US$ 8.8 billion in September 2026, reversing the deficit of US$ -5.6 billion a year earlier.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

What are the key highlights of Scheduled Banks’ position as of October 31, 2026?

  • Growth in deposits. Total deposits (excluding banks) increased to ₹28,251,076.20 crore on October 31, 2026, up from ₹24,175,756.75 crore on October 31, 2025.
  • Increase in bank credit. Bank credit (loans, cash credits, overdrafts) rose to ₹22,699,003.72 crore on October 31, 2026, from ₹19,029,402.61 crore a year earlier.
  • Rise in borrowings from RBI. Scheduled commercial banks’ borrowings from RBI increased sharply to ₹29,134 crore on October 31, 2026, compared to ₹5,489 crore on October 31, 2025.
  • Cash and balances with RBI. Cash held by banks was ₹83,668.29 crore and balances with RBI were ₹8,58,022.76 crore as of October 31, 2026.
  • Number of banks. As of the current fortnight, there were 120 scheduled commercial banks, 26 scheduled state co-operative banks, and 55 scheduled primary (urban) co-operative banks.

Back to basics: Balance of Payments and Banking System

The Balance of Payments (BoP) records all economic transactions between residents of a country and the rest of the world, including trade, services, income, and capital flows. It has two main components: the current account and the capital account. The banking system's position shows the financial health of banks, including deposits, loans, borrowings, and liquidity, which affect credit availability and economic growth.
