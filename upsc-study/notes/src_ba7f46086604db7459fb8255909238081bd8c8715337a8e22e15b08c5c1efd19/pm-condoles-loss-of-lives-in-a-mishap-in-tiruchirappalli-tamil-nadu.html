Static anchor

PMNRF ex-gratia is immediate discretionary relief announced from the Prime Minister's National Relief Fund for specified distress situations; it is not, by itself, a judicial finding of fault or a complete substitute for statutory compensation.

Background

  • A mishap can create immediate needs including medical treatment, income support and assistance to families of deceased persons.
  • Ex-gratia is relief assistance rather than a finding of legal liability; investigation, prevention and long-term rehabilitation remain separate public-administration tasks.
