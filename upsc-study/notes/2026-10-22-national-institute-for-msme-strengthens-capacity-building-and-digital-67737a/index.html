National Institute for MSME Strengthens Capacity Building and Digital Empowerment

The National Institute for Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (ni-msme) under the Ministry of MSME has enhanced entrepreneurship, managerial skills, and digital adoption in MSMEs through extensive training and research. This development is crucial for UPSC aspirants to understand MSME sector support mechanisms and their role in inclusive economic growth and employment.

Ni-msme plays a key role in empowering MSMEs by providing training, research, and digital tools to boost entrepreneurship and competitiveness.

Why is this in the news?

On October 21, 2026, the Ministry of MSME highlighted ni-msme's role in capacity building, entrepreneurship development, and digital empowerment of MSMEs.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the role and history of ni-msme?

  • Autonomous institute under Ministry of MSME. Ni-msme is an autonomous institute under the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, Government of India, focusing on entrepreneurship promotion and managerial capacity building for MSMEs.
  • Established in 1960. Originally founded as the Central Industrial Extension Training Institute (CIETI) in New Delhi, it moved to Hyderabad in 1962 and was renamed ni-msme in 2007 following the MSME Development Act, 2006.
  • Focus areas. The institute covers enterprise creation, finance, marketing, international trade, digital transformation, technology adoption, and sustainable business practices to support employment generation and inclusive growth.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does ni-msme support MSME development?

  • Training and capacity building. Ni-msme has conducted 550 international programmes training 11,032 executives from 150 countries and 17,672 national programmes benefiting 6,07,497 participants.
  • Skill development and cluster projects. It conducted 6,840 skill development programmes training 2,10,062 youth and implemented 27 MSME cluster development projects.
  • Research and IP facilitation. The institute completed 962 research studies to aid MSME policymaking and provided 235 services through Intellectual Property Facilitation Centres.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does ni-msme promote digital empowerment and innovation?

  • Focus on emerging technologies. Ni-msme engages with digital marketing, e-commerce, artificial intelligence, technology-enabled manufacturing, intellectual property rights, green energy, and sustainable enterprise practices.
  • National conferences. In March 2026, it organised conferences on Digital Marketing, AI Automation, e-Commerce, and Digital Enabled Factories with Industry 5.0, discussing AI, Industrial Internet of Things, and cloud technologies.
  • Success stories. Examples include a Telangana silk enterprise increasing turnover to ₹2.8 crore through digital marketing and a woman entrepreneur growing millet business turnover tenfold with digital branding.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does ni-msme's work matter for economic growth and employment?

  • Inclusive economic growth. By strengthening entrepreneurship and managerial capabilities, ni-msme supports the growth of MSMEs, which are critical for employment generation and productivity.
  • Digital adoption for competitiveness. Digital empowerment helps MSMEs expand market access, improve operational efficiency, and remain competitive in changing business environments.
  • Women and youth empowerment. Skill development and digital training have enabled women-led enterprises to grow and created employment opportunities for local youth.

Back to basics: MSME sector and capacity building

Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) are vital for economic development, employment, and innovation. Capacity building involves training and skill development to improve managerial and technical capabilities of entrepreneurs. Digital empowerment refers to adopting digital tools and technologies to enhance business operations, marketing, and competitiveness.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the National Institute for Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (ni-msme): 1. Ni-msme was established in 1960 and renamed after the MSME Development Act, 2006. 2. Ni-msme primarily focuses on manufacturing MSMEs and does not engage in digital empowerment initiatives. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because ni-msme actively promotes digital empowerment through training in digital marketing, AI, and e-commerce.
