Ministry of Textiles to Reopen PLI Scheme Application Portal from 1st November 2026

The Ministry of Textiles will reopen the Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme portal for textiles from 1st November to 31st December 2026, focusing on Man-Made Fibre (MMF) apparel, fabrics, and technical textiles. This move aims to boost domestic manufacturing, attract investment, generate employment, and strengthen India's global textile value chain position.

The Ministry of Textiles will reopen the PLI Scheme portal with reduced turnover criteria and extended tenure to promote MMF textile manufacturing.

Why is this in the news?

The Ministry of Textiles announced on 28 October 2026 the reopening of the PLI Scheme application portal for Round 4 from 1st November to 31st December 2026.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the key features of the reopened PLI Scheme for textiles?

  • Focus areas. The scheme targets manufacturing of Man-Made Fibre (MMF) apparel, MMF fabrics, and technical textiles to enhance domestic capabilities and global competitiveness.
  • Reduced turnover criterion. The minimum turnover requirement under Part 2 of the scheme has been reduced from ₹200 crore to ₹100 crore to encourage participation by MSMEs and mid-sized manufacturers.
  • Extended scheme tenure. The scheme tenure is extended up to the financial year 2033–34 exclusively for applicants approved under Round 4, providing a longer horizon for investment and scaling manufacturing.
  • Migration of existing participants. Existing participant companies from Rounds 1, 2, and 3 that have not claimed incentives can migrate to Round 4 after withdrawing previous applications.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does the reopening of the PLI Scheme portal matter for India’s textile sector?

  • Promotes domestic manufacturing. By incentivizing MMF apparel, fabrics, and technical textiles, the scheme aims to boost domestic production capabilities.
  • Attracts investment and employment. The scheme encourages investment from MSMEs and mid-sized units, leading to job creation in the textile sector.
  • Strengthens global value chains. Enhancing manufacturing capacity helps India improve its position in global textile value chains, supporting export competitiveness.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

How can companies apply and participate in the scheme?

  • Application window. The online application portal https://pli.texmin.gov.in will be open from 1st November to 31st December 2026 for new applications under Round 4.
  • Eligibility. Companies with minimum turnover of ₹100 crore under Part 2 category and existing participants who have not claimed incentives can apply or migrate respectively.
  • Compliance with guidelines. Applicants must submit their applications within the stipulated period following the scheme guidelines.

Back to basics: Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme

The PLI Scheme is a government initiative to boost domestic manufacturing by providing financial incentives linked to production output. It aims to attract investment, enhance competitiveness, and generate employment in targeted sectors by encouraging companies to increase production capacity and exports.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the PLI Scheme for textiles: 1. The minimum turnover criterion under Part 2 has been reduced to ₹100 crore in Round 4. 2. The scheme tenure has been extended up to 2033–34 for all participants of previous rounds. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the extension of scheme tenure up to 2033–34 applies only to applicants approved under Round 4, not all previous participants.
