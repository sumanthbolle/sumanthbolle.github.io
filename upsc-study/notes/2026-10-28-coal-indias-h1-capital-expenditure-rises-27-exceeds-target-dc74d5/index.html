Coal India’s Capital Expenditure Rises 27% in H1, Exceeds Target

Coal India Ltd increased its capital expenditure by 27% year-on-year to ₹7,966 crore in the first half of the financial year, surpassing its target of ₹7,286 crore. This reflects a strong investment push to expand mining infrastructure and operational capacity, relevant for understanding government energy investments and economic growth.

Coal India’s H1 capital expenditure rose 27% to ₹7,966 crore, exceeding its target and supporting mining capacity expansion.

Why is this in the news?

On October 27, 2026, The Hindu reported that Coal India’s capital expenditure in the first half of the financial year rose 27% year-on-year, exceeding its target.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What was Coal India’s capital expenditure performance in H1?

  • Expenditure increase. Coal India’s capital expenditure rose 27% year-on-year to ₹7,966 crore in the first half of the current financial year, exceeding the target of ₹7,286 crore.
  • Annual outlay utilisation. The company utilised more than 48% of its annual capital expenditure outlay of ₹16,500 crore by September.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Where was the capital expenditure directed?

  • Mine infrastructure. Mine infrastructure development accounted for the largest share at ₹2,152 crore, focusing on coal evacuation and handling infrastructure such as railway sidings, corridors, coal handling plants, silos, and weighbridges.
  • Land acquisition. Land acquisition was the second-largest expenditure head, accounting for ₹2,033 crore.
  • Mine development and machinery. Over ₹1,000 crore was spent on mine development, and ₹1,394 crore was invested in plant and machinery, including ₹452 crore on heavy earth-moving machinery.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does Coal India’s capital expenditure matter for the economy?

  • Strengthening operational capacity. The investments aim to strengthen operational capabilities and support production and supply targets for the financial year.
  • Addressing infrastructure bottlenecks. Focus on evacuation infrastructure is critical to align production with coal transport capacity, preventing constraints on coal movement.

Back to basics: Capital Expenditure

Capital expenditure refers to funds used by a company to acquire, upgrade, and maintain physical assets such as property, industrial buildings, or equipment. It is essential for expanding operational capacity and infrastructure. For public sector companies like Coal India, capital expenditure reflects government investment priorities and impacts economic growth by enabling increased production and supply.
