Face-off with China continues at multiple Arunachal border locations

The Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP) Director-General confirmed ongoing confrontations with Chinese troops at several sites along the Arunachal Pradesh border since July 2026. This matters for UPSC aspirants as it highlights current India-China border tensions, military preparedness, and diplomatic engagements relevant to internal security and bilateral relations.

India faces multiple face-offs with China along the Arunachal Pradesh border, involving ITBP and army deployments and ongoing diplomatic talks.

Why is this in the news?

On October 23, 2026, the ITBP Director-General officially confirmed continuing face-offs with Chinese troops at multiple locations along the Arunachal Pradesh border since July 2026.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the current situation on the Arunachal border?

  • Ongoing confrontations. Face-offs with Chinese troops have been occurring at several locations in Arunachal Pradesh since July 2026 and continue to persist.
  • Chinese troop deployment. The Chinese PLA pitched tents 6 to 7 km inside India's perceived Line of Actual Control near Maja in Upper Subansiri district after initial confrontations.
  • Indian deployment. India has increased deployment along the border, with the Army and ITBP conducting long-range patrols between June and October due to weather conditions.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How is India responding militarily to the border tensions?

  • Role of ITBP and Shaurya commandos. ITBP commandos, including the newly raised Shaurya high-altitude mountain fighting battalion, have played an active role in the confrontations.
  • Modernisation efforts. ITBP is investing heavily in drone and counter-drone technology, allocating ₹1,000 crore out of ₹3,500 to ₹4,000 crore modernisation funds for drones and training drone pilots.
  • Underground shelters. A decision has been made to build underground shelters equal in number to above-ground structures along the China border over the next three years to protect troops in potential hot-war situations.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What diplomatic efforts are underway to address the border tensions?

  • High-level talks. Since July 2026, India and China have held three high-level border talks, including the 36th WMCC meeting in August, the 25th Special Representatives round in Beijing, and the first-ever Corps Commander-level meeting in Arunachal Pradesh in September.
  • Continued tensions despite diplomacy. Despite these talks and the BRICS Summit attended by Chinese President Xi Jinping in September, the face-offs and Chinese deployments continue.

Back to basics: India-China Border and Line of Actual Control (LAC)

The India-China border is disputed in several sectors, including Arunachal Pradesh. The Line of Actual Control (LAC) is the de facto boundary between the two countries, but its exact alignment is not mutually agreed upon. This leads to patrol confrontations and occasional face-offs. The Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP) is the primary border guarding force in this region, specializing in high-altitude mountain warfare.
