RBI imposes ₹7.50 lakh penalty on Fusion Finance Limited for KYC lapses

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) penalised Fusion Finance Limited ₹7.50 lakh for non-compliance with KYC and Internal Ombudsman guidelines. This case highlights RBI's regulatory role in enforcing financial sector governance and customer protection.

RBI imposed a ₹7.50 lakh penalty on Fusion Finance Limited for failing to comply with KYC norms and Internal Ombudsman complaint escalation rules.

Why is this in the news?

On November 17, 2026, RBI penalised Fusion Finance Limited for regulatory non-compliance related to KYC and Internal Ombudsman directions.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

What penalty did RBI impose on Fusion Finance Limited and why?

  • Penalty details. RBI imposed a monetary penalty of ₹7.50 lakh on Fusion Finance Limited for non-compliance with its directions on Know Your Customer (KYC) norms and Internal Ombudsman procedures.
  • Specific violations. The company failed to obtain PAN or equivalent documents from customers when establishing accounts and did not escalate rejected complaints to the Internal Ombudsman within the prescribed time.
  • Legal basis. The penalty was imposed under sections 58G(1)(b) and 58B(5)(aa) of the Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

Why does this matter for financial regulation and governance?

  • RBI's supervisory role. RBI conducts statutory inspections to ensure compliance with regulatory directions, safeguarding the integrity of financial institutions.
  • Customer protection. Enforcing KYC norms and Internal Ombudsman mechanisms protects customers from fraud and ensures grievance redressal.
  • Regulatory compliance enforcement. Monetary penalties deter non-compliance and promote adherence to RBI guidelines, maintaining trust in the financial system.

Back to basics: Know Your Customer (KYC) and Internal Ombudsman

KYC is a process by which financial institutions verify the identity and address of their customers to prevent fraud and money laundering. The Internal Ombudsman is an independent official within a financial institution who addresses customer complaints that are not resolved satisfactorily by the institution, ensuring accountability and timely grievance redressal.
