India implications

  • Internal security: organised smuggling links border vulnerabilities with domestic logistics and commercial channels.
  • Economic governance: illicit imports can bypass customs compliance and distort legitimate trade and jewellery-market competition.
  • Financial integrity: cash-based disposal and cross-border financing create risks of concealed proceeds and weak audit trails.
  • Operational lesson: enforcement should combine airport intelligence, supply-chain mapping, financial investigation and scrutiny of downstream buyers.

Way forward

  • Strengthen risk-based passenger and cargo profiling while minimising indiscriminate disruption of legitimate travel and trade.
  • Map the entire chain from entry point to end-market, including handlers, processors, financiers and purchasers.
  • Integrate customs, airport, financial-intelligence and state-level investigative inputs through lawful information sharing.
  • Improve documentation and traceability in high-risk precious-metal supply chains and jewellery transactions.
