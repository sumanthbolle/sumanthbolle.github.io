Indian Navy Rescues Hijacked Fishing Vessel Azini off Somalia Coast

The Indian Navy successfully rescued the fishing vessel Azini and its 20 crew members from armed pirates off Somalia's north-eastern coast after a 48-hour operation. This event highlights India's active role in maritime security and anti-piracy efforts in the Indian Ocean Region.

The Indian Navy conducted a coordinated operation to rescue the hijacked fishing vessel Azini, ensuring maritime security and safe release of all crew members.

Why is this in the news?

On 3 November 2026, the Indian Navy announced the successful rescue of the hijacked fishing vessel Azini off the coast of Somalia.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What happened during the rescue operation?

  • Operation duration. The rescue operation lasted over 48 hours and involved persistent surveillance and tactical intervention.
  • Pirate threat. The vessel Azini was hijacked by an armed pirate group and posed a threat as a potential 'mother ship' for further pirate attacks on merchant shipping.
  • Inter-service coordination. The Indian Navy deployed a warship supported by a maritime reconnaissance aircraft and Marine Commandos were airdropped by an Indian Air Force C-17 aircraft to secure the vessel.
  • Outcome. All 20 crew members were rescued safely without any casualties or injuries.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does this rescue matter for India and the region?

  • Maritime security role. The operation demonstrates the Indian Navy’s preparedness and capability to conduct complex maritime security operations.
  • Regional stability. It reflects India’s commitment to safeguarding seafarers and protecting vital maritime trade routes in the Indian Ocean Region.
  • International cooperation. India aims to remain the First Responder and Preferred Security Partner in the region through such coordinated efforts.

Back to basics: Maritime Security and Anti-Piracy Operations

Maritime security involves protecting sea routes and vessels from threats such as piracy, terrorism, and illegal trafficking. Anti-piracy operations are coordinated military or naval actions to prevent or respond to hijacking or attacks by pirates on commercial or fishing vessels. These operations often require surveillance, intelligence gathering, and tactical interventions to ensure safe navigation and trade in international waters.
