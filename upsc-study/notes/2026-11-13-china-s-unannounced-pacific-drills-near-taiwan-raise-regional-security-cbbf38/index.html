China's Unannounced Military Drills Near Taiwan Raise Indo-Pacific Security Concerns

China has increased military and paramilitary activities east of Taiwan, including unannounced drills, raising fears of regional instability. The G7 Foreign Ministers have condemned these actions as threats to peace, freedom of navigation, and international shipping safety, highlighting the strategic tensions in the Indo-Pacific.

China's intensified military presence near Taiwan threatens regional stability and freedom of navigation, drawing international condemnation.

Why is this in the news?

On November 11, 2026, the G7 Foreign Ministers issued a joint statement condemning China's increased military activities east of Taiwan, citing threats to regional stability and freedom of navigation.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What military activities has China undertaken near Taiwan?

  • Increased patrols. Since June 2026, China has regularly conducted coast guard patrols off Taiwan's east coast, involving military, paramilitary, and civilian assets.
  • Area denial drills. China is carrying out 'area denial' drills in the Western Pacific, including the deployment of an aircraft carrier and other naval formations.
  • Unannounced exercises. These activities include unannounced military drills that Taiwan interprets as preparations for a potential maritime blockade.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why do these activities matter for regional security?

  • Threat to stability. The G7 Foreign Ministers stated that China's actions threaten peace and stability across the Taiwan Strait and the wider Indo-Pacific region.
  • Freedom of navigation at risk. China's assertive presence endangers freedom of navigation and the safety of international shipping lanes critical to global trade.
  • Potential conflict escalation. Taiwan views these drills as rehearsals for coercive measures, increasing the risk of conflict in a geopolitically sensitive area.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How has the international community responded?

  • G7 condemnation. The G7 Foreign Ministers reaffirmed opposition to any unilateral attempts to change the status quo by force or coercion.
  • Support for Taiwan. Countries like the US, UK, France, and Germany have expressed concern over China's maritime activities near Taiwan.
  • Calls for peace. The G7 stressed the importance of maintaining peace and stability across the Taiwan Strait.

Back to basics: Freedom of Navigation and Regional Stability

Freedom of navigation refers to the right of ships to travel through international waters without interference. It is crucial for global trade and security. Regional stability in maritime zones depends on respecting sovereignty and avoiding coercive military actions that can escalate conflicts.
