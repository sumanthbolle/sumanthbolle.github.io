Lok Sabha Speaker Advocates Accountability and Digital Audit Platform for Legislatures

Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla emphasized the need for strict accountability in public expenditure and proposed a common digital audit monitoring platform for Parliament and state legislatures. This development is crucial for UPSC aspirants as it highlights evolving parliamentary oversight mechanisms and the role of technology in governance.

The Lok Sabha Speaker called for enhanced financial accountability through timely audit follow-ups and proposed a unified digital platform to monitor audit observations across legislatures.

Why is this in the news?

The Lok Sabha Speaker inaugurated and later concluded the 2026 Conference of Chairpersons of Public Accounts Committees (PACs) of Parliament and State Legislatures in Thiruvananthapuram, calling for stronger financial oversight and technological integration in legislative audit processes.

What role does the Public Accounts Committee play in financial accountability?

  • PAC's core function. The Public Accounts Committee examines public expenditure, ensures financial discipline, and holds the Executive accountable to prevent misuse or waste of public funds.
  • Beyond audit scrutiny. PACs assess whether public funds have achieved their intended objectives and delivered tangible outcomes, including site visits for infrastructure projects and impact evaluation of social welfare schemes.
  • Opposition leadership. A distinctive feature is appointing a Member of the Opposition as PAC Chairperson, promoting inclusiveness and bipartisan commitment to financial probity.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why is a digital audit monitoring platform proposed for legislatures?

  • Need for technological integration. The Speaker proposed a common digital platform for state legislatures on the lines of Parliament's system to enable systematic monitoring and timely disposal of audit reports.
  • Benefits of digital tools. Technology like AI and data analytics can analyze large expenditure data, detect irregularities, and improve audit follow-up, enhancing transparency and efficiency.
  • Human oversight importance. Despite technological advances, human judgment and sensitivity remain essential to maintain accountability and decision-making quality.

[2] Press Information Bureau

What resolutions were adopted to strengthen legislative financial oversight?

  • Standardized timelines. The conference resolved to prescribe timelines for submission of Action Taken Notes on audit paras and PAC recommendations across all States and UTs.
  • Digital oversight systems. Development of digital monitoring systems such as Audit Para Monitoring System (APMS) in all State PACs was mandated.
  • Capacity building. Regular training and orientation for PAC secretariats, members, and CAG officials were emphasized to handle complex financial scrutiny.
  • Increased sittings and sub-committees. State PACs were encouraged to hold at least three sittings monthly and form sub-committees to clear pending audit observations.
  • Collaboration with CAG. Strengthening coordination with the Comptroller and Auditor General to use advanced forensic tools and identify high-risk governance areas was prioritized.
  • Digital solutions with integrity. Effective use of digital tools while maintaining legislative process sanctity was resolved.

How do these developments matter for UPSC preparation?

  • Understanding parliamentary oversight. These updates illustrate the evolving role of PACs in ensuring transparency and accountability in public finance, a key topic in GS Paper 2.
  • Technology in governance. The proposal for digital audit platforms highlights the integration of technology in legislative functions, relevant for questions on e-governance and financial management.
  • Institutional reforms and democracy. The bipartisan nature of PAC leadership and emphasis on timely audit follow-up reflect democratic principles and governance reforms.

Back to basics: Public Accounts Committee (PAC)

The PAC is a parliamentary committee responsible for auditing government expenditure to ensure it is lawful and efficient. It examines reports of the Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) and holds the Executive accountable. The Chairperson is traditionally a senior opposition member to ensure impartial scrutiny. PACs play a key role in financial oversight and governance transparency.

What happens next?

  • Implementing digital platforms. The challenge lies in developing and adopting a common digital audit monitoring platform across all state legislatures to enhance audit follow-up and transparency.
  • Capacity building. Continuous training for PAC members, CAG officials, and legislative staff in emerging technologies is necessary for effective oversight.
  • Timely audit actions. Ensuring audit observations are addressed promptly through standardized timelines and regular PAC sittings remains a critical task.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the Public Accounts Committee (PAC): 1. The PAC is chaired by a senior member of the opposition to ensure bipartisan financial oversight. 2. The PAC's role is limited to examining audit reports without assessing the actual impact of public expenditure. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the PAC also assesses whether public funds have achieved their intended objectives and delivered tangible outcomes, not just audit reports.
