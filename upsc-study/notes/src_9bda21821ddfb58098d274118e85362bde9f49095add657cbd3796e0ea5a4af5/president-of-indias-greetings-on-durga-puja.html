Intangible cultural heritage refers to living practices, expressions, knowledge and skills transmitted by communities across generations; UNESCO’s Durga Puja record illustrates this through ritual, artistic practice, craft, music and community participation.[6][8]

Background

  • Durga Puja is an annual festival centred on the worship of Goddess Durga and is celebrated prominently in West Bengal, elsewhere in India and among the Bengali diaspora.[1][8]
  • UNESCO inscribed Durga Puja in Kolkata on the Representative List of the Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity in 2021.[6][8]
  • UNESCO describes the festival through public religious and artistic performance, large installations, pavilions, Bengali drumming, clay sculpture and community participation.[6][8]
