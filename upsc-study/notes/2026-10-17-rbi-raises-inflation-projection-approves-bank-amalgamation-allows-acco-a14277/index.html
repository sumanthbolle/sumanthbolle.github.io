RBI Raises Inflation Forecast, Approves Bank Merger, and Enables Account Aggregator Interoperability

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has raised its inflation projection, sanctioned the amalgamation of Emirates NBD Bank's Indian branches with RBL Bank, and announced interoperability among account aggregators by December 31, 2026. These developments affect monetary policy, banking consolidation, and financial data integration, relevant for both Prelims and Mains exams.

RBI's recent decisions on inflation outlook, bank amalgamation, and account aggregator interoperability reflect key monetary and financial sector reforms.

Why is this in the news?

On October 16, 2026, RBI announced its inflation projection revision, approved a bank merger, and introduced account aggregator interoperability to enhance financial data integration.

What bank amalgamation did RBI approve?

  • Amalgamation details. RBI sanctioned the Scheme of Amalgamation of Indian branches of Emirates NBD Bank P.J.S.C. with RBL Bank Limited, effective December 1, 2026.
  • Legal basis. The approval was given under sub-section (4) of Section 44A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.
  • Operational impact. Post-amalgamation, all Indian branches of Emirates NBD Bank will operate as branches of RBL Bank.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

What changes did RBI announce for account aggregators?

  • Interoperability introduction. RBI will enable interoperability among Non-Banking Finance Company account aggregators (NBFC-AAs) by December 31, 2026, allowing individuals to access all financial assets on a single platform.
  • Integration with CAS. RBI will facilitate inclusion of bank deposit information in Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI)-regulated consolidated account statements (CAS), filling a significant gap.
  • Benefits for users. This integration provides a consolidated financial overview, helps track dormant deposits, and enables financial advisers to offer informed advice with customer consent.

[4] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does RBI's inflation projection revision matter?

  • Inflation outlook update. RBI raised its inflation projection, reflecting changes in economic conditions that influence monetary policy decisions such as repo rate adjustments.
  • Monetary policy stance. The RBI shifted its stance to 'calibrated tightening' and increased the repo rate by 25 basis points to 5.50% to manage inflation.

Back to basics: Account Aggregator Framework

Account aggregators are entities that collect and consolidate financial information from various sources with customer consent, enabling a unified view of financial assets. Interoperability means different account aggregators can share data, improving accessibility and financial decision-making. Consolidated Account Statements (CAS) provide investors with a summary of their holdings across multiple financial instruments but traditionally did not include bank deposits.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the recent RBI announcements: 1. The RBI approved the amalgamation of Emirates NBD Bank's Indian branches with RBL Bank, effective December 1, 2026. 2. RBI will enable interoperability among account aggregators by December 31, 2026, allowing consolidated access to financial data including bank deposits. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (c). A trap could be confusing the effective date of the bank amalgamation (December 1, 2026) with the interoperability implementation deadline (December 31, 2026).

Mains question (10 marks)

Explain the significance of RBI's recent decisions on bank amalgamation and account aggregator interoperability in the context of financial sector reforms.

Explain: Explain how RBI's bank merger approval and account aggregator interoperability contribute to financial sector efficiency and inclusion.

Intro: RBI's recent policy announcements include bank amalgamation approval and enabling interoperability among account aggregators.

  • Bank amalgamation consolidates banking operations, potentially improving operational efficiency and customer service.
  • Account aggregator interoperability allows consolidated financial data access, enhancing transparency and informed financial decisions.
  • Integration of bank deposits into consolidated account statements fills a critical information gap for investors and families.
  • These reforms support deeper credit inclusion and better financial data usage in credit assessment.

Conclusion: Together, these measures strengthen the financial sector's efficiency, transparency, and inclusiveness.
