RBI Governor meets CEOs of select UCBs, NBFCs, and HFCs to discuss sector issues

On November 23, 2026, the RBI Governor met with leaders of Urban Cooperative Banks (UCBs), Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs), and Housing Finance Companies (HFCs) to discuss credit growth, governance, customer service, and cybersecurity. This engagement highlights RBI's regulatory focus on financial sector stability and customer protection, relevant for understanding India's financial governance.

The RBI Governor's meetings with UCBs, NBFCs, and HFCs emphasized strengthening governance, sustainable credit growth, customer centricity, and cybersecurity to support financial sector stability.

What were the key points discussed with Urban Cooperative Banks (UCBs)?

  • Credit growth and profitability. The RBI Governor noted improved credit growth in UCBs but lagging profitability, urging prudent lending aligned with borrowers' repayment capacity.
  • Governance and training. He emphasized strengthening governance standards and encouraged full utilization of Mission SAKSHAM, RBI's training initiative for UCBs.
  • Technology and cybersecurity. As UCBs adopt technology, the Governor advised implementing robust cybersecurity frameworks to manage cyber risks.
  • Customer centricity. Maintaining high standards in customer service and internal grievance redressal was highlighted as important.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

What issues were addressed with NBFCs and HFCs?

  • Role in credit access. The Governor acknowledged NBFCs and HFCs for supporting economic activity by widening credit access.
  • Sustainable growth and customer focus. He stressed responsible credit growth and urged NBFCs to prioritize customers, ensuring quality service and fair recovery practices.
  • Use of CKYCR. NBFCs were encouraged to use the Central KYC Records Registry for onboarding and maintaining updated customer records.
  • Cybersecurity infrastructure. Strengthening cybersecurity and adopting risk-based IT risk management with board oversight was emphasized.

[2] Reserve Bank of India

What regulatory updates did RBI announce related to electronic banking transactions?

  • Draft amendment directions issued. RBI issued draft amendments to strengthen preventive safeguards for electronic banking transactions, expanding the definition of 'negligence by a bank'.
  • Customer protection focus. The amendments aim to give customers greater control over digital payment channels and ensure uniform practices across banks.
  • Public consultation. Comments on the draft amendments are invited from regulated entities and the public by December 14, 2026.

Back to basics: Reserve Bank of India and financial sector regulation

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) is the central bank responsible for regulating banks and financial institutions in India. It formulates policies to ensure financial stability, protect customers, and promote sustainable credit growth. The RBI engages with entities like Urban Cooperative Banks (UCBs), Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs), and Housing Finance Companies (HFCs) to address sector-specific challenges and oversee governance, technology adoption, and customer protection.

Intro: The RBI regulates diverse financial entities to maintain sector stability and protect customers.

  • RBI's engagement with UCBs emphasizes improving credit growth, profitability, governance, and training through initiatives like Mission SAKSHAM.
  • With NBFCs and HFCs, RBI stresses responsible credit expansion, customer centricity, grievance redressal, and use of CKYCR for KYC compliance.

  • The RBI also updates regulatory frameworks, such as draft amendments for electronic banking safeguards, inviting public feedback.

Conclusion: RBI's proactive regulation and dialogue with financial institutions support sustainable growth and customer protection in India's financial sector.

Quick revision

What is Mission SAKSHAM?
Mission SAKSHAM is the RBI's ongoing training initiative for Urban Cooperative Banks to strengthen their governance and operational capabilities.
Why did the RBI Governor emphasize cybersecurity in meetings with UCBs, NBFCs, and HFCs?
Because cyber risk affects banks of every size, and robust cybersecurity frameworks are essential to protect customers and maintain trust.
