Union Minister Performs Bhoomipujan of Multi-Modal Logistics Park at Sindi Railway

Union Minister Nitin Gadkari performed the bhoomipujan for a Multi-Modal Logistics Park at Sindi Railway on November 1, 2026. This project aims to boost exports, reduce transportation costs, and create local employment, which is significant for India's trade and infrastructure development.

The Multi-Modal Logistics Park at Sindi Railway will enhance export efficiency and reduce logistics costs for Vidarbha's agricultural and industrial products.

Why is this in the news?

The bhoomipujan ceremony of the Multi-Modal Logistics Park at Sindi Railway was held on November 1, 2026, marking the start of a project to improve export logistics and local employment.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the Multi-Modal Logistics Park at Sindi Railway?

  • Project description. The Multi-Modal Logistics Park is being developed on 150 acres near Sindi Railway station to facilitate direct exports and improve logistics infrastructure.
  • Developing bodies. The project is developed by National Highways Logistics Management Limited (NHLML), a special purpose vehicle of NHAI, in partnership with Jawaharlal Nehru Port Authority and AMARA TRANZ Solution.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How will the park benefit trade and transportation?

  • Export facilitation. It will enable direct export of cotton, cotton lint, yarn, oranges, and vegetables from Vidarbha to international markets via Jawaharlal Nehru Port Authority.
  • Cost and time reduction. Transportation costs per container are expected to reduce from ₹1.5 lakh to ₹1 lakh by bypassing Mumbai and using the dry port route via Haldia, and transportation time will decrease on the Mumbai–Colombo–Dhaka route.
  • Cargo operations. The first rake carrying Maruti cars has already started operations, indicating the park's operational readiness.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What facilities and employment opportunities will the park provide?

  • Infrastructure facilities. The park will have cold storage, warehouses, and exporter clusters to consolidate industries from Nagpur and Wardha.
  • Customs and container handling. A customs office will be established to handle containers from across India and abroad, strengthening logistics and export infrastructure.
  • Employment generation. The project is expected to create employment opportunities for local residents.

Back to basics: Multi-Modal Logistics Park

A Multi-Modal Logistics Park integrates various modes of transport such as rail, road, and waterways to streamline the movement of goods. It typically includes facilities like warehouses, cold storage, and customs offices to facilitate efficient cargo handling and export-import operations. Such parks reduce transportation costs and time, benefiting producers and exporters.
