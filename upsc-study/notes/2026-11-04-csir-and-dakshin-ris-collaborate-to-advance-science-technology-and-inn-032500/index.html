CSIR and DAKSHIN-RIS Collaborate to Boost Science and Innovation for Global South

The Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) and DAKSHIN – Global South Centre of Excellence at RIS signed a Letter of Association on 3 November 2026 to advance science, technology, and innovation among Global South countries. This partnership aims to foster capacity building, technology transfer, and collaborative solutions addressing developmental challenges relevant to the Global South.

CSIR and DAKSHIN-RIS partnership enhances South-South cooperation by sharing science and technology solutions tailored for the Global South's development needs.

Why is this in the news?

On 3 November 2026, CSIR and DAKSHIN-RIS signed a Letter of Association to strengthen science and technology collaboration for countries of the Global South.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the purpose of the CSIR and DAKSHIN-RIS collaboration?

  • Shared vision. The partnership aims to strengthen capacity building, research collaboration, and technology partnerships to advance countries of the Global South.
  • Focus areas. It targets sustainable, affordable, and locally relevant solutions through knowledge sharing, technology transfer, and collaborative strategies.
  • Developmental goals. The collaboration supports progress towards the Sustainable Development Goals by addressing common challenges faced by Global South countries.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How will the collaboration operate and what are its objectives?

  • Technology and expertise. CSIR contributes its scientific knowledge and a portfolio of ready-to-deploy, affordable technologies relevant to the Global South's developmental needs.
  • Key objectives. These include fostering joint policy studies, scaling development interventions, promoting capacity building and skill upgradation, and sharing best practices to enhance collective self-reliance.
  • Areas of cooperation. The partnership focuses on healthcare, infrastructure, agriculture, the blue economy, and the digital economy.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does this collaboration matter for India and global cooperation?

  • South-South cooperation. The association provides a platform to enhance South-South cooperation by catalysing international partnerships and facilitating technology transfer among developing countries.
  • Inclusive growth. By leveraging CSIR's expertise and DAKSHIN's global engagement, the collaboration supports inclusive growth and sustainable development across the Global South.
  • Science diplomacy. This partnership exemplifies India's role in science diplomacy and its commitment to collaborative innovation with developing nations.

Back to basics: South-South Cooperation in Science and Technology

South-South cooperation refers to collaboration among developing countries to share knowledge, skills, resources, and technologies for mutual development. It aims to address common challenges through joint efforts rather than relying solely on aid from developed countries. Science and technology partnerships under this framework facilitate capacity building, innovation, and sustainable development tailored to the specific needs of these countries.
