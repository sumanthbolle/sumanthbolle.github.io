Maharashtra Budget Allocates ₹160 Crore for Primitive Tribes Development

The Maharashtra Government has allocated ₹160 crore in the 2026-27 budget for the development of primitive tribes and the Pardhi community. This allocation includes grants for individual and collective benefit schemes to improve basic amenities and social welfare.

Maharashtra's 2026-27 budget earmarks ₹160 crore for the development of primitive tribes and the Pardhi community through targeted grants and welfare schemes.

Why is this in the news?

The Maharashtra Government announced a special package of ₹160 crore for primitive tribes and the Pardhi community in the 2026-27 budget, formalized by a Government Resolution on 2 November 2026.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What does the ₹160 crore package cover?

  • Allocation details. The package includes ₹130 crore reserved for primitive tribes and ₹30 crore for the Pardhi community, with funds divided between basic amenities and individual and collective benefit schemes.
  • Grant provisions. Individual schemes receive a minimum grant of ₹50,000, while collective schemes for two or more beneficiaries can receive up to ₹7.5 lakh.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How will the funds be used for tribal development?

  • Basic amenities and welfare. ₹100 crore of the primitive tribes' allocation is for basic amenities, and ₹30 crore is for benefit schemes; for the Pardhi community, ₹10 crore is for basic amenities and ₹20 crore for benefit schemes.
  • Support for beneficiaries. The Tribal Development Department will cover beneficiaries' share if they cannot pay, encouraging formation of women’s Self-Help Groups with grants up to ₹7.5 lakh for collective ventures.
  • Additional services. Funds will support awareness drives, issuance of essential documents, health check-ups, education initiatives, and residential camps for children of migrant families.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why is this budget allocation important for tribal welfare?

  • Focused tribal support. The package targets primitive tribes like Katkari, Kolam, and Madia, along with the Pardhi community, addressing their socio-economic development needs.
  • Inclusive welfare approach. By providing 100% government grants and supporting women’s empowerment through SHGs, the scheme aims to enhance economic and social inclusion.

Back to basics: Government Budgeting and Welfare Schemes

Government budgeting involves allocating financial resources to various sectors and schemes for planned development. Welfare schemes are designed to support vulnerable groups by providing financial assistance, basic amenities, and social services to improve their living conditions and promote inclusive growth.
