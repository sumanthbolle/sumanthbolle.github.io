RBI Releases Foreign Exchange Turnover Data for Multiple Weeks in October 2026

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) published detailed foreign exchange turnover data for the weeks of September 28 to October 1, October 5 to 9, and October 12 to 16, 2026. This data provides insights into daily merchant and inter-bank transactions in foreign exchange, crucial for understanding forex market activity and external sector health.

The RBI's foreign exchange turnover data for early October 2026 reveals daily merchant and inter-bank forex transactions, reflecting market dynamics and external sector trends.

Why is this in the news?

The RBI released foreign exchange turnover data for three weeks in October 2026 on November 5, 2026, showing detailed daily merchant and inter-bank forex transactions in USD millions.

What does the RBI foreign exchange turnover data show?

  • Data coverage. The data covers daily merchant and inter-bank foreign exchange transactions for the periods September 28 to October 1, October 5 to 9, and October 12 to 16, 2026.
  • Transaction categories. Transactions are categorized into spot, forward, cancellation of forwards, swaps, and are further divided into foreign currency/INR and foreign currency/foreign currency segments.
  • Transaction values. All figures are reported in USD millions, showing both purchases and sales volumes in the merchant and inter-bank segments.

Why does this data matter for UPSC preparation?

  • Indicator of forex market activity. The turnover data reflects the volume and nature of forex market transactions, indicating liquidity and demand-supply dynamics in the external sector.
  • External sector health. Understanding forex turnover helps analyze India's external sector stability, currency demand, and international trade financing.
  • Policy implications. The RBI and policymakers use this data to monitor market functioning and guide interventions in currency and forex markets.

How are the foreign exchange transactions classified?

  • Merchant segment. Includes forex transactions by corporates and other entities for trade and business purposes.
  • Inter-bank segment. Covers transactions between banks and financial institutions in the forex market.
  • Transaction types. Spot transactions involve immediate settlement, forwards are contracts for future settlement, cancellations reverse forward contracts, and swaps combine spot and forward deals.

Back to basics: Foreign Exchange Market

The foreign exchange market is where currencies are bought and sold. It includes spot transactions settled immediately and forward contracts settled on a future date. Banks, corporates, and financial institutions participate in this market to facilitate international trade, investment, and currency risk management. The Reserve Bank of India monitors this market to maintain currency stability and external sector health.
