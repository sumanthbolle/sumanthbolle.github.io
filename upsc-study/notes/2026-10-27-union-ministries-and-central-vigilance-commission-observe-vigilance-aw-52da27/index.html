Union Ministries Observe Vigilance Awareness Week 2026 with Integrity Pledge

Several Union Ministries including Health and Family Welfare, Social Justice and Empowerment, and Law and Justice observed Vigilance Awareness Week 2026 from October 26 to November 1, reaffirming their commitment to transparency, accountability, and ethical governance through Integrity Pledge ceremonies. This observance highlights the government's ongoing efforts to combat corruption and promote probity in public administration, a key topic for governance and accountability in UPSC preparation.

Vigilance Awareness Week 2026 saw Union Ministries taking Integrity Pledges to strengthen transparency, accountability, and ethical conduct in public service.

What is Vigilance Awareness Week and why is it observed?

  • Annual observance. Vigilance Awareness Week is observed annually during the week of October 26 to November 1, coinciding with the birth anniversary of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel on October 31.
  • Purpose. The week aims to promote honesty, integrity, transparency, and accountability in public administration to build public trust and combat corruption.
  • Theme 2026. The theme for 2026 is “सत्यनिष्ठा से समृद्धि” (Probity for Prosperity), emphasizing the role of ethical conduct as a pillar of good governance.

How did Union Ministries participate in Vigilance Awareness Week 2026?

  • Integrity Pledge ceremonies. Ministries including Health and Family Welfare, Social Justice and Empowerment, and Law and Justice conducted Integrity Pledge ceremonies led by senior officials to reaffirm commitment to ethical governance.
  • Key participants. Union Health Minister Shri J. P. Nadda, Union Secretary Shri Sudhansh Pant, and Additional Secretary Dr. Manoj Kumar administered the pledge in their respective ministries.
  • Virtual and physical participation. Officers and officials participated both physically and virtually, demonstrating collective resolve to uphold probity and transparency.

Why does Vigilance Awareness Week matter for governance and public administration?

  • Promotes ethical conduct. The observance reinforces the importance of honesty, integrity, and transparency as essential for effective and citizen-centric public service.
  • Strengthens accountability. It encourages officials to act in public interest, report corruption, and maintain high standards of probity in discharge of duties.
  • Supports welfare delivery. Transparent and efficient administration ensures timely delivery of government welfare schemes to eligible beneficiaries.
  • Builds public trust. Sustained vigilance and ethical governance help sustain public confidence in government institutions.

Back to basics: Integrity and Vigilance in Public Administration

Integrity refers to adherence to moral and ethical principles, ensuring honesty and fairness in public service. Vigilance involves constant watchfulness to prevent corruption and malpractice. Together, they form the foundation of accountable governance, promoting transparency and trust between government and citizens.
