Key Takeaways from Quad, ASEAN-India, and East Asia Summits in Manila

Prime Minister Narendra Modi participated in the Quad, ASEAN-India, and East Asia Summits held in Manila in November 2026, marking India's active engagement in regional multilateral diplomacy. These summits highlighted India's efforts to broaden its 'Act East' and Indo-Pacific policies amid shifting global power dynamics.

India's participation in the 2026 Manila summits reflects its strategic intent to deepen regional cooperation and security partnerships in the Indo-Pacific.

Why is this in the news?

Prime Minister Narendra Modi attended the Quad, ASEAN-India, and East Asia Summits on the sidelines of the ASEAN summits in Manila in November 2026, signaling a renewed focus on multilateral engagement in the Indo-Pacific region.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What were the key summits India participated in at Manila?

  • Three major summits. India took part in the Quad Summit, ASEAN-India Summit, and East Asia Summit held back-to-back in Manila in November 2026.
  • Quad Summit participants. The Quad Summit included India, the US, Japan, and Australia, with leaders such as US President Donald Trump and Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi attending.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does India’s participation in these summits matter?

  • Broadening Act East policy. India’s engagement reflects a growing desire to work more closely with ASEAN and Indo-Pacific partners, broadening its Act East and Indo-Pacific strategies.
  • Countering unilateralism. These multilateral meetings come at a time when global powers are increasingly acting unilaterally, highlighting India’s preference for cooperative regional frameworks.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What was the context of India’s previous ASEAN engagement?

  • 2025 ASEAN summit absence. Prime Minister Modi had skipped the 2025 ASEAN summits in Kuala Lumpur due to domestic commitments and trade tensions with the US.

Back to basics: Multilateral Summits and Indo-Pacific Policy

Multilateral summits like the Quad, ASEAN-India, and East Asia Summit are platforms where multiple countries discuss regional cooperation, security, and economic issues. India's 'Act East' policy aims to strengthen ties with Southeast Asian nations and Indo-Pacific countries to promote stability and development in the region. The Indo-Pacific strategy involves collaboration among democracies to ensure a free, open, and inclusive maritime domain.
