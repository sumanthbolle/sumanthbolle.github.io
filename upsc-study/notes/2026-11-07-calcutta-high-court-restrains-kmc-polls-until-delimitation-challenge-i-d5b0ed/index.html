Calcutta High Court restrains KMC polls over delimitation challenge

The Calcutta High Court has restrained the West Bengal State Election Commission from conducting Kolkata Municipal Corporation elections based on new ward delimitations until a legal challenge is resolved. This case highlights judicial intervention in electoral processes and issues of delimitation and gerrymandering relevant for UPSC aspirants.

The Calcutta High Court has stayed KMC elections pending resolution of a petition challenging the delimitation of wards increasing them from 144 to 209.

Why is this in the news?

On October 6, 2026, the Calcutta High Court restrained the West Bengal State Election Commission from issuing notifications for KMC elections based on new ward delimitations pending a petition challenging the delimitation exercise.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the legal issue behind the KMC election restraint?

  • Delimitation challenge. The West Bengal government increased KMC wards from 144 to 209 through a delimitation exercise challenged in court for alleged gerrymandering to favor the ruling party.
  • Court's interim order. The Calcutta High Court restrained the State Election Commission from issuing election notifications based on the new wards until the petition is decided.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does the delimitation matter for elections?

  • Impact on electoral fairness. The petitioner argued that the delimitation was done to secure partisan advantage without considering geographical contiguity, compactness, or administrative convenience.
  • Legal powers involved. The State government claims it has power under Section 601 of the KMC Act to modify ward schedules irrespective of recommendations from the Corporation.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the significance of the court's intervention?

  • Judicial check on election process. The court's interim protection prevents the election process from proceeding on potentially flawed delimitation, safeguarding democratic fairness.
  • Uncertainty in election timing. The stay creates uncertainty over holding KMC elections, which the new BJP government was keen to conduct soon after taking office.

Back to basics: Delimitation and Judicial Review in Elections

Delimitation is the process of redrawing boundaries of electoral constituencies to reflect population changes and ensure fair representation. Courts can intervene through judicial review if delimitation is challenged as unfair or politically motivated, to uphold democratic principles and prevent gerrymandering.
