Union Cabinet Approves Fertilizer Subsidy and MSME Credit Support

The Union Cabinet allocated ₹62,370 crore for fertilizer subsidies for the Rabi season and ₹5,000 crore for credit support to small businesses. These decisions highlight government priorities in agriculture and MSME sectors, relevant for UPSC aspirants studying economic policies.

The Union Cabinet’s approval of large fertilizer subsidies and MSME credit support reflects focused government intervention in agriculture and small business growth.

Why is this in the news?

On November 11, 2026, the BJP welcomed the Union Cabinet’s decisions on fertilizer subsidies and MSME credit support, emphasizing their importance for farmers and small businesses.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What decisions did the Union Cabinet approve regarding farmers and MSMEs?

  • Fertilizer subsidy allocation. The Cabinet approved ₹62,370 crore for fertilizer subsidies for the Rabi season to ensure adequate availability for farmers.
  • Credit support for MSMEs. An allocation of ₹5,000 crore was earmarked to facilitate credit support for small business enterprises.

  • Support to agriculture. Fertilizer subsidy ensures farmers have access to essential inputs, impacting agricultural productivity and rural livelihoods.
  • Boost to MSMEs. Credit support strengthens micro, small and medium enterprises, which are key for employment and economic growth.
  • Policy focus. The decisions reflect government priorities in sustaining agriculture and MSMEs, important topics in economic development and governance.

Back to basics: Government Subsidies and Credit Support

Subsidies are financial aids provided by the government to reduce the cost of essential inputs like fertilizers for farmers, helping increase agricultural productivity. Credit support involves facilitating loans or financial assistance to small businesses to encourage entrepreneurship and economic growth. Both are tools of economic policy used to support key sectors and promote inclusive development.
