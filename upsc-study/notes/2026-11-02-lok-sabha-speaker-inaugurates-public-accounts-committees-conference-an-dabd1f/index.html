Lok Sabha Speaker Highlights Role of PACs and Dissent in Democracy

Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla inaugurated the Conference of Chairpersons of Public Accounts Committees (PACs) in Thiruvananthapuram on 3 November 2026, emphasizing the evolving role of PACs in audit and accountability. He also called on lawmakers to uphold dissent as a democratic strength during the Haryana Vidhan Sabha's Janpratinidhi Sammelan on 2 November 2026.

The Lok Sabha Speaker underscored the importance of Public Accounts Committees in legislative oversight and the vital role of dissent in strengthening democracy.

What is the role of Public Accounts Committees (PACs) in India?

  • Legislative oversight of public funds. PACs examine public expenditure to ensure funds are used according to legislative intent, promoting transparency, accountability, and financial discipline.
  • Conference theme and purpose. The 2026 conference in Thiruvananthapuram focused on 'Audit, Accountability and Good Governance – The Evolving Role of the PAC' to share best practices and strengthen PAC effectiveness.
  • Participants and significance. Chairpersons and members of PACs from Parliament and State/UT legislatures participated, highlighting PACs as key parliamentary institutions for responsible governance.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why did the Lok Sabha Speaker emphasize dissent in democracy?

  • Dissent as a democratic strength. Om Birla stated that agreement and disagreement are two sides of the same coin, and dissent must be welcomed as it brings fresh perspectives and improves governance.
  • Commitment beyond political differences. He urged lawmakers to focus on the progress of the State and nation regardless of party or ideological differences.
  • Constitution as moral compass. The Speaker described the Constitution as the living soul of India, reflecting national consciousness and aspirations.
  • Governance as grassroots empowerment. He emphasized that governance should empower the last person in society, aiming for socio-economic transformation and happiness for vulnerable sections.

How do these events relate to parliamentary functioning and democratic principles?

  • PACs strengthen accountability. By scrutinizing public expenditure, PACs uphold the principle of responsible governance and legislative accountability.
  • Dissent fosters better governance. Respecting dissent ensures diverse viewpoints are considered, preventing administrative blind spots and enhancing democratic deliberation.
  • Legislative dignity and public welfare. High-quality debates and reasoned dialogue in legislatures, as praised in Haryana, reinforce democratic institutions rooted in public welfare.

Back to basics: Public Accounts Committees and Democratic Dissent

Public Accounts Committees (PACs) are parliamentary committees responsible for examining government expenditure to ensure it complies with legislative approval and promotes accountability. Democratic dissent refers to the right and practice of expressing disagreement within a democracy, which helps bring new ideas, correct errors, and improve governance. Both PACs and dissent are essential for a functioning parliamentary democracy, ensuring transparency, accountability, and inclusive decision-making.
