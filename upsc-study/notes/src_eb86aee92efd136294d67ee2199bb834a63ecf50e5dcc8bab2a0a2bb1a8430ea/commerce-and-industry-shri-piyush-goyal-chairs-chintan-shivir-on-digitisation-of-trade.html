India implications

  • Inclusive digital commerce can widen small-retailer participation if access, affordability and interoperability are preserved.
  • Better procurement visibility may improve supply-chain efficiency and reduce information asymmetry between kiranas and suppliers.
  • A procurement-linked transaction trail could support formal working-capital finance, subject to responsible lending and data protection.
  • Policy design should prevent digital exclusion and ensure that open networks complement, rather than displace, local distribution relationships.

Way forward

  • Provide assisted onboarding, vernacular interfaces, training and low-cost access so digital capability does not become a barrier.
  • Adopt open standards, portable digital identities, interoperable catalogues and transparent participation rules.
  • Protect retailer and transaction data through purpose limitation, consent, cybersecurity and accessible grievance redress.
  • Create responsible procurement-linked credit with transparent pricing, suitability checks and safeguards against over-indebtedness.
