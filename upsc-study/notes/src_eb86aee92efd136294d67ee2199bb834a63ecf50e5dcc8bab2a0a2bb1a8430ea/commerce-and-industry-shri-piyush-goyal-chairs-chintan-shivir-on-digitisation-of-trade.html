Static anchor

Open digital infrastructure is interoperable digital architecture that allows different participants and systems to interact through common interfaces and standards, rather than requiring dependence on one closed platform. In this issue, it is applied to retailer procurement, distribution and related services.

Background

  • General Trade comprises neighbourhood retailers, FMCG companies, distributors and wholesalers. The source frames it as central to India’s retail economy and livelihoods of millions of neighbourhood retailers.
  • ONDC DigiDukaan approaches kirana digitisation from the buyer side: retailers use a single interface to discover and order products from multiple brands, distributors and wholesalers.
  • The proposed ecosystem links digital procurement with possible shared logistics, formal credit and other services; these are intended to complement, not replace, existing distribution relationships.

India implications

  • Inclusive digital commerce can extend the benefits of interoperable infrastructure beyond consumer-facing online sales to small-business procurement.
  • Greater visibility of prices, schemes and availability may improve retailer decision-making and reduce avoidable procurement frictions.
  • Digital procurement records could support more formal working-capital channels, but the source does not establish that credit access has already expanded.
  • Shared logistics and micro-warehousing could improve replenishment and reach in areas outside existing delivery routes, subject to commercially sustainable implementation.

Way forward

  • Create simple, multilingual and assisted onboarding for kirana retailers, with local support for digital capability.
  • Adopt interoperable catalogues, common identifiers and plug-and-play connectors while maintaining clear accountability among network participants.
  • Link any transaction-based credit carefully to transparent consent, privacy protection and responsible lending standards.
  • Design shared logistics and micro-warehousing around commercially viable service levels, including underserved areas.
