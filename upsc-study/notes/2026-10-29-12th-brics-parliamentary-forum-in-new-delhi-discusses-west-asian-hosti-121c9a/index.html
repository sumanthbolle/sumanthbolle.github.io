12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum Addresses West Asian Hostilities and Sustainable Development

The 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum was held in New Delhi on 26-27 October 2026, focusing on sustainability and inclusive development while expressing deep concern over escalating tensions in West Asia. The forum issued a joint declaration supporting a two-state solution for the Israel-Palestine conflict and condemned unilateral sanctions and terrorism.

The 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum reaffirmed commitment to sustainable development and peaceful resolution of West Asian conflicts, emphasizing parliamentary roles in global governance.

Why is this in the news?

The 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum held in New Delhi on 26-27 October 2026 adopted a joint declaration addressing global challenges including West Asian hostilities and sustainable development.

What were the key themes of the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum?

  • Sustainability focus. The forum's theme was 'Fostering Parliaments for People-Centric Development,' emphasizing sustainability as central to parliamentary roles in fostering inclusive futures for nations.
  • Global developmental challenges. Challenges highlighted included climate change, biodiversity loss, resource scarcity, food and energy insecurity, and growing inequalities, with only 15% of SDG targets on track for 2030.
  • Parliamentary role. Parliaments were recognized as vital for legislation, budget oversight, accountability, and bridging global commitments with local action to achieve sustainable development goals.
  • India’s developmental philosophy. India’s approach is rooted in 'Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam' (the world is one family), promoting people-centric, inclusive, and equitable development aligned with its vision for 2047.

[2] Press Information Bureau[3] Press Information Bureau

How did the forum address West Asian hostilities?

  • Expression of deep concern. The forum expressed deep concern over escalating tensions and hostilities in West Asia, urging maximum restraint and avoidance of actions that could worsen the situation.
  • Two-state solution support. The joint declaration reaffirmed support for a peaceful resolution of the Israeli-Palestinian conflict based on a two-state solution, respecting international law and UN resolutions.
  • Condemnation of unilateral sanctions. The forum condemned unilateral coercive measures and economic sanctions in the region as violations of international law that negatively impact human rights and development.
  • Terrorism condemnation. Acts of terrorism were strongly condemned, including the 2025 Pahalgam terror attack, with calls for global cooperation to combat terrorism without associating it with any religion or ethnicity.

What are the implications for India’s foreign policy and parliamentary diplomacy?

  • BRICS Chairship role. India’s 2026 BRICS Chairship guided the forum’s agenda, reflecting its commitment to resilience, innovation, cooperation, and sustainability in multilateral diplomacy.
  • Strengthening BRICS cooperation. The forum reaffirmed legislative support to strengthen BRICS strategic partnerships across political, economic, and cultural pillars within constitutional mandates.
  • Parliamentary diplomacy. Inter-parliamentary dialogue was emphasized as a channel to build trust, support peaceful dispute resolution, and amplify the Global South’s voice in international forums.
  • Economic and security cooperation. Discussions included cooperation on counter-terrorism, digital technologies, AI, and trade, highlighting India-Russia ties and the goal of USD 100 billion bilateral trade by 2030.

Back to basics: Role of Parliament in International Diplomacy

Parliaments play a crucial role in shaping a country's foreign policy through legislation, oversight, and representation. Parliamentary diplomacy involves dialogue and cooperation between legislatures of different countries to build mutual understanding, support peaceful dispute resolution, and promote shared development goals. This complements executive diplomacy and strengthens democratic accountability in international relations.
