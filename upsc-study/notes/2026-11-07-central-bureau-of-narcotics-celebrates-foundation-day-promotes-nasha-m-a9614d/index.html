Central Bureau of Narcotics Foundation Day Highlights Social Campaigns

The Central Bureau of Narcotics celebrated its foundation day by promoting the ‘Nasha Mukt Yuva’ and ‘Bal Vivah Mukt Bharat’ campaigns, addressing drug abuse and child marriage. These campaigns are significant for UPSC aspirants as they reflect government efforts in social welfare, public health, and legal enforcement.

The Central Bureau of Narcotics supports nationwide campaigns to reduce drug abuse and child marriage, combining legal action, awareness, and community participation.

Why is this in the news?

On its foundation day, the Central Bureau of Narcotics promoted the ‘Nasha Mukt Yuva’ drug awareness quiz and the ‘Bal Vivah Mukt Bharat’ campaign against child marriage, highlighting government initiatives in social welfare and public health as of November 2026.

What is the ‘Nasha Mukt Yuva’ campaign?

  • Campaign overview. Launched under the Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment, it aims to reduce drug abuse through preventive education, awareness, treatment, and rehabilitation.
  • Quiz participation. The online ‘Nasha Mukt Yuva, Viksit Bharat Quiz’ started on October 26, 2026, with over 16,517 participants by November 5, 2026, and is open until November 25, 2026.
  • Reach and impact. The campaign has reached over 35.70 crore people, including 14.26 crore youth and 11.24 crore women, with more than 39.91 lakh persons treated and rehabilitated through over 755 de-addiction centres.
  • Community involvement. More than 46.70 lakh Nasha Mukt pledges have been taken, and over 2 lakh Nasha Mukti Mitras registered to support drug-free communities.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the ‘Bal Vivah Mukt Bharat’ campaign?

  • Campaign purpose. Launched on November 27, 2024, by the Ministry of Women and Child Development, it aims to prevent child marriage through legal enforcement, public awareness, and community participation.
  • Legal framework. The campaign operates under the Prohibition of Child Marriage Act, 2006, which prohibits child marriage, defines penalties, and empowers Child Marriage Prohibition Officers (CMPOs) for prevention and enforcement.
  • Campaign achievements. By November 2026, 6.85 lakh awareness programmes were conducted, reaching 12.47 crore participants, with 42.01 lakh pledges registered and 4,536 child marriage incidents reported on the campaign portal.
  • Prevalence decline. Child marriage prevalence among women aged 20-24 declined from 23.3% in NFHS-5 (2019-21) to 20.1% in NFHS-6 (2023-24), showing progress but uneven across states.

[3] Press Information Bureau

How do these campaigns use e-governance and community participation?

  • Digital platforms. The ‘Nasha Mukt Yuva’ quiz is accessible via the MY Bharat portal, while the Bal Vivah Mukt Bharat campaign uses a dedicated portal for reporting, awareness tracking, and pledge registration.
  • Community engagement. Both campaigns mobilise citizens, especially youth, through pledges, quizzes, awareness programmes, and involvement of local institutions and public representatives.
  • Monitoring and enforcement. The Bal Vivah Mukt Bharat portal provides data on CMPOs, incident reporting, and awareness activities, enabling evidence-based monitoring and timely preventive action.

Back to basics: Social Welfare Campaigns and Legal Frameworks

Social welfare campaigns combine legal provisions, public awareness, and community participation to address social issues like drug abuse and child marriage. Laws provide enforcement mechanisms and penalties, while campaigns use education and community engagement to change social norms and encourage reporting and prevention.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements: 1. The ‘Nasha Mukt Yuva, Viksit Bharat Quiz’ is conducted by the Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment under the Nasha Mukt Bharat Abhiyaan. 2. The Bal Vivah Mukt Bharat campaign operates under the Child Marriage Restraint Act, 1929. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the Bal Vivah Mukt Bharat campaign operates under the Prohibition of Child Marriage Act, 2006, which replaced the Child Marriage Restraint Act, 1929.
