NIELIT to Host 3rd National Conference on Cyber Security and Digital Forensics

The National Institute of Electronics & Information Technology (NIELIT) will organize the 3rd National Conference on Cyber Security, Digital Forensics & Intelligence (NCCDFI 2026) in New Delhi on November 16-17, 2026. This conference is important for UPSC aspirants as it highlights India's efforts to strengthen cybersecurity, digital forensics, and collaboration among key stakeholders in the digital ecosystem.

NIELIT's NCCDFI 2026 aims to fortify India's digital future by enhancing cybersecurity and digital forensics capabilities through multi-sector collaboration.

Why is this in the news?

NIELIT announced the 3rd National Conference on Cyber Security, Digital Forensics & Intelligence (NCCDFI 2026) to be held in New Delhi on November 16-17, 2026, focusing on strengthening India's cybersecurity ecosystem.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the NCCDFI 2026 conference and its significance?

  • Conference overview. NCCDFI 2026 is the third edition of a national conference organized by NIELIT under the Ministry of Electronics & IT, scheduled for November 16-17, 2026, in New Delhi, themed 'Cyber Secure Bharat: Fortifying India’s Digital Future.'
  • Importance in digital era. The conference addresses the growing dependence on digital technologies and the rise of sophisticated cyber threats, making cybersecurity and digital investigations critical national priorities.
  • Evolution of the conference. Starting in 2024 as a regional event in Nagaland, NCCDFI expanded nationally with its second edition in Assam in 2025, and now aims for wider participation and stronger collaboration in 2026.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Who participates and what are the focus areas?

  • Stakeholders involved. Participants include government, law enforcement, defence, judiciary, technology providers, industry, academia, and cybersecurity experts.
  • Key thematic tracks. The conference covers 10 tracks: Cybersecurity, Digital Forensics, AI Security, Critical Infrastructure, Threat Intelligence, Blockchain & Crypto, Drone & IoT Forensics, Digital Evidence, Cyber Law, and Cloud Security.
  • Technology and collaboration. It features technology demonstrations, policy dialogues, and explores emerging technologies like Artificial Intelligence, Blockchain Security, and Cyber Intelligence.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the major activities and outcomes planned?

  • Conference format. The two-day event includes inaugural and technical sessions, panel discussions, an industry exhibition, and a cybersecurity and AI hackathon called Cyber Kushti 2026.
  • Hackathon details. Cyber Kushti 2026 is a three-level competition themed on Review, Attack, and Defend, with 1,031 teams registering and 10 teams qualifying for the final level.
  • Institutional partnerships. NIELIT will sign MoUs with industry partners to strengthen cooperation in research, capacity building, and knowledge exchange in cybersecurity and digital forensics.

Back to basics: Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics

Cybersecurity involves protecting computer systems, networks, and data from unauthorized access or attacks. Digital forensics is the process of investigating and analyzing digital devices and data to uncover evidence of cybercrimes. Both fields are essential for national security, law enforcement, and protecting citizens in the digital age.
