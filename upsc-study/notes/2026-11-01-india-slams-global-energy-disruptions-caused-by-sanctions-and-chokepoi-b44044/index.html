India Criticizes Global Energy Disruptions from Sanctions and Chokepoints

At the G20 Foreign Ministers’ Meeting in October 2026, India condemned deliberate disruptions in global energy supplies caused by sanctions and chokepoints. This stance highlights India's concerns about energy security and the impact of geopolitical actions on global markets, relevant for understanding India's foreign policy and economic challenges.

India opposes deliberate global energy supply disruptions through sanctions and chokepoints, emphasizing the need for stable and predictable energy markets.

Why is this in the news?

India’s External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar criticized global energy market disruptions caused by sanctions and chokepoints at the G20 Foreign Ministers’ Meeting on October 30, 2026.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What did India say about global energy disruptions?

  • Condemnation of disruptions. India strongly disapproved of deliberate disruptions in global oil supplies through sanctions and chokepoints, calling them a calculated strategy to hold energy prices hostage.
  • Nature of the crisis. India described the current energy crisis as one of unpredictability and disruption rather than production shortages, leading to unaffordable costs and challenges in access.
  • Impact on low-income countries. India highlighted that continuous distortions in energy markets occur with increasing frequency and complexity, with little regard for the difficulties faced by low-income countries.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does India’s stance on energy disruptions matter for the exam?

  • Energy security and foreign policy. India’s criticism reflects its concern for reliable and predictable energy supplies, which is crucial for its economic stability and foreign relations.
  • Global interdependence. India emphasized the mutual dependence in global energy markets and the need for the G20 to ensure open and predictable energy trade.
  • Focus on clean energy needs. India reaffirmed the importance of stable LPG supplies for clean cooking, linking energy security to social welfare.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What solutions and initiatives did India propose?

  • Critical minerals mission. India mentioned its National Mission on critical minerals, covering exploration to recycling, to support energy transition.
  • G20 Critical Minerals Circularity Initiative. India proposed this initiative to enhance sustainable access to critical minerals needed for energy technologies.
  • Calls for financial and technological support. India urged the G20 to widen access to finance and advance technologies like energy storage, small modular reactors, green hydrogen, and biofuels.

Back to basics: Energy security

Energy security means ensuring reliable, affordable, and uninterrupted access to energy resources. It is vital for economic growth and social welfare. Disruptions in supply due to geopolitical conflicts, sanctions, or chokepoints can cause price volatility and impact development.

  1. 1 only

Answer and trap

Answer: (c). A trap would be to confuse the timing or nature of India’s proposals; both statements are supported by the evidence from the G20 meeting in 2026.

Conclusion: India’s position highlights the importance of stable energy markets for its development and foreign policy.
