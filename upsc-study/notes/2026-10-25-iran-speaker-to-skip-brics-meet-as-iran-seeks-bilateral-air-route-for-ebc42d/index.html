Iran Speaker Skips BRICS Meet Amid India-Iran Air Route Dispute

Iran's parliamentary speaker Mohammad Baqer Ghalibaf will not attend the BRICS Parliamentary Forum in New Delhi due to issues over India's refusal to allow direct flights carrying medicines to Iran. This highlights challenges in India-Iran relations linked to US sanctions and humanitarian aid logistics, relevant for understanding India's foreign policy and regional diplomacy.

Iran's speaker skipped the BRICS meet as India denied direct air route for medicines citing US sanctions, underscoring complexities in humanitarian aid and international diplomacy.

Why is this in the news?

On October 24, 2026, Iran announced its parliamentary speaker would not attend the BRICS Parliamentary Forum in New Delhi due to India's refusal to allow direct flights carrying medicines to Iran, citing US sanctions concerns.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why is Iran's Speaker skipping the BRICS Parliamentary Forum?

  • Official announcement. Iran's Embassy in New Delhi stated that Speaker Mohammad Baqer Ghalibaf will not attend the BRICS Parliamentary Forum on October 26-27, 2026, due to unforeseen circumstances.
  • Underlying cause. The decision follows India's refusal to permit Iran Air to fly medicines directly from India to Tehran, fearing US sanctions on Indian airports and air operators hosting sanctioned Iranian airlines.
  • Iran's position. Iran argues that humanitarian aid, including medicines, is exempt from international sanctions and urges India to operationalise the bilateral air route for humanitarian support.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the challenges in transporting medicines from India to Iran?

  • Third-country transit. India suggests medicines can be sent through third countries like Armenia or Oman to avoid sanctions on direct flights.
  • Risks to medicine quality. Iran highlights that temperature-sensitive medicines have become unusable due to long transit times and multiple cargo transfers through third countries.
  • Historical air route. The direct Delhi-Tehran air connection is one of the oldest in the region, allowing medicines to reach Iran in three hours, compared to 24-48 hours via indirect routes.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for India's foreign policy and regional diplomacy?

  • BRICS membership. Iran is a BRICS member and reaffirms commitment to active participation, making the dispute significant for multilateral relations.
  • Sanctions impact. India balances humanitarian concerns with compliance to US sanctions, affecting its diplomatic relations with Iran.
  • Humanitarian diplomacy. The issue highlights the complexities of delivering humanitarian aid amid geopolitical sanctions and corporate resistance within India.

Back to basics: BRICS and Sanctions Impact on Diplomacy

BRICS is a multilateral grouping of emerging economies that collaborates on economic and political issues. International sanctions are restrictions imposed by countries or groups to influence another country's actions. These sanctions can affect trade, finance, and aviation, complicating humanitarian aid delivery and diplomatic relations. Understanding how sanctions impact bilateral and multilateral diplomacy is key to analyzing India's foreign policy decisions.
