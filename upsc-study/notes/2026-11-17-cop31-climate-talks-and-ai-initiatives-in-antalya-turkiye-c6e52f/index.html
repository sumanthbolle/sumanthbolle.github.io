India's Climate Diplomacy and Big Cat Conservation at COP31 in Türkiye

At COP31 in Antalya, India advocated for a country-driven Just Transition Mechanism emphasizing finance, technology transfer, and capacity building for developing countries. The event also highlighted India's tiger conservation as a nature-based climate solution strengthening ecosystem resilience and livelihoods.

India called for practical, sovereign-respecting climate transition support and showcased big cat conservation as a key climate mitigation strategy at COP31.

Why is this in the news?

India's Environment Minister Bhupender Yadav presented India's climate priorities and conservation initiatives at COP31 in Antalya, Türkiye, on November 16, 2026.

What did India propose at COP31 regarding climate transition?

  • Just Transition Mechanism. India called for a two-tier, Party-guided Just Transition Mechanism that respects national sovereignty and diverse national circumstances, focusing on practical, country-driven implementation.
  • Key focus areas. The mechanism should address employment, social protection, adaptation, poverty eradication, energy access, and sustainable development with adequate finance, technology transfer, and capacity building.
  • Integration across policies. India emphasized integrating just transition considerations across adaptation, livelihoods, and development planning, citing its sub-national heat action and renewable energy expansion plans.
  • Distinct roles. India proposed distinct roles for the UAE Just Transition Work Programme (dialogue platform) and the proposed Mechanism (practical support via technical assistance and cooperation).

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does big cat conservation relate to climate change mitigation?

  • Ecosystem protection. Big cat conservation helps protect carbon-rich ecosystems like forests, grasslands, wetlands, and mountains, which store carbon and prevent emissions from habitat degradation.
  • India's tiger conservation model. India's Project Tiger, supported by legislation and the National Tiger Conservation Authority, has increased tiger population to 3,682 in 2022, protecting 58 Tiger Reserves spanning about 85,000 sq. km.
  • Ecosystem services. Tiger reserves provide vital ecosystem services including carbon sequestration, water purification, soil conservation, groundwater recharge, and support over 600 rivers.
  • Community involvement. Community participation and sustainable livelihood initiatives have made tiger conservation inclusive and a driver of green employment, generating about 6 million man-days annually.
  • International collaboration. The International Big Cat Alliance, envisioned by India's Prime Minister, facilitates knowledge exchange and cooperation for global big cat species recovery.

[2] Press Information Bureau

What challenges and responses were discussed about climate finance at COP31?

  • Slow finance delivery. Developing countries face slow and insufficient climate finance despite pledges to increase funding to $300 billion per year by 2035.
  • Geopolitical and economic hurdles. Western governments prioritize national security and inflation concerns, reducing urgency and funding for climate action amid global conflicts.
  • Administrative barriers. Technical hurdles and complex procedures limit small states' capacity to access climate finance effectively.
  • Calls for implementation. COP31 emphasized accelerating implementation of existing pledges with finance that is faster, simpler, and fairer, rather than setting new targets.
  • Innovative funding. Some small nations pledged their own funds to spur richer countries to match contributions and invest in nature-based solutions like seagrass restoration.

Back to basics: Just Transition and Nature-Based Climate Solutions

Just Transition refers to shifting to a low-carbon economy in a way that is fair and inclusive, addressing social and economic impacts on workers and communities. Nature-based climate solutions use ecosystem conservation and restoration to mitigate climate change by enhancing carbon storage and ecosystem resilience.
