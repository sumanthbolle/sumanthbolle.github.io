Kishore Biyani, Future Retail and the ₹904-crore Bank Fraud Case

Kishore Biyani's Future Group, once a retail leader, faced insolvency after financial troubles and a ₹904.32 crore bank fraud allegation by SBI. This case highlights issues of corporate governance and financial regulation in India’s retail sector.

Future Retail’s collapse due to alleged ₹904.32 crore bank fraud and failed business decisions illustrates challenges in corporate governance and financial oversight.

Why is this in the news?

On November 6, 2026, the CBI charged Future Retail promoters including Kishore Biyani with allegedly defrauding SBI of ₹904.32 crore, triggering renewed focus on the Group’s collapse.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What led to Future Retail’s financial troubles and collapse?

  • Early success and growth. Future Group, led by Kishore Biyani, grew rapidly in the 2000s to become India’s largest retailer, benefiting from India’s consumption boom.
  • Strategic missteps. Biyani delayed pushing for foreign direct investment reforms and doubted e-commerce’s sustainability, opting instead to expand small-format supermarkets, which strained finances.
  • Impact of Covid-19. The pandemic caused huge losses due to rising logistics costs, warehouse management issues, and unpaid rents, worsening Future Retail’s financial position.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the ₹904.32 crore bank fraud allegation about?

  • CBI investigation. The CBI registered a case after SBI alleged Future Retail diverted sanctioned loan funds to associate companies, violating loan terms.
  • Searches and evidence. On November 6, 2026, the CBI conducted searches at promoters’ premises in Mumbai and seized documents relevant to the investigation.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How did the Amazon-Reliance legal battle affect Future Retail?

  • Failed Reliance deal. Reliance Retail’s ₹24,713 crore acquisition deal for Future Group’s retail assets was opposed by Amazon and eventually collapsed amid legal challenges.
  • Hostile takeover claims. Reliance took control of many Future Retail stores after landlords approached it due to Future’s unpaid rents, which Future Group called a hostile takeover.
  • Insolvency filing. Following the failed deal and mounting financial stress, Future Retail filed for liquidation before the National Company Law Tribunal in 2023.

Back to basics: Corporate fraud and insolvency

Corporate fraud involves illegal activities by company promoters or management, such as misusing loan funds. Insolvency is a financial state where a company cannot meet its debt obligations, often leading to liquidation or restructuring under legal frameworks.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about Future Retail and Kishore Biyani: 1. The CBI alleged Future Retail diverted ₹904.32 crore of SBI loan funds to associate companies. 2. Reliance Retail successfully acquired Future Group’s retail assets in a ₹24,713 crore deal without any legal opposition. Which of the statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the Reliance acquisition deal faced legal opposition from Amazon and eventually fell through.
