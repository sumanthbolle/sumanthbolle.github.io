Static anchor

Electoral literacy is the practical understanding of voting rights, electoral procedures, civic responsibilities and reliable information needed for responsible participation in democracy.

Background

  • Mera Yuva Bharat (MY Bharat), through the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, used Yuva Samvads as institutional discussions on electoral awareness in colleges and educational institutions.
  • The mechanism combines direct discussion, interaction with public representatives and Youth Icons, quizzes, the Loktantrik Pledge, and registration on the MY Bharat portal.
  • Electoral awareness is broader than casting a vote: it includes understanding voting rights and procedures, civic responsibility, and verifying information through authentic sources.
