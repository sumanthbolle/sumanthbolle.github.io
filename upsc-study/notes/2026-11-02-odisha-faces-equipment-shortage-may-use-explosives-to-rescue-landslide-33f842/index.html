Odisha Considers Explosives Amid Equipment Shortage for Landslide Rescue

Heavy rains triggered landslides in Odisha, killing five and trapping others under debris. Due to lack of road access and heavy boulders blocking rescue, officials are exploring controlled blasting to aid rescue efforts.

Odisha faces challenges in landslide rescue due to equipment shortages and terrain, prompting use of controlled blasting.

Why is this in the news?

On November 1, 2026, Odisha reported casualties and missing persons from landslides caused by heavy rains linked to Cyclone Arnab, with rescue efforts complicated by blocked roads and large boulders.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What caused the landslides and what is the current rescue situation?

  • Cause of landslides. Heavy and continuous rainfall triggered by Cyclone Arnab caused massive landslides in Odisha, especially in Gajapati, Ganjam, and Kandhamal districts.
  • Casualties and missing persons. Five people died—three in Kandhamal and two in Ganjam—and 10 others are feared trapped under debris in Ganjam district.
  • Rescue challenges. Massive boulders and mud blocks cover roads, preventing cranes and earthmovers from reaching affected areas, delaying rescue operations.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How is Odisha managing rescue operations amid equipment shortages?

  • Use of controlled blasting. Officials are seeking help from railway engineers experienced in controlled blasting to break up boulders and facilitate rescue.
  • Involvement of disaster forces. Odisha Disaster Rapid Action Force (ODRAF), National Disaster Response Force (NDRF), and Indian Railways technical teams have been working for nearly 50 hours in rescue efforts.
  • Evacuation and preparedness. District collectors have been directed to evacuate vulnerable populations ahead of Cyclone Murasu, which threatens further landslides.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for disaster management and geography?

  • Geophysical hazard impact. Landslides are a common geophysical hazard in hilly regions during heavy rainfall, causing loss of life and infrastructure damage.
  • Challenges in remote areas. Remote locations with no road access complicate rescue operations, requiring innovative methods like controlled blasting.
  • Importance of preparedness. Identifying landslide-prone areas and timely evacuation are critical to minimizing casualties during cyclonic storms.

Back to basics: Landslides and Disaster Management

Landslides occur when soil, rock, and debris move down a slope, often triggered by heavy rainfall or seismic activity. They pose risks to life, property, and infrastructure, especially in hilly or mountainous regions. Disaster management involves preparedness, timely evacuation, rescue operations, and rehabilitation to reduce the impact of such natural hazards.
