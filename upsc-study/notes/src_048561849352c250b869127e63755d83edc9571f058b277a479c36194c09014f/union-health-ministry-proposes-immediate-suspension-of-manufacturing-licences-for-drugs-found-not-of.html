Static anchor

Not of Standard Quality (NSQ) refers, in this source context, to a drug declared deficient on specified critical quality parameters—assay, dissolution, related substances, impurities or sterility—through a Government Analyst’s report.

Background

  • The Ministry states that under existing provisions, suspension or cancellation is preceded by a prescribed regulatory procedure, including a show-cause notice.
  • The proposed mechanism is triggered by a Government Analyst’s report declaring a drug NSQ on assay, dissolution, related substances, impurities or sterility.
  • The proposal follows deliberations at the 65th Drugs Consultative Committee meeting on 20 December 2024 and recommendations of the 92nd Drugs Technical Advisory Board meeting on 24 April 2025.
  • The release describes the amendments as proposed and says the draft amendments were notified for public consultation.
