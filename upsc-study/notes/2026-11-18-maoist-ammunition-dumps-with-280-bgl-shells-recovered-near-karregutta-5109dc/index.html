Maoist Ammunition Dumps with 280 BGL Shells Recovered Near Karregutta Hills

Three separate dumps containing around 280 Barrel Grenade Launcher (BGL) shells were recovered near Karregutta hills along the Telangana-Chhattisgarh border in November 2026. This recovery highlights ongoing Maoist insurgency challenges and the impact of surrender and rehabilitation policies on internal security.

Three Maoist ammunition dumps with 280 BGL shells were found near Karregutta hills, indicating the persistence of Maoist threats despite surrenders.

Why is this in the news?

In November 2026, Telangana police recovered three Maoist ammunition dumps containing 280 BGL shells near Karregutta hills based on surrendered Maoists' information.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What was recovered near Karregutta hills?

  • Ammunition details. Three separate dumps containing around 280 Barrel Grenade Launcher (BGL) shells were recovered from a forest area near Karregutta hills along the Telangana-Chhattisgarh border.
  • Recovery operation. The recovery was conducted by a joint team of Charla police, Bomb Disposal Squad personnel, and Special Party police based on information from surrendered Maoists.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Who did the ammunition belong to and why does it matter?

  • Maoist connection. The dumps reportedly belonged to the first company of the outlawed CPI (Maoist)’s People’s Liberation Guerrilla Army (PLGA) Battalion.
  • Security implications. The concealed ammunition indicates that Maoists had prepared for future attacks, showing the persistence of insurgency threats in forest areas once considered strongholds.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the context behind the recovery?

  • Timing of concealment. Police suspect the Maoists concealed the BGL shells in early 2025 for future use.
  • Impact of surrenders. Several Maoist cadres surrendered in late 2025 and early 2026, influenced by Telangana’s surrender and rehabilitation policy, which aims to reintegrate insurgents into mainstream society.

Back to basics: Maoist Insurgency and Internal Security

Maoist insurgency involves armed rebellion by outlawed groups against the state, often in forested and tribal areas. The insurgents use guerrilla tactics and maintain hidden arms caches to sustain their operations. The government counters this through security operations, surrender and rehabilitation policies, and development initiatives to reduce support for insurgents.
