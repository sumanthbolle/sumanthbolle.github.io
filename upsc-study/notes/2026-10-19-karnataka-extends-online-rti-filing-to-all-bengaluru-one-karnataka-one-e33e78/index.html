Karnataka Expands Online RTI Filing to All Service Centres

Karnataka government has extended online Right to Information (RTI) application filing to all Bengaluru One, Karnataka One, and Grama One centres, integrating physical and online RTI submissions under the Sakala Services Act, 2011. This reform enhances transparency and accountability by enabling easier access and timely tracking of RTI applications across urban and rural areas.

Karnataka's integration of RTI filing with Sakala Act timelines at service centres improves transparency and citizen access to information.

Why is this in the news?

On October 18, 2026, Karnataka announced the extension of online RTI filing facilities to all Bengaluru One, Karnataka One, and Grama One centres, making RTI applications more accessible and enforceable under the Sakala Services Act.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What changes has Karnataka made to RTI filing?

  • Online RTI at service centres. The government has enabled online RTI application filing at all Bengaluru One, Karnataka One, and Grama One centres, allowing applicants to submit RTI requests with staff assistance, paying the prescribed fee and a nominal service charge.
  • Integration with Sakala Act. Physical RTI applications received by post or in person must be registered on the RTI online portal within two working days under the Sakala Services Act, 2011, making the registration process time-bound and trackable.
  • Rural access through Grama One. Extension to Grama One centres ensures rural citizens can file RTI applications online at the gram panchayat level, improving accessibility beyond urban centres.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for governance and accountability?

  • Guaranteed service with compensation. RTI filing is now a guaranteed service under the Sakala Act, so delays beyond two days in registering applications entitle applicants to compensation and hold officers accountable.
  • Common platform for monitoring. Bringing physical, direct online, and service centre applications onto a single portal facilitates monitoring of compliance with RTI timelines and identification of delays by Public Information Officers.
  • Timely disposal of appeals. The government has prescribed a seven-working-day deadline for disposing of first and second RTI appeals, enhancing responsiveness and accountability.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does this reform help citizens and government employees?

  • Assisted filing for non-internet users. People unfamiliar with computers or lacking internet access can get help filing RTI applications at service centres, increasing inclusivity.
  • Online acknowledgement and tracking. Applicants receive an online registration number immediately at the centre, enabling them to track the status of their RTI requests transparently.
  • Service deadlines for government staff. The Karnataka government has also included various employee services like salary disbursement and leave sanctioning under the Sakala Act with specific timelines, improving internal governance.

Back to basics: Right to Information (RTI) Act and Sakala Services Act

The RTI Act empowers citizens to seek information from public authorities to promote transparency and accountability. The Sakala Services Act mandates timely delivery of government services with compensation for delays, ensuring citizen rights are protected through enforceable deadlines.

Quick revision

What is the time limit for registering physical RTI applications on Karnataka's online portal?
Physical RTI applications must be registered within two working days under the Sakala Services Act.
Where can citizens file RTI applications online with assistance in Karnataka?
Citizens can file online RTI applications at Bengaluru One, Karnataka One, and Grama One centres with staff assistance.
What is the deadline for disposing of RTI appeals in Karnataka?
First and second RTI appeals must be disposed of within seven working days by the competent appellate authority.
