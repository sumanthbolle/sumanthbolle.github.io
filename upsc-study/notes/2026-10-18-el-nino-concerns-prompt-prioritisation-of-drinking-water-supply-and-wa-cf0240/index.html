El Nino Triggers Drinking Water Prioritisation and Conservation Measures

Amid El Nino-induced drought concerns in Andhra Pradesh, the Chief Secretary has directed district officials to prioritise drinking water supply and implement water use reduction advisories. This is crucial for UPSC aspirants to understand the administrative response to climate-induced water scarcity and its implications for resource management.

El Nino drought concerns have led Andhra Pradesh to prioritise drinking water supply and enforce strict measures against water theft to manage scarcity.

Why is this in the news?

On October 9, 2026, Andhra Pradesh's Chief Secretary directed district collectors to take advance measures against El Nino-induced drought, focusing on uninterrupted drinking water supply and crop protection.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What prompted the water supply prioritisation in Andhra Pradesh?

  • El Nino drought impact. The State recorded a 38.9% rainfall deficit by October 8, 2026, leading to drought declarations in 388 mandals across 21 districts.
  • Administrative directive. Chief Secretary G. Sai Prasad instructed district collectors to prioritise drinking water supply over non-drinking uses and to take strict action against water theft.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What measures are being taken to manage water scarcity?

  • Water supply protection. Collectors are tasked with ensuring uninterrupted drinking water, identifying alternative sources, arranging tanker supplies, repairing borewells, and preventing leakages.
  • Crop and farming support. Measures include promoting alternative crops, distributing seeds, encouraging natural farming and inter-cropping, and protecting horticultural crops through micro-irrigation and emergency water supply.
  • Public awareness and planning. Special summer action plans have been prepared for urban local bodies, with campaigns against water wastage and regular district-level reviews.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for UPSC aspirants?

  • Climate change and governance. The response illustrates how climate phenomena like El Nino affect water resources and how state administration manages drought through policy and enforcement.
  • Disaster management relevance. Understanding these measures aligns with the UPSC syllabus on disaster management and environmental governance.

Back to basics: El Nino and Its Impact on Climate

El Nino is a climatic phenomenon characterized by the warming of surface waters in the central and eastern Pacific Ocean, which disrupts normal weather patterns globally. It often leads to reduced rainfall and drought conditions in parts of India, affecting agriculture and water availability. Understanding El Nino helps explain seasonal climate variability and its socio-economic impacts.
