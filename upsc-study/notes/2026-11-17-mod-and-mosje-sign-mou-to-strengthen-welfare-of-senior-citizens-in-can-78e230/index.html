MoD and MoSJE Sign MoU to Enhance Senior Citizens' Welfare in Cantonments

The Ministry of Defence and Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment signed a Memorandum of Understanding on November 16, 2026, to improve welfare services for senior citizens living in cantonment areas. This collaboration focuses on establishing and strengthening Day Care Centres, Senior Citizen Homes, and training geriatric caregivers, reflecting a whole-of-government approach to social welfare.

The MoU between MoD and MoSJE aims to provide better care, infrastructure, and skill development for senior citizens in cantonment areas under the Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana.

Why is this in the news?

On November 16, 2026, the Ministry of Defence and Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment signed an MoU to strengthen welfare for senior citizens in cantonment areas by setting up and improving care facilities and caregiver training.

What does the MoU between MoD and MoSJE entail?

  • Purpose of MoU. The MoU aims to enhance welfare for senior citizens in cantonment areas by establishing and strengthening Day Care Centres and Senior Citizen Homes and providing skill training for geriatric caregivers.
  • Roles of Ministries. The Ministry of Defence, via the Directorate General Defence Estates, provides infrastructure, premises, and civic amenities, while the Ministry of Social Justice & Empowerment offers financial assistance, technical guidance, training modules, and operational support under the Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY).
  • Scope of Facilities. New Day Care Centres and Senior Citizen Homes with 25/50-bed capacities will be established where feasible, and existing centres in several cantonments will be supported and expanded.
  • Skill Development. The MoU includes training youth from cantonment areas as geriatric caregivers to meet the growing demand for specialized elderly care services.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

Which cantonments are involved and how will the initiative be implemented?

  • Existing Centres. Day Care Centres currently operate in Ambala, Deolali, Jabalpur, Ranikhet, and Lansdowne cantonments, while Senior Citizen Homes exist in Ayodhya and Lucknow cantonments.
  • New Centres Identified. Eight cantonments identified for new Day Care Centres include Barrackpore, Belgaum, Jhansi, Khadki, Pune, Secunderabad, St. Thomas Mount, and Delhi.
  • Additional Locations for Caregiver Training. Training will also cover Ferozepur and Jalandhar cantonments, expanding the pool of trained geriatric caregivers in 12 cantonments.
  • Coordination Mechanism. A Joint Working Group comprising representatives from both ministries will coordinate, implement, and monitor the initiative.

Why does this MoU matter for senior citizen welfare and governance?

  • Social and Moral Responsibility. The MoU reflects the cultural ethos of respecting elders and recognizes their lifelong contributions, emphasizing the government's duty to ensure their dignity, security, and active ageing.
  • Whole of Government Approach. This partnership exemplifies cooperation between ministries to maximize public resources and improve service delivery for vulnerable senior citizens in cantonment areas.
  • Focus on Quality and Accountability. Raksha Mantri highlighted the need for continuous improvement and social audits of Day Care Centres and Senior Citizen Homes to ensure quality services.
  • Centenary Significance. The initiative coincides with the Defence Estates Department's 100 years of service, marking its evolving role from municipal administration to broader social welfare.

Back to basics: Atal Vayo Abhyuday Yojana (AVYAY)

AVYAY is a government scheme aimed at improving the quality of life for senior citizens by supporting facilities like Senior Citizen Homes and Day Care Centres, providing geriatric caregiver training, and operating a national helpline for elderly welfare. It focuses on ensuring dignified, secure, and active ageing through financial assistance, technical support, and awareness programs.
