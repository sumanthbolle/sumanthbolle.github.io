India implications

Way forward

  • Strengthen structured collaboration among government institutions, researchers, healthcare providers, start-ups and investors.
  • Require evidence-based clinical validation, regulatory oversight and post-deployment monitoring for AI-enabled and genomic healthcare applications.
  • Protect privacy, informed consent and responsible use of genetic and health data.
  • Align innovation incentives with affordability, local health needs and access beyond metropolitan centres.
