Rajnath Singh and U.S. Officials Review India-U.S. Defence Pact Progress

Defence Minister Rajnath Singh met U.S. Secretary of War Pete Hegseth on October 28, 2026, to discuss the India-U.S. Major Defence Partnership and related security concerns. This meeting is important for understanding India's defence diplomacy and regional security dynamics.

India and the U.S. reviewed progress under their Major Defence Partnership, emphasizing counterterrorism cooperation and concerns over military technology transfers to Pakistan.

Why is this in the news?

On October 28, 2026, Rajnath Singh and U.S. Secretary of War Pete Hegseth met at the ASEAN Defence Ministers’ Meeting-Plus in Cebu to review the India-U.S. defence pact progress and discuss cross-border terrorism.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What was discussed in the India-U.S. defence meeting?

  • Bilateral meeting details. Rajnath Singh and Pete Hegseth met on October 28, 2026, during the ASEAN Defence Ministers’ Meeting-Plus in Cebu to review the India-U.S. Major Defence Partnership progress.
  • Key agenda items. They discussed implementation of foundational security agreements, operational readiness, technology sharing, defence industrial integration, and military-to-military exchanges.
  • Counterterrorism cooperation. Hegseth reaffirmed U.S. support for India in combating cross-border terrorism, while Singh emphasized zero tolerance for countries supporting terrorist groups.
  • Concerns over Pakistan's F-16 package. Singh raised concerns about the U.S. support package for Pakistan’s F-16 fighter aircraft, warning about possible technology proliferation to third countries and regional security risks.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does this meeting impact regional security and defence cooperation?

  • India-U.S. defence framework. The meeting reviewed the ten-year India-U.S. Defence Framework, focusing on strengthening maritime security cooperation and resilient defence supply chains.
  • India-Japan defence cooperation. Separately, Singh discussed with Japan’s Defence Minister progress on bilateral defence cooperation, including naval shipbuilding and air force exercises.
  • Strategic partnership significance. Singh described the India-U.S. partnership as a cornerstone for a free, open, and secure Indo-Pacific region.

Back to basics: India-U.S. Defence Partnership

The India-U.S. Major Defence Partnership is a strategic agreement that facilitates cooperation in defence technology, military exercises, and security dialogues. It aims to enhance operational readiness and interoperability between the two countries’ armed forces, contributing to regional stability and security.
