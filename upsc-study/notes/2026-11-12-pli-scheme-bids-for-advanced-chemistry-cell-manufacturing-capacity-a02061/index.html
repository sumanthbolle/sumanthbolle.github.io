22 Bids Received for 10 GWh ACC Manufacturing Capacity under PLI Scheme

The Ministry of Heavy Industries received 22 bids for establishing 10 GWh Advanced Chemistry Cell (ACC) manufacturing capacity under the PLI ACC Scheme, part of a 50 GWh national target. This development highlights India's push to build a domestic ACC manufacturing ecosystem to support energy storage and renewable energy expansion.

India's Ministry of Heavy Industries received 22 bids for 10 GWh ACC manufacturing capacity under the PLI scheme to strengthen domestic battery production.

Why is this in the news?

On 12 November 2026, the Ministry of Heavy Industries announced receiving 22 bids for 10 GWh ACC manufacturing capacity under the PLI ACC Scheme.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the PLI ACC Scheme and its objectives?

  • Scheme approval and budget. In May 2021, the Union Cabinet approved the PLI Scheme for the National Programme on Advanced Chemistry Cell Battery Storage with a budgetary outlay of ₹18,100 crore to achieve 50 GWh ACC manufacturing capacity in India.
  • Scheme goals. The scheme aims to enhance domestic ACC manufacturing capabilities and attract large domestic and international players to build a globally competitive ACC ecosystem.
  • Energy security and value chain. The scheme supports India's growing energy storage needs from renewable energy expansion, contributing to energy security and deepening the domestic ACC technology value chain.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the recent development in ACC manufacturing bids?

  • Bid details. The Ministry of Heavy Industries received 22 bids for 10 GWh ACC manufacturing capacity earmarked for Grid-Scale Stationary Storage applications as part of the 50 GWh target.
  • Tender process. The global tender was floated on 15 July 2026 on the Central Public Procurement Portal, with technical bids opened on 11 November 2026 using a transparent two-stage Quality and Cost Based Selection mechanism.
  • List of bidders. Bidders include prominent firms such as ACME Solar Holdings Ltd., Amara Raja Advanced Cell Technologies Pvt. Ltd., Reliance Industries Ltd., JSW Neo Energy Ltd., and others.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does this matter for India's industrial and energy policy?

  • Strengthening domestic manufacturing. The bids reflect strong industry interest and government commitment to building a self-reliant and globally competitive ACC manufacturing ecosystem.
  • Supporting renewable energy. The 10 GWh capacity for Grid-Scale Stationary Storage will support India's rapidly growing renewable energy storage requirements.
  • Vision alignment. This initiative aligns with the vision of Viksit Bharat @2047 to develop advanced technology manufacturing capabilities.

Back to basics: Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme

The PLI Scheme is a government initiative to boost domestic manufacturing by providing financial incentives linked to production output. It aims to attract investment, enhance capacity, and improve competitiveness in strategic sectors such as advanced battery manufacturing. The scheme supports India's goal of self-reliance and reducing import dependence.
