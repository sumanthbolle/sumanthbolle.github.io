PM Highlights Agrivoltaics to Boost Farmer Incomes and Rural Prosperity

The Prime Minister shared articles by Union Ministers Pralhad Joshi and Shivraj Singh Chouhan emphasizing agrivoltaics as a way to combine solar power generation with agriculture to increase farmers' incomes. The articles also highlight the role of technology and research in transforming Indian agriculture through nutrition security, climate resilience, and better market access.

Agrivoltaics integrates solar energy with farming to provide additional revenue, reduce energy costs, and support crop productivity, advancing rural prosperity.

What is agrivoltaics and why does it matter for farmers?

  • Definition of agrivoltaics. Agrivoltaics is the combination of solar power generation with agriculture on the same land, allowing farmers to generate electricity while continuing crop production.
  • Benefits for farmers. It provides additional revenue streams, reduces energy costs, and supports crop productivity, thereby strengthening farmers' incomes.
  • Impact on rural prosperity. By powering farms with solar energy, agrivoltaics advances rural prosperity through sustainable energy use and diversified income.

How does technology and research transform Indian agriculture?

  • Central role of technology. Technology and research are key to transforming Indian agriculture and improving farmers' lives by enabling climate-resilient farming and efficient resource use.
  • Focus on nutrition security. Greater emphasis is placed on high-value crops to ensure nutrition security alongside food security.
  • Improved market access. Better market access for farmers is facilitated through technological advancements and research.

Back to basics: Agrivoltaics

Agrivoltaics is a farming practice where solar panels are installed on agricultural land to simultaneously produce solar energy and grow crops. This dual use of land helps farmers generate electricity and earn additional income without compromising agricultural output. It also contributes to sustainable energy generation and can improve crop microclimates.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about agrivoltaics and agricultural technology: 1. Agrivoltaics combines solar power generation with agriculture to provide additional revenue and reduce energy costs for farmers. 2. Technology and research in agriculture focus only on increasing crop yields without considering climate resilience or market access. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because technology and research also focus on climate resilience and better market access, not just crop yields.
