India at G20 Foreign Ministers’ Meet in Atlanta and Quad Discussions

The G20 Foreign Ministers’ Meeting was held in Atlanta on October 30-31, 2026, hosted by U.S. Secretary of State Marco Rubio, with India’s External Affairs Minister S. Jaishankar attending. This meeting focused on advancing cooperation on economic and security challenges ahead of the G20 Leaders’ Summit.

India participated in the G20 Foreign Ministers’ Meeting in Atlanta to engage in discussions on shared economic and security issues ahead of the G20 Leaders’ Summit.

Back to basics: Multilateral Diplomacy and G20

The G20 is a group of the world’s largest economies that meets to discuss global economic and security issues. Foreign Ministers’ meetings help prepare for Leaders’ Summits by enabling detailed discussions on shared challenges. Multilateral diplomacy involves countries working together in such forums to address issues that affect multiple nations.
