Israeli Airstrike on Gaza's Nasser Hospital Kills Six

An Israeli airstrike on Nasser Hospital in southern Gaza killed six people and wounded at least a dozen, intensifying the humanitarian crisis in the region. UPSC aspirants should understand the incident's implications for international security, humanitarian law, and India's foreign policy.

The Israeli strike on Gaza's Nasser Hospital, a key medical facility, resulted in multiple casualties amid ongoing conflict, highlighting the war's impact on civilian infrastructure.

Why is this in the news?

On November 12, 2026, an Israeli airstrike targeted Nasser Hospital in southern Gaza, killing six and injuring many, amid accusations of militants using hospitals for cover.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What happened in the Israeli strike on Nasser Hospital?

  • Casualties and damage. The airstrike killed six people, including a doctor, and wounded at least a dozen, damaging the hospital's surgery department and parts of its second floor.
  • Hospital's significance. Nasser Hospital in Khan Younis is one of the few functioning medical facilities left in Gaza, whose healthcare infrastructure has been severely damaged by the war.
  • Israeli military's claim. Israel stated the strike targeted a senior Hamas terrorist and other militants but did not provide evidence.
  • Previous attacks. Israeli forces have previously targeted Nasser Hospital, including a 2025 strike that killed five journalists at a local reporters' gathering point.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this incident matter for international relations and security?

  • Humanitarian impact. The strike worsens the humanitarian crisis in Gaza by damaging critical healthcare infrastructure and causing civilian casualties.
  • Conflict dynamics. The incident reflects the ongoing conflict between Israel and Hamas, with accusations of militants using civilian sites for military purposes complicating the situation.
  • Implications for India's foreign policy. India's diplomatic stance on Middle East conflicts and its role in regional stability are influenced by such developments.

Back to basics: Humanitarian impact of conflict on healthcare infrastructure

In armed conflicts, hospitals and medical facilities are protected under international humanitarian law to ensure civilian access to healthcare. Damage to such infrastructure worsens humanitarian crises by limiting medical care and increasing civilian suffering. Understanding the targeting of hospitals helps analyze conflict dynamics and international responses.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the Israeli airstrike on Nasser Hospital in Gaza: 1. The strike killed six people, including a doctor, and damaged the hospital's surgery department. 2. The Israeli military provided evidence that the hospital was used exclusively for military purposes by Hamas militants. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the Israeli military claimed militants were targeted but did not provide evidence that the hospital was used exclusively for military purposes.
