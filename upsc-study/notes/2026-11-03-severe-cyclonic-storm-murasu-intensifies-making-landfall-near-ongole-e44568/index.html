Severe Cyclonic Storm Murasu Makes Landfall Near Ongole

Cyclonic Storm Murasu intensified into a Severe Cyclonic Storm over the Bay of Bengal and made landfall near Ongole, Andhra Pradesh. This event is important for UPSC aspirants studying disaster management and climate impact on coastal regions.

Severe Cyclonic Storm Murasu crossed the south Andhra Pradesh coast near Ongole with wind speeds of 100 to 110 kmph, causing heavy rainfall in several districts.

Why is this in the news?

On November 2, 2026, Cyclonic Storm Murasu intensified into a Severe Cyclonic Storm and moved northwestwards to cross the south Andhra Pradesh coast near Ongole.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is Cyclonic Storm Murasu and where did it make landfall?

  • Storm intensification. Cyclonic Storm Murasu intensified into a Severe Cyclonic Storm over the West Central and Southwest Bay of Bengal in the early hours of November 2, 2026.
  • Landfall location. The storm crossed the south Andhra Pradesh coast near Ongole as a Severe Cyclonic Storm with wind speeds of 100 to 110 kmph, gusting up to 120 kmph.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the impacts and warnings issued due to Murasu?

  • Rainfall and alerts. Extremely heavy rainfall alerts were issued for Eluru, Krishna, Guntur, Bapatla, and SPSR Nellore districts, while very heavy rainfall was expected in NTR, East and West Godavari, Palnadu, Chittoor, and Tirupati districts.
  • Rainfall commencement. Rainfall activity began in parts of Andhra Pradesh as early as 6 a.m. on November 2, 2026.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for UPSC preparation?

  • Disaster management relevance. Understanding the formation, intensification, and impact of cyclones like Murasu is essential for disaster management topics in UPSC, especially related to coastal vulnerability.
  • Climate impact study. The event highlights the importance of studying climate patterns and their effects on Indian coastal regions, which is relevant for environment and geography syllabus.

Back to basics: Cyclonic Storms and Disaster Management

A cyclonic storm is a low-pressure system over warm ocean waters that can intensify into a severe storm with strong winds and heavy rainfall. Disaster management involves preparedness, early warning, and response to minimize damage and loss of life during such events. Coastal regions are particularly vulnerable to cyclones, making timely alerts and evacuation crucial.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about Severe Cyclonic Storm Murasu: 1. It intensified over the Bay of Bengal and made landfall near Ongole with wind speeds of 100 to 110 kmph. 2. Extremely heavy rainfall was forecasted for districts including Eluru and Krishna. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (c). The trap is confusing the exact landfall location and wind speeds; the storm made landfall near Ongole with wind speeds of 100 to 110 kmph, not elsewhere.

Mains question (10 marks)

Explain the significance of timely cyclone warnings and disaster management in mitigating the impact of severe cyclonic storms on coastal India, with reference to Cyclone Murasu.

Explain: Explain how cyclone warnings and disaster management reduce damage and loss during cyclones, using Murasu as an example.

Intro: Cyclones pose a major threat to coastal India, necessitating effective disaster management.

  • Timely warnings like those issued for Cyclone Murasu help in evacuation and preparedness, reducing casualties.
  • Rainfall and wind speed forecasts enable authorities to alert vulnerable districts and mobilize resources.
  • Disaster management includes coordination among meteorological departments, local administration, and relief agencies.
  • Studying cyclones also aids in climate adaptation strategies for long-term resilience.

Conclusion: Effective cyclone warnings and disaster management are vital to safeguard lives and property in coastal regions.
