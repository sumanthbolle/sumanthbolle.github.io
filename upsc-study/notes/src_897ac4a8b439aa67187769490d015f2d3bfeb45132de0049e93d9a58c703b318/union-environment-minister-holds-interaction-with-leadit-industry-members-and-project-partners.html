Static anchor

LeadIT, the Leadership Group for Industry Transition, is a collaborative platform launched by India and Sweden to support decarbonisation of heavy and hard-to-abate industry through government, business and knowledge partnerships.

Background

  • LeadIT was launched by India and Sweden in 2019 at the UN Climate Action Summit, with support from the World Economic Forum, as a platform for industrial decarbonisation.
  • The India-Sweden Industry Transition Partnership operates under the LeadIT framework and links industrial firms, technology partners and research institutions.
  • The source document records that LeadIT 3.0 was scheduled for launch at COP31 in Antalya on 17 November 2026; this note does not treat that planned launch as a completed event.

India implications

  • India can use partnerships to develop and demonstrate low-carbon pathways for steel, cement and transport while aligning climate action with industrial growth.
  • Pre-competitive project preparation, pilot projects and capacity building can help address the gap between laboratory technologies and commercial deployment.
  • The platform can strengthen cooperation among Indian industry, academia and government, but outcomes will depend on finance, domestic capability, transparent monitoring and technology affordability.

Way forward

  • Convert collaboration into time-bound pilot projects with transparent technical and emissions benchmarks.
  • Provide support for pre-competitive feasibility studies, demonstration projects, skills and domestic research capability.
  • Mobilise affordable and predictable finance while protecting competitiveness and avoiding exclusion of smaller firms.
  • Create interoperable measurement, reporting and verification systems for industrial emissions and project outcomes.
