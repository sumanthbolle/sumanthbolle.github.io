Raksha Mantri Reviews Infrastructure and Military Deployment Along LAC

Raksha Mantri Rajnath Singh reviewed infrastructure development and advanced military equipment deployment along the Line of Actual Control (LAC) during his visit to Trishakti Corps at Sukna on November 7, 2026. This update is important for UPSC aspirants focusing on India's internal security and defence preparedness against evolving threats on the India-China border.

The Raksha Mantri's review highlighted enhanced infrastructure, modern equipment deployment, and the need for vigilance against hybrid warfare along the LAC.

Why is this in the news?

On November 7, 2026, Raksha Mantri Rajnath Singh visited Trishakti Corps at Sukna to review infrastructure and military equipment deployment along the LAC.

What infrastructure and military updates were reviewed along the LAC?

  • Infrastructure development. The Raksha Mantri was briefed on infrastructure development along the LAC, including roads, tunnels, and border facilities to enhance operational capabilities.
  • Advanced equipment deployment. Deployment of advanced military equipment and emerging technologies such as drones and anti-drone systems was reviewed to improve combat effectiveness.
  • Logistics hub importance. Sukna was described as a critical hub for moving supplies, weaponry, food, medical provisions, and personnel to forward locations, making it vital for Eastern India's security.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does this matter for India’s security strategy?

  • Changing nature of warfare. Modern warfare now includes hybrid warfare, proxy wars, cyber-attacks, drone incursions, and information warfare beyond traditional battles.
  • Need for vigilance. Adversaries use social media rumors, drones for surveillance, and cyber-attacks to compromise military systems, requiring constant alertness.
  • Government support. The government is committed to strengthening supply chains and increasing budget allocations for border infrastructure and modern equipment.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does this impact the morale and welfare of Defence Forces personnel?

  • Troop morale. Raksha Mantri urged officers to spend quality time with soldiers to foster belonging and confidence that the nation supports them.
  • Mental health focus. He emphasized the importance of mental health, encouraging soldiers to communicate openly about stress and seek support.
  • Community welfare. The Corps’ role in nation-building through infrastructure and community welfare initiatives strengthens ties with local populations in border areas.

Back to basics: Line of Actual Control (LAC)

The LAC is the demarcation line that separates Indian-controlled territory from Chinese-controlled territory in border areas. It is not a formally agreed international boundary but serves as the practical control line between the two countries. The LAC is significant for India's security as it is the site of frequent military stand-offs and infrastructure development to maintain territorial integrity.
