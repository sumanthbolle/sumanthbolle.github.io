Keralam Senior Citizens Bill Focuses on Rights and Care of Elderly

The Keralam Senior Citizens (Care and Protection) Bill, 2026 aims to protect the rights, dignity, and social security of elderly people by promoting active ageing and regulating care services. UPSC aspirants should note its provisions on caregiver certification, protection units, and legal frameworks for elderly welfare.

The Keralam Senior Citizens Bill establishes a legal and institutional framework to ensure elderly rights, dignity, and care through registration of care centres, caregiver certification, and protection units.

Why is this in the news?

The Keralam Senior Citizens (Care and Protection) Bill, 2026 was tabled in the Kerala Assembly on October 13, 2026, focusing on elderly rights and welfare.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the main objectives of the Keralam Senior Citizens Bill?

  • Promotion of active ageing. The Bill aims to promote healthy and active ageing among senior citizens.
  • Protection of rights and dignity. It focuses on protecting the rights, dignity, and social security of elderly people.
  • Regulation of care centres. The Bill provides for registration and regulation of senior citizen care centres.
  • Capacity-building for caregivers. It proposes training and certification for caregivers through a state framework.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does the Bill address elderly care and protection mechanisms?

  • State and district protection units. The government will establish a State Senior Citizen Protection Unit and District Senior Citizen Protection Forums and Units to monitor policies and address complaints.
  • Caregiver registration and certification. Home-care agencies using digital platforms must maintain updated caregiver lists, and caregivers will undergo phased certification under the Keralam Geriatric Care Certification Framework.
  • Cyber fraud and social security. The Bill includes setting up Senior Citizen Cyber Fraud Cells in cyber police stations and a State reserve fund for elderly welfare.
  • Legal provisions for violations. It lays down punishments for violations of the Act and related rules.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this Bill matter for UPSC preparation?

  • Focus on vulnerable sections. The Bill exemplifies government efforts to protect vulnerable sections, relevant to social welfare topics.
  • Institutional mechanisms. It highlights the role of state and district bodies in policy implementation and grievance redressal.
  • Rights and dignity in policy. The emphasis on dignity, legal aid, and digital inclusion connects to governance and rights-based approaches.

Back to basics: Social Welfare Legislation

Social welfare legislation aims to protect and promote the rights and well-being of vulnerable groups such as the elderly. It often includes mechanisms for care, protection, grievance redressal, and empowerment through institutional frameworks and legal provisions. State governments can enact such laws to address local needs and complement central laws.
