RBI Governor's Five Priorities to Maintain Financial Stability Amid Emerging Risks

RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra outlined five key priorities to safeguard India's financial stability against various shocks and systemic risks. Understanding these priorities is essential for UPSC aspirants as they highlight the challenges and regulatory focus areas in India's financial sector.

The RBI Governor emphasized building a resilient financial system through better data, systemic risk assessment, and innovation that preserves trust.

Why is this in the news?

On October 15, 2026, RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra delivered a special address outlining five priorities to maintain financial stability amid emerging global and domestic risks.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the key priorities outlined by the RBI Governor to maintain financial stability?

  • Strengthening systemic resilience. Financial stability is not about preventing shocks but about building a system that can withstand and contain their amplification, whether shocks are endogenous or exogenous.
  • Assessing new systemic risks. Emerging risks are increasingly exogenous, cross-border, and interconnected, potentially arising from geopolitical events, cyberattacks, or technological failures, requiring better understanding of dependencies and contagion.
  • Improving data and monitoring. Better and more granular data on non-banking financial institutions, interconnected exposures, technology dependencies, and cross-border positions are needed to enhance risk assessment.
  • Ensuring system-wide resilience. Resilience must extend beyond banks to include non-bank financial institutions, financial markets, payment systems, technology infrastructure, critical third parties, and cross-border financial networks.
  • Promoting trustworthy innovation. Innovation such as artificial intelligence and tokenisation should strengthen the foundations of trust by preserving sound institutions, settlement finality, singleness of money, and financial integrity.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does maintaining financial stability matter for India’s economy?

  • Preventing systemic crises. Financial instability anywhere can threaten financial stability everywhere, making it crucial to build a robust system that can handle shocks without amplifying them.
  • Supporting economic growth. A resilient financial system ensures continuous provision of financial services even under severe shocks, which is vital for sustained economic development.
  • Managing emerging risks. With risks becoming more complex and interconnected globally, proactive regulation and supervision are necessary to anticipate and mitigate potential crises.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does the RBI plan to address challenges in data and risk assessment?

  • Enhancing data quality. The RBI stresses the need for better, more granular data on non-banking financial institutions, technology dependencies, and cross-border exposures to improve risk monitoring.
  • Scenario analysis. Making scenario analysis a cornerstone of risk management helps understand complex interactions and contagion channels within the financial system.
  • Forward-looking supervision. Regulation and supervision must be proactive, forward-looking, and proportionate to effectively manage emerging and unforeseen risks.

Back to basics: Financial Stability

Financial stability means having a financial system that can withstand shocks and continue to provide essential financial services. It involves resilient institutions, effective regulation, and mechanisms to manage risks and crises. Stability is crucial for economic growth and preventing systemic failures that can spread across sectors and borders.
