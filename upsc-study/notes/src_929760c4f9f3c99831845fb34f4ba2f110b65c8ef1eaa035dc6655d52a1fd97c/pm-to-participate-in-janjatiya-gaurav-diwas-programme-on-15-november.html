India implications

  • Education: EMRS expansion can improve access to residential schooling for tribal students, subject to culturally responsive curricula, language support and safe residential facilities.
  • Health and basic services: PM-JANMAN Multi-Purpose Centres may improve local access to schemes and community activities, but their effectiveness depends on staffing, connectivity and coordination with state departments.
  • Livelihoods: Tribal Multipurpose Marketing Centres can help producers obtain market access; outcomes will depend on aggregation, fair pricing, value addition and transport.
  • Culture and governance: Tribal Research Institutes and museums can preserve heritage, but preservation should avoid reducing living communities to static cultural exhibits and should support community participation.

Way forward

  • Shift monitoring from inauguration and expenditure counts to outcomes such as service utilisation, learning, health access, livelihood returns and beneficiary satisfaction.
  • Make Tribal Research Institutes, local bodies and tribal communities partners in programme design, cultural documentation and evaluation.
  • Provide language-sensitive education and culturally appropriate health and nutrition services, with adequate staffing and connectivity.
  • Strengthen producer aggregation, value addition, transparent pricing and transport links for tribal products.
