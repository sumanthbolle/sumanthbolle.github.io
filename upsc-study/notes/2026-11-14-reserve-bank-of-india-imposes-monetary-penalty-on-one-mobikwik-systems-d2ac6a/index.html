RBI Imposes ₹9 Lakh Penalty on Mobikwik for PPI Rule Violations

The Reserve Bank of India fined One Mobikwik Systems Limited ₹9 lakh for breaching rules on prepaid payment instruments and fraud reporting. This case highlights RBI's regulatory enforcement powers and compliance requirements in the digital payments sector.

RBI penalized Mobikwik ₹9 lakh for allowing fund transfers beyond limits and delayed fraud reporting under PPI regulations.

Why is this in the news?

On November 6, 2026, RBI imposed a monetary penalty on Mobikwik for non-compliance with its directions on prepaid payment instruments and fraud reporting.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

What penalty did RBI impose on Mobikwik and why?

  • Penalty details. RBI imposed a monetary penalty of ₹9.00 lakh on One Mobikwik Systems Limited for violating RBI's directions on prepaid payment instruments (PPIs) and fraud reporting.
  • Specific violations. Mobikwik allowed fund transfers by certain PPI holders to non-pre-registered beneficiaries beyond prescribed limits and failed to report certain frauds within the prescribed timeline.
  • Legal basis. The penalty was imposed under section 30(1) read with section 26(6) of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

How did RBI arrive at the penalty decision?

  • Inspection period. RBI conducted a statutory inspection of Mobikwik's operations from June 2025 to March 2026.
  • Due process. After supervisory findings of non-compliance, RBI issued a show-cause notice, considered Mobikwik's written and oral submissions, and then imposed the penalty.
  • Nature of action. The penalty addresses regulatory compliance deficiencies and does not affect the validity of any customer transactions or agreements.

Back to basics: Prepaid Payment Instruments and RBI Regulation

Prepaid Payment Instruments (PPIs) are instruments that facilitate the purchase of goods and services against the value stored on them. The Reserve Bank of India regulates PPIs to ensure secure and transparent digital payments. RBI issues directions on operational limits, fraud reporting, and customer protection to maintain trust in the payment ecosystem.
