Cyclone Arnab Intensifies, Andhra Pradesh and Telangana on High Alert

Cyclone Arnab over the Bay of Bengal is forecast to intensify into a very severe cyclonic storm and make landfall on Andhra Pradesh coast by October 28, 2026. This has prompted extensive disaster preparedness and evacuation measures in Andhra Pradesh and neighbouring Odisha, highlighting the importance of coordinated state response to natural disasters.

Cyclone Arnab is expected to hit Andhra Pradesh with very severe winds, leading to large-scale evacuations and disaster management efforts.

Why is this in the news?

On October 25, 2026, the India Meteorological Department issued a bulletin forecasting Cyclone Arnab to intensify into a very severe cyclonic storm and cross the Andhra Pradesh coast by October 28, prompting high alert and preparedness measures.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the current status and forecast of Cyclone Arnab?

  • Cyclone intensification. Cyclone Arnab started as a depression over the eastcentral Bay of Bengal and intensified into a deep depression, expected to become a very severe cyclonic storm by October 28, 2026.
  • Projected landfall. The cyclone is forecast to cross the Andhra Pradesh and Yanam coasts between Bapatla and Visakhapatnam with wind speeds of 115-125 kmph, gusting up to 140 kmph, on the morning of October 28.
  • Movement path. It is moving southwestwards and then west-northwestwards over the Bay of Bengal, intensifying as it approaches the coast.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What disaster preparedness measures are being taken by Andhra Pradesh and Odisha?

  • Evacuations planned. People in vulnerable coastal districts of Andhra Pradesh including Bapatla, Krishna, West Godavari, and Visakhapatnam are being evacuated to cyclone shelters on or before October 27.
  • Deployment of forces. Andhra Pradesh has deployed 12 battalions of the State Disaster Response Force and seven battalions of the National Disaster Response Force, with three more NDRF battalions requisitioned.
  • Special care groups. Expectant mothers near delivery, senior citizens, children, and persons with disabilities are being relocated to safe health centres and shelters in advance.
  • Odisha's response. Odisha has assigned ministers and IAS officers to supervise disaster management in five districts expected to face heavy rainfall, deployed ODRAF and NDRF teams, and closed schools on October 27 and 28.
  • Communication and control. 24x7 control rooms and dedicated helpline numbers have been established for public assistance in affected districts.

Why does this matter for UPSC preparation?

  • Climatology and geography. The cyclone's formation and path relate to Bay of Bengal climatology, a key topic in physical geography.
  • Social and health aspects. The special focus on vulnerable groups during evacuations highlights the social sector and health preparedness dimensions of disaster management.

Back to basics: Cyclones and Disaster Management

A cyclone is a large-scale air mass that rotates around a strong center of low atmospheric pressure, often causing heavy rain and strong winds. The Bay of Bengal is prone to cyclones due to warm sea surface temperatures and atmospheric conditions. Disaster management involves preparedness, response, and recovery activities by governments and agencies to reduce the impact of natural disasters on people and property.

Answer and trap

Mains question (15 marks)

Discuss the disaster preparedness and management measures taken by states in response to cyclones, using Cyclone Arnab as an example.

Discuss: Explain the steps taken by state governments to prepare for and respond to cyclones, highlighting coordination and social aspects.

Intro: Cyclones pose a recurring threat to coastal states, requiring comprehensive disaster preparedness and management.

  • Forecasting and early warning systems enable timely alerts and evacuation plans.
  • Deployment of disaster response forces and establishment of control rooms ensure operational readiness.
  • Special care for vulnerable groups such as pregnant women and persons with disabilities is crucial.
  • Coordination between state departments and with central agencies enhances effectiveness.

Conclusion: Effective disaster management reduces loss of life and property during cyclones, as seen in the preparations for Cyclone Arnab.
