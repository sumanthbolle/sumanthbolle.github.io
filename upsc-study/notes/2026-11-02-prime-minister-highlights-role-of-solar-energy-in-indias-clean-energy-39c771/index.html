Prime Minister Highlights Solar Energy's Role in India's Clean Energy Transition

The Prime Minister shared articles emphasizing solar power's expanding role in India's renewable energy growth, driven by initiatives like PM Surya Ghar and PM-KUSUM. This shift supports reducing electricity bills, creating jobs, and strengthening energy security, relevant for UPSC aspirants studying renewable energy policies and sustainable development.

India's solar energy expansion, supported by government schemes, is central to its clean energy transition and renewable energy targets.

Why is this in the news?

On November 2, 2026, the Prime Minister shared articles by Union Minister Pralhad Joshi and Mike Bloomberg highlighting solar energy's growing role in India's clean energy transition and new opportunities arising from it.

What is driving India's solar energy growth?

  • Government initiatives. Solar power growth is driven by schemes such as PM Surya Ghar and PM-KUSUM that promote rooftop solar and solar-powered agriculture.
  • Investment focus. There are increasing investments in solar energy storage and transmission infrastructure to support the expanding renewable capacity.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

How does solar energy impact India's economy and energy security?

  • Electricity cost reduction. The shift to solar energy helps reduce electricity bills for consumers.
  • Job creation. Expansion of solar power and related industries is creating new employment opportunities.
  • Energy security. Solar power strengthens India's energy security by diversifying energy sources.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the future prospects of solar energy in India?

  • Renewable energy target. India is working towards a 500 GW renewable energy target, with solar power playing a key role.
  • Emerging sectors. Future growth areas include solar-powered agriculture, energy storage solutions, manufacturing, and green jobs.

Back to basics: Renewable Energy Transition

Renewable energy transition refers to the shift from fossil fuel-based energy sources to sustainable alternatives like solar and wind power. This transition aims to reduce greenhouse gas emissions, enhance energy security, and promote sustainable economic growth. Solar energy harnesses sunlight using photovoltaic cells to generate electricity, making it a key component of clean energy strategies.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about India's solar energy initiatives: 1. PM Surya Ghar and PM-KUSUM are government schemes promoting solar power expansion. 2. India aims to achieve a 500 GW renewable energy target primarily through wind energy. Which of the statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the 500 GW renewable energy target includes multiple sources, with solar power playing a central role, not wind energy alone.
