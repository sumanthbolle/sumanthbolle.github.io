IITs Have Fewer Mental Health Counsellors Than UGC Norms: RTI Data

RTI data reveals that most Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs) have fewer mental health counsellors than the University Grants Commission (UGC) recommends. This shortfall is linked to rising student suicides and raises concerns about mental health governance in premier educational institutions.

Most IITs have fewer mental health counsellors than UGC guidelines, impacting student wellbeing and accountability.

Why is this in the news?

RTI responses obtained in October 2026 revealed the gap between UGC's recommended mental health counsellor norms and the actual counsellor strength in IITs, amid rising student suicides.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the UGC guidelines for mental health counsellors in higher education?

  • UGC recommendation. The UGC guidelines recommend one mental health professional or psychologist for every 500 students in higher educational institutions.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How do IITs compare with UGC norms on mental health counsellors?

  • Counsellor-student ratios. IIT Kharagpur has one counsellor for every 1,136 students, IIT Roorkee one for every 1,317 students, and IIT BHU one for every 4,750 students, all below UGC norms.
  • Only IIT Ropar meets norms. IIT Ropar reported having six counsellors and two visiting psychiatrists for about 3,000 students, meeting UGC's recommended ratio.
  • Other IITs' counsellor strength. IIT Kharagpur has 12 regular counsellors and one contract psychiatrist for over 14,000 students; IIT Roorkee has eight counsellors for 10,500 students; IIT BHU has two counsellors for over 9,500 students.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for student wellbeing and governance?

  • Link to student suicides. IIT Kharagpur reported 10 student suicides in five years, IIT Roorkee and IIT BHU reported three each, highlighting mental health challenges.
  • Protests and accountability. Student protests at IIT Bombay demand institutional accountability following a Dalit student's suicide, underscoring the urgency of adequate mental health support.
  • Faculty diversity issues. RTI data also revealed underrepresentation of SC, ST, OBC, and EWS faculty in IITs, with only IIT Ropar meeting SC and ST representation percentages.

Back to basics: Right to Information (RTI) Act

The RTI Act empowers citizens to request information from public authorities to promote transparency and accountability. It is a tool to obtain data on government and institutional functioning, including on social sector issues like education and health. RTI helps reveal gaps in policy implementation and resource allocation.

Prelims MCQ

The UGC recommends one mental health counsellor for every 500 students in higher education institutions. 2. IIT Ropar is the only IIT meeting the UGC's recommended counsellor-student ratio. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (c). Statement 1 is correct as per UGC guidelines; statement 2 is correct because only IIT Ropar meets the recommended ratio, unlike other IITs.
