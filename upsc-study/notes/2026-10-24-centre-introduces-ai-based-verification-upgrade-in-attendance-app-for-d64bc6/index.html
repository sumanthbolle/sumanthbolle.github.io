Centre Upgrades Rural Jobs Attendance App with AI Verification

The Union Rural Development Ministry introduced AI-based verification features in the attendance app for the rural employment scheme VB-G-RAM-G to prevent fraudulent attendance and ensure transparency. This upgrade matters for UPSC aspirants as it highlights the use of technology in governance and welfare scheme implementation.

The government enhanced the rural jobs attendance app with AI-powered liveness detection and anti-tampering features to secure wage payments and improve transparency.

Why is this in the news?

On October 23, 2026, the Union Rural Development Minister announced AI-based upgrades to the attendance app used in the rural employment scheme VB-G-RAM-G to address misuse and improve verification.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the new AI features in the attendance app?

  • Liveness detection and anti-tampering. The upgraded app includes AI-based liveness detection to verify that the person is a live worker, preventing use of dummy images or old photos.
  • Enhanced face verification. Face verification has been refined to account for regional differences across States, improving accuracy.
  • Photo de-duplication module. An AI module identifies suspicious or repeated photographs to detect fraudulent attendance entries.
  • Automatic withholding of payments. When suspicious entries are detected, wage lists and Fund Transfer Orders are automatically withheld until checks are completed.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why was the AI upgrade necessary?

  • Previous misuse of attendance app. Despite earlier face authentication, fake attendance was recorded using photo-to-photo capturing instead of live images.
  • Discrepancies detected. There were mismatches in worker counts, gender composition, repeated photos in multiple muster rolls, and inconsistencies between morning and afternoon attendance.
  • Protecting wages and public funds. The government aims to safeguard workers' wages and ensure public funds are transparently used for their intended purpose.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does the system handle offline and exceptional cases?

  • Offline attendance recording. Attendance can be recorded offline where network connectivity is unavailable at worksites.
  • Exception mechanism. Face authentication exceptions are allowed for elderly workers, persons with disabilities, or when technical issues occur.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the current scale and impact of the rural employment scheme?

  • Active worksites and workers. As of October 2026, 31.23 lakh worksites are active, employing over 3.65 crore workers from 2.89 crore rural households.
  • Person-days generated. Nearly 46 crore person-days have been generated, with approximately 40 crore recorded through face authentication.

Back to basics: Use of AI in Governance

Artificial Intelligence (AI) in governance refers to the application of AI technologies to improve public service delivery, enhance transparency, and reduce fraud. In welfare schemes, AI can verify beneficiary identity, detect duplicate or fake entries, and ensure funds reach the intended recipients. This helps in efficient monitoring and accountability of government programs.
