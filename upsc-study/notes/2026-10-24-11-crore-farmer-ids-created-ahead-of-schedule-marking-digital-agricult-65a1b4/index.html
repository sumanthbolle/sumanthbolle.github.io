11 Crore Farmer IDs Created Ahead of Schedule, Boosting Digital Agriculture

The Government of India created 11 crore unique Farmer IDs five months before the March 31, 2027 deadline under the Digital Agriculture Mission. This digital identity aims to improve transparency, reduce paperwork, and ensure faster access to government schemes, loans, insurance, and disaster relief for farmers.

The creation of 11 crore Farmer IDs ahead of schedule marks a key step in making Indian agriculture more transparent and efficient through digital technology.

Why is this in the news?

On October 24, 2026, the Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers’ Welfare announced the creation of 11 crore Farmer IDs, five months ahead of the March 31, 2027 target, under the Digital Agriculture Mission.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the Farmer ID initiative and why was it launched?

  • Unique digital identity. The Farmer ID initiative provides farmers with a unique digital identity to enable transparent, efficient, and timely access to government schemes, bank credit, crop insurance, and disaster relief.
  • Reducing paperwork. It reduces the need for farmers to repeatedly submit documents or visit government offices and banks for different schemes, saving time and money.
  • Improving transparency. The initiative helps eliminate duplication and fraud by linking a verified farmer database with agricultural services.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does the Farmer ID benefit farmers and agricultural services?

  • Simplified loan process. Bank loans and Kisan Credit Card applications become easier with less repeated document verification.
  • Transparent crop insurance. Under Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana, compensation for crop losses is assessed and paid more transparently directly into farmers’ bank accounts.
  • Faster disaster relief. Disaster relief assistance reaches genuine and eligible farmers more quickly through the digital system.
  • Fair crop procurement. The digital system eliminates intermediaries in Minimum Support Price (MSP) procurement, ensuring farmers receive full value for their produce.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What does the achievement signify for governance and federal cooperation?

  • Ahead of schedule. The target of creating 11 crore Farmer IDs was completed five months ahead of the March 31, 2027 deadline.
  • Cooperative federalism. The success is credited to coordinated efforts of the Central and state governments, Union Territories, and field officials.
  • Inclusive approach. States and UTs are working to include tenant farmers and actual cultivators on the digital platform to ensure no eligible farmer is left out.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does this matter for agricultural development and policy?

  • Supports PM Modi’s vision. The Digital Agriculture Mission advances the vision of a prosperous farming sector and a developed India.
  • Strengthens policy and monitoring. A verified farmer database improves coordination among departments, policy formulation, scheme monitoring, and benefit delivery.
  • Foundation for modern agriculture. Farmer ID is a foundation for a transparent, accountable, and farmer-centric governance system using modern technology.

Back to basics: Digital Identity in Agriculture

A digital identity for farmers is a unique identifier that helps link farmers to various government schemes and services. It reduces duplication, fraud, and paperwork by creating a verified database. This enables faster and more transparent delivery of benefits such as loans, insurance, and subsidies, improving governance and service efficiency in the agricultural sector.
