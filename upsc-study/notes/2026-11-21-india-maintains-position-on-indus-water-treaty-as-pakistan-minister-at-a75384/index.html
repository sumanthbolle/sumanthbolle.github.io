India's stance on Indus Water Treaty amid Pakistan's SCO forum participation

India reaffirmed its unchanged position on the Indus Water Treaty (IWT) during the visit of Pakistan's Climate Change Minister Musadik Malik to the SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum in Delhi in November 2026. This event marked a rare diplomatic engagement between the two countries following recent tensions, highlighting the complexity of bilateral water disputes within multilateral platforms.

India maintains its firm position on the Indus Water Treaty despite Pakistan's participation in the SCO forum hosted by India.

Why is this in the news?

India clarified its unchanged position on the Indus Water Treaty on November 20, 2026, coinciding with Pakistan's minister attending the SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum in Delhi.

What is India's position on the Indus Water Treaty amid recent developments?

  • Unchanged stance. India's Ministry of External Affairs stated on November 20, 2026, that there has been absolutely no change in its position on the Indus Water Treaty despite Pakistan's participation in the SCO forum.
  • Suspension context. India suspended the treaty in 2025 as a punitive measure following the April 22 Pahalgam terror attack that resulted in 26 deaths.

Why does Pakistan's participation in the SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum matter?

  • Rare diplomatic engagement. Pakistan's Climate Change Minister Musadik Malik attended the forum in Delhi, marking one of the first times a Pakistani minister participated in an event hosted by India since military hostilities in May 2025.
  • Multilateral framework. The forum is a multilateral event within the SCO framework, with invitations extended to all member countries including Pakistan, aiming to foster dialogue.

Back to basics: Indus Water Treaty and SCO framework

The Indus Water Treaty is a water-sharing agreement between India and Pakistan, governing the use of rivers originating in India and flowing into Pakistan. The Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) is a multilateral regional grouping that includes India and Pakistan as permanent members, providing a platform for dialogue and cooperation among member states.
