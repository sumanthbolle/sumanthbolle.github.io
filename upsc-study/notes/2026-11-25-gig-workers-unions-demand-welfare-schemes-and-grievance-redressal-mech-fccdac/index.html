Gig Workers Demand Welfare Schemes and Grievance Redressal in Karnataka

Gig workers’ unions in Karnataka have urged the state government to implement social security benefits and activate grievance redressal mechanisms under the Karnataka Platform-Based Gig Workers Act, 2025. This issue highlights emerging labour rights challenges in the gig economy, relevant for social welfare and labour policy discussions in UPSC exams.

Karnataka gig workers demand health insurance, paid leave, educational scholarships, and transparent welfare fee management under the 2025 Act.

Why is this in the news?

On behalf of platform-based gig workers, unions in Karnataka submitted a memorandum demanding immediate rollout of welfare schemes and grievance redressal mechanisms under the Karnataka Platform-Based Gig Workers (Social Security and Welfare) Act, 2025.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What welfare demands have gig workers made?

  • Health insurance coverage. Gig workers demand comprehensive health insurance for themselves and their families.
  • Paid leave provisions. They seek paid leave during periods of hospitalisation to protect their livelihoods.
  • Educational scholarships. Workers want scholarships for their children to support education.
  • Financial transparency. Unions demand strict auditing and reconciliation of welfare fees deposited by aggregators based on actual transaction data.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What grievance redressal issues do gig workers face?

  • Activation of grievance mechanism. Workers demand immediate activation of the official grievance registration and resolution system.
  • Arbitrary deactivation concerns. They report that aggregator platforms arbitrarily deactivate or block worker IDs without due process, severely impacting livelihoods.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the legal and administrative context?

  • Karnataka Act provisions. The Karnataka Platform-Based Gig Workers (Social Security and Welfare) Act, 2025 mandates aggregators to pay quarterly welfare fees for social security starting July 2026.
  • Court challenges. Several aggregator platforms challenged the Act's constitutional validity, leading to an interim court order requiring them to deposit contributions with the court registry.
  • Registration process. Unions call for expediting and simplifying the registration of gig workers under the State welfare board.

Back to basics: Social Security for Informal Sector Workers

Social security schemes provide protection to workers against risks such as illness, injury, and loss of income. Informal sector workers, including gig workers, often lack formal employment benefits, making welfare schemes and grievance redressal mechanisms essential for their livelihood security and social justice.
