  • Regional connectivity can support more balanced metropolitan development by linking major urban centres and economic nodes, provided benefits reach commuters across the corridor.
  • Integration with metro systems, buses and first- and last-mile services is central to converting a rapid rail corridor into a dependable multimodal mobility network.
  • Renewable-energy measures in transport infrastructure may reduce operational emissions and energy costs, but their actual impact requires transparent performance assessment.

Way forward

  • Plan regional rail together with metro, bus, walking, cycling and feeder networks from the outset.
  • Use transparent indicators for affordability, accessibility, reliability, safety, energy use and emissions.
  • Coordinate corridor development with land-use planning to avoid disconnected stations and unbalanced urban expansion.
  • Assess proposed corridors through demand, social-equity, fiscal and environmental criteria before approval.
