Supreme Court Upholds Bail Condition for Delhi Riots Accused to Stay in Delhi

The Supreme Court refused to relax bail conditions for Shadab Ahmad, an accused in the 2020 Delhi riots case, barring him from leaving Delhi to attend law classes in Ghaziabad. This ruling highlights the judiciary's approach to bail in sensitive public order cases and its emphasis on trial integrity.

The Supreme Court maintained strict bail conditions for Delhi riots accused, emphasizing the need to remain within jurisdiction during trial.

Why is this in the news?

On October 26, 2026, the Supreme Court refused to modify bail conditions for Shadab Ahmad, accused in the 2020 Delhi riots, preventing him from attending law classes outside Delhi.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What did the Supreme Court decide regarding bail conditions?

  • Refusal to modify bail. The Supreme Court declined to relax the bail condition that required Shadab Ahmad to remain in Delhi during his trial, rejecting his plea to attend law classes in Ghaziabad.
  • Reason for refusal. The Bench questioned why Ahmad had not sought modification of bail conditions from the trial court before approaching the Supreme Court.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the bail conditions imposed on the accused?

  • Stay within Delhi. The accused must remain in Delhi and are barred from leaving the national capital during the trial.
  • Restrictions on public activities. They are prohibited from attending or addressing public functions or rallies and from circulating any posts or materials related to the case until the trial concludes.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for law and order and judiciary?

  • Trial integrity. Strict bail conditions ensure that accused persons remain within the court's jurisdiction, which helps maintain the integrity of the trial process.
  • Judicial caution in sensitive cases. The court’s decision reflects caution in handling cases related to public order and communal violence, balancing liberty with public safety.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the background of the case?

  • 2020 Delhi riots. The accused are charged under the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act and Indian Penal Code for alleged conspiracy in the 2020 northeast Delhi riots, which resulted in 53 deaths and over 700 injuries.
  • Bail granted with conditions. In January 2026, the Supreme Court granted bail to Shadab Ahmad and others with stringent conditions, while denying bail to some accused pending further examination.

Back to basics: Bail and Judicial Discretion

Bail is a legal provision that allows an accused person to be released from custody during trial under certain conditions. Courts impose bail conditions to ensure the accused's presence during trial and to prevent interference with the investigation or public order. The judiciary balances individual liberty with societal safety when deciding bail terms.

Common mistake. The Supreme Court did not deny bail altogether but refused to relax specific bail conditions related to travel during the trial.
