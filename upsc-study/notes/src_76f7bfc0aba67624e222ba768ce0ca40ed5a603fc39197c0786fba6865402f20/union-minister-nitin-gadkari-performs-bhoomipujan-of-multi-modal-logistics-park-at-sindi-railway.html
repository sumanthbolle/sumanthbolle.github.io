A multi-modal logistics park is an integrated logistics node that combines transport connectivity with cargo aggregation, storage, handling and related services; in the Sindi Railway proposal, the release specifically describes a dry port, cold storage, warehouses, exporter clusters and a customs office.

Background

  • The Press Information Bureau reported on 1 November 2026 that the Union Minister for Road Transport and Highways performed bhoomipujan for a Multi-Modal Logistics Park at Sindi Railway.
  • The project is described as a joint development of National Highways Logistics Management Limited and Jawaharlal Nehru Port Authority over 150 acres near Sindi Railway station.
  • The release connects the proposed dry port with exports from Vidarbha and with facilities including cold storage, warehouses, exporter clusters and a customs office.
