India’s First AI × Food Tech Hackathon Hosted by NIFTEM-K

NIFTEM-K hosted 'Hack the Fork', India's first AI × Food Tech Hackathon focused on AI solutions for the alternative protein industry. This event highlights the integration of AI in food technology, relevant for innovation and sustainable food systems in UPSC preparation.

NIFTEM-K’s AI × Food Tech Hackathon promotes AI-driven innovation to address challenges in alternative protein and sustainable food production.

Why is this in the news?

On October 24-25, 2026, NIFTEM-K hosted 'Hack the Fork', India's first AI × Food Tech Hackathon, focusing on AI applications in the alternative protein industry.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What was the AI × Food Tech Hackathon about?

  • Event focus. The hackathon aimed to leverage Artificial Intelligence to solve real-world challenges in the alternative protein industry, including plant-based food innovation, fermentation technologies, and sustainable food systems.
  • Organisers and participants. Organised by Hack the Fork, a France-based non-profit, in collaboration with NIFTEM-K, the event brought together over 90 participants from 22 teams across 10 states, including students and researchers from IITs, IIITs, and the University of Delhi.
  • Duration and format. The intensive 24-hour innovation challenge involved teams presenting AI-driven solutions before an expert jury.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does the hackathon matter for food technology and innovation?

  • Encouraging interdisciplinary innovation. The event fostered creative thinking and collaboration across disciplines to develop technology-driven solutions for emerging challenges in food processing and alternative proteins.
  • Focus areas. Key exploration areas included nutritional optimisation, food safety, sustainable food production, and food waste reduction, all critical for building a resilient and sustainable food ecosystem.
  • Support and mentorship. Participants received expert guidance from mentors affiliated with Good Food Institute India, IIIT Sonepat, and the University of Delhi, enhancing the quality and practicality of their solutions.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the outcomes and future prospects of the hackathon?

  • Prizes and incubation. Winning teams competed for ₹1.5 lakh in prizes and opportunities to join NIFTEM-K’s Startup Incubation Programme, supporting entrepreneurship in food technology.
  • Institutional commitment. The hackathon reinforced NIFTEM-K’s commitment to fostering sustainable food technology, interdisciplinary innovation, and entrepreneurship.

Back to basics: Artificial Intelligence in Food Technology

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to computer systems that can perform tasks typically requiring human intelligence, such as learning and problem-solving. In food technology, AI can optimize processes like nutritional analysis, food safety monitoring, and sustainable production methods. Hackathons are time-bound innovation contests that encourage collaborative problem-solving and rapid prototyping of new ideas.
