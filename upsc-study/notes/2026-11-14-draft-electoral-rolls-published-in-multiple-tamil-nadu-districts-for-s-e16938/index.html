Draft Electoral Rolls Published in Tamil Nadu's Nilgiris District for Special Intensive Revision 2027

The Nilgiris district administration published draft electoral rolls on November 12, 2026, as part of the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) process for 2027. This exercise updates voter details and is vital for maintaining accurate electoral rolls, impacting democratic governance and electoral integrity.

The Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls in Nilgiris updates voter data to ensure accurate and inclusive electoral participation for 2027.

Why is this in the news?

The Nilgiris district administration published the draft electoral rolls on November 12, 2026, initiating the Special Intensive Revision for 2027.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls?

  • Definition and purpose. The Special Intensive Revision (SIR) is an exercise to update electoral rolls by including new voters, deleting ineligible names, correcting details, and updating addresses to maintain accurate voter lists.
  • Duration and process. In Nilgiris, the SIR runs from November 12 to December 11, 2026, allowing eligible residents to apply for inclusion, deletion, or correction of voter details.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the key features of the Nilgiris draft electoral rolls?

  • Voter statistics. The draft rolls list 5,54,580 voters: 2,64,741 men, 2,90,073 women, and 26 third-gender voters, marking an increase of 2,834 voters since the final rolls published on April 6, 2026.
  • Assembly constituencies. Nilgiris has three Assembly constituencies: Udhagamandalam with 1,89,824 voters, Gudalur (reserved) with 1,86,275 voters, and Coonoor with 1,78,741 voters.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How can voters participate in the revision process?

  • Application submission. Eligible voters can submit applications for inclusion, deletion, or correction at designated polling station officers during working hours or at Revenue Divisional Officers and Tahsildar offices.
  • Special camps. Special camps are organized at polling stations on November 21, 22, and 29, 2026, to facilitate voter applications.
  • Deadlines. The last date for submitting claims and objections is December 11, 2026, with final electoral rolls scheduled for publication on January 4, 2027.

Back to basics: Electoral Roll Revision

Electoral roll revision is the process of updating the voter list to include new eligible voters, remove names of those no longer eligible, and correct errors in details. It ensures that the electoral rolls are accurate and inclusive, which is essential for free and fair elections. Special Summary Revision or Intensive Revision exercises are conducted periodically to maintain updated voter records.
