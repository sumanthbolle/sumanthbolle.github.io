RBI Auctions 91-Day, 182-Day and 364-Day Treasury Bills

The Reserve Bank of India announced the auction of Government of India Treasury Bills with notified amounts of ₹8,000 crore for 91-day and 182-day bills, and ₹7,000 crore for 364-day bills. This auction is important for understanding government debt instruments, monetary policy operations, and financial market mechanisms.

RBI conducts price-based auctions for short-term government securities to manage liquidity and government borrowing.

Why is this in the news?

On November 6, 2026, RBI announced the auction details for 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day Treasury Bills to be held on November 11, 2026.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

What are the details of the RBI Treasury Bill auction?

  • Auction amounts. The auction includes ₹8,000 crore each for 91-day and 182-day Treasury Bills, and ₹7,000 crore for 364-day Treasury Bills, totaling ₹23,000 crore.
  • Auction and settlement dates. The auction is scheduled for November 11, 2026, with settlement on November 12, 2026.
  • Auction method. The auction uses a price-based multiple price method where bids are submitted electronically through RBI’s Core Banking Solution (E-Kuber).

[1] Reserve Bank of India

Who can participate and how?

  • Eligible participants. State Governments, Union Territories with legislatures, eligible Provident Funds, designated Foreign Central Banks, and RBI-specified persons or institutions can participate on a non-competitive basis.
  • Retail investor participation. Individuals can participate as retail investors on a non-competitive basis with allocation capped at 5% of the notified amount, and can also bid via the Retail Direct portal.
  • Bid submission timing. Competitive bids are accepted from 10:30 am to 11:30 am, and non-competitive bids from 10:30 am to 11:00 am on auction day.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

Why does this auction matter for the economy and exams?

  • Government borrowing. Treasury Bills are short-term government securities that help the government raise funds to meet its fiscal needs.
  • Monetary policy tool. Auctions influence liquidity in the banking system and help RBI manage short-term interest rates.
  • Financial market understanding. Knowledge of auction mechanisms and participants is essential for understanding government securities markets and their role in the economy.

Back to basics: Treasury Bills and Government Securities

Treasury Bills are short-term debt instruments issued by the government to meet temporary funding needs. They have maturities of less than one year and are issued at a discount to face value, with the difference representing interest. Auctions for these bills are conducted by the Reserve Bank of India to manage government borrowing and liquidity in the financial system.
