President's Intervention Sought as JNU Hunger Strike Hits 17 Days

JNU students have been on an indefinite hunger strike since October 3, 2026, protesting unresolved grievances and rustication orders. CPI(M) MP John Brittas has appealed to the President and Education Minister to intervene due to deteriorating health of the students and administrative inaction.

The President, as Visitor of JNU, was urged to revoke rustication orders and address student grievances amid a prolonged hunger strike.

Why is this in the news?

On October 19, 2026, CPI(M) Rajya Sabha MP John Brittas sought President Droupadi Murmu's intervention as the JNU students' hunger strike entered its 17th day.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What triggered the JNU students' hunger strike?

  • Start and cause. The hunger strike began on October 3, 2026, following the university administration's alleged failure to address longstanding student concerns.
  • Key grievances. Students protested rustication orders against elected student union representatives and objectionable provisions in the Chief Proctor's Office Manual.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why is President's intervention being sought?

  • Role as Visitor. The President is designated as the Visitor of JNU under the Jawaharlal Nehru University Act, 1966, which includes powers to intervene in university affairs.
  • Urgency due to health. The hunger strike's prolonged duration and deteriorating health of participants prompted calls for urgent intervention.
  • Democratic traditions at stake. John Brittas emphasized preserving JNU's democratic traditions and ensuring students' voices are heard, not silenced.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the constitutional and governance issues involved?

  • Foundational principles. JNU's founding principles include social justice, secularism, and a democratic way of life as per its Act.
  • Impact of rustication. Rustication of elected representatives affects the students' union's functioning and collective grievance articulation.
  • Disciplinary measures. The enforcement of the CPO Manual with fines and disciplinary actions may discourage legitimate protests and grievances.

Back to basics: Role of the President as Visitor of Central Universities

The President of India is designated as the Visitor of certain central universities, including JNU. This role includes oversight powers such as reviewing university administration, intervening in disputes, and ensuring the institution adheres to its founding principles. The Visitor can take actions like revoking disciplinary measures and ensuring democratic functioning within the university.
