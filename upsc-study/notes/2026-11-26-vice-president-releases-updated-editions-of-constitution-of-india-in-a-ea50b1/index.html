Vice-President Releases Updated Constitution Editions in 22 Scheduled Languages

On Constitution Day 2026, the Vice-President of India released updated editions of the Constitution in all 22 Scheduled Languages, enhancing accessibility and promoting linguistic diversity. This event is important for UPSC aspirants as it reflects India's commitment to constitutional awareness and the role of languages in democratic participation.

The updated Constitution editions in all Scheduled Languages deepen democratic engagement by making constitutional knowledge accessible in citizens' mother tongues.

Why is this in the news?

The Vice-President released updated editions of the Constitution of India in all 22 Scheduled Languages on Constitution Day, 26 November 2026.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

What was released on Constitution Day 2026?

  • Updated editions. The Vice-President released updated editions of the Constitution of India in all 22 Scheduled Languages listed in the Eighth Schedule.
  • Event details. The release took place at Vigyan Bhawan, New Delhi, with the presence of Union Minister of State for Law & Justice Shri Arjun Ram Meghwal and other dignitaries.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

Why does this release matter?

  • Promoting linguistic diversity. Making the Constitution available in all Scheduled Languages celebrates India's linguistic diversity and democratic inclusiveness.
  • Empowering citizens. Access to the Constitution in one's mother tongue brings governance closer to citizens and helps them understand their constitutional rights, duties, and values better.
  • Constitution as living spirit. The Constitution is described as not merely a legal framework but the living spirit of the nation, reflecting the vision of Dr. B. R. Ambedkar and the Constituent Assembly.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

How does this relate to India's cultural and linguistic heritage?

  • Respect for all languages. The Vice-President emphasized mutual respect for all Indian languages, highlighting that languages carry culture, tradition, and identity.
  • Government initiatives. Initiatives like Gyan Bharatam and Kashi Tamil Sangamam under the Prime Minister's leadership aim to preserve and promote India's languages and knowledge traditions.
  • National unity. The release embodies the spirit of Ek Bharat, Shreshtha Bharat and supports the vision of a strong, united nation essential for preserving all languages.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the broader goals of this initiative?

  • Constitutional awareness. The release aims to promote constitutional literacy and make the Constitution accessible beyond Parliament and courts to every village, home, and school.
  • Inclusive access. It seeks to bring the Constitution closer to citizens in rural areas, tribal communities, and the younger generation.
  • Strengthening democracy. By enhancing citizens' awareness of their constitutional rights and duties, the initiative strengthens the democratic framework of India.

Back to basics: Scheduled Languages and the Constitution

The Constitution of India recognizes 22 Scheduled Languages in its Eighth Schedule, reflecting the country's linguistic diversity. Making constitutional texts available in these languages helps citizens understand their rights and duties in their mother tongue, which supports democratic participation and cultural preservation.
