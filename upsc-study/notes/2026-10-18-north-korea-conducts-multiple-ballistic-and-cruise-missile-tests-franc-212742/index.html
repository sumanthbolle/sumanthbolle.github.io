North Korea and France Conduct Strategic Missile Tests

North Korea has carried out multiple ballistic and cruise missile tests, while France test-fired its new M51.3 nuclear-capable ballistic missile from a submarine. These developments highlight ongoing global security challenges and advancements in missile technology relevant for UPSC preparation.

North Korea's missile tests and France's nuclear-capable missile trial reflect evolving global security dynamics and technological progress in strategic weapons.

Why is this in the news?

On October 6, 2026, France test-fired its new M51.3 nuclear-capable ballistic missile from a nuclear-powered submarine amid rising European security tensions.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What missile tests did France conduct recently?

  • M51.3 missile test. France test-fired the M51.3, a variant of its M51 nuclear-capable ballistic missile, from the Le Vigilant nuclear-powered submarine off Brittany.
  • Test details. The missile impacted in the North Atlantic hundreds of kilometres from any coastline and was launched without a nuclear warhead, complying with France’s international commitments.
  • Significance. The test demonstrated the advanced high-tech capabilities of the French defence industry in nuclear deterrence.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why do these missile tests matter for global security?

  • European security context. The test occurred amid increased tensions in Europe, with French President Emmanuel Macron warning of evolving Russian aggression and hybrid attacks.
  • Nuclear deterrence. France is the EU’s sole nuclear weapons power and has proposed that European partners join its advanced nuclear deterrence framework.

Back to basics: Ballistic missile and nuclear deterrence

Ballistic missiles are weapons that follow a high-arc trajectory to deliver warheads over long distances. Nuclear deterrence is a strategy where a country maintains nuclear weapons to discourage attacks by promising retaliation, thus aiming to preserve peace through the threat of mutually assured destruction.

Consider the following statements: 1. France test-fired its M51.3 nuclear-capable ballistic missile from a nuclear-powered submarine in October 2026. 2. The missile test was conducted with a live nuclear warhead to demonstrate France's deterrence capability.

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the missile was test-fired without a nuclear warhead, complying with international commitments.
