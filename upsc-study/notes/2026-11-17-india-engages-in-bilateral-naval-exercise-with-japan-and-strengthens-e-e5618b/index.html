India Strengthens Maritime and Economic Ties with Japan, Egypt, and Thailand

India conducted the bilateral naval exercise JAIMEX 2026 with Japan and held ministerial-level trade meetings with Egypt and Thailand in November 2026. These engagements enhance defence cooperation and expand bilateral trade, crucial for India's strategic and economic interests.

India's naval exercise with Japan and trade talks with Egypt and Thailand reflect its focus on deepening defence and economic partnerships in key regions.

Why is this in the news?

In November 2026, India participated in the JAIMEX naval exercise with Japan and held high-level trade committee meetings with Egypt and Thailand to boost bilateral cooperation.

What happened in the India-Japan naval exercise JAIMEX 2026?

  • Exercise details. INS Sahyadri participated in JAIMEX 2026 with Japan Maritime Self-Defense Force's JS Ise on 13–14 November 2026, conducting tactical manoeuvres, communication drills, and cross-deck landings.
  • Purpose of the exercise. The exercise aimed to strengthen interoperability, operational coordination, and mutual understanding between the two navies, reinforcing the India-Japan maritime partnership.
  • Strategic significance. JAIMEX 2026 reflects growing maritime cooperation contributing to a safe and stable Indo-Pacific region.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How is India expanding economic ties with Egypt?

  • Trade growth and targets. India-Egypt bilateral trade grew 35% to USD 6.5 billion in FY 2025-26, with a target to reach USD 12 billion.
  • Joint Working Group formation. Both countries agreed to form a Joint Working Group to explore a Preferential or Free Trade Agreement to enhance market access and investment.
  • Key sectors and cooperation. Priority sectors include pharmaceuticals, engineering, textiles, renewable energy, and digital economy, with over 300 companies participating in the India-Egypt Business Forum.
  • Strategic location advantage. Egypt's proximity to the Suez Canal and connectivity to African, Arab, and European markets offers export-oriented manufacturing and regional supply chain opportunities.

[2] Press Information Bureau

What are the highlights of India-Thailand economic cooperation?

  • Trade growth. India-Thailand bilateral trade reached USD 21.49 billion in FY 2025-26, growing 12.6% over the previous year.
  • Trade facilitation and agreements. Discussions focused on enhancing utilization of ASEAN–India Trade in Goods Agreement and India–Thailand Early Harvest Scheme, and resolving market access and non-tariff barrier issues.
  • Sectoral cooperation. Cooperation opportunities include pharmaceuticals, AYUSH, textiles, petrochemicals, gems, and services sectors.
  • Financial connectivity. Both sides discussed linking India's UPI with Thailand's PromptPay, local currency settlement, and FinTech cooperation between their central banks.

Back to basics: Bilateral Trade Agreements and Naval Exercises

Bilateral trade agreements are negotiated between two countries to reduce trade barriers and increase economic cooperation, often including preferential tariffs or free trade provisions. Naval exercises between countries are coordinated military activities aimed at improving interoperability, operational coordination, and mutual trust, which enhance strategic partnerships and regional security.

Mains question (15 marks)

Examine the role of defence diplomacy and economic cooperation in strengthening India's bilateral relations with countries like Japan, Egypt, and Thailand.

Intro: Defence diplomacy and economic cooperation are key tools for India to enhance bilateral relations and regional influence.

  • Defence exercises like JAIMEX 2026 improve interoperability and trust, reinforcing strategic partnerships and regional security.

Conclusion: Combined defence and economic engagements strengthen India's bilateral relations and support its broader strategic and economic objectives.
