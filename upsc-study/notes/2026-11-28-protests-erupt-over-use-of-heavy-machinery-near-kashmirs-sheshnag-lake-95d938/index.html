Protests Over Heavy Machinery Use Near Kashmir’s Sheshnag Lake

A viral video showing an earthmover operating near Sheshnag Lake in Jammu and Kashmir sparked protests and environmental concerns. The controversy highlights issues of ecological fragility, pilgrimage infrastructure, and governance in the region.

Use of heavy machinery near Sheshnag Lake raised environmental alarms and public protests in Jammu and Kashmir.

Why is this in the news?

On November 26, 2026, a viral video of an earthmover near Sheshnag Lake triggered protests and official clarifications about the nature of the work being done.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What caused the protests near Sheshnag Lake?

  • Viral video sparks outrage. A video showing an earthmover operating close to Sheshnag Lake went viral, leading to public protests and criticism from environmentalists and political parties.
  • Concerns over ecological damage. Activists warned that heavy machinery in this ecologically fragile zone risks destabilising the lake’s morphology and harming downstream areas.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the significance of Sheshnag Lake?

  • High-altitude alpine lake. Sheshnag Lake is located at 11,780 feet altitude, spanning about 1.1 km by 0.7 km, and is an important stopover for the annual Amarnath pilgrimage.
  • Pilgrimage route challenges. Traditionally, pilgrims trek steep, unpaved paths after the macadamised road ends at Chandanwari to reach the Amarnath cave shrine.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the official responses and environmental risks?

  • BRO clarifies work nature. The Border Roads Organisation stated the work is limited to widening and maintaining existing pilgrimage tracks, not new construction.
  • Inspection finds work away from lake. A government inspection team found the earthmoving activity is 200 to 300 feet away from the lake and ruled out any earth falling into it.
  • Glacial retreat and flood risks. The region's glaciers, including the Kolahoi glacier, have retreated substantially, and several glacial lakes are flagged as potentially dangerous for floods.

Back to basics: Environmental conservation in ecologically fragile zones

Ecologically fragile zones are areas sensitive to environmental disturbances, requiring careful regulation of human activities to prevent damage. High-altitude lakes and glacial regions are particularly vulnerable due to their role in water supply and biodiversity. Environmental governance involves balancing development needs with conservation through laws, inspections, and public participation.
