Quad Summit Scheduled in Manila on November 17, 2026

The Quad leaders from India, the U.S., Australia, and Japan will meet in Manila on November 17, 2026, marking the first time the summit is held in a non-Quad country. This meeting will focus on critical minerals, energy supply chains, and maritime security, important for India's Indo-Pacific strategy.

The 2026 Quad Summit in Manila will address supply chain security and maritime safety, reflecting India's strategic priorities in the Indo-Pacific.

Why is this in the news?

The Quad Summit is set for November 17, 2026, in Manila, confirmed by sources in New Delhi, highlighting a key event in the Indo-Pacific regional security framework.

What is the significance of the Quad Summit in Manila?

  • First non-Quad host. The 2026 Quad Summit is the first to be held in a non-Quad country, the Philippines, indicating the group's expanding diplomatic outreach.
  • Focus areas. The summit will address critical minerals and energy supply chains, and the safety of commercial shipping lanes, which are vital for regional stability.
  • Maritime security importance. India prioritizes maritime security due to attacks on Indian seafarers in conflict zones like the Hormuz Strait and the Black Sea.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Who are the participants and what is the context?

  • Participating leaders. Leaders include India's Prime Minister Narendra Modi, U.S. President Donald Trump, Japanese Prime Minister Sanae Takaichi, and Australian Prime Minister Anthony Albanese.
  • Timing and location. The summit will occur on November 17, 2026, on the sidelines of ASEAN summits in Manila.
  • Challenges in hosting. India has not hosted the Quad Summit yet due to the U.S. President’s unavailability, but retains chairship and may host next year.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does the Quad Summit matter for India and the region?

  • Indo-Pacific strategy. The Quad is a key grouping for India’s Indo-Pacific policy, focusing on regional security and cooperation.
  • Supply chain security. Securing critical minerals and energy supply chains is crucial for economic and strategic stability in the region.
  • Maritime safety. Protecting commercial shipping lanes and seafarers aligns with India’s interests amid regional conflicts affecting maritime routes.

Back to basics: Quad grouping and Indo-Pacific strategy

The Quad is a strategic forum comprising India, the U.S., Japan, and Australia, focused on promoting a free, open, and inclusive Indo-Pacific region. It addresses issues like maritime security, supply chain resilience, and regional cooperation. The grouping meets at leader-level summits to coordinate policies and strengthen partnerships.
