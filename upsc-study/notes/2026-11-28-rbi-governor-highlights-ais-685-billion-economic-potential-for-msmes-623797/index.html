RBI Governor Highlights AI’s $685 Billion Economic Potential for MSMEs

RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra stated that AI adoption could unlock an economic value of $490-685 billion for India's MSMEs, but only about 15% currently use the technology. He urged faster AI adoption to boost competitiveness, productivity, and access to finance, linking technology with economic growth and financial policy.

AI adoption in MSMEs can add up to $685 billion in economic value, but uptake remains low at 15%, requiring urgent acceleration.

Why is this in the news?

On November 27, 2026, RBI Governor Sanjay Malhotra highlighted AI's potential economic value for MSMEs at the SME Finance and Investment Summit in Mumbai.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the economic potential of AI for MSMEs?

  • Potential value. AI adoption could unlock an economic value between $490 billion and $685 billion for India's micro, small and medium enterprises (MSMEs).
  • Current adoption rate. Only around 15% of India's MSMEs currently use AI technology, risking missing out on substantial productivity gains.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why should MSMEs adopt AI faster?

  • Competitiveness and productivity. AI can help MSMEs improve speed, quality, and personalisation, enabling them to compete better with larger enterprises.
  • Bridging productivity gap. Value added per worker in Indian MSMEs is just 26% of that in large enterprises; raising productivity to top-quartile advanced economy levels could add gains equivalent to 11% of India's GDP.
  • Practical applications. MSMEs are encouraged to start with digital accounting, inventory management, quality control, and customer engagement before expanding AI use.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How can technology adoption be supported?

  • Workforce and management. Investments in workforce skills, research and development, and professional management practices are essential to convert AI advances into better products and lower costs.
  • Access to finance. Easier credit access through the RBI’s Unified Lending Interface, which integrates GST returns, bank statements, land records, and utility data, can speed up lending to MSMEs.
  • Credit growth. Bank credit to MSMEs grew 27% year-on-year up to September 2026, higher than the 19.4% growth in overall bank credit.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What broader role do MSMEs play in India’s economic goals?

  • Scaling up. MSMEs should scale up through partnerships, industrial clusters, and shared facilities to enhance their impact.
  • Technology-driven growth. Stronger, technology-driven MSMEs are critical to India’s ambition of becoming a developed economy by 2047.

Back to basics: Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs)

MSMEs are businesses with limited scale in terms of investment and turnover, playing a crucial role in employment and economic development. They face challenges like lower productivity and limited access to finance compared to larger firms. Technology adoption, such as AI, can help MSMEs improve efficiency, competitiveness, and market reach.
