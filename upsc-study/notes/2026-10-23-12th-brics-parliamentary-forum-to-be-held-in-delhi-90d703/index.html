12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum to be Held in Delhi in October 2026

India will host the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum on 26-27 October 2026 in the Central Hall of Samvidhan Sadan, New Delhi. The forum will focus on parliamentary cooperation for people-centric development and global governance reforms, highlighting India's role in multilateral diplomacy.

The 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum in Delhi will deliberate on strengthening parliaments for people-centric development under India's chairship.

Why is this in the news?

The 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum is scheduled for 26-27 October 2026 in New Delhi, hosted by India under its chairship, with participation from BRICS member and partner countries.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum and when will it be held?

  • Event details. The 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum will be held on 26 and 27 October 2026 in the Central Hall of Samvidhan Sadan, New Delhi, hosted by the Parliament of India.
  • Participants. Presiding Officers, Members of Parliament, and dignitaries from 11 BRICS member countries and 10 partner countries will participate.
  • India's role. India is hosting the forum under its chairship, with Prime Minister Narendra Modi attending and engaging with visiting delegation leaders.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the main themes and agenda of the forum?

  • Central theme. The forum's overarching theme is 'Strengthening Parliaments for People-Centric Development'.
  • High-level sessions. Four sessions will focus on resilience of institutions, human-centred innovation in AI and digital technologies, parliamentary cooperation for global governance reform, and sustainability for an inclusive future.
  • Additional meetings. A special meeting of Chairpersons of International Affairs Committees from BRICS parliaments will discuss parliamentary diplomacy, capacity building, and best practice exchange.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum matter for India and global governance?

  • Parliamentary diplomacy. The forum marks a new chapter in India's parliamentary diplomacy by fostering bilateral and multilateral dialogue among BRICS and partner countries.
  • Global governance reforms. Deliberations will address reforms for a more representative global governance system through parliamentary cooperation.
  • Democratic strengthening. The event aims to strengthen democratic traditions, enhance accountability to people, and promote consensus-building through dialogue.
  • Technology and sustainability. Focus on ethical AI, digital transformation, and sustainable development reflects contemporary global challenges.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the key features of the forum's conduct and cultural aspects?

  • Bilateral and multilateral interactions. The forum will include bilateral and multilateral meetings with visiting delegations and parliamentary friendship groups.
  • Cultural programme. Alongside plenary sessions, a cultural programme will be held, and delegates will visit the Parliament of India and cultural sites in Delhi.
  • Banquet hosted. The Vice-President of India will host a banquet in honour of the distinguished guests.

Back to basics: BRICS and Parliamentary Diplomacy

BRICS is a multilateral grouping of emerging economies that collaborates on economic, political, and social issues. Parliamentary diplomacy involves legislatures engaging with counterparts internationally to promote cooperation, share best practices, and address global challenges through dialogue and consensus.
