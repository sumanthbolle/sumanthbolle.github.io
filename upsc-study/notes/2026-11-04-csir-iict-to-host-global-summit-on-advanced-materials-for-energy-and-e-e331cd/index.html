CSIR-IICT to Host Global Summit on Advanced Materials for Energy and Environment

CSIR-Indian Institute of Chemical Technology (CSIR-IICT) will organize the International Conference on Innovative Functional Materials for Energy and Environmental Technologies (iMEET-2026) from November 4 to 6. This summit highlights emerging research on advanced materials critical for energy, environment, and strategic applications, relevant for UPSC aspirants studying science and technology's role in sustainable development.

CSIR-IICT's iMEET-2026 conference focuses on cutting-edge materials for energy and environmental technologies, reflecting India's scientific advancements in sustainable solutions.

Why is this in the news?

CSIR-IICT announced hosting the iMEET-2026 international conference from November 4 to 6, 2026, focusing on advanced materials for energy and environmental technologies.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the iMEET-2026 conference about?

  • Conference focus. iMEET-2026 will cover advances in rare earth and critical materials, magnetic and multifunctional materials, advanced ceramics, nanomaterials, energy materials, optoelectronic materials, advanced coatings, surface engineering, and materials for strategic, environmental, and defence applications.
  • Host and venue. The conference is hosted by CSIR-IICT at its Tarnaka campus in Hyderabad from November 4 to 6, 2026.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Who are the key participants and speakers?

  • Distinguished speakers. The event features eminent experts including DRDO director-general (Naval Systems and Materials) R. Balamurali Krishnan, MIDHANI chairman and managing director S.V.S. Narayana Murty, and Sri Venkateswara University-Tirupati Vice-Chancellor T. Narasinga Rao.

Back to basics: Advanced Materials in Energy and Environment

Advanced materials are specially engineered substances with superior properties used to improve energy efficiency, environmental protection, and strategic applications. They include rare earth elements, nanomaterials, ceramics, and coatings that enable innovations in energy storage, conversion, and pollution control, which are critical for sustainable development.
