Static anchor

Drug-demand reduction is a public-policy approach that seeks to prevent substance use and reduce its harms through awareness, early identification, counselling, treatment and rehabilitation, rather than relying only on supply control.

India implications

  • A public-health framing can complement supply-control and internal-security measures by addressing demand and vulnerability.
  • Implementation requires coordination among the Union government, states, educational institutions, health services, communities and civil society.

Way forward

  • Link every awareness drive to confidential counselling, referral and accessible treatment pathways.
  • Use district-level dashboards with separate indicators for outreach, referrals, treatment continuity, completion, relapse prevention and rehabilitation.
  • Strengthen coordination among social-welfare, health, education, police, local-government and community institutions.
  • Adopt non-stigmatising communication and protect privacy so that affected persons seek help early.
