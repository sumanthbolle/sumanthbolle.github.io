Way forward

  • States should enact or amend Municipal Acts to clarify city-government authority, ensure timely elections and define accountable relationships among mayors, councils and state agencies.
  • Devolve functions together with funds, personnel, data and technical capacity; avoid transferring responsibilities without implementation capability.
  • Strengthen property-tax administration, user-charge recovery and regular State Finance Commissions while protecting affordability for vulnerable households.
  • Integrate land use, transport, housing, water and environmental planning through metropolitan and city-level coordination mechanisms.
