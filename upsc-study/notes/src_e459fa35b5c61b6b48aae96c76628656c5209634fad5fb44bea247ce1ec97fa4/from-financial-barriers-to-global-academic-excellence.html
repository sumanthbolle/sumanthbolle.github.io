India implications

  • Improves equality of opportunity by addressing the financial dimension of social exclusion in higher education.
  • Can contribute to human-capital formation and specialised expertise, with possible long-term benefits for infrastructure, construction and public development sectors.
  • Requires transparent selection, timely disbursal, grievance redressal and outcome monitoring so that affirmative assistance reaches deserving applicants.
  • Policy design should balance overseas opportunity with sustained investment in accessible, high-quality domestic higher education.

Way forward

  • Publish clear eligibility, selection and disbursal information in accessible formats.
  • Strengthen outreach and application support for eligible disadvantaged students, including those lacking institutional guidance.
  • Track disbursal timeliness, academic progression, completion and post-study outcomes while protecting personal data.
  • Provide grievance redressal and independent review of selection and payment delays.
