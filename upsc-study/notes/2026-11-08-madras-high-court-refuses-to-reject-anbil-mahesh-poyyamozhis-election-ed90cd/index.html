Madras High Court Allows Election Petition Against TVK MLA Vijayakumar

The Madras High Court refused to reject an election petition filed by Anbil Mahesh Poyyamozhi challenging the 2026 election victory of TVK MLA Vijayakumar. This case highlights the judiciary's role in electoral dispute resolution and enforcement of election laws.

The Madras High Court upheld the validity of an election petition alleging violations in the 2026 assembly election, allowing judicial scrutiny of electoral conduct.

Why is this in the news?

On November 7, 2026, the Madras High Court dismissed an application to reject an election petition filed by Anbil Mahesh Poyyamozhi against TVK MLA Vijayakumar's 2026 election win.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What did the Madras High Court decide regarding the election petition?

  • Petition not rejected. The Madras High Court refused to reject the election petition filed by Anbil Mahesh Poyyamozhi challenging Vijayakumar's election victory.
  • Application dismissed. Justice K. Kumaresh Babu dismissed Vijayakumar's application seeking rejection of the petition.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What allegations are involved in the election petition?

  • Children in campaign. The petition alleges that Vijayakumar engaged children in his election campaign, which the Election Commission of India prohibits.
  • Statutory force of ECI instructions. The court noted that the Election Commission's instructions against involving children in campaigns have statutory force and must be tested in trial.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the election results and margin involved?

  • Vote counts. Vijayakumar won with 89,837 votes, while Poyyamozhi secured 81,132 votes.
  • Victory margin. The winning margin was 8,705 votes in the Thiruverumbur Assembly constituency.

Back to basics: Election Petitions and Judicial Review

An election petition is a legal challenge to the validity of an election result, filed in a competent court. The judiciary examines allegations such as electoral malpractice or violation of election laws. Courts ensure that elections are conducted fairly and can annul results if violations are proven. The Election Commission of India issues instructions to regulate conduct during elections, which have statutory force and can be enforced through judicial scrutiny.
