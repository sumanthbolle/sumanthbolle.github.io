Drought Conditions in Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh Due to Southwest Monsoon Deficit

Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh are experiencing substantial drought following a significant deficit in the southwest monsoon rainfall. This situation highlights challenges in agricultural distress, water management, and disaster relief relevant for UPSC preparation on climate and disaster management.

A severe southwest monsoon rainfall deficit has caused drought in Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh, affecting agriculture and water resources.

Why is this in the news?

The drought in Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh was reported on October 13, 2026, following a significant southwest monsoon rainfall deficit.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What caused the drought in Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh?

  • Monsoon rainfall deficit. Karnataka suffered a close to 35% rainfall deficit during June-September, while Andhra Pradesh experienced a 53% shortfall in June-August, leading to drought conditions.
  • Crop damage. The drought has caused extensive damage to crops such as red gram, maize, cotton, sugarcane in Karnataka, and put horticulture crops over nearly 18,000 hectares in Andhra Pradesh at risk.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How are droughts officially declared and managed?

  • National drought framework. Drought declarations combine agricultural and hydrological indicators and ground-truthing studies before states can access additional support from the National Disaster Response Fund (NDRF).
  • State declarations. Karnataka declared 217 of 240 taluks drought-hit, and Andhra Pradesh declared 388 of 688 mandals drought-hit.
  • Funding requests. Karnataka has petitioned the Centre for ₹11,000 crore from the NDRF along with higher input subsidies, while Andhra Pradesh has allocated ₹190 crore to protect horticultural crops and conserve drinking water.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this drought matter for UPSC aspirants?

  • Climate change impact. The drought exemplifies the effects of a deficient southwest monsoon and the potential influence of a strengthening El Niño, relevant to climate change and its impact on India.
  • Disaster management. Understanding the drought declaration process, relief mechanisms like the NDRF, and water conservation measures is important for disaster management topics.
  • Agricultural distress. The drought highlights challenges in agriculture, including crop damage, farmer indebtedness, and the need for sustainable water use and crop choices.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What measures are suggested to mitigate drought effects?

  • Water conservation and crop choices. States have advised adopting micro-irrigation and cultivating less water-intensive crops but have not imposed binding limits on water use.
  • Centre's role. The Centre should release NDRF support promptly after verification by the Inter-Ministerial Central Team and consider incentives for drought resilience and broadening agricultural insurance.
  • Sustainable groundwater use. Sustainable use of groundwater and income support based on local rainfall and aquifer availability are essential to reduce drought vulnerability.

Back to basics: Southwest Monsoon and Drought

The southwest monsoon is the primary rainy season in India, crucial for agriculture and water resources. A deficit in monsoon rainfall can lead to drought, characterized by insufficient water for crops and drinking needs. Drought is declared based on agricultural and hydrological data, triggering relief measures and support from the government.

What happens next?

  • Timely release of funds. The Centre must release NDRF assistance promptly after the Inter-Ministerial Central Team's findings are publicly verified to avoid delays in relief.
  • Promote water-use discipline. States need to enforce stricter water conservation measures and rationalize consumption to reduce drought impact.
  • Incentivize drought resilience. The Centre should consider incentives for drought resistance, including broadening agricultural insurance and discouraging imprudent projects like river interlinking that overlook sustainable water use.
