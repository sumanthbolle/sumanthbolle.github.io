India-Nepal Inaugurate Ayodhya-Janakpur Rail Link with ₹15,000 Crore Development Package

India and Nepal launched the Sarayu-Kamala Express rail service connecting Ayodhya and Janakpur, enhancing religious tourism and cultural ties. India also announced a ₹15,000 crore development assistance package to support Nepal's post-disaster reconstruction and infrastructure projects.

The Ayodhya-Janakpur rail link and India's ₹15,000 crore development package mark a new phase in India-Nepal connectivity and cooperation.

Why is this in the news?

The rail link was inaugurated and the development package announced during Nepal Prime Minister Balendra Shah's state visit to India on 22-23 November 2026.

What are the key features of the Ayodhya-Janakpur rail link?

  • Rail service details. The Sarayu-Kamala Express connects Ayodhya in India to Janakpur in Nepal, covering 604 kilometres in about 19 hours without requiring passengers to change trains.
  • Train configuration. The train uses modern 17-coach LHB rakes including eight air-conditioned coaches, seven sleeper-class coaches, and coaches for luggage and disabled-friendly facilities.
  • Frequency and route. It operates once a week, departing Ayodhya every Monday evening and returning from Janakpur every Saturday afternoon, passing through key stations like Gorakhpur, Raxaul, and Jaynagar.
  • Cultural significance. Ayodhya and Janakpur are important religious sites linked to Lord Ram and Goddess Sita respectively, and the rail link supports the Ramayana Circuit and religious tourism.

What does India's ₹15,000 crore development package for Nepal include?

  • Package components. India announced development assistance of ₹15,000 crore comprising ₹3,500 crore in grant assistance for post-disaster reconstruction and infrastructure, and ₹11,500 crore as a Rupee-denominated Line of Credit for energy sector projects.
  • Project focus. The assistance targets reconstruction after the August 2026 floods, new infrastructure projects, hydropower development including the Lower Arun Hydro-Electric Project, and capacity building.
  • Additional cooperation. India will support Nepal in sectors like education, agriculture, health, digital connectivity, petroleum supply infrastructure, and sports development.

How does this cooperation enhance India-Nepal relations?

  • Strengthening cultural ties. The rail link and joint initiatives reinforce centuries-old cultural, religious, and people-to-people connections symbolized by shared reverence for Lord Ram and Goddess Sita.
  • Improving connectivity. New railway lines, motorable bridges, Integrated Check Posts, and petroleum pipelines improve trade, transit, and mobility between the two countries.
  • Joint Vision Document. The leaders adopted a Joint Vision for 'Viksit Bharat and Samunnat Nepal' outlining cooperation in economic partnership, connectivity, energy, climate resilience, technology, education, and tourism.
  • Regional development. Enhanced connectivity and infrastructure projects aim to boost Nepal’s economic growth, disaster resilience, and integration with India’s markets and supply chains.

What are the future connectivity and infrastructure plans?

  • Railway projects. Survey for the Raxaul-Kathmandu electrified broad-gauge railway line is finalized, with plans to develop the East-West Railway in Nepal and complete Jayanagar-Bardibas and Jogbani-Biratnagar lines.
  • Border infrastructure. New Integrated Check Posts at Banbasa and Dodhara Chandani are being developed, alongside operational ICPs at Bhairahawa, Sunauli, and others.
  • Petroleum pipelines. Phase II of the Motihari-Amlekhgunj Pipeline was inaugurated, and a petroleum terminal at Jhapa is planned, with future gas pipeline cooperation under discussion.
  • Bridges and roads. A new motorable bridge between Dharchula (India) and Darchula (Nepal) was inaugurated to improve direct connectivity in western Nepal.

Back to basics: Bilateral connectivity and development cooperation

Bilateral connectivity projects like rail links and border infrastructure facilitate trade, tourism, and cultural exchange between neighbouring countries. Development cooperation involves financial assistance, technical support, and joint initiatives to promote economic growth and disaster resilience. Such partnerships strengthen diplomatic ties and regional integration.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the India-Nepal Ayodhya-Janakpur rail link and development package: 1. The Sarayu-Kamala Express covers 604 kilometres and operates once a week. 2. India's development assistance to Nepal includes a ₹15,000 crore package with a grant component and a Line of Credit for energy projects. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (c). Statement 1 is correct about the rail link; Statement 2 is correct about the development package. A trap would be confusing the total package amount or the frequency of the train.
