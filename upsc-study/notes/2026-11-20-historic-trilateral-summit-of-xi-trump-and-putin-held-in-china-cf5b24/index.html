Historic Trilateral Summit of Xi, Trump, and Putin Held in China

On November 19, 2026, the leaders of the US, China, and Russia met in Shenzhen on the sidelines of the 33rd APEC Economic Leaders’ Meeting for a historic trilateral summit. This was the first formal three-way meeting of the heads of the world's three most influential military powers amid significant global fractures, marking a key event in global geopolitics.

The trilateral summit of Xi Jinping, Donald Trump, and Vladimir Putin in China was the first formal meeting of the three major military powers, reflecting shifting global power dynamics.

Back to basics: Trilateral Summit

A trilateral summit is a formal meeting involving the leaders of three countries to discuss issues of mutual interest, often related to diplomacy, security, and economic cooperation. Such summits are significant as they can shape international relations and influence global power structures.
