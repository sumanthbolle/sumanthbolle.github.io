India’s Nuclear Programme Focuses on Peaceful Energy for AI Future

Union Minister Dr. Jitendra Singh reaffirmed India’s nuclear programme is dedicated to peaceful purposes and highlighted nuclear energy’s role in powering AI-driven growth. The country targets 100 GW of nuclear power capacity by 2047 and Net Zero emissions by 2070, linking nuclear energy to clean power and technological advancement.

India’s nuclear programme prioritizes peaceful applications and clean energy to support future AI and data centre demands.

Why is this in the news?

On October 20, 2026, Union Minister Dr. Jitendra Singh spoke at the Bloomberg Economy Forum about India’s nuclear programme’s peaceful focus and its role in meeting future energy needs for AI and data centres.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the focus of India’s nuclear programme?

  • Peaceful purposes. India’s nuclear programme is dedicated to peaceful applications and national development, following the vision of Dr. Homi Bhabha.
  • Clean energy commitment. The programme aims to provide reliable, continuous clean electricity to support India’s energy security and technological growth.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does nuclear energy fit into India’s future energy goals?

  • Capacity target. India targets 100 GW of nuclear power capacity by 2047 to complement other power sources.
  • Net Zero emissions. Nuclear energy is a key part of India’s strategy to achieve Net Zero emissions by 2070.
  • Support for AI and data centres. Nuclear power will meet the substantial and continuous electricity demands of AI technologies and data centres.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the role of Artificial Intelligence in this context?

  • AI as a cross-sector tool. AI is seen as a tool applicable across sectors, including space and life sciences, requiring dependable power.
  • Human-led AI use. Responsible application of AI depends on human intelligence and integrity to prevent misuse.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How is private sector participation evolving in nuclear and critical sectors?

  • Wider private participation. The Modi Government has opened critical and sensitive sectors to non-government players, expanding opportunities for private industry.
  • Technological advancement. This approach encourages innovation and national development through private sector involvement.

Back to basics: Nuclear Energy for Peaceful Purposes

Nuclear energy involves harnessing atomic reactions to generate electricity without emitting greenhouse gases. Many countries, including India, have committed to using nuclear technology only for peaceful purposes such as power generation and scientific research, avoiding military applications. This aligns with global non-proliferation norms and supports sustainable development goals by providing a reliable source of clean energy.
