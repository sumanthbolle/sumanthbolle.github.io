Cyclone Akvan Causes Heavy Rain and Disruptions in Odisha, Andhra Pradesh, and Jharkhand

Cyclone Akvan made landfall near Gopalpur in Odisha on November 20, 2026, causing heavy rainfall and disruptions in Odisha, Andhra Pradesh, and Jharkhand. The cyclone led to school closures and weather alerts, highlighting challenges in disaster management and climate resilience.

Cyclone Akvan's landfall caused heavy rains and precautionary measures in eastern Indian states, emphasizing the need for effective disaster preparedness.

What happened during Cyclone Akvan's landfall?

  • Landfall location and timing. Cyclone Akvan commenced landfall near Gopalpur in Odisha around 8 a.m. on November 20, 2026.
  • Rainfall and weather alerts. Parts of Jharkhand experienced light rain initially, with an 'orange' alert issued for heavy rainfall in five Jharkhand districts and a 'yellow' alert for seven others.
  • Precautionary measures. East Singhbhum and Saraikela-Kharswan district administrations suspended primary classes on November 20 as a precaution.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does Cyclone Akvan matter for disaster management?

  • Advance weather warnings. The India Meteorological Department issued timely alerts to prepare districts for heavy rainfall and associated risks.
  • School closures as safety measures. Suspension of primary classes in vulnerable districts reflects proactive steps to protect children during extreme weather.
  • Forecast of worsening conditions. Rainfall was expected to intensify with thunderstorms, lightning, and gusty winds up to 50 kmph, requiring readiness for escalating impact.

What are the expected developments post-cyclone?

  • Improvement timeline. The situation was expected to improve from November 22, with clear skies likely from November 23.

Back to basics: Cyclones and Disaster Management

Cyclones are intense circular storms originating over warm ocean waters, causing heavy rain, strong winds, and flooding. Disaster management involves forecasting such events, issuing warnings, and implementing safety measures like evacuations and school closures to minimize damage and protect lives.
