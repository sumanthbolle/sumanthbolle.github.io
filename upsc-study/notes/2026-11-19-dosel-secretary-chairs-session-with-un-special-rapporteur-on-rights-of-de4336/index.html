DoSEL Secretary Chairs Session with UN Special Rapporteur on Disability Rights

The Secretary of the Department of School Education & Literacy (DoSEL) chaired a session with the UN Special Rapporteur on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities on November 17, 2026. This interaction highlighted India's efforts to implement the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (CRPD) and promote inclusive education for children with special needs.

India is advancing inclusive education through initiatives under Samagra Shiksha and the PRASHAST disability screening tool, as discussed with the UN Special Rapporteur on Disability Rights.

Why is this in the news?

On November 17, 2026, the DoSEL Secretary chaired an interactive session with the UN Special Rapporteur on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities to discuss India's inclusive education initiatives.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What was discussed in the session chaired by DoSEL Secretary?

  • Session overview. The session provided a platform to share India's government initiatives to strengthen inclusive education and ensure equitable access for children with special needs (CwSN).
  • Implementation of CRPD. The Department highlighted key interventions under Samagra Shiksha aimed at inclusive education through a life-cycle approach covering early identification, individualized support, accessibility, therapeutic interventions, skilling, and exam-related facilities by CBSE.
  • PRASHAST tool. PRASHAST, a disability screening checklist for early identification of 21 disabilities among children aged 3 to 18, was discussed, including its revamped 2.0 mobile app integrated with UDISE+ that reduced screening time from three days to 15 minutes.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What are the key achievements in inclusive education highlighted?

  • Screening coverage. As of October 2026, over 6 crore children have been screened using PRASHAST, and more than 7.26 lakh schools have been onboarded on the PRASHAST portal.
  • Teacher sensitisation. Over 79 lakh teachers have been sensitised to support children with special needs.
  • Field visit. The UN Special Rapporteur visited a Government Co-ed Senior Secondary School in R. K. Puram to review resource rooms and support mechanisms for CwSN students and interacted with their parents.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Back to basics: Inclusive Education and CRPD

Inclusive education means providing equal learning opportunities to all children, including those with disabilities, by adapting teaching methods and infrastructure. The Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (CRPD) is an international treaty that obliges countries to ensure rights and accessibility for persons with disabilities, including in education. Governments implement policies and schemes to meet these obligations and promote social inclusion.
