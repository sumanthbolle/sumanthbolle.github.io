Green Crackers and Pollution Control Challenges in India

The Supreme Court allowed limited use of green crackers to reduce pollution during Deepavali, but their real-world impact on air and noise pollution remains limited. UPSC aspirants should understand the regulatory, environmental, and enforcement challenges surrounding green crackers as part of pollution control policies.

Green crackers reduce particulate emissions in theory but have limited effectiveness in practice due to enforcement and usage issues.

Why is this in the news?

The Hindu editorial on October 14, 2026, highlighted the limited pollution control benefits of green crackers and the challenges in enforcing regulations during Deepavali in Delhi and NCR.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are green crackers and how are they regulated?

  • Supreme Court orders. Since 2016, the Supreme Court has regulated fireworks in Delhi NCR, suspending licenses after severe pollution spikes and later permitting only reduced-emission green crackers with restrictions such as a two-hour bursting window.
  • Green crackers' emission claims. CSIR-NEERI promoted green crackers claiming a 45%-60% reduction in PM10 emissions compared to conventional crackers, but these figures do not reflect real-world conditions.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why do green crackers have limited impact on pollution?

  • Real-world pollution levels. In 2025, post-Deepavali PM2.5 levels in Delhi averaged 488 g/m³ with hourly peaks at 675 g/m³, showing high pollution despite green cracker use.
  • Increased usage offsets benefits. When more green crackers are burst, the expected reductions in particulate pollution disappear, and noise pollution remains unaddressed.
  • Enforcement challenges. QR codes for green crackers were sold to unregistered manufacturers, and police had difficulty distinguishing green crackers from conventional ones, undermining regulation.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the broader pollution sources and implications?

  • Multiple pollution contributors. Fireworks are not the main cause of Delhi’s air pollution; construction dust, vehicles, stubble burning, and weather conditions also contribute significantly.
  • Acute pollution spikes. Firecrackers cause sharp spikes in pollution during severe winter conditions, worsening air quality temporarily.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What needs to be done to improve pollution control?

  • Formalising manufacturing. Moving firecracker production into the formal sector could improve compliance and enforcement of pollution norms.
  • Public awareness. Increasing awareness about health impacts and pollution from fireworks is essential to reduce their use and associated pollution.

Back to basics: Air Pollution and Regulatory Measures

Air pollution consists of harmful substances like particulate matter (PM2.5 and PM10) that affect health. Regulatory bodies set limits and rules to control pollution sources. Enforcement and public compliance are critical for these measures to succeed. Pollution spikes can be caused by multiple sources, and controlling one source alone may not solve the problem.

What happens next?

  • Strengthen enforcement. Improving testing facilities and cracking down on unregistered manufacturers can help ensure only certified green crackers are sold.
  • Promote formal sector. Encouraging formalisation of firecracker manufacturing can improve regulation and reduce pollution.
  • Raise public awareness. Educating citizens about the health impacts of fireworks pollution can reduce demand and usage.
