Coal Supplies to Power Plants Rise 36% from September Lows

Coal supplies to India's power sector increased by 36% from September lows, with rail loading reaching 465 rakes per day in October. This improvement signals better fuel availability for power generation, which is crucial for energy security and infrastructure planning.

Coal supplies to power plants rose 36% from September lows, supported by enhanced rail loading of 465 rakes daily, stabilizing fuel availability for power generation.

Why is this in the news?

On October 17, 2026, the government reported a 36% rise in coal supplies to power plants from September lows, with rail loading averaging 465 rakes daily in October.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What caused the rise in coal supplies to power plants?

  • Increased coal lifting. Coal India and its subsidiaries allowed power plants to lift additional coal beyond their contracted quantities through road transport, supplementing rail supplies.
  • Improved rail loading. Rail-based coal supplies strengthened to an average of 465 rakes dispatched daily in October, supporting higher coal availability.
  • Moderating power demand. The Coal Ministry expects power demand to moderate by mid-October based on historical trends, easing pressure on coal stocks.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this rise in coal supply matter for the power sector?

  • Fuel availability stabilisation. The rise in coal supplies helps stabilize fuel availability for power generation, reducing the risk of power plants running critically low on coal.
  • Stock depletion slowing. Coal stock depletion slowed from 0.243 million tonnes per day in September to 0.116 million tonnes per day in October, indicating improving inventory levels.
  • Early-warning mechanism. Plants classified as having 'critical stock' are flagged for early warning, not immediate shutdown risk, helping manage supply proactively.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does rail loading impact coal supply and power generation?

  • Rail loading as supply backbone. Rail transport is a major mode for coal delivery to power plants, and increasing daily rake dispatches directly improves coal availability.
  • Target rail loading. The Coal Ministry expects daily rail loading of 465–475 rakes to be sufficient to meet power demand during the stabilisation period.

Back to basics: Coal Supply Chain for Power Generation

Coal is a primary fuel for thermal power plants in India, transported mainly by rail and road. The supply chain involves mining, transportation, and stock management at power plants. Rail loading refers to the number of coal rakes dispatched daily, which is a key indicator of coal supply adequacy. Maintaining adequate coal stocks at power plants is essential to ensure uninterrupted power generation and avoid outages.
