CJI Surya Kant on Constitution Guiding Judiciary When Law is Silent

Chief Justice of India Surya Kant emphasized that when statutes do not provide answers, the judiciary must rely on the Constitution’s promises of dignity, liberty, and equality. This principle is crucial for UPSC aspirants to understand the judiciary’s role in constitutional interpretation and adapting law to new challenges like technology.

Where the law is silent, the Constitution must guide judicial decisions to uphold fundamental rights and values.

Why is this in the news?

On October 30, 2026, CJI Surya Kant spoke at the Delhi High Court’s 60th anniversary event about the judiciary’s reliance on the Constitution where statutory law is silent.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What did CJI Surya Kant say about the judiciary’s role where law is silent?

  • Constitution as guide. CJI Surya Kant stated that when statutes do not provide answers, the judiciary must be guided by the Constitution’s promises of dignity, liberty, and equality, which remain constant even as technology and society evolve.
  • New challenges. He highlighted emerging legal questions such as copyright for AI-generated works and regulation of deepfakes, which lack ready answers in existing laws.
  • Judicial openness. The judiciary must meet unknown challenges with an open mind while protecting individual rights, reflecting the Constitution’s enduring values.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for UPSC aspirants?

  • Understanding constitutional role. The statement clarifies the judiciary’s constitutional role in interpreting laws and filling gaps where statutes are silent, a key concept in Indian polity.
  • Separation of powers. It illustrates the balance between legislative silence and judicial interpretation, showing how courts uphold constitutional morality without overstepping legislative functions.
  • Contemporary relevance. The emphasis on technology-related legal questions shows how constitutional principles adapt to new societal challenges, relevant for governance and law topics.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the significance of the Delhi High Court in this context?

  • Strategic role. CJI Surya Kant noted the Delhi High Court’s extraordinary responsibility as it serves the nation’s political and administrative capital, handling complex and varied disputes.
  • Judicial burden. The Delhi High Court reduces the Supreme Court’s caseload by resolving many matters at the original civil jurisdiction level.
  • Innovation in judiciary. The court has pioneered India’s first high court annexed arbitration centre and embraced digital processes, enhancing judicial efficiency.

Back to basics: Judicial Interpretation and Constitutional Supremacy

The judiciary interprets laws passed by the legislature but also relies on the Constitution when statutes are silent or ambiguous. The Constitution is the supreme law that guarantees fundamental rights and principles such as dignity, liberty, and equality. Courts balance respect for legislative intent with protecting constitutional values, especially in new or unforeseen situations.

Answer and trap

Answer: (c). A trap could be confusing the Delhi High Court’s role with other High Courts; the CJI specifically noted its original civil jurisdiction and burden reduction for the Supreme Court.
