NLC India Limited Signs MoU for 3200 MW Ultra Super-Critical Coal Power Plant in Odisha

NLC India Limited has signed a memorandum of understanding with Odisha's IPICOL to establish a 3200 MW ultra super-critical coal-based thermal power plant. This project is significant for UPSC aspirants as it highlights India's ongoing energy infrastructure development, investment scale, and the balance between industrial growth and environmental concerns.

NLC India Limited and Odisha will collaborate to set up a 3200 MW ultra super-critical coal power plant, investing ₹35,000 crore and generating around 4,000 jobs.

Why is this in the news?

On October 23, 2026, NLC India Limited signed an MoU with Odisha's IPICOL for a 3200 MW ultra super-critical coal power plant, marking a major energy infrastructure development.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the MoU between NLC India Limited and Odisha about?

  • Project details. The MoU is for establishing a 3200 MW (4x800 MW) ultra super-critical coal-based thermal power plant in Odisha.
  • Investment and employment. The project involves a potential investment of ₹35,000 crore and is expected to generate employment for approximately 4,000 persons.
  • Additional initiatives. NLCIL also signed MoUs for a coal gasification project and a beach sand mineral processing facility in Odisha, with investments of ₹2,000 crore and employment for 500 persons.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this MoU matter for UPSC aspirants?

  • Energy infrastructure development. The project reflects India's focus on expanding power infrastructure using ultra super-critical technology, which is more efficient and environmentally better than older coal plants.
  • Economic impact. With a ₹35,000 crore investment and thousands of jobs, the project contributes to Odisha's economic growth and industrial development.
  • Environmental and policy relevance. The use of ultra super-critical technology and coal gasification links to debates on balancing energy needs with environmental sustainability.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the technical and industrial aspects involved?

  • Ultra super-critical technology. This technology operates at higher temperatures and pressures, improving efficiency and reducing emissions compared to conventional coal plants.
  • Coal gasification and fertilizer linkage. NLCIL will explore coal gasification to produce synthesis gas for potential use in fertilizer production, integrating coal and fertilizer sectors.
  • Mineral processing facility. The beach sand mineral processing project aims to develop mineral-based industries in Odisha, supporting industrial diversification.

Back to basics: Ultra Super-Critical Coal Power Technology

Ultra super-critical coal power plants operate at higher temperature and pressure than conventional plants, resulting in higher efficiency and lower emissions. This technology helps reduce coal consumption per unit of electricity generated, contributing to better environmental performance compared to older coal power plants. It is a key part of India's strategy to balance energy demand with environmental concerns.
