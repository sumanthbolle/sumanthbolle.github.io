Background

  • The Ministry of Tourism organises the International Tourism Mart as an annual flagship event for promoting the NER in domestic and international markets.
  • The event mechanism is a convergence of buyers, sellers, tourism businesses, entrepreneurs, government agencies and media, supported by destination presentations, B2B sessions and technical discussions.
  • The Mart’s NER focus covers Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Sikkim and Tripura.
  • The Ministry of Tourism has organised International Tourism Mart events in the NER since 2013.[1]
