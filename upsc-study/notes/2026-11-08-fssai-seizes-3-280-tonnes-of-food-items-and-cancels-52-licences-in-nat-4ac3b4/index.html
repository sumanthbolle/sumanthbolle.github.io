FSSAI Seizes 3,280 Tonnes of Food Items, Cancels 52 Licences in Nationwide Drive

The Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) conducted a nationwide inspection from September 1 to November 5, 2026, seizing 3,280 tonnes of banned and non-compliant food products worth ₹82 crore and taking strict action against violators. This enforcement drive highlights the regulatory framework ensuring food safety and consumer protection, relevant for both Prelims and Mains exams.

FSSAI's nationwide inspection seized 3,280 tonnes of unsafe food and cancelled 52 licences to enforce food safety standards during the festive season.

Why is this in the news?

FSSAI conducted a nationwide enforcement drive between September 1 and November 5, 2026, inspecting over 56,000 food establishments to check adulteration during the festive season.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What actions did FSSAI take during the inspection drive?

  • Scope of inspection. FSSAI inspected 56,431 food establishments across States and Union Territories during the drive from September 1 to November 5, 2026.
  • Seizure of food items. It seized 3,279.57 tonnes of banned and non-compliant food products, including paneer, ghee, milk, and sweets, worth ₹81.80 crore.
  • Licences cancelled and suspended. The regulator cancelled 52 licences, suspended 1,133 licences, and sealed 314 establishments for food safety violations.
  • Improvement notices issued. FSSAI issued 10,047 improvement notices to food business operators to ensure compliance with food safety standards.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this inspection drive matter for food safety and governance?

  • Ensuring consumer protection. The drive targets adulteration and substandard food products, protecting consumers from health hazards during the festive season.
  • Regulatory enforcement. It demonstrates FSSAI's role as a statutory regulatory body enforcing food safety laws and standards across India.
  • Public health governance. Such nationwide inspections help maintain public confidence in food quality and safety, which is crucial for health governance.

Back to basics: Food Safety Regulation

Food safety regulation involves setting standards and enforcing laws to ensure that food products are safe for consumption. Regulatory bodies inspect food establishments, seize unsafe products, and take action against violations to protect public health. Such regulation is essential to prevent food adulteration and contamination, especially during periods of high food consumption like festivals.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the FSSAI nationwide inspection drive in 2026: 1. FSSAI inspected over 56,000 food establishments and seized around 3,280 tonnes of banned food products. 2. The regulator cancelled over 1,000 licences during the drive.

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because FSSAI cancelled 52 licences and suspended 1,133 licences, not cancelled over 1,000.
