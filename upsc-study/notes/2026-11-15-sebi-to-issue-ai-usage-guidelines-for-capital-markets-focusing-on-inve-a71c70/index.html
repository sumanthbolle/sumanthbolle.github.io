SEBI to Issue AI Guidelines for Capital Markets with Investor Protection Focus

SEBI plans to release detailed guidelines for responsible AI use in capital markets, emphasizing investor protection and accountability. This development is important for understanding regulatory approaches to emerging technologies in financial governance.

SEBI will regulate AI in capital markets by setting guardrails to protect investors and maintain market integrity.

Why is this in the news?

On November 15, 2026, SEBI Chairman Tuhin Kanta Pandey announced upcoming AI usage guidelines for capital markets focusing on investor protection and accountability.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are SEBI's planned AI guidelines for capital markets?

  • Guideline focus. SEBI will issue guidelines to ensure responsible AI use in capital markets, emphasizing disclosures, accountability of regulated entities, and a 'human-in-the-loop' approach for critical decisions.
  • Objective. The guidelines aim to balance technological innovation with investor protection, market integrity, and financial stability.
  • Consultation process. In June 2025, SEBI floated a consultation paper proposing guiding principles for AI and machine learning applications in securities markets.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does SEBI's AI regulation matter for investor protection?

  • Guardrails for AI. The guidelines will establish guardrails such as mandatory disclosures and human oversight to prevent risks from automated decisions.
  • Cybersecurity and fraud concerns. SEBI is strengthening cybersecurity frameworks to address rising cyber threats, digital frauds, and risks from emerging technologies like quantum computing.
  • Maintaining market integrity. Regulation ensures AI adoption does not compromise investor safety or market fairness.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How is SEBI preparing for technological risks beyond AI?

  • Quantum computing risks. SEBI is inventorying cryptographic systems to assess vulnerabilities from quantum computing, with global concerns expected around 2032-35.
  • Supervisory tools. SEBI has developed tools like 'Sudarshan' to monitor markets and is enhancing AI capabilities through international cooperation.

Back to basics: Regulation of Artificial Intelligence in Financial Markets

Artificial Intelligence (AI) in financial markets involves using algorithms and machine learning to automate decisions and analyze data. Regulators set guidelines to ensure AI use does not harm investors or market integrity by requiring transparency, accountability, and human oversight. These rules help balance innovation with protection against risks like fraud, errors, or systemic instability.
