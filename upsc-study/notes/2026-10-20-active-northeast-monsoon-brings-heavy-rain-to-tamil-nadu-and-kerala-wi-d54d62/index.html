Active Northeast Monsoon Causes Heavy Rainfall in Tamil Nadu and Kerala

The Northeast monsoon intensified early in its season, causing heavy rainfall and flooding in Chennai and nearby areas. This event is relevant for UPSC aspirants studying monsoon patterns, climatology, and disaster management.

The active Northeast monsoon brought heavy rainfall to Tamil Nadu, leading to urban flooding and government response efforts.

Why is this in the news?

On October 18-19, 2026, Chennai experienced extremely heavy rainfall due to an active Northeast monsoon, leading to flooding and government intervention.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What caused the heavy rainfall in Tamil Nadu?

  • Northeast monsoon activity. The Northeast monsoon intensified in its first week, causing widespread convective activity and cyclonic circulation that led to heavy rainfall in Chennai and surrounding areas.
  • No cloudburst event. The India Meteorological Department clarified that the rainfall was not a cloudburst, which requires 100 mm or more in one hour in a small area, but rather organised rainfall from cyclonic circulation.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What were the impacts of the heavy rainfall?

  • Flooding in Chennai. Several localities and arterial roads in Chennai were flooded due to more than 20 cm of rain in many areas, with Parrys recording 36 cm and Kathivakkam 34.4 cm.
  • Government response. Government machinery worked overnight to clear stagnant water, and the Chief Minister reviewed preparedness at the Integrated Command and Control Centre, urging coordinated efforts.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for UPSC preparation?

  • Monsoon patterns. Understanding the Northeast monsoon's behaviour and its impact on southern India is crucial for physical geography and climatology topics.
  • Disaster management. The event highlights the importance of government preparedness and response to urban flooding caused by monsoon rains, relevant for disaster management studies.

Back to basics: Northeast Monsoon

The Northeast monsoon is a seasonal wind pattern that brings rainfall primarily to the southeastern coast of India, including Tamil Nadu and Kerala, typically from October to December. It is caused by the reversal of wind direction after the Southwest monsoon and is influenced by weather systems over the Bay of Bengal. This monsoon is critical for agriculture and water resources in these regions.
