RBI Announces Redemption Prices for Premature Redemption of Sovereign Gold Bonds

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) announced the redemption prices for premature redemption of Sovereign Gold Bonds (SGB) 2019-20 Series VI and 2021-22 Series VII, effective October-November 2026. This update is important for UPSC aspirants as it highlights government securities' mechanisms and investor protections under the Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme.

RBI sets redemption prices for premature redemption of Sovereign Gold Bonds based on the average gold price of preceding business days, effective after five years from issue.

Why is this in the news?

On October 29 and 30, 2026, RBI announced the redemption prices for premature redemption of Sovereign Gold Bonds issued in 2019-20 and 2021-22, respectively.

What are the conditions for premature redemption of Sovereign Gold Bonds?

  • Premature redemption timing. Premature redemption of Sovereign Gold Bonds is permitted only after the fifth year from the date of issue, on the date when interest is payable.
  • Applicable series. The RBI announced premature redemption prices for SGB 2019-20 Series VI and SGB 2021-22 Series VII, with redemption dates on October 30, 2026, and November 2, 2026, respectively.

[1] Reserve Bank of India[2] Reserve Bank of India

How is the redemption price for premature redemption calculated?

  • Basis of price calculation. The redemption price is based on the simple average of the closing price of gold of 999 purity for the three business days preceding the redemption date, as published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Ltd (IBJA).
  • Redemption prices announced. For SGB 2019-20 Series VI, the redemption price is ₹14,213 per unit, and for SGB 2021-22 Series VII, it is ₹14,167 per unit.

Back to basics: Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme

The Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme allows investors to buy gold in the form of government securities instead of physical gold. It offers interest payments and redemption linked to gold prices, reducing risks related to storage and purity. Premature redemption is allowed only after a specified period, ensuring investment stability.
