RBI Clarifies Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve via FAQs

The Reserve Bank of India issued FAQs on October 15, 2026, to clarify the Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve (FERR) requirements introduced earlier that month. This update is important for understanding RBI's approach to forex risk management, relevant for both Prelims and Mains.

RBI issued FAQs to explain the Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve requirements introduced in October 2026.

Why is this in the news?

On October 15, 2026, RBI released FAQs to clarify the recently introduced Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve requirements.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

What is the Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve (FERR)?

  • Definition. FERR refers to the reserve requirements related to managing foreign exchange risk, introduced by RBI through circulars in October 2026.
  • Purpose of FAQs. The FAQs issued by RBI provide clarifications on the FERR requirements to help stakeholders understand the policy details.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

Why did RBI issue FAQs on FERR?

  • Clarification need. The FAQs were issued to address queries and provide detailed explanations following the introduction of FERR requirements in RBI circulars dated October 10 and 12, 2026.
  • Policy transparency. Issuing FAQs enhances transparency and aids smooth implementation of the new forex risk management framework.

Back to basics: Foreign Exchange Risk Management

Foreign exchange risk management involves strategies and regulatory measures to protect against losses from fluctuations in currency exchange rates. Central banks like RBI set rules and reserves to ensure financial stability and reduce volatility in forex markets.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about RBI's Foreign Exchange Risk Reserve (FERR): 1. RBI introduced FERR requirements through circulars in October 2026. 2. RBI issued FAQs on FERR to clarify the requirements shortly after the circulars.

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (c). The trap is confusing the dates of circulars and FAQs; circulars came first on October 10 and 12, followed by FAQs on October 15.
