Cabinet approves 3% hike in Dearness Allowance for Central Government employees and pensioners

The Union Cabinet approved a 3% increase in Dearness Allowance (DA) and Dearness Relief (DR) for central government employees and pensioners effective from July 1, 2026. This decision impacts over 1.17 crore beneficiaries and has a fiscal implication of ₹10,016.10 crore annually, reflecting government welfare and fiscal policy management.

The Cabinet approved a 3% DA and DR hike from July 2026 benefiting 48.54 lakh employees and 68.71 lakh pensioners with an annual cost of ₹10,016.10 crore.

What did the Cabinet approve regarding Dearness Allowance?

  • DA and DR hike. The Cabinet approved an additional instalment of Dearness Allowance and Dearness Relief representing a 3% increase over the existing 60% of Basic Pay/Pension.
  • Effective date. The increase is effective from July 1, 2026.
  • Beneficiaries. This hike will benefit about 48.54 lakh central government employees and 68.71 lakh pensioners.
  • Fiscal impact. The combined annual impact on the exchequer due to this increase is ₹10,016.10 crore.
  • Basis of increase. The increase follows the accepted formula based on the recommendations of the 7th Central Pay Commission.

Back to basics: Dearness Allowance and Dearness Relief

Dearness Allowance (DA) is a cost of living adjustment allowance paid to government employees to offset inflation. Dearness Relief (DR) is the equivalent allowance paid to pensioners. Both are calculated as a percentage of basic pay or pension and are revised periodically based on inflation indices to protect the purchasing power of employees and pensioners.
