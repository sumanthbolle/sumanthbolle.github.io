Karnataka Releases Three Drought-Resistant Crop Varieties to Support Farmers

Karnataka introduced three new drought-resistant crop varieties developed by University of Agricultural Sciences, Bengaluru, to help farmers cope with drought and water scarcity. This development is important for UPSC aspirants as it highlights state-level agricultural innovation addressing climate resilience and farmer welfare.

Karnataka released drought-resistant varieties of maize, ragi, and grain amaranth to improve yields under drought conditions and support farmers.

Why is this in the news?

On November 16, 2026, Karnataka's Agriculture Minister and Union Minister of State released three new drought-resistant crop varieties at the Krishi Mela organised by University of Agricultural Sciences, Bengaluru.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the new drought-resistant crop varieties released?

  • Crop varieties. Karnataka released three new drought-resistant crop varieties: Maize variety MH-20-45, Ragi variety GPU-101, and Grain Amaranth variety KBGA-18, developed by scientists at University of Agricultural Sciences, Bengaluru.
  • Yield and duration improvements. These varieties show increased grain yield by 14.6% to 18% and fodder yield by 27% for ragi, with crop durations ranging from 110 to 130 days, enabling farmers to get good yields in shorter time with less water.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does this matter for farmers and agriculture policy?

  • Drought impact in Karnataka. About 226 out of 240 taluks in Karnataka are affected by drought, with 90% crop loss due to lack of rains and El Nino effects, making drought-resistant crops crucial for sustaining agriculture.
  • Government support schemes. The state plans to launch Negila Yogi Yojane to provide ₹4,500 annually to small and marginal farmers owning less than three acres, and has already provided ₹5,000 per farmer as a drought-relief package.
  • Farmer Producers’ Organisations (FPOs). The government is working on a programme allowing FPOs to lease land from small farmers to grow high-demand crops, linking farmers to markets and improving income stability.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the broader agricultural strategies highlighted?

  • Focus on drought resilience. Agricultural universities are urged to develop more drought-resistant varieties and educate farmers on rainwater conservation and government schemes.
  • Infrastructure and marketing. Strengthening FPOs, improving warehouse and cold storage facilities, and enhancing marketing channels aim to reduce farmers' dependence on middlemen and improve profitability.

Back to basics: Drought-resistant crop varieties

Drought-resistant crop varieties are developed through scientific breeding to tolerate water scarcity and adverse climatic conditions. They require less water and can sustain yields under drought stress, helping farmers reduce crop losses and maintain income stability during dry spells.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about Karnataka's new drought-resistant crop varieties: 1. The new maize variety MH-20-45 has a grain yield increase of about 14.6% over the existing variety. 2.   1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2

Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because Negila Yogi Yojane plans to provide ₹4,500 annually only to small and marginal farmers owning less than three acres, not ₹5,000 to all farmers.
