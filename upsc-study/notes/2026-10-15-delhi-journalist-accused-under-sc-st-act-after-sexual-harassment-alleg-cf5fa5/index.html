Delhi Journalist Faces SC/ST Act Charges After Sexual Harassment Allegation Against ACP

A Delhi journalist accused an Assistant Commissioner of Police (ACP) of sexual harassment during a protest, but the ACP subsequently filed a complaint alleging casteist remarks by the journalist, leading to FIR registration under the SC/ST Act. This case highlights the complex interaction between caste-based legal protections and gender rights, relevant for understanding social justice and legal safeguards in India.

A sexual harassment allegation against a Delhi ACP led to a counter-complaint invoking the SC/ST Act over casteist remarks, illustrating tensions in applying social justice laws.

Why is this in the news?

On October 13, 2026, Delhi Police registered an FIR against a journalist under the SC/ST Act following a complaint by ACP Vivek Bhagat, who was earlier accused by the journalist of sexual harassment.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What triggered the legal cases involving the journalist and ACP?

  • Sexual harassment allegation. An independent journalist accused ACP Vivek Bhagat of sexual harassment during a protest in Delhi on October 3, 2026.
  • Counter complaint by ACP. ACP Bhagat filed a complaint alleging caste-based humiliation, intimidation, and obstruction of official duty by the journalist, leading to an FIR under the SC/ST Act.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the legal provisions involved in the FIR against the journalist?

  • SC/ST Act sections. The FIR was registered under Sections 3(1)(p), 3(1)(q), 3(1)(r), and 3(1)(s) of the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act.
  • Other legal sections. Additional charges include Sections 221, 356(2), and 3(5) of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) related to obstruction of duty and intimidation.

Why does this case matter for social justice and legal safeguards?

  • Intersection of caste and gender issues. The case illustrates the complex challenges in balancing protections against caste-based atrocities with addressing sexual harassment allegations.
  • Legal safeguards scrutiny. It raises questions about the application and possible misuse of the SC/ST Act in contexts involving gender rights and law enforcement accountability.

Back to basics: Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes (Prevention of Atrocities) Act

The SC/ST Act is a legal framework designed to prevent atrocities and discrimination against members of Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes. It provides special protections and penalties for offenses motivated by caste-based hatred or discrimination. Understanding this Act is essential to analyze cases where caste identity and legal protections intersect with other social issues like gender rights.

Quick revision

What legal sections were invoked against the journalist?

Has the sexual harassment complaint against ACP Vivek Bhagat been registered as an FIR?
No, the sexual harassment complaint is under inquiry by the Crime Branch and no FIR has been registered yet.

The ACP alleged caste-based humiliation, intimidation, and obstruction of official duty by the journalist.
