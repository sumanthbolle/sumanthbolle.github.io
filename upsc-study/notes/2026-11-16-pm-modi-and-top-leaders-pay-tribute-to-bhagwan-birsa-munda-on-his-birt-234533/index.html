Tributes to Bhagwan Birsa Munda on His 151st Birth Anniversary

On November 15, 2026, top Indian leaders including the President, Vice President, Prime Minister, and Lok Sabha Speaker paid floral tributes to tribal freedom fighter Bhagwan Birsa Munda at the Parliament premises. This day is observed as Janjatiya Gaurav Divas since 2021 to honor tribal contributions to India's freedom struggle and nation-building.

Bhagwan Birsa Munda's leadership in tribal resistance against British rule is commemorated annually on Janjatiya Gaurav Divas, highlighting tribal identity and rights.

Who was Bhagwan Birsa Munda and why is he remembered?

  • Tribal freedom fighter. Birsa Munda, revered as Dharti Aaba, was a tribal leader who mobilised tribal communities against British colonial rule through the Ulgulan movement, asserting their rights over water, forests, and land.
  • Champion of tribal identity. He combined the struggle for freedom with the defence of tribal cultural dignity, self-respect, and social justice, spreading awareness against social evils and exploitation.
  • Legacy and inspiration. His courage, sacrifice, and leadership remain an enduring source of inspiration, strengthening collective responsibility and vigilance among tribal communities.

What is Janjatiya Gaurav Divas and its significance?

  • Observance since 2021. November 15 has been observed as Janjatiya Gaurav Divas since 2021 to honour the sacrifices of tribal freedom fighters and recognise their contributions to India's freedom struggle and nation-building.
  • Celebration of tribal heritage. The day celebrates the rich history, culture, and heritage of tribal communities, fostering unity, pride, and recognition of their role in India's progress.
  • Nationwide events. Events include cultural performances by tribal folk artists and tributes by top leaders at the Parliament premises, highlighting tribal contributions.

How did top leaders honour Bhagwan Birsa Munda in 2026?

  • Floral tributes at Parliament. The President, Vice President, Prime Minister, Lok Sabha Speaker, and other dignitaries paid floral tributes at Birsa Munda's statue at Prerna Sthal in the Parliament House Complex.
  • Messages highlighting his ideals. Leaders praised his leadership qualities, the Ulgulan movement's role against British exploitation, and his efforts to protect tribal rights and identity.
  • Government commitment. The Prime Minister stated that the government is committed to ensuring dignity, opportunities, and prosperity for tribal communities, inspired by Birsa Munda's legacy.

Back to basics: Janjatiya Gaurav Divas

Janjatiya Gaurav Divas is observed annually on November 15 to commemorate the birth anniversary of tribal freedom fighter Bhagwan Birsa Munda. It honours the sacrifices and contributions of tribal communities in India's freedom struggle and nation-building, celebrating their cultural heritage and identity.

Mains question (15 marks)

Discuss the significance of tribal movements like that led by Bhagwan Birsa Munda in shaping Indian society and constitutional safeguards.

Discuss: Explain the impact of tribal freedom struggles on Indian society and the constitutional recognition of tribal rights.

Intro: Tribal movements, exemplified by Bhagwan Birsa Munda's leadership, played a crucial role in India's freedom struggle and social awakening.

  • Birsa Munda's Ulgulan movement mobilised tribal communities against British exploitation, asserting their rights over land, forests, and water.
  • These movements highlighted the importance of preserving tribal identity, culture, and social justice, influencing post-independence policies.
  • The observance of Janjatiya Gaurav Divas reflects the constitutional and social recognition of tribal contributions and rights.
  • Government initiatives inspired by such leaders aim to ensure dignity, opportunities, and prosperity for tribal populations.

Conclusion: Tribal movements have left an enduring legacy that continues to inspire inclusive nation-building and protection of tribal rights.
