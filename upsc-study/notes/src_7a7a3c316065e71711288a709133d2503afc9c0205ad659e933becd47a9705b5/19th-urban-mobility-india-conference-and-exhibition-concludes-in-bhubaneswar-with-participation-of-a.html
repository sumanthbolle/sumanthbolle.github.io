India implications

  • Urban transport planning should be linked with land-use planning so that essential services are reachable and transport investment supports orderly urban growth.
  • Multimodal integration can reduce interchange frictions, but requires coordinated planning among metro, bus, rail and local authorities.
  • Walking, cycling, people-centric streets and universal accessibility can improve equity and reduce dependence on private vehicles.
  • Data-based planning can improve service design, while requiring safeguards for privacy, affordability and digital exclusion.

Way forward

  • Integrate land-use and transport planning from the project-design stage, with accessibility to essential services as an outcome.
  • Prioritise multimodal interchange, reliable buses and safe first- and last-mile walking and cycling connections.
  • Apply universal design and gender-responsive planning, with participation by affected users.
  • Use real-time and digital data transparently for service planning, while protecting privacy and addressing digital exclusion.
