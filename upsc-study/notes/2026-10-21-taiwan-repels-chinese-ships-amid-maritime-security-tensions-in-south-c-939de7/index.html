Taiwan Repels 16 Chinese Ships in South China Sea Maritime Tensions

Taiwan's coast guard drove away 16 Chinese vessels involved in coordinated maritime harassment near Taiwan and the disputed Pratas Islands on October 18, 2026. This incident highlights ongoing security tensions in the South China Sea, relevant for understanding regional geopolitics and Indo-Pacific maritime security.

Taiwan repelled coordinated Chinese maritime operations involving 16 vessels near its waters, underscoring rising tensions in the South China Sea.

Why is this in the news?

On October 18, 2026, Taiwan's coast guard repelled 16 Chinese vessels engaged in simultaneous maritime harassment operations near Taiwan's main island and the Pratas Islands in the South China Sea.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What happened during the maritime harassment incident?

  • Chinese vessels involved. Sixteen Chinese coast guard, research, and fishing vessels conducted coordinated operations around Taiwan's main island and the Pratas Islands.
  • Taiwan's response. Taiwan's coast guard took forceful action immediately, including driving away the vessels and using water cannons against a Chinese fishing boat near Pratas earlier in October.
  • Locations of operations. Operations took place off Taiwan's eastern, southern, and southwestern coasts, and around the Pratas Islands, which are disputed between Taiwan and China.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for regional security and UPSC?

  • Territorial claims. China claims Taiwan as part of its territory and asserts jurisdiction over surrounding waters, which Taiwan rejects, leading to frequent maritime confrontations.
  • Maritime security implications. The incident reflects China's increasing maritime pressure and hybrid warfare tactics in the South China Sea, a strategic waterway with global trade significance.
  • Indo-Pacific strategic relevance. Understanding these tensions is crucial for UPSC aspirants studying regional security dynamics and India's strategic environment in the Indo-Pacific.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How has China escalated maritime operations near Taiwan recently?

  • Increase in Chinese vessels. China deployed a record 244 coast guard, research, and other ships around Taiwan in July 2026, up from 55 in June and 30 in May.
  • Law enforcement patrols. Since June 2026, China has conducted law enforcement patrols east of Taiwan, which Taipei calls provocative and expansionist.
  • Use of hybrid warfare. Chinese vessels engaged in simultaneous operations at multiple locations, combining coast guard, research, and fishing boats to alter the status quo.

Back to basics: Maritime Security and Territorial Disputes

Maritime security involves protecting a nation's sovereignty and economic interests in its surrounding waters. Territorial disputes arise when two or more countries claim sovereignty over the same maritime areas or islands. In such disputes, coast guards and naval forces often patrol and enforce claims, sometimes leading to confrontations. The South China Sea is a key example where multiple countries assert overlapping claims, making it a hotspot for regional security tensions.
