Why in news

On 30 October 2026, the Department of Land Resources reviewed watershed-development works in villages of Khed, Pune, under WDC-PMKSY 2.0. The review foregrounded rainwater conservation, drought mitigation, village water security and community responsibility for maintaining water-harvesting structures.

Static anchor

Watershed development is an integrated, drainage-basin-based approach that manages soil, runoff and water storage to improve infiltration, groundwater recharge, agricultural productivity and ecological sustainability.

Background

  • The Department of Land Resources under the Ministry of Rural Development implements watershed-development activities under the Watershed Development Component of Pradhan Mantri Krishi Sinchayee Yojana 2.0.
  • Watershed development links soil conservation, rainwater storage, groundwater recharge and agricultural productivity within a drainage basin.
  • The source describes works such as Continuous Contour Trenches, earthen and cement nala bunds, farm ponds, storage ponds and Kolhapuri-type weirs; their effectiveness depends on location-specific design and continued maintenance.

India implications

  • Drought-prone rural regions can use watershed planning to improve local water availability and reduce agriculture’s exposure to rainfall variability.
  • The approach can support more stable cropping, horticulture, rural livelihoods and women’s self-help-group-linked activities when benefits are inclusive.
  • Effective implementation requires Central-State coordination, local participation, quality monitoring, timely fund utilisation and post-construction maintenance.
  • Watershed works should complement, rather than substitute for, demand management, efficient irrigation and equitable groundwater governance.

Way forward

  • Plan watershed works according to local drainage, soil, rainfall and groundwater conditions.
  • Make post-construction maintenance, social monitoring and periodic repair integral to project design.
  • Strengthen convergence among watershed programmes, efficient irrigation, soil-health measures and livelihood activities.
  • Ensure transparent beneficiary selection, equitable access to stored or recharged water and meaningful participation of local institutions.
