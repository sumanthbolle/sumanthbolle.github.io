TDB-DST Supports Astrome's Sovereign AI-ELINT Space Constellation

The Technology Development Board (TDB) under the Department of Science & Technology (DST) signed an agreement with Astrome Technologies for ₹64.05 crore to develop an indigenous AI-enabled Electronic Intelligence (ELINT) satellite constellation. This project aims to advance critical space and AI technologies for national security, relevant for UPSC aspirants studying science and technology in defence.

The TDB-DST agreement with Astrome aims to develop a sovereign AI-ELINT satellite constellation to enhance India's space-based intelligence capabilities.

Why is this in the news?

On 12 November 2026, the Technology Development Board of DST signed a ₹64.05 crore agreement with Astrome Technologies to develop a sovereign AI-ELINT satellite constellation for national security.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the TDB-DST agreement with Astrome about?

  • Financial support. TDB under DST provided ₹64.05 crore through the Research, Development and Innovation Fund to Astrome Technologies for this project.
  • Project goal. The project aims to develop an indigenous space-based AI-enabled Electronic Intelligence (ELINT) constellation for national security applications.
  • Total cost and funding. The total project cost is ₹128.11 crore, with TDB funding half via Optionally Convertible Debt.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What technologies and capabilities does the project involve?

  • Key components. The project integrates three technologies: ELINT payloads, AI-based ground processing software, and inter-satellite communication links.
  • Satellite design. It involves developing 50 kg class microsatellites operating in low Earth orbit as part of a constellation architecture.
  • Technology advancement. The programme aims to advance technologies from Technology Readiness Level (TRL)-4 to TRL-9 through development, integration, testing, and validation.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does this project matter for India’s national security and technology?

  • Strategic importance. Space-based systems provide persistent surveillance over large areas, crucial for intelligence gathering and national security.
  • Indigenous capability. Developing indigenous ELINT and AI technologies reduces dependence on foreign systems and strengthens strategic autonomy.
  • Technology convergence. The project combines satellite engineering, electronic intelligence, artificial intelligence, and secure space communications.

Back to basics: Technology Readiness Level (TRL)

Technology Readiness Level (TRL) is a scale from 1 to 9 that measures the maturity of a technology, from basic principles observed (TRL-1) to actual system proven in operational environment (TRL-9). Advancing technologies to TRL-9 means they are fully developed, tested, and ready for deployment.
