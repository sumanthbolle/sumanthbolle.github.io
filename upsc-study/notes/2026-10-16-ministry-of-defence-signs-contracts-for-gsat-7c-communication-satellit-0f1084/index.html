MoD Signs Rs 4,106 Crore Contracts for GSAT-7C Satellite and Varunastra Torpedoes

The Ministry of Defence signed contracts worth Rs 4,106 crore for the GSAT-7C communication satellite for the Indian Air Force and Varunastra heavy-weight torpedoes for the Indian Navy. These acquisitions aim to enhance secure communication and anti-submarine warfare capabilities, promoting indigenous defence manufacturing under the Aatmanirbhar Bharat initiative.

The MoD's contracts for GSAT-7C and Varunastra torpedoes strengthen India's defence communication and naval warfare capabilities while advancing self-reliance in defence technology.

Why is this in the news?

On October 15, 2026, the Ministry of Defence signed contracts worth Rs 4,106 crore for the GSAT-7C communication satellite and Varunastra heavy-weight torpedoes to enhance the Indian Air Force and Navy capabilities.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What contracts did the Ministry of Defence sign?

  • Total contract value. The Ministry of Defence signed contracts worth approximately Rs 4,106 crore.
  • GSAT-7C satellite contract. A Rs 2,563 crore contract was signed with New Space India Limited, Bengaluru, for the GSAT-7C communication satellite dedicated to the Indian Air Force.
  • Varunastra torpedoes contract. A Rs 1,543 crore contract was signed with Bharat Dynamics Limited, Hyderabad, for Varunastra heavy-weight anti-submarine torpedoes for the Indian Navy.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

Why are these acquisitions important?

  • Enhanced communication for IAF. GSAT-7C is an indigenously designed, developed, and manufactured satellite that will provide secure, resilient, and seamless communication for Indian Air Force assets, strengthening operational effectiveness.
  • Improved naval anti-submarine warfare. Varunastra is an indigenous ship-launched torpedo with advanced maneuvering capabilities, capable of engaging enemy submarines at long ranges, enhancing the Indian Navy's underwater warfare capabilities.
  • Promotion of self-reliance. Both contracts support the Government’s Aatmanirbhar Bharat and Make-in-India initiatives by advancing domestic defence manufacturing and critical technology development.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

Who were the key signatories and stakeholders?

  • Contract signatories. The contracts were signed in New Delhi in the presence of Shri A Anbarasu, Additional Secretary and Director General (Acquisition), Department of Defence.
  • Involved organisations. New Space India Limited (NSIL) and Bharat Dynamics Limited (BDL) are the manufacturing agencies for GSAT-7C and Varunastra respectively.

Back to basics: Indigenous Defence Manufacturing

Indigenous defence manufacturing refers to the design, development, and production of defence equipment within the country to reduce dependence on foreign suppliers. It supports strategic autonomy, enhances security, and promotes economic growth by developing domestic industries and technologies. Initiatives like Aatmanirbhar Bharat and Make-in-India aim to boost self-reliance in critical defence technologies.
