Government Extends ESIC New Amnesty Scheme Till December 31, 2026

The Employees’ State Insurance Corporation (ESIC) has extended the New Amnesty Scheme 2025 by three months till December 31, 2026, allowing more time for employers and insured persons to resolve pending disputes. This extension is important for UPSC aspirants as it relates to social security and labour welfare policies under the erstwhile ESI Act.

The ESIC New Amnesty Scheme 2025 was extended till December 31, 2026, to reduce litigation and promote voluntary compliance among employers and insured persons.

Why is this in the news?

On October 29, 2026, the Ministry of Labour & Employment announced the extension of the ESIC New Amnesty Scheme 2025 till December 31, 2026, following stakeholder representations.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the ESIC New Amnesty Scheme 2025?

  • One-time dispute resolution. The scheme is a one-time initiative to reduce the backlog of court cases related to the erstwhile ESI Act by promoting out-of-court settlements between employers and ESIC.
  • Settlement terms. Employers can settle ad-hoc assessments by paying actual contributions and interest without damages, and cases where dues are paid can be withdrawn by paying 10% of disputed damages.
  • Eligibility criteria. Court cases filed up to March 31, 2025, are eligible for settlement under this scheme.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

Why was the scheme extended till December 31, 2026?

  • Stakeholder representations. The extension followed a review of representations from various stakeholders seeking more time to resolve pending cases.
  • Promoting compliance. The extension supports ESIC’s commitment to reducing litigation and promoting voluntary compliance among employers and insured persons.
  • Ease of doing business. The scheme aligns with the government’s broader goal of enhancing ease of doing business while protecting the rights of insured persons.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

What benefits does the scheme provide to employers and insured persons?

  • Reduced financial burden. Employers can avoid paying damages on ad-hoc assessments and settle dues by paying actual contributions and interest.
  • Withdrawal of cases. Cases filed by ESIC for non-submission of records or delayed payments, which were later complied with, can be withdrawn with court permission.
  • Additional time. The extension gives eligible employers and insured persons more time to avail the benefits of the scheme until December 31, 2026.

Back to basics: Employees’ State Insurance Scheme

The Employees’ State Insurance (ESI) Scheme is a social security and health insurance scheme for Indian workers. It provides medical care and cash benefits to insured persons and their families in case of sickness, maternity, disability, or death due to employment injury. The scheme is implemented by the Employees’ State Insurance Corporation (ESIC) under the Ministry of Labour & Employment. Disputes can arise over contributions or compliance, which the amnesty scheme aims to resolve amicably.
