Recent Counter-Terrorism Operations in Kashmir's Kulgam District

Security forces conducted anti-terror operations in Kulgam, Jammu and Kashmir, resulting in the death of a Lashkar-e-Taiba militant Lateef Bhat's accomplice. These operations highlight ongoing internal security challenges and the impact on local civilian life, relevant for understanding conflict dynamics in the region.

Security forces in Kulgam eliminated an accomplice of Lashkar-e-Taiba militant Lateef Bhat during ongoing counter-terrorism operations.

Why is this in the news?

On November 17, 2026, security forces continued anti-terror operations in Kulgam, Jammu and Kashmir, following an attack on CRPF personnel, leading to the killing of a local accomplice of Lashkar-e-Taiba terrorist Lateef Bhat.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What happened during the Kulgam counter-terrorism operations?

  • Operation details. Security forces conducted a seven-day anti-terror operation in Kulgam, focusing on the Laroo Bypass area, aiming to track down Lashkar-e-Taiba militant Lateef Bhat and his accomplice.
  • Militant killed. Umar Nisar Dar, a baker by profession and a local accomplice of Lateef Bhat, was found dead at the encounter site; he was not previously listed as a terrorist.
  • Attack context. Dar and Bhat were seen together on November 10 and 11 surveying multiple locations, and Dar is suspected to have participated in an attack on a CRPF checkpoint in Akhran that injured three personnel.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why do these operations matter for internal security?

  • Counter-terrorism challenge. The operations reflect ongoing efforts to neutralize militant threats from groups like Lashkar-e-Taiba in Jammu and Kashmir.
  • Impact on civilians. Prolonged restrictions and searches in civilian areas during the operations have caused hardships, raising concerns about collective punishment and alienation among local populations.
  • Political responses. Local political parties, including the National Conference and Peoples Democratic Party, have criticized the extended restrictions on civilians, emphasizing the need to balance security with humanitarian considerations.

Back to basics: Counter-terrorism operations

Counter-terrorism operations involve coordinated actions by security forces to detect, prevent, and neutralize terrorist threats. These operations often include intelligence gathering, surveillance, targeted strikes, and area security measures. They aim to maintain internal security and protect civilians while managing the challenges of operating in populated or sensitive areas.
