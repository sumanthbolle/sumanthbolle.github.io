Bar Council of India Stays Telangana Bar Council’s Committee Constitution

The Bar Council of India (BCI) issued an interim order on October 23, 2026, staying the constitution and operation of committees formed by the Telangana Bar Council. This matters for UPSC as it highlights the statutory powers and procedural compliance required in legal governance under the Advocates Act, reflecting the rule of law and constitutional authority of statutory bodies.

The BCI stayed Telangana Bar Council’s committee formation citing procedural lapses under the Advocates Act.

Why is this in the news?

On October 23, 2026, the Bar Council of India passed an interim order staying the constitution and operation of committees recently formed by the Telangana Bar Council after examining appeals by advocates.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What did the Bar Council of India order regarding Telangana Bar Council’s committees?

  • Interim stay order. The BCI passed an interim order staying the constitution and operation of committees recently constituted by the Telangana Bar Council.
  • Reason for stay. The stay was issued after appeals claimed that objections and dissent by some advocates were not reflected in the minutes and that statutory procedures under the Advocates Act 1961 were not followed.
  • Committees involved. The committees affected included Executive, Enrolment, Legislative, Privileges, Rules, Library, Finance, and Disciplinary committees.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why did the BCI find the Telangana Bar Council’s actions problematic?

  • Lack of consultation. A resolution contemplated consultation with 10 identified members for committee constitution, but such consultation was allegedly not undertaken.
  • Non-compliance with statutory procedure. The Telangana Bar Council chairman proceeded without complying with the Advocates Act’s prescribed procedures for election or co-option of committee members.
  • Statutory limits on powers. The BCI noted that a resolution cannot enlarge the power of an individual office-bearer inconsistent with express statutory requirements.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How did the BCI address ongoing enrolment work despite the stay?

  • Temporary enrolment committee. The BCI directed that enrolment matters be handled by a committee comprising the State Bar Council chairman, vice-chairman, and a member to ensure enrolment work is not hampered.

Back to basics: Statutory Bodies and Legal Governance

Statutory bodies like Bar Councils are created by law to regulate professions and ensure governance according to statutory provisions. They must follow procedures prescribed by the relevant Acts, such as the Advocates Act for Bar Councils, to maintain legality and accountability. The Bar Council of India oversees State Bar Councils to ensure compliance with these laws.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the Bar Council of India’s interim order on Telangana Bar Council’s committees: 1. The BCI stayed the constitution of committees due to non-compliance with the Advocates Act 1961. 2. The BCI chairman personally constituted the committees without any resolution. Which of the statements is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the Telangana Bar Council chairman, not the BCI chairman, constituted the committees, and the issue was procedural non-compliance, not unilateral action by the BCI chairman.

Quick revision

Why did the Bar Council of India stay the Telangana Bar Council’s committee constitution?
Because the Telangana Bar Council did not follow the statutory procedures under the Advocates Act 1961 and failed to consult identified members.
Which committees’ constitution was stayed by the Bar Council of India?
Executive, Enrolment, Legislative, Privileges, Rules, Library, Finance, and Disciplinary committees.
How will enrolment matters be handled during the stay?
By a committee comprising the State Bar Council chairman, vice-chairman, and a member to ensure enrolment work continues.
