India Advances Disaster Prevention and Molecular-Level Healthcare Research

India is shifting its disaster management approach from post-disaster relief to prevention, especially for landslides, while healthcare research is focusing on prevention through molecular and genetic strategies. These developments are crucial for UPSC aspirants studying disaster management and health sector reforms.

India is enhancing disaster resilience through advanced landslide forecasting and mitigation, alongside pioneering preventive healthcare research at molecular and genetic levels.

Why is this in the news?

In November 2026, Union Minister Dr. Jitendra Singh highlighted India's shift towards disaster prevention in landslide management and futuristic healthcare research at molecular and genetic levels.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

How is India shifting its approach to landslide disaster management?

  • Focus on prevention. India is moving from post-disaster relief to anticipating risks, strengthening early warning systems, and preventing loss of lives and infrastructure in landslide-prone areas.
  • Landslide mapping and data. National landslide susceptibility mapping covers approximately 4.3 lakh sq km, and an inventory of nearly 91,000 historical landslides has been compiled.
  • Forecasting centres and technology. The National Landslide Forecasting Centre in Kolkata, established in 2024, now extends forecasting activities to 20 districts, using AI, geospatial technologies, satellite monitoring, and sensor networks.
  • Funding and projects. ₹1,000 crore has been approved for the National Landslide Mitigation Project across 15 states, and ₹1,115.67 crore sanctioned for disaster mitigation and capacity building.
  • Interdisciplinary collaboration. Institutions like Geological Survey of India and Wadia Institute of Himalayan Geology collaborate on hazard assessment and AI-enabled landslide prediction, integrating weather and geological data.

[2] Press Information Bureau

What are the key developments in India's healthcare research focus?

  • Shift to prevention. Healthcare research is increasingly focusing on prevention, early detection, and advanced molecular and genetic-level studies to address metabolic and lifestyle diseases affecting younger populations.
  • Genomic research. Genome sequencing of 10,000 Indians across 84 populations has enhanced understanding of genetic diversity and disease susceptibility, enabling precision medicine.
  • Vaccination and indigenous innovation. Over 50 lakh adolescent girls have been vaccinated against HPV to prevent cervical cancer, reflecting progress in indigenous vaccine development.
  • Precision medicine and rare diseases. Research includes biomarkers for gestational diabetes and expanding diagnosis and treatment coverage for rare genetic diseases to improve affordability and access.
  • Advances in therapies. Indian researchers are developing genetic therapies, new antibiotics, and gene therapy for haemophilia, indicating growing indigenous medical research capabilities.
  • Healthcare infrastructure. Expansion of dialysis facilities in district hospitals and research on early kidney disease detection improve treatment access and disease management.
  • Collaboration and awareness. Greater cooperation among government, research bodies, healthcare professionals, and private sector is emphasized, alongside cautioning youth against misinformation.

Back to basics: Disaster Management and Preventive Healthcare

Disaster management involves strategies to reduce risk and impact of natural hazards, including preparedness, early warning, and mitigation. Preventive healthcare focuses on measures to prevent diseases or detect them early, reducing severity and improving outcomes. Advances in technology enable precise risk assessment and personalized medicine based on genetic and molecular data.

What happens next?

  • Enhance integration of data. Closer integration of weather forecasts with geological, hydrological, and real-time ground observations is needed to improve landslide early warning accuracy.
  • Expand rare disease coverage. Efforts to improve diagnosis and affordable treatment for rare genetic diseases require sustained scientific and institutional support.
  • Promote private and philanthropic participation. Increasing involvement of private sector and philanthropy can accelerate medical research and innovation.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about India's recent initiatives in disaster management and healthcare research: 1. India has completed landslide susceptibility mapping over approximately 4.3 lakh sq km and established a National Landslide Forecasting Centre in Kolkata. 2. Over 50 lakh adolescent girls have been vaccinated against HPV as part of India's preventive healthcare expansion. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (c). Statement 1 is correct but the National Landslide Forecasting Centre was established in 2024, not recently; statement 2 correctly states the HPV vaccination figure.
