Supreme Court and ED Dispute Over Interim Bail to Azam Khan in Money Laundering Case

The Supreme Court declined to freeze an Allahabad High Court order granting interim bail to Azam Khan in a money laundering case but agreed to hear the ED's appeal. This case highlights judicial procedures and law enforcement actions in high-profile criminal cases.

The Supreme Court refused to stay interim bail granted to Azam Khan by the Allahabad High Court but agreed to hear the ED's appeal urgently.

Why is this in the news?

On November 18, 2026, the Supreme Court declined the ED's plea to freeze interim bail granted to Azam Khan by the Allahabad High Court in a money laundering case but agreed to hear the ED's appeal on November 19.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the Supreme Court's response to the ED's plea on Azam Khan's bail?

  • Refusal to freeze bail. The Supreme Court declined the ED's oral plea to immediately freeze the Allahabad High Court's interim bail order for Azam Khan.
  • Urgent hearing scheduled. The Court agreed to urgently list the ED's appeal against the bail order on November 19, 2026.
  • Interim remarks. Justice Joymalya Bagchi remarked that 'heavens will not fall' if Azam Khan remains on interim bail.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why did the Allahabad High Court grant interim bail to Azam Khan?

  • Arrest deemed motivated and mala fide. The High Court found Azam Khan's arrest on October 31, 2026, was motivated and mala fide, occurring immediately after his release on bail in another case.
  • Violation of legal safeguards. The Court held that the ED did not follow the required procedure under Article 22(1) of the Constitution and Section 19 of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act during the arrest.
  • Political context. Azam Khan's lawyers argued the arrest aimed to keep an opposition leader incarcerated ahead of Uttar Pradesh Assembly elections.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the key facts about Azam Khan's arrest and custody?

  • Arrest timing. Azam Khan was arrested by the ED on October 31, 2026, at the jail gate shortly after his release on bail in a PAN card case.
  • Judicial custody. He was remanded to judicial custody by a trial judge on November 1, 2026.
  • Supreme Court advice. On November 5, 2026, the Supreme Court advised Azam Khan to approach the High Court against his arrest and requested priority hearing.

Back to basics: Interim Bail and Legal Safeguards in Arrest

Interim bail is a temporary relief granted by courts allowing an accused to be out of custody during trial or appeal. Legal safeguards such as those under Article 22(1) of the Constitution and Section 19 of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act ensure proper procedure during arrest, including informing the arrested person of grounds and rights. Courts examine whether these safeguards were followed when granting bail or quashing arrests.
