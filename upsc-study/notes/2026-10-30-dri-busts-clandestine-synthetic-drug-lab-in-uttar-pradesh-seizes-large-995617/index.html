DRI Busts Large Synthetic Drug Lab in Uttar Pradesh, Seizes 1,100 kg Drugs

The Directorate of Revenue Intelligence (DRI) uncovered a major clandestine synthetic drug manufacturing lab in Jalaun district, Uttar Pradesh, seizing approximately 1,100 kg of drugs and precursor chemicals. This operation is crucial for UPSC aspirants to understand India's internal security challenges related to illicit drug production and enforcement mechanisms.

The DRI dismantled a large synthetic drug manufacturing facility in Uttar Pradesh, seizing about 1,100 kg of drugs and chemicals and arresting two persons under the NDPS Act.

Why is this in the news?

On 29 October 2026, the DRI conducted an intelligence-led operation that busted a fully functional clandestine synthetic drug manufacturing laboratory in Jalaun district, Uttar Pradesh.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What happened in the DRI drug lab bust?

  • Operation details. The DRI, with assistance from UP ATS, conducted a coordinated raid on 29 October 2026, uncovering a fully functional illicit synthetic drug lab in a remote village in Jalaun district.
  • Seizures made. Approximately 1,100 kg of drugs and precursor chemicals were seized, including 184 kg semi-prepared drugs, 582 kg precursor chemicals, 15.4 kg finished methaqualone, and manufacturing equipment.
  • Arrests. Two persons associated with the clandestine manufacturing facility were arrested under the provisions of the Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act, 1985.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does this bust matter for internal security and drug control?

  • Impact on illicit drug ecosystem. The bust dismantled a large clandestine production facility, removing substantial quantities of drugs and chemicals from the illicit supply chain and preventing further manufacture of synthetic drugs.
  • Organised crime indication. The recovery of finished drugs, semi-prepared material, precursor chemicals, and equipment indicates an organised illicit drug manufacturing operation.
  • DRI's role. The DRI is committed to disrupting drug manufacturing networks and trafficking of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances to enhance internal security.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What legal provisions and agencies are involved?

  • Legal framework. The arrests were made under the NDPS Act, 1985, which governs control and regulation of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances in India.
  • Agencies involved. The Directorate of Revenue Intelligence led the operation with operational assistance from the Uttar Pradesh Anti-Terrorism Squad (UP ATS).

Back to basics: Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act

The NDPS Act, 1985, is a law in India that regulates the production, manufacture, possession, sale, transport, and consumption of narcotic drugs and psychotropic substances. It aims to control illicit drug trafficking and abuse by prescribing penalties and empowering enforcement agencies to take action against offenders.
