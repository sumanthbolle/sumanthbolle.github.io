Cabinet Approves Sale of IDBI Bank to Fairfax, India's Largest Financial Sector FDI

The Union Cabinet approved the sale of a 60.72% combined stake in IDBI Bank to Fairfax Financial Holdings for ₹52,878 crore, marking the largest foreign direct investment in India's financial sector. This strategic restructuring reduces government and LIC stakes, impacting banking ownership and financial sector reforms.

The government approved the sale of majority IDBI Bank shares to Fairfax, reducing its stake to 15% and marking the largest FDI in India's financial sector.

Why is this in the news?

On October 14, 2026, the Union Cabinet approved the strategic restructuring of IDBI Bank by selling a majority stake to Fairfax Financial Holdings for ₹52,878 crore.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What did the Cabinet approve regarding IDBI Bank?

  • Stake sale details. The Cabinet approved the sale of a combined 60.72% stake held by the government and LIC in IDBI Bank to Fairfax Financial Holdings for ₹52,878 crore.
  • Change in ownership. Post-sale, the government's stake will reduce to 15% and LIC's stake to 19%, transferring control of the bank to Fairfax.
  • Nature of transaction. The government termed this deal a strategic restructuring rather than a strategic disinvestment.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this sale matter for India's financial sector?

  • Largest FDI in financial sector. This transaction represents the largest foreign direct investment ever in India's financial sector.
  • Regulatory continuity. Despite the change in ownership, the Reserve Bank of India continues full regulatory and prudential supervision over IDBI Bank.
  • Customer impact. Depositors and borrowers are unaffected; deposits remain insured up to ₹5 lakh per depositor, and priority sector lending targets continue to apply.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the background and financial health of IDBI Bank?

  • Previous ownership and PCA. The government held 45.5% and LIC 49.2% before the sale; IDBI Bank was under RBI's Prompt Corrective Action framework since 2017 due to poor financial health.
  • LIC's intervention. In January 2019, LIC infused ₹21,624 crore into IDBI Bank, acquiring a controlling stake and leading RBI to classify it as a private bank.
  • Financial turnaround. Since becoming a private bank, IDBI's gross non-performing asset ratio fell from 31.8% in September 2018 to 2.3% in March 2026, and its capital adequacy ratio rose from 6.22% to 26.65%.
  • Profitability. The bank reported a record net profit of ₹9,513 crore in 2025-26 after moving out of PCA restrictions.

Back to basics: Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) in Banking

Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) involves investment by a foreign entity into an Indian company, often bringing capital, technology, and management expertise. In the banking sector, FDI can influence ownership, control, and governance structures but remains subject to regulatory oversight by the Reserve Bank of India to ensure financial stability and protect depositors. The government can reduce its stake in public sector banks by strategic sales to private or foreign investors as part of financial sector reforms.
