RBI Announces Conversion/Switch of State Government Securities Worth ₹10,800 Crore

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has announced an auction for the conversion or switch of State Government Securities (SGS) aggregating ₹10,800 crore face value. This process affects state finances and debt management, which is important for understanding fiscal federalism in India.

RBI's conversion/switch auction allows states to manage their debt by exchanging existing securities for new ones with different maturities, impacting fiscal management.

Why is this in the news?

On November 20, 2026, RBI announced the conversion/switch auction of State Government Securities worth ₹10,800 crore to help states manage their debt portfolios.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

What is the conversion/switch of State Government Securities?

  • Definition. Conversion or switch is a process where state governments exchange existing securities (source securities) for new securities (destination securities) with different maturities through an auction.
  • Purpose. It helps states manage their debt by altering the maturity profile of their liabilities without changing the overall amount significantly.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

How does the RBI conduct the conversion/switch auction?

  • Auction details. The auction is multiple-price based, where bids specify the amount and price of source and destination securities, and successful bids are accepted at their quoted prices.
  • Bidding process. Market participants submit bids electronically on RBI's e-Kuber platform, specifying the face value of source securities they want to sell and the price for destination securities they want to buy.
  • Settlement. The auction settlement happens on T+1 basis, with fund settlement covering net accrued interest and any cash consideration due to rounding-off.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

Which State Government Securities are involved in the current auction?

  • Aggregate amount. The auction covers an aggregate face value of ₹10,800 crore across Madhya Pradesh, Punjab, Rajasthan, Tamil Nadu, and Uttar Pradesh.
  • Source and destination securities. Source securities are mostly shorter-term SDLs maturing between 2027 and 2029, switched to longer-term SGS maturing between 2034 and 2044.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

Why does this matter for fiscal federalism and state financial management?

  • Debt management tool. Conversion/switch auctions allow states to manage their debt maturity profiles, improving fiscal sustainability and reducing rollover risks.
  • Impact on state finances. By extending maturities, states can ease short-term repayment pressures, which is crucial for maintaining fiscal discipline under the federal structure.

Back to basics: State Government Securities and Debt Management

State Government Securities (SGS) are debt instruments issued by state governments to finance their fiscal deficits. Debt management involves managing the timing and cost of repayments to ensure fiscal sustainability. Conversion or switch auctions allow states to exchange existing securities for new ones with different maturities, helping them manage liquidity and rollover risks effectively.
