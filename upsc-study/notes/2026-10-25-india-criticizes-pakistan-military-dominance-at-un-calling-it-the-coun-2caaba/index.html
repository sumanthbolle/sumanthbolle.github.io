India Criticizes Pakistan Military as 'Real Rulers' at UN

At the UN General Assembly's Third Committee in October 2026, India accused Pakistan's military of dominating its political system and undermining civilian authority. This highlights ongoing India-Pakistan tensions and Pakistan's internal governance issues, relevant for understanding South Asian geopolitics and diplomatic relations.

India accused Pakistan's military of being the real rulers, overshadowing its civilian government, during a UN session on minority rights.

Why is this in the news?

India's First Secretary to the UN, Raghoo Puri, criticized Pakistan's military dominance and minority rights record during the UN General Assembly's Third Committee session on October 25, 2026.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What did India say about Pakistan's military at the UN?

  • Military dominance. India accused Pakistan's military of being the country's 'real rulers' who override the civilian government's instructions.
  • Form 47 government. India referred to Pakistan's ruling dispensation as a 'Form 47 government', implying alleged election manipulation in the 2024 general elections.
  • Political control. India stated that the interests of the Pakistani armed forces take precedence over the civilian government.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why did India raise this issue at the UN?

  • Countering propaganda. India accused Pakistan of trying to paint a propagandist picture of India to divert attention from its own domestic challenges.
  • Highlighting minority rights. India questioned Pakistan's record on protecting minority rights, noting the dramatic shrinking of minorities in Pakistan since independence.
  • Jammu and Kashmir stance. India reiterated that Jammu and Kashmir is an integral part of India and urged Pakistan to focus on its own citizens' welfare.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What broader issues does this reflect?

  • Pakistan's internal challenges. India pointed to Pakistan's economic distress, lawlessness, and human rights violations as underlying problems.
  • Sovereignty and minority rights. India emphasized that protection of minority rights is a sovereign responsibility and advocated handling internal issues per national laws.
  • India's diversity. India highlighted its own multi-religious, multi-ethnic society and constitutional safeguards for minorities as a contrast to Pakistan.

Back to basics: Role of the Military in Civil Governance

In some countries, the military can exert significant influence over political decisions, sometimes overshadowing elected civilian governments. This affects the country's democratic processes, sovereignty, and international relations. Understanding civil-military relations helps explain internal political dynamics and foreign policy positions.
