IAS Officer Shikhar Agrawal Seeks Supreme Court Stay in Rajasthan Corruption Probe

Senior IAS officer Shikhar Agrawal has moved the Supreme Court seeking a stay on the investigation into his alleged corruption by the Rajasthan Anti-Corruption Bureau. This case highlights issues of procedural fairness and judicial oversight in administrative accountability.

Shikhar Agrawal challenged the procedural irregularities in the approval of his corruption investigation, seeking Supreme Court intervention.

Why is this in the news?

On November 24, 2026, IAS officer Shikhar Agrawal moved the Supreme Court seeking relief against a corruption investigation initiated by the Rajasthan government.

[1] The Indian Express · Secondary

What triggered the Supreme Court petition by Shikhar Agrawal?

  • Investigation approval challenged. Agrawal contended that the state government granted approval for his investigation hastily and without proper consideration, raising questions about its legality and propriety.
  • High Court proceedings. The Rajasthan High Court declined immediate relief but directed the state to file a reply within three weeks, setting the next hearing for December 18, 2026.
  • Allegations in FIR. The FIR alleges Agrawal and his family bought 64 villas at throwaway prices from a company that received undue benefits during his administrative tenure.

[1] The Indian Express · Secondary

Why does this case matter for governance and accountability?

  • Procedural safeguards under law. The case raises issues about adherence to procedural requirements under Section 17A of the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, which governs approval for investigating public servants.
  • Judicial oversight role. The judiciary's role in examining procedural lapses ensures checks on executive actions and protects civil servants from arbitrary investigations.
  • Transparency and administrative ethics. The case exemplifies the need for transparency and ethical conduct in governance, reinforcing probity in public administration.

[1] The Indian Express · Secondary

What are the key allegations against Shikhar Agrawal?

  • Undue benefits to company. The company Goyal Fashions allegedly received customised financial packages and benefits under the Rajasthan Investment Promotion Scheme 2019 during Agrawal's tenure.
  • Suspicious property transactions. Agrawal and his family are accused of purchasing 64 villas at throwaway prices from the company between October 2023 and October 2026.
  • Administrative actions taken. Following media reports, Agrawal and his IPS officer wife were placed on Awaiting Posting Order and served show-cause notices regarding their assets.

Back to basics: Prevention of Corruption Act and procedural safeguards

The Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988, regulates investigation and prosecution of corruption by public servants. Section 17A mandates prior approval from the government before investigating certain public officials to prevent misuse of power and ensure procedural fairness.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements regarding the Shikhar Agrawal corruption case: 1. The Rajasthan government granted approval for investigation under Section 17A of the Prevention of Corruption Act, 1988. 2. The Supreme Court immediately granted relief to Shikhar Agrawal upon his petition.

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). The trap is statement 2; the Supreme Court petition was filed seeking relief, but immediate relief was not granted as the High Court had declined it and set a future hearing date.
