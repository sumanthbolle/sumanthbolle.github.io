Bihar Mandates Bank Accounts for Government School Children by 2026

Bihar's Education Department has directed all government schools to maintain birth certificates and ensure bank accounts are opened for every enrolled child by December 31, 2026. This move supports financial inclusion and facilitates direct benefit transfers of scholarships and other government funds.

Bihar requires all government school children to have bank accounts linked to verified birth certificates to streamline financial inclusion and scholarship disbursement.

Why is this in the news?

On November 5, 2026, Bihar's Education Department issued instructions to open bank accounts for all children studying in government schools, linking it to the maintenance of birth certificates.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the new directives for children in Bihar government schools?

  • Mandatory birth certificates. All children studying in government schools must have their birth certificates maintained in school records to facilitate bank account opening and school admission.
  • Bank accounts for all children. Headmasters must coordinate with nearby government or nationalised banks to open individual or joint bank accounts for every enrolled child by December 31, 2026.
  • Priority for scholarship recipients. Children eligible for scholarships will be given special priority in opening bank accounts to enable direct benefit transfers.
  • Role of local bodies. Village panchayat Mukhiya or municipal bodies are to assist in issuing birth certificates to children, especially in rural areas.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for financial inclusion and welfare?

  • Linking identity and finance. Maintaining birth certificates ensures proper identity verification, which is essential for opening bank accounts under the government's financial inclusion initiative.
  • Facilitating direct benefit transfers. Having bank accounts in children's names allows the government to transfer scholarship funds and other benefits directly, reducing leakages and delays.
  • Supporting education access. This initiative aligns with efforts to improve education access and support vulnerable children through financial empowerment.

Back to basics: Financial Inclusion

Financial inclusion means making financial services accessible and affordable to all individuals, especially vulnerable and marginalized groups. It involves providing bank accounts, credit, insurance, and payment services to those without access, enabling them to participate in the economy and benefit from government schemes. Birth certificates serve as identity proof necessary for opening bank accounts and accessing these services.
