Why in news

On 8 November 2026, the Press Information Bureau reported that the seven-day exposition of the sacred relics of Shakyamuni Buddha at Tawang Monastery had concluded on 7 November after attracting 31,000 devotees and visitors. The relics were scheduled to move to Bomdila as the next exposition destination.

Background

  • The Ministry of Culture organised the exposition in collaboration with the Government of Arunachal Pradesh and the International Buddhist Confederation.
  • The relics arrived at Tawang from the National Museum, New Delhi, on 31 October 2026; the Tawang leg concluded on 7 November 2026.
  • The programme included exhibitions on the Piprahwa relics and Buddhist cultural legacies, as well as an international conference on dissemination and preservation of Buddhist teachings.
