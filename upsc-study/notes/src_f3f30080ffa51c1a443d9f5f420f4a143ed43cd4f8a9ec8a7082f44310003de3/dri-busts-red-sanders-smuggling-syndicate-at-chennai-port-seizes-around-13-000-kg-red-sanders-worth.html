India implications

  • India must balance export facilitation with stronger traceability and risk-based inspection for sensitive commodities and routes.
  • Wildlife trafficking links biodiversity loss with organised economic crime, requiring coordination among DRI, Customs, forest authorities, port operators and international counterparts.
  • Interception before loading limits illicit outward movement, but sustained investigation is necessary to identify financiers, suppliers, logistics intermediaries and overseas buyers.

Way forward

  • Use risk-based profiling for sensitive commodities, routes, consignments and unusual declarations rather than blanket inspection.
  • Strengthen end-to-end container traceability after self-sealing, including accountability for diversion to private premises and cargo substitution.
  • Integrate DRI, Customs, forest and wildlife authorities, port operators and international partners for intelligence sharing and investigation.
  • Pursue the complete trafficking network, including financiers, suppliers, transporters and overseas buyers, while protecting due process for legitimate exporters.
