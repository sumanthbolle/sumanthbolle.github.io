India-UK Deepen Cooperation in Animal Husbandry and Launch Digital Platform

India and the UK held bilateral talks to strengthen cooperation in animal health, sustainable livestock production, and trade under the India-UK Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA). Concurrently, India launched a DBIM-compliant website for the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying to enhance digital governance and citizen-centric services.

India and the UK are expanding bilateral cooperation in animal husbandry sectors and India has enhanced digital governance with a new DBIM-compliant animal husbandry website.

Why is this in the news?

On 12-13 October 2026, India and the UK held bilateral talks focusing on animal husbandry cooperation, and India launched a DBIM-compliant digital platform for the Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

What was agreed in India-UK bilateral talks on animal husbandry?

  • Focus areas. The talks between Union Minister Rajiv Ranjan Singh and UK Secretary of State Angela Eagle focused on cooperation in animal health, sustainable livestock production, scientific research, genomics, advanced reproductive technologies, and capacity-building through technical expertise exchange.
  • Trade and regulatory issues. Both countries explored opportunities under the India-UK Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA) to address market access and regulatory challenges related to livestock, dairy, marine, and other animal-origin products.
  • Commitment to growth. The engagement reaffirmed the commitment to enhance bilateral cooperation, facilitate trade, and promote sustainable growth in animal husbandry and allied sectors.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does the new digital platform strengthen governance in animal husbandry?

  • DBIM compliance. The Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying launched a website compliant with the Digital Brand Identity Manual (DBIM) Version 3.0 to strengthen digital presence and ensure accessible, transparent, and citizen-centric services.
  • Enhanced features. The revamped website offers advanced search, multi-language support via Bhashini, persona-led navigation, centralized content management, and serves as a one-stop hub for flagship schemes and initiatives.
  • Information access. Users can easily access information on livestock health, animal husbandry schemes, dairy development, livestock management, and departmental programs like the Animal Husbandry Infrastructure Development Fund and Rashtriya Gokul Mission.

[2] Press Information Bureau

What is the broader context of India-UK economic cooperation?

  • Trade ambitions. Commerce Minister Piyush Goyal called for doubling India-UK bilateral trade by 2030, leveraging the India-UK Comprehensive Economic and Trade Agreement (CETA) which provides zero-duty access for most Indian exports to the UK.
  • Complementary strengths. India offers scale, manufacturing base, digital infrastructure, and young talent, while the UK contributes research, design, advanced technologies, and a leading financial center.
  • Priority cooperation areas. Five key cooperation areas include clean energy, advanced manufacturing, technology and innovation, financial services, and healthcare and consumer sectors.
  • Implementation focus. Both countries emphasize translating agreements into tangible business outcomes, supporting MSMEs, and fostering mutual understanding and capacity-building.

Back to basics: Digital Governance

Digital governance involves using digital tools and platforms to deliver government services efficiently, transparently, and accessibly to citizens. It enhances service delivery by providing centralized information, multi-language support, and user-friendly navigation, aligning with principles of transparency and citizen-centricity.
