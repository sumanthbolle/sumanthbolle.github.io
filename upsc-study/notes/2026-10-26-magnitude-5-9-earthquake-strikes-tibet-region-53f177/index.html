Back to basics: Earthquake and Seismic Activity

An earthquake is the shaking of the Earth's surface caused by the sudden release of energy in the crust, resulting in seismic waves. The magnitude measures the energy released, while the depth affects the intensity of shaking felt on the surface. Regions near tectonic plate boundaries, like Tibet, are prone to earthquakes due to crustal movements.

Mains question (10 marks)

Explain the significance of studying seismic activity in regions like Tibet for disaster management in India.

Explain: Explain how understanding seismic events in neighboring regions helps in disaster preparedness and management in India.

Intro: Seismic activity in regions adjacent to India, such as Tibet, has implications for disaster management in India.

  • Tibet's location near tectonic plate boundaries causes frequent earthquakes, which can impact neighboring areas including India.
  • Studying such seismic events helps in assessing risks and preparing early warning systems for India.
  • Disaster management strategies in India can be improved by learning from the impact and response to earthquakes in Tibet.
  • Regional cooperation and information sharing enhance preparedness for seismic hazards affecting India and its neighbors.

Conclusion: Understanding seismic activity in Tibet is crucial for strengthening India's disaster management framework.
