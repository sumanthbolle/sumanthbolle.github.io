India and Vietnam deepen bilateral defence cooperation

India's National Security Adviser Ajit Doval visited Vietnam in November 2026 to review and strengthen defence ties, focusing on maritime security and strategic partnership. This visit follows the fourth India-Vietnam Security Dialogue and aligns with India's Act East Policy and Indo-Pacific vision, making it relevant for understanding India's regional security diplomacy.

India and Vietnam agreed to deepen defence cooperation, emphasizing maritime security and strategic partnership under India's Act East Policy.

Why is this in the news?

On November 2, 2026, NSA Ajit Doval met Vietnam's Defence Minister to review and enhance bilateral defence cooperation during his official visit to Vietnam.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What was agreed during Ajit Doval's visit to Vietnam?

  • Defence cooperation review. NSA Ajit Doval and Vietnam's Defence Minister General Phan Van Giang reviewed ongoing bilateral defence cooperation and agreed to deepen engagement in the sector.
  • Focus areas. The cooperation emphasizes maritime security, counter-terrorism, cybersecurity, economic security, and emerging technologies.
  • Strategic partnership. The visit aimed to strengthen the India-Vietnam Enhanced Comprehensive Strategic Partnership.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for India's foreign policy?

  • Act East Policy. Vietnam is a key strategic partner under India's Act East Policy and broader Indo-Pacific vision.
  • Regional stability. Both countries emphasized maintaining peace, stability, and freedom of navigation in the Indo-Pacific region.
  • High-level political direction. Doval's visit provides political guidance for implementing outcomes of the India-Vietnam Security Dialogue held in October 2026.

Back to basics: India's Act East Policy

India's Act East Policy is a diplomatic initiative to strengthen economic, strategic, and cultural relations with countries in the Asia-Pacific region, including Southeast Asia. It aims to enhance regional cooperation, connectivity, and security partnerships to support India's broader Indo-Pacific vision.
