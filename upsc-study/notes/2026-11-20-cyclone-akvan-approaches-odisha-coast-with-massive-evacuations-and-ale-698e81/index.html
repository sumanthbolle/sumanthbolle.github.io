Cyclone Akvan Approaches Odisha Coast with Massive Evacuations

Severe Cyclonic Storm Akvan is set to cross the Odisha and Andhra Pradesh coasts near Gopalpur by November 20, 2026, prompting evacuation of over three lakh people and deployment of extensive rescue teams. This event highlights disaster preparedness and management crucial for UPSC aspirants studying climate impact and state response mechanisms.

Cyclone Akvan's approach to Odisha and Andhra Pradesh coasts led to large-scale evacuations and alerts, demonstrating India's disaster management efforts.

Why is this in the news?

Cyclone Akvan, a severe cyclonic storm, is forecast to cross the Odisha and Andhra Pradesh coasts near Gopalpur by noon on November 20, 2026, triggering massive evacuations and alerts.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the current status and forecast of Cyclone Akvan?

  • Cyclone trajectory. Cyclone Akvan is moving north-northwestwards over the west-central Bay of Bengal and is expected to cross the North Andhra Pradesh and South Odisha coasts near Gopalpur by noon on November 20, 2026.
  • Wind and rainfall forecast. The cyclone is expected to bring maximum sustained wind speeds of 90-100 kmph, gusting to 110 kmph, along with heavy to very heavy rainfall in several districts.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How has Odisha prepared for Cyclone Akvan?

  • Evacuations and shelters. Odisha has evacuated nearly three lakh people from vulnerable areas and set up 9,299 cyclone and flood shelters to ensure safety.
  • Rescue and communication readiness. The state deployed 423 rescue teams including Odisha Disaster Rapid Action Force, National Disaster Response Force, and fire service teams, and strengthened telecom infrastructure with mobile generators and satellite phones.
  • Dam and reservoir management. Water Resources Department has opened gates of major and medium irrigation dams to manage water levels and prevent flooding.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the precautionary measures taken by railways and other sectors?

  • Train cancellations and rescheduling. East Coast Railway cancelled 39 trains and rescheduled 28 others, while South Eastern Railway cancelled at least 28 express trains, especially those passing through Puri.
  • Public advisories. Authorities advised fishermen not to venture into the sea and closed tourist sites and beaches, including the Shree Gundicha Temple in Puri, while the Jagannath Temple remains open with rituals continuing.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Which areas are most vulnerable and under alert?

  • District alerts. The India Meteorological Department issued a red alert for Srikakulam and Vizianagaram districts, orange alert for Parvatipuram Manyam, Alluri Sitaramaraju and Anakapalli districts, and yellow alert for Kakinada.
  • Landslip prone zones. Over 941 landslip-prone locations were identified in Odisha, including districts like Gajapati, Ganjam, Rayagada, Kandhamal, Koraput, and Malkangiri, with barricades placed on vulnerable ghat roads.

Back to basics: Cyclone and Disaster Management

A cyclone is a large-scale air mass that rotates around a strong center of low atmospheric pressure, often causing heavy rain and strong winds. Disaster management involves preparedness, response, and recovery measures to reduce the impact of such natural calamities. This includes early warning systems, evacuation plans, shelter arrangements, and coordination among various agencies to protect lives and property.

Mains question (15 marks)

Discuss the importance of disaster preparedness and management in the context of Cyclone Akvan's approach to the Odisha coast.

Discuss: Explain the measures taken for disaster preparedness and their significance in minimizing cyclone impact.

Intro: Disaster preparedness is critical to reduce the adverse effects of natural calamities like cyclones.

  • Early warning and timely evacuation of vulnerable populations help save lives.
  • Deployment of rescue teams and strengthening communication infrastructure ensure effective response.
  • Management of water reservoirs and infrastructure safeguards against flooding and damage.
  • Coordination among government departments and public advisories enhance community resilience.

Conclusion: Effective disaster management reduces casualties and facilitates quicker recovery post-cyclone.
