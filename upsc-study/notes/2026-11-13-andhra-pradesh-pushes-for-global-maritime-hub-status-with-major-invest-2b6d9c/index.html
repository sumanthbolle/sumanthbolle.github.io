Andhra Pradesh's Push for Global Maritime Hub with Major Investments

Andhra Pradesh aims to become a global maritime and logistics hub by leveraging its 1,053-km coastline and strategic location. The state is investing in port infrastructure, shipbuilding, and industrial development, attracting large-scale investments and promoting smart port technologies.

Andhra Pradesh is positioning itself as India's eastern maritime gateway with major port projects, a ₹30,000-crore shipbuilding cluster, and investments exceeding ₹22 lakh crore.

Why is this in the news?

The 31st CII Partnership Summit held in Visakhapatnam on November 12, 2026, showcased Andhra Pradesh's maritime ambitions with announcements of major port projects and investment commitments.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

What are Andhra Pradesh's maritime development plans?

  • Strategic location. Andhra Pradesh has a 1,053-kilometre coastline along the Bay of Bengal, access to key international shipping routes, and proximity to major Asian manufacturing and trading economies, positioning it as a potential maritime gateway to ASEAN, East Asia, and the Indo-Pacific.
  • Port infrastructure expansion. Four greenfield port projects are under construction at Mulapeta, Machilipatnam, Ramayapatnam, and Kakinada SEZ to strengthen cargo-handling capacity and support industrial growth.
  • Shipbuilding cluster. A greenfield shipbuilding cluster at Dugarajapatnam has received in-principle approval under the National Shipbuilding Mission, with an investment of approximately ₹30,000 crore and a targeted annual capacity of 1.2 million gross tonnes.
  • Smart port technology. The state plans to develop smart ports powered by artificial intelligence, predictive analytics, automation, and digital logistics platforms to improve efficiency and competitiveness.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How is Andhra Pradesh attracting investments and industrial growth?

  • Investment commitments. Since June 2024, Andhra Pradesh has attracted ₹22.6 lakh crore in investment commitments, with 73% of projects already receiving regulatory clearances.
  • Major investors. Key investments include Google's upcoming AI hub in Visakhapatnam, the largest FDI of its kind in India, and manufacturing ventures by LG Electronics and Premier Energies.
  • Government support. The state government promotes a pro-business environment by dismantling bureaucratic red tape, offering escrow-backed industrial incentives, and planning an Investor Protection Act.
  • Corporate relocation. Officials urge corporate leaders to relocate their primary headquarters to Andhra Pradesh, highlighting superior quality of life, skilled talent, and connectivity via new ports and Bhogapuram International Airport.

[2] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does Andhra Pradesh's maritime push matter for India?

  • National maritime goals. India aims to become one of the world's top five shipbuilding nations, with ₹70,000 crore allocated for shipbuilding and maritime activities, supporting domestic and international investors.
  • Part of larger initiatives. Andhra Pradesh's development aligns with national programs like Sagarmala, Maritime India Vision 2030, and Maritime Amrit Kaal Vision 2047, which envision ₹80 lakh crore investment in a globally competitive maritime economy.
  • Cargo handling growth. Visakhapatnam Port handled a record 91.17 million tonnes of cargo in FY 2025-26, growing about 10% over the previous year and maintaining growth for four consecutive years.
  • Global investment attraction. Four Global Maritime Investment Summits have attracted approximately ₹25 lakh crore in investments in India's maritime sector, indicating strong investor interest.

Back to basics: Maritime Infrastructure and Port-Led Development

Maritime infrastructure includes ports, shipyards, and logistics facilities that enable sea trade and transport. Port-led development leverages these facilities to boost regional industrial growth, trade, and connectivity. Smart ports use technology like AI and automation to improve efficiency and competitiveness in global shipping.
