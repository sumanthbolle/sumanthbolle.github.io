India's Semiconductor Vulnerabilities Amid Global Chip War

Industry leaders highlight India's gaps in semiconductor manufacturing, materials, and talent amid geopolitical tensions reshaping the global chip industry. Understanding these challenges is key for UPSC aspirants preparing for technology self-reliance and industrial policy topics.

India faces critical semiconductor vulnerabilities in manufacturing, materials, and skilled workforce, requiring focused policy and ecosystem development.

Why is this in the news?

On October 27, 2026, industry leaders at the Semiconductor Summit 2026 in Chennai discussed India's semiconductor vulnerabilities amid the global chip war and geopolitical tensions.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are India's semiconductor vulnerabilities?

  • Gaps in manufacturing. India lacks domestic semiconductor fabrication facilities despite a large market, with major companies not establishing fabs in India over decades.
  • Material shortages. India produces only 4 of the 37 gases and few of the 42 chemicals needed for silicon carbide semiconductor fabrication, creating critical supply dependencies.
  • Skill deficits. The semiconductor workforce lacks expertise beyond chip design, including manufacturing, assembly, testing, materials handling, and clean room operations.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does semiconductor capability matter for India?

  • Technology sovereignty. Semiconductor sovereignty means building resilience and multiple supply-chain options, not producing everything domestically.
  • Strategic industries. Semiconductors are critical for sectors like energy, electric mobility, railways, defence, and data centres, where India needs domestic device manufacturing.
  • Geopolitical risks. U.S.–China tensions and global chip wars expose supply-chain vulnerabilities, urging India to reduce dependencies and build resilient partnerships.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How can India address these semiconductor challenges?

  • Build ecosystem progressively. India should start with outsourced semiconductor assembly and testing (OSAT) and advance to more complex manufacturing over time.
  • Focus on advanced packaging. Advanced packaging technologies integrating multiple chips offer an opportunity for India to narrow the technological gap.
  • Develop domestic materials and recycling. Investing in mining, refining, materials production, and urban mining (recycling) can reduce import dependence.
  • Enhance education and skills. Curricula must cover mechanical, chemical engineering, materials safety, and pollution control to prepare engineers for semiconductor manufacturing.
  • Policy support. Granting semiconductor sector industry status and easing tax burdens during long project lead times can encourage investment.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Can India compete globally in semiconductors?

  • Cost-effective innovation. India's experience in space technology, digital payments, and software shows it can solve complex problems at one-tenth the cost of Western countries.
  • Identify competitive niches. India must find areas of competitive advantage while understanding and mitigating supply-chain vulnerabilities.

Back to basics: Semiconductor manufacturing and supply chains

Semiconductors are essential components in electronic devices, requiring complex manufacturing processes including fabrication, assembly, and testing. The supply chain involves raw materials like specialized gases and chemicals, skilled talent, and advanced equipment. Geopolitical tensions can disrupt supply chains, making domestic capabilities and diversified partnerships important for resilience.

What happens next?

  • Develop domestic materials production. India needs to invest in mining, refining, and producing semiconductor materials locally while securing multiple supply sources.
  • Expand semiconductor education. Curricula should include practical training in manufacturing, materials safety, and pollution control to build a skilled workforce.
  • Policy incentives. Granting industry status and reducing tax burdens for semiconductor ecosystem players can attract investment.
