PM Modi to Attend ASEAN and East Asia Summits in Manila

Prime Minister Narendra Modi will visit Manila from 16 to 17 November 2026 to participate in the 23rd ASEAN-India Summit and the 21st East Asia Summit hosted by the Philippines. The visit focuses on enhancing maritime security cooperation and trade ties, reflecting India's strategic priorities in the Indo-Pacific region.

PM Modi's Manila visit highlights India's commitment to strengthening maritime security and economic cooperation with ASEAN and Indo-Pacific partners.

Why is this in the news?

Prime Minister Modi's visit to Manila from 16-17 November 2026 for the ASEAN-India Summit and East Asia Summit, hosted by the Philippines as ASEAN Chair, is a key diplomatic event focusing on maritime security and trade.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the purpose of PM Modi's visit to Manila?

  • Summit participation. PM Modi will attend the 23rd ASEAN-India Summit and the 21st East Asia Summit on 17 November 2026, hosted by the Philippines as the ASEAN Chair.
  • Focus areas. The visit aims to boost maritime security cooperation and enhance trade and investment ties with ASEAN countries.
  • Strategic context. India-ASEAN relations are central to India's Act East Policy and vision for the Indo-Pacific, with 2026 designated as the ASEAN-India Year of Maritime Cooperation.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this visit matter for India's foreign policy?

  • Maritime security importance. The visit emphasizes deeper maritime cooperation among like-minded Indo-Pacific nations to address regional security challenges.
  • Economic ties. Enhancing trade and investment ties with ASEAN supports India's economic engagement in a key regional grouping.
  • Regional cooperation. The East Asia Summit provides a platform to discuss regional and global issues with ASEAN and other major powers including the US, China, Japan, and Australia.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the key diplomatic engagements during the visit?

  • Bilateral meetings. PM Modi will hold bilateral meetings and attend a dinner hosted by the Philippine President for visiting dignitaries.
  • Potential Quad summit. Modi is expected to join leaders of Australia, the US, and Japan for the Quad summit in Manila, though no official confirmation is available.
  • Community outreach. There is a strong demand from the Indian community in the Philippines to meet the Prime Minister during his visit.

Back to basics: ASEAN and East Asia Summit

The Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN) is a regional grouping of 10 Southeast Asian countries promoting economic, political, and security cooperation. The East Asia Summit is a forum including ASEAN members and key partners like India, China, Japan, and the US, focusing on strategic and economic issues in the Asia-Pacific region. These summits facilitate dialogue and cooperation on regional security, trade, and development.
