Raksha Mantri Highlights Indigenous Defence and Maritime Cooperation

Raksha Mantri Rajnath Singh emphasized indigenous defence production as key to military preparedness and called for stronger maritime cooperation amid security challenges. UPSC aspirants should note the interconnected pillars of defence reforms, self-reliance, collaborations, and exports, alongside India's strategic maritime vision in the Indo-Pacific.

Indigenous defence capabilities and resilient maritime cooperation are central to India's strategic autonomy and security in the 21st century.

Why is this in the news?

Raksha Mantri Rajnath Singh addressed the Delhi Defence Dialogue on November 3, 2026, and the Indo-Pacific Regional Dialogue on November 4, 2026, highlighting indigenous defence production and maritime cooperation.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

What is the significance of indigenous defence production for India?

  • Technological sovereignty. Indigenous production ensures India’s critical defence needs are not dependent on external sources, achieving technological sovereignty through design, manufacturing, intellectual property, and lifecycle sustainment.
  • Interconnected pillars. Defence reforms, Aatmanirbharta (self-reliance), collaborations, and exports are mutually reinforcing pillars aimed at making India a developed nation by 2047.
  • Recent initiatives. Initiatives include Defence Industrial Corridors, corporatisation of Ordnance Factory Board into seven DPSUs, and increased private sector participation in advanced defence projects.
  • Production and exports growth. Defence production reached approximately ₹1.78 lakh crore in FY 2025-26, with exports rising to ₹38,424 crore, supplying over 80 countries.
  • Future targets. The government aims for ₹3 lakh crore in defence production and ₹50,000 crore in defence exports by 2029.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does maritime cooperation matter for India’s security and development?

  • Maritime inter-dependence. Diversification of supply chains, reliable maritime infrastructure, and enhanced cooperation are needed to make regional inter-dependence secure and resilient amid a strained maritime security environment.
  • Evolving maritime threats. Maritime security challenges include conventional military threats and non-traditional threats like piracy, trafficking, illegal fishing, cyber-attacks, and natural disasters.
  • Technology and equity. Emerging technologies such as AI, autonomous systems, and new sensors must be used wisely to address real challenges without increasing inequality among Indo-Pacific nations.
  • Maritime ecosystem and vision. The Maritime Amrit Kaal Vision 2047 aims to develop a comprehensive maritime ecosystem through port infrastructure, coastal economic activities, and maritime industrial base strengthening.
  • Regional cooperation frameworks. India supports rule-based maritime order and initiatives like MAHASAGAR and the Indo-Pacific Oceans Initiative for collective security and prosperity.

[2] Press Information Bureau

How do defence reforms and collaborations support India’s strategic autonomy?

  • Reforms for enabling environment. Defence reforms focus on jointness, integration, emerging technologies, innovation, and ease of doing business to strengthen the domestic defence ecosystem.
  • Collaborations for advanced technology. International partnerships are encouraged based on co-development, co-production, shared innovation, intellectual property sharing, and global supply chain participation.
  • Role of Defence Forces in R&D. Operational experience of Defence Forces informs research priorities, but R&D must also pursue unconventional ideas beyond immediate operational needs.
  • Exports as strategic partnerships. Defence exports reflect industrial maturity and competitiveness, and successful exports should build enduring strategic partnerships including maintenance and technology cooperation.

Back to basics: Defence Self-Reliance and Maritime Security

Defence self-reliance means developing and sustaining critical defence technologies and manufacturing capabilities within the country to reduce dependence on foreign sources. Maritime security involves protecting sea lanes, ports, and maritime resources from threats, ensuring safe and uninterrupted trade and strategic stability. Both concepts are crucial for national security and economic development in a globalised and interconnected world.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about India's defence and maritime strategy: 1. Defence reforms, Aatmanirbharta, collaborations, and exports are interconnected pillars for India's defence preparedness. 2. The Indo-Pacific Oceans Initiative aims to promote maritime cooperation across seven interconnected areas. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (c). A careless reader might think the Indo-Pacific Oceans Initiative is a national initiative rather than a regional cooperation framework announced by the Prime Minister in 2019.

Mains question (15 marks)

Examine how indigenous defence production and maritime cooperation contribute to India's strategic autonomy and security in the 21st century.

Examine: Explain the role of self-reliance in defence manufacturing and the importance of regional maritime cooperation for India's security and development.

Intro: Indigenous defence production and maritime cooperation are key to India's strategic autonomy and security.

  • Discuss the pillars of defence reforms, Aatmanirbharta, collaborations, and exports in strengthening India's defence industrial base.
  • Explain the evolving nature of maritime security threats and the need for resilient regional cooperation in the Indo-Pacific.
  • Analyse the role of technology, international partnerships, and policy initiatives in advancing defence and maritime capabilities.

Conclusion: Together, indigenous defence production and maritime cooperation enhance India's preparedness and position in global security.
