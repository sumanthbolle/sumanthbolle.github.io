RBI Issues Directions to Cooperative Banks under Banking Regulation Act

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has issued regulatory directions to Puducherry Co-operative Urban Bank Ltd and extended restrictions on The Suri Friends’ Union Co-operative Bank Ltd under Sections 35A and 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949. These actions highlight RBI's role in supervising cooperative banks to protect depositors and maintain financial stability.

RBI uses its powers under the Banking Regulation Act to impose operational restrictions on cooperative banks to safeguard depositors and ensure financial discipline.

Why is this in the news?

On October 19 and 22, 2026, RBI issued and extended directions under Sections 35A and 56 of the Banking Regulation Act to Puducherry Co-operative Urban Bank Ltd and The Suri Friends’ Union Co-operative Bank Ltd respectively, imposing operational restrictions.

What directions did RBI issue to Puducherry Co-operative Urban Bank Ltd?

  • Operational restrictions. From October 21, 2026, the bank cannot grant or renew loans, make investments, incur liabilities, accept fresh deposits, or dispose of assets without RBI's prior written approval.
  • Withdrawal limits. Depositors can withdraw up to ₹5,000 from their accounts, and the bank may set off loans against deposits under specified conditions.
  • Essential expenditures allowed. The bank may pay for essential items such as employee salaries, rent, and electricity bills as per RBI directions.
  • Reason for directions. RBI issued these directions due to the bank's poor liquidity and inadequate efforts to address supervisory concerns, aiming to protect depositors.
  • Deposit insurance. Depositors are entitled to insurance claims up to ₹5,00,000 from the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation (DICGC) after verification.
  • License status. These directions do not mean cancellation of the bank's license; the bank continues operations under restrictions until its financial position improves.

Why was the directive period extended for The Suri Friends’ Union Co-operative Bank Ltd?

  • Extension details. RBI extended the directive period for The Suri Friends’ Union Co-operative Bank Ltd by three months from October 22, 2026, to January 22, 2027.
  • Legal basis. The extension was issued under Sections 35A and 56 of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.
  • Public interest. RBI stated the extension is necessary in the public interest but clarified it does not imply satisfaction with the bank's financial position.
  • Unchanged terms. All other terms and conditions of the original directive remain unchanged during the extension period.

Back to basics: Banking Regulation Act and RBI's supervisory role

The Banking Regulation Act, 1949, empowers the Reserve Bank of India to regulate and supervise banking institutions, including cooperative banks. Sections 35A and 56 allow RBI to issue directions to banks to protect depositors' interests and ensure financial stability. Cooperative banks are financial entities owned and operated by their members, often serving local communities, and require regulatory oversight to maintain sound governance and liquidity.
