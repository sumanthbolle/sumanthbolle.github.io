Kartarpur Corridor reopening depends on national security resolution

Union Home Minister Amit Shah stated on November 24, 2026, that the Kartarpur Corridor will reopen once national security concerns are fully addressed. This update is important for understanding India-Pakistan relations and the balance between religious access and security.

The Kartarpur Corridor remains closed due to national security issues, with reopening contingent on their resolution.

Why is this in the news?

On November 24, 2026, Union Home Minister Amit Shah announced that the Kartarpur Corridor reopening is pending resolution of national security concerns following its closure after Operation Sindoor.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why is the Kartarpur Corridor currently closed?

  • Security concerns. The Corridor was closed after Operation Sindoor in May 2025 due to national security concerns related to the prevailing security scenario with Pakistan.
  • Government stance. The Ministry of Home Affairs announced in September 2025 that it would not be possible to send Sikh pilgrims to Pakistan under the current security conditions.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the significance of the Kartarpur Corridor?

  • Religious importance. The Corridor connects Gurdwara Darbar Sahib in Pakistan with Gurdwara Dera Baba Nanak in India and allows visa-free pilgrimage for Sikhs.
  • Historical context. Prime Minister Narendra Modi inaugurated the Corridor in November 2019 to facilitate Sikh pilgrims, reflecting its cultural and spiritual significance.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the political perspectives on the Corridor's status?

  • Government view. Amit Shah criticized the Congress party for past carelessness that led to the Corridor not being part of India and emphasized reopening after security issues are resolved.
  • Opposition appeal. Punjab Congress chief Pargat Singh urged reopening, highlighting the Corridor's importance for faith and heritage while calling for a balance between security and religious freedom.

Back to basics: Kartarpur Corridor

The Kartarpur Corridor is a visa-free border crossing that connects the Sikh holy site of Gurdwara Darbar Sahib in Pakistan to Gurdwara Dera Baba Nanak in India. It was created to facilitate Sikh pilgrims visiting the sacred site without requiring a visa, promoting religious tourism and cultural ties between India and Pakistan. However, its operation depends on bilateral security and diplomatic conditions.
