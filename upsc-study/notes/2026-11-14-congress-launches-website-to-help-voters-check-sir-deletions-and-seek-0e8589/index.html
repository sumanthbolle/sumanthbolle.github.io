Congress launches website for voters to check SIR deletions and seek restoration

The Congress party launched a website, Iamvoterakshak.in, enabling voters to verify if their names were deleted during the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls and to apply for restoration. This initiative is part of Congress's campaign against alleged electoral irregularities, emphasizing voter rights and transparency in the electoral process.

Congress's website Iamvoterakshak.in helps voters check deletions from electoral rolls during SIR and seek restoration.

What is the Congress initiative related to SIR deletions?

  • Website launch. Congress launched Iamvoterakshak.in to allow voters to verify if their names were deleted during the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls and to apply for restoration.
  • Campaign against deletions. The initiative is part of the ongoing 'Vote Bachao, Desh Bachao' campaign against alleged electoral irregularities.
  • Role of Vote Rakshaks. Congress workers, called 'Vote Rakshaks', will visit polling booths to verify deletions from electoral rolls.

  • Restoration process. Voters can formally apply for restoration of their names, addressing concerns about wrongful exclusion from electoral rolls.

Back to basics: Electoral Rolls and Special Intensive Revision (SIR)

Electoral rolls are official lists of eligible voters maintained by the Election Commission. Special Intensive Revision (SIR) is a process to update these rolls, including adding new voters and deleting names of those who are ineligible, to ensure accuracy before elections. Voters must verify their inclusion to exercise their right to vote.
