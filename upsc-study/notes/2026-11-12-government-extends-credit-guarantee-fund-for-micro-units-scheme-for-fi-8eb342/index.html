Government Extends Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units Scheme for Five Years

The Union Cabinet approved the continuation of the Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU) scheme until March 2031 to support loans under the Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana (PMMY). This extension facilitates collateral-free loans worth ₹12.6 lakh crore, promoting entrepreneurship and financial inclusion for micro enterprises.

The CGFMU scheme extension provides credit guarantees for micro enterprise loans under PMMY, enabling ₹12.6 lakh crore in collateral-free lending over five years.

Why is this in the news?

On November 11, 2026, the Union Cabinet approved the continuation of the Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units scheme for five years until March 2031.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units scheme?

  • Purpose of the scheme. The CGFMU provides credit guarantee coverage to lending institutions against default risk for loans extended to eligible micro enterprises under PMMY, overdrafts under PMJDY accounts, and loans to Self Help Groups with portfolios between ₹10 lakh and ₹20 lakh.
  • Guarantee coverage and fee. Guarantee coverage is linked to disbursement amounts for working capital and outstanding amounts for term loans, reflecting actual risk exposure; the guarantee fee has been reduced from 1% to 0.75% per annum for PMMY loans, with no change for SHGs.
  • Administration. The scheme operates through the National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company Limited (NCGTC), which provides the guarantee to lending institutions.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

What are the recent developments regarding the CGFMU scheme?

  • Extension approval. The Union Cabinet approved the continuation of the CGFMU scheme for five years up to March 31, 2031, covering the XVIth Finance Commission cycle (FY 2026-27 to FY 2030-31).
  • Corpus and loan facilitation. A corpus of ₹5,000 crore has been approved to facilitate PMMY loans amounting to ₹12.6 lakh crore over the next five years.
  • Impact so far. Since its launch in January 2016, the scheme has catalyzed the sanctioning of over 60.36 crore loans totaling ₹43.34 lakh crore, enabling a new generation of entrepreneurs to access collateral-free loans.

Why does the CGFMU scheme matter for economic development?

  • Promotes entrepreneurship. By providing collateral-free loans, the scheme helps aspiring entrepreneurs who previously could not access formal banking credit to start and grow their businesses.
  • Supports financial inclusion. The scheme extends credit guarantees to loans under PMMY, overdrafts under PMJDY, and loans to Self Help Groups, thereby supporting vulnerable and underserved sections of the economy.
  • Catalyst for MSME growth. The CGFMU acts as a catalyst for micro, small, and medium enterprises by mitigating lending risks for financial institutions, encouraging more credit flow to the sector.

Back to basics: Credit Guarantee Scheme

A credit guarantee scheme provides a guarantee to lending institutions against the risk of default by borrowers, encouraging banks to lend to sectors or groups that may lack collateral or credit history. This mechanism reduces the risk for lenders, thereby promoting financial inclusion and access to credit for small businesses and vulnerable sections.

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about the Credit Guarantee Fund for Micro Units (CGFMU) scheme: 1. The scheme provides credit guarantee coverage for loans extended under the Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana up to ₹20 lakh. 2. The guarantee fee under the scheme has been increased from 0.75% to 1% per annum for all loans. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the guarantee fee has been decreased from 1% to 0.75% per annum for PMMY loans, not increased.
