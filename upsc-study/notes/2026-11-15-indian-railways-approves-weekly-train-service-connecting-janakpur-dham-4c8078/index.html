Indian Railways Launches Weekly Janakpur Dham-Ayodhya Train Service

Indian Railways approved a weekly train connecting Janakpur Dham in Nepal with Ayodhya in India, strengthening cross-border connectivity and religious tourism. This development highlights India-Nepal cultural ties and regional cooperation, relevant for understanding bilateral relations and infrastructure integration.

The new weekly train service between Janakpur Dham and Ayodhya enhances India-Nepal connectivity by linking two key religious sites associated with the Ramayana.

Why is this in the news?

On November 14, 2026, the Ministry of Railways approved a weekly train service connecting Janakpur Dham in Nepal with Ayodhya in Uttar Pradesh, India.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the new train service between Janakpur Dham and Ayodhya?

  • Service details. Indian Railways approved a weekly train covering approximately 604 kilometres between Janakpur Dham in Nepal and Ayodhya Cantt. in India, passing through towns in Bihar and Uttar Pradesh.
  • Route and stops. The train will stop at Jaynagar, Madhubani, Shisho, Sitamarhi, Raxaul, Narkatiaganj, Gorakhpur, Basti, Mankapur, and Ayodhya Dham besides the terminal stations.
  • Operation days. The service will run once a week, originating from Janakpur Dham on Saturdays and from Ayodhya Cantt. on Mondays.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this train service matter for India-Nepal relations?

  • Cultural and religious significance. The train connects Janakpur Dham, revered as the birthplace of Mata Sita, with Ayodhya, the birthplace of Lord Ram, both key sites in the Ramayana tradition.
  • Strengthening bilateral ties. The service enhances centuries-old cultural, historical, and social bonds by improving cross-border connectivity and facilitating people-to-people contact.
  • Bilateral cooperation. This initiative adds a new dimension to India-Nepal cooperation by using railway infrastructure to bring communities closer.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

How will the train service impact tourism and regional development?

  • Boost to religious tourism. The service facilitates pilgrimages and tourism circuits related to the Ramayana, improving access to prominent religious centres.
  • Economic benefits. Improved rail connectivity is expected to support local economies along the route by creating opportunities for tourism operators, hospitality businesses, traders, and artisans.
  • Regional mobility. The train offers an additional transport option for people in border regions of Bihar and Nepal, aiding travel for education, business, and family purposes.

Back to basics: Cross-border railway connectivity

Cross-border railway connectivity involves establishing train services that link cities or regions across international boundaries to facilitate passenger and freight movement. Such connectivity enhances economic integration, tourism, cultural exchange, and diplomatic relations between neighbouring countries. It requires bilateral agreements and infrastructure coordination to operate smoothly.
