AI's Role in Enhancing Parliamentary Processes and Citizen Participation

Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla highlighted that Artificial Intelligence (AI) can improve parliamentary workflows and deepen citizen engagement if guided by human values and ethics. This development is significant for understanding the integration of technology in governance and democratic processes.

AI can optimize parliamentary functions and enhance citizen participation when deployed with transparency, ethics, and accountability.

Why is this in the news?

Lok Sabha Speaker Om Birla spoke at the 12th BRICS Parliamentary Forum on October 27, 2026, about AI's potential to strengthen parliamentary processes and citizen participation.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How can AI strengthen parliamentary processes?

  • AI optimizes workflows. AI can improve parliamentary functioning by streamlining workflows and making legislative processes more effective and transparent.
  • Language translation support. The Indian Parliament uses AI to translate parliamentary speeches and papers into multiple Indian languages, aiding legislators from diverse linguistic backgrounds.
  • Enhances dialogue and accountability. Parliaments serve as centers of dialogue, consensus, accountability, and public trust, which AI can support by making processes more citizen-centric.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does AI matter for citizen participation?

  • Deepens engagement. AI and digital technologies can deepen citizen participation by making parliamentary information more accessible and understandable.
  • Guided by ethics. The use of AI must be guided by human values, ethics, transparency, and accountability to ensure responsible deployment.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What global and democratic values are linked to AI use in parliaments?

  • Respect for sovereignty. The BRICS Parliamentary Forum's joint declaration emphasizes the need for open, safe, secure, and equitable use of emerging technologies while respecting national sovereignty and security.
  • Youth engagement and peace. Engaging young people in democratic processes and promoting peaceful dispute resolution are key themes linked to AI's role in governance.

Back to basics: Role of Parliament in democracy

Parliaments are legislative bodies responsible for making laws, ensuring government accountability, and representing citizens' interests. They function as forums for dialogue, consensus-building, and public trust, and their effectiveness depends on transparency and citizen engagement.
