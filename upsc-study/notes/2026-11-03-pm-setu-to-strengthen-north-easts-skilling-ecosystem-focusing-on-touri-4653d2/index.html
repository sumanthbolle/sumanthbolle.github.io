PM-SETU to Boost North-East Skilling in Tourism and Hospitality

The Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship launched initiatives under PM-SETU to transform ITIs in the North-East, focusing on tourism and hospitality sectors for global employment. This development is key for UPSC aspirants studying regional development, skill policies, and employment generation.

PM-SETU aims to modernize ITIs in the North-East to enhance youth employability in tourism and hospitality, linking skill development with industry and international standards.

Why is this in the news?

On November 2, 2026, the Ministry of Skill Development and Entrepreneurship held a programme in Guwahati focusing on PM-SETU and international mobility, emphasizing tourism and hospitality sectors in the North-East.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the PM-SETU initiative in the North-East?

  • Programme focus. PM-SETU aims to transform Industrial Training Institutes (ITIs) in the North-East through industry-led modernization, aligning skill development with emerging industry needs and global standards.
  • Key sectors targeted. Tourism and hospitality have been identified as priority sectors due to their potential for domestic and international employment opportunities.
  • Strategic partnerships. MoU signed between MSDE and Assam government for setting up a Skill India International Centre (SIIC), and Letters of Intent signed for ITI clusters in Jorhat and Tinsukhia supported by Numaligarh Refinery Limited and Oil India Limited.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does PM-SETU matter for the North-East's development?

  • Youth employment. The initiative aims to equip North-Eastern youth with industry-relevant skills, enhancing their employability in growing sectors both within India and globally.
  • Industry collaboration. Bringing industry closer to skill development ensures ITIs become demand-driven institutions responsive to future work opportunities.
  • Regional potential. The North-East's tourism and hospitality sectors have enormous growth potential, which can be harnessed through a robust skilling ecosystem.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does PM-SETU align with international employment opportunities?

  • International mobility. The programme discussed expanding global workforce mobility through skill development aligned with international standards and initiatives like WorldSkills.
  • Global skill centers. Assam's vision for a Global Skill Center and the CM FLIGHT Scheme aim to connect the regional workforce with emerging international employment markets.

Back to basics: Skill Development and Employability

Skill development involves training individuals to acquire specific competencies required by industries. Employability refers to the ability of a person to gain and maintain employment. Government initiatives often focus on aligning vocational training institutions like ITIs with industry needs to enhance youth employment prospects.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about PM-SETU and its focus in the North-East: 1. PM-SETU aims to transform ITIs through industry-led modernization focusing on tourism and hospitality sectors. 2. The Government of Assam signed an MoU with MSDE to establish a Skill India International Centre (SIIC). Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (c). A trap could be confusing the MoU for SIIC with other unrelated agreements or assuming PM-SETU covers all sectors equally rather than focusing on tourism and hospitality in the North-East.
