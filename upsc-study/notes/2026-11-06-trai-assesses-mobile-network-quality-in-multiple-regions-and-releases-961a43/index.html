TRAI Releases Mobile Network Quality Reports for Multiple Regions

The Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) conducted Independent Drive Tests in July and September 2026 across Nuh & Palwal (Haryana), Mumbai, and parts of Lohit district (Arunachal Pradesh) to assess mobile network quality. These reports inform consumers and encourage telecom service providers to improve service quality, relevant for understanding telecom regulation and digital infrastructure governance.

TRAI's Independent Drive Tests in 2026 evaluated mobile network performance across key Indian regions, highlighting coverage, call quality, and data speeds to guide service improvements.

Why is this in the news?

TRAI released findings of Independent Drive Tests conducted in 2026 across Nuh & Palwal, Mumbai, and Lohit district to assess mobile network quality for consumer information and telecom service improvement.

What is the purpose and methodology of TRAI's Independent Drive Tests?

  • Purpose. The Independent Drive Tests (IDTs) assess and verify real-time quality of mobile network services, including voice and data, provided by Telecom Service Providers (TSPs) to inform consumers and encourage service improvements.
  • Methodology. TRAI uses SIM cards from all TSPs over 2G, 3G, 4G, and 5G networks with multiple advanced test handsets to establish live data and voice sessions, monitored and analysed in real-time using advanced software systems.
  • Test Environments. Tests cover diverse usage environments such as city drives, hotspots, walk tests, public transport hubs, and highways to capture comprehensive network performance.

What were the key findings of the mobile network quality assessments in Nuh & Palwal?

  • Coverage Gap. Coverage gaps (samples with poor signal strength) varied among providers; for example, in Nuh district, Airtel had 346/15867 poor samples, BSNL 4021/15424, RJIL 784/15626, and VIL 327/15836.
  • Call Quality. Dropped calls were low for Airtel and VIL (0 in Nuh), while BSNL had 5 dropped calls out of 140; call silence instances over 3 seconds ranged from 5 to 35 among providers.
  • Data Speeds. Average download speeds in Nuh ranged from 6.78 Mbps (BSNL) to 130.28 Mbps (RJIL); upload speeds ranged from 6.21 Mbps (BSNL) to 14.26 Mbps (Airtel).

[1] Press Information Bureau

How did mobile network quality vary in Mumbai LSA according to TRAI's report?

  • Coverage Gap. Airtel, RJIL, and VIL showed zero coverage gaps in Mumbai, while MTNL had 13057 poor samples out of 3687, indicating data inconsistency or reporting issue.
  • Call Quality. Dropped calls were minimal, with Airtel having 1/510 and VIL 0/509; call silence instances were low, with 2 for Airtel and 1 for VIL.
  • Data Speeds. Average download speeds were 133.86 Mbps for Airtel, 248.93 Mbps for RJIL, and 89.04 Mbps for VIL; MTNL recorded 0 Mbps download and upload speeds.

[2] Press Information Bureau

What were the findings for mobile network quality in Lohit district and NH-13 corridor in Arunachal Pradesh?

  • Coverage Gap. Coverage gaps were significant; for city drives, Airtel had 8393/37595 poor samples, BSNL 13481/31483, RJIL 10750/37912, and VIL 13557/22487.
  • Call Quality. Dropped calls in city areas ranged from 1 (RJIL) to 17 (VIL) out of total calls; on highways, dropped calls were also notable, e.g., 15/50 for BSNL.
  • Data Speeds. Average city download speeds ranged from 2.27 Mbps (BSNL) to 103.49 Mbps (RJIL); highway speeds were slightly lower but comparable.

Back to basics: Telecom Quality of Service (QoS) Parameters

Telecom Quality of Service parameters measure the performance of mobile networks and include coverage (signal strength), call drop rate, call setup success rate, call silence instances, packet loss, and data throughput speeds. These parameters help regulators assess network reliability and user experience to ensure service providers meet standards.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about TRAI's Independent Drive Tests (IDTs) in 2026: 1. IDTs assess mobile network quality by measuring parameters like coverage gap, call drop rate, and data throughput across various technologies including 2G to 5G. 2. The tests are conducted only in urban areas and exclude highways or rural regions. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the IDTs include diverse environments such as cities, hotspots, highways, and rural areas, not only urban areas.

Mains question (15 marks)

Examine the role of the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India (TRAI) in ensuring mobile network quality and its impact on digital infrastructure governance.

Examine: Explain how TRAI's monitoring of mobile network quality through Independent Drive Tests influences telecom regulation and digital infrastructure development.

Intro: TRAI monitors mobile network quality to safeguard consumer interests and promote service improvements.

  • TRAI conducts Independent Drive Tests assessing key Quality of Service parameters like coverage, call drop rate, and data speeds across multiple technologies and regions.
  • These assessments provide transparent information to consumers and incentivize Telecom Service Providers to enhance network performance.
  • By covering diverse environments including urban, rural, and highway areas, TRAI ensures comprehensive evaluation of telecom services.
  • The data from these tests supports policy decisions and regulatory actions to strengthen digital infrastructure and connectivity.

Conclusion: Thus, TRAI's quality assessments are integral to regulating telecom services and advancing India's digital infrastructure.
