Supreme Court to Hear Contempt Pleas in Thirupparankundram Lamp-Lighting Row

The Supreme Court scheduled a hearing on November 27, 2026, for contempt petitions related to the failure to comply with its November 20 order to light the Karthigai Deepam lamp at Deepathoon on Thirupparankundram Hill. This case is significant for UPSC aspirants as it involves judicial enforcement of court orders in religious disputes and highlights the role of the judiciary in upholding the rule of law.

The Supreme Court is hearing contempt pleas over non-compliance with its order to light the Karthigai Deepam lamp at Thirupparankundram Hill, underscoring judicial authority in religious matters.

Why is this in the news?

On November 26, 2026, the Supreme Court agreed to list contempt petitions concerning the failure to light the Karthigai Deepam lamp at Thirupparankundram Hill as per its November 20 order.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What triggered the Supreme Court contempt proceedings?

  • Non-compliance with court order. The Tamil Nadu authorities and temple administration failed to light the Karthigai Deepam lamp at Deepathoon on November 24, 2026, as directed by the Supreme Court's November 20 order.
  • Petitions filed. Multiple contempt petitions were filed seeking action against state officials and temple authorities for not complying with the court order and for allegedly obstructing the lamp-lighting ceremony.
  • Role of government officials. The Supreme Court sought a personal affidavit from the Tamil Nadu Chief Secretary detailing the government's decision-making and any role played by the Chief Minister in the failure to comply.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this case matter for judicial authority and religious disputes?

  • Judicial enforcement. The case illustrates the Supreme Court's use of contempt proceedings and plenary powers under Article 142 of the Constitution to enforce its orders, especially in sensitive religious contexts.
  • Balancing religious customs and law. The dispute involves traditional religious practices and the court's intervention to ensure compliance with legal orders without infringing on religious sentiments.
  • Transparency and accountability. The court's demand for detailed government records and supervision of the lamp-lighting ceremony reflects efforts to maintain transparency and accountability in government actions.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the key legal and procedural aspects involved?

  • Contempt petitions and suo motu action. Petitions urged the court to initiate suo motu criminal contempt proceedings against officials and temple authorities for violating its order.
  • Use of Article 142 powers. The petitioners requested the court to use its plenary powers under Article 142 to direct the lighting of the lamp under official supervision within seven days.
  • Preservation of evidence. The court sought immediate preservation and production of all physical and electronic records related to the implementation of its November 20 order.

Back to basics: Contempt of Court and Article 142

Contempt of court refers to actions that disrespect or disobey court orders, undermining the authority of the judiciary. Courts can initiate contempt proceedings to enforce compliance. Article 142 of the Constitution empowers the Supreme Court to pass any order necessary for doing complete justice in any case before it, including enforcing its directions in sensitive matters.
