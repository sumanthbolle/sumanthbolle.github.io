Northeast Monsoon Arrives in Tamil Nadu Bringing Widespread Rain

The Northeast monsoon is expected to set in over Tamil Nadu from October 15, 2026, bringing widespread rainfall until October 20. This weather shift is significant for agriculture, water resources, and disaster preparedness in the state.

The Northeast monsoon arrival in Tamil Nadu from October 15, 2026, will bring widespread rain and end a prolonged spell of high temperatures.

Why is this in the news?

The Regional Meteorological Centre forecasted on October 14, 2026, that the Northeast monsoon would commence over Tamil Nadu on October 15, bringing widespread rainfall until October 20.

What is the status of the Northeast monsoon in Tamil Nadu?

  • Monsoon onset date. The Northeast monsoon is expected to set in over Tamil Nadu on October 15, 2026.
  • Rainfall pattern. Fairly widespread rainfall is forecasted across the state until October 20, 2026.
  • Temperature impact. The rainfall will bring temperatures closer to the normal range, ending a prolonged spell of soaring heat.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How is the Northeast monsoon arrival indicated?

  • Wind pattern change. The reversal of wind patterns to Easterlies signals the arrival of the Northeast monsoon.
  • Rainfall observations. South coastal Tamil Nadu has already begun receiving fairly widespread rain, which is expected to spread northwards.
  • Temperature readings. Before the monsoon, interior districts recorded high temperatures, such as Erode with 37.8°C on October 14, 2026.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does the Northeast monsoon matter for Tamil Nadu?

  • Agricultural importance. The Northeast monsoon provides critical rainfall for agriculture in Tamil Nadu, supporting crop growth after the dry season.
  • Water resources. The monsoon replenishes water reservoirs and groundwater, essential for drinking water and irrigation.
  • Disaster preparedness. Heavy to very heavy rainfall over isolated coastal and Western Ghats districts requires monitoring for flood and landslide risks.

Back to basics: Northeast Monsoon

The Northeast monsoon is a seasonal wind pattern that brings rain to the southeastern parts of India, especially Tamil Nadu, typically from October to December. It occurs when the wind direction reverses to blow from the northeast, carrying moisture from the Bay of Bengal. This monsoon is crucial for agriculture and water supply in the region, distinct from the Southwest monsoon that affects most of India earlier in the year.
