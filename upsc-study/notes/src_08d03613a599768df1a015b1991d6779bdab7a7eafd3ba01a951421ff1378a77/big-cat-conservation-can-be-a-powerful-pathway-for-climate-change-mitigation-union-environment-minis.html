India implications

  • India can frame tiger and other big-cat landscapes as part of nature-based climate action, alongside emissions reduction.
  • Landscape protection can support water purification, soil conservation, groundwater recharge and community-linked livelihoods.
  • International cooperation through the International Big Cat Alliance can enable sharing of Indian conservation models and capacity-building among range countries.
