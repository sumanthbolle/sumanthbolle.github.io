PM to inaugurate First SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum in Delhi

The Prime Minister will inaugurate the inaugural Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) Civilisational Dialogue Forum in New Delhi from 20 to 22 November 2026, with Pakistan also participating. This event highlights India's role in promoting cultural dialogue and cooperation within the SCO framework, relevant for understanding India's regional diplomacy.

The First SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum in Delhi aims to strengthen cultural ties among SCO countries, with India hosting and Pakistan participating.

Why is this in the news?

The Prime Minister's Office announced on 19 November 2026 that the PM will inaugurate the First SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum in New Delhi on 20 November 2026.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum?

  • Forum purpose. The Forum is a three-day event from 20 to 22 November 2026 to celebrate the shared civilisational heritage of SCO countries, strengthen people-to-people ties, and promote cross-cultural dialogue and cooperation.
  • Origin of the Forum. The Forum was proposed by the Prime Minister at the SCO Summit in Tianjin, China in 2025 and was welcomed in the Bishkek Declaration of SCO.
  • Event features. The inaugural ceremony includes a cultural show themed 'Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam - The World Is One Family', exhibitions on India's archaeological and literary heritage, and thematic sessions on cultural expressions, art, language, and philosophical traditions.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Who is participating and why does it matter?

  • Pakistan's participation. Pakistan will participate with a delegation led by Federal Minister for Climate Change and Environmental Coordination Dr. Musadik Masood Malik, reflecting its active role in the SCO and commitment to dialogue under the 'Shanghai Spirit'.
  • SCO membership context. Pakistan is the current chair of the SCO Council of Heads of State for 2026-27 and will host several SCO meetings leading to the summit next year.
  • Significance for India-Pakistan relations. The Forum provides a platform for cultural diplomacy between India and Pakistan within the SCO framework, despite ongoing bilateral tensions.

[2] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the cultural and historical highlights of the Forum?

  • Venue significance. The Forum is held at Purana Qila in Delhi, a historic fort with archaeological evidence spanning from 1200 BCE to 1200 CE, showcasing India's ancient past.
  • Exhibitions. Exhibitions include 'ASTITVA – The Essence of Our Existence' featuring archaeological artefacts and the 'Mahabharat Scroll Pavilion - Anant', an 18th-century illustrated Mahabharata scroll.
  • Thematic discussions. Sessions will cover music, performing arts, textiles, cuisine, traditional games, art and architecture, language and literary traditions, and monastic and philosophical trails.

Back to basics: Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO)

The SCO is a regional intergovernmental organisation focused on political, economic, and security cooperation among member states primarily in Eurasia. It promotes multilateral dialogue and collaboration on issues like security, trade, and cultural exchange. Understanding SCO's structure and objectives helps contextualise India's diplomatic engagements and regional cooperation efforts.
