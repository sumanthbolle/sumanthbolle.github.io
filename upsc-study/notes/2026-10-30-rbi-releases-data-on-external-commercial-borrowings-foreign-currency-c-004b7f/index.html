Back to basics: External Commercial Borrowings and Bonds

External Commercial Borrowings (ECBs) are loans raised by Indian entities from foreign sources. Foreign Currency Convertible Bonds (FCCBs) are bonds issued in foreign currency that can be converted into equity shares. Rupee Denominated Bonds (RDBs) are bonds issued outside India but denominated in Indian Rupees. These instruments are important for raising foreign capital and affect the country's external debt and currency stability.
