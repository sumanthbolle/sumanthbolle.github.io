Cyclone Murasu Triggers Red Alert in Telangana's Nagarkurnool District

Cyclone Murasu has intensified into a severe cyclonic storm affecting Telangana's Nagarkurnool district with heavy rains and strong winds, prompting authorities to issue safety advisories and cancel official leaves. Understanding the administrative response and precautionary measures is important for disaster management topics in UPSC.

Cyclone Murasu's severe impact on Telangana's Nagarkurnool district led to red alerts, official preparedness, and public advisories to mitigate damage and ensure safety.

Why is this in the news?

On November 2, 2026, Telangana's Nagarkurnool district was placed under red alert due to Cyclone Murasu intensifying into a severe cyclonic storm, prompting administrative actions and public advisories.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the key impacts and warnings issued for Cyclone Murasu?

  • Cyclone intensity. Cyclone Murasu intensified into a severe cyclonic storm over the west central and southwest Bay of Bengal by early November 2, 2026.
  • Red alert in Nagarkurnool. Nagarkurnool district in Telangana was placed under red alert indicating the likelihood of heavy to very heavy rains on November 3, 2026.
  • Weather hazards. Forecasts included strong gusty winds, lightning, heavy rains, and risks of overflowing streams and ponds.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How did the Nagarkurnool district administration respond to the cyclone threat?

  • Public advisories. People were urged to avoid travelling to Somasila, Nallamalla ghat road, and tourist spots, and to stay away from trees, electric poles, hoardings, tanks, and rivers.
  • Safety measures. Residents in low-lying and unstable houses were advised to shift to safer locations, and traffic was to be stopped in areas with overflowing water bodies.
  • Administrative actions. Officials' leaves were cancelled to ensure availability on the ground, and support centres were set up in all mandals to provide food, drinking water, and health facilities.
  • Helpline. A 24x7 helpline number (98667 56825) was provided for emergency assistance.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for UPSC preparation?

  • Disaster management relevance. The administrative response to Cyclone Murasu exemplifies disaster preparedness, early warning dissemination, and coordinated government action, key topics under disaster management.
  • Climate change adaptation. Understanding cyclone impacts and mitigation measures is important for questions on climate change adaptation and governance.

Back to basics: Disaster Management

Disaster management involves preparing for, responding to, and recovering from natural calamities like cyclones. It includes early warning systems, evacuation plans, public advisories, and coordination among government agencies to minimize loss of life and property.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about Cyclone Murasu and the Nagarkurnool district response: 1. Nagarkurnool district was placed under a red alert due to Cyclone Murasu's intensification into a severe cyclonic storm. 2. The district administration advised people to travel to tourist spots to monitor cyclone impact. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect as the administration advised against travelling to tourist spots during the cyclone.

Mains question (15 marks)

Examine the role of district administration in managing natural disasters with reference to Cyclone Murasu in Telangana.

Examine: Discuss how district-level administrative actions contribute to disaster preparedness and response, using Cyclone Murasu as an example.

Intro: Disaster management at the district level is crucial for mitigating the impact of natural calamities.

  • Early warning dissemination and public advisories help reduce risks to life and property.
  • Setting up support centres and cancelling official leaves ensures readiness and resource availability.
  • Evacuation and shifting of vulnerable populations protect lives in high-risk areas.
  • Coordination among officials and use of helplines facilitate effective emergency response.

Conclusion: Effective district administration is vital for implementing disaster management strategies on the ground.
