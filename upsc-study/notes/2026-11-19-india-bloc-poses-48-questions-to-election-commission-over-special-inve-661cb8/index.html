INDIA Bloc Questions Election Commission on Special Intensive Revision

The INDIA bloc has posed 48 questions to the Election Commission of India (ECI) regarding the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls, seeking transparency on the decision-making and authorization process. This matter is important for UPSC aspirants as it involves the accountability of a constitutional body and the integrity of electoral processes.

The INDIA bloc's 48 interrogatories to the Election Commission challenge the authorization and procedural transparency of the Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls.

Why is this in the news?

The INDIA bloc leaders, including Rahul Gandhi, have approached the Supreme Court with 48 questions for the Election Commission concerning the Special Intensive Revision of electoral rolls, seeking to make the Commission's decision-making process public as of November 2026.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the INDIA bloc's concerns about the Special Intensive Revision?

  • Questions on authorization. The INDIA bloc wants to know how the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) was authorized and whether the Election Commission acted collectively in approving it.
  • Approval of procedural changes. They seek clarity on who approved changes to voter registration procedures and digital systems during the SIR.
  • Demand for transparency. The 48 interrogatories aim to make the decision-making process of the Election Commission regarding SIR public.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for the Election Commission and electoral integrity?

  • ECI's accountability. The questions challenge the Election Commission's accountability as a constitutional body responsible for free and fair elections.
  • Electoral roll accuracy. The SIR is a nationwide exercise to revise electoral rolls, so procedural transparency affects voter confidence.
  • Legal scrutiny. The Supreme Court's decision to hear the application highlights the legal importance of transparency in electoral processes.

Back to basics: Election Commission of India and Electoral Rolls

The Election Commission of India is a constitutional body responsible for administering elections and maintaining accurate electoral rolls. Electoral rolls list eligible voters and are periodically revised to include new voters and remove ineligible entries. Special Intensive Revision (SIR) is a comprehensive exercise to update these rolls nationwide, requiring authorization and procedural clarity to ensure transparency and fairness.
