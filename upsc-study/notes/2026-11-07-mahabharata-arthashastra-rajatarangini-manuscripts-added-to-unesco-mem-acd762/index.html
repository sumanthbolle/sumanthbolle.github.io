Mahabharata, Arthashastra, Rajatarangini Manuscripts Added to UNESCO Memory of the World Register

Manuscripts of the Mahabharata, Arthashastra, and Rajatarangini were inscribed on UNESCO's Memory of the World Asia-Pacific Regional Register in November 2026. This recognition highlights India's rich civilisational heritage and its contributions to historical inquiry, philosophy, and governance.

UNESCO's inclusion of these manuscripts marks global recognition of India's intellectual and documentary heritage.

Why is this in the news?

The inscriptions were announced on November 5, 2026, at the 11th Session of the Memory of the World Asia-Pacific Regional Register held in Jakarta, Indonesia.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What manuscripts were added to the UNESCO Memory of the World Register?

  • Manuscripts inscribed. The manuscripts of the Mahabharata, Kautilya’s Arthashastra, and Kalhana’s 12th-century Rajatarangini were added to the register.
  • Official names and locations. The Mahabharata manuscript is housed at the Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute, Pune; Arthashastra at the Oriental Research Institute, Mysuru; and Rajatarangini at the Oriental Research Library, Srinagar.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this inscription matter for India and UPSC aspirants?

  • Recognition of heritage. The inscriptions mark a landmark recognition of India’s extraordinary documentary and civilisational heritage on a global platform.
  • Intellectual contributions. These works embody India’s traditions of historical inquiry, philosophical thought, and statecraft, reflecting its knowledge traditions in history, ethics, philosophy, Dharma, and governance.
  • India’s growing global recognition. With these additions, India’s total inscriptions in the regional register reach six, indicating increasing international acknowledgement of its cultural legacy.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How was the inscription process supported?

  • Role of IGNCA. The Indira Gandhi National Centre for the Arts played a key role in preparing the nomination dossiers for these manuscripts.
  • Decision venue. The decision was taken at the 11th Session of the Memory of the World Asia-Pacific Regional Register held in Jakarta, Indonesia.

Back to basics: UNESCO Memory of the World Programme

The UNESCO Memory of the World Programme aims to preserve and promote documentary heritage of global significance. It identifies and registers valuable manuscripts, archives, and documents to ensure their protection and wider accessibility. The Asia-Pacific Regional Register is a regional subset recognizing important heritage from this geographical area.
