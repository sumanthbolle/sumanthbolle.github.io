Right to Choose a Partner Is Constitutional Freedom, Not Family Privilege

Thol. Thirumavalavan emphasized that choosing a partner is a constitutional right, not a privilege granted by family, caste, or community. This highlights the need for legal protection against honour killings and related crimes, relevant for understanding personal liberty and social justice in India.

The right to choose one’s partner is an aspect of constitutional freedom and dignity, not a family or community privilege.

Why is this in the news?

On November 26, 2026, Thol. Thirumavalavan submitted a memorandum to the Tamil Nadu Commission for the Prevention of Honour Killings asserting that partner choice is a constitutional freedom.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the constitutional significance of the right to choose a partner?

  • Constitutional freedom. Choosing a partner is a constitutional freedom and an expression of individual dignity, not a privilege granted by family, caste, or community.
  • Individual autonomy. The right to choose a partner reflects individual autonomy protected under constitutional rights.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What legal measures did Thirumavalavan propose against honour killings?

  • Comprehensive law. He urged the enactment of a comprehensive Tamil Nadu law against honour killings and honour-based crimes with a broad definition based on the right to choose a partner.
  • Accountability framework. He called for a framework to hold all participants in honour violence criminally responsible, including instigators and facilitators.
  • Police and official duties. Enforceable duties and penalties should be imposed on police and officials who fail to protect couples or neglect complaints.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What protections did he recommend for victims and witnesses?

  • Protection measures. He demanded comprehensive protection including safehouses, relocation, legal aid, counselling, medical care, compensation, and rehabilitation for couples, survivors, witnesses, and their families.
  • Government accountability. He appealed for a draft Bill with time-bound implementation, budget allocations, monitoring, and annual legislative reporting.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Back to basics: Fundamental Rights and Personal Liberty

The Indian Constitution guarantees fundamental rights that protect individual freedoms and dignity, including the right to life and personal liberty. These rights ensure that individuals have autonomy over personal decisions such as choosing a life partner, free from discrimination or coercion by family or community. Legal protections against crimes like honour killings support these constitutional guarantees by safeguarding personal liberty and equality.
