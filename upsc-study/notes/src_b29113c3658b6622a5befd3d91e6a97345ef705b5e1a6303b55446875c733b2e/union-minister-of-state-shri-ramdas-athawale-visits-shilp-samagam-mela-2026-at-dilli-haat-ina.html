The Press Information Bureau reported on 6 November 2026 that Union Minister of State Shri Ramdas Athawale visited Shilp Samagam Mela 2026 at Dilli Haat, INA. Static anchor

Inclusive livelihood promotion means enabling disadvantaged producers to participate in markets through reduced entry barriers, direct consumer access and support that can convert visibility into sustained economic opportunity.

Background

  • The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment organises Shilp Samagam Melas for artisans and beneficiaries belonging to Scheduled Castes, Other Backward Classes, Safai Karamcharis and persons with disabilities.
  • The 2026 mela at Dilli Haat, INA is organised around the theme ‘प्रकाश और परंपरा – ज्योति से ज्योति जलाएँ’ and uses seven thematic zones covering crafts, food, decoration, worship-related products and cultural performances.
  • The mela also connects artisans with wider markets through initiatives such as Bharat TULIP and provides a platform for cultural expression alongside product marketing.
