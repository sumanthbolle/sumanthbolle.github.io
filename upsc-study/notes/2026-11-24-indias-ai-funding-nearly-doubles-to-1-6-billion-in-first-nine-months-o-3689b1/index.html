India’s AI Funding Nearly Doubles to $1.6 Billion in First Nine Months of 2026

India's AI companies raised $1.6 billion in equity funding in the first nine months of 2026, marking a 96% increase from 2025. This surge highlights the growing investor interest in AI and India's rising position globally in AI funding.

India's AI funding nearly doubled to $1.6 billion in the first nine months of 2026, driven by fewer but larger deals and Mumbai overtaking Bengaluru as the top recipient city.

Why is this in the news?

A Tracxn report released in November 2026 revealed that India’s AI funding nearly doubled to $1.6 billion in the first nine months of 2026 compared to the same period in 2025.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the key trends in India’s AI funding in 2026?

  • Funding growth. AI companies in India raised $1.6 billion in equity funding in the first nine months of 2026, a 96% increase compared to the same period in 2025.
  • Deal volume and size. The number of deals fell from 149 in nine months of 2025 to 87 in 2026, but the average deal size more than tripled to $18.5 million from $5.5 million.
  • Concentration of capital. Around 90% of the total capital was raised in just 10 funding rounds, indicating funding is concentrated among a few companies.
  • Stage-wise funding shift. Early-stage and late-stage funding rose by 191% and 4% respectively, while seed-stage funding fell by 42%, showing investor preference for more mature companies.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Which cities and companies led AI funding in India?

  • Mumbai leads. Mumbai overtook Bengaluru as the largest recipient of AI funding, with its share rising from 2% ($15.7 million) in 2025 to 48% ($765 million) in 2026.
  • Bengaluru’s share. Bengaluru-based AI companies raised $711 million in the first nine months of 2026, accounting for 44% of total AI funding, down from 54% in 2025.
  • Top funded companies. Neysa (Mumbai) raised $600 million in a Series B round; Sarvam (Bengaluru) raised $255 million and $90.4 million in two rounds; Yotta raised $150 million in a Series D.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does India rank globally in AI funding?

  • Global position. India ranked ninth among countries in AI funding raised during the first nine months of 2026, accounting for 0.4% of global AI funding.
  • Countries ahead. Countries ahead of India include the US, China, the UK, France, Singapore, Germany, Australia, and Canada.

Back to basics: Equity Funding and Stages of Investment

Equity funding is capital raised by companies by selling shares to investors. It helps companies grow without incurring debt. Funding stages include seed stage (early startup phase), early stage (product development and market entry), and late stage (scaling up operations). Investors prefer later stages for lower risk and proven business models.
