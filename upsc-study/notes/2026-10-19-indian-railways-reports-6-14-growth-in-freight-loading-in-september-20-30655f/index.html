Indian Railways Freight Loading Grows 6.14% in September 2026

Indian Railways reported a 6.14% increase in freight loading in September 2026 compared to the same month in 2025, with notable growth in key commodities. This data reflects the railways' role in supporting economic activity and infrastructure efficiency, important for UPSC economy preparation.

Indian Railways freight loading rose by 6.14% in September 2026, driven by growth in foodgrain, clinker, containers, and coal segments.

Why is this in the news?

On October 13, 2026, the Railway Board announced a 6.14% growth in freight loading for September 2026 over September 2025, highlighting increased freight revenue and commodity segment growth.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What growth did Indian Railways report in freight loading for September 2026?

  • Freight volume increase. Indian Railways loaded 135.35 million tonnes of freight in September 2026, up from 127.52 million tonnes in September 2025, marking a 6.14% growth.
  • Commodity segment growth. Foodgrain loading grew by 25%, clinker by 20%, domestic container freight by 10%, and coal by 9% during September 2026 compared to the previous year.
  • Coal rake supply. The average number of coal rakes supplied per day increased to 443 from 397 in September 2025, an 11.6% rise, supporting power houses.
  • Freight revenue growth. Freight revenue increased by ₹1,266 crore in September 2026 over September 2025, registering a 9.3% growth.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does this growth reflect on Indian Railways' economic role?

  • Indicator of economic activity. Growth in freight loading, especially in essential commodities like foodgrain and coal, indicates increased industrial and agricultural activity.
  • Infrastructure efficiency. The rise in freight volume and revenue shows improved utilization and capacity of railway infrastructure.
  • Cumulative growth. From April to September 2026, freight loading grew by 4.1% and freight revenue by 3.7%, showing sustained performance.

Back to basics: Freight loading in railways

Freight loading refers to the total weight of goods transported by railways over a period. It is a key indicator of economic activity because railways carry bulk commodities essential for industries and agriculture. Growth in freight loading often signals increased production and consumption in the economy. Freight revenue is the income earned by railways from transporting these goods.
