RBI Announces Premature Redemption Price for Sovereign Gold Bond 2020-21 Series I

The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has declared the premature redemption price for the Sovereign Gold Bond (SGB) 2020-21 Series I, due on October 28, 2026. This update is important for UPSC aspirants as it highlights the operational details of a government-backed financial instrument relevant to the economy and investment sectors.

RBI fixed the premature redemption price of Sovereign Gold Bond 2020-21 Series I at ₹14,159 per unit for redemption due on October 28, 2026.

Why is this in the news?

On October 27, 2026, the RBI announced the premature redemption price for Sovereign Gold Bond 2020-21 Series I, which is due for redemption on October 28, 2026.

[1] Reserve Bank of India

What is the premature redemption price for Sovereign Gold Bond 2020-21 Series I?

  • Redemption price fixed. The RBI set the premature redemption price at ₹14,159 per unit based on the simple average of the closing gold prices of the previous three business days.
  • Price calculation method. The price is calculated using the simple average of the closing price of 999 purity gold on October 23, 26, and 27, 2026, as published by the India Bullion and Jewellers Association Ltd (IBJA).

[1] Reserve Bank of India

When and how is premature redemption allowed under the SGB scheme?

  • Premature redemption timing. Premature redemption is permitted after the fifth year from the date of issue of the Gold Bond, specifically on the date when interest is payable.
  • Applicable notification. This is in accordance with the Government of India notification dated April 13, 2020, for the SGB 2020-21 Series I issued on April 28, 2020.

Back to basics: Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme

The Sovereign Gold Bond Scheme is a government initiative that allows investors to buy gold in the form of bonds instead of physical gold. These bonds are issued by the Reserve Bank of India on behalf of the Government of India and offer interest on the investment along with the potential for capital appreciation linked to gold prices. Premature redemption is allowed after a specified period, providing liquidity to investors.
