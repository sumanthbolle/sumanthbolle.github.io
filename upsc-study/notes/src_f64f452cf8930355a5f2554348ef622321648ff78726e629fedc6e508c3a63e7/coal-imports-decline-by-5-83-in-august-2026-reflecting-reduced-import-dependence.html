India implications

  • Lower import dependence can support energy-security objectives and reduce exposure to external supply disruptions.
  • The marginal increase in imports by imported-coal-based plants shows that plant design and fuel requirements constrain substitution.
  • Coking-coal imports remain linked to steel-sector raw-material needs because domestic availability is constrained by the quality and characteristics of indigenous reserves.
  • Policy must balance reliable electricity and industrial inputs with environmental sustainability and responsible coal-sector governance.

Way forward

  • Increase domestic coal production while maintaining responsible environmental and social safeguards.
  • Strengthen first-mile connectivity and evacuation infrastructure so produced coal reaches utilities reliably.
  • Monitor thermal power plant stocks and coordinate with the Ministry of Railways, Coal India Limited and its subsidiaries.
  • Use coal-quality management and differentiated planning for domestic coal-based plants, imported-coal-based plants and steel-sector coking coal.
