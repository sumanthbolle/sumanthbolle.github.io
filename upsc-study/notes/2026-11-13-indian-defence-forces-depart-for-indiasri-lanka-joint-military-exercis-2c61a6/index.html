India-Sri Lanka Joint Military Exercise Mitra Shakti 2026 Begins

A 170-member Indian Defence contingent departed for Sri Lanka to participate in the 12th edition of the annual joint military exercise Mitra Shakti 2026, held from 13 to 26 November 2026. This exercise enhances counter-terrorism capabilities and defence cooperation between the two nations, relevant for understanding India’s neighbourhood security and defence diplomacy.

Mitra Shakti 2026 is an annual India-Sri Lanka joint military exercise focusing on counter-terrorism operations and strengthening bilateral defence ties.

Why is this in the news?

On 13 November 2026, the Indian Defence Forces contingent departed for Sri Lanka to participate in the 12th edition of the India–Sri Lanka joint military exercise Mitra Shakti 2026.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is Exercise Mitra Shakti 2026?

  • Annual bilateral exercise. Mitra Shakti is an annual joint military exercise conducted alternately in India and Sri Lanka to enhance cooperation between their armies.
  • Focus on counter-terrorism. The 14-day exercise focuses on counter-terrorism operations in semi-urban environments under a United Nations mandate.
  • Participating forces. The Indian contingent includes 170 personnel mainly from a battalion of the Rajputana Rifles, other arms and services, and the Indian Air Force.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does Mitra Shakti 2026 enhance military cooperation?

  • Interoperability and experience exchange. The exercise strengthens interoperability, allows exchange of operational experiences, and sharing of best practices in counter-terrorism.
  • Training components. Training includes joint planning, tactical operations rehearsal, road block operations, urban patrolling, convoy protection, area ambush, crowd dispersal, and combined arms operations.
  • Validation exercise. It culminates in a comprehensive validation exercise to jointly apply tactics, techniques, and procedures practiced.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does Mitra Shakti matter for India’s neighbourhood security?

  • Strengthening defence ties. The exercise reinforces longstanding bonds of friendship and mutual trust between India and Sri Lanka.
  • Regional peace and stability. It contributes to regional peace, stability, and security by enhancing joint military capabilities.

Back to basics: Joint Military Exercises

Joint military exercises are coordinated training events conducted by armed forces of two or more countries to improve cooperation, interoperability, and readiness. They help participating forces share operational experiences, practice combined tactics, and build mutual trust, which is vital for regional security and defence diplomacy.

Mains question (15 marks)

Examine the role of joint military exercises like Mitra Shakti in enhancing India's defence diplomacy and regional security in its neighbourhood.

Examine: Explain how joint exercises contribute to defence cooperation and regional stability, using Mitra Shakti as an example.

Intro: Joint military exercises are key tools in defence diplomacy and regional security cooperation.

  • They enhance interoperability and operational coordination between participating countries' armed forces.
  • Exercises like Mitra Shakti focus on counter-terrorism, addressing common security threats in the neighbourhood.
  • Such exercises reinforce bilateral ties, trust, and mutual understanding, contributing to peace and stability.
  • They provide platforms for sharing best practices and improving tactical capabilities under international mandates.

Conclusion: Thus, joint exercises like Mitra Shakti strengthen India's defence diplomacy and promote regional security.
