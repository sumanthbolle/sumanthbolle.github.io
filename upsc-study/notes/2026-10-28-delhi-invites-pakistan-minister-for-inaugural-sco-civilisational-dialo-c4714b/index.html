Delhi to Host First SCO Civilisational Dialogue Including Pakistan Since 2023

India will host the inaugural Civilisational Dialogue Forum of the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) in Delhi from November 20-22, 2026, inviting Pakistan's Culture Minister for the first time since 2023. This event marks a significant step in India-Pakistan engagement within the SCO framework and highlights regional cooperation efforts despite bilateral tensions.

India's hosting of the SCO Civilisational Dialogue with Pakistan's participation reflects a nuanced approach to regional diplomacy and multilateral cooperation.

Why is this in the news?

India invited Pakistan's Minister for National Heritage and Culture to the first SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum in Delhi in November 2026, after a hiatus since 2023.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum and its significance?

  • Forum purpose. The SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum aims to promote awareness and appreciation of the shared civilisational heritage among SCO member countries through thematic sessions on religion, art, language, and cultural traditions.
  • Event details. Scheduled from November 20-22, 2026, the event will be held at Delhi's historic Purana Quila, showcasing India's ancient cultural heritage.
  • Participants. Culture Ministers from the 10 SCO member states, including Pakistan, along with dialogue partners and observers, have been invited.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does Pakistan's participation matter for India and the SCO?

  • First invitation since 2023. This is the first time since 2023 that India has invited a Pakistani minister to an SCO event hosted in India, signaling a cautious reopening of dialogue.
  • Context of bilateral tensions. The invitation comes after the 2025 four-day conflict between India and Pakistan, highlighting the SCO as a platform for engagement despite bilateral issues.
  • India's approach to SCO. India treats the SCO as an important multilateral organisation and engages with it as an entity, maintaining participation even when bilateral relations with Pakistan are strained.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does this event fit into India's broader regional diplomacy?

  • Multilateral engagement. India uses the SCO platform to engage with multiple regional players, including Pakistan, to promote cooperation on cultural and security issues.
  • Contrast with SAARC. India has avoided SAARC meetings in Islamabad since 2016 due to differences with Pakistan but continues to participate in SCO meetings held in Pakistan.
  • Upcoming SCO events. India is preparing for the SCO Heads of State Summit in Pakistan in 2027, showing continued commitment to the grouping despite challenges.

Back to basics: Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO)

The SCO is a regional intergovernmental organisation focused on political, economic, and security cooperation among member states including India, Pakistan, China, Russia, and Central Asian countries. It facilitates dialogue and collaboration on issues like terrorism, cultural exchange, and regional development. The SCO holds summits and meetings to promote multilateral engagement among its members.
