IIT Madras to Train One Million Teachers in AI Literacy Across India

IIT Madras has launched a programme to train one million teachers in AI literacy through partnerships with education organisations, aiming to integrate AI into classroom teaching. This initiative is important for UPSC aspirants as it highlights government efforts to enhance digital education and AI governance in India.

IIT Madras is partnering with educational bodies to train one million teachers in AI literacy to support AI integration in classrooms nationwide.

Why is this in the news?

On November 18, 2026, IIT Madras announced a partnership with education organisations to provide AI literacy training to one million teachers across India.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the IIT Madras AI literacy initiative?

  • Centre of Excellence. Bodhan AI, an IIT Madras-incubated Centre of Excellence in AI for Education, is leading the initiative to train teachers in AI literacy.
  • Scale of training. The programme aims to train one million teachers across India in AI literacy.
  • Partnerships. IIT Madras has partnered with Learning Links Foundation and Vidya Shakti for nationwide implementation, with Centre for Teacher Accreditation (CENTA) supporting rollout in Andhra Pradesh.
  • Programme goal. The initiative seeks to create a scalable model for integrating AI into existing teaching workflows.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for digital education and governance?

  • Enhancing digital education. Training teachers in AI literacy equips them to incorporate AI tools effectively in classrooms, advancing digital education.
  • Supporting AI governance. Building AI literacy among educators contributes to broader AI governance by fostering informed use and understanding of AI technologies.
  • Scalable model. The programme's scalable approach can serve as a blueprint for future AI literacy initiatives in education.

Back to basics: AI Literacy and Digital Education

AI literacy refers to the knowledge and skills needed to understand and use artificial intelligence technologies effectively. Digital education involves using digital tools and technologies to enhance teaching and learning processes. Training teachers in AI literacy helps integrate AI into education, improving learning outcomes and preparing students for a technology-driven world.
