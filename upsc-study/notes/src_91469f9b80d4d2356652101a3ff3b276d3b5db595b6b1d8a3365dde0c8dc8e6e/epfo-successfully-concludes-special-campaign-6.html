India implications

  • For social-security beneficiaries, faster and trackable grievance handling can reduce transaction costs and improve trust in public institutions.
  • EPFO’s experience illustrates how outcome dashboards can combine grievance redress, office management and records governance.
  • The approach should be assessed through reopened complaints, resolution quality, accessibility for digitally excluded users and protection of records before disposal.

Way forward

  • Publish service standards and distinguish closure from satisfactory resolution.
  • Use feedback, reopened-complaint rates and independent sample audits to test quality.
  • Provide assisted, multilingual and offline access so digital systems do not exclude vulnerable users.
  • Link recurring grievances to process reform rather than treating each complaint as an isolated case.
