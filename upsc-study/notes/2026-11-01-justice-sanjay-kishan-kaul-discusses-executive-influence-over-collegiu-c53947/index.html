Justice Sanjay Kishan Kaul on Executive Influence Over Collegium System

Former Supreme Court judge Justice Sanjay Kishan Kaul discusses in his memoir the increasing influence of the executive on the collegium system for judicial appointments. This insight is important for understanding challenges to judicial independence and separation of powers in India.

Justice Kaul’s memoir reveals concerns about the executive’s growing role in the collegium system, highlighting the need for reforms to protect judicial independence.

Why is this in the news?

Justice Sanjay Kishan Kaul’s memoir, Without Fear or Favour, published in November 2026, discusses the executive’s growing influence over the collegium system and judicial appointments.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What does Justice Kaul reveal about the collegium system?

  • Executive influence. Justice Kaul highlights the increasing role of the executive in influencing collegium recommendations for judicial appointments, which traditionally aimed to preserve judicial independence.
  • Delays in appointments. He points out delays in clearing collegium recommendations by the executive, which affect the timely appointment of judges.
  • Need for reform. The memoir stresses the necessity for systemic reforms to address these challenges and safeguard the independence of the judiciary.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for judicial independence and separation of powers?

  • Checks and balances. The collegium system was designed to limit executive interference in judicial appointments, maintaining the separation of powers between the judiciary and the executive.
  • Judicial independence at risk. Growing executive influence can undermine the judiciary’s autonomy, affecting its ability to act as a check on the other branches of government.
  • Implications for governance. Delays and interference in appointments can impact the judiciary’s efficiency and public confidence in the justice system.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What should UPSC aspirants remember about this topic?

  • Collegium system basics. It is a judicial mechanism for appointing judges, intended to ensure independence from the executive.
  • Current challenges. The executive’s growing role and delays in appointments are key issues affecting the collegium system.
  • Reform debates. There is an ongoing discussion on how to reform the collegium system to balance independence with accountability.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Back to basics: Collegium System

The collegium system is a method used in India for appointing judges to the higher judiciary, where senior judges recommend appointments and transfers. It was established to protect judicial independence by reducing executive interference. Understanding this system is essential to grasp the dynamics of separation of powers and judicial autonomy in India.
