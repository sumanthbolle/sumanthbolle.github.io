Political unrest in Bangladesh over Hasina’s return and protests

Violent protests erupted in Bangladesh’s Gopalganj district in November 2026 amid tensions over former Prime Minister Sheikh Hasina’s proposed return from exile in India. These events affect India-Bangladesh relations and regional stability, making them relevant for UPSC preparation.

Political unrest in Bangladesh intensified due to protests against the proposed return of ex-PM Sheikh Hasina and demands for her extradition by the Bangladeshi government.

Why is this in the news?

In November 2026, violent protests occurred in Gopalganj, Bangladesh, over Sheikh Hasina’s planned return from India after fleeing in 2024, raising tensions in bilateral relations.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What triggered the protests in Bangladesh?

  • Hasina’s proposed return. The protests erupted as supporters of the banned Awami League party demonstrated violently in Gopalganj against the government’s refusal to allow Sheikh Hasina’s return from India.
  • Background of Hasina’s exile. Sheikh Hasina fled Bangladesh in August 2024 after a student-led uprising toppled her government, and she has been sentenced to death in absentia for crimes against humanity related to her government’s crackdown on protesters.
  • Government’s stance. The Bangladesh government has sought Hasina’s extradition from India under a bilateral treaty and denied her passport request, linking her return to diplomatic progress with India.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How has the Bangladesh government responded to the unrest?

  • Law enforcement measures. Authorities deployed paramilitary Border Guard Bangladesh alongside riot police in Gopalganj and withdrew key district officials to manage the unrest.
  • Political actions. The Cabinet, chaired by Prime Minister Tarique Rahman, discussed the situation and urged party activists to remain active on the ground to maintain control.
  • Security concerns. The government condemned sabotage and terrorism attempts, including arson attacks on ruling party offices, and vowed to prevent escalation.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for India-Bangladesh relations?

  • Extradition and diplomacy. Bangladesh’s extradition request to India for Hasina is a legal and diplomatic issue affecting bilateral ties and potential high-level visits.
  • Regional stability. The unrest in Hasina’s stronghold and the political tensions pose challenges to regional security and cooperation.
  • Impact on economic relations. Political instability and protests, including those by garment workers, can disrupt economic activities and cross-border engagements.

Back to basics: Extradition and bilateral relations

Extradition is a legal process where one country surrenders a suspected or convicted criminal to another country under a treaty. It involves legal and diplomatic considerations and can influence bilateral relations. Political asylum and legal proceedings in absentia complicate extradition cases, affecting regional cooperation and stability.
