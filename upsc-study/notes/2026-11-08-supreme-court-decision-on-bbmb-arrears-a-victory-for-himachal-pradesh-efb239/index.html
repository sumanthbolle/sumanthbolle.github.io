Supreme Court Settles BBMB Power Arrears Dispute in Himachal's Favor

The Supreme Court directed Punjab and Haryana to supply 11,973 million units of electricity to Himachal Pradesh as dues from Bhakra-Nangal and Beas projects, resolving a long-standing dispute. This ruling highlights the judiciary's role in inter-state resource conflicts, relevant for federalism and dispute resolution in UPSC exams.

The Supreme Court's order mandates Punjab and Haryana to supply electricity worth ₹10,000-20,000 crore to Himachal Pradesh, settling a decades-old BBMB arrears dispute.

Why is this in the news?

On November 6, 2026, the Supreme Court directed Punjab and Haryana to supply 11,973 million units of electricity to Himachal Pradesh as outstanding dues from Bhakra-Nangal and Beas hydroelectric projects.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What was the Supreme Court's decision on BBMB arrears?

  • Electricity supply order. The Supreme Court directed Punjab and Haryana to supply 11,973 million units of electricity to Himachal Pradesh over 20 years to settle outstanding dues.
  • Dispute settled. This order resolves a decades-old dispute over Himachal Pradesh's 7.19% share in power generated from Bhakra-Nangal and Beas projects.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this decision matter for Himachal Pradesh and UPSC aspirants?

  • Revenue impact. Himachal Pradesh will receive nearly 60 crore units annually during lean seasons, generating estimated revenue between ₹10,000 crore and ₹20,000 crore over 20 years.
  • Annual earnings. Depending on electricity prices, the state could earn up to ₹1,000 crore annually, with a minimum of ₹500 crore at ₹6 per unit.
  • Use of funds. The additional revenue will support education, healthcare, road infrastructure, employment generation, and timely payment of government employees.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does this case illustrate federalism and dispute resolution?

  • Judiciary's role. The Supreme Court acted as the dispute redressal authority between states over resource sharing, exemplifying constitutional mechanisms for federal conflict resolution.
  • Inter-state resource sharing. The case highlights challenges in sharing hydroelectric power generated from rivers crossing multiple states.
  • State rights protection. Himachal Pradesh's sustained legal battle to secure its financial rights shows state efforts to protect interests within the federal structure.

Back to basics: Inter-state dispute resolution in Indian federalism

India's federal structure involves sharing resources like rivers and power projects between states. Disputes over such sharing are resolved through constitutional mechanisms, including adjudication by the Supreme Court. The judiciary acts as a neutral arbiter to ensure equitable distribution and uphold state rights.
