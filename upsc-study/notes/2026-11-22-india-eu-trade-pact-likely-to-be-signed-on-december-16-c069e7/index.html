India-EU Free Trade Agreement Likely to Be Signed on December 16

India and the European Union are expected to sign a Free Trade Agreement (FTA) on December 16, 2026, during Prime Minister Modi's visit to Brussels. This pact will create a large market by providing duty-free access for most Indian exports to the EU and reducing tariffs on certain EU goods, impacting trade and economic relations.

The India-EU FTA will grant duty-free access to 93% of Indian exports to the EU and reduce tariffs on luxury EU imports, coming into force in 2027.

Why is this in the news?

The India-EU Free Trade Agreement is likely to be signed on December 16, 2026, coinciding with Prime Minister Modi's visit to Brussels, marking a major step in bilateral trade relations.

What is the India-EU Free Trade Agreement?

  • FTA definition. The India-EU FTA is a trade pact that will reduce tariffs and provide duty-free access for goods and services between India and the 27-nation European Union.
  • Negotiation timeline. Negotiations for the agreement concluded on January 27, 2026, after more than two decades of discussions.
  • Market size. The agreement will create a market of about 2 billion people, combining India's large economy with the EU's economic bloc.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the key features of the India-EU FTA?

  • Duty-free access. Under the FTA, 93% of Indian shipments will enjoy duty-free access to the EU market.
  • Tariff reduction on imports. Imports of luxury cars and wines from the EU will become less expensive due to reduced tariffs.
  • Economic significance. India and the EU together account for 25% of global GDP and about one-third of international trade.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does the India-EU FTA matter for India’s economy and trade?

  • Trade partner importance. The EU is India's largest trading partner in goods among individual blocs, making this agreement crucial for trade expansion.
  • Services trade. India ranks as the EU's ninth-largest goods trading partner globally and sixth in services, highlighting the importance of this pact for services trade.
  • Economic integration. The FTA will enhance economic integration, potentially boosting exports, investments, and bilateral cooperation.

Back to basics: Free Trade Agreement (FTA)

A Free Trade Agreement is a pact between two or more countries to reduce or eliminate tariffs, import quotas, and preferences on most goods and services traded between them. FTAs aim to increase trade and economic cooperation by making it easier and cheaper to exchange products and services. They often cover various sectors and include provisions on intellectual property, investment, and dispute resolution.
