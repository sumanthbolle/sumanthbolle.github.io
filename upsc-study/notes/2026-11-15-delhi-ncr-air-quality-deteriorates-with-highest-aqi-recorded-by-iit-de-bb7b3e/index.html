Delhi-NCR Air Quality Hits Very Poor Levels with IIT Delhi Recording Highest AQI

On November 15, 2026, Delhi's air quality deteriorated to the 'very poor' category with an overall AQI of 350, while IIT Delhi recorded the highest AQI of 428. This worsening pollution situation is important for UPSC aspirants to understand the health and environmental challenges faced by urban India and the policy responses required.

What was the air quality situation in Delhi-NCR on November 15, 2026?

  • Overall AQI level. Delhi's air quality index was 350, placing it in the 'very poor' category.
  • Highest AQI recorded. IIT Delhi recorded the highest AQI of 428, which falls under the 'severe' pollution category.
  • Monitoring stations data. Out of 54 monitoring stations, 47 reported 'very poor' air quality while seven stations recorded 'severe' levels.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How is air quality classified and why does it matter?

  • AQI classification. The Central Pollution Control Board classifies AQI from 0-50 as 'good', 51-100 'satisfactory', 101-200 'moderate', 201-300 'poor', 301-400 'very poor', and 401-500 'severe'.
  • Health implications. Very poor and severe AQI levels indicate hazardous air quality that can cause breathing difficulties, cough, and eye irritation, especially affecting outdoor workers.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What were the weather conditions influencing pollution levels?

  • Temperature and humidity. Delhi recorded a minimum temperature of 18.6°C, which was 5.1°C below the season's average, and relative humidity was 91% at 8:30 a.m.
  • Weather forecast. The India Meteorological Department predicted cloudy skies with light rain or drizzle and a maximum temperature of 27°C, conditions that can affect pollutant dispersion.

Back to basics: Air Quality Index (AQI)

The Air Quality Index is a numerical scale used to communicate how polluted the air currently is or how polluted it is forecast to become. It ranges from 0 to 500, with higher values indicating worse air quality. AQI categories help the public understand the health risks associated with air pollution levels and take necessary precautions.
