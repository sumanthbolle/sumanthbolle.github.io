India Releases Key Biodiversity Publications at CBD COP-17

At the 17th Conference of the Parties (COP-17) to the Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD) in Yerevan, India released four important publications on biodiversity conservation, biosafety, and access and benefit sharing. This highlights India's role in global biodiversity governance and its efforts to implement international commitments through policy, finance, and innovation.

India showcased its biodiversity conservation leadership by releasing key publications and emphasizing effective implementation and international cooperation at CBD COP-17.

Why is this in the news?

India released four key publications on biodiversity conservation and biosafety at the CBD COP-17 held in Yerevan, Armenia, highlighting its commitment to global biodiversity goals on October 28, 2026.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What publications did India release at CBD COP-17?

  • Four key publications. India released Guidelines for the Designation of India's Other Effective Area-Based Conservation Measures (I-OECMs) 2026, the 5th National Report on Cartagena Protocol on Biosafety, the Convention on Biological Diversity Handbook: Reference Guide and Indian Perspective, and India's ABS Success Story book.
  • Purpose of publications. These publications support recognizing conservation beyond protected areas, promote transparent biosafety governance, provide accessible reference material for CBD implementation, and highlight India's leadership in Access and Benefit Sharing (ABS).

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does this matter for biodiversity conservation?

  • Implementation focus. India emphasized translating global biodiversity commitments into effective implementation, sustainable financing, and lasting conservation outcomes.
  • Integration with livelihoods. Biodiversity in India is closely linked to people's lives, knowledge, and livelihoods, requiring environmental protection and development to reinforce each other.
  • LiFE principle. The Lifestyle for Environment (LiFE) principle was highlighted to promote sustainable choices and responsible consumption for harmony with nature.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does India contribute to global biodiversity goals?

  • Global Biodiversity Framework. India supports the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework by focusing on capacity building and resource mobilization beyond just adopting targets.
  • Access and Benefit Sharing. India stresses the Nagoya Protocol's principle of fair and equitable sharing of benefits from genetic resources, backed by policy reforms, capable institutions, digital innovation, and community engagement.
  • International cooperation. India advocates for international cooperation respecting national priorities, sharing practical knowledge, and expanding access to finance and technology, including South-South learning.

Back to basics: Convention on Biological Diversity (CBD)

The CBD is an international treaty aimed at conserving biodiversity, promoting sustainable use of its components, and ensuring fair sharing of benefits arising from genetic resources. Parties to the CBD meet regularly at Conferences of the Parties (COP) to negotiate and implement biodiversity goals globally. India, as a party, aligns its policies and actions with the CBD framework to protect its rich biodiversity.

Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because the event was organized by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change, Government of India, in partnership with India's National Biodiversity Authority (NBA) and UNDP, not solely by UNDP.
