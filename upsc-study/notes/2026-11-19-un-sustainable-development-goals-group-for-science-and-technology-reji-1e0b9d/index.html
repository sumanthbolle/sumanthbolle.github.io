UN Reconstitutes Science and Technology Group for Sustainable Development Goals

The United Nations Secretary-General reconstituted a 10-member group focused on promoting science, technology, and innovation to achieve the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs). This includes India's Shekhar Mande, highlighting India's engagement in global science and technology efforts for sustainable development.

The UN's revamped expert group on science and technology for SDGs includes India’s Shekhar Mande, reflecting India's role in global sustainable development initiatives.

Why is this in the news?

On November 18, 2026, the UN Secretary-General reconstituted a 10-member expert group to promote science, technology, and innovation for achieving the Sustainable Development Goals, with India represented by Shekhar Mande.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the reconstituted UN group for science and technology?

  • Group composition. The group consists of 10 distinguished experts from diverse geographic and disciplinary backgrounds, including developing and developed countries.
  • Purpose. It promotes science, technology, and innovation to support the achievement of the UN Sustainable Development Goals by 2030.
  • India's representation. Shekhar Mande, former CSIR Director General and current President of the Indian National Science Academy, is the sole Indian member.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for India and the UN SDGs?

  • India’s global role. India’s inclusion through Shekhar Mande highlights its active participation in international science and technology cooperation for sustainable development.
  • Link to national events. India is preparing to host the India International Science Festival in Pune, emphasizing its commitment to science and technology with international resonance.
  • UN’s inclusive approach. The group includes experts from civil society, private sector, and scientific communities, underscoring a comprehensive approach to sustainable development.

Back to basics: Sustainable Development Goals and Science-Technology Role

The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are a set of global objectives adopted by the United Nations to address challenges like poverty, inequality, and environmental degradation by 2030. Science, technology, and innovation are critical enablers for achieving these goals by providing solutions for sustainable development, improving health, education, and infrastructure, and fostering economic growth.
