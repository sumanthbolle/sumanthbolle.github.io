RBI Auctions Treasury Bills and Conducts Variable Rate Reverse Repo

The Reserve Bank of India conducted auctions for 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day Treasury Bills and held an Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction in November 2026. These operations influence liquidity and interest rates, important for monetary policy and economic stability.

RBI's Treasury Bill and variable rate reverse repo auctions manage liquidity and signal monetary policy stance.

Why is this in the news?

RBI announced the results of Treasury Bill auctions on November 11, 2026, and the Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction on November 12, 2026.

What are Treasury Bills and how did the RBI auction them?

  • Types and amounts. RBI auctioned 91-day, 182-day, and 364-day Treasury Bills with notified amounts of ₹8,000 crore, ₹8,000 crore, and ₹7,000 crore respectively.
  • Auction results. All notified amounts were fully accepted with cut-off prices at ₹98.5581, ₹96.8521, and ₹93.7900, corresponding to yields of 5.8681%, 6.5183%, and 6.6394% respectively.
  • Bids received and accepted. Competitive bids received exceeded notified amounts, with partial allotment percentages varying; non-competitive bids were mostly accepted near full amounts.

[1] Reserve Bank of India[2] Reserve Bank of India

What is the Variable Rate Reverse Repo (VRRR) auction and its outcome?

  • Auction purpose and tenor. The VRRR auction is a monetary policy tool where RBI absorbs excess liquidity from banks; the recent auction was for a 1-day tenor with a notified amount of ₹50,000 crore.
  • Auction results. Offers worth ₹58,149 crore were received, out of which ₹50,012 crore were accepted at a cut-off rate of 5.49% and a weighted average rate of 5.47%.
  • Partial acceptance. Partial acceptance percentage of offers received at the cut-off rate was 59.56%, indicating RBI's selective absorption of liquidity.

[3] Reserve Bank of India

Why do these auctions matter for monetary policy and the economy?

  • Liquidity management. Treasury Bills and VRRR auctions help RBI manage short-term liquidity in the banking system, influencing overnight interest rates.
  • Interest rate signaling. The yields on Treasury Bills and the cut-off rate on VRRR auctions signal the RBI's monetary policy stance to the market.
  • Economic stability. By adjusting liquidity and interest rates, these operations contribute to controlling inflation and supporting economic growth.

Back to basics: Monetary Policy Instruments

Monetary policy instruments include tools used by the central bank to regulate money supply and interest rates. Treasury Bills are short-term government securities used to manage liquidity. Reverse repo operations involve the central bank borrowing money from commercial banks to absorb excess liquidity, influencing short-term interest rates and inflation control.

Consider the following statements about the RBI's recent auctions: 1. The RBI accepted the full notified amount in the 91-day Treasury Bill auction. 2. The cut-off rate for the Overnight Variable Rate Reverse Repo auction was 5.49%.

  1. Both 1 and 2
  2. 1 only
  3. 2 only

Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 1 might be confused if one assumes partial allotment means less than full acceptance; however, the full notified amount was accepted. Statement 2 is correct as per the official auction result.

Mains question (15 marks)

Explain how RBI uses Treasury Bills and Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions as monetary policy tools to manage liquidity and inflation.

Explain: Describe the mechanism of these auctions and their impact on liquidity and inflation control.

Intro: RBI uses Treasury Bills and Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions to regulate liquidity and influence interest rates.

  • Treasury Bills are short-term government securities auctioned to absorb or inject liquidity based on market conditions.
  • Variable Rate Reverse Repo auctions allow RBI to borrow excess funds from banks, thereby reducing liquidity and managing inflation.
  • The cut-off yields and rates signal RBI's monetary stance and influence market interest rates.
  • These tools help maintain economic stability by balancing liquidity and controlling inflation.

Conclusion: Thus, these auctions are key instruments for RBI's monetary policy to ensure financial stability and growth.
