Cyclone Formation Over Bay of Bengal Expected to Hit Odisha-Andhra Coasts

A low-pressure system over the Bay of Bengal is forecasted to intensify into a cyclone by October 26, 2026, and make landfall on the Odisha-Andhra Pradesh coast by October 28. This development is crucial for disaster preparedness and climate impact assessment in coastal regions.

A cyclonic storm is likely to form over the Bay of Bengal and make landfall between Odisha and Andhra Pradesh by October 28, 2026, prompting extensive disaster management measures.

Why is this in the news?

On October 23, 2026, The Hindu reported that a weather system over the Bay of Bengal may intensify into a cyclone and cross the Odisha-Andhra coasts by October 28.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the expected timeline and path of the cyclone?

  • Formation timeline. A low-pressure area over east-central and adjoining southeast Bay of Bengal persisted on October 23, 2026, and is expected to intensify into a depression by October 24, a deep depression by October 25, and a cyclonic storm by October 26 morning.
  • Movement and landfall. The cyclone is likely to move west-northwestwards and make landfall between Odisha’s Gopalpur and Andhra Pradesh’s Kakinada coasts by October 28, 2026.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the government and disaster management responses?

  • Government preparedness. The Odisha government cancelled leaves of all government employees to ensure readiness and ordered officials to return to duty.
  • Warnings and advisories. A red alert for heavy rain was issued for Puri, Ganjam, and Gajapati districts on October 27, with several districts expected to experience very heavy rainfall from October 26.
  • Disaster response forces. National Disaster Response Force and Odisha Disaster Rapid Action Force are on standby, and district collectors can requisition their services during the crisis.
  • Fishermen advisories. Fishermen have been advised to return to the coast and trawler operators warned against forcing fishermen to fish in deep sea.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Back to basics: Cyclone formation and disaster management

Cyclones form over warm ocean waters when low-pressure areas intensify due to atmospheric conditions. They bring heavy rainfall and strong winds, causing damage especially in coastal regions. Disaster management involves forecasting, issuing warnings, mobilizing response forces, and evacuating vulnerable populations to minimize loss of life and property.
