PM Modi inaugurates Pune Metro Line 3 to enhance urban connectivity

Prime Minister Narendra Modi virtually inaugurated Pune Metro Line 3 and laid the foundation for four new metro corridors under Phase II, aiming to improve connectivity and boost economic growth in Pune. This development highlights the role of urban transport infrastructure in enhancing productivity and quality of life, relevant for UPSC aspirants studying inclusive growth and urban development.

The inauguration of Pune Metro Line 3 and expansion plans exemplify how improved urban transport infrastructure can drive economic growth and ease of living.

Why is this in the news?

On November 3, 2026, PM Modi inaugurated Pune Metro Line 3 and laid the foundation stone for four new metro corridors under Phase II to boost connectivity and economic productivity in Pune.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the key features of Pune Metro Line 3?

  • Line details. Pune Metro Line 3, also known as the Pink Line, is a 23.203-km corridor connecting Maan-Hinjawadi to Shivajinagar with 23 elevated stations.
  • Operational timeline. Passenger services started on November 4, 2026, initially covering 17 stations, with the remaining six stations expected to open in phases by January 2028.
  • Travel time and workforce. The corridor reduces travel time between Hinjawadi and Shivajinagar to around 40–45 minutes and employs 90 women train operators, with women constituting 50% of the workforce.
  • Sustainability. About 30% of the electricity for the project is expected to be sourced from solar power.
  • Development model. The project was developed under a public-private partnership (PPP) Design, Build, Finance, Operate and Transfer (DBFOT) model by Tata Projects and Siemens.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How will Pune Metro Phase II expand the network?

  • New corridors. Phase II includes four corridors: Line 2A (Vanaz to Chandani Chowk, 1.12 km), Line 2B (Ramwadi to Vitthalwadi, 11.63 km), Line 4 (Kharadi to Khadakwasla, 25.52 km), and Line 4A (Nal Stop to Manik Baug, 6.12 km).
  • Network expansion. These corridors will add approximately 44.4 km to Pune’s Metro network, enhancing connectivity across eastern, western, and southern parts of the city.
  • Investment. The estimated cost for Phase II is around ₹13,500 crore.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does Pune Metro expansion matter for economic growth?

  • Urban mobility and productivity. Improved metro connectivity reduces commuting time, which increases productivity and supports economic growth benefiting both poor and middle-class citizens.
  • Economic multiplier effect. Investments in public transport generate employment, attract businesses, and support broader economic activity.
  • Pune’s economic profile. Pune is an important hub for IT, automobiles, pharmaceuticals, startups, and manufacturing, making efficient public transport essential for its continued growth.
  • Government support. The Centre provided ₹5,000 crore for developing Pune Metropolitan Region as a major urban infrastructure and economic growth centre.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the broader context of metro development in India?

  • Network growth. India’s operational metro network has expanded from 248 km in 2014 to nearly 1,200 km in 26 cities.
  • Passenger usage. More than one crore passengers use metro services daily across the country.
  • Maharashtra’s metro infrastructure. Maharashtra has about 200 km of operational metro lines with over 680 km under construction, involving investments exceeding ₹2.5 lakh crore.
  • PPP model example. Pune Metro Line 3 is an example of government-private sector cooperation that can accelerate infrastructure development.

Back to basics: Urban Public Transport Infrastructure

Urban public transport infrastructure includes systems like metro rail that provide efficient, high-capacity transit within cities. Such infrastructure reduces travel time, eases traffic congestion, and supports economic activities by improving connectivity. Public-private partnership (PPP) models are often used to finance and operate these projects, combining government support with private sector efficiency.
