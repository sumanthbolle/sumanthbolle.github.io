Indian Railways Freight Loading Hits 145.07 Million Tonnes in October 2026

Indian Railways achieved its highest-ever freight loading in October 2026 with 145.07 million tonnes, marking an 8.32% growth over October 2025. This reflects improvements in logistics infrastructure and supports the country's economic growth, a key topic for UPSC aspirants studying infrastructure and economy.

Indian Railways recorded 145.07 million tonnes of freight loading in October 2026, growing 8.32% over the previous year, indicating enhanced logistics capacity and economic support.

Why is this in the news?

On 4 November 2026, the Ministry of Railways announced Indian Railways' highest-ever freight loading for October 2026, highlighting growth in key commodities and overall logistics performance.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What was the freight loading performance in October 2026?

  • Record freight loading. Indian Railways transported 145.07 million tonnes of freight in October 2026, an 8.32% increase from 133.93 million tonnes in October 2025.
  • Growth in major commodities. Food grains loading rose by 26.3%, domestic coal by 16.0%, other goods by 11.0%, clinker by 10.4%, domestic containers by 6.3%, iron ore by 5.0%, mineral oil by 1.9%, and finished steel by 0.5%.
  • Coal supply to power houses. To meet power house demand, coal transportation increased by 19.5%, averaging 469.8 rakes per day in October 2026 compared to 393.2 rakes per day in October 2025.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How did freight loading perform in the April–October 2026 period?

  • Cumulative growth. During April–October 2026, Indian Railways loaded 978.78 million tonnes of freight, a 4.67% increase over 935.10 million tonnes in the same period of the previous year.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What about passenger traffic during this period?

  • Passenger growth. Indian Railways carried 64.72 crore passengers in October 2026, up from 63.52 crore in October 2025.
  • April–October passenger figures. From April to October 2026, passenger count was 443.8 crore compared to 431.4 crore in the previous year, with non-suburban segments growing 4.2% and suburban segments 1.7%.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Back to basics: Freight loading and infrastructure

Freight loading measures the total weight of goods transported by rail, reflecting the capacity and efficiency of the railway network. It is a key indicator of logistics infrastructure performance and economic activity. Increased freight loading indicates better connectivity, operational efficiency, and support for industries like agriculture, mining, and manufacturing.
