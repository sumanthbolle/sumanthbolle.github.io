Prince William’s Earthshot Prize 2026 held in Mumbai highlights climate innovation

The sixth Earthshot Prize ceremony, founded by Britain’s Prince William to promote innovative climate solutions, was held in Mumbai on November 17, 2026. The event showcased global and Indian finalists, emphasizing India’s leadership in climate innovation and its role in addressing environmental challenges.

The Earthshot Prize 2026 in Mumbai highlighted innovative climate solutions with significant participation and recognition of Indian environmental initiatives.

Why is this in the news?

The Earthshot Prize 2026 ceremony was held in Mumbai on November 17, 2026, marking the first time the event took place in India and spotlighting Indian finalists and winners in global climate innovation.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the Earthshot Prize and its purpose?

  • Prize overview. The Earthshot Prize, launched by Prince William in 2020, is a global environmental award that encourages innovative solutions to climate change and major environmental challenges such as air pollution, waste, ocean conservation, and biodiversity loss.
  • Award structure. Each year, five winners are selected from 15 global finalists across five categories, receiving £1 million each to support their environmental work and scale their impact.
  • Five categories. The prize categories are Protect and Restore Nature, Clean Our Air, Revive Our Ocean, Build a Waste Free World, and Fix Our Climate.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does the Earthshot Prize 2026 in Mumbai matter for India and the world?

  • First India ceremony. The 2026 ceremony in Mumbai is the first time the Earthshot Prize awards have been held in India, highlighting the country's growing role in global climate innovation.
  • India’s leadership. India has produced more finalists and winners than any other country since the prize’s inception, with four Indian winners including Boomitra, Keyti, S4S Technologies, and Takachar.
  • Economic and environmental synergy. The Earthshot Prize CEO noted that India is undergoing a massive transformation in renewable energy and that environmental protection aligns with economic progress, with Indian companies attracting significant international investment after winning the prize.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Who were some notable finalists and what innovations were recognized?

  • Protect and Restore Nature. Finalists include Bhutan’s Princess Chimi Yangzom Wangchuck for forest protection sustaining 1.6 billion people downstream, and initiatives reconnecting wilderness across North America.
  • Clean Our Air. Innovations include affordable electric rickshaws by Mahindra Last Mile Mobility and air-pollution monitors for African countries.
  • Build a Waste Free World. Efforts to remove single-use plastics in the Philippines and South Korea’s food waste recycling revolution were recognized.
  • Revive Our Oceans and Fix Our Climate. Finalists working on ocean conservation and renewable energy transitions were also shortlisted.

What was the significance of the Mumbai event’s programme and outreach?

  • Event scale and participants. The event gathered 5,000 guests including environmental innovators, business leaders, philanthropists, and celebrities, with cultural performances and global streaming planned.
  • Focus areas. Discussions and initiatives focused on climate change, air quality, waste reduction, ocean conservation, and nature restoration.
  • Global visibility. The ceremony was hosted by filmmaker Karan Johar and streamed globally on Prime Video in over 40 languages starting November 30, 2026.

Back to basics: Climate Change and Environmental Innovation

Climate change refers to long-term shifts in temperature and weather patterns, mainly due to human activities like burning fossil fuels. Environmental innovation involves developing new technologies and solutions to reduce pollution, conserve natural resources, and mitigate climate impacts. Global awards like the Earthshot Prize encourage such innovations by providing recognition and funding to scale effective solutions.
