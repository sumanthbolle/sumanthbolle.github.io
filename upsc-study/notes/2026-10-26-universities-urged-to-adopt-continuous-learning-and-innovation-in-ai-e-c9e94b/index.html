Universities urged to adopt continuous learning and innovation in AI era

At a symposium organised by the Paari School of Business at SRM University-A.P., experts urged universities to embrace continuous learning, experimentation, and innovation to keep pace with rapid AI growth. This is important for UPSC aspirants as it highlights the evolving role of education in technology integration and governance.

Universities must move beyond incremental changes and integrate AI deeply into teaching, research, and organisational transformation to stay relevant in the AI era.

Why is this in the news?

The call for continuous learning and innovation in AI was made during a symposium on advancing management knowledge in the AI era held at SRM University-A.P. in October 2026.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why must universities embrace continuous learning and innovation in AI?

  • Need for continuous learning. Universities must move beyond incremental changes and adopt continuous learning, experimentation, and innovation to keep up with the exponential growth of artificial intelligence.
  • Technological capability. Developing countries like India need to strengthen technological capabilities and research networks to participate effectively in global AI innovation, as computing infrastructure is concentrated in wealthy economies.
  • Societal impact. Academic research should deliver tangible societal benefits beyond publications, adapting to intelligent environments to strengthen India's global research standing.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How are universities integrating AI in education and research?

  • Curriculum integration. The Paari School of Business is integrating AI into its curriculum and departmental workflows to prepare students for changing industry requirements.
  • Faculty development. Faculty development programmes are being conducted to improve the use of AI in teaching and research.
  • Research mentoring. An Early Career Researchers’ Publishing Boot Camp is planned to mentor scholars in preparing manuscripts for leading international academic publications.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What challenges and cautions are associated with AI adoption in organisations?

  • Cognitive engagement. Experts emphasised the importance of maintaining cognitive engagement and independent thinking, warning against excessive dependence on automated AI tools.
  • Organisational transformation. AI impacts organisational strategy and decision-making, requiring adaptation to systemic interdependence and operational complexity.
  • Financial system updates. There is a need to update financial systems to withstand emerging computational challenges posed by AI.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Back to basics: Continuous learning and AI integration in education

Continuous learning is an educational approach where individuals and institutions constantly update their knowledge and skills to keep pace with changing technologies and environments. In the context of AI, this means integrating artificial intelligence tools and concepts into teaching, research, and organisational practices to remain relevant and effective. This approach helps institutions adapt to rapid technological advancements and prepares students for future industry demands.
