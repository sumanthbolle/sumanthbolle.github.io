Kerala High Court Seeks State Response on Witness Protection Scheme Delay

The Kerala High Court has asked the State government to respond to a PIL highlighting the delay in implementing the witness protection scheme formulated in 2018. This issue is important for understanding judicial activism and the criminal justice system's challenges in protecting witnesses.

The Kerala High Court has urged the State government to urgently implement the witness protection scheme approved by the Supreme Court in 2019 due to ongoing delays.

Why is this in the news?

On October 30, 2026, the Kerala High Court sought the State government's response to a PIL pointing out the delay in implementing the witness protection scheme approved by the Supreme Court in 2019.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the witness protection scheme and its status in Kerala?

  • Scheme provisions. The scheme provides for threat assessment, protection measures, constitution of competent authorities and witness protection cells, periodic review, and a witness protection fund.
  • Status in Kerala. Though formulated in 2018 and approved by the Supreme Court in 2019, the scheme has not been implemented in Kerala, with the State government still considering a draft notification titled ‘Kerala Witness Protection Scheme 2026’.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why has the Kerala High Court intervened?

  • PIL filed. A retired Superintendent of Police filed a PIL highlighting the State's delay in implementing the scheme despite Supreme Court orders.
  • Court's observation. The Division Bench noted the State was lagging and expected urgent finalisation of the scheme's implementation.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What examples illustrate the need for witness protection?

  • Case references. The petitioner cited cases like the Attapady Madhu murder, criminal proceedings against former Bishop Franco Mulakkal, and the Nenmara double murder where witness protection was granted or needed.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the current procedural status?

  • Government responses. The State Police Chief informed that the draft notification is under consideration; the Home department and Kerala State Legal Services Authority have not issued orders but stated the matter is being considered.
  • Next steps. The State is expected to file a detailed affidavit by November 18, 2026.

Back to basics: Witness Protection Scheme

A witness protection scheme is a legal framework designed to safeguard witnesses who may face threats or intimidation for their testimony in criminal cases. It typically includes threat assessment, protective measures, dedicated authorities, and funds to ensure witness safety, thereby strengthening the criminal justice system by encouraging truthful testimony without fear.

  • The Kerala High Court’s intervention highlights judicial oversight in enforcing the Supreme Court-approved witness protection scheme.
  • By urging the State government to act, the judiciary addresses delays that undermine witness safety and justice delivery.
  • Such activism strengthens the criminal justice system by safeguarding witnesses, encouraging truthful testimony, and deterring intimidation.
  • However, it also requires cooperation from the executive for effective implementation.

Conclusion: Judicial activism plays a critical role in enforcing protective measures that uphold the rule of law and justice.
