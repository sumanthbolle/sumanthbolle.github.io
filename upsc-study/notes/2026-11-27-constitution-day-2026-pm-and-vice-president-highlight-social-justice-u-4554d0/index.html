Constitution Day 2026: PM and Vice-President Emphasize Social Justice

On Constitution Day 2026, the Prime Minister shared an OpEd highlighting the uniqueness of India’s Constitution and the government’s efforts to strengthen social and economic justice. The Constitution was also updated in all 22 Scheduled Languages, underscoring its role as a timeless compass for social justice and nation-building.

Constitution Day 2026 highlighted the Constitution’s role in advancing social justice and its updated editions in all Scheduled Languages.

Why is this in the news?

On November 26, 2026, Constitution Day was observed with the Prime Minister and Vice-President sharing articles emphasizing social justice and the Constitution’s significance, along with the release of updated Constitution editions in all 22 Scheduled Languages.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] Press Information Bureau

What did the Prime Minister highlight about the Constitution?

  • Uniqueness and journey. The Prime Minister highlighted the uniqueness of India’s Constitution and Constituent Assembly and described the Constitution as an inseparable part of his personal journey.
  • Strengthening the Constitution. He emphasized how the NDA Government has strengthened the Constitution and made efforts to boost social and economic justice.
  • Historical perspective. The Prime Minister noted that the Constitution was trampled over by previous rulers for five decades and criticized a political party’s shift from opposition to obstruction.

[1] Press Information Bureau

How does the Constitution guide social justice and nation-building?

  • Timeless compass. The Constitution remains a timeless compass guiding India towards social justice and a developed nation (Viksit Bharat).
  • Welfare initiatives. Welfare initiatives and landmark constitutional provisions focus on backward classes, economically weaker sections, and women, advancing Babasaheb Ambedkar’s vision for India.
  • Constitutional values. The importance of constitutional values in nation-building was underlined, emphasizing inclusiveness and social justice.

[2] Press Information Bureau

What is the significance of updating the Constitution in all Scheduled Languages?

  • Accessibility and inclusiveness. Updating the Constitution in all 22 Scheduled Languages ensures wider accessibility and promotes inclusiveness among India’s diverse linguistic communities.
  • Strengthening democratic conscience. These updated editions help citizens understand constitutional provisions better, fostering democratic awareness and participation.

Back to basics: Constitution of India

The Constitution of India is the supreme law that lays down the framework defining fundamental political principles, establishes the structure, procedures, powers, and duties of government institutions, and sets out fundamental rights, directive principles, and duties of citizens. It aims to secure justice, liberty, equality, and fraternity for all citizens, thereby promoting social and economic justice.
