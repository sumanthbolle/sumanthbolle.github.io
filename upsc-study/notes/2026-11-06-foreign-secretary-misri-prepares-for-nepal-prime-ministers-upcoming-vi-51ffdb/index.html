Foreign Secretary Misri Prepares for Nepal PM’s Visit to India

Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri met Nepal Prime Minister Balendra Shah in Kathmandu on November 5, 2026, to discuss initiatives for the upcoming Nepal PM visit to India. This meeting highlights ongoing India-Nepal bilateral cooperation and regional diplomacy important for UPSC aspirants studying India’s neighbourhood relations.

Foreign Secretary Misri’s talks with Nepal’s leadership set the stage for enhanced bilateral cooperation during the Nepal PM’s visit to India.

Why is this in the news?

Foreign Secretary Vikram Misri’s November 5, 2026 meeting with Nepal PM Balendra Shah in Kathmandu focused on preparing for the PM’s upcoming visit to India.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What was discussed during Foreign Secretary Misri’s visit to Nepal?

  • Bilateral cooperation. Misri highlighted wide-ranging engagement and progress in diverse areas of India-Nepal cooperation, discussing possible outcomes and initiatives for the Nepal PM’s upcoming visit to India.
  • Key sectors discussed. Talks covered commerce and development partnership, hydropower, physical connectivity, border management, energy, people-to-people ties, digital connectivity, and artificial intelligence.
  • Disaster assistance. Nepali leaders expressed gratitude for India’s swift humanitarian and technical assistance after the Bhotekoshi floods.
  • Border dispute context. The Lipulekh-Kalapani-Limpiyadhura border dispute was acknowledged as a sticking point, with Nepal seeking multilateral talks but India preferring bilateral resolution.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for India-Nepal relations and UPSC?

  • Diplomatic engagement. The meeting reflects India’s approach to neighbourhood diplomacy by engaging directly with Nepal’s leadership to strengthen ties and address contentious issues.
  • Regional stability. Resolving border disputes and enhancing cooperation in sectors like energy and connectivity contribute to regional stability and development.
  • Exam relevance. Understanding India-Nepal bilateral relations, including border issues and cooperation areas, is important for GS Paper 2 topics on India and its neighbourhood.

Back to basics: India’s Neighbourhood Policy

India’s neighbourhood policy focuses on maintaining friendly and cooperative relations with neighbouring countries through diplomacy, economic cooperation, and conflict resolution. It aims to promote regional stability, connectivity, and development while addressing bilateral issues through dialogue. Understanding this policy helps explain India’s engagement with Nepal and other neighbours.

  2. 2 only

Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because India prefers bilateral resolution of the border dispute, while Nepal sought multilateral talks.
