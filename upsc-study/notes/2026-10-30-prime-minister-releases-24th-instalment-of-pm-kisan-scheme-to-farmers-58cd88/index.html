PM Releases 24th Instalment of PM-KISAN Scheme to Over 9.34 Crore Farmers

On October 30, 2026, Prime Minister Narendra Modi released the 24th instalment of the PM-KISAN scheme, transferring more than ₹18,690 crore directly to over 9.34 crore farmers. This event highlights the government's ongoing commitment to farmer welfare and rural economic empowerment through direct income support and agricultural infrastructure development.

The 24th PM-KISAN instalment transferred ₹18,690 crore directly to 9.34 crore farmers, reinforcing government support for agricultural income and infrastructure.

Why is this in the news?

Prime Minister Narendra Modi released the 24th instalment of the PM-KISAN scheme on October 30, 2026, transferring ₹18,690 crore to over 9.34 crore farmers, marking a significant step in agricultural income support.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is the PM-KISAN scheme and its purpose?

  • Income support to farmers. PM-KISAN provides income support to eligible farmer families through Direct Benefit Transfer to help meet agricultural and household expenses, supporting livelihood security.
  • Scale of beneficiaries. Over 9.34 crore farmers across India benefit from the scheme, with more than ₹4.47 lakh crore disbursed through the first 23 instalments.
  • Complementary initiatives. The scheme is part of broader government efforts including Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana, Kisan Credit Card, MSP, and Per Drop More Crop to improve farmer incomes and agricultural resilience.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What happened during the 24th instalment release event?

  • Release details. On October 30, 2026, the Prime Minister released the 24th instalment of PM-KISAN at Banaskantha, Gujarat, transferring over ₹18,690 crore to farmers' bank accounts.
  • Participation and regional impact. Union Agriculture Minister Shivraj Singh Chouhan participated from Raisen, Madhya Pradesh, and over 35.38 lakh farmers in Odisha received ₹3,000 crore as part of this instalment.
  • Agricultural infrastructure focus. The Prime Minister dedicated 521 units developed under the Agriculture Infrastructure Fund and inaugurated 12 horticulture clusters to promote high-value crops and enhance farmer incomes.

[1] Press Information Bureau[2] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does PM-KISAN and related initiatives matter for farmers and the economy?

  • Supporting farmer welfare. Direct income support helps farmers manage agricultural and household expenses, reducing financial stress and improving livelihood security.
  • Agricultural infrastructure development. Investments of approximately ₹1.75 lakh crore under the Agriculture Infrastructure Fund across 2.28 lakh projects strengthen storage, processing, and market access.
  • Promoting crop diversification. Horticulture clusters encourage cultivation of high-value crops, increasing income potential and making farming more remunerative.
  • Alignment with self-reliance goals. These initiatives support the vision of a prosperous, resilient, and self-reliant agricultural sector, contributing to food and nutritional security.

Back to basics: Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) in Welfare Schemes

Direct Benefit Transfer is a mechanism where government subsidies or financial assistance are transferred directly into the bank accounts of beneficiaries. This reduces leakages, ensures timely payments, and increases transparency and efficiency in welfare delivery. PM-KISAN uses DBT to provide income support directly to farmers, helping them meet agricultural and household needs without intermediaries.
