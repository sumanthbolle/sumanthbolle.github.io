SEBI Proposes Mandatory AML/CFT Certification for Principal Officers

The Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI) has proposed mandatory Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT) certification for principal officers of all registered market intermediaries. This aims to enhance their expertise in identifying suspicious transactions and complying with AML/CFT regulations, which is crucial for maintaining market integrity.

SEBI's proposal mandates AML/CFT certification for principal officers to strengthen compliance and oversight in financial markets.

Why is this in the news?

On November 6, 2026, SEBI proposed mandatory AML/CFT certification for principal officers of registered intermediaries to improve their competence in regulatory compliance.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is SEBI's new proposal on AML/CFT certification?

  • Mandatory certification. SEBI proposes that principal officers of all registered intermediaries must obtain AML/CFT certification to ensure they have adequate expertise in anti-money laundering and counter-terror financing measures.
  • Certification scope. The certification covers knowledge of the Prevention of Money Laundering Act, customer due diligence, beneficial ownership, risk-based client classification, enhanced due diligence, transaction monitoring, targeted financial sanctions, reporting obligations, and emerging risks from new products and technologies.
  • Compliance timeline. Both existing and newly appointed principal officers would have six months to comply with the certification requirement.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does SEBI want mandatory AML/CFT certification?

  • Ensuring expertise. Principal officers play a central role in identifying suspicious transactions, assessing money laundering risks, and reporting to the Financial Intelligence Unit-India, so SEBI wants to standardize their knowledge and competence.
  • Current gaps. Currently, there is no specific AML/CFT certification requirement, leading to varying levels of expertise among principal officers across intermediaries.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How will SEBI implement this certification?

  • Collaboration with NISM. SEBI has collaborated with the National Institute of Securities Markets (NISM) to develop a certification examination covering AML/CFT provisions in the securities market.
  • Existing certification. NISM launched the Series-XXIV AML/CFT certification examination in February 2025, which will be used for this purpose.
  • Consultation process. SEBI has invited public comments on the proposal until November 27, 2026.

Back to basics: Anti-Money Laundering and Countering the Financing of Terrorism (AML/CFT)

AML/CFT refers to laws, regulations, and procedures designed to prevent criminals from disguising illegally obtained funds as legitimate income and to stop the financing of terrorism. Financial institutions and intermediaries must monitor transactions, conduct customer due diligence, and report suspicious activities to regulatory authorities to maintain financial system integrity.
