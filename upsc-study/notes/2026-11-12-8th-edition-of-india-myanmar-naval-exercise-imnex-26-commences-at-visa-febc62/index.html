8th Edition of India-Myanmar Naval Exercise (IMNEX-26) Begins at Visakhapatnam

The 8th India-Myanmar Naval Exercise (IMNEX-26) is underway at Visakhapatnam from 10 to 14 November 2026, involving ships from both navies. This exercise is important for UPSC aspirants as it highlights India's maritime cooperation with a neighbouring country and its strategic vision for regional security.

IMNEX-26 strengthens India-Myanmar naval cooperation and supports India's regional maritime security goals.

Why is this in the news?

The Ministry of Defence announced the commencement of the 8th India-Myanmar Naval Exercise (IMNEX-26) at Visakhapatnam on 10 November 2026.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What is IMNEX-26 and where is it held?

  • Exercise details. IMNEX-26 is the 8th edition of the bilateral naval exercise between India and Myanmar, conducted at Visakhapatnam from 10 to 14 November 2026.
  • Participating ships. Myanmar Navy Ship UMS King Sin Phyu Shin and Indian Navy ships INS Rana and INS Sumedha are participating in the exercise.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What activities are included in IMNEX-26?

  • Harbour Phase activities. The Harbour Phase includes professional interactions, Subject Matter Expert Exchanges (SMEEs), cross-deck visits, sporting engagements, and operational discussions.
  • Sea Phase activities. The Sea Phase involves coordinated maritime operations, tactical manoeuvres, and exercises to enhance interoperability between the navies.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does IMNEX-26 matter for India’s maritime strategy?

  • Maritime cooperation platform. IMNEX provides a platform for the Indian and Myanmar navies to exchange expertise, improve mutual understanding, and develop operational synergy.
  • Regional security alignment. The exercise aligns with India's vision of MAHASAGAR, reflecting a shared commitment to maritime cooperation and a secure, stable regional environment.

Back to basics: Bilateral Naval Exercises

Bilateral naval exercises are coordinated training operations between two countries' navies to improve mutual understanding, operational compatibility, and maritime security cooperation. They typically include professional exchanges, tactical drills, and joint operations at sea to build trust and enhance regional stability.

Mains question (15 marks)

Examine the significance of India-Myanmar naval exercises like IMNEX-26 in enhancing regional maritime security and bilateral relations.

Examine: Explain how naval exercises contribute to maritime security and strengthen bilateral ties between India and Myanmar.

Intro: India-Myanmar naval exercises serve as key instruments for maritime cooperation and regional security.

  • They facilitate professional exchanges and build operational synergy between the two navies.
  • Such exercises support India's strategic vision of a secure and stable maritime environment in the region.
  • They enhance mutual trust and understanding, contributing to stronger bilateral relations.
  • These exercises also demonstrate India's commitment to cooperation with neighbouring countries in the Indo-Pacific.

Conclusion: IMNEX-26 exemplifies India's approach to regional maritime security through practical naval collaboration.
