India implications

  • Telecom consumers may gain easier access to complaint and appeal channels through provider mobile applications in addition to existing channels.
  • Persons with disabilities receive an explicit inclusion focus through dedicated desks or helplines and accessibility features on provider digital platforms.
  • Internet Service Providers covered by the amended applicability criterion must organise round-the-clock complaint-centre support, with human assistance available for at least sixteen hours daily.
  • Service providers face stronger internal compliance responsibilities through appellate authority requirements, feedback publication, CEO or board review and an oversight committee.

Way forward

  • Publish clear service-level standards and reasons for complaint and appeal decisions.
  • Ensure multilingual, low-bandwidth, screen-reader-compatible and assisted offline access alongside mobile applications.
  • Audit automated and AI-enabled support for escalation failures, bias, accessibility and availability of human assistance.
  • Track outcome indicators such as resolution quality, repeat complaints, appeal reversals and accessibility-related grievances.
