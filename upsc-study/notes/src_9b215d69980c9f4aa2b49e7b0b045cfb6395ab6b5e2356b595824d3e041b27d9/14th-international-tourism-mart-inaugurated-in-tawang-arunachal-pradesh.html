India implications

  • Inclusive tourism can support livelihood diversification and employment for youth in remote and culturally diverse areas.
  • Destination development requires coordination among the Union Government, States, local communities and private tourism stakeholders.
  • Tourism policy in ecologically sensitive regions must balance economic opportunity with biodiversity conservation, cultural protection, visitor safety and carrying capacity.
  • Better visitor experiences require last-mile connectivity, reliable infrastructure, skills, digital promotion and responsible waste management.

Way forward

  • Adopt destination-level carrying-capacity and environmental-management plans before scaling visitor promotion.
  • Ensure local communities participate in planning and gain access to homestays, guiding, handicrafts, transport and other tourism value chains.
  • Improve last-mile connectivity, visitor safety, sanitation, waste management, digital access and disaster preparedness.
  • Link tourism promotion with skill development, quality standards and transparent monitoring of locally retained benefits.
