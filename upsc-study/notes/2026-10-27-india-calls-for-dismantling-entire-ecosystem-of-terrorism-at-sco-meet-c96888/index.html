India urges dismantling terrorism ecosystem at SCO meet

At the 25th SCO Council of Heads of Government meeting in Dushanbe, India called for dismantling the entire ecosystem of terrorism, emphasizing no justification or double standards in tackling terrorism. This stance highlights India's approach to regional security and counter-terrorism diplomacy relevant for UPSC preparation.

India called on SCO members to collectively dismantle the entire ecosystem of terrorism, rejecting any justification or double standards in addressing terrorism.

Why is this in the news?

India's Defence Minister Rajnath Singh spoke at the 25th SCO Council of Heads of Government meeting on October 20, 2026, calling for collective action against terrorism and supporting economic cooperation among SCO members.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What was India's main message on terrorism at the SCO meet?

  • No justification for terrorism. India stated there can be no justification for terrorism and no double standards in addressing it, emphasizing that those who finance, support, or provide safe havens to terrorists have no place.
  • Call to dismantle terrorism ecosystem. India called on SCO member states to dismantle the entire ecosystem of terrorism collectively.
  • Impact of terrorism. Terrorism causes loss of innocent lives, disrupts investment and commerce, radicalizes societies, and deprives youth of opportunities.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How did India link terrorism and regional cooperation at SCO?

  • Adherence to SCO principles. India urged the organisation to follow sovereign equality, consensus, and mutual respect among members.
  • Economic cooperation focus. India supported an action plan for multilateral trade and economic cooperation for 2026-2030 to strengthen supply chains, trade, investment, and technology use amid global uncertainties.
  • Connectivity and sovereignty. India highlighted connectivity projects like the International North-South Transport Corridor and Chabahar Port but stressed that such projects must respect sovereignty and territorial integrity.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What new initiatives and forums did India highlight at the SCO meeting?

  • New SCO bodies. India welcomed establishing the Universal Centre in Tashkent and the Anti-Drug Centre in Dushanbe as permanent SCO bodies.
  • SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum. India invited SCO members to participate in the inaugural SCO Civilisational Dialogue Forum to be hosted by India later in 2026.
  • India's development initiatives. India showcased its digital public infrastructure, start-up ecosystem, women-led development initiatives, and efforts on clean energy and climate resilience.

Back to basics: Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO)

The SCO is a Eurasian political, economic, and security alliance aimed at promoting cooperation among member states. It addresses issues like terrorism, trade, connectivity, and regional development through consensus and respect for sovereignty. Understanding SCO helps explain India's regional diplomacy and multilateral security efforts.
