India-Myanmar Bilateral Tri-Services HADR Exercise 'Rahat Mitra' Concludes

The inaugural India-Myanmar bilateral Tri-Services Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief (HADR) Exercise 'Rahat Mitra' was held on 5-6 November 2026 in Chennai. This exercise enhanced operational interoperability and coordination between the defence forces of both countries, which is important for regional security and disaster management.

The 'Rahat Mitra' exercise strengthened India-Myanmar defence cooperation and collective preparedness for disaster response.

Why is this in the news?

The Ministry of Defence announced the conclusion of the inaugural India-Myanmar bilateral Tri-Services HADR Exercise 'Rahat Mitra' held on 5-6 November 2026 in Chennai.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What was the 'Rahat Mitra' exercise?

  • Exercise overview. 'Rahat Mitra' was a two-day bilateral Tri-Services Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief exercise conducted between the Defence Forces of India and Myanmar on 5 and 6 November 2026 in Chennai.
  • Participating forces. Personnel from the Indian Navy, Indian Army, Indian Air Force, and a delegation from the Myanmar Defence Forces participated to enhance interoperability and coordination.

[1] Press Information Bureau

What activities were conducted during the exercise?

  • Table-Top Exercise. A Table-Top Exercise based on a severe cyclone scenario facilitated discussions on coordinated response mechanisms and joint disaster management protocols.
  • Demonstrations. Demonstrations included obstacle clearance and bridge-laying by the Indian Army, shipborne HADR capabilities by the Indian Navy, multi-domain HADR capabilities across maritime, land and air domains, flood relief operations, maritime search and rescue, and casualty evacuation by the Indian Air Force.
  • Infrastructure and medical support. INS Adyar and Communication Network Centre showcased shore-based disaster response and communication infrastructure, while Military Hospital Chennai demonstrated field medical triage and disaster relief camp management.

[1] Press Information Bureau

Why does the 'Rahat Mitra' exercise matter for India and Myanmar?

  • Enhancing disaster response. The exercise improved mutual understanding, operational experiences, and best practice exchange in planning and executing disaster relief operations.
  • Strengthening bilateral defence ties. It reinforced the commitment of both countries to advance bilateral defence cooperation and collective preparedness for disaster response.
  • Contributing to regional stability. The exercise supports regional stability, maritime cooperation, and effective humanitarian assistance and disaster relief.

Back to basics: Humanitarian Assistance and Disaster Relief (HADR)

HADR refers to coordinated efforts by military and civilian agencies to provide aid and relief during natural disasters or humanitarian crises. It involves planning, logistics, medical aid, search and rescue, and infrastructure support to reduce human suffering and restore normalcy. Exercises like 'Rahat Mitra' help improve interoperability and coordination among forces for effective disaster response.
