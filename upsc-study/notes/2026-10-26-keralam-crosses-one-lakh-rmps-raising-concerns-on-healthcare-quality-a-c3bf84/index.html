Keralam Crosses One-Lakh Registered Medical Practitioners Mark

Keralam's number of registered medical practitioners (RMPs) has exceeded one lakh, raising concerns about healthcare quality and unemployment among medical graduates. This development is important for understanding challenges in healthcare delivery and employment in the medical sector.

Keralam has over one lakh registered medical practitioners, with a high registration rate between 2021 and 2025, leading to concerns about medical graduate unemployment and healthcare quality.

Why is this in the news?

On October 25, 2026, The Hindu reported that the number of registered medical practitioners in Keralam crossed the one-lakh mark, based on data from the Kerala State Medical Councils.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the current status of registered medical practitioners in Keralam?

  • Number of RMPs. There are 1,13,023 registered medical practitioners in Keralam as of October 8, 2026, according to Kerala State Medical Councils data.
  • Recent registrations. Between 2021 and 2025, 31,446 doctors were registered, accounting for 32.75% of the total RMPs.
  • Annual registration details. Registrations per year were 5,981 in 2021, 5,734 in 2022, 5,283 in 2023, 7,065 in 2024, and 7,383 in 2025, with 2,695 registered till October 8, 2026.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this growth in RMPs matter for healthcare and employment?

  • Doctor to population ratio. With Keralam's population around 3.62 crore, there is roughly one MBBS doctor for every 450 people if at least 80,000 registered doctors remain in the state.
  • Concerns on unemployment. The large number of medical graduates has led to high unemployment among new doctors, with reports of vacant MBBS seats in other states indicating a similar risk for Keralam.
  • Healthcare quality risks. Experts warn that this trend could lead to commercialization of healthcare and stress on existing medical infrastructure.
  • Need for infrastructure improvement. The Indian Medical Association suggests focusing on improving infrastructure and faculty deployment in existing medical colleges rather than sanctioning new ones.

Back to basics: Registered Medical Practitioners and Healthcare Workforce

Registered Medical Practitioners (RMPs) are doctors officially registered with a medical council to practice medicine. The number of RMPs in a region affects healthcare access and quality. A balanced doctor-to-population ratio is essential to meet healthcare needs and avoid unemployment or underemployment among medical professionals.
