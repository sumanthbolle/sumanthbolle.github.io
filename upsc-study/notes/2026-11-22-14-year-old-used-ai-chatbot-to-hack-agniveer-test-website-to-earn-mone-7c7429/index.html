14-Year-Old Used AI Chatbot to Hack Agniveer Test Website

A teenager from Haryana used an AI chatbot to hack a test website for former Agniveers and tried to sell personal data on the dark web. This case highlights cybersecurity and AI misuse concerns relevant to defence recruitment and national security.

A 14-year-old exploited AI tools to breach the Agniveer test portal, exposing personal data of around 4,000 personnel.

Why is this in the news?

On November 21, 2026, Delhi Police revealed that a 14-year-old used AI chatbots to hack the Agniveer test website and attempted to sell sensitive personnel data on the dark web.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What happened in the Agniveer test website hack?

  • AI chatbot used for hacking. The teenager used an AI chatbot to identify valuable data and to find a way to hack into the Agniveer test portal.
  • Data accessed. Preliminary investigations show the hacker accessed only names and addresses of around 4,000 Agniveer personnel, with no evidence of marks, ranks, or sensitive records being compromised.
  • Motive and method. The boy was motivated by financial gain and attempted to sell the data for $500 on the dark web.
  • Investigation status. Delhi Police’s Intelligence Fusion and Strategic Operations unit recovered data from the juvenile’s devices and is verifying the breach's extent and authenticity.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for cybersecurity and governance?

  • AI misuse in cybercrime. This case illustrates how AI tools can be misused to breach digital infrastructure, raising concerns about AI governance and cybersecurity in sensitive sectors.
  • Impact on defence recruitment. The breach targeted a portal used by former Agniveers for employment opportunities, exposing vulnerabilities in defence-related digital systems.
  • Role of government bodies. The National Informatics Centre hosts the portal, but the Armed Forces and ministries own the personnel data, highlighting the need for coordinated security measures.
  • Juvenile justice considerations. Since the accused is a minor, the police have involved the Juvenile Justice Board for further action.

Back to basics: Cybersecurity and AI governance

Cybersecurity involves protecting digital systems and data from unauthorized access or attacks. AI governance refers to the policies and controls that regulate the use of artificial intelligence to prevent misuse, especially in sensitive areas like national security and public digital infrastructure. Understanding these concepts helps in analyzing how AI tools can be exploited and how to safeguard against such threats.
