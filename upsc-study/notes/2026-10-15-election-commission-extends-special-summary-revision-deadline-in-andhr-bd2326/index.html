ECI Extends Special Intensive Revision Deadline in Andhra Pradesh

The Election Commission of India (ECI) has extended the deadline for the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls in Andhra Pradesh to October 22, 2026, with the final electoral rolls to be published on October 30, 2026. This update is important for UPSC aspirants as it highlights the ongoing constitutional process of electoral roll revision, which impacts voter registration and election preparedness.

The ECI extended the SIR deadline in Andhra Pradesh, setting October 30, 2026, for final electoral roll publication.

Why is this in the news?

On October 14, 2026, the ECI announced the extension of the SIR deadline in Andhra Pradesh and the new date for publishing the final electoral rolls.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What is the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls?

  • Definition and purpose. The SIR is a comprehensive process to update and revise electoral rolls to ensure accuracy and inclusion of eligible voters.
  • Qualifying date. The revision in Andhra Pradesh is being conducted with July 1, 2026, as the qualifying date for voter eligibility.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What changes did the ECI announce regarding the SIR in Andhra Pradesh?

  • Deadline extension. The ECI extended the deadline for the notice phase and disposal of claims and objections to October 22, 2026.
  • Final roll publication. The final electoral rolls will now be published on October 30, 2026.
  • Reason for extension. The extension followed a request from the State Chief Electoral Officer of Andhra Pradesh.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does this matter for elections and voter rights?

  • Ensuring accurate voter lists. The SIR process helps maintain updated and accurate electoral rolls, which are essential for free and fair elections.
  • Inclusion of eligible voters. Extending deadlines allows more time to address claims and objections, ensuring eligible voters are not excluded.
  • Transparency and communication. The ECI directed authorities to widely publicize the revised schedule and inform political parties to maintain transparency.

Back to basics: Electoral Roll Revision

Electoral roll revision is the process of updating the list of eligible voters to ensure accuracy before elections. It involves adding new voters, removing ineligible names, and correcting errors. This process is essential to uphold the democratic principle of universal adult suffrage and to conduct free and fair elections.
