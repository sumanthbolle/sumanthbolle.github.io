Need for a Comprehensive Space Security Doctrine in India

India's growing dependence on space-based infrastructure makes it vulnerable to disruptions that can affect national security and daily life. Experts call for a clear doctrine to address evolving threats in space, including non-physical interference.

India requires a comprehensive space security doctrine to safeguard its critical space infrastructure against evolving threats like jamming, spoofing, and cyber interference.

Why is this in the news?

On November 18, 2026, experts highlighted the need for India to develop a comprehensive doctrine on space security to address the changing nature of threats in space.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

Why does India need a space security doctrine?

  • Growing dependence on space. India increasingly relies on space infrastructure for communications, navigation, weather forecasting, disaster management, agriculture, banking, logistics, and critical infrastructure.
  • Military reliance. Space capabilities support the armed forces through communications, positioning, surveillance, timing, and situational awareness, making space integral to national resilience and strategic autonomy.
  • Impact of disruptions. Disruptions in space can affect markets, governance, security, and society, highlighting the need for protective measures.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

What are the evolving threats in space?

  • From destruction to disruption. Threats have shifted from physical destruction of satellites to disruption of the services they provide, complicating security challenges.
  • Non-physical interference. Methods like jamming, spoofing, cyber interference targeting satellite networks and ground stations, and electronic warfare can degrade signals without damaging spacecraft.
  • Complex attribution. Non-physical attacks make it difficult to attribute responsibility and respond effectively, increasing the complexity of space security.

[1] The Hindu · Secondary

How does space security affect India’s national security?

  • Strategic autonomy. Space is an essential component of India's strategic autonomy, and vulnerabilities in space can undermine national resilience.
  • Integrated infrastructure. The vulnerability lies not only in satellites but also in the wider architecture connecting space assets to users on Earth.
  • Need for clarity. Greater clarity is needed on how different forms of interference should be understood and assessed to formulate effective responses.

Back to basics: Space Security

Space security involves protecting satellites and space-based infrastructure from threats that can disrupt their functioning. These threats include physical attacks like destruction and non-physical methods such as jamming, spoofing, cyber attacks, and electronic warfare. Since many critical services depend on space assets, ensuring their security is vital for national security, economic activity, and everyday life.

Practice: MCQ, Mains and revision

Prelims MCQ

Consider the following statements about India's space security challenges: 1. Threats to space assets include both physical destruction and non-physical disruptions like jamming and cyber interference. 2. Disruptions in space infrastructure affect only military operations and have minimal impact on civilian sectors. Which of the statements given above is/are correct?

  1. 1 only
  2. 2 only
  3. Both 1 and 2
  4. Neither 1 nor 2
Answer and trap

Answer: (a). Statement 2 is incorrect because disruptions in space infrastructure affect a wide range of civilian sectors such as communications, banking, agriculture, and disaster management, not just military operations.

Quick revision

Why does India need a space security doctrine?
Because India depends heavily on space infrastructure for critical civilian and military functions, making it vulnerable to disruptions.
What are some non-physical threats to space assets?
Jamming, spoofing, cyber interference, and electronic warfare can disrupt satellite services without physically damaging the spacecraft.
How do non-physical space threats complicate security responses?
They make it difficult to attribute attacks and respond effectively since the spacecraft may not be physically damaged.
